Your Journey Begins

Long before your name is etched into the annals of the shinobi world,
before your chakra is shaped into jutsu that shake the earth,
there is a single moment that defines every warrior’s fate.

A moment where the world is quiet…
where destiny leans in…
and whispers your true calling.

You stand at the threshold of legend —
a path walked by heroes, rogues, and ghosts of forgotten clans.
The villages watch from the shadows.
The winds carry rumors of rising threats.
And somewhere, deep within you,
a spark waits to ignite into something far greater.

Today, young shinobi…
your story begins.
