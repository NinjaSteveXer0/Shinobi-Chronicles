# 1. Purpose

All ten Academy Origins are GOLDEN / FROZEN at the player-facing Origin-content level.

This document does **not** reopen Story, scene order, actors, Battle mechanics, PL, route outcomes, dialogue, backdrop, or Golden presentation.

It performs the missing reward-spectrum pass for the eight non-Kakashi / non-Wasabi Origins using the Stephen-approved Wasabi benchmark method.

Binding method:

1. **Choices matter.** A player choice that materially resolves into action/history must create the exact authorised consequence channel(s) supported by what happened.
2. **Origin completion pays.** Every valid sealed Origin gets the universal one-shot **100 Ryō Starting Purse**.
3. **PL Battle victory pays.** Every player PL Battle victory gets non-zero Ryō; special material loot remains optional and causal.
4. **Relevant specialist actions develop the relevant thing.** Tracking, investigation, sealing, sensing, extraction, endurance, technical training, etc. do not collapse into cash.
5. **Chronicle consequences persist.** Knowledge, Shared History, observer assessment, future opportunity eligibility and exact factual outcomes remain available to CE.
6. **CE stays invisible.** The player sees outcomes, rewards and development; never raw source IDs, hidden evidence weights, relationship maths or event-eligibility flags.
7. **No filler loot.** Common -> Uncommon -> Rare -> Legendary remains the reward rarity ladder, but no Origin is forced to drop an Item/Weapon just to make the reward list look varied.
