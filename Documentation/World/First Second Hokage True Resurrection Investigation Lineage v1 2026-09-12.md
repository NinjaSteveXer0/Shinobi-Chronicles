## 1. Purpose

This document converts the closed CE/Codex history of Hashirama Senju and Tobirama Senju's true non-Edo resurrection into a reusable **history-generated investigation lineage** for Shinobi Chronicles.

It is deliberately not one exposition quest and not a resurrection mechanic.

The lineage exists so that a Chronicle whose Character develops the right Knowledge, capabilities, relationships, service history and evidence chain may gradually discover that the official/expected historical picture is incomplete.

Core rule:

> **Expected History != Recorded History.**

And:

> **A Character may discover that a true resurrection occurred without gaining Access, Competence, Power or Mastery over the method.**

This consumes:

- `Documentation/Coordination/First Second Hokage Non-Edo Living Resurrection Canon and Discovery Boundary 2026-09-12.md`;
- `Documentation/Coordination/Hashirama Tobirama True Resurrection Recovered Causal Canon Addendum 2026-09-12.md`;
- `Documentation/Coordination/Expected History vs Recorded History Chronicle Divergence Doctrine 2026-09-12.md`;
- World capability-responsive event ecology / multi-path synergy authority.

Where those authorities preserve protected gaps, this World lineage also fails closed.

## 2. Closed World Truth consumed by this lineage

The investigation is uncovering a real hidden causal history, not inventing one.

Binding World Truth:

1. Hashirama and Madara both survive the Valley of the End.
2. Hashirama returns to Konoha through Asura-derived chakra + Sage chakra.
3. Hashirama later chooses to let his life end naturally under a restoration plan devised with Mito Uzumaki and Tobirama Senju.
4. the broad method basis is Uzumaki Fūinjutsu/Kinjutsu informed by Tobirama's Edo-Tensei-derived knowledge of life/death/soul-state boundaries.
5. Hashirama genuinely dies.
6. Mito + Tobirama genuinely restore Hashirama to ordinary living state.
7. Hashirama remains the same persistent person.
8. Hashirama remains publicly dead and leaves the First-Hokage role behind.
9. Tobirama later names Hiruzen Third Hokage during the Kinkaku-squad crisis.
10. Tobirama genuinely dies.
11. Hashirama + Mito genuinely restore Tobirama through the same broad true-restoration principle.
12. Tobirama remains the same persistent person.
13. neither restoration is Edo Tensei.
14. neither restoration erases the genuine death that preceded it.
15. resurrection does not restore prior political office by itself.

Protected gaps remain protected:

- exact technique/formula name;
- exact seal/Kinjutsu/medical implementation;
- exact body/soul transition mechanics;
- exact material, sacrifice, chakra or life cost;
- exact preparation/post-death viability window;
- exact failure conditions;
- exact repeatability;
- precise post-restoration biological age/body-state/chakra consequences;
- exact surviving documentation package;
- exact institutional Knowledge distribution;
- player reproducibility.

The Hashirama-attends-Tobirama's-funeral-anonymously idea remains a **strong Story candidate, not World-recorded fact**, unless later promoted by Writing/CE.

## 3. Lineage identity and activation model

Stable lineage ID:

`world_chain_first_second_true_restoration_investigation_v1`

Content class:

`history_generated_followup + multi_path_any + compound_path_synergy`

Default Story status:

`OPTIONAL / NON-MANDATORY`

It may later intersect Main Story, but Main Story participation is not required for the lineage to exist.

The lineage never activates because the engine notices that Hashirama/Tobirama resurrection is interesting lore.

Activation requires a **committed factual lead** already legitimate for that Chronicle.

Valid lead families include:

- archival contradiction;
- witnessed living-Hokage contradiction;
- medical evidence inconsistent with reanimation/death assumptions;
- Uzumaki seal provenance;
- Kinjutsu/life-death research provenance;
- Tobirama research history;
- Senju/Uzumaki/clan/service history;
- ANBU/Root/institutional records legitimately accessed;
- rare historical Knowledge;
- recovered physical evidence/provenance;
- trusted witness testimony;
- political/service access to restricted history;
- Tracking/False Identity/counter-surveillance history tied to a post-death sighting.

Capability possession alone does not spawn evidence.

## 4. Observer-Knowledge ladder

The lineage uses a staged Knowledge ladder. These are World authoring states, not mandatory engine enum names.

### K0 — Expected/public history only

The observer has no legitimate resurrection evidence.

Projection:
- no resurrection quest marker;
- no hidden denominator;
- no `Hashirama alive?` codex tease;
- no secret-location leak.

### K1 — Historical contradiction

The Character possesses legitimate evidence that the ordinary historical account does not fully reconcile.

Possible conclusion:

`some accepted chronology is incomplete or deliberately simplified`

Not yet authorised:
- resurrection conclusion;
- performer identity;
- method lineage.

### K2 — Survival / post-death inconsistency

The Character can establish one bounded impossible-looking fact, e.g. evidence of Hashirama after a date/event where expected history would place him absent/dead, or a Tobirama-related contradiction after his genuine death.

Possible conclusion:

`the expected death-state/history is incomplete`

Still not necessarily:

`true resurrection occurred`.

### K3 — Genuine-living-state evidence

Medicine or equivalent factual evidence may establish that the relevant subject was/ is genuinely biologically alive rather than an Edo manifestation, corpse-state or temporary summon.

Possible conclusion:

`this was a living person, not merely a dead manifestation`

### K4 — Uzumaki / forbidden-method provenance

Fūinjutsu/Kinjutsu/provenance evidence may establish:

- Uzumaki sealing lineage participated;
- forbidden life/death methodology was involved;
- Tobirama's death-state/reanimation research is conceptually relevant;
- the outcome does not match ordinary Edo Tensei.

### K5 — Hashirama true-restoration proof

Sufficient independent evidence may establish the broad hidden history:

`Hashirama genuinely died later in life and was genuinely restored to ordinary living state through the Hashirama/Mito/Tobirama plan.`

This also establishes:

`Valley of the End != Hashirama death`.

It does not reveal the exact ritual.

### K6 — Tobirama true-restoration proof

Sufficient independent evidence may establish:

`Tobirama genuinely died after naming Hiruzen Third Hokage and was later genuinely restored by Hashirama + Mito.`

Hiruzen's succession remains valid.

### K7 — Broad causal synthesis

A Chronicle with the required evidence and relevant expertise may establish the strongest currently authorised historical conclusion:

- both restorations were true-living resurrection;
- both preserved persistent person identity;
- broad method lineage = Uzumaki Fūinjutsu/Kinjutsu + Tobirama-derived death-state knowledge;
- the result was not Edo Tensei;
- exact formula/cost/reproducibility remain unknown.

### K8 — Protected frontier

The Character may know that deeper questions exist:

- exact formula;
- exact cost;
- exact timing/failure conditions;
- surviving components;
- reproducibility.

K8 does **not** authorise any answer merely because the player reached the frontier.

Fail closed until another owner closes those facts.

## 5. Investigation stage matrix

The following stages are authored opportunities. They are not required to occur in a fixed linear order unless the exact Chronicle predicates demand it.

### Stage A — `restoration_chain_archival_contradiction`

Purpose:
Create the first legitimate discrepancy without naming resurrection.

Preferred Konoha hosts:
- `KON-O17` Academy Archive Annex;
- `KON-A08` Shinobi Record Archive — Legacy Chamber when authorised;
- `KON-S22` Missing Names Wall after legitimate records contradiction;
- Hokage Administration internal archive contexts.

Possible evidence classes:
- date conflict;
- duty/service record that should not exist under expected history;
- sealed correction page;
- witness reference whose chronology is impossible under public assumptions;
- provenance mismatch between public memorial history and restricted record.

Outcomes:
- preserve evidence;
- copy only partial information;
- report it;
- conceal it;
- leave it untouched;
- lose one source while retaining the contradiction itself.

### Stage B — `restoration_chain_valley_survival_trace`

Purpose:
Separate the Valley of the End from Hashirama's true later death.

Host class:
- Land of Fire / Valley-of-the-End historical route or surviving records;
- Konoha historical archive;
- Uchiha/Senju provenance where legitimately available.

Binding discoverable fact:

`Hashirama survived the Valley of the End and returned to Konoha through Asura-derived chakra + Sage chakra.`

Do not turn Asura/Sage survival into the later resurrection mechanism.

### Stage C — `restoration_chain_uzumaki_provenance`

Purpose:
Establish that Mito/Uzumaki sealing-forbidden knowledge participated in a hidden life/death project.

Preferred Konoha secret host:
- `KON-S08` Uzumaki Seal Chamber, **only after its own discovery/access predicates are satisfied**.

Other valid hosts:
- Public Fūinjutsu Workshop through legitimate expert referral;
- authenticated Uzumaki records/objects;
- external provenance recovered by a mission/event.

Fūinjutsu may identify lineage/style/interface evidence.
Kinjutsu may identify forbidden life/death provenance.
Neither alone reconstructs the full method.

### Stage D — `restoration_chain_tobirama_research_overlap`

Purpose:
Establish conceptual overlap between Tobirama's Edo research and the separate true-restoration project.

Preferred hosts:
- restricted Konoha archive;
- research provenance object;
- ANBU/Root or Hokage records only where legitimately accessible;
- authenticated Tobirama research references.

Permitted conclusion:

`Tobirama's understanding of death/reanimation states informed another project.`

Forbidden collapse:

`therefore the resurrection was Edo Tensei`.

### Stage E — `restoration_chain_hashirama_true_life_proof`

Purpose:
Allow a sufficiently prepared Chronicle to prove that Hashirama's later death was genuine and that he later existed as a genuinely living person.

Evidence should require at least two independent classes unless Story directly supplies a witnessed authenticated current encounter.

Possible evidence classes:
- medical/vital record;
- seal/provenance record;
- independent witness history;
- identity-confirmed post-death action;
- restricted institutional file;
- authenticated current encounter.

Medicine can distinguish living physiology from reanimation only where exact evidence exists.

Outcome boundary:

`Hashirama true restoration proven` != `exact technique learned`.

### Stage F — `restoration_chain_hashirama_anonymous_afterlife`

Purpose:
Explore the consequence of Hashirama choosing public death and anonymity after restoration.

This stage may produce:
- post-death sightings;
- altered appearance/presentation traces;
- observer-history contradictions;
- False Identity / Tracking / counter-surveillance branches;
- relationship/political consequences if the Character considers disclosure.

Do not hard-lock the funeral-attendance scene here.

If Writing later promotes that beat, this stage may consume it.

### Stage G — `restoration_chain_tobirama_death_succession`

Purpose:
Preserve the real Kinkaku-squad death and Hiruzen succession before any resurrection revelation.

Required historical conclusion:

`Tobirama named Hiruzen Third Hokage and genuinely died.`

This stage exists specifically so later resurrection discovery cannot accidentally retcon the succession.

### Stage H — `restoration_chain_tobirama_true_life_proof`

Purpose:
Prove that the same persistent Tobirama later returned to ordinary living state.

Possible evidence classes:
- authenticated post-death activity;
- medical living-state evidence;
- Mito/Hashirama provenance;
- institutional record;
- trusted witness;
- direct current encounter.

Outcome:

`Tobirama genuinely restored` while preserving:
- real death;
- Hiruzen's legitimate appointment;
- same persistent person identity.

### Stage I — `restoration_chain_causal_synthesis`

Purpose:
Combine the evidence into the strongest currently authorisable conclusion.

This is the flagship Medicine + Fūinjutsu + Kinjutsu payoff when that exact Chronicle genuinely earned all required evidence.

Result may establish:

- genuine life after genuine death;
- Uzumaki sealing/forbidden-method provenance;
- Tobirama death-state research contribution;
- clear non-Edo outcome;
- same-person continuity;
- broad participant chain.

Still protected:
- exact ritual;
- exact cost;
- exact reproducibility;
- player resurrection capability.

### Stage J — `restoration_chain_protected_frontier`

Purpose:
Generate later unresolved pressure rather than fake closure.

Possible questions:
- does any exact formula survive?
- did Mito preserve a component?
- was the method intentionally fragmented?
- what did it cost?
- can it fail?
- can it be repeated?

Until future authority closes those questions, valid outcomes include:
- `insufficient evidence`;
- `record missing`;
- `fragment authentic but incomplete`;
- `source refuses disclosure`;
- `evidence deliberately destroyed/withheld`;
- `question remains open`.

An unresolved mystery is a legitimate Chronicle outcome.

## 6. Capability-responsive lenses

### Medicine

May legitimately establish, where evidence supports it:
- living physiological viability;
- healing/biological activity inconsistent with corpse/reanimation state;
- that a record describes an actually living body;
- that evidence is insufficient for exact resurrection mechanics.

Medicine does not provide soul mechanics or the ritual.

### Fūinjutsu

May establish:
- Uzumaki seal lineage;
- continuity/anchoring/interface/stabilisation provenance where present;
- relation between surviving seal fragments without granting Mastery;
- that a seal structure is not ordinary public craft.

Fūinjutsu does not equal resurrection capability.

### Kinjutsu

May establish:
- forbidden life/death methodology;
- historical technique family/provenance;
- conceptual overlap with reanimation/death-state research;
- risk significance that a normal researcher may miss.

Kinjutsu does not invent the exact forbidden Technique.

### Medicine + Fūinjutsu

Possible stronger conclusion:

`the evidence indicates a seal-mediated process associated with restoration to genuine living physiology.`

Still missing:
- exact forbidden-method lineage / complete causal explanation.

### Fūinjutsu + Kinjutsu

Possible stronger conclusion:

`the process used forbidden Uzumaki-style life/death sealing principles distinct from ordinary Edo reanimation.`

Still missing:
- independent biological proof of true living state unless another evidence class supplies it.

### Medicine + Kinjutsu

Possible stronger conclusion:

`the subject returned to living physiology through forbidden life/death intervention rather than remaining in a dead/reanimated state.`

Still missing:
- exact seal lineage unless other evidence provides it.

### Medicine + Fūinjutsu + Kinjutsu

With sufficient independent evidence, this triad may unlock `restoration_chain_causal_synthesis`.

It may establish the broad historical truth.

It may **not** produce:
- resurrection Technique ownership;
- recipe/formula;
- automatic Skill;
- automatic Mastery;
- resurrection service button;
- generic `revive dead Character` action.

## 7. Other high-value route interactions

### Rare Knowledge / clan history

May recognise old Senju/Uzumaki/Tobirama references, terminology, dates or participants that would otherwise look mundane.

### Political / service recognition

May create legitimate audience/archive access, but also institutional obligation or scrutiny.

### ANBU / Root history

May provide access to observer-specific records only when the Character legitimately possesses the required Knowledge/access.

Being inside ANBU/Root through Story does not automatically reveal all historical records.

### Tracking

May support investigation of post-death movement/sightings where a real trace exists.

Tracking does not prove identity by itself.

### False Identity / counter-surveillance

May become relevant to Hashirama's deliberate anonymous post-restoration existence and to observer-history contradictions.

Altered presentation != new Registry person.

### Crafting / material provenance

May authenticate a surviving object/component as period-correct or participant-linked without identifying the whole method.

### Relationship / witness history

A trusted elder, clan member, institutional contact, Hashirama/Tobirama themselves or another authorised witness may open or close different evidence routes.

Trust does not guarantee disclosure.

## 9. Outcome, failure and non-action doctrine

This lineage explicitly consumes:

**Battle loss != mission failure**  
**mission failure != zero value**

Examples:

- the Character may fail to secure a witness but preserve the document;
- lose a pursuit but establish that the sighting was physically possible;
- damage one seal fragment but still authenticate Uzumaki provenance;
- be denied archive access but create a political obligation/contact;
- prove genuine living state while failing to identify the method lineage;
- identify forbidden provenance but choose not to report it;
- walk away and preserve anonymity / institutional neutrality;
- allow another team/institution to take custody of evidence;
- deliberately destroy a copy to protect a living person while preserving the fact that the evidence once existed.

Legitimate earlier Knowledge/evidence is not erased because the final encounter was lost.

Non-action is not automatically punishment.

## 11. Recurrence / persistence

The historical truth is one causal history.

Do not replay:

`discover Hashirama resurrection` repeatedly as unrelated quests.

Instead:

- each evidence occurrence has its own stable occurrence ID;
- committed evidence remains committed through save/load;
- later events consume accumulated Knowledge/history;
- source-loss may change future access without deleting already committed Knowledge;
- repeated archive/map reopen does not reroll the conclusion;
- one-shot evidence/reward entitlement cannot double-grant;

`world_occ_restoration_chain_*`
