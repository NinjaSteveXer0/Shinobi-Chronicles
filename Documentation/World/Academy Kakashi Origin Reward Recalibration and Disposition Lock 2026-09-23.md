# Academy Kakashi Origin — Reward Recalibration and Disposition Lock

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Owner:** Stephen / World — Missions / Events / Rewards  
**Status:** **STEPHEN-APPROVED REWARD RECALIBRATION — KILL-SPECIFIC MATERIAL REWARD EXCLUDED / UNRESOLVED**

## 1. Purpose

This lock records Stephen's approval of the revised Academy Kakashi Origin reward structure after the full route/reward-anchor review.

It supersedes older Kakashi reward values only where this document states a new value or new tier.

Preserve the project separation:

- Battle reward != Story/debrief reward;
- material reward != specialist-development evidence;
- History/Knowledge/observer consequence != material reward;
- capture != kill != release;
- no universal morality score;
- no hidden morality punishment;
- no reward may be silently removed because an authored route is morally darker or lighter.

## 2. Approved Battle-cash recalibration

Immediate Battle Ryō is based on the factual encounter difficulty, not on one privileged Story config ID.

### 1-v-1

A qualifying Kakashi Origin one-opponent Battle victory pays:

**50 Ryō immediate**

This must apply consistently to legitimate MI / PS / AMT one-opponent configurations rather than paying on one route ID and zero on another equivalent legitimate one-opponent fight.

The existing MI-specific **Field Recovery Pill ×1** source remains unchanged and still obeys its once-per-Origin/idempotence rule.

### 2-v-1

A qualifying Kakashi Origin two-opponent Battle victory pays:

**100 Ryō immediate**

No generic Item, loot, or generic Character EXP is implied by this cash value.

### Failed-pickpocket 3-v-1

The failed direct-pickpocket Battle:

`academy_kakashi_origin_battle_amt_ps_mi_3v1`

is the hardest current Academy Kakashi Origin Battle.

A committed Kakashi victory pays:

**200 Ryō immediate**

This is separate from:

- action-derived discipline/Stamina development;
- package recovery;
- custody;
- intelligence;
- exceptional-field-execution recognition;
- Academy Training Tantō eligibility;
- terminal/debrief rewards.

The existing **+25 exceptional-field-execution** source and **Academy Training Tantō** eligibility remain valid in addition to the 200 Ryō Battle reward when their predicates are met.

## 3. Existing approved objective/intelligence rewards retained

The following remain approved:

## 4. Live-capture reward tiers

The former flat rule where one live captive and three live captives both paid the same +25 is superseded.

For participants actually delivered alive to legitimate institutional custody through Kakashi's occurrence:

- **one live captive delivered:** **25 Ryō total live-custody reward**;
- **two live captives delivered:** **50 Ryō total live-custody reward**;
- **all three live captives delivered:** **100 Ryō total full-extraction reward**.

These are outcome tiers, not per-body farmable additive sources.

### Institution-neutral material value

Delivering the full live captive set to:

- ANBU; or
- Uchiha Police Force

receives the **same material full-extraction reward**.

The institution selected remains meaningful Chronicle/history/Knowledge/Access context and may affect later World/CE opportunities. It must not become a hidden morality-based better/worse payout.

## 5. Release

Release receives:

- **no immediate material reward** merely for release;
- **no material penalty or clawback**;
- all Battle/action/objective rewards legitimately earned before release remain intact.

The principal value of release is Chronicle/shared-history consequence: a surviving participant can remember the decision and remain available for later CE/World/Story use, including recurring named-character or hotspot-event consequences where later authored.

## 7. Lethal action and Special Jōnin development — semantic direction

Separate from the unresolved kill-specific material reward:

An authored lethal action can be legitimate specialist-development work.

Current Special Jōnin authority already uses persistent qualification evidence rather than a generic `Assassination EXP` currency.

For `covert_operations.assassin`, relevant competencies include:

- `target_isolation`;
- `covert_execution`.

The correct rule is:

> A kill is not automatically Assassin evidence. A committed authored assassination attempt may project Assassin qualification evidence when the factual resolver outcome demonstrates the exact authorised competency.

Kakashi Origin lethal resolution is intentionally simplified to only:

- `KILLED`; or
- `ESCAPED`.

Do **not** restore the older SURVIVED / INTERRUPTED outcome set.

`KILLED` may support successful Assassin evidence where exact competency predicates are factually demonstrated.

`ESCAPED` preserves lethal intent / attempted-assassination Chronicle history and may support partial specialist evidence only for competencies genuinely demonstrated; it must not counterfeit successful execution.

Exact evidence tags/significance/capstone treatment remain Progression / Development authority.
