# Shinobi Chronicles — Battle Loss, Mission Failure and Alternate Reward Outcome Doctrine

**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Owner:** World / Missions / Events / Rewards  
**Status:** **BINDING WORLD AUTHORING REINFORCEMENT — CONSOLIDATES EXISTING DURABLE AUTHORITY; NO NEW XP CURRENCY**

---

## 1. Purpose

This document makes explicit a long-standing Shinobi Chronicles principle that is already distributed across current Combat, Progression, Writing and World authority:

> **Battle loss != mission failure.**
>
> **Mission failure != zero reward, zero development or erased Chronicle value.**
>
> **A later loss does not erase legitimate participant-attributable development already earned through committed actions.**

This is a consolidation/reinforcement document. It does not create a new EXP system, override domain ownership, or make every failed mission pay the same material rewards as success.

Authoritative sources already preserving the doctrine include:

## 2. Separate the result layers

A resolved occurrence may have several independent result dimensions:

1. **Battle result** — victory, defeat, withdrawal, disengage, unresolved or another exact Combat result;
2. **mission/objective result** — success, partial success, failure, preserved objective, lost objective, aborted objective or another authored state;
3. **factual participant actions** — what each participant actually did before resolution;
4. **Knowledge / evidence gained**;
5. **Chronicle / CE history created**;
6. **relationship/shared-history consequences**;
7. **recognition / qualification / development evidence**;
8. **domain-owned Skill / discipline / proficiency development where already authorised**;
9. **future access / opportunity / suppression consequences**;
10. **material/economic entitlement**.

These dimensions must not be collapsed into one binary `WIN = rewards / LOSS = nothing` switch.

---

## 3. Battle loss is not automatically mission failure

Combat answers what happened in Battle.

World/Story answers what happened to the mission/objective from the full factual occurrence.

Examples:

- the team may lose a confrontation but successfully extract the protected person;
- the player may disengage while preserving the dispatch/evidence/objective;
- a hostile may escape while the player still acquires critical intelligence;
- a participant may be defeated after completing specialist work that remains historically true;
- a Battle may be lost while another autonomous participant completes a different objective;
- a mission may succeed without defeating every hostile.

Therefore:

> **Battle victory != mission success automatically.**
>
> **Battle defeat != mission failure automatically.**

---

## 4. Mission failure is still a real Chronicle outcome

Where an authored mission genuinely fails, that failure remains a committed historical result rather than a null state.

A failed/partial occurrence may still legitimately preserve or produce:

- Chronicle history;
- observer-bounded Knowledge;
- Rumour/evidence/intelligence;
- relationship/shared-history evidence;
- specialist-action evidence;
- recognition or institutional interpretation evidence;
- future opportunity changes;
- new problems, contacts, enemies, witnesses or follow-up chains;
- exact domain-owned development consequences from legitimate actions already performed;
- different material/economic entitlements where separately authored.

Failure may close one opportunity while opening another. Difference is not punishment.

Do not author failure as a mandatory rollback to a pre-occurrence world unless an exact system explicitly owns that retry/reset behaviour.

---

## 5. EXP / development rule

The project does **not** author a universal `loss EXP` or `failure EXP` bundle.

Instead, development follows the owning domain and the committed action that actually occurred.

### Existing-domain EXP / proficiency / discipline development

Where an existing owning system authorises EXP/development from legitimate use, the participant may receive that development from the committed action even if the later Battle or mission is lost.

A later defeat does not erase a valid earlier committed learning-by-doing occurrence merely because the overall outcome was unfavourable.

Exact amount, category, eligibility and persistence remain owned by the relevant progression/Skill/proficiency/discipline authority.

### Special Jōnin development

Special Jōnin does **not** use a generic Specialisation EXP ledger.

A legitimate specialist action may instead create exact provenance-bearing qualification evidence at occurrence time under the existing Progression contract.

Mission failure does not automatically erase qualifying evidence.

Conversely, mere participation in a failed mission does not automatically prove competency.

Preserve:

> **mission failure != zero evidence automatically**
>
> **attempt != successful competency automatically**
>
> **committed partial work != erased history**

---

## 6. Choice / CE consequence rule

Player action, inaction, withdrawal, observation, refusal, protection, pursuit, concealment, disclosure and other legitimate choices may all create factual Chronicle consequences.

CE should retain what actually happened and may later use that history to alter the eligible possibility space.

Examples:

- losing a fight after protecting a witness may preserve relationship/shared-history value;
- failing to recover an object may make a later recovery/investigation chain possible;
- choosing not to intervene may allow another actor to resolve the situation and create different observer/history networks;
- being defeated while exposing an enemy method may still preserve Knowledge;
- withdrawing to protect evidence may later support judgement/qualification evidence;
- a failed deception may still create observer history that matters later.

Do not reduce these to hidden good/bad morality scoring.

---

## 7. Material reward boundary

Material/economic reward is independent from development/history value.

A failure may legitimately have:

- no Ryō;
- reduced Ryō;
- partial contractual payment;
- reimbursement only;
- retained recovered material where custody permits;
- a different authorised item/access/contact reward;
- no material reward but substantial Chronicle/Knowledge/development value.

Success may also have no material reward where the occurrence does not author one.

Therefore:

> **material reward difference != punishment**
>
> **meaningful outcome != material loot required**

---

## 8. No outcome erasure

Once a factual occurrence and its authorised consequences are committed:

- later Battle defeat must not delete prior Knowledge;
- later mission failure must not delete prior committed History;
- save/load must not reroll the result into success;
- retry must not duplicate one-shot development/reward from the same occurrence;
- a later successful re-attempt is a new occurrence with its own provenance;
- participants retain only the consequences they factually earned/experienced.

---

## 9. World authoring requirement going forward

For every activated Story mission, formal mission, World event or side occurrence, World should author the relevant outcome channels independently rather than assuming one success/failure reward switch.

When loss/failure is possible, content should ask:

1. What factual objective actually failed?
2. What still happened before that failure?
3. What Knowledge/history survives?
4. What participant-specific specialist/development work was legitimately committed?
5. What relationships/observer histories changed?
6. What future World possibilities change?
7. What material entitlement remains, changes or disappears?
8. Does the mission continue, transform, close or create a follow-up?

This is especially important for the 500-seed World reservoir and capability-responsive event ecology.

---

## 10. Canonical shorthand

Preserve these as authoring rules:

- **Battle loss != mission failure.**
- **Mission failure != zero reward/value.**
- **Loss does not erase legitimate action-time EXP/development/evidence.**
- **Failure may reward differently, not necessarily less in every dimension.**
- **Material reward != total Chronicle reward.**
- **Non-action can have consequences without being punishment.**
- **CE records factual history; it does not convert all outcomes into a morality score.**
- **Different outcome != bad ending automatically.**
- **No new generic XP ledger is created by this doctrine.**
