**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Owner:** World / Missions / Events / Rewards

## 1. Decision

The World content reservoir is expanded from the previously intended 500-seed target to **710 authored seed addresses**.

The new Wave 2 expansion adds **210 new seeds**, distributed evenly across all seven current country/village pairs:

- Land of Wind + Sunagakure — 30;
- Land of Water + Kirigakure — 30;
- Land of Lightning + Kumogakure — 30;
- Land of Fire + Konohagakure — 30;
- Land of Earth + Iwagakure — 30;
- Land of Grass + Kusagakure — 30;
- Land of Rain + Amegakure — 30.

Each pair receives **15 regional** and **15 village** seeds.

This expansion is not a requirement to surface 210 markers at once and is not an Alpha mandate to implement every row. It enlarges the legitimate authored possibility space from which CE / World selection can surface a bounded, Chronicle-relevant subset.

Canonical rule remains:

> **A deeper world means a deeper eligible deck, not a noisier map.**

## 2. Why this expansion exists

The earlier reservoir correctly established ordinary village/regional free play, capability-responsive events and history-generated follow-ups, but the current production repo does not contain the five detailed legacy `001–500` catalogue wave files referenced by the v0.1 index. The v0.1 index itself warns that the index is not proof those detailed files landed.

Therefore this package does not pretend those missing rows are durable production content. It preserves the older 500-target concept as historical reservoir intent and adds a **new, fully durable 501–710 wave** whose rows actually exist on `main`.

The expansion also increases two areas Stephen explicitly wants to feel substantially richer:

1. **Main Story choice → optional World consequence → later Story recognition**;
2. **relationship-building / shared-history events** with teammates, mentors, rivals, foreign contacts, civilians, service providers, family and other recurring people.

Main Story does not need to solve every mystery, debt, relationship, witness, object, clue or survivor it creates.

Later Story may recognise:

- what the player learned;
- whom they trusted;

- who owes whom;
- what they kept secret;
- what they disclosed;
- who survived;
- who took custody;
- what evidence was preserved or lost;
- which optional lead was never pursued;

Story must query the actual facts. It must never reduce this to `sideQuestComplete=true`.

## 5. Relationship ecology — binding World rule

Relationship content is not a universal `friendship XP` system.

World relationship events consume factual relationship/shared-history state and create more factual relationship/shared-history state.

A relationship opportunity may become eligible from combinations such as:

- current team membership;
- repeated mission participation;
- prior rescue/protection;
- prior abandonment or non-participation;
- shared secret;
- disagreement;
- debt/obligation;
- trust already shown;
- rivalry or competition;
- mentor/student history;
- family history;
- foreign-contact history;
- recurring civilian/service-provider contact;
- shared loss;
- shared success;
- witnessed capability;
- previous lie or concealed truth;
- political/institutional recognition;
- prior World-event participation.

Possible committed outcomes include, without a universal score:

- increased practical trust;
- increased personal openness;
- respect without affection;
- rivalry;
- resentment;
- disappointment;
- repaired trust;
- unresolved tension;
- obligation/debt;
- boundary respected;
- boundary violated;
- shared joke / private reference;
- shared secret;
- learned preference/value/fear;
- willingness to cooperate later;
- refusal to cooperate later;
- new contact route;
- invitation/access;
- participant remembering the player's absence;
- no material change when that is the honest outcome.

**Relationship change != morality score.**  
**Relationship event != romance.**  
**Relationship event != automatic recruitment.**  
**Relationship event != ownership.**

Romance may exist only where separately authored and appropriate; it is one possible relationship expression, never the default interpretation of closeness.

World may author relationship opportunities for:

- institutional contact;
- former opponent who survived;
- protected witness;
- person previously failed or disappointed;
- participant created by an earlier optional event.

Natural contexts include:

- mission downtime;
- travel;
- camp/watch rotation;
- meals;
- post-Battle recovery;
- hospital waiting;
- training;
- equipment preparation;
- border delays;
- rain/sandstorm/weather shelter;
- festival/market/civic gathering;
- memorial/funeral/remembrance;
- escort downtime;
- repair/service errands;
- shared investigation;
- quiet return from a failed or successful mission.

The point is to let people become meaningful through repeated attributable history, not through dialogue menus detached from the world.

## 7. Four relationship-content modes

These are authoring semantics, not mandatory engine enum names.

### `relationship_baseline`

Ordinary social/shared-life event that can occur because participants are currently together and have enough existing context.

### `relationship_history_followup`

Exists because a prior occurrence created a specific interpersonal fact: rescue, lie, secret, disagreement, debt, loss, promise, shared discovery, etc.

### `relationship_capability_intersection`

A relationship becomes relevant because one participant observed or can participate in a real capability route. Example: a teammate who has repeatedly watched the protagonist use Fūinjutsu asks to understand the risk rather than simply praising the Stat.

### `relationship_world_pressure`

An external problem forces an interpersonal choice: protect the teammate or pursue evidence, reveal a secret for their safety, accept help, refuse help, trust their judgement, split the team, take responsibility, etc.

These modes may overlap with existing `baseline_world`, `single_path_responsive`, `multi_path_any`, `compound_path_synergy` and `history_generated_followup` ecology.

## 8. Team content must not become compulsory affection

A current team should have many chances to build shared history, but the game must not assume every team becomes best friends.

Repeated play may legitimately produce:

- strong trust;
- functional professionalism;
- mutual irritation but reliable field cooperation;
- rivalry;
- protectiveness;
- emotional distance;
- ideological disagreement;
- fracture;
- reconciliation;
- one-sided attachment;
- loyalty to the team but not to a particular member.

World provides situations. Characters answer according to their own Knowledge, personality and history.

## 9. Country/village identity without stereotype locking

Each country/village wave uses geography and institutional texture to make content feel local, while preserving individual autonomy.

- Wind/Suna may naturally use desert travel, wells, caravans, canyon routes, puppet/craft culture, border logistics and resource pressure.
- Water/Kiri may use islands, harbours, channels, fog, fishing, ferries, coastal settlements and maritime custody/trade.
- Lightning/Kumo may use high ridges, bridges, waterfalls, mountain roads, storm infrastructure, training heights and difficult logistics.
- Fire/Konoha may use forests, rivers, pilgrimage/courier roads, dense civic institutions, archives, team life and current Story-generated consequences.
- Earth/Iwa may use plateaus, quarries, mines, canyon settlements, tunnels, rock routes and heavy logistics.
- Grass/Kusa may use borderland routes, wetlands, overgrown ruins, medicinal/botanical spaces, brokers and competing local interests.
- Rain/Ame may use towers, canals, elevated walkways, industrial waterworks, flooded lower districts, surveillance pressure and information exchange.

These are World hosting textures, not claims that every person from a country shares one personality or politics.

**seed authored != host calibrated != executable != implemented != runtime validated != Golden GREEN**.

## 12. Discovery, non-action and recurrence

Relationship/offshoot events follow existing World doctrine:

- eligibility precedes selection;
- selection does not imply discovery;
- discovery does not imply actionability;
- non-action is legitimate history;
- leaving a conversation unresolved is not automatically failure;
- another participant may act without the protagonist where causally legitimate;
- repeatable family != literal replay of one historical occurrence;
- a new occurrence gets a new occurrence identity;
- UI reopen/save-load does not reroll committed occurrence state.

## 13. Rewards

Many relationship events intentionally have **no material reward**.

Valid value may instead be:

- shared history;
- Knowledge;
- trust/refusal facts;
- contact route;
- access/invitation;
- future assistance eligibility;
- obligation/debt;
- later Story recognition;
- future event eligibility;
- specialist evidence only where an owning system already authorises it.

Do not pay the player Ryō merely for having a conversation unless the situation independently carries a legitimate material entitlement.

## 14. Main Story reintegration contract

Later Story may ask narrow factual questions such as:

- did this participant become a trusted contact?
- does this participant know the secret?
- did the player keep the promise?
- who currently has custody?
- was the witness protected?
- was the courier spared/captured/lost?
- did the team member observe this capability?
- was the foreign contact helped, coerced or ignored?
- was the Sealed Wall investigated, shared, surrendered or deliberately left untouched?

It may then change dialogue, available interpretation, participant willingness, support, routes or consequences where causally relevant.

It may **not** fabricate a completed side event because the plot would be easier that way.

## 15. Canon Research First note

Before this expansion, current official Naruto material was rechecked to preserve baseline village identity: official material recognises the Hidden Sand, Hidden Stone, Hidden Mist and Hidden Cloud as major Kage villages; official Boruto material places the Hidden Mist in the Land of Water; and official Naruto material explicitly depicts the Hidden Rain Village. The exact quest content below remains Shinobi Chronicles-authored World content and is not claimed as canon history.

## 16. Production intent

This is deliberately a **large reservoir expansion**. It should give CE enough authored material to make revisiting the same country with a different team, different capabilities and different prior decisions feel meaningfully different.

The target experience is not:

`open map -> clear every icon -> map empty`.

It is:

`live in the world -> create history -> qualify for different possibilities -> meet people again -> see consequences return -> Story notices what actually happened`.
