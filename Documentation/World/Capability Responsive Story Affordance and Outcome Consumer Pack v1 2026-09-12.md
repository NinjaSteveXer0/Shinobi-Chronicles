## 1. Purpose

This pack converts the CE outcome-family grammar into practical World/Story content consumers.

These are **not new engine systems**, not universal Skills, and not promises that every Story mission exposes every route.

The design target is:

> **The same factual pressure can produce materially different legitimate outcomes because different Chronicles produced different capable people.**

A specialist route may change:

- what can be perceived;
- where the protagonist can reach;
- who can be protected/captured/extracted;
- what evidence/object/location survives;
- what physical state is left behind;
- which observers trust/fear/recognise the protagonist;
- what development evidence exists;
- which future opportunities become eligible;
- what cost or unresolved pressure remains.

Preserve:

- something for everyone != everything for everyone;
- capability predicate != automatic success;
- specialist route != objectively superior route;
- different route != cosmetic dialogue;
- different route != same loot with another explanation;
- Battle victory != Story outcome automatically;
- Battle loss != zero Chronicle value;
- Story outcome != automatic Progression;
- Knowledge != Access != Competence != Power != Mastery;
- one Character's earned outcome != universal campaign canon.

---

## 2. Consumer grammar

Each consumer below has:

- **consumerId** — stable World authoring identity;
- **mode** — `baseline_world_route`, `single_path_responsive`, `multi_path_any`, `compound_path_synergy`, or `history_generated_followup`;
- **predicate family** — a capability/history fact supplied by its owning system;
- **factual pressure** — the kind of situation that can legitimately care about the route;
- **affordance** — what the route can attempt;
- **outcome classes** — materially different factual consequences it may support;
- **Chronicle facts** — what World/CE may commit if it actually happens;
- **reward/consequence envelope** — legal downstream value families, not guaranteed exact rewards;
- **limits** — what the route does not magically grant.

World authors the occurrence and exact predicate use. Capability owners define whether the predicate is true. Resolver owners determine factual success where appropriate.

# 3. TAIJUTSU CONSUMERS

### `story_aff_tai_01_controlled_intercept`
**Mode:** `baseline_world_route`  
**Predicate family:** exact Taijutsu/control capability recognised by owning authority.  
**Pressure:** fleeing witness/suspect, collapsing access corridor, moving civilian, narrow physical interception window.  
**Affordance:** close distance, body-block, redirect movement, controlled restraint, physically protect another person.  
**Outcome classes:** living witness preserved; suspect redirected but not identified; protagonist reaches person before hazard; pursuit succeeds but evidence elsewhere is lost.  
**Chronicle facts:** who was reached, who was protected, whether force was used, whether custody was actually established.  
**Envelope:** witness/contact history, service recognition, development evidence, future testimony chain.  
**Limits:** no impossible-speed teleport, no guaranteed capture, no automatic custody from Battle defeat.

### `story_aff_tai_02_hazard_body_control`
**Mode:** `single_path_responsive`  
**Pressure:** unstable bridge, collapsing roof, vertical shaft, moving machinery, flood debris, ravine traversal.  
**Affordance:** traverse dangerous geometry, brace another person, maintain position while acting.  
**Outcome classes:** route remains usable; another participant survives; protagonist reaches a different side of the pressure; route succeeds but leaves injury/visibility/cost.  
**Envelope:** access, rescue history, later route availability, relationship consequence.  
**Limits:** high Taijutsu alone does not negate authored lethal hazards or create flight.

### `story_aff_tai_03_nonlethal_control_history`
**Mode:** `history_generated_followup`  
**Pressure:** someone previously subdued without killing later becomes a witness, rival, informant or hostile observer.  
**Affordance:** later occurrence can care that the protagonist chose control over lethal resolution.  
**Outcome classes:** testimony; grudging respect; revenge; institutional praise/criticism; new request.  
**Envelope:** relationship/reputation/future eligibility.  
**Limits:** nonlethal choice is not automatically morally better.

# 20. LOSS / FAILURE / NON-ACTION CONSUMPTION

Every Story consumer must preserve the standing World reward/outcome doctrine:

> **Battle loss != mission failure. Mission failure != zero value.**

If a specialist action legitimately happened before a later loss/failure, committed Knowledge, observer history, development evidence, relationship change, preserved/destroyed state or future eligibility is not erased merely because the formal mission objective failed.

Examples:

- tracker identifies the route but team loses later Battle;
- medic stabilises the witness but target escapes;
- Genjutsu specialist proves sensory evidence was false but objective item is lost;
- Taijutsu specialist rescues a civilian while mission target escapes;
- Fūinjutsu specialist preserves seal evidence despite failing to secure the site;
- political route gains lawful audience but institution refuses the request.

Non-action is also factual history where context supports it:

- do not intervene;
- refuse to expose a capability;
- preserve anonymity;
- let authority handle it;
- leave evidence untouched;
- decline obligation;

# 21. STORY INSERTION RULE

Writing should not bolt these consumers onto every scene.

For a factual Story situation, the authoring sequence is:

1. Writing/World establishes the mission invariant and factual pressure.
2. World identifies which consumer families naturally fit that pressure.
3. Exact capability/history predicates are checked from owning authorities.
4. Only eligible affordances enter the player/NPC decision space.
5. Chosen intent goes to the correct resolver.
6. World commits factual consequences.
7. CE/Knowledge/Relationship history receives only what legitimately occurred.
8. Progression/Rewards/Items/etc. consume the result through their own authority.

Dedicated responsive content is invisible when its predicate is absent.

Do not show greyed `requires X` options for capabilities the Character never established.

# 22. CAMPAIGN USE / NON-CONVERGENT FAIRNESS

Campaign-level target:

- every healthy Chronicle should periodically encounter content where its established paths matter;
- no guarantee that every individual mission provides a specialist route;
- no requirement that every Origin receives equal material value scene-by-scene;
- meaningfulness is assessed across a campaign, not by making all outcomes converge.

Examples of legitimate non-convergence:

- one build saves a witness;
- another preserves infrastructure;
- another recovers a weapon/material;
- another keeps identity secret;
- another gains institutional access;
- another learns a dangerous truth;
- another avoids the whole Battle;
- another fails the formal objective but creates a powerful future contact;
- another never receives that event at all and instead receives a different capability-responsive chain later.
