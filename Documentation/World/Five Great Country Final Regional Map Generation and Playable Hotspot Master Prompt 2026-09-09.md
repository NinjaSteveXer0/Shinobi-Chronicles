# 1. LOCKED ARCHITECTURE

Preserve exactly:

> **terrain artwork = geographic world truth / reusable spatial affordance**

> **World = location / event / opportunity meaning**

> **UI/runtime = marker / Knowledge / discovery / access / hover / focus / current presentation state**

And:

> location != hotspot != event != opportunity

> stable geography != fixed opportunity population

> semantic ID != coordinate

> secret reservation != visible secret identity

> discovered != actionable

> opportunity != Battle

> Battle availability != mandatory Battle

> loot opportunity != ownership

> training opportunity != automatic development

> Story opportunity != exclusive ownership of a location

> same regional map != same event population across Chronicles

> random selection among eligible possibilities != randomness creating eligibility

---

# 2. REQUIRED MASTER FORMAT

Produce each final regional master as:

- PNG;
- **1536 × 1024 px**;
- **3:2 aspect ratio**;
- same camera family and rendering language across all five maps;
- premium modern semi-realistic / crisp 3D environmental presentation;
- high readability at browser scale;
- geographic detail strong enough for precise hotspot anchoring;
- no baked UI.

Use a high three-quarter / elevated regional-map viewpoint with readable terrain hierarchy.

Do not use an extreme vertical satellite view where buildings/roads become unreadable.

Do not use a low cinematic horizon where geography cannot be calibrated.

The five maps should feel like the same cartographic/art family while remaining strongly country-specific.

---

# 3. CRITICAL WORLD-CONTINUITY RULE — DO NOT TURN EVERY COUNTRY INTO AN ISLAND

The five Great Countries live inside the same world geography.

The replacement regional art must not accidentally imply that Fire, Wind, Earth and Lightning are four standalone ocean islands simply because a generated image looks attractive that way.

### Land of Water

An archipelago / island-heavy regional presentation is correct and encouraged.

### Fire / Wind / Earth / Lightning

These may contain coastlines where geographically appropriate, but non-coastal country edges should read as:

- terrain continuing beyond the regional frame;
- distant mountain/forest/desert/border country;
- cloud/mist/map-edge concealment;
- neighbouring-land approach;

rather than ocean enclosing the whole country.

Border roads should visibly continue toward off-map neighbouring countries where appropriate.

This matters for Arc 2+ travel and geopolitical continuity.

# 4. CLICKABLE-VOCABULARY ACCEPTANCE TARGET

A map is **REJECTED** as a final interactive master if it contains only several giant landmarks surrounded by decorative filler.

Each regional master must support at least:

**40 distinct credible hotspot-capable contexts**

with:

**50–60 preferred** where visually natural.

This is not a simultaneous marker count.

It is the amount of physical geography World can attach content to over the life of the game.

### Target composition

Each map should visibly support approximately:

- **8–12 major/public destination contexts**;
- **12–18 secondary settlement / civic / structure / route-stop contexts**;
- **12–20 natural investigation / training / encounter contexts**;
- **8–12 crossings / junctions / passes / border / travel contexts**;
- overlap is expected;
- at least **one third of strong usable geography must remain semantically neutral** for future Chronicle content.

A single bridge can later host:

- travel;
- patrol;
- escort;
- investigation;
- ambush;
- rescue;
- Story;
- temporary event;

without the bridge being a fixed "bridge quest".

# 5. COMMON REUSABLE TERRAIN VOCABULARY — ALL FIVE MAPS

Every country should contain a country-appropriate selection of the following, distributed naturally rather than arranged like theme-park icons:

## Major human geography

- Hidden Village — unmistakable major man-made landmark;
- country/daimyō capital or equivalent major civilian centre where authored;
- 2–5 secondary settlements;
- 2–5 small hamlets/camps/waystations;
- at least one major service/logistics location outside the Hidden Village;
- border post / fortress / watch approach;
- isolated compound(s);
- temple/shrine/monastery-capable site(s) where country-appropriate;
- workshop / quarry / harbour / mining / caravan infrastructure where appropriate.

## Travel geography

- 3–5 major route chains;
- multiple secondary tracks/spurs;
- 6–10 readable junctions/forks;
- bridges, causeways or canyon crossings where geography permits;
- fords/stepping crossings where rivers permit;
- at least two routes into/out of the Hidden Village region;
- at least one off-map border route;
- multiple loops — **not one linear path from A to B**.

## Natural event geography

- forest/woodland clearings where biome permits;
- ravines/gorges/canyons;
- caves/cavern-capable cliff faces;
- high overlooks/ridges;
- river banks/bends;
- lakes/ponds/oases/water basins where appropriate;
- waterfalls where appropriate;
- open training fields/plateaus/beaches/dune flats;
- ruins/abandoned-route affordances;
- hidden recesses that can contain secrets without looking like a glowing quest entrance.

## Marker readability

Distribute enough visually calmer micro-areas beside major terrain features so runtime halo/circle markers and hover/focus treatments remain legible.

Do not make every anchor sit directly over extremely noisy white water, cloud banks, dense rooftops or high-contrast rock texture.

No empty generic circles should be painted for markers. The map remains natural terrain.

The art may include a subtle physically interesting feature:

- unusual stone arrangement;
- burned patch;
- broken torii-like generic structure;
- partially collapsed cave;
- strange but natural formation;

but runtime decides whether the observer is allowed to perceive it as suspicious.

# 16. SPATIAL COMPOSITION GUIDANCE

## Avoid

- isolated "encounter islands" of terrain;
- every road terminating at a named landmark;
- giant empty decorative plains with no route/settlement vocabulary;
- all settlements clustered in one corner;
- all secret-capable geography clustered in one obvious dark corner;
- perfectly symmetrical layout;
- one giant circular road;
- roads that vanish under mountains with no plausible continuation;
- decorative tiny islands that cannot support anything;
- enormous settlements occupying half the map and leaving no world geography.

## Prefer

- 3–5 macro-biome/territory clusters per country;
- interconnected routes;
- secondary paths;
- crossings;
- loops;
- natural chokepoints;
- several neutral clearings/plateaus/coves/canyons;
- settlements placed where water/trade/defence makes sense;
- Hidden Village defended by geography but connected to country life;
- border routes that visibly continue beyond the regional frame.
