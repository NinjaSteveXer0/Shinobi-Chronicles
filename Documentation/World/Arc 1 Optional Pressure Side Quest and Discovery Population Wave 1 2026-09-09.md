## 1. Purpose

Arc 1 Writing is closed. World now needs to ensure the player can spend substantial time **not advancing Story** while still encountering worthwhile Chronicle content.

This document authors the first concrete population wave around the closed Arc-1 Story spine.

It contains three separate layers:

1. **12 Arc-1-specific contextual pressure events** — optional events that make Konoha/Fire Country feel increasingly probed, exploited and penetrable;
2. **12 ordinary standing side-quest occurrence templates** — everyday Fire Country life that remains worthwhile even when the player ignores Story;
3. **8 finite discoveries / secrets** — some openly investigable, some concealed, some known-unknown.

These sit on top of the existing Land-of-Fire standing event ecology contract and do not replace it.

Preserve:

> Arc pressure event != standing side quest

> standing family != repeated historical occurrence

> secret != automatically invisible

> hidden world truth != player Knowledge

> visible anomaly != canonical identity

> location != hotspot != event != opportunity

> optional content != filler gate before Story

> Battle availability != mandatory Battle

> side quest outcome != automatic Progression / Rank / PL / ownership

# 2. Arc-1 contextual pressure pool

Pool ID:

`fire_arc1_pressure_pool_v1`

These 12 definitions are **Arc-specific** and separate from `fire_alpha_standing_pool_v1`.

Typical Chronicle target:

- encounter roughly 5–8 of these 12 depending on Story state, location, Knowledge and prior outcomes;
- never require all 12 for Story progression;
- never expose all 12 simultaneously;
- exact eligibility follows factual Arc history, not a generic `securityPressureLevel` number.

## ARC-P01 — Courier Who Never Arrived

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_missing_courier`

**Theme:** strategic information pressure

**Eligible hosts:** Fire Country routes, courier stations, Konoha approaches.

**Earliest window:** after Mission 1 establishes outside-operative pressure.

**Initial presentation:** delayed/missing courier report; no attacker identity.

**Actions:**
- inspect last-known route;
- question route witnesses;
- inspect courier station records;
- search likely deviation points;
- report and leave.

**Possible outcomes:**
- courier located alive;
- courier located injured;
- abandoned dispatch/equipment discovered;
- only route evidence recovered;
- no definitive cause established.

**Arc contribution:** shows official movement can be disrupted or observed.

No universal Battle. Hostile contact requires a separately eligible participant/encounter.

---

## ARC-P02 — The Marked Door

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_marked_residence`

**Theme:** civilian/clan targeting

A civilian, minor clan household or shinobi-connected residence has acquired a subtle external mark/surveillance indicator.

**Knowledge boundary:** player initially knows only that something unusual was noticed.

**Actions:**
- inspect mark;
- watch from distance;
- ask occupant;
- remove/alter mark;
- report to local authority;
- ignore.

**Possible outcomes:**
- surveillance pattern established;
- mark removed before purpose known;
- watcher later becomes separately actionable;
- false alarm / unrelated practical mark established where authored.

Do not turn every marked door into one mastermind signature.

---

## ARC-P03 — False Patrol

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_false_patrol`

**Theme:** institutional impersonation / route probing

A small group presents themselves as legitimate patrol personnel but current evidence contains inconsistencies.

**Actions:**
- accept their explanation;
- verify credentials through available channels;
- ask route-specific questions;
- discreetly follow;
- openly challenge;
- leave/report.

**Possible outcomes:**
- legitimate patrol confirmed;
- impersonation established;
- impersonators withdraw/escape;
- separate confrontation becomes eligible;
- only unresolved suspicion persists.

No role label may reveal faction before Knowledge supports it.

---

## ARC-P04 — The Empty Safehouse

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_empty_safehouse`

**Theme:** intelligence infrastructure already moved

Player discovers or is sent to inspect a small temporary site that has been recently stripped.

**Actions:**
- inspect burn/cleaning traces;
- search concealed compartments;
- reconstruct occupancy;
- secure remaining material;
- leave untouched for observation.

**Possible outputs:**
- recent occupation established;
- deliberately sanitised site established;
- one bounded object/source survives;
- nothing attribution-grade remains.

Arc contribution: hostile actors can prepare and abandon infrastructure inside Fire Country.

---

## ARC-P05 — Bloodline Watcher

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_bloodline_surveillance`

**Theme:** clan/bloodline targeting

Eligibility requires a currently present/known character whose lineage or unusual capability is legitimately targetable in current Story/world context.

**Initial projection:** suspicious observation, repeated sighting or route coincidence — **not** `Bloodline Kidnapper`.

**Actions:**
- alter route;
- counter-watch;
- confront;
- use another participant as observer;
- report;
- continue and gather more evidence.

**Outcomes:**
- surveillance established;
- watcher lost;
- watcher contacted;
- no hostile intent proven;
- later extraction event becomes eligible only with additional causal authority.

---

## ARC-P06 — Civilian Extraction Attempt

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_civilian_extraction`

**Theme:** ordinary people exposed to shinobi pressure

Requires a factual target and an authored reason the target matters to the current perpetrators.

**Actions may include:**
- intervene;
- pursue carrier;
- protect target;
- call assistance;
- identify vehicle/route;
- allow departure for surveillance where legally authored.

Battle is conditional, not automatic.

Local success may save the person while still leaving unresolved questions about who ordered the attempt.

---

## ARC-P07 — Missing Seal Ledger Page

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_records_tampering`

**Theme:** records/intelligence theft

A routine log/ledger is physically intact except for one precisely removed page or altered entry.

**Actions:**
- inspect binding/tear;
- compare duplicate records;
- question custodian;
- trace who accessed archive/log;
- preserve tamper state.

**Outcomes:**
- tampering established;
- missing information bounded;
- suspect access window narrowed;
- innocent administrative cause established where authored.

This event reinforces that useful intelligence may be small and procedural rather than a giant defence blueprint.

---

## ARC-P08 — Border Signal Out of Sequence

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_border_signal`

**Theme:** outer Fire Country probing

**Earliest window:** middle/late Arc 1 when Story pressure has expanded beyond Konoha proper.

A watch/signal point reports or displays an unexpected sequence.

**Actions:**
- inspect signal station;
- compare neighbouring reports;
- travel to originating point;
- wait/watch;
- report inconsistency.

**Outcomes:**
- equipment fault;
- human error;
- false/imitated signal;
- observation/probing attempt;
- unresolved anomaly.

Randomness cannot decide whether hostile probing exists; that must be authored current-world truth.

## ARC-P09 — Night Cargo With the Wrong Seal

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_night_cargo`

**Theme:** logistics exploitation / smuggling

A cargo movement is legal-looking but contains inconsistent seal/route/custody evidence.

**Actions:**
- inspect documents;
- inspect physical seal;
- follow cargo;
- question driver/porter;
- alert authority;
- allow movement under observation.

Outcomes may establish smuggling, forged paperwork, innocent mismatch, or a wider trail.

Loot is not automatically granted by discovering contraband.

---

## ARC-P10 — Watcher on the Roofline

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_rooftop_observer`

**Theme:** Konoha surveillance

Konoha-only contextual event.

Player or another legitimate observer notices repeated elevated observation of a protected route/person/location.

**Actions:**
- approach directly;
- flank;
- continue pretending not to notice;
- send teammate/authority;
- observe destination of attention.

Possible outcomes:
- observer escapes;
- identity/contact established;
- innocent explanation;
- future occurrence enabled.

Do not bake a hostile marker onto the Konoha map.

---

## ARC-P11 — Sabotaged Waystation

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_waystation_sabotage`

**Theme:** route/logistics degradation

A small route facility is operationally damaged in a way that might be deliberate.

**Actions:**
- assist immediate safety;
- inspect damage;
- trace tool/chakra evidence where legitimate;
- secure travellers/cargo;
- report and continue.

Possible outcomes:
- accidental failure;
- deliberate sabotage established without perpetrator identity;
- active perpetrators remain nearby only if separately authored;
- route suffers temporary consequence.

---

## ARC-P12 — Two Problems, One Team

**eventFamilyId:** `fire_arc1_pressure_competing_incidents`

**Theme:** prioritisation / systemic vulnerability

Late Arc-1 optional event.

Two legitimate incidents become actionable in different directions/locations at the same time.

The player chooses where to commit the protagonist/team. Independent participants may make their own intentions known.

The unchosen incident resolves according to authored world state and available actors — not automatically as catastrophic failure.

Arc contribution:

> Konoha/Fire Country cannot answer every pressure with the same people at the same time.

This is a smaller optional rehearsal of the Arc capstone's systemic lesson, not a duplicate mandatory Story mission.

# 5. Opportunity presentation density

When Arc 1 is active and enough legitimate content exists, a healthy Fire/Konoha free-play snapshot may include approximately:

- 1–2 Arc-pressure opportunities;
- 3–5 ordinary standing opportunities;
- 0–2 active discovery/anomaly opportunities;
- Story marker(s) separately;
- persistent destinations/services separately.

This is a rhythm target, not an eligibility override.

Never fill the map just to hit a number.

# 7. Rewards — Wave-1 rule

Ordinary side content may author modest contextual economy rewards later, but this document does not assign exact Ryō/item values yet.

Arc-pressure events default to **no universal cash payout**. Their primary value may be:

- Knowledge;
- Chronicle history;
- evidence/intelligence;
- access/future opportunities;
- reputation/recognition evidence;
- relationship/shared-history evidence;
- factual rescue/protection outcomes.

Any material reward requires an exact reward package tied to resolved outcome and grant-once semantics.

No universal:

`helped someone -> Ryō + EXP`

rule is authorised.
