# Shinobi Chronicles — World / Mission / Event Non-Loot Outcome Authoring Contract

**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** **WORLD / MISSIONS / EVENTS / REWARDS CLOSED — NON-LOOT OUTCOME CHANNELS ARE FIRST-CLASS AUTHORING OUTPUTS**  
**Source issue:** GitHub issue #22  
**Related Rank authority:** `Documentation/Rank/Special Jonin Contextual Development Evidence Contract.md`  
**Related downstream producer integration:** GitHub issue #20

---

## 1. Decision

Shinobi Chronicles World / Mission / Event / Reward authoring must not treat `Ryō + item drops + direct Skill unlocks` as the complete reward model.

A resolved occurrence may legitimately produce one or more independent outcome channels when the committed facts support them.

For Alpha, authored content may therefore produce or request consequences through channels including:

- factual Chronicle / mission history;
- source-bounded Knowledge;
- Rumour / evidence / intelligence;
- relationship / Shared History evidence;
- reputation / recognition evidence;
- access, permissions or newly actionable opportunities;
- future world-event eligibility or suppression;
- exact contextual development evidence where another owning system authorises the projection;
- ordinary material/economic rewards when separately authored.

These channels are independent. A mission does not need a loot drop merely because something meaningful happened.

Preserve:

**meaningful outcome ≠ material reward required**

**failure / partial success ≠ no valuable history**

---

## 2. Author the factual occurrence first

World / Mission / Event authoring starts from what actually happened.

A reward or consequence must be caused by a committed occurrence, not by the player merely selecting a menu option.

Authoring order:

`eligibility`

→ `player / participant action or event occurrence`

→ `resolver outcome`

→ `committed factual history`

→ `legitimate consequence / entitlement channels`

Do not author:

`choice clicked -> arbitrary reward bundle`

Preserve:

**choice ≠ action**

**action ≠ success**

**success ≠ every reward channel automatically**

---

## 3. Chronicle / history channel

Every significant mission/event resolution may commit factual history where appropriate.

Examples include:

- participant protected a civilian;
- target escaped;
- evidence was recovered;
- prohibited material was deliberately observed;
- a teammate independently performed specialist-relevant work;
- a mission objective failed while useful intelligence was still acquired.

World authors the occurrence facts.

It must not attach moral/personality interpretation unless another authoritative system explicitly owns and commits that interpretation.

Preserve:

**occurrence ≠ interpretation**

---

## 4. Knowledge / Rumour / evidence / intelligence channel

Investigation, observation, conversation and technical exposure may produce observer-specific information.

Authoring must preserve:

- exact observer / recipient;
- exact source where relevant;
- what was directly observed versus reported;
- confidence / confirmation state where the owning Knowledge model distinguishes it;
- provenance back to the committed occurrence.

Examples:

- a source reports that a convoy travelled east;
- the player directly observes an unfamiliar sealing process;
- physical evidence corroborates or contradicts an earlier statement;
- recovered documents establish a new intelligence lead.

Do not collapse:

**source says X ≠ X automatically true**

**Rumour ≠ confirmed Knowledge**

**observation ≠ competence**

**Knowledge ≠ Access ≠ Power ≠ Mastery**

The Shinobi Record presents legitimately acquired information; it does not create truth by presentation.

---

## 5. Relationship / Shared History channel

A mission/event may create relationship-relevant factual history between participants.

World may author facts such as:

- A protected B;
- A abandoned B;
- A cooperated with B;
- A concealed information from B;
- A and B survived the same occurrence.

World does not automatically assign permanent relationship state, affinity, friendship, rivalry, loyalty or morality from those facts.

Relationship/CE authority interprets where appropriate.

Preserve:

**shared occurrence ≠ automatic relationship state**

**relationship evidence ≠ relationship mutation automatically**

---

## 6. Reputation / recognition channel

A committed occurrence may create factual evidence capable of later supporting institutional or social recognition.

Examples:

- a named authority witnessed competent field work;
- a village received a documented result;
- a specialist act was formally recorded;
- a mission failure exposed misconduct or exceptional judgement.

World may author the facts and observer/source authority.

World does not directly grant formal Rank, Promotion or another institution-owned status unless that exact institution/Rank contract delegates the mutation.

Preserve:

**recorded performance ≠ Promotion automatically**

**reputation evidence ≠ formal Rank**

---

## 7. Access / permissions / future-opportunity channel

Mission/event outcomes may legitimately alter what becomes actionable later.

Examples:

- discovering a location reveals a future opportunity;
- learning an access phrase permits a later interaction;
- helping a source makes a later conversation eligible;
- recovering evidence unlocks an investigation branch;
- failing to preserve an asset suppresses a later opportunity.

World owns future event/opportunity eligibility where the condition is world-contextual.

This does not create acquisition/ownership unless Acquisition authority separately commits it.

Preserve:

**world opportunity ≠ acquisition**

**access ≠ ownership**

**eligibility ≠ success**

---

## 8. Special Jōnin contextual development — exact boundary

Issue #19 is now closed by PL / Registry / Rank.

Authoritative Alpha rule:

- there is **NO new hidden `specializationXP` or family-level XP currency**;
- contextual Story / Mission / World / Combat development uses the existing Special Jōnin qualification-evidence architecture;
- evidence must target an exact closed qualification path and exact demonstrated competency/supporting tags;
- a Story choice alone is not evidence;
- the exact participant must actually perform or resolve the specialist-relevant work;
- evidence is participant-owned and provenance-bearing;
- contextual specialist evidence creates no automatic PL or Stat gain;
- qualification remains separate from Recognition and formal Rank.

World / Mission authoring therefore must **not** write rewards such as:

- `+5 Interrogation XP`;
- `+10 Medical XP`;
- `+Diplomacy Level`;
- `hidden Assassination EXP`.

When an authored occurrence appears specialist-relevant, World should preserve the exact factual contribution and source provenance.

Where the exact qualification path is already authoritative, a content contract may annotate the likely exact path / competency relevance for downstream projection, but that annotation is not itself persistent qualification evidence.

Persistent producer-side projection belongs to Progression / Development and remains queued in GitHub issue #20.

Preserve:

**contextual specialist relevance ≠ persistent qualification evidence automatically**

**qualification evidence ≠ XP currency**

**evidence accumulation ≠ qualification automatically**

**qualification ≠ Recognition ≠ Rank**

---

## 9. Participant ownership

Outcome channels must attach to the participant who factually earned/experienced them.

If an autonomous teammate performs meaningful specialist work, observes evidence, protects a civilian or creates a relationship-relevant occurrence, the teammate may receive the corresponding factual history/evidence where appropriate.

Do not copy the outcome to the protagonist or entire team merely because they were present.

Preserve:

**team presence ≠ participant contribution**

**shared event ≠ identical participant rewards**

---

## 10. Failure and partial success may still produce value

World / Rewards authoring must not assume:

`mission failed -> zero meaningful outcome`

A failed or partial mission may still legitimately preserve:

- acquired Knowledge;
- direct observations;
- Chronicle history;
- evidence/intelligence;
- relationship consequences;
- future opportunity changes;
- specialist-relevant factual work;
- reputation/recognition evidence, including negative or mixed institutional interpretation where separately owned.

Material reward entitlement may still be absent.

The actual objective result and the informational/developmental/history outcomes remain separate facts.

---

## 11. Anti-farming / idempotence authoring rule

Do not author repeatable non-loot consequences from UI click count.

The meaningful unit is the committed occurrence and its factual provenance.

Save/load/retry must not duplicate one-shot consequence records from the same committed occurrence.

Repeated legitimate development requires genuinely distinct occurrences.

Preserve:

**repeated option click ≠ repeated historical work**

---

## 12. Recommended content-contract outcome block

When useful, World / Mission / Event contracts should distinguish outcome channels explicitly rather than use one undifferentiated `reward` field.

Conceptual authoring form:

```text
OUTCOME / CONSEQUENCE CHANNELS

History:
- committed occurrence fact(s)

Knowledge / Rumour / Evidence:
- recipient
- source / provenance
- information gained

Relationship / Shared History:
- factual participant interaction evidence only

Reputation / Recognition Evidence:
- observing authority / record source
- factual performance/result

Future World / Access:
- reveal / eligibility / suppression condition

Development Evidence Intent:
- exact participant factual contribution
- exact closed qualification relevance only when authoritative
- persistent projection owned by Progression / Development

Material Reward:
- exact Ryō/item entitlement, or NONE
```

Not every event needs every channel.

Do not create empty boilerplate merely to satisfy the template.

---

## 13. Mission 2 purge-observation example

If a player deliberately permits a dangerous Fūinjutsu/Kinjutsu purge process to execute so it can be observed, the immediate authored result may legitimately include:

- the purge actually occurred;
- the exact observing participant witnessed it;
- source-bounded Fūinjutsu/Kinjutsu-related Knowledge/evidence was acquired where the scene supports it;
- the Shinobi Record can later present that acquired evidence;
- later technique-development eligibility may become possible if Progression/Skills authority has a corresponding contract;
- relationship/Chronicle consequences may arise from the decision and outcome;
- material reward may be NONE.

It must **not** automatically mean:

- the observing participant learned the technique;
- the participant gained a new Skill;
- the participant gained generic specialization XP;
- the participant gained PL/Stats;
- the participant has access to reproduce the process.
