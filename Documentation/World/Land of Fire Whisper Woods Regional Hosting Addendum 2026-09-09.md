## 5. Regional artwork requirement

The final replacement Land-of-Fire regional master must visibly support one substantial, coherent woodland tract suitable for `fire:O21` Whisper Woods.

Required regional-scale environmental reading:

- dense mature Fire Country forest, visibly distinct enough to support a stable regional anchor;
- believable connection to a merchant/caravan road;
- at least one readable approach track into the woodland;
- creek/watercourse and higher ravine-capable terrain may be suggested at regional scale;
- enough geographic mass that entering a separate 1536×1024 contained local map feels believable;
- near enough to the Konoha/merchant-route ecosystem that Arc 1 Mission 1 travel is plausible;
- not inside the visual footprint of Konohagakure itself.

Do **not** attempt to paint the seven Whisper Woods local anchors as seven regional landmarks.

The following remain local-map geography only:

- South Trailhead;
- Split-Cedar Fork;
- Old Warden Shelter;
- Whisper Creek Ford;
- North Ravine;
- Old Watch Ledge;
- Hollow Cedar.

At regional scale, Whisper Woods is one stable forest destination/area host.
