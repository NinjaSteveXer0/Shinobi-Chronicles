# Shinobi Chronicles — 500-Quest Reservoir Activation Policy

**Date:** 2026-09-10  
**Owner:** World / Missions / Events / Rewards  
**Status:** **WORLD CONTENT POLICY — SEED RESERVOIR AUTHORED / ACTIVATION IS SELECTIVE**

## Decision

The project may carry **500 distinct World quest/event/opportunity seeds** without making all 500 Alpha blockers.

The reservoir is distributed across seven Hidden Villages, seven regional interactive maps, and cross-map Chronicle chains. It exists so ordinary free play, investigation, training, social consequences, service activity, discoveries, threats and character matters remain available outside mandatory Story.

The current working reservoir contains:

- 500 total seeds;
- 140 Alpha activation candidates;
- 214 Alpha content-reserve seeds;
- 146 post-Alpha reserve seeds.

The 140 activation candidates are **candidates**, not a requirement to ship all 140 before the first Alpha build. Final activation should be driven by coverage quality, caller readiness, map geometry, reward/source availability and runtime validation.

## What makes seeds distinct

A seed is distinct by its combination of:

- host geography;
- initiating situation;
- causal participants/objects;
- available decision axes;
- possible non-action;
- CE history emphasis;
- reward/value channels;
- discovery/access consequences;
- any exact special unlock hook.

Reusable event grammar is allowed. A new occurrence of a family is not the same historical quest replayed.

## Minimum activated content quality bar

Before a seed becomes executable Alpha content it needs:

1. stable event/opportunity ID;
2. exact current host/coordinate binding;
3. eligibility and suppression/expiry rules;
4. Knowledge/discovery/actionability contract;
5. factual resolution boundaries;
6. exact reward entitlement or explicit no-material-reward result;
7. exact owner-approved Combat/Skill/Item/Weapon/Summon/representation dependencies;
8. occurrence identity persisted across save/load;
9. no reward duplication or UI reroll;
10. browser/runtime proof before GREEN.

## Non-action policy

For every applicable activated seed, a player may sometimes decline, observe, withhold action, defer, leave, or allow another participant to proceed.

This is not automatically failure and never feeds a universal morality score.

It may instead cause:

- someone else to resolve the situation;
- a later opportunity to change;
- different Knowledge;
- different relationships/shared history;
- different recognition/credit;
- different material reward eligibility;
- the problem to persist or expire;
- a participant to remember the player's absence;
- no further consequence at all when that is factually appropriate.

The engine records attributable history rather than punishing the player for not consuming content.

## Reward policy

Reward value can include:

- Ryō;
- consumables;
- weapons/equipment;
- crafting materials;
- Knowledge;
- Access/new opportunities;
- relationship/shared-history evidence;
- recognition/service evidence;
- Special Jōnin qualification evidence where an exact authorised path is exercised;
- Skill/Progression development only through existing owner-authorised semantics;
- Summon opportunities, never automatic ownership;
- special Character representation eligibility, never automatic Registry/Acquisition admission;
- CE Chronicle history itself.

Different legal outcomes should often produce different forms of value rather than one universal `best reward` route.

## Relationship to Story

Mandatory Story remains separately authored and does not exhaust or own ordinary map content. Arc-sensitive contextual events may enter/leave eligibility as Story changes the world, but the standing ecosystem also contains ordinary village/country life unrelated to the current Arc.

## Status distinction

**500 seeds designed** does not mean **500 quests implemented**.

The content reservoir is deliberately larger than the current implementation slice so World can populate the fourteen maps coherently without making Story continuation the only remaining activity.
