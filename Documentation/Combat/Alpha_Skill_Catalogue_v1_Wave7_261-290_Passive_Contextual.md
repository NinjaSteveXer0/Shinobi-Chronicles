## Wave-7 guardrails

1. `skill_false_identity` is the passive contextual surface of earned Identity Rebinding capability for current Arc-1 Menma; it is not a second Progression unlock and not a rename of the Identity Rebinding Seal.
2. A passive being owned does not force Story/World to offer a compatible event route.
3. Passive eligibility polling never commits Chronicle history.
4. Evidence-producing passives cannot manufacture evidence that the current context does not contain.
5. Capability-gated passives require actual Access, not lineage/card art/Stats/possession alone.
6. Hosted/Echo/Kurama passives preserve exact source identity and never create a second turn, slot or PL ledger.
7. Passive information is observer-relative and bounded; World Truth is never silently copied into Character Knowledge.
