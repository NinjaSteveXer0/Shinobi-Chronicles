**Date:** 2026-09-12

## 1. Purpose

Stephen reviewed the first SC-original Bloodline / Kekkei Tōta concept wave and made explicit keep/veto/rename decisions. This document preserves those decisions so rejected concepts are not accidentally reintroduced later as though still active candidates.

This consumes:

## 2. Candidate #2 — mechanics retained, name rejected

Original candidate name:

**Resonance Release** — Earth + Wind + Lightning.

Stephen rejects the name because `Resonance` is already heavily used elsewhere in Shinobi Chronicles, especially around weapon, equipment and item history/provenance, and further reuse would create terminology collision.

The underlying candidate mechanic remains viable:

- vibration through solids and air;
- structural fault detection;
- controlled fracture;
- vibration sensing;
- barrier/fortification stress;
- weapon/material disruption.

Status:

> **MECHANICS RETAINED AS CANDIDATE / NAME MUST CHANGE.**

No replacement name is locked by this document.

## 5. Candidate #10 — Resonant Blood

Stephen vetoes **Resonant Blood** as a Bloodline.

Reasoning is binding design guidance:

- `Resonance` is already a major concept in weapon/equipment/item history and provenance;
- making one Bloodline increase the effects/stats of that general system would create a dominant universal optimisation choice;
- if the Bloodline broadly improved everything the player owns, rational players would be strongly pressured to take it;
- that would make many other Bloodlines / Kekkei Genkai / Kekkei Tōta comparatively redundant;
- a mechanic that should benefit all characters through normal item/provenance development should not be monopolised by one hereditary route.

Canonical project lesson:

> **A good universal progression mechanic should not be converted into an exclusive Bloodline if doing so creates a mandatory best-in-slot hereditary choice.**

The useful conceptual material may inform existing Equipment / Weapon / Item provenance-resonance systems, but `Resonant Blood` itself is removed from the Bloodline candidate pool.

Status:

> **VETO AS BLOODLINE / REUSE ONLY THROUGH EXISTING GENERAL PROVENANCE-RESONANCE SYSTEMS WHERE APPROPRIATE.**

---

## 6. Candidate #11 — Dreaming Eye

Stephen initially raised a veto concern because trained Genjutsu users and Sharingan users can already gain strong perception / Genjutsu-analysis capability, and a dedicated eye that simply detects Genjutsu would risk making Genjutsu investment redundant.

After reconsideration, Stephen explicitly withdrew the veto because the concept may still fit if its role remains distinct.

Status:

> **RETAIN AS ACTIVE CANDIDATE, BUT MUST NOT BECOME GENERIC GENJUTSU DETECTION / IMMUNITY.**

Future design must preserve a distinct niche, for example:

- detecting contradictions or discontinuities between sensory/perceptual evidence streams;
- indicating that something does not agree, without identifying the cause automatically;
- no automatic Genjutsu break;
- no automatic caster identification;
- no automatic truth detection;
- no replacement for Genjutsu training;
- no replacement for Sharingan-specific analysis/counterplay.

Canonical differentiation target:

> **Dreaming Eye may notice inconsistency; Genjutsu skill and Sharingan capability still determine whether the character understands, resists, counters or exploits it.**

Exact mechanics remain open.

---

## 7. Candidate #12 — Living Shadow

Stephen vetoes **Living Shadow** because its identity is too close to the Nara clan's existing shadow-based capability family while merely changing what the shadow does.

Status:

> **VETO — REMOVE FROM ACTIVE BLOODLINE CANDIDATE POOL.**

SC-original Bloodlines should avoid feeling like a canon Clan Art with a renamed function unless there is a genuinely different underlying capability grammar.

---

## 8. Candidate #15 — White Flame

Stephen vetoes **White Flame**.

Reasoning:

- its proposed specialisation against chakra-made constructs/effects has too narrow a practical target pool in the current game;
- most active combatants and encounter targets are ordinary biological characters rather than chakra constructs;
- a Bloodline whose primary utility applies only to a small minority of current entities/effects risks being dead content across most play.

Status:

> **VETO — REMOVE FROM ACTIVE BLOODLINE CANDIDATE POOL.**

Do not preserve the concept merely because it is novel; Bloodlines must have a meaningful enough play surface to justify their opportunity cost.

## 10. Design lessons from this triage

Future SC-original Bloodline proposals should be rejected or redesigned when they:

1. duplicate a major existing project term and create semantic confusion;
2. reproduce an existing Clan Art with only cosmetic changes;
3. make a general system materially stronger for one hereditary route such that the Bloodline becomes an obvious mandatory choice;
4. invalidate investment in an existing Stat/discipline such as Genjutsu;
5. occupy too narrow a target/use surface to justify the opportunity cost;
6. add power without adding a meaningful new verb.

Preserve:

**distinctive != useful automatically**  
**strong synergy != mandatory best choice**  
**Bloodline identity != replacement for core Stat progression**  
**SC originality != canon mechanic with a different coat of paint**

## Final lock

> **Namebinding, Resonant Blood, Living Shadow and White Flame are removed from the active SC Bloodline candidate pool. Dreaming Eye remains a candidate only if it does not obsolete Genjutsu training or Sharingan capability. The mechanics behind former `Resonance Release` and `Furnace Release` remain candidates, but both names are rejected and require replacement terminology.**
