## 1. Player decision

Stephen explicitly wants both of the following retained in Shinobi Chronicles:

- **Curse Mark — Level 1**
- **Curse Mark — Level 2**

These are KEEP.

They are not to be collapsed into Bloodline taxonomy merely because they can materially alter a character's capability.

Canonical SC classification direction:

> **Curse Mark = externally applied curse/seal transformation package**

not:

> **Curse Mark = hereditary Bloodline**

Preserve:

**Bloodline != Curse Mark**  
**Curse Mark possession != current activation**  
**activation != mastery**  
**Level 1 != Level 2**  
**Level 2 != a new persistent person**

## 3. Stage distinction

### Curse Mark — Level 1

SC retains the first/partial Curse Mark activation state.

Conceptual identity:

- the mark spreads/activates without the full second-state body transformation;
- this is a materially stronger state than dormant possession;
- it should normally be cheaper, less committed and/or less exposing than Level 2 once exact mechanics are authored;
- exact visual spread, costs, Skills, modifiers and activation predicates remain open.

This lock does not author a universal percentage bonus.

### Curse Mark — Level 2

SC retains the second-state/full transformation state.

Conceptual identity:

- the user undergoes a substantial transformed-body state;
- the state may legitimately alter exact Skills/actions and not merely apply a passive numeric multiplier;
- it should represent a larger commitment than Level 1 in both capability and PvP exposure/counterplay;
- exact transformation visuals remain representation-specific and canon/source-specific where applicable;
- exact costs, risks, duration, modifiers, Skill substitutions and recovery semantics remain open.

This lock does not author a universal percentage bonus.

---

## 4. Level 1 and Level 2 are not interchangeable flags

Runtime/progression authority must preserve separate state semantics.

Do not implement:

`curse_mark_owned == true -> always use strongest state`

Instead distinguish at minimum:

- possession/source fact;
- executable Access;
- dormant state;
- Level 1 active state;
- Level 2 active state;
- exact representation-specific Skill/effect package;
- current control/competence;
- any source-specific cost or instability;
- removal/suppression/sealing state where later authored.

A character may legitimately possess the Curse Mark without currently activating either state.

A character may legitimately have Level 1 Access without Level 2 Access.

Level 2 Access does not imply that Level 1 ceases to matter tactically.

---

## 5. Not a generic universal transformation template

Curse Mark states should remain source-specific enough to preserve character identity.

Do not assume every Curse Mark bearer receives:

- identical appearance;
- identical Stats;
- identical Skill replacements;
- identical duration;
- identical resistance;
- identical control;
- identical PvP role.

Canon already demonstrates different bearers and different transformed presentations.

SC may author representation-specific packages while keeping Level 1 / Level 2 as the shared transformation-stage grammar.

## 7. Whole-build audit requirement

Curse Mark values may not be tuned in isolation.

Competitive review must include combinations such as:

`Curse Mark state + Character Stats/PL + prepared Skills + Bloodline + weapon + gear + Summon + Fūin attachments + Provenance + consumables + teammates + battlefield context`

A Level 2 package that is fair naked may become broken when combined with:

- high-output Bukijutsu weapons;
- elemental amplification;
- Summon modifiers;
- provenance-bearing weapons;
- Bloodline multipliers;
- action compression;
- team support;
- Hosted Entity or another transformation state.

Stacking must be explicitly audited.
