## 2. Canon Research First anchors

### 2.1 Space-Time

Canon establishes that Space-Time Ninjutsu is a technique classification rather than a sixth elemental nature.

- Flying Raijin is Ninjutsu that teleports to marked coordinates / objects.
- Kamui warps targets and space into an alternate dimension through Mangekyō Sharingan.
- Amenotejikara is a Rinnegan-derived Space-Time Ninjutsu that can swap/shift people, objects and attacks.

References:

Preserve:

**Space-Time Skill Access != hereditary Space-Time lineage**  
**Space-Time != basic chakra nature**  
**learned teleportation != Bloodline possession**

### 2.2 Fire / Amaterasu / Kirin

Canon establishes Amaterasu as an exceptional Mangekyō-derived black-flame Fire Release expression, while Kirin represents Sasuke weaponising naturally occurring lightning as a decisive technique rather than merely emitting a larger ordinary Lightning attack.

References:

SC divergence therefore aims for **qualitative rule changes**, not colour ladders or simple output inflation.

### 2.3 Yin / Yang / Yin–Yang

Canon describes Yin as form/imagination/spiritual-energy-facing, Yang as physical/vital-energy-facing, and Yin–Yang as their combination. Hagoromo's Creation of All Things is the major high-end canon anchor.

References:

## 3. Four-level SC development ladder — taxonomy remains separate

The project's four levels are treated here as **development grades**, not false canon taxonomy.

A capability can be a Kekkei Genkai, Kekkei Tōta, dōjutsu, Space-Time lineage, Yin/Yang lineage or another exact taxonomy while separately having a developed expression grade.

### Grade I — personal rule deviation

The user can violate one ordinary rule of the capability on self, a touched target, one anchor, or a very small local space.

Design expectation:
- one clear new verb;
- narrow targeting;
- strong readability;
- ordinary counterplay remains broad.

### Grade II — relational / network control

The user can manipulate relationships among multiple valid anchors, targets, connected media or prepared states.

Design expectation:
- setup matters;
- battlefield geometry begins to matter;
- build synergy becomes important;
- counterplay attacks connections and dependencies.

### Grade III — bounded domain rule projection

The user can impose the lineage's rule over a prepared/bounded domain rather than one isolated target.

Design expectation:
- high commitment;
- strong visible state;
- real action/resource opportunity cost;
- specialist counters become more valuable but ordinary tactical counterplay still exists.

### Grade IV — causal / existential / local-law expression

The lineage can alter the **relationship by which the phenomenon participates in the current event** without casually rewriting committed Chronicle history.

Design expectation:
- exceptional provenance/Access/development prerequisites;
- severe setup or exposure;
- no invisible one-check victories;
- Story/Hotspot/contextual value may equal or exceed raw Battle value.

Preserve:

**development grade != rarity**  
**development grade != Base PL**  
**higher grade != automatic access to every lower expression**  
**taxonomy != development grade**

Learned families may include:

The hereditary lineage therefore cannot justify itself as `Flying Raijin but easier`.

## 5. Hereditary Space-Time lineage — four-grade mechanics

### Grade I — spatial relation sense / local fold

The user can perceive exact local adjacency and temporarily alter effective distance between two nearby valid points.

Battle roots:
- shorten a committed movement path;
- make one short step resolve from a slightly different endpoint;
- place a hereditary spatial anchor without ordinary inscription where exact Access permits;
- detect that two visible points are spatially linked/warped.

Contextual roots:
- identify false geometry, hidden spatial seams or folded storage;
- judge whether a passage is physically contiguous even when presentation is misleading;
- detect that an object occupies impossible local geometry.

Counterplay:
- tight range;
- line/anchor validity;
- forced movement can spoil intended geometry;
- unpredictable multi-vector attacks reduce value.

### Grade II — historical spatial reassertion

The user may interact with a **real committed spatial relationship from the current Battle/event**.

Mechanic roots:
- return a thrown object toward a real prior point/path;
- reassert a prior attack endpoint so a weapon partially misses or redirects;
- swap/reposition only through exact previously established anchors rather than arbitrary free teleportation;
- retrieve an object from a recent legitimate position-history window.

#### Weapon interception package

This family is explicitly not a free universal disarm.

Preferred action-economy implementation:
- user deliberately prepares a Causal/Space-Time stance on their own action;
- stance records one exact qualifying spatial relation/weapon path;
- the first qualifying weapon-tagged direct attack before the user's next action may be altered;
- thrown/projected weapons permit stronger return/redirection than a melee weapon still physically held by the attacker;
- reflection is based on an exact prevented/redirected component, not automatic full negation + full retaliation;
- triggering the effect creates a source-owned **causal lockout** until the user's next action, preventing immediate Space-Time chaining.

Possible resolutions, each separately authored:
- partial avoidance;
- partial redirection;
- return along prior path;
- partial reflection of prevented force/damage;
- short quarantine of a thrown weapon with explicit return;
- mirrored recoil where the user still accepts some incoming consequence.

Generic counters:
- feints;
- disposable projectiles;
- changing weapons/attack vectors;
- multi-vector attacks;
- non-weapon Skills;
- forcing the stance early;
- attacking during causal lockout.

### Grade III — causal anchors / deferred relationships

The user can bind one **committed action** to one authored future trigger.

Canonical SC example root:

`opponent commits step/stance/weapon use -> visible red fate-thread anchor -> opponent later repeats exact authored relation -> bounded consequence resolves`

The red-thread presentation is valuable because the target can alter behaviour.

Possible triggers:
- use the same limb in the same movement family;
- repeat the same weapon path;
- cross the same spatial boundary;
- return to the same exact anchor;
- repeat a declared movement relationship.

Possible bounded consequences:
- minor repeated Battle-PL damage over exact actions;
- forced endpoint shift;
- reduced movement/reposition availability;
- mirrored portion of an earlier committed force;
- activation of another prepared exact Skill.

Not allowed:
- arbitrary `you acted before, therefore I can punish anything you do`;
- invisible trigger with no readable evidence;
- permanent limb disable from one setup;
- rewriting the earlier action.

Contextual roots:
- bind a doorway/key relationship for one occurrence;
- make an escape route react if crossed again;
- establish a future trigger around an exact object interaction;
- create Story choices where using an already-seen route carries a known consequence.

### Grade IV — Causal Space-Time

**Causal Space-Time** is the approved high-end SC direction.

It manipulates **current/future causal participation** while preserving committed historical truth.

Approved roots:

1. **causal severance** — cut one exact active dependency/relationship;
2. **causal quarantine** — remove one target/object/effect from the current causal sequence for a bounded interval;
3. **future exclusion** — prevent one exact relationship from resolving again during the authored window;
4. **redaction scar / impossible absence** — an annihilated present object leaves intact history, witnesses and Provenance referring to something that no longer exists;
5. **historical aperture** — contextual observation of a bounded prior spatial arrangement where legitimate evidence/anchor exists, without interaction or history rewrite;
6. **causal thread contest** — advanced users may sever, reroute or defend exact active causal anchors rather than merely teleport around each other.

Never implied:
- undo Battle result;
- erase committed Chronicle occurrence;
- retroactively prevent death;
- restore consumed resource by saying it was never used;
- save-state rollback;
- global future sight.

## 7. Fire lineage route — four-grade mechanics

### Grade I — heat separated from visible flame

The lineage can move/withdraw/deposit heat without requiring the ordinary visible flame body to travel the entire route.

Uses:
- heat one exact surface while leaving intervening air relatively unaffected;
- pull heat from one small object into another compatible sink;
- create deceptive low-visibility ignition setup without becoming Genjutsu.

Counterplay:
- range;
- thermal mass;
- insulation;
- breaking contact/line-of-effect;
- forcing rapid target changes.

### Grade II — selective combustion

The user can author what compatible matter/chakra medium the flame is allowed to consume within one exact expression.

Examples:
- burn cloth around a captive without automatically burning the captive;
- attack a seal medium/ink while limiting adjacent material damage;
- burn a selected equipment layer while leaving another untouched where material facts support it.

This is precision, not universal immunity or `burn only enemies`.

### Grade III — destructive contact without persistence

The fire ceases behaving primarily as sustained combustion and instead destroys material integrity at the exact contact surface.

The effect is brief and does not endlessly spread.

This is the developmental bridge to White Fire.

### Grade IV — White Fire

**White Fire is KEEP.**

Core rule:

> **White Fire does not persist forever. What it genuinely contacts can cease to exist in the present state.**

Battle boundaries:
- contact geometry matters;
- interception by sacrificial material/construct can matter;
- it does not automatically erase an entire Character because one packet connects;
- Battle PL/outcome authority remains intact for living participants;
- persistent inventory destruction is not automatically committed by ordinary competitive PvP;
- object annihilation requires an exact target/object interaction and mode/stakes that permit persistent destruction.

Story/Hotspot roots:
- remove a small impossible obstruction;
- annihilate one exact hostile seal component/object;
- erase contaminated material rather than burn/spread it;
- destroy an object so completely that only Chronicle/Provenance evidence remains.

PvP counters:
- intercept with expendable material;
- evade finite geometry;
- force the user to fire through valuable allied terrain/objectives;
- bait premature use;
- exploit its non-persistent nature;
- attack after high-commitment activation.

---

## 8. White Fire + Causal Space-Time — forbidden seed and CE-safe roots

Forbidden thought experiment:

`White Fire contact + Causal Space-Time = target ceases to exist from history`

Literal retroactive deletion remains **NOT AUTHORISED**.

The useful roots are:

### 8.1 Burn an active relationship

White Fire may destroy one current causal connection rather than the historical fact that the connection existed.

Examples:
- sever a currently active weapon-to-technique dependency;
- burn out one prepared causal anchor;
- destroy one current seal/object activation relation;
- temporarily disconnect an exact Provenance-derived expression from use while Provenance history remains intact.

### 8.2 Burn a future possibility

An exact target may be excluded from one future authored relation for the current Battle/event.

Examples:
- one weapon cannot strike one exact target again during the active exclusion;
- one key cannot reopen one door during the occurrence;
- one prepared thread cannot resolve twice.

### 8.3 Create an impossible absence

If present-state White Fire annihilates an object:
- records still mention it;
- witnesses still remember it;
- Provenance still records it;
- observer-safe systems may detect the contradiction.

Attempted erasure creates **more Chronicle evidence**, not less.

### 8.4 PvP persistence rule

Competitive PvP should default to match-local causal suppression / equipment projection loss unless the mode explicitly authorises persistent stakes.

No normal PvP match should let one rare ability permanently delete another player's years-old Provenance weapon from account history simply because the attack connected.

# PART C — WATER

## 9. Water apex root — continuity and force transmission

Do not make Water apex merely a larger wave or fictional named super-pressure attack.

### Grade I — connected-water awareness/control

Within bounded range, the user can treat genuinely connected water as one controllable medium and sense pressure/flow changes through it.

### Grade II — hydraulic force transfer

Force/pressure introduced at one point in a connected water body can be deliberately transmitted to another authored point.

Battle uses:
- strike through an unexpected point in a water field;
- reinforce a water barrier by routing force away;
- convert an incoming impact into movement elsewhere in the connected volume.

Context uses:
- move pressure through pipes/channels;
- stabilise or rupture flooded structures;
- manipulate locks/mechanisms through a connected fluid route where physically compatible.

### Grade III — momentum capture and relay

A prepared water network can absorb part of a compatible kinetic event and re-express it elsewhere rather than merely blocking it.

This is not free reflection:
- capacity is finite;
- network continuity matters;
- multiple simultaneous impacts overload it;
- captured force must resolve somewhere legitimate.

### Grade IV — bounded hydraulic topology

Within one prepared domain, separate connected sections of the same authored water network can function as one force-routing system even when direct line of sight is absent.

This does **not** teleport arbitrary objects through water and does not grant Ice/Boil Release.

Counterplay across the route:
- dry/absorb/remove water;
- break network continuity;
- freeze/boil/disperse through separately authorised effects;
- fight airborne/on isolated surfaces;
- overload with multiple vectors;
- contaminate or split the medium where relevant.

# PART D — WIND

## 10. Wind apex root — Dead Air / subtraction rather than projection

**Dead Air direction is KEEP**, with final display naming still refinable.

### Grade I — local air removal

Create a small low-air pocket or directional suction gradient.

Uses:
- disturb breathing briefly;
- pull smoke/mist/loose material;
- extinguish compatible combustion;
- alter projectile drag locally.

### Grade II — shaped vacuum route

Create a bounded path/volume where air is rapidly removed or drawn.

Uses:
- pull rather than push;
- collapse smoke screens;
- drag loose projectiles/particles off course;
- create movement hazards.

### Grade III — pressure-well control

Multiple air-removal zones can be arranged so surrounding atmosphere collapses toward selected points.

This can create strong pull without becoming universal telekinesis.

### Grade IV — gravity-adjacent mutation candidate

At the highest developed expression, the lineage may begin preserving a **pull vector** even when ordinary atmospheric pressure is insufficient to explain the full effect.

This is a deliberate SC mutation candidate, not automatically true gravity authority.

If finalised, preserve distinction from Rinnegan attraction/repulsion:
- this route is primarily pull-oriented;
- it develops from prepared subtraction/well geometry;
- setup/anchors remain readable;
- no generic omnidirectional `push anything` authority is implied.

PvP counters:
- leave the zone;
- break concentration/setup;
- force movement;
- use ranged pressure;
- sealed-air/protective equipment where authored;
- short-duration Yang adaptation where legitimately developed;
- exploit ally/objective risk;
- attack during high-maintenance zones.

Dead Air never equals `cannot breathe -> instantly loses`.

# PART E — LIGHTNING

## 11. Lightning apex root — electrical potential / route authorship

Do not solve Lightning by inventing another colour.

### Grade I — conductor potential sense

The user can read and deliberately establish potential differences among touched/marked compatible conductors.

### Grade II — discharge route preparation

The user can pre-ionise or otherwise prepare exact paths so a later Lightning discharge follows authored geometry rather than merely the shortest obvious route.

Uses:
- curve lightning around cover through prepared paths;
- route around an ally;
- seed a weapon/wire/wet surface network.

### Grade III — environmental circuit control

The user can treat a prepared set of conductive battlefield objects/surfaces as one routing network.

Weapons, wet ground, wire, armour and authored conductive gear become strategic nodes.

The power source still matters; this is not free infinite electricity.

### Grade IV — domain-scale potential field

Within a prepared bounded domain, the user controls how available electrical potential is distributed and discharged across the network.

This is the qualitative route beyond Kirin:

> **Kirin weaponises a natural lightning event. This apex route turns the whole prepared battlefield into the lightning-routing mechanism.**

Contextual uses:
- power/sabotage compatible mechanisms;
- safely discharge a dangerous electrical system;
- reroute natural storm charge;
- identify conductive fault paths.

PvP counters:
- insulation;
- grounding;
- sever conductive routes;
- dry/leave wet terrain;
- sacrifice a conductor to redirect charge;
- force rapid relocation so network setup becomes stale;
- pressure before enough nodes exist;
- avoid stacking metal gear without protection.

No automatic Magnet Release or electromagnetic control is granted.

# PART F — EARTH

## 12. Earth apex root — structural load / stress authorship

Do not reduce high Earth mastery to larger rocks or a fantasy-named earthquake.

### Grade I — stress-path perception

The user senses where load, compression and structural stress actually travel through a contiguous solid structure.

### Grade II — stress redistribution

The user can move an existing load/stress concentration from one valid path to another within bounded connected solid matter.

Uses:
- reinforce one wall by moving stress elsewhere;
- make armour/weapon material fail at a legitimate weak point;
- redirect an impact into surrounding ground.

### Grade III — structural function reassignment

The user can temporarily decide which parts of a prepared connected structure carry its load.

Contextual uses:
- hold a collapsing tunnel;
- deliberately drop one section of a fortress while preserving another;
- create safe evacuation time;
- expose a hidden support dependency.

### Grade IV — bounded structural network control

A prepared contiguous solid domain can be treated as one stress/load system.

A force applied at one point can be distributed across many supports or concentrated into one legitimate failure route.

This does not create free matter, Crystal Release, Magnet Release or universal gravity.

PvP counters:
- airborne combat;
- break terrain continuity;
- flexible/soft materials;
- avoid rigid armour dependence;
- rapid repositioning;
- attack the user before the structural read/setup completes;
- multi-vector threats that exceed one chosen load path.

# PART G — YIN

## 13. Yin Release — form/schema authoring, not Genjutsu reskin

Core SC rule:

> **Yin defines non-physical form, structure and schema that chakra systems can genuinely interact with.**

### Grade I — schema extraction / imprint

After legitimate observation/contact, the user can create a non-physical pattern record of an exact chakra shape/system.

This records **structure**, not power, nature, ownership or Mastery.

Uses:
- compare two seal/barrier patterns;
- preserve the shape of a damaged system;
- identify which portion of a repeated technique changed.

### Grade II — schema projection

The user can externalise a non-physical template that compatible chakra may flow through.

Battle roots:
- pre-shape one compatible allied technique route;
- create an intangible mould that narrows/splits/curves compatible chakra crossing it;
- establish a temporary bypass frame around one damaged chakra pathway without giving it living function.

Physical attacks can simply ignore many Yin-only forms.

### Grade III — bounded rule frame

A prepared Yin framework can impose one exact structural rule on compatible chakra entering its domain.

Examples:
- one technique family must follow a defined route;
- a chaotic chakra construct is forced into a stable bounded shape;
- a known seal pattern can be reconstructed as a **blueprint state** without automatically activating it.

This is not Genjutsu because it changes chakra structure, not what someone thinks they perceive.

### Grade IV — persistent abstract architecture

The user can sustain a complex non-physical structure independently long enough for other compatible systems to consume it.

Contextual roots:
- reconstruct the schema of a destroyed mechanism from sufficient evidence;
- preserve the form of something White Fire annihilated without recreating the original object;
- create a complex chakra architecture that Yang/Yin–Yang may later give function.

PvP counters:
- physical/non-chakra pressure;
- change technique family/shape;
- overload the frame;
- destroy/deny its anchor;
- force the user to maintain too many schemas;
- use unobserved/novel patterns.

Yin does not grant Genjutsu immunity, free Skill copying or Fūinjutsu Mastery.

# PART H — YANG

## 14. Yang Release — living function authorship, not healing reskin

Core SC rule:

> **Yang changes how a living body functions. It does not automatically repair what was damaged.**

### Grade I — function redistribution

The user can deliberately prioritise one physiological system by drawing functional capacity from another.

Examples:
- increase breathing efficiency while sacrificing explosive movement;
- preserve circulation while reducing peripheral strength;
- suppress pain response while losing fine sensory feedback.

This creates tradeoffs, not generic Stat buffs.

### Grade II — temporary adaptive function

The user can create a bounded temporary biological adaptation to one exact environment/threat after legitimate exposure/preparation.

Examples:
- improved oxygen extraction against Dead Air;
- temporary toxin filtration pathway;
- heat shedding / cold conservation;
- altered tissue response to one exact hostile condition.

Not permanent immunity.

### Grade III — functional rerouting / continuity

The body can remain operational while one ordinary subsystem is disabled by routing vital function around it.

Examples:
- continue acting despite a temporarily failed respiratory route;
- maintain circulation through an alternate pathway;
- keep a damaged limb biologically viable without repairing it.

Battle PL/injury authority still applies. The body functioning is not the same as being healed.

### Grade IV — vital sovereignty

For a bounded high-cost state, the user's life process can operate under a deliberately rewritten internal functional priority rather than ordinary anatomical dependency.

This may allow survival/operation through otherwise impossible temporary conditions, but:
- it does not restore Battle PL by itself;
- it does not erase injury;
- it cannot be maintained indefinitely;
- multiple simultaneous system failures can overwhelm it;
- post-state collapse/metabolic debt is a legitimate punish surface.

Contextual roots:
- survive a vacuum/toxin/hazard long enough to solve an event;
- preserve a dying ally's function until actual treatment arrives where exact transfer/support Skill exists;
- support dangerous transformations without granting permanent mastery.

PvP counters:
- extend the fight;
- force multiple simultaneous physiological problems;
- seal/suppress the Yang source where authored;
- exploit metabolic debt;
- pressure before adaptation is established;
- use control/denial rather than attempting one decisive physiological disable.

# PART I — YIN–YANG

## 15. Yin–Yang — give authored form bounded function

Core SC rule:

> **Yin supplies the schema. Yang supplies living/functional expression. Yin–Yang can make a known form temporarily function.**

### Grade I — functional patch

Create one bounded temporary functioning bridge across a known damaged chakra/body/system pattern.

Does not restore historical undamaged state.

### Grade II — functional construct

Give one Yin-authored non-physical form one narrow autonomous function.

Examples:
- a temporary chakra conduit that routes one Skill family;
- a non-sentient construct that performs one exact repeated mechanical action;
- a temporary replacement pathway.

No new independent Character/person is created.

### Grade III — reconstitution from known schema

Given sufficient accurate pattern evidence plus required matter/chakra/vital source, reconstruct a damaged/lost functional structure as a **new present-state instance**, not retroactively restore the old history.

This creates a major CE distinction:

> **reconstruction != history reversal**

A White-Fire-destroyed object may someday be reproducible from schema/provenance, but the result is a new object with new identity/provenance unless separate authority says otherwise.

### Grade IV — bounded reality instantiation

A sufficiently developed user can instantiate one complex known schema with bounded function for a limited domain/time.

Possible contextual uses:
- temporarily restore a destroyed bridge/mechanism long enough for evacuation;
- recreate a working seal/barrier from a known schema for one occurrence;
- create a temporary functional body/chakra structure that keeps someone alive until real intervention.

Hard prohibitions:
- resurrection by history rewrite;
- creating a sentient person from nothing;
- restoring identity/ownership simply because a form is reproduced;
- arbitrary wish fulfilment;
- permanent world mutation without exact Story/CE authority.

PvP counters:
- destroy/deny schema anchor;
- interrupt the lengthy setup;
- exhaust the vitality source;
- force incompatible simultaneous demands;
- target the created function's narrow dependency rather than raw durability.

# PART J — WHOLE-BUILD INTERACTION

## 16. Required competitive integration

Every expression above must be audited with the assembled build:

`Character + Stats/PL + Skills + Bloodline/lineage + weapon + gear + Summon + consumables + Fūin + Provenance + transformation/Hosted Entity + teammates + battlefield context`

Priority interaction examples:

### Space-Time + weapons / Provenance

- a Provenance weapon with repeated wielder history may provide richer legitimate spatial/history predicates **only when an exact Skill queries them**;
- more Provenance does not mean stronger Causal Space-Time automatically;
- weapon-history interaction must never become a universal provenance multiplier.

### White Fire + Equipment

- exact material/construct interception may matter;
- ordinary PvP defaults to match-local equipment projection suppression rather than permanent account destruction;
- high-stakes Story destruction may permanently retire a created instance only through an exact committed occurrence.

### Dead Air + Yang

- Yang adaptation may create temporary breathing counterplay;
- that does not make Yang mandatory because movement, interruption, range, Gear and team rescue remain other answers.

### Lightning + weapons / wire / Water

- conductive Equipment and prepared wire can become deliberate build synergy;
- Water users may create routes that help or catastrophically expose either team;
- Fūin insulation/grounding effects can exist only where exact definitions author them.

### Earth + heavy Equipment

- rigid/heavy armour may be excellent against ordinary attacks but create structural interaction risk against stress-manipulation builds;
- flexible/light builds trade mitigation for reduced structural exploitability.

### Yin + Fūinjutsu

- Yin may provide schema/pattern information;
- Fūinjutsu still owns seal Knowledge/Access/Competence and exact executable effects;
- one does not make the other redundant.

### Yin–Yang + Crafting / Provenance

- a reconstructed object from known schema is a **new created/provenance instance** unless exact identity authority says otherwise;
- recreation does not copy historical ownership/mastery or erase the original object's destruction.

## 17. Contextual / Hotspot mandate

Apex capability value should not be measured only by Battle damage.

Every Grade III/IV family should support at least one meaningful non-Battle opportunity class.

Examples:
- Space-Time: investigate prior spatial arrangement; quarantine hazard; bind route consequences;
- White Fire: remove impossible contamination/obstruction; create redaction-scar evidence;
- Water: stabilise flooded systems; transfer pressure through channels;
- Dead Air: clear gas/smoke; create temporary vacuum isolation;
- Lightning: reroute dangerous electrical systems; exploit storm infrastructure;
- Earth: stabilise/collapse structures deliberately;
- Yin: recover/compare lost pattern schemas;
- Yang: survive or maintain function through extreme environments;
- Yin–Yang: temporarily restore function to a known damaged system.

Story/World owns the event and opportunity. Combat owns what the exact capability can do when a compatible factual context is supplied.
