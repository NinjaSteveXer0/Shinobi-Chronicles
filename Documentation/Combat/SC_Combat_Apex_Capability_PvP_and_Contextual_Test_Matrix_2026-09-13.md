# Shinobi Chronicles — Apex Capability PvP and Contextual Test Matrix

**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Owner:** Combat / Skills / Items / Weapons  
**Status:** **BINDING COUNTERPLAY / CONTEXTUAL AUDIT CONTRACT — NUMBERS AND IMPLEMENTATION SEPARATE**

## 1. Purpose

This matrix applies the existing Counterplay-First and Whole-Build doctrines to the advanced capability package authored in:

`Documentation/Combat/SC_Combat_Apex_Chakra_Four_Level_Mechanics_Package_2026-09-13.md`

Every Grade III/IV capability must pass this matrix before numerical tuning or runtime activation.

Canonical test:

> **The owner should feel dangerous. The opponent should still be playing.**

A capability fails if its practical PvP meaning is only `you did not bring the correct Bloodline, therefore you lose` or if its contextual meaning is merely flavour text attached to Battle numbers.

---

## 2. Required audit fields

Every advanced capability package must declare:

- **Threat:** what the capability actually threatens;
- **Readable tell:** what a legitimate opponent can observe before or during resolution;
- **Setup / commitment:** what the user must establish or spend;
- **Ordinary answers:** actions available to builds that do not possess a special counter-Bloodline;
- **Specialist answers:** exact capability families that can contest it more efficiently;
- **Punish window:** when the user is exposed if the plan fails or is forced early;
- **Team interaction:** how allies/enemies change the result;
- **Contextual / Hotspot verbs:** meaningful non-Battle uses;
- **Non-game rejection test:** the version we explicitly refuse to build.

## 3. Space-Time / Causal Space-Time

### Threat

- invalidates predictable geometry;
- manipulates recent legitimate spatial history;
- can bind committed actions to future triggers;
- at Grade IV can sever/quarantine/exclude one exact current or future causal relationship.

### Readable tell

- prepared spatial stance / anchor;
- visible or observer-detectable anchor relationship where exact sensory authority allows;
- Grade III deferred anchors should normally have a visible/readable fate-thread or equivalent state;
- Grade IV causal suppression should expose the exact relation affected rather than silently changing unrelated rules.

### Setup / commitment

- recent real position/path/history predicate;
- prepared stance or authored anchor;
- normal action opportunity for deliberate Battle setup unless an exact Skill says otherwise;
- lockout after interception/redirect family to prevent chained universal defence.

### Ordinary answers

- change attack vector;
- feint and expend the prepared response;
- use disposable projectiles;
- switch from weapon to non-weapon Skill;
- multi-vector pressure;
- force the user to move away from prepared anchors;
- deliberately avoid repeating a visible deferred trigger;
- attack during causal lockout.

### Specialist answers

- Fūinjutsu/barrier effects that lock or define exact local geometry;
- sensory/contextual Skills that expose anchors/routes;
- another Space-Time/Causal user contesting exact active threads;
- Yin schema analysis of a known anchor structure where separately compatible.

### Punish window

- after stance consumed;
- during lockout;
- when forced off anchor geometry;
- when too many concurrent relationships exceed authored capacity.

### Team interaction

- allies may intentionally bait or absorb a prepared interception;
- coordinated multi-vector attacks pressure one-relation limits;
- friendly anchors can provide mobility but also become predictable attack surfaces;
- Causal users can create route-control roles rather than pure damage carries.

### Contextual / Hotspot verbs

- inspect bounded prior spatial arrangement;
- quarantine hazard/object/effect;
- create conditional route consequences;
- identify impossible geometry;
- preserve or sever one active object/system relationship.

### Non-game rejection

Reject:
- universal auto-dodge;
- reaction to every attack without prior commitment;
- `you used this weapon before, so I own its future forever`;
- retroactive Battle-result rewrite;
- invisible delayed punishment with no behavioural answer.

## 4. White Fire

### Threat

Finite-contact present-state annihilation of valid contacted matter/object state.

### Readable tell

- extremely distinct activation/presentation;
- finite contact geometry should be readable;
- no invisible aura that passively erases everything entering melee range.

### Setup / commitment

- high commitment / limited expression;
- exact line/contact/target relation;
- persistent object annihilation only in modes/events that explicitly allow persistent stakes.

### Ordinary answers

- evade geometry;
- intercept with expendable material/construct;
- force fire through terrain/objectives the user values;
- bait use on low-value target;
- attack after use/commitment;
- use multiple threats rather than one irreplaceable object.

### Specialist answers

- Space-Time displacement/quarantine;
- exact barrier/construct designed to sacrifice a layer;
- Fūinjutsu containment if definition specifically supports the interaction;
- Causal severance against an active White-Fire relation at very high development.

### Punish window

- after finite discharge;
- during aim/setup;
- after consuming a scarce/high-cost expression;
- when target uses sacrificial interception.

### Team interaction

- body-blocking with living allies must not become desirable due to permanent-deletion rules; living participant outcomes remain Battle resolver-owned;
- disposable constructs/tools can deliberately protect a carry;
- area denial creates friendly-fire/objective risk.

### Contextual / Hotspot verbs

- annihilate exact contamination;
- remove an impossible obstruction;
- destroy one hostile seal component;
- permanently retire a Story-authorised object, leaving Chronicle/Provenance evidence.

### Non-game rejection

Reject:
- one pixel touch = Character erased;
- permanent account-item deletion in ordinary PvP;
- infinite spread/persistence;
- erase identity/history/ownership/memory.

## 5. White Fire + Causal Space-Time

### Threat

Destroys one active/future relationship rather than deleting the historical fact that the relationship existed.

### Readable tell

The affected relation must be exact and communicable: `weapon -> target`, `seal -> host`, `anchor -> trigger`, etc.

### Setup / commitment

Requires both legitimate White-Fire expression and Causal relationship authority. No generic `touch anything -> erase all related history` inference.

### Ordinary answers

- switch weapon/route/target;
- force the exclusion to apply to a low-value relation;
- use an unrelated Skill/system;
- wait out bounded suppression where authored;
- break setup before relation-burn resolves.

### Specialist answers

- Causal thread defence;
- reconstruct a usable new present-state relation through exact compatible Yin–Yang/Fūin/Crafting route, without restoring erased history;
- provenance-aware alternate expression that does not depend on the severed relation.

### Punish window

Extreme opportunity/resource cost; narrow target relation; user becomes vulnerable if opponent simply changes strategy.

### Team interaction

Can surgically remove one key synergy from a team, but cannot strip an entire team build at once.

### Contextual / Hotspot verbs

- burn out one dangerous key/door relation;
- sever an active seal dependency;
- quarantine one catastrophic mechanism;
- create an impossible absence that future investigation can consume.

### Non-game rejection

Reject literal committed-history deletion.

## 6. Water — connected-medium force routing

### Threat

Turns legitimately connected water into a route for pressure/force/momentum rather than merely a projectile source.

### Readable tell

- visible connected water network;
- prepared water field/routes;
- flow/pressure changes can provide tells.

### Setup / commitment

Requires usable connected medium and network continuity. Stronger Grade III/IV behaviour requires prepared volume/domain.

### Ordinary answers

- leave/dry the network;
- split the water body;
- fight airborne/on isolated surfaces;
- overwhelm from multiple simultaneous vectors;
- contaminate/disperse medium where mechanics allow;
- force user into terrain with little available water.

### Specialist answers

- Ice to alter continuity;
- Boil/Fire to vaporise/disrupt where sufficient;
- Earth to break channels;
- Wind to disperse smaller bodies/mist;
- exact sealing/absorption tools.

### Punish window

Network setup time; lost value when continuity breaks; capacity overload when capturing force.

### Team interaction

Allies can seed water routes, but careless Water teammates can also create enemy routing surfaces. Team positioning around the same medium becomes meaningful.

### Contextual / Hotspot verbs

- stabilise/rupture flooded infrastructure;
- route pressure through pipes/channels;
- operate compatible mechanisms indirectly;
- redirect dangerous fluid force during disasters.

### Non-game rejection

Reject arbitrary force transmission through disconnected droplets or teleporting objects through water without separate authority.

## 7. Wind — Dead Air / vacuum subtraction

### Threat

Removes air, creates suction, interferes with breathing/combustion/particles and at higher development creates prepared pull wells.

### Readable tell

- visible movement of smoke/dust/clothing/debris toward low-pressure zone;
- breathing/environmental cues;
- prepared well geometry.

### Setup / commitment

Bounded zone, concentration/maintenance, ally/objective risk, and escalation time for serious breathing pressure.

### Ordinary answers

- leave zone;
- interrupt user;
- attack from range;
- force user movement;
- break concentration;
- use protective/sealed-air equipment where equipped;
- act quickly before cumulative breathing pressure matters.

### Specialist answers

- Yang temporary respiratory adaptation;
- Space-Time displacement;
- Wind contest / pressure restoration where exact Skill supports it;
- equipment/Fūin air-support packages.

### Punish window

Maintaining strong vacuum limits flexibility; close placement threatens allies; user is vulnerable when opponent refuses to remain in the zone.

### Team interaction

Can pull enemies into ally attacks, clear smoke for teammates, or ruin allied Fire/poison-gas strategies. Team composition matters.

### Contextual / Hotspot verbs

- remove toxic gas/smoke;
- vacuum-isolate one hazardous area;
- pull loose material through confined spaces;
- assist environmental rescue/sabotage.

### Non-game rejection

Reject instant suffocation and universal gravity/telekinesis from low-grade Dead Air.

## 8. Lightning — prepared potential/discharge network

### Threat

Turns prepared conductors into authored discharge paths, eventually making battlefield electrical topology the attack mechanism.

### Readable tell

- placed wire/conductors;
- wet terrain;
- seeded weapons/nodes;
- visible charge/potential buildup;
- storm/environment state.

### Setup / commitment

Requires nodes/conductors/potential source. Strong domain play cannot appear from an empty battlefield with no setup.

### Ordinary answers

- break wire/routes;
- leave wet terrain;
- discard or reposition conductive gear;
- use insulation/grounding equipment where owned;
- pressure before network completes;
- force user to move so nodes become stale.

### Specialist answers

- Earth grounding;
- Water can either contest/remove network or dangerously amplify it depending use;
- Space-Time reposition beyond nodes;
- exact Fūin insulation/grounding.

### Punish window

Preparation investment; stale network; diverted/sacrificed discharge; self/team exposure if routing is mismanaged.

### Team interaction

Weapons, wire, Water Skills, metal Gear and conductive summons/tools can intentionally complete circuits. Opponents can exploit the same topology if their kit permits.

### Contextual / Hotspot verbs

- safely discharge infrastructure;
- reroute power;
- sabotage mechanisms;
- route storm charge;
- diagnose electrical fault paths.

### Non-game rejection

Reject `all metal-equipped opponents are automatically stunned/damaged every turn` and automatic Magnet Release.

## 9. Earth — structural stress/load control

### Threat

Finds and manipulates the actual routes by which solid structures carry load and failure.

### Readable tell

- contact/preparation with connected structure;
- cracking/load cues;
- altered support geometry;
- target must actually depend on rigid structure/material.

### Setup / commitment

Connected solid medium; read/setup period for precise high-end manipulation.

### Ordinary answers

- go airborne;
- move onto disconnected/flexible material;
- avoid rigid heavy equipment dependence;
- break continuity;
- reposition faster than the user can re-read structure;
- multi-vector pressure.

### Specialist answers

- Wind/Space-Time mobility;
- Water to change terrain support;
- exact flexible/anti-shock Gear;
- another Earth specialist contesting load route.

### Punish window

Poor value against airborne/flexible targets; setup interruption; overload when too many forces arrive from different directions.

### Team interaction

Excellent terrain support for allies, but altering load can compromise friendly cover/objectives. Heavy-armour allies may want explicit coordination.

### Contextual / Hotspot verbs

- hold collapsing tunnel;
- controlled demolition;
- preserve one structure while sacrificing another;
- reveal hidden support dependencies.

### Non-game rejection

Reject `touch ground -> all grounded enemies take unavoidable damage` and arbitrary matter/gravity control.

## 10. Yin — schema/form architecture

### Threat

Records and projects real non-physical chakra structure, allowing compatible chakra to be shaped/routed through authored frameworks.

### Readable tell

- visible/sensory chakra framework where observer capability permits;
- exact known pattern relation;
- prepared schema anchor.

### Setup / commitment

Requires legitimate observation/knowledge of the pattern for high-fidelity use; maintaining multiple frameworks creates capacity pressure.

### Ordinary answers

- use physical/non-chakra attack route;
- switch to a novel/changed Skill shape;
- break anchor;
- overload framework;
- attack before accurate schema is extracted.

### Specialist answers

- Genjutsu can attack the observer/user without being made redundant by Yin;
- Fūinjutsu can contest exact seal architecture;
- Space-Time can bypass local schema geometry;
- White Fire may annihilate present anchors but not erase learned schema/history.

### Punish window

Novel/unobserved technique; too many simultaneous schemas; physical pressure; false/partial evidence can lead to an incomplete template if exact Knowledge supports only that.

### Team interaction

Yin can pre-shape allied chakra routes, stabilise allied complex techniques or provide blueprint information to Fūinjutsu/Yin–Yang users. It should be a high-skill enabler, not a universal damage multiplier.

### Contextual / Hotspot verbs

- compare seal/barrier patterns;
- preserve a damaged blueprint;
- reconstruct nonfunctional schema from evidence;
- identify where two apparently similar systems differ.

### Non-game rejection

Reject free Skill copying, automatic seal mastery, Genjutsu immunity, mind reading or `I know the pattern so I own the power`.

## 11. Yang — living-function control

### Threat

Changes how a living body functions, allowing tradeoffs, adaptive physiology and temporary functional rerouting through subsystem failure.

### Readable tell

- physiological transformation/state cues;
- explicit source-owned adaptation state;
- metabolic cost/debt should be observable or inferable where known.

### Setup / commitment

Tradeoff required. Higher adaptation normally needs preparation/exposure and cannot answer every hazard simultaneously.

### Ordinary answers

- extend fight;
- attack multiple systems/problems rather than one;
- use control, sealing, movement denial or resource pressure instead of one physiological shutdown;
- change damage/condition type after adaptation commits.

### Specialist answers

- Fūinjutsu/Kinjutsu suppression of exact Yang route where authored;
- Genjutsu attacks perception rather than physiology;
- White Fire/object/space control bypasses many ordinary biological adaptations;
- Dead Air can increase scale/duration beyond one adaptation's capacity.

### Punish window

Metabolic debt, post-state collapse, sacrificed function, wrong adaptation chosen, adaptation setup interrupted.

### Team interaction

Yang can create rescue windows and enable dangerous frontliners but consumes meaningful action/resource budget; teams can focus on exploiting the sacrificed function.

### Contextual / Hotspot verbs

- survive vacuum/toxin/extreme temperature long enough to act;
- maintain someone's living function until actual medical treatment;
- support dangerous transformations without conferring mastery;
- adapt to environmental conditions for bounded exploration.

### Non-game rejection

Reject passive immunity to conditions, free healing, Battle-PL refill, injury erasure or permanent every-hazard adaptation.

## 12. Yin–Yang — known-form bounded function

### Threat

Combines accurate schema with functional expression to patch, reconstruct or temporarily instantiate known systems.

### Readable tell

- schema + vitality/chakra source;
- significant setup for higher grades;
- created function should have identifiable anchor/dependency.

### Setup / commitment

Requires correct pattern/schema, compatible matter/chakra/vital source and time/attention. High-end instantiation is not a one-click reaction.

### Ordinary answers

- destroy/deny schema anchor;
- interrupt construction;
- exhaust source;
- attack narrow dependency of created function;
- force incompatible simultaneous repairs/constructs.

### Specialist answers

- Yin can contest schema;
- Yang can contest functional source;
- White Fire can annihilate present instantiated matter/anchor;
- Causal Space-Time can sever one exact active support relation.

### Punish window

Large resource/setup cost; narrow authored function; new construct can have new vulnerabilities even if modelled on something historically powerful.

### Team interaction

Major support/engineering role: temporary bridge, barrier, chakra pathway, life-support structure, etc. Teammates must protect setup and exploit the temporary window.

### Contextual / Hotspot verbs

- temporarily restore a destroyed mechanism;
- rebuild a known seal/barrier for one occurrence;
- create temporary life-support/chakra structure;
- reconstruct a lost object as a new present-state instance where exact Crafting/identity authority supports it.

### Non-game rejection

Reject resurrection-by-history-rewrite, sentient creation from nothing, automatic ownership restoration, wish fulfilment or permanent world edits without Story/CE authority.

## 13. Team PvP mandatory stress tests

Before any Grade III/IV package is declared PvP-ready, test at least:

1. **solo owner vs ordinary flexible build** — opponent must have non-specialist decisions;
2. **owner + ideal support teammate** — ensure synergy can be brutal without removing counterplay;
3. **mirror match** — interaction must not deadlock or become arbitrary priority roulette;
4. **multi-vector pressure team** — test capacity/setup limitations;
5. **control-heavy opponent** — ensure owner does not bypass action economy simply because capability is rare;
6. **Provenance weapon specialist opponent** — test causal/equipment interactions without deleting account history;
7. **Summon-supported opponent** — ensure summon actions remain controller-action constrained unless exact authority says otherwise;
8. **Hosted Entity/transformation carry** — no additive PL or immunity collapse;
9. **objective-based Battle** — capability must interact with objectives/terrain without making `kill all` the only balance lens.

---

## 14. Final gate

No exact numerical package should be accepted merely because its individual value looks reasonable.

A Grade III/IV capability is ready for number tuning only when:

- threat is clear;
- opponent tells are legitimate;
- at least two ordinary answer families exist unless the capability is deliberately exceptional and narrowly authored;
- specialist counters complement rather than replace ordinary play;
- setup and punish windows are explicit;
- team interaction does not create an obvious compulsory meta pick;
- contextual use exists where the capability design calls for it;
- no persistent-history or ownership contradiction is introduced;
- whole-build stacking can be audited separately.

> **Counterplay is part of the power's definition, not a nerf applied later.**
