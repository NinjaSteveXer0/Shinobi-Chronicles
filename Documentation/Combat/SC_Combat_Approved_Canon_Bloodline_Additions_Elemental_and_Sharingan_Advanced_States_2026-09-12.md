## 1. Player-approved additions

Stephen explicitly approves adding the following canon-derived Bloodline families/states to the Shinobi Chronicles retained roster:

- **Ice Release** — KEEP
- **Lava Release** — KEEP
- **Boil Release** — KEEP

- **Explosion Release** — KEEP
- **Mangekyo Sharingan** — KEEP as an advanced Sharingan state/family surface
- **Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan** — KEEP as a further advanced Sharingan state/family surface

This approval follows the project-wide Canon Research First protocol. Online research confirms the six elemental releases are established Naruto kekkei-genkai nature transformations, while Mangekyo Sharingan is an advanced Sharingan state with user-specific ocular techniques and deterioration costs, and Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan is a further evolved state reached through an exceptional eye-transplant/blood-relative provenance route in demonstrated canon cases.

## 2. Important non-collapse

Mangekyo Sharingan and Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan may appear as distinct progression/research milestones or Bloodline-surface entries in UI, but semantically they are **not unrelated hereditary Bloodlines separate from Sharingan**.

Preserve:

**Sharingan possession != Mangekyo Access**  
**Mangekyo Access != Eternal Mangekyo Access**  
**Mangekyo possession != every user-specific Mangekyo technique**  
**Eternal Mangekyo != generic blindness immunity item**  
**advanced ocular state != free Base PL mutation**

Exact awakening/provenance, eye-specific techniques, transplant rules, compatibility and progression are separate authority.

## 3. Elemental Bloodline direction

The six elemental Bloodlines are included because they are important Naruto identity anchors and support highly popular characters such as Haku.

They should not be implemented only as passive elemental-damage multipliers.

Where canon supports it, different wielders of the same elemental Bloodline may have materially different expressions, media, shapes and tactical verbs. Shinobi Chronicles should preserve that diversity through exact Skills, contextual uses, development and representation-specific capability rather than treating each release as one universal five-skill package.

No automatic Base PL bonus is created by this inclusion decision.

## 4. Research anchors used

Current online research consulted on 2026-09-12 includes:

- Naruto official site material for Haku / Ice Release and canon character/technique context;
- Naruto official site and current reference material for advanced Sharingan states;
- current Naruto reference catalogue for combined-nature kekkei-genkai classifications and known manifestations of Ice, Lava, Boil, Magnet, Scorch and Explosion Release.

Exact technique-by-technique mechanical authoring must perform proportional research again when those packages are activated for production.

## Final lock

> **Ice Release, Lava Release, Boil Release, Magnet Release, Scorch Release and Explosion Release are KEEP for Shinobi Chronicles. Mangekyo Sharingan and Eternal Mangekyo Sharingan are also KEEP as advanced Sharingan state/family surfaces, not unrelated standalone hereditary Bloodlines.**
