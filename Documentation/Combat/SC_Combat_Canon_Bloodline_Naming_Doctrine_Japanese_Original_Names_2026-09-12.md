# Shinobi Chronicles — Canon Bloodline Naming Doctrine: Japanese / Original Names

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Owner:** Combat / Skills / Items / Weapons  
**Status:** **BINDING PLAYER-FACING NAMING DOCTRINE FOR CANON BLOODLINES**

## 1. Player decision

Stephen explicitly prefers established Japanese/original canon Bloodline names over invented English translations for Shinobi Chronicles presentation.

Examples:
- **Ketsuryūgan**, not `Blood Dragon Eye`;
- **Shikotsumyaku**, not `Dead Bone Release` or `Macabre Bone Pulse` unless a separate SC alias is deliberately authored;
- **Jōgan**, not `Pure Eye`;
- **Senrigan**, not `Clairvoyant Eye`;
- **Kokugan**, not `Black Eye`;
- **Rinne Sharingan**, retaining the established canon name;
- **Mangekyō Sharingan** and **Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan**, retaining the established canon names.

## 2. Scope

For canon-derived Bloodlines, dōjutsu, kekkei genkai, kekkei tōta, kekkei mōra and advanced canon lineage states:

> **Prefer the established Japanese/original canon name used by authoritative Naruto/Boruto sources or the standard established romanisation over a newly invented literal English translation.**

Do not replace a recognisable canon term merely because an English phrase sounds more descriptive.

## 3. Exceptions

An English display alias may still be used when:
- the canon ability is genuinely unnamed;
- Stephen/Codex explicitly approves an SC-facing name;
- the English name is itself the established official/localised canon term;
- UI readability requires a subtitle or explanatory gloss.

When the canon ability is unnamed, clearly distinguish:

**canon unnamed ability != SC-authored display name**.

## 4. Existing SC-original Bloodlines

This naming doctrine does not rename original Shinobi Chronicles Bloodlines such as:
- Chronicle Scar;
- Ink Blood;
- Grave Pulse;
- Stormheart;
- Chakra Palimpsest;
- Echo Flesh.

Those remain their current English SC-original names unless separately changed.

## Final lock

> **Canon Bloodlines keep their established Japanese/original names in Shinobi Chronicles. Invented English literal translations are not the default player-facing names.**
