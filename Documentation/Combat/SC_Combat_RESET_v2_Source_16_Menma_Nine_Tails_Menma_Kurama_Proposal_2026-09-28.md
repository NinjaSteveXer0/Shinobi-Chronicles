# E. Menma Nine-Tails Enhancement — Cloak Bargain

ID:

`menma_nine_tails_cloak_bargain`

Activation:

**HOSTED / MENMA-SPECIFIC / PRE-PARTNERSHIP YIN STATE**

Limit:

**once per Battle**

Use:

Before Menma commits one direct `menma_nine_tails`-assisted ATTACK.

Player text:

> **Ask Kurama for more chakra. That attack gains 25% ATK, but Kurama holds back from offensive help on Menma's next action.**

Effect:

- qualifying attack gains **+25% pre-Stamina Attack PL**;
- after the attack resolves, establish `menma_kurama_holds_back`;
- through Menma's next normal action opportunity, Menma cannot choose another **Menma-Nine-Tails-assisted ATTACK**;
- Menma may still use:
  - ordinary Menma Skills;
  - Items;
  - non-offensive contextual/source actions where separately legal;
  - exact defensive source reactions where separately authorised;
- after Menma completes that next action opportunity, the holdback ends;
- no PL/Stat transfer;
- no extra action;
- no partnership evidence generated merely from the bargain.

Decision:

> **Take a bigger Kurama hit now, knowing your next turn must rely on Menma rather than another fox attack.**

This reflects a cordial but still negotiated relationship:

> **Kurama helps because he agrees to this use, not because Menma owns his power.**

# G. Menma Kurama Enhancement — Two Minds, One Move

ID:

`menma_kurama_two_minds_one_move`

Activation:

**HOSTED / MENMA-SPECIFIC / GENUINE PARTNERSHIP / YIN STATE**

Limit:

**once per Battle**

Trigger:

Menma resolves one legal **non-damaging Menma-owned Skill** that:
- applies setup;
- applies control;
- provides support;
- or creates another exact tactical state,

and the enemy Active remains a legal hostile target.

Player text:

> **Menma makes the play; Kurama sees the opening. After one non-damaging Skill, Kurama follows with a 20 ATK chakra strike on the enemy Active.**

Effect:

- Menma's chosen non-damaging Skill resolves first;
- if the enemy Active remains a legal target, resolve one source-owned **ATK20** Kurama follow-through packet against that Active;
- ordinary Stamina mitigates the follow-through;
- both effects belong to the same Menma action opportunity;
- no second Kurama turn;
- no second action opportunity;
- if the first Skill makes the enemy Active no longer legal, no follow-through packet is created;
- cannot trigger from Items unless separately authored;
- cannot trigger from another action that already contains an unauthorised extra-action/follow-up chain.

Identity:

> **Partnership lets Menma and Kurama act as two minds inside one turn.**

This is not a friendship damage buff. It changes how Menma's tactical Skills connect to Kurama pressure.

# H. Menma Kurama host-assisted Skills

## 1. `menma_kurama_follow_through` — **Kurama Follow-Through**

Class:

**COOPERATIVE SOURCE-ASSISTED ATTACK / COMBO**

Target:

one hostile

Base ATK:

**40**

Opening ATK:

**52**

Player text:

> **Strike for 40 ATK. If Menma's last action hit or controlled this same enemy without Kurama, Kurama follows that opening and the attack hits for 52 instead.**

Rules:
- one direct packet;
- ordinary Stamina mitigation;
- ATK52 requires Menma's immediately previous action opportunity to have:
  - used a Menma-owned action not sourced from Kurama;
  - targeted this same hostile;
  - dealt positive damage OR established a still-relevant control/setup state;
- no second packet;
- no extra action;
- no hidden combo counter.

This rewards alternating **Menma -> Kurama**, instead of spamming Kurama Skills every turn.

---

## 2. `menma_kurama_one_look_is_enough` — **One Look Is Enough**

Class:

**COOPERATIVE SOURCE-ASSISTED REACTION / ADAPTATION**

Limit:

**once per Battle**

First trigger:

one hostile direct Skill deals positive Battle-PL damage to Menma.

Effect:
- record:
  - that hostile;
  - the exact Skill/action ID used.

If the same hostile later uses that same exact direct Skill against Menma again in the same Battle:

- reduce that repeated direct Attack-PL packet **50% pre-Stamina**;
- ordinary Stamina then resolves;
- consume One Look Is Enough.

Player text:

> **Menma and Kurama only need to see a move once. If that enemy uses the same move on Menma again, cut its ATK in half.**

Rules:
- the first use is only the read;
- no damage is prevented on the first occurrence;
- only the same hostile + same exact Skill qualifies;
- a different Skill does not trigger it;
- an AREA action only qualifies if Menma is directly and legitimately affected and the exact action identity matches on repeat;
- control-only effects with no Attack-PL packet are not reduced by this rule;
- no Dodge/Accuracy/Speed Stat is created.

This is partnership expressed as **shared learning during the fight**.

---

## 3. `menma_kurama_plan_b` — **Plan B**

Class:

**COOPERATIVE SOURCE-ASSISTED REACTION / ADAPTATION**

Limit:

**once per Battle**

Trigger:

Menma commits one direct ATTACK against a hostile, but that attack resolves with **0 positive Battle-PL damage specifically because a legal guard/prevention/substitution-style defence stopped the damage**.

Player text:

> **If Menma's attack is completely stopped, Kurama immediately tries the backup plan with an 18 ATK chakra strike.**

Effect:
- after the original attack fully resolves, if the original target is still a legal hostile target, resolve one **ATK18** Kurama follow-through packet;
- ordinary Stamina mitigation applies;
- no follow-through if:
  - target is no longer legally present;
  - the attack failed because the target was invalid before commit;
  - Menma chose a non-damaging action;
  - the original result was 0 for a reason other than an exact defensive resolution;
- no bonus action;
- no second turn;
- one follow-through packet only.

This gives the partnership a real backup plan without making every failed attack automatically succeed.
