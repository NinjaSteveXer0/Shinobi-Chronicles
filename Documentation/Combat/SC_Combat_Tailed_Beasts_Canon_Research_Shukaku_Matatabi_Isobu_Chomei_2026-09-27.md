# 2. Shared Tailed Beast baseline

The four researched sources are members of the same canonical Tailed Beast family.

Safe shared canon anchors:

- extremely large chakra reserves;
- capacity to transfer chakra;
- Tailed Beast Ball as a family technique;
- Tailed Beast Telepathy / Mental World communication among Tailed Beasts;
- persistent chakra-being nature rather than ordinary animal/Summon ontology.

Combat caution:

- **shared family capability does not mean every SC representation automatically receives the same button/action package**;
- Tailed Beast Ball may be shared canon, but SC still needs exact access/action-economy authoring;
- Shukaku is notably not shown personally using a Tailed Beast Ball in the manga, despite the family technique being attributed to Tailed Beasts generally;
- no generic friendship/synergy multiplier is justified by canon;
- no Entity PL transfers wholesale to a host.

# 3. SHUKAKU / ONE-TAIL

## 3.1 Secure identity anchors

- canonical identity: **Shukaku = One-Tail**;
- tanuki-like Tailed Beast;
- strongly associated with sand;
- natural cursed-seal-like markings are mechanically relevant;
- known nature transformations include **Wind Release**, **Earth Release**, and **Magnet Release**;
- Shukaku takes special pride in sealing/defensive capability.

## 3.2 Secure combat anchors

### Sand manipulation / sand body

Shukaku's body and combat identity are strongly sand-based.

Safe design foundation:
- sand offense;
- sand defense;
- sand restraint/control;
- unusual resilience/body-reformation logic may be represented where exact SC state permits.

Do not automatically convert this into:
- blanket physical immunity;
- infinite regeneration;
- Gaara's entire personal sand toolkit.

### Wind Release

**Wind Release: Drilling Air Bullet** is a manga-supported Shukaku technique, debuting in chapter 135.

Safe design foundation:
- strong ranged Wind attack;
- pressure/projectile identity.

### Wind + sand cooperation

**Wind Release: Sand Buckshot** is manga-supported cooperation between Shukaku and Gaara, chapter 658.

Safe design foundation:
- Shukaku can amplify/project sand through Wind in cooperation.

Important boundary:
- this is cooperation authority, not proof that any arbitrary host automatically owns Gaara's sand manipulation.

### Fūinjutsu / cursed-seal markings

Shukaku can use its natural markings in sealing techniques and has a strong defensive/sealing identity.

Safe design foundation:
- restraint/sealing;
- defensive control;
- Magnet Release/sealing interplay where exact mechanic is later authored.

### Defence

Manga material supports Shukaku's pride in its defence and its use of sand to intercept powerful attacks.

Safe design foundation:
- a real defensive package is canon-supported.

## 3.3 Personality / cooperation anchor

- childish;
- short-tempered;
- boastful;
- proud of sealing/defence;
- later Gaara relationship can become cooperative.

This matters for representation flavour and relationship presentation, but does not create a numeric friendship multiplier.

## 3.4 Do-not-infer boundary

Do not automatically infer:
- every Gaara sand technique;
- generic sleep mechanics for every host;
- automatic Magnet Release access for a host merely because Shukaku has it;
- full physical immunity because the body is sand;
- manga-proven personal Shukaku Tailed Beast Ball usage.

# 4. MATATABI / TWO-TAILS

## 4.1 Secure identity anchors

- canonical identity: **Matatabi = Two-Tails**;
- bakeneko / giant cat-like Tailed Beast;
- body is visually composed/engulfed in **blue flames**;
- known nature transformation: **Fire Release**.

## 4.2 Secure combat anchors

### Fire Release

**Cat Flame Roaring Fire** is manga-supported from chapter 313.

Safe design foundation:
- direct Fire Release projectile/blast;
- blue-flame presentation.

Important boundary:
- blue colour does not automatically prove exotic heat, soul damage, special chakra burn, healing fire or Amaterasu-like properties.

### Feline physical combat

**Great Cat Claw Attack** is manga-supported from chapter 313.

Matatabi is also described as highly agile/reflexive despite its size.

Safe design foundation:
- claw attacks;
- pounce/swipe pressure;
- mobility/agility identity.

### Tailed Beast Ball

Matatabi is a normal Tailed Beast Ball user under family canon.

Exact SC access still requires later mechanical authoring.

## 4.3 Personality / cooperation anchor

Matatabi is notably formal, respectful and polite in its introduction to Naruto.

This supports a distinct cooperative presentation from more aggressive Tailed Beasts, but again does not author a numeric relationship multiplier.

## 4.4 Do-not-infer boundary

Do not automatically infer:
- blue-flame damage-over-time;
- unquenchable flame;
- Fire immunity;
- healing flames;
- stealth merely because Matatabi is feline;
- automatic host Fire Release if exact relationship authority does not grant it.

# 5. ISOBU / THREE-TAILS

## 5.1 Secure identity anchors

- canonical identity: **Isobu = Three-Tails**;
- turtle/crustacean-like armoured Tailed Beast;
- known nature transformation: **Water Release**;
- unique trait: **coral production**;
- exceptionally hard shell/body armour.

## 5.2 Secure combat anchors

### Rolling shell / body-charge combat

Manga chapter 571 supports Isobu/Yagura curling into a spiked ball and attacking/moving through the battlefield.

Secondary technique names:
- **Shadow Stroke**
- **Leaping Bullet Shell Tower**

Safe design foundation:
- armoured rolling charge;
- mobility through body-roll;
- collision/impact pressure;
- shell-based defence.

### Coral

**Coral Palm** is manga-supported in chapter 566.

The struck target develops coral from the impact point.

Safe design foundation:
- coral-based restraint/control/debilitation;
- contact-driven coral growth.

Do not decide exact restraint duration/damage until the mechanics pass.

### Water Release

Water affinity is canonical.

Safe design foundation:
- Water Release can legitimately appear in Isobu's source identity.

### Shell / defence

The hard shell is a central canonical physical trait.

Safe design foundation:
- mitigation/guard/armour mechanics are strongly supported.

## 5.3 Personality / cooperation anchor

Isobu is presented as comparatively timid/soft-spoken. Project authority separately gives Isobu the explicit wild/pre-cooperation vs high-relationship representation distinction.

This makes Isobu's SC relationship state especially important, but the exact relationship-history threshold is project authority, not canon-derived generic law.

## 5.4 Anime-only / lower-authority material — DO NOT silently baseline

Anime expands Isobu with:
- illusionary/hallucinogenic mist;
- large water blasts;
- miniature internal clones;
- space-time/dimensional hiding;
- an eye weakness.

These are **not safe manga-baseline mechanics** for SC unless Stephen deliberately chooses them as an explicit adaptation.

## 5.5 Do-not-infer boundary

Do not automatically infer:
- dimension travel;
- hallucination mist;
- mini-clone swarms;
- explicit eye weak-point system;
- generic Water immunity;
- coral on every attack.

# 6. CHŌMEI / SEVEN-TAILS

## 6.1 Secure identity anchors

- canonical identity: **Chōmei = Seven-Tails**;
- insect / rhinoceros-beetle-like Tailed Beast;
- six of the seven tail structures function as wings in the mature form;
- capable of flight;
- strong horn/body-charge physical identity;
- current SC Registry authority has **one accepted exact Chōmei representation**, not a separate numbered wild row.

## 6.2 Secure combat anchors

### Flight

Flight is a safe core identity.

Safe design foundation:
- aerial mobility;
- reposition/access advantages where the Battle system actually models them;
- airborne physical attacks.

Important boundary:
- **flight does not prove Wind Release**.

No elemental nature should be assigned to Chōmei merely because it flies.

### Horn attack

**Spear Attack Shining Horn** is manga-supported in chapter 658.

Safe design foundation:
- direct horn charge;
- high-impact physical attack.

### Insect physical combat

**Bug Bite** is manga-supported in chapter 566 through Fū's transformed state and is associated with Chōmei.

Safe design foundation:
- biting/insect physical pressure may be used cautiously as a Chōmei-family body mechanic.

## 6.3 Scale Powder — IMPORTANT SOURCE BOUNDARY

Fū's **Scale Powder / Hiding in Scale Powder** is manga-supported in chapter 564.

However:

- direct use by **Chōmei itself** is not cleanly confirmed by the manga evidence reviewed;
- Narutopedia marks Chōmei as a direct Scale Powder user in games, with anime/game expansions;
- therefore **Scale Powder must NOT be silently treated as a baseline Chōmei-own Skill**.

Possible later SC choices:
1. keep Scale Powder host/Fū-linked only;
2. deliberately adapt it into Chōmei's package and record that as an explicit SC divergence;
3. avoid it entirely.

That decision belongs to the mechanics/sign-off phase, not this research document.

## 6.4 Cocoon — anime-only

Chōmei's Cocoon / chakra-absorption-slowing material is anime-only.

Do not baseline it without explicit SC divergence.

## 6.5 Personality / cooperation anchor

Chōmei is upbeat/happy-go-lucky and repeatedly uses the "lucky" motif.

Useful for presentation/voice and relationship flavour; not a numeric mechanic by itself.

## 6.6 Do-not-infer boundary

Do not automatically infer:
- Wind Release;
- scale-powder blindness as Chōmei's own manga-proven technique;
- combustible powder;
- Cocoon;
- generic insect poison;
- automatic evasion merely because it flies.
