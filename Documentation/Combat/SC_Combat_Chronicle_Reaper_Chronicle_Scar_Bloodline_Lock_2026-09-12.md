## 3. Core Chronicle Scar concept

Chronicle Scar is a history-responsive Bloodline.

Its defining rule is:

> **Major legitimate committed life events may leave persistent chakra scars that later Chronicle Scar techniques, passives or compatibility checks can reference.**

Examples of potentially qualifying event families may include severe sealing, possession/host interaction, catastrophic Battle survival, betrayal, imprisonment, near-death, major loss, transformation exposure, or other sufficiently significant authored Chronicle events.

These examples are categories only. They do **not** automatically create a scar merely because an event belongs to the category.

A Chronicle Scar expression must be backed by exact committed provenance/history and explicit compatibility authority.

## 4. No generic power-from-trauma shortcut

Chronicle Scar must not become:

`bad thing happened -> free permanent power`

Instead preserve:

`committed qualifying history -> potential scar fact -> legitimate development/access -> exact authored expression`

The Bloodline supplies the mechanism for history-responsive development. Progression still owns persistent development/access gates. Combat owns exact Skill/passive/effect semantics. World/Story owns the events that actually happen. Registry owns persistent identity.

No direct or hidden Base PL bonus is implied by Bloodline possession alone.

## 5. Divergent Reaper development

Chronicle Scar is deliberately suitable for Chronicle divergence.

Two valid Chronicle Reaper histories may develop different scar records and therefore different later compatible expressions, even when both begin from the same Bloodline.

This does not mean UI refresh, random generation or arbitrary authoring may reroll scars. Divergence must come from materially different committed history.

Preserve:

- Bloodline possession != every scar expression;
- event occurrence != automatic scar;
- scar fact != Skill Access;
- Skill Access != Competence != Power != Mastery;
- different history may legitimately produce different later Bloodline expressions;
- same history must not reroll merely because presentation is refreshed.
