# 2. Shared Kurama canon baseline

## 2.1 Nine-Tails and Kurama are one canonical being

Canon does **not** contain one persistent creature called "Nine-Tails" and a second unrelated creature called "Kurama".

Safe baseline:

> **Nine-Tails = title/numbered identity of Kurama. Kurama = personal/true name of the same canonical Tailed Beast.**

Therefore:

- SC may legitimately author different exact representations called `nine_tails` and `kurama_complete`;
- but the mechanical distinction must come from explicit SC historical/relationship/body-state authority;
- filename/title difference alone is insufficient.

## 2.2 Core source capabilities

Safe Kurama-family canon anchors include:

- extraordinary Tailed-Beast chakra reserve;
- Tailed Beast Ball;
- chakra transfer / lending;
- powerful physical force;
- large-scale destructive roar / chakra pressure;
- sensory capability;
- **Negative Emotions Sensing**;
- Tailed Beast Telepathy;
- host/relationship-assisted chakra expression;
- partial/full Tailed Beast manifestations where the exact host relationship permits them.

These are foundations, not automatic buttons for every SC representation.

## 2.3 Elemental nature caution

Anime sources give Kurama Fire/Wind nature use.

The manga-baseline identity does **not** require us to make Fire Release or Wind Release the defining Kurama enhancement.

Do not baseline an elemental bonus from anime-only nature material unless SC deliberately adopts it.

## 2.4 Baryon Mode boundary

Baryon Mode is a special Naruto + Kurama terminal interaction, not a generic "Kurama own Skill" to attach to every Kurama representation.

Do not infer:

- every Kurama card has Baryon Mode;
- every host can access it;
- Complete Kurama automatically exposes it;
- Reborn Kurama automatically retains a reusable Baryon action.

Any Baryon gameplay requires its own exact host/source/history contract.

# 3. Yin Kurama / Yang Kurama

## 3.1 Canon origin

Minato split Kurama's chakra into two halves during Naruto's birth:

- **Yin Kurama** — sealed with Minato;
- **Yang Kurama** — sealed into Naruto.

The two halves remain partitions of the **same Kurama**, not two unrelated foxes.

## 3.2 Canon behaviour

Manga chapter 643 establishes:

- Yin and Yang halves can directly recognise and communicate with each other;
- they regard each other as halves of the same self/source;
- they can share chakra/cooperate;
- each retains Kurama's personhood rather than becoming a mindless energy quantity.

## 3.3 Appearance

Secondary manga/anime indexing records:

- Yang retains Kurama's familiar/original colour;
- Yin is represented darker.

This visual distinction does not itself define different power schools.

## 3.4 Hard design boundary

Do **NOT** invent by default:

- Yin = Genjutsu / defence / "dark";
- Yang = Ninjutsu / offence / "light";
- Yin = evil;
- Yang = good;
- one half as "healing" and the other as "damage";
- arbitrary opposite-element packages.

The terms Yin/Yang describe the canon partition event. They are not permission to create RPG polarity classes.

If SC wants different Enhancements, those differences need a real source/representation rationale.

# 4. Complete Kurama

## 4.1 Canon state

After the Fourth Shinobi World War, Kurama's Yin and Yang halves are reunited.

Chapter-700 material depicts Kurama whole again.

Safe baseline:

> **Complete Kurama = reunited mature Kurama after the Yin/Yang partition is resolved.**

## 4.2 Mechanical consequence boundary

Complete Kurama must **not** be represented as:

`Yang package + Yin package + Complete package`

unless one exact downstream contract deliberately authors that combination and prevents double-counting.

The safe model is:

> one exact complete-source package representing the reunited whole.

## 4.3 Power ceiling caution

Canon supports Complete Kurama as the whole beast and therefore at a greater total chakra state than either half alone.

SC still owns:

- exact Stats;
- exact Base PL;
- exact Enhancement;
- exact actions;
- action economy;
- host compatibility.

Do not derive those by simply doubling a half.
