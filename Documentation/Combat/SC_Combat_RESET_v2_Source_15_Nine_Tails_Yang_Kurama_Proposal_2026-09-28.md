# A. Batch-4 representation law

Kurama-family design must keep separate:

1. persistent Kurama identity;
2. exact relationship state;
3. Yin/Yang partition state;
4. complete/reunited body state;
5. host/history-specific representation;
6. Hosted / Forced Manifestation lifecycle.

For this Source 15 pair:

## `nine_tails`

Naruto-associated **pre-genuine-partnership relationship-state** projection of the Yang half.

It may be:
- hostile;
- manipulative;
- transactional;
- self-interested;
- temporarily cooperative;
- cordial.

None of those alone equals partnership.

## `yang_kurama`

Naruto-associated **genuine voluntary partnership** projection of the same Yang partition.

This is not:
- a different fox;
- an alignment class;
- "good Kurama";
- automatically Complete Kurama.

Preserve:

> **same Yang half + changed relationship state = different exact representation**

# C. Kurama-family common capability layer

Stephen's global rule applies:

> **family-common capability != signature identity budget**

Kurama-family representations may legitimately share foundational capabilities where exact lifecycle/source authority permits them.

Examples:
- Tailed Beast Bomb;
- fox-body claw/tail attacks;
- chakra roar/pressure;
- telepathic communication where a communication route exists;
- chakra lending/transfer as a source capability;
- Negative Emotions Sensing as Kurama's sensory capability.

These do NOT automatically become:
- host Skills;
- learned Skills;
- partnership evidence;
- second Entity turns.

## Tailed Beast Bomb

Tailed Beast Bomb belongs to the common Bijū/Kurama capability surface where legitimate.

It should not consume the main creative signature slot of every exact Kurama representation.

Exact common-layer numeric normalisation may be reconciled across the full Batch-4 family after all exact representations are authored.

# D. Automatic contextual Kurama capabilities

Global inherent-context law applies.

## Kurama sensing

Kurama's own Negative Emotions Sensing is a source capability.

However:

- Kurama sensing something != host automatically knowing it;
- in `nine_tails` state, Kurama may withhold, distort, bargain over or simply not volunteer the information according to actual authored relationship/context;
- in genuine cooperative `yang_kurama` state, a legitimate current communication route may automatically expose bounded source-supported warnings/negative-emotion evidence to the host where Story/World supplies the factual context.

No separate "learn how Kurama senses negative emotions" Skill is required merely for Kurama to possess the sense.

Specialised host interpretation/training may still become learnable development later.

# F. Nine-Tails manifested signatures

## `nine_tails_tail_cage_isolation` — **Nine-Tail Isolation**

Class:

**ATTACK / TAIL CONTROL / FORMATION ISOLATION**

Limit:

**once per Battle**

Target:

enemy Active

ATK:

**34**

Player text:

> **Close all nine tails around the enemy Active for 34 ATK. If it deals damage, their Benched allies cannot use support or reaction actions that target that Active until the end of the Active's next action.**

Effect:
- one direct packet;
- ordinary Stamina mitigation;
- positive final damage applies `nine_tails_isolated_active`;
- while active, enemy Benched participants cannot target that Active with support/reaction actions;
- does NOT stop the Active from acting;
- does NOT block self-targeting;
- does NOT stop enemy Benched actions whose target is someone else;
- does NOT affect Reserve unless another rule separately says so;
- exact action that does not require access to the isolated Active remains legal;
- not Stun.

Decision:

> **Use the tails to cut the current fighter off from their formation, not merely to deal more damage.**

---

## `nine_tails_source_refusal` — **Caged Source Refusal**

Class:

**REACTION / SOURCE SELF-PRESERVATION**

Limit:

**once per Battle**

Trigger:

a hostile action attempts one compatible:
- source extraction;
- hosted-source suppression;
- forced transfer;
- hostile control of the exact Kurama source.

Player text:

> **Nine-Tails refuses another hand on its chakra. Cancel one ordinary hostile attempt to suppress, extract or force-control the Kurama source.**

Effect:
- cancel the compatible source-targeting rider/effect;
- any independently valid direct damage packet from the triggering action still resolves normally unless its whole effect requires the source-control success;
- cannot reject a superior exact authority explicitly authored to overpower this refusal;
- does not block voluntary separation;
- does not prove partnership;
- no bonus action.

This is self-interest:

> **Kurama can refuse an enemy without agreeing to obey the host.**

# G. Nine-Tails Enhancement — Caged Fox Intervention

ID:

`nine_tails_caged_fox_intervention`

Activation:

**HOSTED / PRE-PARTNERSHIP YANG KURAMA STATE**

No generic Stat bonus.

One shared use per Battle.

The player may spend that one use on **either** branch below.

## Branch A — Preserve the Container

Trigger:

one direct hostile Attack-PL packet would reduce the host to 0 Remaining Battle PL.

Effect:
- before withdrawal commits, Kurama forces enough chakra through the host to leave them at exactly **1 Remaining Battle PL**;
- the triggering packet is otherwise considered resolved;
- no restoration above 1;
- no Injury erasure;
- no automatic extra action;
- no permanent power unlock.

This is not friendship.

Kurama's own survival/freedom interest is sufficient reason to preserve the container.

## Branch B — Refuse the Hand

Trigger:

one compatible hostile effect attempts to:
- suppress the Kurama source;
- extract it;
- force-transfer it;
- seize/control the source route.

Effect:
- cancel that ordinary source-targeting effect;
- independent damage remains normal where separable.

One Branch A or Branch B use consumes the Enhancement for the Battle.

Decision:

> **Spend Kurama's self-interested intervention to keep the host standing, or save it in case somebody attacks the source relationship itself.**

This is deliberately different from cooperative Yang Kurama.

These require exact authorised `nine_tails` hosted access.

## 1. `nine_tails_bargained_chakra_flood` — **Bargained Chakra Flood**

Classification:

**SOURCE-ASSISTED ATTACK / PRE-PARTNERSHIP LOAN / FORMATION PRESSURE**

Limit:

**once per Battle**

Targets:
- enemy Active;
- one occupied enemy Benched participant.

ATK:
- Active **44**
- Benched **30**

Player text:

> **Accept a violent Nine-Tails chakra loan. Hit the enemy Active for 44 ATK and one Benched enemy for 30 ATK. Kurama then withholds further assisted attacks through your next action.**

Effect:
- one packet per target;
- ordinary Stamina mitigation independently;
- if no Benched target exists, Active packet still resolves;
- after resolution establish `nine_tails_withholding`;
- through the host's next action opportunity, the host cannot choose another `nine_tails`-assisted ATTACK;
- ordinary host Skills remain legal;
- defensive/source-refusal effects remain legal where separately authorised;
- no Reserve targeting;
- no second turn.

The tradeoff is not "uncooperative = weaker":

> **Nine-Tails gives a nastier burst now, then stops feeding offensive chakra for one action.**

# I. Yang Kurama manifested signatures

## `yang_kurama_comrade_chakra_transfer` — **Comrade Chakra Transfer**

Class:

**SUPPORT / VOLUNTARY CHAKRA SHARE**

Limit:

**once per Battle**

Target:

one allied participant

Player text:

> **Kurama voluntarily gives part of his current Battle strength to an ally. Transfer up to 20 Remaining Battle PL from Yang Kurama to that ally.**

Effect:
- choose transfer amount from **1–20**;
- Yang Kurama loses exactly that much Remaining Battle PL;
- target restores the same amount of underlying Remaining Battle PL, capped at target's authoritative Battle maximum;
- invalid if transfer would reduce Yang Kurama below 1;
- invalid if target is already at full underlying Battle PL;
- no new total Battle PL is created beyond cap loss;
- does not heal Injury;
- no Stat rewrite.

This expresses cooperative chakra sharing as **redistribution**, not a generic heal button.

---

## `yang_kurama_comrade_mantle` — **Comrade Mantle**

Class:

**SUPPORT / ALLY SOURCE STATE**

Limit:

**once per Battle**

Target:

one allied Active or Benched participant other than Yang Kurama.

Player text:

> **Wrap one ally in Kurama's chakra. Until that ally finishes their next action, the first direct hostile packet against them is reduced by 35%.**

Effect:
- establish `yang_kurama_comrade_mantle`;
- first qualifying direct hostile Attack-PL packet is reduced **35% pre-Stamina**;
- ordinary Stamina then resolves;
- mantle ends after protecting one packet or after target completes their next action;
- does not change Stats;
- no second health bar;
- no generic aura.

The support is willingly given to another person; it is not a hidden friendship multiplier.

# J. Yang Kurama Enhancement — Comrade Chakra Network

ID:

`yang_kurama_comrade_chakra_network`

Activation:

**HOSTED / GENUINE PARTNERSHIP / YANG PARTITION**

No generic Stat bonus.

Limit:

**once per Battle**

Use:

host spends their normal action opportunity.

Target:

one allied Active or Benched participant.

Player text:

> **Route Kurama's chakra to one ally. Give up to 18 of your Remaining Battle PL to restore the same amount to them.**

Effect:
- host chooses transfer amount **1–18**;
- host loses that much underlying Remaining Battle PL;
- ally restores same amount, capped at their authoritative Battle maximum;
- host cannot reduce self below 1;
- full-PL target is invalid precommit;
- transfer does not heal Injury or alter Stats;
- source provenance records Yang Kurama as voluntary chakra source;
- no Kurama second turn.

This creates a real cooperative decision:

> **How much of your own current fighting capacity are you willing to give to keep a comrade in the Battle?**
