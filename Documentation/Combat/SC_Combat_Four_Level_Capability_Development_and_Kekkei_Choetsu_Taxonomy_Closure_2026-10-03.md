# PART I — THE FOUR-LEVEL DEVELOPMENT LADDER

## 3. Exact four-level ladder — LOCKED

The universal SC four-level advancement surface is the:

# **Capability Expression Grade**

It describes **how far an exact capability/source/discipline has been developed in this Chronicle**.

It does not say what the capability is.

The four exact grades are:

1. **Grade I — Initial Expression**
2. **Grade II — Developed Expression**
3. **Grade III — Advanced Expression**
4. **Grade IV — Apex Expression**

Player-facing shorthand may use Expression I / II / III / IV where space is limited.

### 3.1 Grade I — Initial Expression

The Character can produce a reliable bounded expression of the exact capability.

Typical qualities may include local/self/single-target expression, one clear technique family or contextual verb, basic safe activation/control, narrow setup/environmental dependency and limited interaction breadth.

Grade I is not the same as possession, awakening, first sight of the capability or merely knowing a technique exists.

### 3.2 Grade II — Developed Expression

The Character has integrated the capability into a broader usable repertoire.

Typical qualities may include more than one exact application, better control or efficiency, multi-step/multi-target use where authored, interaction with another developed discipline, more reliable contextual use and deliberate adaptation rather than accidental manifestation.

### 3.3 Grade III — Advanced Expression

The capability can produce qualitatively more complex or persistent effects.

Typical qualities may include compound interactions, persistent/bounded state manipulation, demanding multi-system integration, advanced technique branches, rule-changing verbs that remain exact/counterable and significant setup/cost/risk/provenance requirements.

### 3.4 Grade IV — Apex Expression

The Character has reached the highest currently defined development grade for that exact capability family.

Typical qualities may include exceptional local-rule manipulation, advanced topology/creation/restoration/transformation effects where legitimate, highly integrated multi-system expression and Character-defining applications with severe provenance, Access, cost and PvP constraints.

**Apex does not mean unbeatable.**

One Grade IV capability is not universally stronger than another Character's Grade II or Grade III capability. Grade is relative to the exact capability family and authored route.

## 4. The ladder is not Bloodline taxonomy

Expression Grade may be used for ordinary Nature development, Bloodline/Kekkei Genkai capability, Kekkei Tōta, Kekkei Chōetsu, exact dōjutsu development, Space-Time Ninjutsu, Yin Release, Yang Release, Yin–Yang Release, Fūinjutsu capability families, Clan Arts, body/chakra adaptations, exact transformations and other capability families.

Therefore:

> **Grade I–IV is SC development grammar, not canon taxonomy.**

Do not relabel a Grade III Space-Time user as Kekkei Tōta.

Do not relabel a Grade IV ordinary Fire user as a Bloodline holder.

Do not relabel a Grade IV Byakugan user as Kekkei Chōetsu merely because development is exceptional.

## 5. Possession/source truth sits outside the four grades

A capability can factually exist before executable Battle access or before the current Chronicle has developed it far.

Examples:

- Hinata may factually possess Byakugan lineage/capability while Battle benefits remain locked.
- An Uchiha may have lineage/potential before Sharingan awakening.
- A Hosted Entity may already be attached while communication/transformation/Battle access is locked.
- A Character may have an acquired/developed capability source but possess only Grade I expression so far.

Possession itself is not Grade 0.

# PART II — TAXONOMY

## 6. Kekkei Genkai — taxonomy, not Level 1

**Kekkei Genkai** remains a capability/source taxonomy.

It is not automatically Expression Grade I.

It is not a mandatory prerequisite for Kekkei Tōta or Kekkei Chōetsu.

For elemental cases, an exact integrated two-nature capability may be classified as Kekkei Genkai where canon/SC authority establishes that source.

For non-elemental cases, canon/SC may classify exact hereditary or body/chakra capabilities as Kekkei Genkai without any element-count formula.

SC may establish a Kekkei Genkai source through inherited/lineage truth, legitimate transplant/acquisition, induced biological/chakra alteration or extraordinary earned developmental transformation where exact authority closes that route.

Provenance must remain visible.

**Acquired/developed Kekkei Genkai != retroactive ancestral inheritance.**

## 7. Kekkei Tōta — taxonomy, not a development grade

**Kekkei Tōta** remains the retained advanced nature-transformation taxonomy.

Current canon anchor:

- Dust Release;
- integrated Earth + Wind + Fire.

SC-original Kekkei Tōta may exist.

The locked SC taxonomy test is:

> **an exact capability source produces one integrated advanced nature transformation from three basic chakra natures.**

Important:

- three developed natures do not automatically create Kekkei Tōta;
- a user does not need a pre-existing Kekkei Genkai as a universal SC prerequisite;
- a Character may engineer a three-Nature technique without possessing a Kekkei Tōta source;
- extraordinary development may eventually establish a persistent three-Nature integrated source, at which point exact authority may classify the new source as Kekkei Tōta.

Therefore:

> **Kekkei Genkai -> Kekkei Tōta is NOT a mandatory evolutionary staircase.**

A Tōta holder may have inherited the source, acquired/transmitted it through an exact authorised route, or reached it through an extraordinary source-creation transformation where SC authority explicitly closes that history.

## 8. Kekkei Chōetsu — exact SC role — LOCKED

**Kekkei Chōetsu** is now an official **Shinobi Chronicles-original capability/source taxonomy**.

It is not Naruto/Boruto canon terminology.

It is not merely a stronger Kekkei Tōta.

It is not four elements.

It is not Expression Grade IV.

It is not automatically inherited.

### 8.1 Definition

A **Kekkei Chōetsu** exists when an exact Chronicle establishes:

> **a persistent intrinsic transformation of the Character's body and/or chakra system that creates a new capability source with rules the Character could not obtain merely by executing, combining or mastering ordinary techniques.**

The new source becomes part of the Character's persistent capability truth.

It can then support contextual capability, Battle packages, exact Skills, further Expression Grade development and Character-specific variants.

### 8.2 Provenance may differ

A Kekkei Chōetsu may be:

- **Inherited** — lineage truth already carries the source;
- **Acquired / Induced** — a legitimate external, biological or chakra process establishes the source;
- **Developed / Earned** — extraordinary persistent development causes the Character's own body/chakra system to cross into a new stable source state.

The exact provenance must be recorded.

A Developed Chōetsu must never be presented as ancestral inheritance merely because the final capability is intrinsic.

### 8.3 Chōetsu source-creation test

Before an exact capability may be called Kekkei Chōetsu, all of the following must be true:

1. **Persistent source truth** — the change survives beyond one technique/Battle/temporary transformation unless a later exact event removes or alters it.
2. **Intrinsic body/chakra change** — the Character's own body/chakra system now contains the capability source.
3. **New rule-space** — the source enables at least one exact verb/rule that ordinary technique execution alone did not grant.
4. **Causal provenance** — inherited, acquired, induced or earned developmental origin is explicit.
5. **No simple Stat threshold** — high Stats/Nature numbers alone cannot flip classification.
6. **No element-count shortcut** — 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 natures alone do not determine Chōetsu.
7. **Exact Access remains separate** — source truth does not automatically grant every contextual/Battle/Skill expression.
8. **Counterplay exists** — Combat production still passes the full PvP/whole-build gate.

### 8.4 Chōetsu can be elemental or non-elemental

Kekkei Chōetsu is not restricted to elemental composites.

An exact non-elemental advanced family may qualify only if it passes the same persistent intrinsic source-creation test.

Possible design spaces include extraordinary Space-Time body/chakra adaptation, Yin/Yang intrinsic transformation, perception/body topology change or another SC-original capability source.

But:

- high Space-Time mastery alone != Chōetsu;
- high Yin/Yang mastery alone != Chōetsu;
- a powerful dōjutsu state != Chōetsu automatically;
- a Hosted Entity != Chōetsu;
- a Transformation != Chōetsu merely because it is strong.

## 9. There is no universal tier above Chōetsu

SC does not currently define a universal Chōetsu II, Chōetsu+, fourth-element tier or higher universal taxonomy.

The project's four-level structure is the **Expression Grade** ladder, not a Genkai -> Tōta -> Chōetsu -> ??? ladder.

Existing canon categories remain separate, including Kekkei Mōra, advanced dōjutsu states, shinjutsu-linked capabilities, Ōtsutsuki-specific capability, Clan Arts, Curse/Seal states, Hosted Entities and transformations.

**Kekkei Mōra is not SC Expression Grade IV.**

**Kekkei Chōetsu does not automatically outrank every Kekkei Mōra, dōjutsu, Hosted Entity or ordinary technique user.**

Exact matchup and capability authority controls actual power.

# PART III — ENGINEERED TECHNIQUES VS SOURCE POSSESSION

## 10. Technique engineering lane — LOCKED

A Character may legitimately create techniques that **look or act like** advanced Bloodline/Tōta/Chōetsu phenomena without possessing the corresponding source.

This is an important SC development lane.

Examples include combining two Nature techniques into one authored hybrid effect, synchronising three Nature transformations through extreme control, producing a temporary material/field/result that resembles an advanced release, or combining elemental and Bukijutsu/Fūinjutsu/Space-Time capability into a higher-order technique.

These remain **engineered techniques** unless/until an exact source-creation event establishes a persistent intrinsic capability.

Engineered techniques may be rare, legendary and extremely strong.

They still do not fabricate source possession.

### 10.1 No retroactive rewrite

If a Character later develops a genuine intrinsic source:

- prior engineered techniques remain historically engineered techniques;
- they do not become retrospectively inherited Bloodline use;
- later versions may legitimately become source-assisted techniques where Progression/Combat closes that development.

History remains history.

## 11. Ordinary Nature Development is prerequisite/evidence, not a possession generator

Nature Development may legitimately gate advanced ordinary elemental Skills, multi-Nature engineering, training efficiency, technique stability, resistance/tolerance, eligibility for an extraordinary source-creation route and later source-specific Skills after a source exists.

It may not directly assert:

- high Fire + Wind = Scorch Release;
- high Water + Wind = Ice Release;
- high Earth + Wind + Fire = Dust Release;
- high four Natures = Kekkei Chōetsu.

Correct causal shape:

ordinary Nature development  
+ exact Chronicle evidence  
+ exact source-creation opportunity  
+ exact successful transition  
-> persistent capability source established

Not:

numbers crossed threshold  
-> Bloodline silently appears

# PART V — SKILL ELIGIBILITY

## 13. Exact Skill eligibility grammar — LOCKED

A Skill may consume only the predicates it genuinely needs.

Possible requirements include:

1. exact capability/source;
2. awakening/manifestation;
3. Expression Grade;
4. Nature/discipline development;
5. Knowledge / curriculum / trainer / discovery;
6. exact Skill-learning occurrence;
7. Competence / Power / Mastery where exact authority defines them;
8. current contextual or Battle Access;
9. target / environment / geometry / source state;
10. cost / availability / once-per-Battle / action economy.

Canonical shape:

source truth  
+ required awakening/state  
+ required development evidence  
+ Knowledge/training eligibility  
+ exact learning occurrence  
-> Skill Access

Skill Access  
+ current Battle/context legality  
-> executable use

Not every Skill requires a Bloodline source.

## 14. Engineered composite Skill eligibility

An engineered composite technique requires the actual disciplines/components it uses.

Example:

Fire Development sufficient  
+ Wind Development sufficient  
+ exact hybrid-technique Knowledge  
+ exact learning/invention occurrence  
-> engineered Fire/Wind Skill Access

It does not require or grant Scorch Release unless that exact Skill is separately authored as Scorch-source-exclusive.

## 15. Source-exclusive Skill eligibility

A true Bloodline/Tōta/Chōetsu-exclusive Skill requires the exact source.

Example:

exact source present  
+ current Access  
+ required Expression Grade  
+ exact Skill learned  
+ current legality  
-> executable source-exclusive Skill

A visually similar engineered technique does not satisfy the source predicate.

Yin, Yang and Yin–Yang remain their own capability/nature-transformation spaces outside the five basic elements.

# PART VIII — PvP / WHOLE-BUILD GATE

## 20. No Chōetsu exemption

Every exact Bloodline/Tōta/Chōetsu package must include a **PvP Counterplay Surface** before Combat design closure.

Minimum required:

- primary threat;
- readable tells;
- setup/dependencies;
- ordinary-build counterplay;
- specialist counterplay;
- interruption/denial routes where appropriate;
- punish/failure windows;
- resource/action opportunity cost;
- team-PvP interaction;
- anti-non-game safeguards.

A Chōetsu may be rare, dangerous and powerful.

It still cannot mean:

> own the exact counter or lose.

No source taxonomy grants deterministic victory.

## 21. Whole-build diversity gate

A Chōetsu or advanced Bloodline must not become a universal hereditary tax.

The package should create distinctive verbs, matchup pressure, build identity, opportunity cost and alternative strategic routes.

A no-Chōetsu Character must remain capable of legendary endgame viability through other legitimate development paths.

For Kakashi:

> **no-Sharingan Kakashi remains a complete legendary route.**

Likewise:

> **no-Chōetsu route becomes mandatory merely because Chōetsu exists.**
