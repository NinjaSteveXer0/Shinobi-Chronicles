# 2. Character / Story foundation

Mirai's Academy Origin already establishes that she:
- takes responsibility seriously;
- observes carefully;
- compares what she knows against what is currently happening;
- can execute the apparent mission correctly while the deeper reality is more complicated;
- must distinguish World Truth from what she merely perceives or assumes.

Do **not** repeat the Origin through:
- another escort substitution;
- an object replacement;
- a forged weapon;
- a disguised participant;
- a smaller remix of identity verification.

The signature route must begin from a new Main-Story / hotspot problem.

# 3. First impossible occurrence

A future Main Story / hotspot occurrence exposes a genuine **Time-Space anomaly**.

Examples may include:
- a spatial fracture;
- impossible interior/exterior geometry;
- a route that exits somewhere it should not;
- an attack/object crossing an impossible spatial seam;
- another exact authored space-time disturbance.

Mirai responds using ordinary capability available to her at that point.

During the occurrence she creates an ordinary spatial Genjutsu:
- false position;
- false exit;
- false route;
- false apparent attack origin;
- another exact perceptual spatial deception.

Because the real Time-Space environment is already unstable, something impossible happens:

> **the false spatial location briefly becomes usable as a real coordinate.**

A real actor, object or attack crosses through a position that existed only as Mirai's authored illusion.

That occurrence is the route seed.

Mirai does not instantly understand it.

She investigates and develops it through later Chronicle history.

---

# 4. Core SC invention

The route eventually proves:

> **A sufficiently coherent false spatial model can become a temporary Time-Space coordinate when Mirai deliberately stabilises and commits it through Time-Space Ninjutsu.**

Preserve the causal split:

**Genjutsu**
- authors the false / possible spatial model.

**Time-Space Ninjutsu**
- makes one eligible spatial possibility physically actionable.

Genjutsu alone does not rewrite World Truth.

Belief alone does not create space.

Time-Space Ninjutsu is the physical causal mechanism.

---

# 5. Relationship to “Mirai / Future”

Mirai does **not** gain precognition.

She does **not**:
- see the future;
- know future World Truth;
- travel backwards in time;
- rewrite committed history.

The thematic use of "future" is:

> **Mirai authors several plausible next spatial states, then commits one of them as the next real spatial outcome.**

She does not predict what happens next.

She makes one deliberately prepared spatial possibility become real.

# 6. Battle identity

Mirai's mature route may support original Skills in several distinct families.

## Time-Space Genjutsu

Examples of the underlying grammar:
- present several false Mirai positions;
- one eligible false position can become Mirai's real position when the Time-Space commitment resolves;
- present several apparent attack exits;
- one eligible exit can become the real spatial aperture;
- create false distance / false approach information around a real fold;
- make an opponent solve which spatial possibility is illusion and which one Mirai can actually materialise.

Counterplay matters:
- Genjutsu resistance;
- sensory capability;
- correct inference;
- disruption before Time-Space commitment;
- spatial restrictions;
- invalid/unsafe endpoints.

## Time-Space Bukijutsu

Bukijutsu may become a substantial route branch if Story / Combat development proves it.

Possible grammar:
- blade motion defines/cuts a spatial line;
- chakra weapon opens or stabilises a short aperture;
- a weapon attack travels through an authored spatial fold;
- a thrown weapon defines one side of an exact temporary geometry;
- physical weapon control determines which spatial line is legal.

Hard rule:

> Bukijutsu is an earned route expression, not an automatic requirement merely because Mirai canonically uses chakra blades.

## Time-Space Ninjutsu

Mirai may also develop pure spatial techniques that no longer require a visible Genjutsu layer every time.

Those techniques must still arise from the route's original false-coordinate discovery rather than becoming a generic teleport curriculum.

Possible later capability families:
- bounded short folds;
- temporary spatial apertures;
- displacement/repositioning;
- hazard bypass;
- attack redirection;
- rescue movement.

Exact Skills/numerics belong to Combat / Skills.
