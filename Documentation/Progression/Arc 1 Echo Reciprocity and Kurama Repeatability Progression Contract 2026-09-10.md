The receipt may commit only when the current Chronicle factually reaches the authored reciprocal-cooperation transition: the Echo is still the exact hosted Entity, its cooperation is voluntary, and Menma and the Echo have demonstrated mutual operational cooperation sufficient for the Story/CE route to establish the reciprocal state.

- one or more valid Mission-12 early reciprocal cooperative action occurrences carrying `arc1_menma_echo` provenance;

The final Progression commit is therefore **not** inferred from `mission12Complete`, Battle victory, Remaining Battle PL, a displayed card, a selected dialogue option, an action count, or the existence of `arc1_menma_echo` alone.

`arc1_m11_echo_recall_refusal` is strong evidence of Echo autonomy/preference but is **not by itself sufficient** to establish reciprocal cooperation with Menma.

Likewise, one early cooperative Combat action is evidence of contextual cooperation but is **not by itself a universal automatic permanent unlock**. The persistent state requires the authored reciprocal relationship transition to have actually occurred.

Conversely, Progression must not fabricate a universal requirement that every legal Chronicle contain the exact same supporting action sequence if Story/CE legitimately establishes reciprocal cooperation through another authored factual route.

> **supporting cooperation evidence ≠ persistent reciprocity until the reciprocal relationship transition is factually established.**
