## 1. Owner direction

Kurenai's signature Chronicle route is built around **invention, not discovery**.

She does not:
- find a lost Genjutsu school;
- inherit forbidden Uchiha knowledge;
- simply raise Genjutsu;
- receive a stronger ordinary illusion.

Instead, Kurenai becomes competitively dissatisfied with the fact that Uchiha ocular Genjutsu analysis/countering represents an enormous advantage in the field she cares about most.

That competition is SC Chronicle development, not a claim that canon Academy Kurenai possessed a formal Uchiha rivalry.

Her route question is:

> **Can Kurenai create a category of Genjutsu that does not depend on the ordinary external caster-target structure the Sharingan is best positioned to read and exploit?**

The legendary payoff is:

> **Kurenai becomes the originator of an SC-original school of Genjutsu that did not previously exist.**

## 2. Core invention

Working technical description:

**self-generated / internally sustained Genjutsu**

Names are NOT locked.

The core architecture changes from:

`caster -> sustained foreign illusion structure -> target perception`

toward:

`Kurenai plants bounded seed / feedback condition -> target's own chakra-perception system sustains the active false experience`

Kurenai supplies the initiating structure.

The target's own chakra/perceptual loop becomes the ongoing engine.

This does NOT mean:
- mind control;
- personality rewrite;
- ownership of the target;
- automatic unbreakable illusion;
- Uchiha immunity is removed;
- Sharingan becomes useless.

It changes the problem an ocular counter-specialist must solve.

## 3. Story spine

Preferred route shape:

1. Kurenai develops as an unusually serious Genjutsu specialist during ordinary Main Story play.
2. A legitimate encounter/spar/mission involving an Uchiha or another elite ocular/genjutsu counter-user demonstrates that a conventional stronger illusion does not solve her problem.
3. Kurenai becomes competitive with the limitation itself.
4. She studies:
   - her own failed Genjutsu;
   - caster/target chakra interaction;
   - resistance/break mechanics;
   - how the target's own perception participates in an illusion.
5. She experiments on herself first.
6. The first major breakthrough causes Kurenai's own chakra/perception to continue generating a false experience after the ordinary external-caster model is absent.
7. This is the route's conceptual break:
   - not madness;
   - not corruption;
   - a new understanding of Genjutsu architecture.
8. She learns to reproduce bounded versions on herself.
9. Then consenting training partners.
10. Then hostile targets under exact Battle legality/resistance.
11. Later she develops a full new technique family/school.

No checklist:
`complete N missions -> school unlocked`.

The school must be invented through actual experiments, failures, observations and committed breakthroughs.

## 4. Uchiha relationship

The route may legitimately intersect:
- Sasuke;
- Itachi;
- other Uchiha;
- Sharingan-bearing Kakashi;
- another ocular counter-user;

where current World Truth permits.

Their role is:
- benchmark;
- rival;
- pressure test;
- collaborator;
- critic;
- opponent;

depending on current Chronicle history.

Hard rule:

> **Kurenai inventing a school meant to challenge ocular superiority != Kurenai automatically defeating Sharingan users.**

Uchiha remain autonomous.

Counterplay remains real.

## 5. Battle identity

The mature school may support techniques where:

- Kurenai plants an exact initiating Genjutsu seed;
- the target's own chakra/perception sustains the active false loop;
- breaking the effect requires solving/disrupting that internal loop rather than only identifying Kurenai's external control;
- exact target history, resistance and technique-specific predicates determine outcome.

Potential later technique families may exploit:
- expectation;
- sensory habit;
- reflexive correction;
- self-generated visual/auditory/tactile error;
- recursive misinterpretation of the target's own chakra signals.

Exact Skill names and effects remain Combat / Skills-owned.

## 6. Kinjutsu boundary

Kinjutsu may become relevant at invasive/high-risk stages.

It is NOT:
- the route reward;
- the source of the knowledge;
- required merely because the route is dangerous.

Possible Kinjutsu-classified later techniques may involve:
- deep self-experimentation;
- dangerous recursive perception loops;
- hostile manipulation of a target's own internal chakra-perception feedback.

Exact classification remains downstream.

## 7. Out-of-Battle value

The route may produce:
- new interrogation/deception possibilities;
- counter-Genjutsu research;
- training tools;
- diagnostic understanding of unusual perception failures;
- investigation of self-sustaining illusion phenomena;
- Uchiha / specialist Story routes;
- Cross-Origin assists where another Character is trapped in or generating a false perception.

Do not turn it into universal memory editing or free truth detection.

## 8. Failure / risk

Meaningful failures may include:
- Kurenai traps herself in an internally sustained loop;
- the target's own mind/chakra generates content Kurenai did not predict;
- a seed mutates into a more dangerous perceptual loop;
- the target recognises the architecture and hijacks it;
- Kurenai cannot terminate an experiment cleanly;
- an Uchiha/counter-specialist proves the new school still has exploitable assumptions.

Failure creates Chronicle history.

## 9. Differentiation

Preserve:

- Iwabee = discovers forbidden Earth-affinity Knowledge.
- Metal = invents Phantom Fist, a Tai/Gen martial fusion.
- Mirai = invents a Time-Space/Genjutsu spatial paradigm.
- Kurenai = **changes the caster-target grammar of Genjutsu itself.**

Her payoff is the founding of a new school, not possession of a secret school.
