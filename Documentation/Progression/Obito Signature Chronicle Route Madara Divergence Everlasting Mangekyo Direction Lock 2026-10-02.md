**Date:** 2026-10-02

## Core route

Academy Obito's signature route is a **hotspot-driven Madara relationship and divergence campaign**.

It is not:
- mandatory canon replay;
- a Sharingan/Mangekyō unlock route;
- a morality meter;
- a single transformation reward;
- a forced Kiri route;
- a forced Madara route.

The route begins only from legitimate discovered history.

## Madara discovery and relationship

A later Main Story / World / hotspot occurrence may expose an underground anomaly, investigation lead, hidden chamber or other authored route to Madara.

No committed seed -> no fabricated Madara route.

If discovered:
- Shinobi Record preserves the encounter;
- later Madara hotspots may become eligible;
- Obito may pursue, ignore, exploit, fear, respect or oppose him.

Madara develops through repeated optional conversations, tasks, Knowledge exchange, research access, tests and manipulation rather than an immediate route switch.

## Madara's offer

After sufficient earned history, Madara may offer Obito power, knowledge and the means to pursue his ambitions.

The response creates two broad route families.

### ACCEPT

Obito accepts Madara's programme.

This may:
- alter later Madara-refusal assumptions;
- feed a future Alt Konoha campaign;
- leave Black Zetsu attempting continued manipulation later;
- still allow Obito to comply, pretend to comply, exploit or betray the plan.

No exact political endpoint is locked.

### REFUSE

Obito rejects Madara.

This may open sustained Land of Water / Kirigakure content:
- missions;
- relationships;
- local political/service problems;
- cooperation with Kiri/Mizukage authority;
- eventual role similar to Mizukage's Aide / Konoha shadow-liaison if actually earned.

This is not an automatic card unlock.

## Preserve #64

Current authority remains:

`Academy Student Obito != Mizukage's Aide Obito`

Mizukage's Aide Obito may exist as:
- NPC/World person;

## Rin / Kakashi / ocular history

Jinchūriki Rin / Kakashi-eye / paired Mangekyō history may become one valid hotspot chain.

It is not mandatory canon.

Rin may survive.
Kakashi may obtain Sharingan another way.
Obito may never transfer an eye.
Paired Mangekyō may never occur.

Writing creates opportunities.
CE/history determines actionability.
Resolvers determine outcomes.
Recorded History governs later content.

## Everlasting Mangekyō

Working preferred name:

**Everlasting Mangekyō**

Core route truth:

> Obito develops an SC-original method to prevent, stabilise or repair Mangekyō degeneration without the ordinary close-relative eye-transplant requirement.

Do not call it canonical EMS unless later authority intentionally makes it so.

Possible research inputs:
- Madara ocular research;
- Hashirama cultivation/artificial-body material;
- Stone Tablet interpretation;
- Indra/Asura research;
- Black Zetsu knowledge/manipulation;
- Nagato/Rinnegan observation;
- Obito's own experiments.

No single ingredient automatically solves the route.

The result must be researched into existence through committed Chronicle history.

## Research can cross both route families

Accepting or refusing Madara does not automatically decide whether Obito can obtain laboratory research, Hashirama material, ocular notes or Stone Tablet knowledge.

Possession follows factual events.

## Sharing the research

Obito may later share:
- Knowledge;
- research;
- treatment process;
- source material;
- experimental method;
- training opportunity

with another Character where history permits.

He does not simply transfer `Everlasting Mangekyō` by relationship.

Preserve:

**Knowledge transfer != capability transfer**

Recipients require their own compatible source/body/problem and separately earned Access/Competence.

## Nagato's Rinnegan

Do not hardcode:

`Obito cannot attempt to take Nagato's Rinnegan because canon`.

Current World Truth is that Nagato owns it.

An attempt requires legitimate Knowledge, location, opportunity, world state, access, capability/team and participant autonomy.

The attempt may fail catastrophically.

If legitimate success ever commits:

> **The consequences must be as extraordinary as the achievement.**

Rinnegan acquisition is not a prerequisite for Everlasting Mangekyō.

## Kiri integration

Do not interpret this as a requirement to build all of Kiri from scratch or complete a full Beta village campaign before Alpha.

Use current regional/hotspot authority when implementation begins.
