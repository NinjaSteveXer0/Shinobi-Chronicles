## 1. Owner sign-off

Stephen locks **Phantom Fist** as Academy Metal Lee's signature Chronicle route.

This supersedes all earlier #470 Metal concepts.

Hard owner rule:

> **Do not make Metal's signature route about fixing, curing or weaponising his anxiety.**

Metal's anxiety remains ordinary Character/Chronicle material. CE may later let his confidence, nerves, habits or personality evolve naturally from lived history.

Phantom Fist survives regardless.

The route is SC-original.

It is not:
- Eight Gates progression;
- Rock Lee inheritance;
- a generic Genjutsu curriculum;
- a generic Taijutsu upgrade;
- an "anxiety power."

---

# 2. Core invention

## Stable SC noun

**Phantom Fist**

Working precursor/family names may include:

- **Phantom Pulse**
- **Phantom Step**
- **Phantom Kick**
- **Phantom Barrage**

Final Skill names remain Combat authority.

## Core truth

Metal develops an original martial discipline where **real Taijutsu actions generate tightly coupled Genjutsu distortions through the physical exchange itself**.

This is not:

`Taijutsu action + separately cast Genjutsu`

It is:

`Taijutsu execution -> Genjutsu pulse is born from the strike/movement`

Metal's body is the delivery medium.

---

# 3. Development identity

Metal's current Academy identity begins strongly physical.

Phantom Fist deliberately requires him to develop a discipline that is not naturally dominant in his opening package:

**Genjutsu**

The signature route should therefore feel earned because the player deliberately turns Metal into a genuinely unusual Tai/Gen hybrid rather than receiving the route from inheritance.

Exact thresholds remain open.

Taijutsu and Genjutsu are causally central.

Stamina may later matter for sustained high-end sequences if Combat/Progression evidence proves it.

No other discipline is added merely to fill a matrix.

---

# 4. What Phantom Fist actually does

Phantom Fist manipulates **combat perception around real physical attacks**.

Allowed design families include:

- false strike count;
- false limb trajectory;
- false follow-up;
- early perceived impact;
- delayed perceived impact;
- phantom pain/impact echo;
- false range;
- false approach vector;
- lingering afterimage of a completed movement;
- fake continuation of a real combo;
- apparent strike from a line Metal never actually used.

The hallucination must remain causally attached to:

- Metal's real body motion;
- the opponent's perception of the exchange;
- the exact authored Phantom Fist technique.

Hard boundary:

> **Phantom Fist is not arbitrary unrestricted Genjutsu.**

Metal does not get a free universal illusion toolbox merely because he can use Phantom Fist.

---

# 5. Route shape

## Stage One — Phantom Pulse

A real strike accidentally creates a brief perceptual echo.

Possible first manifestations:

- target feels a second impact that did not occur;
- target sees the fist continue after Metal already changed line;
- target reacts to a phantom follow-up.

The first occurrence should initially be confusing rather than understood as a complete style.

## Stage Two — Phantom Fist

Metal learns to deliberately seed the distortion through punches/blocks/body contact.

The target must now distinguish:

- the real attack;
- its Genjutsu echo;
- the real follow-up.

## Stage Three — whole-body style

Metal extends the technique through:

- kicks;
- steps;
- feints;
- defensive movement;
- combination transitions.

Working branches:

- Phantom Step
- Phantom Kick

The core remains Tai-driven Genjutsu delivery.

## Stage Four — Phantom Barrage

High-end Metal may create a sequence where a small number of factual physical actions are surrounded by many believable phantom strike trajectories.

Some attacks are real.

Some are Genjutsu.

The target must solve that under real Taijutsu pressure.

Exact action economy / hit counts / Skill package remain Combat-owned.

---

# 6. Why this is not Drunken Fist with a reskin

The thematic parallel may be fun for Naruto fans:

- Lee has Drunken Fist;
- Metal develops Phantom Fist.

But mechanically/progression-wise they are not the same grammar.

Phantom Fist is a deliberately trained SC-original hybrid discipline with:

- persistent Genjutsu development;
- physical delivery;
- authored perceptual effect families;
- exact resistance/counterplay;
- its own later Skill tree.

No intoxication state is required.

---

# 7. Counterplay and failure

Phantom Fist must not become unavoidable.

Possible counters/limits:

- strong Genjutsu resistance;
- sensory capability;
- legitimate anti-Genjutsu techniques;
- breaking the Genjutsu effect;
- reading Metal's real body despite the phantom layer;
- forcing Metal into an exchange where the required physical trigger never lands;
- disruption before the pulse commits.

Possible Metal-side failures:

- over-layering distortions and ruining his own timing;
- attempting a Genjutsu complexity beyond current control;
- desynchronising phantom and physical action;
- creating an obvious phantom tell;
- maintaining a phantom after the physical situation has already invalidated it.

Failure may contribute factual training history where authorised.

---

# 8. Out-of-Battle possibilities

Phantom Fist is primarily a martial progression route, but later authored non-Battle uses may emerge from the underlying Tai/Gen control.

Possible bounded examples:

- demonstration/training;
- deception during a physical escape;
- confusing eyewitnesses to one exact movement exchange;
- controlled sparring/assessment;
- Cross-Origin Chronicle Assist where Metal's false physical read creates an opening another Character can exploit.

Do not turn Phantom Fist into generic social Genjutsu.

---

# 9. Personality boundary

Metal's anxiety:

- may remain;
- may improve;
- may worsen;
- may become irrelevant;
- may change differently in different Chronicles.

None of those outcomes are required for Phantom Fist progression.

Hard rule:

> **Personality history may colour how Metal experiences Phantom Fist. It does not own the technique's existence.**

The route should still make sense if later CE history produces a confident, composed or highly experienced Metal.

---

# 10. Cross-Origin compatibility

Phantom Fist is intentionally compatible with later Cross-Origin Chronicle Assist.

Examples:

- Hinata exploits a phantom opening with Living Thread;
- Kushina prepares a seal while Phantom Fist distorts an enemy's read;
- Obito/another teammate follows a false attack line with a real joint technique.

Preserve:

**joint action != Skill ownership transfer**

Metal remains the owner of Phantom Fist capability/history.
