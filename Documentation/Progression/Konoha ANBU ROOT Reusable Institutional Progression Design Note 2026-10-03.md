# Shinobi Chronicles — ANBU / ROOT Reusable Institutional Progression Design Note

**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Owner:** Progression / Development  
**Status:** **DURABLE REUSABLE DESIGN NOTE — NOT KAKASHI-EXCLUSIVE / IMPLEMENTATION SEPARATE**  
**Purpose:** Preserve the expanded ANBU / ROOT progression possibilities developed during #470 without collapsing them into Academy Kakashi's signature route.

---

# 1. Core correction

ANBU and ROOT are **Konoha institutional progression routes**, not Kakashi-exclusive systems.

A Character may become eligible through:
- Main Story history;
- side missions;
- institutional relationships;
- specialist evidence;
- direct recruitment;
- exact prior actions;
- another authored Chronicle route.

No Character receives ANBU / ROOT membership merely because:
- they are powerful;
- they are an Origin;
- they are already in Konoha;
- they have a particular Rank;
- another Character joined.

Preserve:

> **institutional route != Character-exclusive signature route**

Kakashi may have unusually strong / early / bespoke entry because his Academy Origin can function as a hidden ANBU suitability evaluation, but that does not make ANBU or ROOT his private content.

# 3. Progression shape

Recommended reusable progression grammar:

## Stage A — discoverability / contact
Character gains legitimate Knowledge / access to the institution or one of its operatives.

Possible sources:
- Main Story;
- hotspot;
- mission;
- prior faction member relationship;
- covert event;
- institutional referral;
- exact specialist performance.

## Stage B — evaluation / trial
Institution tests the Character.

Tests may consume:
- Stealth;
- Tracking;
- Investigation;
- assassination/covert fieldcraft;
- restraint / capture;
- Bukijutsu;
- Ninjutsu;
- Genjutsu;
- Fūinjutsu;
- Kinjutsu;
- interrogation;
- reporting;
- secrecy;
- judgement under incomplete information.

No universal stat threshold should replace authored evaluation.

## Stage C — provisional work
Character may perform one or more missions before formal commitment.

Where current secrecy/history permits, the same Character may receive contact from both ANBU and ROOT before final allegiance.

## Stage D — commitment
Character formally joins one institutional route or rejects/fails it.

This should not be reduced to an early naked:
`ANBU | ROOT`

Commitment should follow actual Chronicle history.

## Stage E — mature faction campaign
Membership begins a continuing progression campaign.

Joining is **not** the ending.

# 4. ANBU route identity

Theme:

> **Hokage-directed black operations with retained field judgement and institutional legitimacy inside Konoha's covert apparatus.**

ANBU should not be reduced to:
- generic assassin guild;
- better Genin/Jōnin missions;
- one costume;
- one Rank title.

## Candidate ANBU mission families

- covert surveillance;
- counter-surveillance;
- high-value capture;
- assassination where authorised;
- extraction / rescue;
- infiltration;
- classified courier / transfer;
- counter-intelligence;
- protection of sensitive witnesses/assets;
- internal security;
- rogue-nin pursuit;
- border / foreign-village black operations;
- recovery of stolen Konoha intelligence;
- tracking escaped high-value targets;
- suppression of dangerous sealed/forbidden material;
- investigation of compromised operatives;
- deniable support to Hokage-directed Story operations;
- emergency response where public shinobi presence is undesirable.

Mission success != Battle victory automatically.

## Candidate ANBU entry rewards

On legitimate induction, an exact Character may receive:
- ANBU representation/card where authorised;
- ANBU mask;
- covert field armour / vest;
- ANBU-specific tantō / short blade;
- classified utility kit;
- first ANBU-only Skill opportunity;
- ANBU HQ access;
- named ANBU contacts;
- ANBU mission board / briefing access where UI/World later supports it.

Exact equipment identity is Combat / Items / Weapons-owned.

## Candidate ANBU Skill families

Possible institutional Skill opportunities:
- silent takedown;
- rapid restraint;
- covert Bukijutsu;
- counter-surveillance;
- classified clone / substitution variants;
- extraction support;
- tactical concealment;
- pursuit interruption;
- field interrogation support;
- squad-cover techniques;
- assassination techniques;
- specialised sensory/counter-sensory methods.

Membership does not grant every ANBU Skill automatically.

## ANBU mature progression

Potential higher-order development:
- greater clearance;
- restricted records access;
- specialised equipment;
- advanced ANBU technique access;
- named operative relationships;
- temporary mission-team assignment;
- operational cell responsibility;
- briefing authority;
- mission planning;
- command of available institutional support;
- direct Hokage trust / black-ops relationship where earned.

Assignment != roster ownership.

## ANBU late-game consequences

A developed ANBU route can affect:
- who trusts the Character;
- what classified Story information they see;
- which black-ops missions exist;
- whether they can intervene in covert institutional events;
- how they interact with ROOT;
- foreign-village covert relationships;
- later Canon / Alt Story possibilities.

# 5. ROOT route identity

Theme:

> **Danzō-directed deniable operations, compartmentalised Knowledge, restricted methods and access to Konoha's buried machinery.**

ROOT must offer genuinely different opportunities from ANBU or the choice becomes cosmetic.

ROOT should not be reduced to:
- evil ANBU;
- darker costume;
- automatic Kinjutsu shop;
- morality route.

## Candidate ROOT mission families

- deniable assassination;
- infiltration of Konoha or foreign institutions;
- surveillance on Konoha actors;
- suppression / recovery of forbidden research;
- covert experimentation support;
- asset recruitment;
- blackmail / leverage operations;
- witness silencing / extraction;
- sabotage;
- false-flag operations;
- clandestine counter-intelligence;
- internal faction monitoring;
- retrieval of compromised ROOT material;
- hidden facility defence / abandonment;
- deep-cover placement;
- surveillance of Uchiha / clans / sensitive institutions where current history supports it;
- anti-ANBU / anti-Hokage operations where current ROOT goals support it;
- secret containment of dangerous sources/entities/Knowledge.

Exact Story ownership remains Writing / World.

## Candidate ROOT entry rewards

On legitimate induction:
- ROOT representation/card where authorised;
- ROOT mask / covert accessory;
- concealed armour / wraps;
- ROOT-specific weapon/tool;
- restricted field item;
- first ROOT-only Skill opportunity;
- ROOT HQ access;
- faction contacts;
- access to exact restricted rooms/records.

## Candidate ROOT Skill families

Potential institutional opportunities:
- assassination / silent disable;
- counter-tracking;
- sensory suppression;
- covert infiltration;
- compartmentalisation / interrogation resistance;
- restricted Fūinjutsu;
- restricted Kinjutsu;
- deniable Ninjutsu;
- concealment;
- covert weapon methods;
- capture / restraint tools;
- dangerous suppression techniques.

Hard rule:

> **ROOT membership != all forbidden Knowledge.**

Every Skill/research item still requires exact provenance and eligibility.

## ROOT named relationships

Current project already contains persistent ROOT actors, including:

**Shiori Kuroda**

Her exact participation depends on current World Truth / Knowledge / custody / allegiance.

Future ROOT content may also use other canon/SC Characters where separately authorised.

## ROOT mature progression

Potential later opportunities:
- deeper Records Vault access;
- Sealed Lower Junction content;
- Danzō private operations;
- restricted research;
- control or influence over exact deniable missions;
- mission-cell responsibility;
- sabotage of ROOT from within;
- double-agent play;
- faction betrayal;
- institutional reform / takeover attempt where extraordinary history permits;
- exposure / fugitive state after betrayal.

No generic:
`10 missions -> ROOT Captain`.

Institutional influence must be earned from exact history.

# 6. Material rewards matter

Joining either institution should create a tangible reward moment.

Faction induction should feel materially important.

Potential reward classes:
- Character representation;
- mask/accessory;
- armour;
- weapon;
- unique tools;
- Skill access;
- restricted facility access;
- named contacts;
- Knowledge;
- mission access;
- relationship state;
- specialist evidence;
- future World opportunities.

Players should not receive only:
- a title;
- a dialogue line;
- a new portrait.

Likewise, material equipment alone is not enough.

The faction reward is the combination of:
**gear + capability + access + Story + relationships + future opportunity.**

---

# 7. Equipment progression

ANBU / ROOT should support their own equipment ecology.

Potential progression pattern:

## Entry
- mask;
- basic covert armour;
- standard faction weapon/tool.

## Mid-route
- specialised mission gear;
- upgraded weapon;
- anti-sensor / anti-surveillance equipment;
- exact role-specific accessories;
- rare seals/tools;
- faction-crafted items.

## Late-route
- rare/legendary institutional equipment;
- Character-specific reward;
- exact item with meaningful provenance;
- equipment whose effect depends on exact institutional history.

Do not duplicate another Character's unique relic merely to fill the faction reward slot.

Example:
**White Fang Tantō remains Hatake/Sakumo provenance, not generic ANBU equipment.**

---

# 8. Skill progression

Faction Skill access should be:
- mission-earned;
- instructor-earned;
- evidence-earned;
- relationship-earned;
- exact research-earned.

Do not create:
`membership=true -> all faction Skills`.

Different Characters may leave the same faction route with different capabilities because their:
- disciplines;
- missions;
- mentors;
- choices;
- opportunities

differed.

---

# 9. Institutional command / responsibility

Late institutional progression should change what the Character is allowed to **do**, not merely the text next to their name.

Potential ANBU / ROOT authority effects:
- choose mission support from available institutional actors;
- assign exact operatives;
- select one of several operational plans;
- request equipment/resources;
- access higher-level briefings;
- decide custody / extraction / containment priorities;
- influence which operation is attempted;
- accept responsibility for subordinate outcomes.

Hard rule:

> **command authority != ownership**

If an operative dies because of a Character's order:
- that is Chronicle history.

If an operative refuses an order:
- autonomy remains real.

---

# 10. ANBU / ROOT cross-faction interaction

Before commitment, limited interaction with both sides may be possible when secrecy/history supports it.

After commitment:
- some opportunities close;
- some relationships change;
- some equipment/techniques become hostile to obtain;
- some missions become mutually exclusive;
- new cross-faction operations appear.

Defection / theft / double-agent routes remain possible if earned.

Do not enforce impossible walls solely for game-balance convenience.

But:

> **The consequences must be as extraordinary as the achievement.**

Stealing ROOT research as ANBU, betraying ANBU to ROOT, infiltrating one faction for the other, or leaving after deep access must produce proportionate history.

---

# 11. Canon / Alt campaign interaction

ANBU / ROOT affiliation may become input into future Canon / Alt route eligibility.

Do not hard-lock:

- ANBU = Canon
- ROOT = Alt

A Character's actions inside the faction matter more than the label.

Examples:
- ANBU operative causes Alt divergence;
- ROOT operative secretly protects Hokage interests;
- double-agent history changes later campaign access.

Institution = historical input.
Institution != automatic route verdict.

---

# 12. Kakashi-specific use without exclusivity

Academy Kakashi may consume this reusable architecture unusually strongly because:

- his Origin can be interpreted as a hidden ANBU suitability trial;
- Minato personally receives his evaluation;
- MI / AMT / PS provide private-history callbacks;
- Sakumo is alive;
- Danzō may independently seek the same prodigy.

Possible Kakashi advantages:
- earlier recruitment;
- special trial bypass;
- unique Minato dialogue;
- unique ROOT poaching;
- Kakashi-only missions;
- bespoke equipment / Skills;
- special institutional reputation.

But:

> **Kakashi privileged route != ANBU / ROOT exclusive ownership**

---

# 13. Alpha / implementation boundary

This note does NOT automatically make full ANBU / ROOT faction campaigns an Alpha blocker.

Current purpose:
- preserve the richer design;
- prevent future reduction to two location unlocks;
- provide Progression / Writing / World / Combat a durable target.

Implementation remains separate.

When activated:
- Progression closes gates/stages;
- Writing closes faction Story;
- World closes missions/opportunities;
- Combat/Items/Weapons closes Skills/equipment;
- Registry closes representations/rank implications;
- Acquisition closes ownership/recruitment consequences;
- Coding implements only after contracts are exact.

---

# 14. Canonical shorthand

> **ANBU / ROOT membership is a gateway into an institutional campaign, not the campaign's ending.**

> **Faction reward = gear + capability + access + relationships + Story + future opportunity.**

> **ANBU and ROOT must feel materially and historically different.**

> **Institutional progression changes what the Character may do, not only what title they display.**
