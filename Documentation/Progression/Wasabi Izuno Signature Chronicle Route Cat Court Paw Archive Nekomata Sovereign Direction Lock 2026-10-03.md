# 5. Paw Archive

## Stable route concept

**Paw Archive**

The Paw Archive is more than a collectible checklist.

It records exact feline encounters / histories / techniques / traces across the Chronicle.

World content may scatter eligible cats / Ninneko / feline sources across regions.

Finding one may require:
- Investigation;
- Tracking;
- Sensory work;
- relationship access;
- puzzle/history discovery;
- exact travel/world opportunity;
- Combat;
- another authored route.

## Rewards

A Paw Archive discovery may produce one or more of:

- exact archive Knowledge;
- new cat-network relationship;
- hidden location;
- dialogue opportunity;
- special route Skill eligibility;
- special weapon / accessory / gear opportunity;
- cat-network service/contact;
- Investigation / Tracking development evidence;
- Hunter-Nin / specialist evidence where exact current progression authority recognises it;
- new Sasuke / Itachi / Matatabi / Ninneko Story opportunities;
- later Nekomata Sovereign development evidence.

Hard rule:

> **Paw discovery != material loot only.**

The meaningful reward may be Knowledge, evidence, relationship, access or future route possibility.

Also preserve:

> **Paw Archive completion count != automatic power meter.**

No generic `find 10 cats -> legendary form` requirement is authorised.

Exact findings matter.

- conversation opportunity != predetermined reaction.

The value is **new Chronicle possibility**, not guaranteed fan-service appearance.
