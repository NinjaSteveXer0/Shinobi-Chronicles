## 1. Owner sign-off

Stephen accepts **human chakra puppetry through Tenketsu manipulation** as Academy Hinata's signature Chronicle route direction.

This supersedes every earlier #470 Hinata proposal.

Road-to-Ninja Hinata remains the Character's ordinary identity/voice direction and is not the signature reward.

Curse Mark / Curse Seal content remains part of the separate global source system and is not this route.

The route is SC-original.

---

# 2. Core invention

Hinata does **not** use ordinary external puppet strings.

She learns to manipulate a living target's **own chakra directly through their Tenketsu**, pulling exact sections of that chakra outward into controllable living lines while those lines remain causally part of the target's own circulation.

Working route nouns:

- **Living Thread**
- **Tenketsu Thread**
- **Tenketsu Marionette**
- **Chakra Loom**
- **Chakra Shear**
- **Dead Line**

Final display names may change later without changing the locked route identity.

---

# 3. Why this belongs to Hinata

Academy Hinata's Origin already establishes:

- close observation of another person's movement;
- adaptation across repeated exchanges;
- willingness to stop relying on a rehearsed answer;
- precise response timing;
- observation of another student's movement problem;
- confidence/assertiveness as a persistent SC character direction.

The signature route extends that history into something nobody in ordinary Hyūga/Taijutsu/Bloodline progression automatically receives:

> **Hinata stops manipulating chakra points only as strike targets and starts using them as live control interfaces into another person's active chakra network.**

Byakugan / Tenketsu perception may be a prerequisite/ingredient where separately legal.

They are not the signature reward.

---

# 4. Stage One — Twin Pulse / assisted circulation

Working precursor:

**Twin Pulse**

The route begins in a rescue/support context.

A living ally is:

- conscious or partially responsive;
- physically unable to coordinate one part of their chakra network;
- suffering a route-local disruption, obstruction or loss of control.

Hinata discovers she can temporarily pull/guidе part of the ally's own chakra through exact Tenketsu and help the body complete a movement or maintain circulation.

She is not donating her own chakra as the main mechanic.

She is guiding the other person's existing chakra.

## Early support possibilities

Later authored content may permit:

- guiding a failing limb;
- bypassing one local circulation problem;
- pre-aligning an ally before entry to Battle;
- helping an ally complete one exact action their current circulation would otherwise prevent;
- temporarily improving a teammate's opening readiness where Combat later authorises it.

These are exact authored outcomes, not a permanent generic buff aura.

---

# 5. Stage Two — Living Thread

Hinata deliberately reproduces the phenomenon.

She learns to pull one or more **Living Threads** from exact Tenketsu.

These threads remain part of the subject's own chakra system.

That creates the critical distinction:

**ordinary puppet string = external control medium**  
**Living Thread = exposed continuation of the target's own circulation**

Breaking one therefore cannot be treated like cutting inert string.

## Cooperative use

With legitimate consent/coordination, Hinata may eventually:

- assist movement;
- stabilise chakra timing;
- coordinate more than one limb/action;
- prepare an ally's chakra network before deployment;
- help a disrupted ally regain limited functional control.

## Hostile use

Against a resisting target, later stages may permit:

- interrupting a hand-seal sequence;
- forcing a limb off-line;
- buckling a leg;
- disturbing a technique route;
- temporarily pulling one body segment against the user's intended motion.

Hostile use must consume exact resistance/contest semantics.

It is not automatic domination.

---

# 6. Stage Three — Tenketsu Marionette

At advanced route stage, Hinata can maintain several Living Threads at once and coordinate them as a control lattice.

Working state:

**Tenketsu Marionette**

This is the route's dark centre.

The victim:

- remains mentally themselves;
- remains aware where current Story/Combat state permits;
- may resist;
- does not become an owned puppet/Entity;
- is not mind-controlled.

Hinata is manipulating the body's movement by directly controlling the target's own chakra flow through selected Tenketsu.

Possible high-end authored uses:

- force an enemy arm away from a target;
- disrupt stance;
- ruin hand-seal timing;
- partially force movement;
- temporarily immobilise one body section;
- control an ally's body with explicit cooperation during a rescue/escape sequence;
- create unusual team-combination actions.

Full-body hostile marionette control, if ever authorised, requires extreme progression plus strong contest limits.

---

# 7. Stage Four — Chakra Shear / Dead Line

The route eventually discovers the consequence of severing a Living Thread.

Working nouns:

**Chakra Shear**  
**Dead Line**

When a thread that is still causally part of the target's active circulation is severed, the consequence is not:

> puppet string broke.

Instead, the attached Tenketsu/circulation route may go functionally silent or scarred.

Possible authored consequences:

- local Tenketsu cluster becomes temporarily inactive;
- chakra reinforcement through that route fails;
- jutsu routing requiring that path becomes harder/impossible;
- hand/limb coordination degrades;
- affected limb/body section may become chakra-functionally "dead" for a bounded period;
- severe misuse may produce persistent chakra scarring requiring actual recovery/medical content.

This does not physically amputate the limb.

It does not automatically permanently cripple every target.

Exact duration, severity, recovery and boss/major-character boundaries remain Combat / World / Medicine authority.

## Ethical/personal handling

The route must not impose a morality meter.

Hinata may use or refuse brutal applications based on:

- current Story;
- player intent;
- autonomous Character choice when she is not player-controlled;
- relationships;
- perceived necessity;
- current Knowledge.

Route access != mandatory cruelty.

# 8. Suna puppetry crossover

Later Suna puppetry training is an **ingredient / refinement path**, not the route source.

Hinata may encounter ordinary puppet masters and realise:

> they control inert bodies from outside; she controls living chakra systems from inside.

Suna training can teach:

- multi-point tension;
- independent-limb coordination;
- line interference;
- load balancing;
- control under resistance;
- safe release;
- simultaneous thread management.

Hinata then applies those principles back to Living Threads.

This creates an SC-original inversion of ordinary puppetry rather than turning Hinata into a standard puppet user.

---

# 9. Out-of-Battle possibilities

The route must matter outside Combat.

Potential future consumers include:

- rescue of an ally whose chakra/movement is failing;
- assisting treatment where movement/chakra circulation must be controlled;
- safely moving an injured Character;
- stabilising a person during a dangerous seal/poison/chakra disruption;
- temporarily coordinating a teammate through a hostile environmental condition;
- detecting where a Living Thread cannot safely be pulled;
- Cross-Origin Chronicle Assist where another Character's unresolved thread contains a living-body/chakra-network problem.

Exact medicine/healing authority remains separate.

Hinata is not automatically a medic.

---

# 10. Stat / discipline principle

Do **not** pre-lock a simplistic pair such as:

`Hinata = Taijutsu + Ninjutsu`

The route itself determines what later thresholds are causally necessary.

Likely consumers may include:

- precision/control development;
- Taijutsu because body/timing matter;
- Ninjutsu if sustained chakra shaping proves necessary;
- Stamina if multi-thread maintenance taxes the body;
- Genjutsu only if later Story proves perception manipulation is part of the route.

No discipline is mandatory merely for thematic symmetry.

Exact thresholds remain future Progression tuning.

---

# 11. Failure states

The route must support meaningful failure.

Possible failures:

- thread snaps unexpectedly;
- Hinata pulls the wrong Tenketsu;
- circulation feedback hits both participants;
- hostile target overwhelms the control contest;
- ally panics/resists and destabilises the link;
- multi-thread control tangles two circulation paths;
- a Dead Line is created accidentally;
- route temporarily stalls until recovery/training occurs.

Failure can create later Story rather than simply "skill failed."

---

# 12. Cross-Origin compatibility

This route is intentionally compatible with future **Cross-Origin Chronicle Assist** semantics.

Examples:

- Hinata helps another Origin Character move/use chakra through a specific unresolved injury/disruption;
- another Origin Character's capability creates the situation where Hinata can establish a Living Thread;
- joint use creates Shared History only when an actual committed joint occurrence happens.

Preserve:

**assist != route ownership transfer**  
**ally benefit != Hinata Skill ownership transfer**  
**team assignment != automatic route availability**
