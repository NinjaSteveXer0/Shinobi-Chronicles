# 11. Dedupe / causal-cap law

## Numeric development

One stable progression slot may grant at most once per committed source occurrence and discipline.

Retry, reload, replay, Receipt reopen, renderer refresh and browser back/forward must return the existing receipt.

Standing causal caps continue to apply:

- ordinary non-Battle technical discipline: **3 EXP per discipline per causal root**;
- ordinary non-Battle Stamina: **2 EXP per subject per causal root**, unless stronger exact authority says otherwise;
- Battle action development remains under its separate 6 technical / 2 Stamina Battle caps.

Where this contract states a smaller exact cap, the smaller cap governs.

## Special Jōnin evidence

Same source may support several tags or even several exact qualifications only where factual work independently supports them.

It does not become several independent sources.

No mapping in this document is a capstone or `specialistLevel=true`.
