# Branch Timeline Isolation and Knowledge Containment Lock

**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Owner:** Writing / Story — Konoha  
**Status:** **BINDING STORYWIDE AUTHORING REQUIREMENT**

## Core rule

> **Treat each Story branch as its own sealed timeline.**
>
> **Nothing from another branch outcome exists in the current timeline unless the current timeline actually produced it.**
>
> **No character may know, imply, react to, or report information they have not legitimately learned inside the current timeline.**

Writing must author branching Story as parallel, self-consistent timelines rather than as one blended pool of remembered outcomes.

## Timeline isolation

For every branch, track only the factual history produced by that branch, including:

- who was encountered;
- who was defeated;
- who escaped;
- who was released;
- who was detained or transferred;
- who was killed;
- who survived an attempted kill;
- who witnessed what;
- who learned what later;
- objective/package state;
- turn/timing outcomes;
- injuries and custody;
- location and presence;
- Chronicle history created by that exact sequence.

Do not import consequences, dialogue, knowledge, emotional reactions, injuries, deaths, survivals, custody, or jokes from sibling branches.

## Knowledge containment

Every line must be checked against the speaker's Knowledge in the current timeline.

The narrator/writer may know the full branch tree. Characters do not.

Do not allow:

- ANBU to reveal hidden evaluation knowledge to Kakashi through careless phrasing;
- a character to know someone survived before that survival is discovered;
- a participant to react to an event they never witnessed or were never told about;
- a later office scene to assume a participant is present if the current branch left them dead, imprisoned, elsewhere, or otherwise unavailable;
- a character to refer to outcomes that happened only in a different branch;
- World Truth, observer Knowledge and presentation to collapse into one another.

## Narrator discipline

Authoring should mentally reset when entering a new branch.

Before writing a scene, establish the current branch state:

1. What has happened in this timeline?
2. Who is alive?
3. Who is present?
4. What does each present character know?
5. What does the protagonist know?
6. What does the player know that the protagonist does not?
7. What facts remain hidden?
8. Which prior branch outcomes are explicitly NOT part of this timeline?

Only then write dialogue, reaction and consequence.

## Kakashi Origin immediate benchmark

Academy Kakashi's Origin is the active benchmark.

Examples:

- If Kakashi reports the ANBU Marked Target dead, ANBU and Minato may only know that Kakashi reported him dead unless they independently learn otherwise.
- If the Marked Target actually survived, nobody may speak as though they know that before the survival reveal occurs in that timeline.
- If Kakashi took someone to the Uchiha Police Force in one branch, that custody state must not appear in a sibling branch where he released or killed that person.
- If Kakashi never learns the mission was an evaluation, no pre-reveal dialogue may leak that fact to him.

## Final lock

> **One branch = one sealed timeline.**
>
> **Author from the current timeline only.**
>
> **Do not mix branch memory, hidden truth, or sibling outcomes into the scene.**
