# 4. Opening retrofit — Konoha rooftop

## Scene intent

Establish Kakashi as observant before he ever speaks, establish the ANBU operative as controlled rather than exposition-heavy, establish Hokage authority, and put the package objective into Kakashi's hands without turning the scene into a briefing report.

## Player-facing performance

Kakashi watched Konoha from the rooftop.

Evening traffic moved below in pieces: a shop shutter rolling down, two Academy students arguing over the fastest way home, somebody calling from an upper window. None of it required his attention.

The change behind him did.

A presence registered at the edge of his awareness.

His eye shifted first.

An ANBU operative stood several paces back, masked and motionless.

**ANBU OPERATIVE**  
“Kakashi Hatake.”

Kakashi turned his head slightly.

The operative did not offer a greeting.

**ANBU OPERATIVE**  
“You have orders. Stop this package from falling into the wrong hands.”

A sealed envelope appeared in one gloved hand.

Kakashi studied the mask for a moment, then pushed away from the roofline and crossed the tiles toward him.

**KAKASHI**  
“Why are you coming to me with this?”

He took the envelope.

**ANBU OPERATIVE**  
“Hokage's orders.”

That changed the weight of the paper in Kakashi's hand.

His attention sharpened. His eye dropped to the seal.

Inside: the person he was expected to follow, enough identifying material to recognise him, and the objective already stated aloud.

No invitation to join ANBU.

No claim that Kakashi was one of them.

A limited assignment under Hokage authority.

Kakashi folded the material once and tucked it away.

The ANBU operative was already gone by the time he looked up.

## Exit state

- Kakashi knows the mission came under Hokage authority;
- Kakashi knows his operative objective: **stop the package from falling into the wrong hands**;
- Kakashi is not ANBU;
- no hidden evaluation truth is revealed.

# 5. Rooftop -> pursuit transition retrofit

Kakashi did not need long to find the man from the envelope.

The difficult part was making sure the man never realised he had been found.

Konoha changed shape when Kakashi followed someone through it. Streets stopped being streets and became sightlines. Crowds became cover. Roof edges became distances to clear before the person below could turn his head.

ANBU Marked Target moved without the nervous scanning of someone who expected immediate pursuit.

Kakashi kept it that way.

He followed from above until the route tightened into older streets and narrower angles, then dropped lower when the rooftops would have made him too obvious.

The target never looked directly at him.

Not once.

That did not make Kakashi relax.

It made him wonder who the man expected to meet.

By the time the route bent toward the Sakura tree and the alley beyond it, Kakashi had his answer.

Someone was waiting.

The Sakura tree stood at the edge of the main street, its branches reaching over the quieter mouth of the alley.

ANBU Marked Target slowed before he reached it.

Not enough to look hesitant.

Enough for Kakashi to notice.

Package Smuggler waited deeper in the shadowed side street, positioned where anyone passing on the main road would have little reason to look twice.

Kakashi settled into concealment above and behind the exchange.

No wasted movement.

No conversation loud enough to carry cleanly to him.

ANBU Marked Target kept one arm close to his body.

The package.

The objective had finally become visible.

Kakashi measured the distance between himself, the two men and the open street beyond them.

Intervene now and the clean exchange stops.

Move closer, and he risks changing the scene merely by being seen.
