# ACADEMY WASABI IZUNO
## THE PURSUIT

# SCENE 1 — HEAD START

The target is already gone.

Wasabi looks down the empty street.

Then at the instructor.

Then back at the empty street.

**WASABI:** “You started without me?”

The instructor checks the route marker around his wrist.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “I gave the target a head start.”

Wasabi points after them.

**WASABI:** “How much?”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Enough.”

Her hands go out.

**WASABI:** “That is not a time.”

A student behind her laughs.

Wasabi looks over her shoulder.

**WASABI:** “You can laugh when you're ahead of me.”

The laugh stops.

The instructor points toward the village.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Your target is heading for extraction. Catch them before they reach it.”

Wasabi rocks forward onto the balls of her feet.

**WASABI:** “That's better.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “I haven't said go yet.”

Wasabi freezes.

Barely.

The instructor watches her for one more second.

Drops his hand.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Go.”

Wasabi is gone.

Behind her—

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “You were waiting for that?”

**WASABI:** “Obviously!”

---

# SCENE 2 — THE TRAIL

Wasabi takes the first wall instead of the street.

Roof.

Awning.

Fence.

Back to the roof.

Ahead, the pursuit target disappears around a corner.

Wasabi grins.

**WASABI:** “Found you.”

She hits the corner.

Stops.

The street offers too many answers.

Fresh tracks cut right.

A basket is still rocking.

Mud marks the drainage edge.

One roof tile sits crooked.

Two Academy students land behind her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Which way?”

Wasabi crouches.

Looks at the tracks.

Looks at the roof.

Looks toward extraction.

Every second she spends thinking is another second the target gets.

### CHOICE

**TAKE THE OBVIOUS TRAIL**

**LOOK FOR SOMETHING BETTER**

**WORK WITH THE OTHERS**

**FORGET THE TRAIL — WHERE ARE THEY GOING?**

## TAKE THE OBVIOUS TRAIL

Wasabi points right.

**WASABI:** “That way.”

She takes off.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “You sure?”

**WASABI:** “No!”

The student nearly misses a step.

Wasabi vaults a crate.

**WASABI:** “But I'm not standing here until I am!”

The trail stays strong.

Scuffed dirt.

Moved boxes.

A footprint on a low wall.

Then it splits.

The freshest marks stop beside an empty alley.

Wasabi skids to a halt.

Looks at the wall.

Looks at the ground.

The student catches up.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Still confident?”

Wasabi points at them.

**WASABI:** “You are enjoying this too much.”

She crouches again.

This time she takes longer than half a second.

---

## LOOK FOR SOMETHING BETTER

Wasabi starts right.

Stops.

The student behind her almost runs into her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “What now?”

Wasabi points at the tracks.

**WASABI:** “They're too obvious.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “They're footprints.”

**WASABI:** “Exactly. They're practically waving.”

She drops to the drainage edge.

Mud is smeared across the stone.

Higher up, a roof tile has shifted sideways.

Wasabi's eyes move between them.

Then forward.

**WASABI:** “There.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “What did you see?”

Wasabi is already climbing.

**WASABI:** “A better answer!”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “That explains nothing!”

Wasabi's hand appears above the roof edge.

Points ahead.

**WASABI:** “Then keep up!”

---

## WORK WITH THE OTHERS

Wasabi turns on the two students behind her.

Points to one.

**WASABI:** “Roofs.”

Points to the other.

**WASABI:** “Street.”

The first student stares at her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Since when are you in charge?”

Wasabi starts up the wall.

**WASABI:** “Since you both asked me!”

**ACADEMY STUDENT 2:** “What are you checking?”

**WASABI:** “Everything you miss.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “That's not a route.”

Wasabi reaches the roof.

**WASABI:** “Then stop arguing and find one!”

They split.

A block later—

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Nothing north!”

From below—

**ACADEMY STUDENT 2:** “Marks near the canal!”

Wasabi stops on the ridge.

**WASABI:** “Fresh?”

A pause.

**ACADEMY STUDENT 2:** “How would I know?”

Wasabi throws both hands out.

**WASABI:** “Touch them!”

Another pause.

**ACADEMY STUDENT 2:** “...Oh.”

Wasabi groans.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Great team.”

**WASABI:** “We're doing amazing.”

The student laughs.

Wasabi does too.

Then all three move.

---

## FORGET THE TRAIL — WHERE ARE THEY GOING?

Wasabi looks at the tracks.

Then past them.

Toward the far side of the village.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “You're not following those?”

**WASABI:** “No.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Why?”

Wasabi points toward extraction.

**WASABI:** “Because they can lie about where they've been.”

She hops onto the wall.

**WASABI:** “They can't lie about where they're going.”

The student looks at the trail.

Then at Wasabi.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “That's the wrong direction.”

Wasabi grins.

**WASABI:** “Only if I'm wrong.”

She jumps.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “That was not reassuring!”

# SCENE 3 — THE SPLIT

The pursuit stretches farther into the village.

The rooftops thin near the river.

A stronger trail cuts inland.

A shout breaks from another street.

Extraction lies beyond all of it.

Wasabi catches a flash of movement ahead.

Maybe the target.

Maybe bait.

Her foot taps once.

She has time for one call.

Not two.

### CHOICE

**TAKE THE RIVER**

**FOLLOW THE STRONGER TRAIL**

**CHECK THE SHOUTING**

**CUT FOR THE INTERCEPT**

# SCENE 4A — THE RIVER

Wasabi drops to the river path.

The route costs her height immediately.

She looks up at the rooftops she just gave away.

**WASABI:** “You'd better be worth it.”

The river path bends wider than the streets above.

Longer.

Open.

No alleys.

No corners.

No place for the target to vanish.

Wasabi leans forward.

**WASABI:** “Fine.”

Then she runs.

The first stretch burns.

The second hurts.

By the third, all she can hear is water, sandals and breathing.

She keeps going.

The target appears ahead where the path curves back toward extraction.

Wasabi's whole face changes.

**WASABI:** “HEY!”

The target looks back.

Their eyes widen.

**TARGET:** “You took the river?”

**WASABI:** “And you're still in front of me!”

The target runs harder.

Wasabi laughs.

**WASABI:** “Not for long!”

The last stretch becomes a race.

No clues.

No guessing.

Just distance.

The target reaches the straight first.

Wasabi starts closing.

One stride.

Then another.

The target looks back.

Wasabi gains again.

**TARGET:** “Seriously?!”

**WASABI:** “KEEP RUNNING!”

The target reaches for the extraction marker.

Wasabi launches.

Her hand catches the back of their shirt before their foot crosses the line.

Both nearly go down.

Wasabi plants one foot, slides, and hangs on.

The proctor drops an arm.

**PROCTOR:** “Target secured.”

Wasabi bends over, still holding the shirt.

Raises one finger.

**WASABI:** “Again.”

The proctor stares at her.

**PROCTOR:** “Target secured.”

Wasabi releases the target.

**WASABI:** “Thank you.”

The target is still trying to breathe.

**TARGET:** “You're horrible.”

Wasabi grins.

**WASABI:** “You made me take the long way.”

---

# SCENE 4B — THE STRONGER TRAIL

The inland trail looks perfect.

Fresh marks.

Broken twig.

Scuffed wall.

A scrap caught against a fence.

Wasabi gets faster with every sign.

**WASABI:** “There you are.”

The trail turns into a narrow yard.

And ends.

No target.

No exit.

Nothing.

Wasabi stops.

The scrap moves in the breeze.

She pulls it free.

Turns it over.

A faint scrape sits on the wall behind her.

Not into the yard.

Away from it.

Wasabi's shoulders drop.

**WASABI:** “Oh, come on.”

She points the scrap at the empty wall.

**WASABI:** “You planted this.”

Nobody answers.

She looks at it again.

**WASABI:** “...Fine.”

A beat.

**WASABI:** “That was good.”

She stuffs it into her pocket.

**WASABI:** “Still annoying.”

Then she runs back out.

This time she checks everything.

---

# SCENE 4C — THE SHOUTING

Wasabi takes three steps toward extraction.

A shout cuts across the next street.

She stops.

Looks toward the target route.

Looks toward the shout.

**WASABI:** “Seriously?”

She changes direction.

Around the corner, an Academy student is backed against a wall.

A Rogue Genin stands between them and the street.

Older.

Bigger.

The student sees her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Wasabi!”

The Rogue Genin glances over.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Keep running.”

Wasabi's eyes narrow.

**WASABI:** “Didn't ask you.”

Somewhere beyond him, the target is still moving.

The Rogue Genin notices where she looked.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “You've got somewhere to be.”

Wasabi's jaw tightens.

## KEEP PURSUING

Wasabi stops at the corner.

The student sees her.

The Rogue Genin sees her.

The target.

Still moving.

Wasabi's hands curl into fists.

**WASABI:** “Shut up.”

# SCENE 4D — THE INTERCEPT

Wasabi stops chasing the trail.

Cuts across two streets.

Behind the shops.

Over a wall.

Down a narrow lane.

Then she stops.

Actually stops.

Nothing.

Her foot taps.

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

**WASABI:** “Come on.”

Still nothing.

**WASABI:** “If I guessed wrong—”

Footsteps.

Wasabi freezes.

The target appears at the far end of the lane.

They stop too.

**TARGET:** “How did you—”

Wasabi's grin arrives immediately.

**WASABI:** “You were always going to extraction.”

The target turns around.

Wasabi throws her hands out.

**WASABI:** “Oh, no. You don't get to leave now!”

She runs.

This time, the target is the one trying to escape her.
