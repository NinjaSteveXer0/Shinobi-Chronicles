# 2. Existing Origin-team authority consumed

The Alpha opening creates exactly:

> **1 selected Origin protagonist + 2 distinct selected Academy teammates.**

Those two selected teammates become actual current-Chronicle participants from the legitimate Academy Team Formation point onward.

The current opening/team authority also explicitly allows later retention or reform of the squad. Therefore:

- the two Origin teammates do **not** become permanently welded to every future team formation;
- later replacement does **not** delete ownership or Shared History;
- later assignment elsewhere does **not** delete the person;
- a former teammate remains someone the protagonist actually began the Chronicle with;
- team membership remains autonomous and can change from committed Story/World/team history.

For forward Story shorthand, **Origin team** means the protagonist plus the exact two Academy teammates selected at the initial three-person formation, regardless of whether all three remain on the same formal team later.

The ten production Origin representations remain:

1. `academy_hinata`
2. `academy_izuno` — Wasabi Izuno
3. `academy_mirai`
4. `academy_menma`
5. `academy_kushina`
6. `academy_kurenai`
7. `academy_iwabee`
8. `academy_metal_lee`
9. `academy_kakashi`
10. `academy_obito`

The selected protagonist's own Origin occurs. Unselected Origin prologues do not occur off-screen merely because one of those representations later becomes a teammate.

# 3. Binding Origin-team forward-continuity rule

> **The player's original Academy trio must remain meaningful long-term Chronicle history and must be capable of entering the international Main Story as real participants rather than being discarded once later roster systems exist.**

Where each person remains alive, active, reachable and legitimately available:

- the protagonist may travel with one or both original teammates;
- one teammate may receive a foreign assignment while the protagonist travels elsewhere;
- both teammates may operate independently in separate countries;
- the trio may reunite abroad after having accumulated different Knowledge/relationships;
- later Story may consume what those teammates learned or did without converting it into protagonist Knowledge automatically;
- teammates may disagree politically with the protagonist or with one another;
- foreign contacts may form different relationships with each member of the original trio.

The Main Story should periodically recognise:

> **these were the people who began the Chronicle together, even if life stopped assigning them to the same team.**

Do not force a nostalgic reunion if current history says a teammate is dead, missing, hostile, assigned elsewhere, unavailable or otherwise legitimately absent.

Do not resurrect or teleport somebody merely because they were an Origin teammate.

---

# 4. Foreign-deployment requirement

The Canon Route must not keep the original team inside Konoha while the protagonist alone experiences the wider shinobi world.

Binding requirement for detailed Arc 4–8 authoring:

> **By the end of Arc 5, each still-legitimately-available original Origin teammate must have at least one materially authored foreign-deployment opportunity outside the Land of Fire.**

A materially authored foreign deployment means more than appearing in one travel montage or summit room. It should allow at least one of:

- autonomous mission responsibility;
- relationship formation with foreign actors;
- bounded Knowledge unavailable to the protagonist unless communicated;
- investigation/evidence contribution;
- political/diplomatic friction;
- World opportunity or hotspot participation;
- Battle caller/participant possibility where Combat authority supports it;
- a decision or action whose consequences survive beyond the local Mission.

Where both teammates remain legitimately available, detailed Arc 4–6 authoring should also create at least one credible opportunity for the **original trio to operate together outside the Land of Fire**, unless Recorded History makes that causally inappropriate.

This is a Story opportunity/authoring obligation, not a requirement that the player permanently reassign those people to their current formal squad.

# 5. Revisited CANON ROUTE geography

The original Arc 4–8 thematic spine remains intact, but its Canon Route is now explicitly world-mobile.

## ARC 4 — THE FOUNDERS RETURN

Arc 4 remains the historical-legitimacy crisis created by Hashirama and Tobirama's genuine living return.

Revision:

> **The founder return must begin generating on-the-ground foreign Story before Arc 4 ends.**

Konoha cannot own the interpretation of founder history. Old wars, diplomatic memories and foreign archives create legitimate reasons to leave Fire Country.

Preferred macro use, exact Mission placement TBD:

- one Origin-team member may accompany the protagonist into a Great-Nation historical/diplomatic investigation;
- the other may be sent on a separate verification/liaison/security assignment;
- foreign evidence can contradict Konoha's preferred version without automatically proving a conspiracy;
- returning teammates may possess different Knowledge and different impressions of the same crisis.

Arc 4 need not visit all four Great Nations, but it should begin breaking the assumption that `Main Story = Konoha with foreign visitors`.

## ARC 5 — THE WORLD THEY BUILT

Arc 5 becomes the principal **travelling-team / international-order Arc**.

Binding:

> **Land of Wind / Sunagakure, Land of Earth / Iwagakure, Land of Lightning / Kumogakure and Land of Water / Kirigakure must all receive substantive Canon-Route Main Story participation.**

The campaign should physically move through the shinobi world rather than solving Great-Nation politics entirely from Konoha.

The four countries must have distinct dramatic functions rather than existing as four copies of one summit plot.

### Wind / Sunagakure — survival, dependence, reform and weapon history

Potential Canon-Route functions, continuity permitting:

- desert/logistics/resource realities;
- Sand's own answer to village survival and dependence;
- Shukaku/Jinchūriki history as later Arc-6 groundwork;
- a country that has known both conflict and cooperation with Konoha;
- a useful environment for an Origin teammate to build independent foreign trust rather than acting only as protagonist support.

### Earth / Iwagakure — war memory, sovereignty and historical grievance

Potential functions:

- old-war evidence that does not flatter Konoha;
- hard sovereignty and distrust of Fire-country assumptions;
- founder-era consequences remembered differently outside Leaf archives;
- strategic/institutional continuity;
- Origin teammate investigation or negotiation where local actors may trust that teammate more—or less—than the protagonist.

### Lightning / Kumogakure — deterrence, military competence and strategic autonomy

Potential functions:

- a security-first Great Power that does not need Konoha's moral approval;
- testing whether Konoha's fractured legitimacy changes regional deterrence;
- Jinchūriki operational history as Arc-6 setup;
- missions where strength, preparedness and bargaining leverage matter differently from Konoha political rhetoric;
- a credible place for one teammate to return with respect for policies the protagonist dislikes.

### Water / Kirigakure — institutional brutality, reform and maritime fragmentation

Potential functions:

- maritime/island travel and access complications;
- reform after a violent institutional past where continuity supports it;
- clan/exile/non-state pressures;
- a different case study in what happens when a village's own system becomes dangerous to its people;
- separated Origin-team assignments across islands/ships/ports that later converge.

Arc 5 should leave the protagonist and Origin teammates with **different foreign relationships and memories**, not one shared `international reputation` value.

## ARC 6 — THE BEASTS IN CHAINS

Arc 6 now consumes the relationships built by actually travelling through the Great Nations.

The Tailed-Beast/Jinchūriki debate should not begin as an abstract Five-Kage meeting.

The player and relevant former/current teammates have already seen how different countries operate.

Origin-team use may include:

- teammate acting as trusted liaison to a host/village because of earlier Arc-5 history;
- teammate opposing protagonist policy on custody/refuge/coercive sealing;
- teammate protecting civilians while protagonist handles diplomatic or beast-level pressure;
- split missions where one member investigates seal architecture while another verifies political claims;
- trio reunion during a genuine multinational host/beast crisis.

Preserve:

**shared team history != shared opinion about Jinchūriki policy.**

## ARC 7 — THE LONG SHADOW

The Madara/Black-Zetsu long-game investigation must use the world now established.

Origin teammates can become especially valuable because they may possess independent foreign access the protagonist lacks.

Possible structures include:

- one old teammate already stationed/embedded/working in a Great Nation when a Madara-linked event emerges;
- protagonist receiving an incomplete warning from them;
- parallel investigations across different countries;
- teammate being the first observer of evidence without the protagonist automatically knowing it;
- reunion after each participant has independently reached different conclusions.

Madara's threat should feel capable of touching the world the team actually travelled through.

## ARC 8 — THE LAST DREAM

The final campaign should consume eight Arcs of relationship history.

The original trio should not automatically reform simply for a sentimental final shot.

Instead, exact current state determines whether:

- all three fight/operate together;
- one commands/supports another front;
- one opposes the protagonist's endgame answer;
- one is absent/dead/estranged;
- all three reunite after divergent histories;
- a former teammate's foreign relationships become strategically decisive.

The emotional payoff should be strongest precisely because their starting bond was real but **not frozen**.

# 6. Player not omnipresent

The Great-Nations campaign should deliberately exploit participant autonomy.

The protagonist does not need to personally witness every important foreign event.

Origin teammates may legitimately have:

- assignments elsewhere;
- conversations the protagonist was not present for;
- foreign relationships not automatically shared with the protagonist;
- knowledge not automatically transferred;
- successes/failures returned through legitimate reports, encounters or later evidence;
- changing opinions caused by experiences the player did not directly choose for them.

Preserve:

**NPC parallel occurrence != protagonist occurrence.**

**NPC Knowledge != protagonist Knowledge.**

**Origin teammate != player puppet.**

This gives Chronicle Engine real material for later reunion/disagreement without fabricating off-screen history arbitrarily.

This addendum does **not** override Acquisition/team lifecycle.

Preserve:

# 8. Canon-first anchors consumed

Current official Naruto material was rechecked before this revision.

Safe baseline anchors include:

- the Five Great Nations operate as distinct Kage-led sovereign military powers rather than one unified foreign bloc;
- Gaara/Sunagakure and Shukaku establish a major Sand/Jinchūriki historical identity;
- Killer Bee/Kumogakure establish a materially different example of Jinchūriki operational competence/control;
- Iwagakure carries distinct war-era history and sovereignty, including the Kannabi Bridge conflict line and Tsuchikage history;
- Kirigakure has distinct Mist institutions, trackers/swordsmen/maritime history and later reform/conflict material;
- canon repeatedly sends Leaf teams outside Fire Country for missions, investigation and development rather than keeping meaningful team growth inside Konoha alone.

These anchors establish identity/context only.

Exact Shinobi Chronicles-era Kage, hosts, deaths, alliances and political states remain future continuity-specific Story/Codex authoring.
