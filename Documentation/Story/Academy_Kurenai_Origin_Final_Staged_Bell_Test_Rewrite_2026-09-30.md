# PLAYER-FACING STORY

# SCENE 1 — AFTER CLASS

Most of the Academy has emptied out.

Kurenai is still in the practical yard.

Her instructor is putting away training markers near the wall.

A small bell hangs from one hand.

Kurenai notices it before the instructor says anything.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You can go home.”

**KURENAI:** “You said there might be another exercise.”

The instructor keeps stacking the markers.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “I said there might be.”

Kurenai looks at the bell.

**KURENAI:** “Then what's that for?”

The instructor turns.

She raises the bell between two fingers.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Come and find out.”

Kurenai leaves her bag by the edge of the yard and walks over.

The bell barely moves.

Her eyes go from the bell, to the instructor's hand, to her feet.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Take it.”

Kurenai looks up.

**KURENAI:** “That's all?”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “If that's not enough, you're already in trouble.”

Kurenai's attention returns to the bell.

# SCENE 2 — BATTLE OF ILLUSIONS

## CHOICE SET 1 — FIRST DECEPTION

### `kur_stage1`

How does Kurenai begin?

- **Send a false Kurenai**
- **Hide my real movement**
- **Distort her sense of distance**
- **Make the direct approach look real**

---

## IF: SEND A FALSE KURENAI

A second Kurenai breaks toward the bell.

The real Kurenai stays out of sight behind the movement.

The instructor's eyes follow the false approach for half a second.

Then stop.

She has not committed yet.

---

## IF: HIDE MY REAL MOVEMENT

Kurenai gives the instructor something obvious to track.

Footsteps.

A shoulder turning.

A clean line toward the bell.

Her real movement slips the other way.

The instructor turns with the visible approach.

Kurenai is already somewhere else.

---

## IF: DISTORT HER SENSE OF DISTANCE

Kurenai leaves the direction alone.

Only the distance changes.

The instructor steps toward her.

The ground seems to take less of that step than it should.

She notices.

Not enough to stop.

---

## IF: MAKE THE DIRECT APPROACH LOOK REAL

Kurenai rushes her.

No careful angle.

No visible setup.

The sort of approach the instructor should dismiss immediately.

Instead, the instructor moves to meet it.

That is what Kurenai wanted.

# CHOICE SET 2 — EXPLOIT THE REACTION

### `kur_stage2`

The instructor has reacted.

What does Kurenai do with it?

- **Rush the bell**
- **Draw her attention away**

---

## STAGE 2 PERFORMANCE — FALSE KURENAI + RUSH

The false Kurenai commits straight for the bell.

The instructor does not chase it.

Her attention snaps toward the space the real Kurenai is using.

Kurenai has to decide whether to force the attempt or change the layer.

---

## STAGE 2 PERFORMANCE — FALSE KURENAI + DRAW ATTENTION

The false Kurenai reaches past the bell instead of for it.

The instructor turns with the movement.

For the first time, her focus leaves the real approach.

Kurenai gets a narrow opening.

---

## STAGE 2 PERFORMANCE — HIDDEN MOVEMENT + RUSH

Kurenai uses the concealed angle immediately.

She closes on the bell before the instructor finishes turning.

The opening is real.

So is the risk of committing too soon.

---

## STAGE 2 PERFORMANCE — HIDDEN MOVEMENT + DRAW ATTENTION

Kurenai lets the visible movement keep travelling.

The instructor follows it.

The real Kurenai circles farther behind her.

The bell is close enough now that Kurenai can hear it move.

---

## STAGE 2 PERFORMANCE — DISTORTED DISTANCE + RUSH

Kurenai attacks the false distance she created.

The instructor reaches for a Kurenai who is not quite where she appears to be.

Kurenai gets inside her guard.

Her fingers brush the bell cord.

---

## STAGE 2 PERFORMANCE — DISTORTED DISTANCE + DRAW ATTENTION

Kurenai shifts the apparent distance again and lets another movement pull the instructor's eyes aside.

The instructor does not fully follow it.

But she has to check.

That costs her time.

---

## STAGE 2 PERFORMANCE — DIRECT APPROACH + RUSH

Kurenai doubles down.

She makes the obvious attack look even more obvious.

The instructor commits to stopping it.

Her hand closes toward Kurenai's wrist.

---

## STAGE 2 PERFORMANCE — DIRECT APPROACH + DRAW ATTENTION

At the last instant, Kurenai gives the instructor another movement to watch.

The instructor hesitates.

The direct attack is no longer quite as convincing.

Kurenai still has an opening, but it is smaller than she wanted.

Kurenai commits.

# SCENE 3 — THE RESULT

## COMPLETE LOSS

Kurenai's hand closes on empty air.

The false bell vanishes with it.

The instructor is still standing where Kurenai last expected her to be.

The real bell hangs from two fingers.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Too early.”

Kurenai looks at the failed layer.

Then at the bell.

**KURENAI:** “I showed you where to look.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You did.”

Kurenai's expression tightens.

She already wants the attempt back.

---

## PARTIAL LOSS

The bell jingles in Kurenai's hand.

The instructor turns.

Kurenai lets herself enjoy it for exactly one second.

The sound stops.

Her fingers are empty.

The real bell is still with the instructor.

**KURENAI:** “I had it.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You believed you had it.”

Kurenai looks at her empty hand.

That answer irritates her because it is accurate.

---

## PARTIAL WIN

Kurenai gets the bell.

The instructor's expression changes.

Only slightly.

Enough for Kurenai to know one of the layers worked.

Then the instructor is behind her.

Two fingers lift the bell away before Kurenai can turn.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Better.”

Kurenai looks over her shoulder.

**KURENAI:** “I took it.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You did.”

**KURENAI:** “Next time I keep it.”

---

## COMPLETE WIN

The instructor catches Kurenai before she reaches the bell.

Her hand closes around Kurenai's wrist.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Got you.”

The yard bends.

Kurenai is behind her with the bell between two fingers.

**KURENAI:** “Have you?”

The yard bends again.

The instructor is behind Kurenai.

The bell is back in her hand.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Yes.”

The yard folds one final time.

They are both standing exactly where the exercise began.

Same distance.

Same quiet courtyard.

Except Kurenai is holding the real bell.

She looks down at it.

Then up at the instructor.

**KURENAI:** “You were saying?”

The instructor almost smiles.

---

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Your first lie arrived before I had any reason to believe it.”

**KURENAI:** “Tomorrow.”

The instructor looks back.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You're assuming you get the same test.”

Kurenai looks at the bell.

**KURENAI:** “Not enough.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “No.”

Kurenai accepts that faster than she likes.

---

## PARTIAL WIN

The instructor holds the bell up.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You broke the first answer I had.”

**KURENAI:** “Then you found another one.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “That's the exercise.”

Kurenai watches the bell.

**KURENAI:** “Next time I make you find three.”

---

## COMPLETE WIN

The instructor holds out her hand.

Kurenai gives the bell back.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You kept track of the real one.”

**KURENAI:** “So did you.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Eventually.”

Kurenai smiles.

She does not need to say anything else.
