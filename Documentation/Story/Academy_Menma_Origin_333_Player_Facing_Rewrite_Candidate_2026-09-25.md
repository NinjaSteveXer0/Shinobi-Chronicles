# PLAYER-FACING STORY

## SCENE 1 — ACADEMY / RESTRICTION

**BACKDROP — CODING:** `Scene backdrops/academy_classroom.png`

Iruka drops Menma's practice sheet onto the desk.

Every mark is clean.

Menma looks at it.

Then at the stack still waiting beside Iruka.

**MENMA:** “Again?”

**IRUKA:** “Again.”

Menma leans back in his chair.

**MENMA:** “You already know I can do it.”

**IRUKA:** “Once isn't the same as knowing it.”

Menma's eyes move toward the cabinet at the side of the classroom.

Higher-level material.

Locked.

Iruka notices.

**IRUKA:** “Don't.”

Menma looks back at him.

**MENMA:** “I didn't move.”

**IRUKA:** “You were thinking loudly.”

A few students nearby laugh.

Menma does not.

**MENMA:** “You've seen what I can do.”

**IRUKA:** “I've seen some of it.”

**MENMA:** “Exactly.”

Iruka folds his arms.

**IRUKA:** “That wasn't agreement.”

Menma stands.

**IRUKA:** “Sit down.”

**MENMA:** “Why?”

**IRUKA:** “Because class isn't finished.”

Menma picks up his bag.

**MENMA:** “You keep saying I'm not ready.”

Iruka's expression changes.

Not softer.

More careful.

**IRUKA:** “I'm saying there's more between being talented and being ready than you think.”

Menma slings the bag over one shoulder.

**MENMA:** “Then maybe I need somewhere I can actually find out.”

He walks toward the door.

**IRUKA:** “Menma.”

Menma stops with his hand on the frame.

**IRUKA:** “If you walk out now, that's your decision.”

Menma glances back.

**MENMA:** “I know.”

He leaves.

## SCENE 2 — FOREST / FREEDOM

**BACKDROP — CODING:** `Scene backdrops/whisper_woods_forest_route.png`

The village drops away behind him.

Trees replace walls.

Branches replace ceilings.

No desks.

No locked cabinet.

No one telling him to slow down.

Menma runs.

Not because he needs to get anywhere.

Because he can.

He clears a fallen trunk without breaking stride.

Pushes harder.

Faster.

The path bends beneath him.

For the first time all morning, nobody is setting the pace except him.

Then—

A crack somewhere ahead.

Not wood.

Too heavy.

Menma slows.

Another impact follows.

Farther through the trees.

Birds burst from the canopy.

Menma stops.

The forest goes still around the sound.

---

## SCENE 3 — MENMA AND THE NINE-TAILS

**BACKDROP — CODING:** continue `Scene backdrops/whisper_woods_forest_route.png`

A familiar presence shifts somewhere behind Menma's thoughts.

**NINE-TAILS — INTERNAL:** “Interesting.”

Menma looks toward the trees ahead.

**MENMA — INTERNAL:** “You heard it.”

**NINE-TAILS:** “I felt it.”

Another impact rolls through the woods.

Closer.

The Nine-Tails' amusement presses against the edge of the silence.

**NINE-TAILS:** “You wanted something worth testing yourself against.”

Menma's mouth pulls into a small smile.

**MENMA:** “And?”

**NINE-TAILS:** “There it is.”

Menma starts forward.

**MENMA:** “You sound excited.”

A low, humourless rumble inside him.

**NINE-TAILS:** “I want to see whether your confidence is deserved.”

Menma's smile widens.

**MENMA:** “So do I.”

He runs toward the fighting.

---

## SCENE 4 — DISCOVERY

**BACKDROP — CODING:** `Scene backdrops/forest_clearing_day.png`

Menma reaches the edge of a clearing and drops behind the exposed roots of a fallen tree.

The fight is already underway.

Anko is in the middle of it.

Three opponents surround the clearing.

One is built wrong—too much mass over movements that still remember training.

Another moves in violent, uneven bursts, never quite settling into a rhythm.

The third still looks the most like a shinobi.

That somehow makes her worse.

Marks along exposed skin.

Old discipline under something damaged.

Anko turns through one attack and barely has time to meet the next.

Menma's eyes sharpen.

This is not an Academy exercise.

Nobody here is pulling a strike because somebody is twelve.

---

## SCENE 5 — THE DECISION TO ENTER

**BACKDROP — CODING:** continue `Scene backdrops/forest_clearing_day.png`

Inside him, the Nine-Tails watches.

**NINE-TAILS:** “Well?”

Menma looks at the clearing.

At Anko.

At the three opponents.

A grin touches one corner of his mouth.

**MENMA:** “Much better.”

He steps out from behind the roots.

Then jumps into the clearing.

---

## SCENE 6 — MENMA AND ANKO

**BACKDROP — CODING:** continue `Scene backdrops/forest_clearing_day.png`

Anko catches the movement.

Her head snaps toward him.

For half a second she simply stares.

**ANKO:** “Menma?”

Menma lands.

**ANKO:** “What the hell are you doing here?”

**MENMA:** “Finding out.”

Anko blocks another attack and throws the attacker off-line.

**ANKO:** “Wrong answer.”

Menma's eyes stay on the three test subjects.

**MENMA:** “You look busy.”

**ANKO:** “I was handling it.”

**MENMA:** “Then keep handling your side.”

Anko actually looks offended.

**ANKO:** “My side?”

The Altered Shinobi turns toward Menma.

The Brute shifts with her.

The Unstable Subject jerks around at the new movement.

The clearing changes.

Anko sees it happen.

So does Menma.

There is no more room for an argument.

**ANKO:** “Fine.”

She moves beside him.

**ANKO:** “Stay where I can see you.”

Menma settles into stance.

**MENMA:** “Keep up.”

Anko gives him a look that promises a conversation later.

The three test subjects close.

### ALL ROUTES CONVERGE

Anko looks toward the former test subjects.

Whatever edge was in her voice changes.

Not gone.

Different.

**ANKO:** “Do you know what they are?”

Menma looks toward them.

**MENMA:** “Orochimaru.”

Anko glances at him.

**ANKO:** “Close enough.”

Menma waits.

Anko does not immediately continue.

**ANKO:** “He was good at finding people who wanted more.”

**MENMA:** “I'm not them.”

Anko looks back at him.

**ANKO:** “Didn't say you were.”

**ANKO:** “I know that look.”

**MENMA:** “What look?”

**ANKO:** “The one that says everybody else is wasting your time because they won't let you near the interesting stuff.”

Menma folds his arms.

**MENMA:** “Maybe they are.”

Anko lets out one short laugh.

No humour in it.

**ANKO:** “Yeah.”

That answer catches Menma more than an argument would have.

Anko looks back at the test subjects.

**ANKO:** “I used to think that too.”

Menma watches her.

She does not explain herself just to fill the silence.

## SCENE 10 — FOREST / FUTURE

**BACKDROP — CODING:** `Scene backdrops/whisper_woods_forest_route.png`

Menma is running again.

The forest feels different now.

Not quieter.

Smaller.

He clears the same kind of fallen trunks that slowed him earlier without thinking about them.

Inside him, the Nine-Tails stirs.

**NINE-TAILS:** “Satisfied?”

Menma keeps running.

**MENMA:** “No.”

A low chuckle.

**NINE-TAILS:** “Good.”

Menma slows near the top of a rise.

Through the trees, Konoha is visible again.

The Academy is somewhere beyond the rooftops.

So are all the things they keep locked away until somebody decides he is ready.

Menma looks at the village.

Then farther past it.

### FINAL CHOICE — FUTURE AMBITION

**MASTER WHAT THEY WON'T TEACH ME**

**BECOME TOO STRONG TO HOLD BACK**

**CREATE SOMETHING THAT'S MINE**

**FIND OUT HOW FAR I CAN GO**

---

### MASTER WHAT THEY WON'T TEACH ME

Menma's eyes settle on the village.

**MENMA:** “If they won't teach me yet, I'll find out what I'm missing.”

The Nine-Tails is quiet for a moment.

**NINE-TAILS:** “Hungry.”

Menma smiles.

**MENMA:** “Ambitious.”

---

### BECOME TOO STRONG TO HOLD BACK

Menma looks down at his hands.

Then toward Konoha.

**MENMA:** “I'll get strong enough that nobody gets to decide I'm not ready.”

The Nine-Tails laughs softly.

**NINE-TAILS:** “That sounds familiar.”

**MENMA:** “Good.”

---

### CREATE SOMETHING THAT'S MINE

Menma looks back toward the forest.

Toward the clearing.

Toward everything he just saw.

**MENMA:** “Maybe I don't need what they're keeping from me.”

A pause.

**MENMA:** “Maybe I'll make something better.”

The Nine-Tails' interest sharpens.

**NINE-TAILS:** “Now that's more interesting.”

---

### FIND OUT HOW FAR I CAN GO

Menma looks beyond the village.

**MENMA:** “I want to know where the limit actually is.”

**NINE-TAILS:** “And when you find it?”

Menma starts running again.

**MENMA:** “I'll decide then.”
