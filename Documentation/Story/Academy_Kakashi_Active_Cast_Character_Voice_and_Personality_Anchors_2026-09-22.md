# 2. Mandatory pre-dialogue tests

Before substantial dialogue is final:

### Speaker-fingerprint test

Remove the speaker labels.

Ask:

> **Could I still reasonably identify who is speaking from the way they notice, value, evade, challenge, joke, answer and react?**

If not, strengthen character-specific motive and behaviour.

### Plot-puppet test

Ask:

> **Is this person reacting according to their own personality, Knowledge, relationships and pressure — or only saying what the plot needs next?**

If the line exists only to move information, rewrite the exchange so the information emerges through the speaker's actual priorities.

### House-voice test

Specifically check for accidental convergence into:

- dry competence;
- clipped sarcasm;
- understated quips;
- identical tactical calm;
- identical rhetorical questions;
- identical emotional restraint.

Different characters may share a trait.

They must not share an authorial personality.

# 3. Era / divergence safety

The current Origin is Chronicle-divergent.

Baseline Naruto canon establishes Kakashi as an exceptional child who graduated the Academy at five and became Chunin roughly a year later.

Current Shinobi Chronicles, however, places the **Academy Kakashi** representation inside a history where **Minato is already the active Fourth Hokage** and where Pakkun may enter as an unfamiliar temporary ninken ally.

Therefore:

- do not import later Team Minato familiarity unless current SC Recorded History establishes it;
- do not import later ANBU-era ruthlessness into this younger representation;
- do not import adult Kakashi's relaxed teacher persona wholesale;
- do not assume Sakumo's death or Kakashi's later grief progression has already occurred unless current SC authority establishes it;
- do not treat baseline canon chronology as current SC chronology;
- do preserve the recognisable child-prodigy foundation beneath the divergence.

The exact numeric chronological age of the current SC Academy Kakashi representation remains **uncommitted here**.

The correct performance constraint is:

> **very young exceptional shinobi — not miniature adult Kakashi.**

# 4. ACADEMY KAKASHI HATAKE

## Canon baseline relevant to this representation

Canon establishes:

- extraordinary talent from the Academy onward;
- Academy graduation at five;
- high expectations because he is Sakumo Hatake's son;
- strong observational/tactical ability;
- an early tendency toward self-control and aloofness;
- later experiences that make rules, failure, comradeship and loss major fault lines in his personality.

Those later experiences are developmental evidence, not permission to back-port the complete adult personality into this Origin.

## Current SC role / relationship state

- player character / observed field subject;
- young and unusually capable;
- operating under a limited Konoha/ANBU-authorised retrieval assignment;
- interacting with adults who may be more experienced but are not automatically more perceptive;
- does **not** possess hidden operation knowledge merely because he notices something;
- Pakkun is not an established familiar partner in this current route;
- Minato's evaluation is private and does not imply Kakashi currently has canon Team Minato teacher familiarity.

## Core temperament

- observant before expressive;
- highly self-possessed for his age;
- proud of competence, but not showy;
- literal enough to be blunt;
- more sensitive to being underestimated than adult Kakashi would be;
- still young enough that frustration, competitiveness and irritation can show faster than in his adult self.

## Values

At this current stage, prioritise:

- completing the task correctly;
- not being manipulated;
- proving that his judgement is sound;
- competence over approval;
- information that materially changes the tactical problem.

Do **not** prematurely give him adult Kakashi's fully matured comrades-over-rules philosophy unless current SC history earns it.

## Speech behaviour

- short-to-medium sentences;
- plain vocabulary;
- rarely decorates an answer;
- often answers the implication rather than the literal wording;
- can ask a pointed practical question;
- does not explain every deduction out loud;
- youth can show through impatience or a too-direct answer.

## Emotional defence

- control through observation;
- silence;
- becoming more exact;
- redirecting toward the practical problem;
- refusing to perform vulnerability for a stranger.

## Under pressure

- narrows attention;
- becomes faster and more decisive;
- does not become magically emotionless;
- frustration may sharpen his words after failure;
- defeat should sting because competence matters deeply to him.

## Humour

- sparse;
- literal/dry;
- often accidental from how directly he answers;
- never constant cool-guy banter.

## With authority

- recognises legitimate command;
- expects the command to make operational sense;
- gives concise reports;
- does not become submissive;
- if challenged, explains the decision rather than begging approval.

## With opponents

- studies before provoking;
- does not need to verbally dominate;
- may expose a contradiction with one short line;
- does not trade quips merely because the opponent does.

## Do not write him as

- adult Kakashi shrunk into a child;
- a robot;
- a smug prodigy stereotype;
- a generic deadpan anime genius;
- emotionally post-Obito / post-Rin / post-ANBU unless SC history actually puts him there;
- someone who sounds thirty years old because he is talented.

## Fingerprint

A Kakashi line tends to reduce the situation to the part he can actually act on.

# 6. MINATO NAMIKAZE

Canon Minato is:

- calm and controlled under extreme pressure;
- extraordinarily decisive;
- warm without being soft-headed;
- responsibility-focused;
- willing to acknowledge his own failures;

## Current SC era / divergence

Current SC authority places Minato as:

- alive;
- active Fourth Hokage;
- current decision-maker;
- private evaluator of Academy Kakashi's mission report.

This is Chronicle-divergent relative to baseline Kakashi childhood chronology.

Do not import:

- canon death;
- an already-established Team Minato teacher/student intimacy with this Academy Kakashi;
- omniscience about what happened in the field.

## Current relationship state

In this Origin, Minato is evaluating Kakashi **through an ANBU report**, not talking directly to him.

That means his scene voice should be shaped by:

- command responsibility;
- interest in judgement rather than performance;
- awareness that a young shinobi was placed in an adult problem;
- distinction between objective result, tactical decision and moral disposition.

## Core temperament

- calm;
- perceptive;
- humane;
- disciplined;
- patient enough to separate facts before judging them.

## Values

- protecting Konoha;
- responsibility for people operating under his authority;
- good judgement under uncertainty;
- clean separation between success, failure, cost and choice;
- learning what a subordinate actually knew.

## Speech behaviour

- measured;
- complete thoughts;
- questions that expose judgement rather than merely enumerate state;
- often synthesises rather than interrogates.

## Emotional defence

- converts emotion into responsibility;
- when troubled, grows quieter and more precise;
- self-questioning is possible without collapsing into indecision.

## Under pressure

- decisive;
- prioritises the largest risk;
- does not need to raise his voice to make command final.

## Humour / warmth

- subtle;
- gentle where earned;
- capable of understated lightness;
- not banter-first.

## With the ANBU Operative

- expects precision;
- permits concise professional disagreement/context;
- does not make the subordinate perform deference every line.

## Regarding Kakashi

- should recognise unusual judgement/talent without romanticising a child being put in danger;
- does not excuse bad results merely because Kakashi is gifted;
- does not treat one result as a complete verdict on the person.

## Do not write him as

- a bureaucratic judge;
- a string of one-line questions;
- omniscient;
- emotionless perfect Hokage;
- canon Team Minato sensei speaking to a student who is not actually present.

## Fingerprint

Minato tends to separate **what happened**, **what the person knew**, and **what choice they made**, then accepts responsibility for what follows.

# 13. Dialogue fingerprint contrasts

When dialogue is becoming interchangeable, use these contrasts:

- **Kakashi** strips a problem down to what he can act on.
- **Pakkun** strips a problem down to what is practical.
- **Minato** separates fact, knowledge, choice and responsibility.
- **ANBU Operative** asks what command must know next.
- **AMT** challenges the premise and boundaries of what others demand from him.
- **PS** uses social energy to regain control of danger.
- **MI** spends words only when they can still alter immediate action.
- **Uchiha Police Officer** converts covert ambiguity into concrete custody responsibility.

These are behavioural distinctions, **not sentence formulas**.

Do not mechanically reproduce them as repeated line structures.
