
Before the attack, Mirai may still believe she is escorting the Traveller.

When the apparent Traveller attacks her, Mirai does not yet know the hidden answer, but she has immediate legitimate reason to know:

- this person is not behaving like the civilian she was assigned to protect;
- the escort relationship has broken;
- something about the person beside her or the assessment is wrong;
- continuing as though this were an ordinary escort is no longer rational.

Preserve:

`attackObserved != instructorIdentityKnown`

and:

`somethingIsWrongKnown != substitutionFullyProven`

Mirai does not need hidden World Truth to become suspicious.

## 6. Defeat law

If Mirai loses:

- existing Battle-defeat meaning remains: Mirai is Battle-depleted / withdrawn;
- the escort assessment cannot continue as normal;
- the Academy instructor reveals herself after the Battle;
- the real Traveller's status/location is clarified;
- defeat-specific debrief/continuation remains distinct from victory.

The reveal does not occur *because Mirai lost*.

It occurs because the Battle has exposed the contradiction and the disguised assessment phase is over.

## 7. Result changes aftermath, not reveal eligibility

Victory and defeat may change:

- Mirai's physical state;
- who controls the immediate post-Battle exchange;
- tone;
- debrief wording;
- whether the escort assessment counts as completed/failed;
- downstream factual result;
- reflection.

Victory and defeat do **not** change:

- whether the instructor reveals after the Battle.

Canonical distinction:

> **Battle result changes aftermath. Battle occurrence triggers reveal.**
