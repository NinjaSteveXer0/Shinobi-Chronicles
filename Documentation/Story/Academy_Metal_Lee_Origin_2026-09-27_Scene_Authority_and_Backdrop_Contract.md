# 9. Protective-response outcome

MET-03 requires:

- `protectiveResponseAttempted`
- `protectiveResponseKind`
- `protectiveResponseOutcome`

Stephen's current scene authority requires the factual outcome to matter.

Writing therefore defines three result classes for every protective Action:

- **success**
- **partial**
- **failure**

The resolver is now closed at CE level as a **contextual capability resolver, not a PL Battle**.

Capability channels:
- `redirect_dummy` -> Taijutsu redirection;
- `take_impact` -> Stamina stopping capacity;
- `destroy_dummy` -> Taijutsu striking/control.

Exact deterministic success/partial/failure thresholds remain Combat-owned downstream.

MET-02 pressured-performance class must not be used as a direct MET-03 lookup.

No RNG, confidence/anxiety stat, Protector Trait or morality score is authorised.

## REDIRECT THE DUMMY

### success
Metal cleanly changes the dummy's path and sends it into an empty part of the training area.

Student is protected by Metal's action.

### partial
Metal changes the path enough to prevent the direct hit, but another person must finish clearing/stopping the hazard.

Metal made a real causal contribution but did not resolve it alone.

### failure
Metal attempts the redirect but does not change the path enough.

Another legitimate actor/intervention prevents the threatened student from being hit.

Do not credit Metal with successful protection.

## TAKE THE IMPACT

### success
Metal gets between the dummy and the student and absorbs/stops the moving apparatus sufficiently to protect them.

No automatic injury is inferred merely from the word impact.

### partial
Metal gets into the line and removes most of the danger, but another person must help stop/clear the apparatus.

Metal made a real causal contribution without sole resolution.

### failure
Metal commits to the intercept but does not reach/control the hazard in time.

Another legitimate intervention protects the student.

Do not infer cowardice or incompetence.

## DESTROY THE DUMMY

### success
Metal's strike destroys/stops the dangerous apparatus before it reaches the student.

### partial
Metal breaks/deflects enough of it to reduce the danger, but another person must finish the protection.

### failure
Metal's strike does not stop the dangerous path.

Another legitimate intervention prevents harm.

## Injury boundary

Do not infer injury to:
- Metal;
- threatened student;
- Genin;
- other observers;

unless a separately resolved outcome explicitly establishes it.
