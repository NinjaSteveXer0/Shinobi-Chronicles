# Shinobi Chronicles — Interactive Story Player Agency, Character Autonomy, Cadence and Earned Possibility Authority

**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Owner:** Writing — Konoha  
**Status:** **BINDING WRITING / STORY AUTHORING AUTHORITY — CE/CHRONICLE STATE CONSUMER; IMPLEMENTATION / RUNTIME VALIDATION SEPARATE**

---

## 1. Purpose

This document locks the player-facing authored-story grammar used by Shinobi Chronicles so that future Writing work, chat succession and runtime compression do not regress into passive fanfiction, static NPC archetypes, party-hive behaviour or predetermined morality routes.

It consumes existing Chronicle Engine / Structured Story Autonomy authority and applies it at the Writing surface.

Core principle:

> **The Origin Character is the player's character. The player is in the driving seat for meaningful protagonist decisions. The world, NPCs and consequences remain autonomous.**

For the current developer-authoring Chronicle, Stephen controls Menma's meaningful intent. At runtime, the end user controls their own Origin Character under the same contract.

---

## 2. Player agency contract

At every meaningful protagonist decision:

1. Story establishes the current situation from legitimate World Truth, observer Knowledge and presentation.
2. NPCs may independently react, form intent or act where their own Knowledge, role, personality and history justify it.
3. Where another participant's intended response is reasonably perceivable before protagonist commitment, Story should surface that intent before the player chooses.
4. Story presents the player with understandable choices when useful, while always permitting a legitimate player-authored alternative where the interface/runtime supports it.
5. **STOP. The player chooses the Origin Character's intent.**
6. Player intent is resolved through the appropriate authority; intent is not guaranteed occurrence.
7. The resolved occurrence becomes Chronicle history/evidence and may change later Knowledge, Memory, Relationships, Disposition, trust, access, opportunity and route affinity.

Hard boundaries:

- protagonist choice != party command;
- player intent != guaranteed occurrence;
- selected dialogue option != automatic World Truth;
- choosing violence != guaranteed death;
- choosing restraint != guaranteed safety;
- choosing detention != automatic custody;
- mission completion != Progression automatically;
- Battle victory != Promotion or universal reward.

The player may persuade, inspire, frighten, anger, disappoint or impress another character. They do not possess direct ownership of that character's mind.

---

## 3. Autonomous characters — no permanent party hive

Every NPC remains a Chronicle person with their own:

- Knowledge and uncertainty;
- relationships and memories;
- loyalties and obligations;
- fears and ambitions;
- capabilities and limitations;
- moral flexibility or inflexibility;
- current emotional state;
- history with the protagonist and other people;
- institutional, clan, family, team and political pressures.

Characters may agree with the Origin Character, disagree, refuse, split off, cooperate reluctantly, act first, change their mind or later reinterpret the same event.

A teammate agreeing now does **not** guarantee agreement later.

A rival or enemy now does **not** guarantee permanent hostility.

Friendship, rivalry, resentment, loyalty, romance, betrayal, respect, fear, dependence, ideological alignment and practical cooperation are all **history-backed states/trajectories**, not immutable labels.

A relationship may legitimately travel from hostility to trust, or trust to fracture, when Chronicle history earns that change.

> **What happens matters.**

The game should preserve provenance for meaningful changes rather than snapping characters to a predetermined plot requirement.

---

## 4. Personality continuity without personality imprisonment

Writing must preserve recognisable identity and voice anchors, but must not treat a character's starting personality as an unchangeable script.

### Identity / voice anchors

Characters should remain distinguishable through combinations of:

- sentence length and rhythm;
- vocabulary;
- humour style;
- directness / evasiveness;
- confidence;
- body language;
- emotional visibility;
- tactical habits;
- social habits;
- tolerance for risk;
- silence and hesitation;
- facial reaction;
- physical expression;
- habitual questions, assumptions and priorities.

Do not distribute one character's cadence or humour across the whole cast.

### Chronicle evolution

CE / committed Chronicle history may legitimately make a character:

- more or less trusting;
- more confident or more guarded;
- more merciful or more ruthless;
- more politically engaged;
- more fearful or more reckless;
- more loyal or more resentful;
- more willing to deceive, kill, protect, forgive, compromise or defect;
- closer to or more distant from the protagonist;
- more morally flexible in one context while remaining rigid in another.

Such change requires provenance in lived history, relationships, evidence and consequences. Writing must not invent a personality reversal merely to make a scene convenient.

> **Character continuity means recognisable causality, not permanent stasis.**

## 6. Player-facing cadence — interactive story, not a fanfic wall

Shinobi Chronicles should deliver story in **compact interactive beats**.

Writing may use fuller prose during authoring, but the intended player-facing rhythm is:

> **scene beat -> character reactions -> autonomous NPC intent -> player decision -> legitimate resolution -> consequence / next beat**

Guidelines:

- prefer short exchanges and actionable revelations over long passive monologues;
- create frequent meaningful stop points when the protagonist could reasonably form an opinion, ask a question, choose a priority or act;
- do not force a decision every two sentences merely for UI frequency; choices must matter or meaningfully express Chronicle intent;
- do not let ten minutes of exposition pass when the player could already make a legitimate decision;
- NPCs should often express their own intended course before the Origin Character commits, where perceivable;
- after a substantial clue or disagreement, consider whether the player should be allowed to interpret/respond before Story continues;
- move toward investigation, traversal, opportunity, Battle and consequence rather than keeping the player trapped in dialogue;
- runtime compression may shorten authored prose while preserving causal facts, personality, choice points and consequence meaning.

The game should feel like the player is **participating in events**, not reading a novel about somebody who happens to share their name.

## 7. Body language and presentation

Personality must not exist only in dialogue text.

Use, where natural:

- changes in posture;
- eye movement;
- facial expression;
- tightening / relaxing hands or jaw;
- sighs, gasps, grunts, groans, laughter, swallowed reactions and silence;
- physical distance;
- fidgeting or stillness;
- interrupted speech;
- movement toward or away from people/objects;
- habitual gestures;
- refusal to answer;
- reactions characters attempt to hide.

Do not mechanically attach a gesture/noise to every line. Non-verbal expression should clarify character state and cadence, not become decorative spam.

Silence can be a complete reaction.

---

## 8. Moral flexibility and disagreement

There is no universal morality personality assigned merely by character identity.

CE / Chronicle history may produce different levels of moral flexibility for different people and different Chroncles.

A character may support a hard action in one context and reject a superficially similar action later because:

- the target is different;
- Knowledge changed;
- trust changed;
- consequences from an earlier choice changed them;
- a relationship matters;
- institutional obligations differ;
- they believe the protagonist crossed a line;
- they have become more or less willing to cross that line themselves.

This is not inconsistency when the causal history supports it.

Do not reduce route or personality to hidden morality points.

Record what happened and why; allow later route affinity and relationship state to emerge from provenance-bearing history.

---

## 9. Knowledge / autonomy / consequence boundary

Preserve:

> **World Truth != observer Knowledge != presentation.**

A character's belief may be wrong.

Multiple intelligent characters may legitimately reach different conclusions from the same evidence because their Knowledge, priorities and histories differ.

NPC action should be based on what that NPC can legitimately know, infer or believe.

The Origin Character can disagree with everyone present and propose another course.

Story must not manufacture hidden Knowledge merely so an NPC can deliver the next plot beat.

## 10. Earned possibilities — Echo is not Menma-exclusive

The Echo / hosted-entity style scenario is **not permanently reserved for Menma**.

Menma is currently unusually well positioned for that possibility because his committed base capabilities/history legitimately support it. That is Chronicle advantage, not exclusive protagonist privilege.

Any Origin Character may become eligible for an Echo/hosted-entity route or analogous authored opportunity **if they have legitimately earned the required state** through the owning systems and Chronicle history.

Eligibility may depend on combinations of things such as:

- relevant capabilities / Stats / technique competence;
- survival or containment history;
- Fūinjutsu or compatible Access/Competence where required;
- specific relationships or witnesses;
- prior mission choices;
- exposure to the relevant phenomenon;
- Knowledge;
- physical / spiritual / narrative compatibility defined by the owning authority;
- successful opportunity resolution.

Hard boundaries:

- authored possibility != universal entitlement;
- eligibility != success;
- latent potential != Access;
- Access != Competence;
- Competence != Power;
- Menma's current route does not grant every protagonist a cosmetic substitute;
- another Origin Character who genuinely earns the necessary conditions must not be barred merely because the first developer Chronicle reached the opportunity through Menma.

Story may expose the opportunity only when CE/current production authorities establish legitimate eligibility and causal access.

## 11. Arc 2 Mission 2 application

For **THE LEAK** specifically:

- opening begins with Menma + Mikoto in Konoha streets hearing conflicting gossip about the prior Sazan operation / recent mission aftermath;
- Anko recalls them to the Hokage office;
- Minato and Shikaku frame the problem as classified information/rumours escaping through multiple channels;
- ANBU is the natural counter-intelligence/internal-security reference point in dialogue, while ROOT remains a distinct organisation and must not be collapsed into ANBU;
- ANBU Headquarters and ROOT Headquarters remain separate concealed locations whose player-facing availability depends on legitimate discovery/Knowledge;
- Anko may raise the possibility of investigating ANBU and the ROOT rumours without Story declaring either guilty;
- Shizune is not the Hokage's assistant in this continuity; she is Genin Shizune and appears only through legitimate team/Chronicle participation;
- Mikoto and Hinata independently notice different patterns in the rumour set rather than jointly functioning as exposition machinery;
- NPC conclusions/intended investigative preferences should be surfaced before Menma's first major choice where reasonably perceivable;
- Menma then chooses whose interpretation/course he agrees with, combines them, rejects them or proposes his own theory/route;
- Story stops for that choice;
- later investigation should move toward field action and real Battle pressure rather than another prolonged dialogue wall.

### Rumour-analysis differentiation

Current recommended authored distinction:

- **Mikoto** notices social/vocabulary/source differences — different narratives are being seeded to different audiences;
- **Hinata** notices factual blind spots / Knowledge-boundary differences — the sources do not possess the same underlying information;
- **Shikaku** can translate independent observations into strategic implications without taking the player's decision away;
- **Anko** reacts from field instinct and suspicion, including the obvious ANBU/ROOT institutional danger;
- **Menma** remains free to agree, combine, challenge or originate another conclusion.

## 12. Durable authoring rule

When future chats or specialists inherit Writing work, they should recover this authority before continuing interactive authored Story.

Do not regress to:

- fixed protagonist decisions;
- passive text-wall delivery;
- NPC hive-mind obedience;
- static anime personalities regardless of Chronicle history;
- universal morality scores;
- hard route labels disguised as dialogue;
- Menma-exclusive authored possibilities when another Origin Character legitimately earns equivalent eligibility;
- consequence-free choices.

Canonical shorthand:

> **The player chooses the Origin Character. NPCs choose themselves. CE resolves what the world permits. History changes what everyone may choose next.**
