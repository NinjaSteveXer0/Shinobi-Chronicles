## 2. Production rules for this proposal

1. **Each Mission has exactly 12 Sections.**
2. A Section is a pacing unit, not automatically one dialogue scene, Battle, location, event or choice.
3. **Meaningful decisions only.** Connective dialogue, NPC reactions, travel and routine investigation should be compressed rather than forcing constant choices.
4. Player-facing choices represent protagonist **intent**, not guaranteed outcome.
5. NPCs/factions remain autonomous and Knowledge-bounded.
6. Main Story creates authored functions and destinations; CE binds legitimate Chronicle-specific causal carriers.
7. Kusa and Ame are mandatory Arc-2 destinations.
8. M4 onward becomes materially more dangerous and less institutionally controlled.
9. Arc 2 creates political/foreign history for Arc 3; it does not maintain hidden route points.
10. Minato remains current Hokage. Sasuke becomes a credible challenger with substantial support through Arc 2; his support must be earned rather than announced.
11. Supporting Minato or Sasuke never automatically assigns a route family.
12. Uchiha, Sarutobi, ANBU, clans, teams and families are not political hive minds.
13. No single operative must explain the entire information-war campaign.
14. Strong unresolved threads may become `WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE`; Main Story does not have to solve every loose end it creates.
15. Exact Battle actions, PL, rewards, Progression, Rank, acquisition, World host IDs and implementation remain with their owning systems.

# ARC 2 / MISSION 4 — THE EMPTY CELL

## Universal mission function

Convert the early Arc's abstract response-model problem into a direct contradiction inside Konoha's own custody/authority machinery.

The authored invariant is that one legitimately secured **person** tied to the current Arc-2 investigation is discovered missing from custody under an apparently authorised transfer/removal story.

CE/Story binds the exact person from legitimate Chronicle history. The Menma developer Chronicle uses the older ROOT-linked woman captured in A2M3. Another Chronicle must use a real prior detainee/witness/operative; if none exists, that is a causal bridge/content gap to solve rather than permission to fabricate history.

## 12 Sections

1. **Custody contradiction.** The secured person is missing; official-looking records say they were moved. Immediate evidence does not establish escape, extraction, death, cooperation or actual transfer.
2. **Identity/Knowledge challenge.** Claimed custodians, witnesses and/or an informed detainee/contact are tested without feeding them unnecessary new information. The transfer narrative begins to show inconsistency.
3. **Reconstruct material reality.** Physical cell/route evidence, record timestamps, witness memory and duty continuity are compared. At least one element of the official transfer story cannot all be true at once.
4. **Trace information access.** Investigation asks who could know current operational identities, routes, shift data, custody procedures or recognition markers well enough to make the false story plausible.
5. **Konoha reacts.** ANBU/Hokage/security resources move independently according to their own Knowledge and roles. This movement becomes visible and itself creates information.
6. **The second pressure.** A decoy, attempted evidence destruction, false sighting, secondary breach or targeted field incident exploits the investigation's movement and proves that somebody anticipated at least part of Konoha's response.
7. **Priority decision.** The protagonist must choose what matters most now: pursue the active actor, protect remaining custody/evidence, shadow the apparent escape route, or trace the operational-information leak.
8. **Tactical resolution.** Pursuit/infiltration/Battle/counter-surveillance resolves the selected priority through owning systems. Success does not automatically reveal the mastermind or missing person's fate.
9. **External footprint.** Recovered factual evidence establishes that the custody event intersects a Land-of-Fire transit, finance, courier, supply or communication route outside the holding annex.
10. **Narrative reconstruction.** The protagonist/Konoha can now prove that the transfer story was constructed to shape investigation. Whether the missing person cooperated, was taken or remains otherwise unresolved may legitimately stay open.
11. **Next causal lead.** The strongest verified external footprint points toward a Fire-country field route that cannot be solved from a desk in Konoha.
12. **Closing Window.** Bounded final options may include disclosure scope, final custody questions, evidence compartmentalisation or reporting. Mission completion commits on departure/report/explicit exit into the field operation.

## Neutral meaningful decision windows

### Decision A — first investigation priority
- inspect physical custody scene / seals / route;
- audit records and transfer authority;
- question a bounded witness/detainee before updating their Knowledge;
- trace/locate the claimed custodians;
- another legitimate capability-backed approach.

### Decision B — when the response is exploited
- pursue the active actor;
- protect remaining people/evidence;
- shadow the false route rather than stop it immediately;
- trace who had access to the operational information;
- withdraw/reposition if current risk legitimately demands it.

### Decision C — closing disclosure
- give Konoha the full verified external lead;
- provide enough to continue while protecting a legitimate sensitive source/secret;
- request compartmentalised handling;
- retain a lawful/credible piece of leverage where current history permits;
- custom disclosure/concealment intent subject to consequence.

## Required outputs

- transfer/custody story proven unsafe to interpret naively;
- at least one real Konoha process/identity data source was observed, copied, leaked or modelled somehow — exact mechanism may remain unknown;
- field lead into Land of Fire;
- no single actor automatically explains the Arc;
- missing person may remain an optional off-shoot if not required immediately.

## Current Menma overlay

Current played Menma material can map naturally into this proposal:

- Section 1: older ROOT-linked woman's empty cell + supposed ANBU custodians / copied operational presentation;
- Section 2: Sai clean-state interrogation, including `Sometimes the response is the objective.`

The Sealed Wall remains Menma-specific and does not define universal M4 completion.

### WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE candidates
- missing detainee's ultimate fate if not resolved in Main Story;
- copied ANBU presentation / operational-identity provenance;
- any surviving forged-transfer tool/material/source that warrants its own World chain.

# ARC 2 / MISSION 5 — THE BURNED ROAD

## Universal mission function

Move the Arc physically beyond Konoha and prove that the destabilisation depends on external logistics, routes, money, couriers, safe sites or communications.

This is the first mission where the protagonist should feel materially farther from institutional protection and where preparation, equipment, contacts and development can matter without becoming mandatory grind gates.

## 12 Sections

1. **Field briefing and preparation.** The M4 lead identifies a Fire-country relay/courier/waystation corridor. Team/participants are selected from legitimate current state.
2. **Departure.** The protagonist leaves Konoha and detects signs that the route is being watched, cleared or abandoned ahead of them.
3. **Burned relay.** The expected node has been destroyed, evacuated or sanitised shortly before arrival. Destruction itself becomes evidence of response anticipation.
4. **Three surviving threads.** A fleeing courier/mover, vulnerable witness/local contact, and recoverable physical/evidence trail each offer different value.
5. **Priority decision.** The protagonist chooses pursuit, protection/evidence preservation, or covert shadowing rather than being allowed to obtain every advantage.
6. **Field chase / movement.** Terrain and time make the choice costly. Other threads may degrade, move or be resolved by autonomous actors.
7. **Real tactical pressure.** Ambush/interception/Battle or equivalent hostile field resolution occurs. Battle victory does not itself secure every witness or document.
8. **Aftermath evidence.** The resolved route exposes a Kusa-facing logistics, payment, courier or contact chain without proving Kusagakure's government is responsible.
9. **Outside perspective.** A civilian, trader, shinobi contact, border actor or local authority provides bounded information showing that Konoha's crisis already has meaning outside the village.
10. **Operational posture decision.** Continue toward Kusa overtly under Konoha authority, use a local/foreign intermediary, or approach more covertly where current access allows.
11. **Bridge secured.** Exact route/credential/contact/evidence becomes sufficient for World to author legitimate Story access into Land of Grass.
12. **Closing Window / departure.** Final preparation, reporting or bounded local action may occur before the protagonist commits to the Kusa leg.

## Neutral meaningful decision windows

### Decision A — relay aftermath
- pursue the courier/operative;
- secure/protect the witness;
- preserve/search the evidence site;
- shadow the route and allow a target to move;
- capability-backed alternative.

### Decision B — border posture
- proceed overtly as Konoha shinobi;
- use a legitimate local intermediary/contact;
- proceed covertly where capability/access permits;
- request additional institutional support at the cost of visibility/time;
- abandon one lead to protect another commitment.

## Required outputs

- external Fire-country infrastructure proven material;
- real tactical cost beyond Konoha walls;
- legitimate Kusa bridge;
- protagonist may create a Fire-country contact, enemy, debt or unresolved survivor;
- news from Konoha may establish the first visible domestic argument over how the custody/security failures should be handled. Sasuke may begin pressing an alternative accountability/security position, but support remains developing rather than dominant.

### WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE candidates
- surviving courier;
- burned relay provenance;
- local Fire-country contact/debt;
- abandoned evidence thread the player did not prioritise.

# ARC 2 / MISSION 6 — THE GRASS LINE

## Universal mission function

Enter Kusa and prove that Konoha cannot command the political board outside Fire Country.

Grass actors must have their own Knowledge, motives, limits and grievances. The mission should create at least one durable Kusa relationship and one external evidence chain that changes how the protagonist understands the crisis.

## 12 Sections

1. **Crossing into Grass.** Story grants only the exact access/Knowledge needed for the authored destination, not blanket regional discovery.
2. **The expected contact is changed.** Missing, watched, compromised, unwilling, reassigned or politically constrained; Konoha's original plan cannot simply be followed as written.
3. **Approach decision.** The protagonist chooses an official Kusa channel, a local/civilian/broker channel, or a covert observation/infiltration route when legitimately available.
4. **Partial truth.** Each route reveals different bounded facts rather than one route being objectively correct.
5. **Kusa is not one actor.** Local interests diverge: officials, shinobi, civilians, brokers, criminal/mercenary routes or other authored participants may want different outcomes.
6. **Source under pressure.** A source/contact/evidence custodian with knowledge of the Fire-facing network becomes exposed or targeted.
7. **Priority decision.** Protect the source, pursue the attacker, secure the evidence, or manipulate the pursuit to learn more.
8. **Resolver seam.** Battle, pursuit, stealth, negotiation or escape resolves what actually survives.
9. **Ame-facing clue.** The aftermath reveals a legitimate Rain/Ame intermediary, route, payment chain, witness or intelligence exchange without yet solving the foreign network.
10. **Relationship consequence.** Kusa actors remember how the protagonist operated: respectful, coercive, covert, destructive, protective, deceptive or otherwise. No universal morality label is applied.
11. **Deeper Grass lead.** A protected/exposed person or evidence package becomes central enough to require a second Kusa mission before leaving for Ame.
12. **Closing Window.** Return to a safe base/contact or commit to the next Kusa operation; bounded final disclosure/relationship actions remain possible.

## Neutral meaningful decision windows

### Decision A — who to work through
- official Kusa authority;
- local broker/civilian/merchant/contact;
- covert independent investigation;
- split information between channels without assuming cooperation;
- custom legitimate route.

### Decision B — when the source is hit
- protect the source/person;
- pursue the attacker;
- secure evidence/object;
- let the attacker run under surveillance;
- withdraw with what is already known.

### Decision C — information sharing
- share verified Konoha evidence with the Kusa counterpart;
- share selectively to protect a source/secret;
- demand reciprocal information first;
- keep evidence private and accept the trust cost.

## Required outputs

- Kusa is materially involved in the causal map without being declared collectively guilty;
- at least one Kusa relationship/obligation/enmity is created;
- legitimate Ame-facing clue exists;
- next mission centres the human/political cost of carrying that clue forward.

### WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE candidates
- Kusa contact relationship;
- local faction problem unrelated to Main Story completion;
- surviving attacker/mercenary;
- regional evidence/history discovered but not needed for the Ame bridge.

# ARC 2 / MISSION 7 — THE PRICE OF SHELTER

## Universal mission function

Turn foreign evidence into a personal/operational obligation. The protagonist cannot simply collect information and leave; someone or something carrying the next bridge needs protection, extraction, negotiated custody or another costly arrangement.

This mission manufactures durable Kusa history for Arc 3.

## 12 Sections

1. **The carrier/source.** A legitimate person/evidence custodian can connect the investigation toward Ame, but has their own terms, fears and priorities.
2. **Competing claims.** Konoha, one or more Kusa actors and hostile/third-party interests all have reasons to control, question, protect, expose or silence the source.
3. **Safe location becomes unsafe.** Surveillance, political pressure or direct attack means staying still is no longer viable.
4. **Extraction/arrangement decision.** Choose route, decoy, official handover, local protection, negotiation or another legitimate plan.
5. **Movement.** The plan unfolds; NPCs may cooperate, refuse or alter behaviour from their own history.
6. **Interception.** A hostile or competing force attacks, blocks, bargains or attempts seizure.
7. **Real tactical resolution.** Battle/chase/escape/negotiation through owning resolvers. Protecting the source and defeating the attacker remain separate outcomes.
8. **Source autonomy.** The source may refuse Konoha custody, demand protection for someone else, seek asylum/payment/immunity, or otherwise act as a person rather than a quest item.
9. **Second meaningful decision.** Respect the source's terms, pressure/coerce where legally/capably possible, abandon the arrangement, or secure the Ame bridge through another factual carrier already exposed by the mission.
10. **Ame bridge survives through history.** Testimony, route evidence, attacker provenance, a recovered packet or another exact causal result provides the Rain destination. Failure of one carrier does not permit fabricated history; the mission must author legitimate fallbacks from facts already present.
11. **Konoha instruction collides with the promise made in Grass.** The protagonist must reconcile institutional duty with the arrangement/relationship they actually created.
12. **Closing Window / leave Grass.** Final custody, protection, disclosure or promise decisions commit; protagonist departs toward Ame/Rain with durable Kusa consequences.

## Neutral meaningful decision windows

### Decision A — extraction method
- official protected transfer;
- covert route;
- decoy/misdirection;
- negotiate local shelter rather than moving;
- allow a pursuer to follow under controlled observation.

### Decision B — source autonomy
- honour the source's condition;
- negotiate a compromise;
- insist on Konoha custody/authority and accept consequences;
- walk away/use alternate evidence;
- custom relationship-backed solution.

### Decision C — final Kusa obligation
- disclose the full arrangement to Konoha;
- protect a legitimate promise/source detail;
- place the source with a trusted Kusa actor;
- take direct responsibility where the protagonist can actually do so.

## Required outputs

- legitimate Ame route;
- at least one durable foreign relationship, obligation, betrayal or enemy;
- Konoha authority shown to have limits abroad;
- the player's treatment of people/evidence becomes later political history rather than a morality score.

### WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE candidates
- protected/abandoned source's later life;
- Kusa debt or retaliation;
- local safehouse/host relationship;
- unresolved pursuer or competing claimant.

# ARC 2 / MISSION 8 — UNDER THE RAIN

## Universal mission function

Enter Ame and reveal that the crisis is sustained by an **information economy**, not merely one leak or one enemy cell.

Actors may buy, sell, alter, suppress, verify or weaponise Konoha information for unrelated motives.

## 12 Sections

1. **Arrival in Rain/Ame.** The environment and political/intelligence pressure immediately reduce Konoha's normal freedom of movement.
2. **Observation pressure.** The protagonist notices or is informed that outsiders asking the wrong questions are tracked, taxed, redirected or tested.
3. **Contact protocol fails.** The expected bridge from Kusa is incomplete, compromised or only one of several possible channels.
4. **Channel decision.** Use an official Ame-facing channel, an underground/intermediary channel, or independent observation where legitimate.
5. **Information market revealed.** Multiple actors possess different versions of Konoha events; some are true, false or deliberately incomplete.
6. **The trade.** A source/intermediary offers material intelligence in exchange for evidence, a favour, protection, money, access or another contextually legitimate price.
7. **Response decision.** Trade, refuse, shadow the buyer/seller, steal/intercept where legitimate, or try to verify independently.
8. **Resolver seam.** Negotiation, stealth, chase, exposure or Battle produces factual consequences; no route guarantees clean acquisition.
9. **Network node identified.** A larger exchange/site/person/communication system is discovered as the place where Konoha reaction data and political information converge.
10. **Konoha-facing destinations.** Evidence shows material is travelling back toward more than one Fire/Konoha interest. Exact recipient identities remain bounded by what was actually recovered.
11. **Transmission decision.** Warn Konoha immediately with incomplete information, hold until verified, send only urgent facts, or use another trusted channel created by Chronicle history.
12. **Closing Window.** Commit to locating/entering the major exchange that becomes Mission 9; optional bounded Ame contact actions may remain.

## Neutral meaningful decision windows

### Decision A — Ame channel
- official/diplomatic contact;
- underworld/intelligence intermediary;
- independent surveillance/investigation;
- use a prior foreign relationship if legitimately earned.

### Decision B — information trade
- pay/provide requested consideration;
- refuse and seek another route;
- shadow the intermediary;
- seize evidence through legitimate tactical intent and accept political cost;
- bargain another obligation.

### Decision C — warn Konoha or verify
- transmit immediately;
- verify first;
- transmit only the urgent minimum;
- route through a trusted nonstandard contact rather than the normal chain.

## Required outputs

- information marketplace/network proven;
- multiple motives/actors, no universal mastermind collapse;
- exact major exchange target for M9;
- some Konoha-facing recipients/consumers become knowable, but only through actual evidence.

### WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE candidates
- Ame intermediary relationship;
- unpaid obligation/debt;
- side buyer/seller whose identity is irrelevant to Main Story now;
- hidden packet/rumour whose later relevance depends on player action.

# ARC 2 / MISSION 9 — THE MARKET OF TRUTHS

## Universal mission function

Strike or infiltrate the information exchange and prove that competing actors are trading **truth, falsehood and response data** for different goals.

This is the foreign tactical climax of Arc 2 and should create the evidence that sends the protagonist back to Konoha with urgency.

## 12 Sections

1. **Approach to the exchange.** Protagonist establishes observation/infiltration/assault posture from current Knowledge and capabilities.
2. **Multiple buyers and sellers.** Participants from different interests interact; none automatically knows the whole network.
3. **Mixed-information proof.** The protagonist sees/recovers evidence that some packets are accurate, some false and some intentionally incomplete.
4. **Target decision.** Prioritise records, a source/seller, a buyer/recipient, or the live meeting/communication channel.
5. **Execution.** The chosen approach begins; other targets may move or become inaccessible.
6. **Counter-intelligence closes.** The exchange detects Konoha/protagonist interest and springs a trap, evacuation, burn protocol or counterattack.
7. **Major tactical resolution.** Real PL Battle/infiltration/escape/pursuit seam. Battle result remains distinct from evidence/custody outcomes.
8. **Aftermath custody/evidence.** Determine what people, records, routes and proof actually survive.
9. **Next-stage operation discovered.** Surviving evidence proves that a coordinated release, provocation, false order, political incident or related information operation is timed toward Konoha.
10. **Evidence disposition decision.** Preserve everything, sabotage/destroy dangerous components, mark/trace recipients, plant a bounded false trail where legitimate, or prioritise immediate warning.
11. **Foreign consequences.** Ame actors react to the protagonist's methods; alliances, enemies, obligations and observer histories become durable.
12. **Closing Window / return.** Protagonist departs or begins emergency return toward Konoha with enough verified warning to justify the next mission.

## Neutral meaningful decision windows

### Decision A — exchange target
- records/evidence;
- source/seller;
- buyer/recipient;
- live communications/meeting;
- broader surveillance over immediate seizure.

### Decision B — when the exchange burns
- hold position for evidence;
- pursue a key actor;
- protect a witness/contact;
- withdraw with current proof;
- sabotage the network before escape.

### Decision C — evidence handling
- preserve for Konoha review;
- mark/trace downstream recipients;
- destroy only material whose continued circulation creates immediate danger where authority permits;
- send urgent warning before full analysis.

## Required outputs

- multiple competing actors use the same information ecosystem;
- major planned pressure is aimed back at Konoha;
- foreign evidence/relationships exist for late Arc 2 and Arc 3;
- urgency to return home.

### WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE candidates
- escaped buyer/seller;
- unrecovered market records;
- Ame retaliation/contact;
- traced recipient chain not needed for immediate Main Story completion.

# ARC 2 / MISSION 10 — THE STORY THEY NEED

## Universal mission function

Bring foreign evidence back into Konoha and show that **the same facts can support competing political conclusions without either side needing to be stupid or secretly evil**.

This is where Sasuke becomes a visibly credible alternative political centre through actions, competence and argument — not through an arbitrary `Sasuke support +X` switch.

## 12 Sections

1. **Return to a changed Konoha.** The information operation has already begun; multiple versions of recent events are circulating.
2. **Minato's response.** Hokage administration prioritises controlled verification, continuity and prevention of panic/false action. This is defensible but creates real costs around secrecy/delay/central control.
3. **Sasuke's response.** Sasuke has independently organised or supported a competing accountability/security response based on genuine institutional failures and credible relationships. He is not automatically correct or malicious.
4. **Evidence briefing decision.** The protagonist chooses how broadly to disclose foreign evidence, whom to protect, and which uncertainties to state explicitly.
5. **Autonomous political reactions.** ANBU, clans, officials, teammates, families and other actors react from their own Knowledge/history rather than surname/faction hive rules.
6. **A live leak/provocation.** During the political response, a new selective disclosure, forged claim or attack attempts to force Konoha into visible overreaction.
7. **Immediate priority decision.** Protect participants/evidence, chase the leak/attacker, keep the meeting intact, or secure a threatened institution/location.
8. **Tactical resolution.** Pursuit/Battle/protection/counter-surveillance produces real history.
9. **Sasuke demonstrates value.** Sasuke's independent organisation saves/protects/secures something materially important or exposes a failure in the existing response. His support grows for factual reasons.
10. **Minato demonstrates value and cost.** Minato prevents a wider failure through continuity/command/verification, while the cost of his chosen institutional posture remains visible.
11. **Political response decision.** The protagonist may advocate disclosure, source protection, institutional audit, controlled secrecy, independent verification, or another history-backed action without route labels.
12. **Closing Window.** Konoha establishes the next emergency/security arrangement. Sasuke is now a credible opposition/alternative leader, but the village has not yet become two governments or entered open civil war.

## Neutral meaningful decision windows

### Decision A — foreign evidence disclosure
- full verified disclosure to authorised review;
- compartmentalised disclosure protecting sources;
- public-facing disclosure of only facts safe/known enough to release;
- hold unverified elements until corroborated;
- use a trusted alternative channel if ordinary chain is compromised.

### Decision B — provocation response
- protect people/evidence;
- pursue the actor/source;
- preserve institutional continuity/meeting;
- secure a vulnerable site/record;
- another legitimate immediate priority.

### Decision C — institutional recommendation
- back controlled verification/continuity;
- demand independent audit/accountability;
- demand stronger unified command/security;
- protect individual/source autonomy from both camps;
- refuse endorsement and retain an evidence-driven independent stance if history supports it.

## Required outputs

- Sasuke gains visible, causally justified support;
- Minato remains credibly defensible rather than a strawman;
- Konoha participants divide for different reasons;
- foreign evidence changes domestic politics;
- no route family is declared.

### WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE candidates
- source-protection consequences;
- domestic political contact or grievance;
- escaped provocateur;
- independent audit/record thread not required for M11.

# ARC 2 / MISSION 11 — THE SECOND ORDER

## Universal mission function

Turn political disagreement into **operationally conflicting authority**.

A coordinated emergency creates two plausible commands/priority structures. Minato's formal order and a Sasuke-backed counter-directive/independent emergency response each address real danger but cannot both receive all available resources.

The point is not `one order is secretly fake`. The deeper crisis is that even after forgery/manipulation is exposed, Konoha genuinely disagrees about who should decide.

## 12 Sections

1. **Simultaneous emergencies.** Two or more real threats/incidents erupt close enough together that Konoha cannot fully resource every objective.
2. **The Hokage order.** Minato issues a legitimate formal priority based on his Knowledge and institutional obligations.
3. **The second order.** Sasuke/coalition issues or organises a conflicting emergency directive/response because they believe Minato's priority exposes another critical target. It is politically contested but not merely a forged document.
4. **Authority decision.** Verify further, follow Minato, follow Sasuke's operation, split effort where command/capability allows, or act independently toward an immediate factual objective.
5. **First objective pressure.** The protagonist confronts consequences of the selected priority; unchosen objectives continue through autonomous actors rather than freezing.
6. **Second-front news.** The player learns what is happening elsewhere without receiving omniscient certainty. Teammates/NPCs may disagree or act under their own obligations.
7. **Major Battle / field crisis.** Tactical resolution determines local survival, custody, infrastructure/evidence or other exact outcomes.
8. **Tradeoff becomes public.** Something was protected and something else was lost/exposed/damaged or resolved by another faction. No path gets perfect credit.
9. **Manipulation revealed, disagreement remains.** Evidence shows hostile actors intentionally created simultaneous pressure to expose/strain Konoha's command structure, but that does not erase the real conflict over priorities.
10. **Credit and blame.** Different groups interpret the same outcomes through their own Knowledge/history. Sasuke's coalition gains further credibility among those who believe his response protected what Minato failed to prioritise; Minato retains support from those who value lawful continuity/what his order preserved.
11. **Open political fracture.** Sasuke openly argues the current command structure can no longer be trusted to monopolise emergency authority; Minato rejects parallel command as itself dangerous. Sasuke now has **substantial support**.
12. **Closing Window.** The protagonist records an immediate practical stance/relationship through action or disclosure, but no permanent route lock is imposed. Konoha ends the mission visibly divided and preparing for another confrontation.

## Neutral meaningful decision windows

### Decision A — conflicting orders
- obey Minato's formal priority;
- join Sasuke's competing emergency response;
- verify before committing and accept time cost;
- split/coordinate if current authority/team capability legitimately permits;
- act toward a third immediate objective if Chronicle history makes it credible.

### Decision B — tactical tradeoff
- protect people;
- secure evidence/intelligence;
- protect critical infrastructure/command site;
- pursue the hostile coordinator;
- withdraw/rescue team when survival state demands it.

### Decision C — aftermath evidence
- release full factual account;
- support one emergency interpretation;
- insist both commands contributed to the failure/success;
- protect a source/secret even if it weakens the protagonist's political credibility;
- refuse to simplify the event for either camp.

## Required outputs

- command legitimacy openly fractured;
- Sasuke has substantial support before Arc 3;
- Minato retains meaningful legitimate support;
- protagonist/NPC loyalties remain history-backed and non-monolithic;
- final Arc-2 crisis becomes imminent.

### WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE candidates
- unchosen emergency site's unresolved aftermath;
- civilian/institutional grievance created by the tradeoff;
- escaped coordinator;
- teammate/faction relationship fallout.

# ARC 2 / MISSION 12 — THE LAST COMMON GROUND

## Universal mission function

Deliver the Arc-2 climax: Minato and Sasuke's blocs can still cooperate long enough to stop an immediate catastrophe, but the operation proves that shared political legitimacy is gone.

The mission must prevent an easy ending where catching one enemy restores trust.

## 12 Sections

1. **Emergency common ground.** A final joint summit/operation forms because both camps recognise an imminent threat they cannot safely ignore.
2. **Foreign history returns.** Kusa/Ame evidence, witnesses, obligations, contacts or losses from the actual Chronicle materially affect what can be proven and who will cooperate.
3. **Final information-war play.** Hostile/competing actors launch a coordinated disclosure/false-order/sabotage/assassination/infrastructure operation designed to make one Konoha bloc interpret the other as responsible or treacherous.
4. **Immediate priority decision.** Protect leadership, civilians/participants, the foreign witness/evidence chain, communications/command infrastructure, or pursue the active strike team.
5. **First resolver.** Battle/protection/infiltration/containment resolves the selected crisis while other actors act autonomously elsewhere.
6. **The second front.** New factual consequences arrive. Some allies follow Minato, some Sasuke, some their own obligations. Team/clan/family does not guarantee allegiance.
7. **Coalition decision.** Work directly with Minato's command, Sasuke's operation, a task-specific joint coalition, or an independent/foreign-backed route only if current history legitimately makes it available.
8. **Climactic operation.** Major PL Battle / pursuit / counter-intelligence resolution. Victory may stop immediate catastrophe but does not automatically solve the political crisis.
9. **Enough truth, not enough unity.** Evidence proves significant manipulation and exposes part of the hostile network/operation. It also confirms that many grievances and institutional failures were real rather than entirely fabricated.
10. **Political aftermath.** Minato and Sasuke both retain credible supporters. Foreign actors remember the Chronicle's conduct. Konoha can no longer plausibly return to pre-Arc-2 political normality.
11. **Final Arc-2 history decision.** Decide how to handle decisive evidence/leverage: entrust it to Hokage authority under terms, support a joint/public/independent process, place it with Sasuke's coalition, retain/protect part of it where legitimate, or use another earned nonconforming arrangement. Exact projected choices depend on history; there is no free secret option.
12. **Arc Closing Window.** Bounded final conversations/disclosures may occur. On explicit exit, record `ARC 2 / MISSION 12 COMPLETE` and **ARC 2 COMPLETE**. Sasuke stands as the established challenger with substantial support; Minato remains Hokage; open civil war belongs to Arc 3 rather than being silently consumed here.

## Neutral meaningful decision windows

### Decision A — immediate crisis priority
- protect people/civilians;
- protect Minato/Sasuke/leadership target according to context;
- secure foreign evidence/witness;
- secure command/communications infrastructure;
- pursue the strike team/master operational node.

### Decision B — who to coordinate with
- Minato's lawful command;
- Sasuke's operational coalition;
- a bounded joint task group;
- independent action from earned relationships/capability;
- foreign/nonconforming coalition only where actual history makes it credible.

### Decision C — evidence and legitimacy
- entrust verified evidence to Minato under explicit conditions;
- support independent/joint/public adjudication;
- entrust/share decisive evidence with Sasuke because current history made that credible;
- retain/protect sensitive evidence from both camps where legitimately justified;
- negotiate another arrangement from earned foreign/institutional relationships.

## Required outputs

- immediate catastrophe prevented or bounded according to actual resolver outcomes;
- hostile manipulation proven materially but not treated as the sole cause of all political division;
- Minato remains Hokage;
- Sasuke becomes the established challenger with substantial support;
- Kusa/Ame history remains available for Arc 3;
- route-relevant evidence/relationships are preserved without a hidden morality score;
- Arc 3 begins from a real political fracture, not an announced plot twist.

### WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE candidates
- surviving hostile-network fragments;
- foreign contacts/witness obligations;
- unresolved evidence/provenance threads;
- civilian/faction consequences from the final operation;
- any Chronicle-specific mystery that remained optional rather than becoming Main Story canon.
