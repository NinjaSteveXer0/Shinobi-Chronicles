Package Smuggler is coming toward the alley.

Package Smuggler catches the motion at the edge of the alley.

There.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**

Too slow.

The alley falls away behind Kakashi.

Kakashi follows.

Then:

## 5.1 Pursuit SUCCESS

Kakashi cuts inside the target's route.

For several seconds, neither can see the other through the Sakura tree's enormous trunk.

Kakashi hears footsteps on wet stone.

Counts them.

One.

Two.

Three—

He changes direction.

ANBU Marked Target comes around the far side and sees him.

Too late.

His eyes widen.

He jerks sideways toward the remaining street.

Then stops.

Not because of Kakashi.

A small ninken stands in the middle of the exit.

ANBU Marked Target stares at him.

The ninken stares back.

The man takes one cautious step to the side.

So does the ninken.

Other side.

The ninken matches him again.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“Seriously?”

The ninken's ears lift.

The target reaches toward his pouch.

A low growl answers him.

His hand stops.

Kakashi arrives behind him.

Now the target looks from the dog—

to Kakashi—

and understands the shape of his problem.

The ninken glances past him at Kakashi.

**PAKKUN**  
“This yours?”

Kakashi looks at ANBU Marked Target.

Then at the unfamiliar ninken.

**KAKASHI**  
“Apparently.”

Pakkun looks back at the man.

Kakashi steps closer.

**KAKASHI**  
“Thanks.”

Pakkun's ear flicks.

No introduction.

No names exchanged.

ANBU Marked Target slowly turns so he can keep both of them in view.

His breathing is harder now.

The package remains beneath his clothing.

Kakashi notices that his hand has moved over it again.

The man's eyes follow Kakashi's.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“You've been chasing this thing all night.”

Kakashi stops just outside striking distance.

**KAKASHI**  
“You've been running with it.”

A beat.

The target almost smiles.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“Fair.”

Pakkun looks between them.

He appears to have wandered into an argument without being told what the argument is about.

His eyes settle on the target's hand.

Then the shape beneath the clothing.

He figures enough of it out.

**PAKKUN**  
“Trouble?”

Kakashi keeps his attention on the man.

**KAKASHI**  
“Probably.”

Pakkun gives him a sideways look.

**PAKKUN**  
“Useful.”

The target lets out one quiet breath.

Then his expression changes.

The brief amusement disappears.

His fingers tighten over the package.

Kakashi sees it.

So does Pakkun.

Nobody moves.

Yet.

The pursuit is over.

The package problem isn't.

## 5.2 Pursuit FAILURE

Kakashi cuts inside the target's route.

For several seconds the Sakura tree blocks them from one another.

Kakashi listens.

Footsteps.

Fast.

Then slowing.

Kakashi changes direction.

The target isn't there.

His eye moves across the square.

A shadow disappears beyond one of the lanterns.

Wrong exit.

Kakashi turns immediately.

By the time he reaches the far side of the tree, ANBU Marked Target is already at the edge of the square.

He looks back once.

Their eyes meet across the distance.

The package is still pressed beneath his arm.

Then two late-night pedestrians step into the street between them.

Kakashi changes line.

The target doesn't.

He uses the interruption.

A turn between buildings.

Gone.

Kakashi reaches the corner seconds later.

Empty street.

Three possible routes.

No movement.

No sound he can separate from the rest of Konoha.

Kakashi stands still.

Listens again.

Nothing.

His visible eye narrows.

He looks back toward the Sakura tree.

Package Smuggler isn't there either.

Of course he isn't.

Kakashi had chosen the package.

The other man had used the choice.

For the first time since the ANBU operative handed him the assignment, Kakashi has neither person in sight.

And the package is still in the wrong hands.

He remains there for another second.

Not because he expects the street to give the answer back.

Because committing the mistake to memory takes less time than repeating it.

Then he turns toward the only place left where somebody is expecting him.

ANBU.
