# Mission Debrief Factual Completeness Lock

**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Owner:** Writing / Story — Konoha  
**Status:** **BINDING STORYWIDE AUTHORING REQUIREMENT**

## Core rule

> **When a protagonist gives a mission/debrief report, every materially relevant person, objective and event the protagonist actually knows about must be accounted for.**

A debrief must not silently omit an encountered opponent, ally, objective state, casualty, custody outcome, escape, survival uncertainty, or other major result simply because the scene is moving toward another branch.

## Knowledge boundary

Reporting remains limited to what the reporting character legitimately knows.

Do not:

- report later survival the protagonist did not discover;
- report hidden evaluation truth the protagonist does not know;
- infer another actor's later destination after the protagonist left;
- collapse World Truth into observer Knowledge.

If an outcome is unresolved from the protagonist's perspective, report that uncertainty honestly, e.g. `Defeated. Left her there.` or `I don't know.`

## Debriefer spoiler boundary

The character receiving the report must also remain inside the reporter's legitimate Knowledge boundary when speaking back to them.

A debriefer may secretly know more than the protagonist, but must not reveal hidden affiliations, role relationships, staged-mission structure, evaluation truth, operative identities, package ownership, internal terminology, or any other concealed World Truth merely by how they phrase a question.

Do not let a supposedly neutral debrief question become an accidental reveal.

Bad example for Kakashi Origin before the reveal:

`ANBU OPERATIVE: "The masked attacker?"`
`KAKASHI: "Defeated. Left her there."`
`ANBU OPERATIVE: "The man carrying the package?"`

That phrasing tells Kakashi the ANBU operative already knows who those people are and how they relate to the package.

Prefer observer-safe prompting such as:

`ANBU OPERATIVE: "Report."`

and let Kakashi supply the people and outcomes from his own perspective:

`KAKASHI: "A masked woman attacked. I defeated her and left her there."`
`KAKASHI: "The man who took the package ran. I caught him and recovered it."`
`KAKASHI: "The original target escaped / was defeated / was taken to the Uchiha Police Force."`

The debriefer may ask neutral follow-ups such as `Then?`, `And after that?`, `The package?`, or `Anything else?` only where those questions do not expose hidden knowledge.

## Kakashi Origin immediate correction

On any Kakashi branch where he fought or otherwise materially encountered the Masked Interceptor, the subsequent ANBU debrief must account for her outcome as Kakashi knows it.

For the common branch where Kakashi defeats the Masked Interceptor and immediately pursues the Package Smuggler, Kakashi should report the masked woman's defeat/left-behind state from his own perspective before moving on to the package carrier and original target outcomes.

The ANBU operative must not identify or classify those people for Kakashi before the Hokage Office reveal.

This correction applies retroactively to all already-authored Kakashi debrief scenes in that route family.

## Final lock

> **A debrief is a factual accounting of the mission as the reporting character knows it. Do not drop major participants or outcomes from the report.**
>
> **The debriefer may know more than the protagonist, but must not leak that hidden knowledge through their questions.**
