## Narration
The recovered package rests on Minato’s desk.

ANBU Marked Target stands near the window.

Package Smuggler is beside him.

Masked Interceptor stands apart from both.

One hand remains pressed against the fresh dressing beneath her clothing.

The ANBU operative enters.

Masked Interceptor looks toward him first.

## Dialogue
**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“He reported it?”

**ANBU OPERATIVE:**  
“All of it.”

Her eyes narrow slightly.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“Including me?”

**ANBU OPERATIVE:**  
“Yes.”

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“What did he say?”

**ANBU OPERATIVE:**  
“That he tried to kill you.”

A pause.

**ANBU OPERATIVE:**  
“That you survived.”

Package Smuggler glances toward her injury.

She notices.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“Barely.”

Package Smuggler’s expression changes.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:**  
“He actually landed it.”

She looks at him.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“He meant to.”

ANBU Marked Target studies her.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:**  
“Did he know you were alive?”

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“He saw me breathe.”

Silence.

Minato finally speaks.

**MINATO:**  
“Did he hesitate after that?”

Masked Interceptor thinks about it.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“No.”

She looks toward the recovered package.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“I left before he got another chance.”

Minato’s eyes move to the ANBU operative.

**MINATO:**  
“Did he explain himself?”

**ANBU OPERATIVE:**  
“I asked.”

Masked Interceptor watches him.

**ANBU OPERATIVE:**  
“He said, ‘That was my choice.’”

She is quiet for a moment.

Then:

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“Good.”

Package Smuggler looks at her.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:**  
“Good?”

Masked Interceptor meets his eyes.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“Now I know.”

No one asks her to explain.

ANBU Marked Target looks toward Minato.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:**  
“Does Kakashi know she made it back?”

**MINATO:**  
“No.”

Masked Interceptor’s gaze settles on Kakashi’s report.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“Leave it that way.”

Minato studies her.

**MINATO:**  
“For now.”

She accepts that.

Not forgiveness.

Not closure.

Just an answer.
