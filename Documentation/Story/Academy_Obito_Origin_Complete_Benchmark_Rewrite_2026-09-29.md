# SCENE 1 — LATE AGAIN

Obito knows he is late before he reaches the end of his street.

The Academy bell has not rung yet.

That is the good news.

The bad news is that he can already hear the village waking up between him and the training ground.

Shutters opening.

Carts rolling.

People calling to one another.

Too many things happening in the exact direction he needs to run.

Obito pulls his goggles into place and accelerates.

Today is supposed to be simple.

Get there.

Train.

Stop giving Kakashi another reason to look smug.

He clears a low wall, lands badly, catches himself and keeps moving.

The Hokage Monument appears between the rooftops.

Obito points at it while running.

**OBITO:** “One day.”

A shopkeeper looks up.

**SHOPKEEPER:** “What?”

Obito drops his hand.

**OBITO:** “Nothing!”

He turns the corner harder than necessary.

### OBJECTIVE

**GET TO TRAINING.**

# SCENE 2 — THE WARDROBE

Obito nearly runs into a wardrobe.

Not beside one.

Into one.

The thing is wedged sideways through a doorway while an older civilian pushes from the other end.

She catches it before it tips.

**CIVILIAN:** “Careful!”

Obito stumbles back.

**OBITO:** “Why is that in the road?”

The woman leans around the wardrobe.

**CIVILIAN:** “Because my doorway has declared war.”

Obito looks at the trapped corner.

Then at the Academy road.

The woman follows his eyes.

**CIVILIAN:** “Training?”

**OBITO:** “Yes.”

**CIVILIAN:** “Then go.”

She puts her shoulder back against the furniture.

The wardrobe does not move.

Obito takes one step toward the Academy.

Behind him:

Wood scrapes.

The woman mutters something unfriendly at the doorway.

### CHOICE

**HELP HER**

**KEEP GOING**

---

## HELP HER

Obito turns around.

Drops his bag.

**OBITO:** “You're turning it too early.”

The woman stops pushing.

Very slowly:

**CIVILIAN:** “I've moved furniture longer than you've been alive.”

Obito points at the wardrobe.

**OBITO:** “And it's winning.”

She gives him a look.

Obito gets both hands under the lower edge before she can send him away.

**OBITO:** “Lift when I say.”

**CIVILIAN:** “Bossy.”

**OBITO:** “Efficient.”

They lift.

The wardrobe catches again.

Obito shifts his grip.

**OBITO:** “Wait—back. Back.”

**CIVILIAN:** “You said lift.”

**OBITO:** “I changed my mind!”

They angle the bottom.

The corner clears.

The wardrobe slides through.

Obito almost goes with it.

The woman catches the other side before it falls.

They stand there for a second.

Breathing.

Looking at the now-defeated furniture.

**CIVILIAN:** “You were right.”

Obito straightens immediately.

**OBITO:** “Obviously.”

The Academy bell sounds faintly in the distance.

Obito's face changes.

The woman points at the road.

**CIVILIAN:** “Go.”

Obito grabs his bag.

**OBITO:** “Already going!”

He runs.

---

## KEEP GOING

Obito makes himself keep walking.

Then running.

The woman is not trapped.

Nobody is underneath the wardrobe.

It is annoying.

Not an emergency.

He knows that.

That does not stop him looking back once.

The woman has already changed her grip and is trying another angle.

Obito faces forward again.

**OBITO:** “She's got it.”

He says it like he is arguing with somebody.

There is nobody beside him.

He runs faster anyway.

---

# SCENE 3 — THE VEGETABLES

A daikon rolls across Obito's path.

He jumps it.

A tomato follows.

He catches that one by reflex.

The vegetable vendor kneeling beside a split basket sees him holding it.

**VENDOR:** “Good. Start with that.”

Obito stares at the tomato.

Then at her.

**OBITO:** “I didn't volunteer.”

Another vegetable rolls toward the gutter.

The vendor lunges and misses it.

**VENDOR:** “Then put it down and break its heart.”

Obito looks toward the Academy.

The tomato is still in his hand.

### CHOICE

**HELP HER**

**KEEP GOING**

---

## HELP HER

Obito snatches the runaway vegetable before it reaches the gutter.

**OBITO:** “These shouldn't move this fast.”

**VENDOR:** “Neither should you, apparently.”

Obito looks up.

**OBITO:** “I'm going to training.”

**VENDOR:** “Eventually.”

That earns her a glare.

She is already smiling.

Obito crawls halfway under a bench for the last daikon.

His bag catches on the seat.

**OBITO:** “Ow—”

**VENDOR:** “Elite shinobi work?”

Obito emerges holding the vegetable.

**OBITO:** “Exactly.”

They get the basket upright.

The vendor tests the split handle.

It holds.

Obito is already backing away.

**VENDOR:** “Thank you.”

He stops.

Just long enough.

**OBITO:** “Yeah.”

Then runs.

The vendor calls after him.

**VENDOR:** “Wrong way!”

Obito skids.

Looks.

She points across the intersection.

He changes direction without answering.

Her laugh follows him for half a street.

---

## KEEP GOING

Obito places the tomato on the edge of the vendor's stall.

**OBITO:** “Sorry.”

She waves him on before he can explain.

**VENDOR:** “Run, Academy boy.”

That somehow makes it harder.

Obito goes anyway.

Behind him, another pedestrian crouches to help gather the vegetables.

He sees it.

Good.

Obito turns forward.

This time he does not slow.

---

# SCENE 4 — THE MISSING EQUIPMENT

The Academy custodian is standing beside two equipment bundles.

There should be three.

Obito knows that before the man says anything.

**CUSTODIAN:** “Obito.”

Obito winces.

**OBITO:** “You know I'm late, right?”

**CUSTODIAN:** “I know you're going where this was supposed to go.”

He holds up an empty binding strap.

Academy markings.

Obito looks down the lane behind him.

**OBITO:** “You lost an entire bundle?”

**CUSTODIAN:** “I prefer ‘temporarily misplaced.’”

Obito points at the empty strap.

**OBITO:** “That's just losing with confidence.”

The custodian folds his arms.

Obito hears himself.

**OBITO:** “...I have to go.”

### CHOICE

**HELP SEARCH**

**KEEP GOING**

---

## HELP SEARCH

Obito crouches by the bindings.

**OBITO:** “East storage?”

The custodian nods.

**OBITO:** “Straight here?”

Another nod.

Obito looks down the route.

Not everywhere.

Places a bundle could fall and stay hidden.

Low wall.

Drainage turn.

Delivery platform.

He checks the wall.

Nothing.

The drainage turn.

Nothing.

Then spots Academy cloth beneath a loading platform.

Obito drops flat and reaches under.

**OBITO:** “Found it!”

He pulls the bundle free.

Dust covers the front of his shirt.

The custodian takes it.

**CUSTODIAN:** “Good eye.”

Obito grins.

Then remembers the time.

The grin dies.

**OBITO:** “You owe me.”

**CUSTODIAN:** “For finding Academy property?”

Obito is already running.

**OBITO:** “YES!”

---

## KEEP GOING

Obito points toward the drainage turn.

**OBITO:** “Check there.”

The custodian looks.

**CUSTODIAN:** “Why?”

**OBITO:** “Because if it fell off the cart, that's where it would slide.”

The custodian starts toward it.

Obito starts toward the Academy.

**CUSTODIAN:** “You could be right.”

Obito runs backward for two steps.

**OBITO:** “I usually am!”

He turns around before the custodian can answer.

---

# SCENE 5 — THE DELIVERY CART

The next cart is not lost.

It is sideways.

Crates cover half the street.

A delivery worker is trying to hold one stack upright while levering the cart wheel out of a rut.

There is a clear path around it.

The worker sees Obito slow.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** “You can get through on the left.”

Obito looks at the gap.

Then at the worker's arms shaking under the crate.

**OBITO:** “You sure?”

**DELIVERY WORKER:** “I'm sure you standing there isn't helping.”

Fair.

### CHOICE

**HELP WITH THE DELIVERY**

**KEEP MOVING**

---

## HELP WITH THE DELIVERY

Obito drops his bag.

**OBITO:** “Move your hand.”

The worker blinks.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** “What?”

**OBITO:** “Your right hand. If we lift there, the wheel's going straight back into the rut.”

The worker changes position.

Together they heave.

The cart rises.

Tilts too far.

**OBITO:** “Other way!”

**DELIVERY WORKER:** “You said up!”

**OBITO:** “Up isn't supposed to become over!”

They wrestle it level.

One crate falls.

Obito catches it against his chest.

The worker stares.

**OBITO:** “What?”

**DELIVERY WORKER:** “Nothing.”

They get the last crate aboard.

The worker points toward the Academy.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** “Go.”

Obito grabs his bag.

**OBITO:** “Everybody keeps saying that after I help them.”

**DELIVERY WORKER:** “Maybe listen faster.”

Obito opens his mouth.

Decides he has no time to win this argument.

Runs.

---

## KEEP MOVING

The worker nods toward the open gap again.

Obito takes it.

**OBITO:** “The wheel's caught on the inside.”

The worker looks down.

Adjusts the lever.

The cart shifts.

Obito keeps moving.

He helped exactly enough to be annoying.

It is still faster than stopping.

---

# SCENE 6 — THE RUNAWAY CART

The Academy roofs are finally in sight.

Obito can see the training-ground wall.

Then somebody screams.

A handcart has broken loose on the slope ahead.

It bounces once.

Twice.

Gains speed.

People move.

Two adults run toward it from one side.

Another civilian starts clearing the lane.

Nobody is standing still waiting for Obito.

That almost makes the choice easier.

Then the cart hits a rut.

One adult jumps back.

The cart turns toward the crowded side of the street.

The Academy bell rings.

Obito hears both sounds at once.

### CHOICE

**STOP AND HELP**

**GO TO TRAINING**

## STOP AND HELP

Obito throws his bag against the wall.

Runs downhill.

**CIVILIAN:** “Kid!”

Obito gets beside the cart instead of in front of it.

Grabs the side rail.

The cart jerks him forward.

**OBITO:** “Okay—!”

His feet scrape.

**OBITO:** “Bad idea!”

One of the adults reaches the opposite rail.

Together they force the front wheel toward the curb.

It hits.

Bounces.

Stops.

Obito keeps holding it for a second after it is already still.

Then lets go.

His palms sting.

The adult beside him looks at them.

**CIVILIAN:** “You all right?”

Obito closes his hands.

**OBITO:** “Obviously.”

He opens one.

Looks at the scrape.

**OBITO:** “Mostly.”

The Academy bell rings again.

Obito's eyes widen.

**OBITO:** “Training!”

The adults have the cart now.

Obito grabs his bag and runs.

This time there is nothing left behind him that needs deciding.

---

## GO TO TRAINING

Obito watches the adults move.

One takes the rail.

Another clears the street.

A third reaches the front wheel.

They have it.

Or they are going to.

Obito makes the decision before his feet make it for him.

**OBITO:** “They've got it.”

He turns toward the Academy.

Runs.

He hears the cart crash into something behind him.

Does not turn around.

The gate is in front of him.

---

# SCENE 7 — ARRIVAL

The training ground tells Obito how late he is before the instructor does.

Which equipment is still out.

Which marks are already cut into the dirt.

How tired everybody else looks.

What remains depends on the time he actually spent getting here.

---

## FULL TRAINING

Students are still lining up.

Obito skids into the yard.

The instructor looks at him.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Cutting it close.”

Obito straightens.

Checks the yard.

No drill has started.

**OBITO:** “But I made it.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “That wasn't praise.”

**OBITO:** “Still true.”

A whistle sounds.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Line up.”

Obito grins and does.

---

## SUBSTANTIAL TRAINING

Obito reaches the yard as the conditioning group breaks.

The instructor looks at him.

Then at the students stretching their legs.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “You missed conditioning.”

Obito bends forward, catching his breath.

Raises one finger.

**OBITO:** “I conditioned myself getting here.”

The instructor waits.

Obito lowers the finger.

**OBITO:** “Doesn't count.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “No.”

The weapon rack rolls out.

Obito stands immediately.

**OBITO:** “I'm here for that.”

---

## REDUCED TRAINING

The wooden weapons are already being put away when Obito arrives.

His eyes follow them.

Then the marks from the finished drills.

Then the instructor.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “You're late.”

**OBITO:** “I noticed.”

A few students look over.

Obito refuses to look as tired as he is.

**OBITO:** “What's next?”

The instructor points toward the Ninjutsu markers.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Fundamentals.”

Obito drops his bag.

**OBITO:** “Good.”

Frustration can wait until after he uses what is left.

---

## MINIMAL TRAINING

Obito smells scorched air before he reaches the gate.

The Ninjutsu block is over.

Students are pairing off for Taijutsu.

The last drill.

The instructor sees him.

Obito sees everything he missed.

Neither needs to say it.

The instructor does anyway.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “You're here.”

Obito swallows whatever excuse arrived first.

**OBITO:** “What's left?”

The instructor points at the pairs.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Closing drill.”

Obito drops his bag.

**OBITO:** “Put me in.”

---

# SCENE 8 — FORMAL TRAINING

Only the blocks still available play.

---

## STAMINA — FULL ONLY

Obito attacks the first conditioning lap like the Hokage position is waiting at the finish line.

By the third lap, his lungs object.

The instructor passes him.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “You don't win conditioning on the first lap.”

Obito pushes harder.

**OBITO:** “Wasn't trying to.”

The instructor looks at him.

Obito adjusts his pace.

Barely.

Enough.

---

## BUKIJUTSU — FULL / SUBSTANTIAL

The first Academy weapon leaves Obito's hand harder than it should.

It hits worse than he wants.

Obito stares at the target.

**OBITO:** “It moved.”

Nobody answers.

Obito looks around.

**OBITO:** “Fine.”

Second throw.

Cleaner.

Third.

Cleaner again.

By the end, he has stopped trying to overpower the exercise and started paying attention to it.

He does not announce that discovery.

---

## NINJUTSU — FULL / SUBSTANTIAL / REDUCED

This is the block Obito wanted.

Fire.

Uchiha fundamentals.

Something that feels closer to the person he keeps insisting he will become.

His first flame bursts too hard and collapses.

Obito glares at the empty air.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Slow down.”

**OBITO:** “I know.”

He absolutely did not.

He tries again.

Less force.

More control.

The flame lasts.

Not long.

Longer.

Obito watches the last heat fade.

**OBITO:** “Again.”

The instructor is already moving down the line.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Again.”

Obito grins.

---

## TAIJUTSU — ALL ENTITLEMENTS

No weapon.

No fire.

No future Sharingan to imagine solving the problem for him.

Just another student.

Distance.

Timing.

The signal comes.

Obito goes first.

Too hard.

Gets redirected.

Hits the ground.

Gets up.

Again.

The second attempt lasts longer.

The third makes his opponent actually adjust.

By the final whistle, Obito is breathing hard and smiling anyway.

However much training he reached, this part belongs to him because he was here for it.

---

# SCENE 9 — END OF DAY

The yard empties.

Obito sits on the edge of the training space and reties one sandal.

The instructor passes with the last bundle of equipment.

Stops.

Looks at him.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Tomorrow?”

Obito knows exactly what he means.

**OBITO:** “Earlier.”

The instructor raises an eyebrow.

Obito points at him.

**OBITO:** “I said earlier.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “I heard you.”

He walks away.

Obito waits until he is gone.

Then looks at the road home.

The same village he crossed this morning.

It feels much longer now that nobody is timing him.

He starts walking.

---

## IF OBITO HELPED ALL FIVE

His hands still remember the wardrobe.

The vegetables left dirt on one knee.

The cart left a scrape across his palm.

Training left the rest.

Obito turns that hand over as he walks.

The things he stopped for did not disappear just because they cost him time.

Neither did the things he missed.

He looks toward the Hokage Monument.

Keeps walking.

---

## IF OBITO HELPED NONE

His clothes are cleaner than they might have been.

His training day is fuller too.

Obito knows exactly why.

On the road home he passes a delivery worker he recognises.

The cart is upright now.

Farther on, the vegetable stall is open like nothing happened.

Konoha kept moving without him.

Obito looks at both.

Then toward the Monument.

Keeps walking.

---

## IF OBITO HELPED SOME

Some places on the road feel familiar because he stopped there.

Others because he made himself keep going.

Nobody on the street knows those decisions belong together in his head.

Obito does.

He walks past the Academy equipment route.

Past the market.

Toward home.

---

# SCENE 10 — HOME

Obito drops his bag beside the wall.

Takes off his goggles.

Puts them down.

Then picks them back up almost immediately and turns them over in his hands.

Through the window, the Hokage Monument catches the last light.

He usually knows what to say when he looks at it.

Tonight takes longer.

Training mattered.

He can feel that in every sore muscle.

The road mattered too.

He can still see the moments where he stopped.

And the moments where he did not.

Obito leans back.

Nobody is asking him for an answer.

That makes it harder.

### CHOICE

**“I'M NOT GOING TO STOP HELPING PEOPLE.”**

**“I NEED TO TAKE TRAINING MORE SERIOUSLY.”**

**“I NEED TO GET BETTER AT BOTH.”**

**“MAYBE I'M LOOKING AT THIS WRONG.”**

# ENDING A — KEEP HELPING PEOPLE

Obito looks at the scrape on his palm.

If he earned it helping, he remembers exactly where.

If he did not, the hand is still tired from training.

Either way, he closes it into a fist.

**OBITO:** “I'm not going to stop helping people.”

He says it once.

Then immediately frowns.

Because saying it does not solve the part where training starts at the same time tomorrow.

Obito looks toward the Monument.

**OBITO:** “Fine.”

He puts his goggles back on.

**OBITO:** “Then I leave earlier.”

A beat.

**OBITO:** “A lot earlier.”

That sounds possible.

More importantly, it sounds like work.

Obito knows what to do with work.

---

# ENDING B — TAKE TRAINING MORE SERIOUSLY

Obito sets the goggles beside him.

Looks at the training marks still visible on his hands.

**OBITO:** “I need to take training more seriously.”

The words annoy him almost as soon as he says them.

Not because they are wrong.

Because they sound like something an instructor would say.

Obito leans forward.

**OBITO:** “No.”

He tries again.

**OBITO:** “If I say I'm becoming Hokage, I have to show up for the part that gets me there.”

Better.

He looks toward the village outside.

That does not mean he stops seeing people.

It means seeing a problem does not automatically make it his.

Obito puts his goggles back on.

Tomorrow, he intends to arrive before anyone can comment on it.

That alone is motivation enough.

---

# ENDING C — GET BETTER AT BOTH

Obito counts on his fingers.

Leave earlier.

Waste less time before leaving.

Stop when somebody actually needs him.

Keep moving when somebody else already has it.

He reaches four.

Stops.

**OBITO:** “This is getting complicated.”

Nobody answers.

Obito looks toward the Monument.

Then starts counting again.

**OBITO:** “Fine.”

A grin starts.

**OBITO:** “I'll get better at both.”

The statement is probably too confident.

That has never stopped him before.

This time, at least, he has an actual plan to fail at first.

---

# ENDING D — QUESTION THE FRAME

Obito looks at the Hokage Monument.

Usually the answer is immediate.

Become stronger.

Awaken the Sharingan.

Make the Uchiha take him seriously.

Become Hokage.

He has repeated those things so many times that they fit together automatically.

Tonight one of them catches.

Obito frowns.

**OBITO:** “Maybe I'm looking at this wrong.”

He studies the faces carved into the mountain.

Not his imaginary face.

The ones already there.

**OBITO:** “Why do I want it?”

No answer.

Obito waits anyway.

That is new.

He thinks about training.

About being noticed.

About helping people.

About wanting the village to know his name.

None of them disappear.

They just stop arranging themselves for him.

Obito picks up his goggles.

**OBITO:** “Fine.”

He puts them on.

**OBITO:** “I'll figure it out.”

For once, he does not point at the Monument.

He just looks.

---

# ORIGIN CLOSE

Morning will come again.

The Academy will start on time.

Konoha will still put people, problems and distractions between Obito and wherever he thinks he is going.

Tonight, Obito opens the window.

The village noise reaches him.

He smiles.

**OBITO:** “Tomorrow.”

Then closes it.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**
