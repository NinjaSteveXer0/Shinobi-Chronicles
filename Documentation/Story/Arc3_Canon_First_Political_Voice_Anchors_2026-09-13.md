## Sasuke Uchiha — baseline anchor

Useful canon identity anchors:

- intense attachment to family/clan is a genuine emotional driver beneath reserve;
- highly self-directed once he believes authority or received history is false;
- willing to abandon accepted institutions when he concludes the existing order itself is culpable;
- can form a radical political theory around the meaning of `Hokage` / leadership rather than merely seeking personal revenge;
- canon `Revolution` proves a believable Sasuke endpoint can include severe centralisation and a willingness to carry hatred/authority himself;
- terse/reserved presentation should not be confused with absence of conviction or emotion.

### SC divergence boundary

Arc-3 Sasuke does **not** inherit canon massacre / Itachi-revenge / Five-Kage-execution history merely because those canon beats inform his baseline temperament.

Current SC Recorded History instead gives him:

- a living/plural Uchiha political context;
- substantial support earned through Arc 2;
- repeated evidence that central command/information custody failed in material ways;
- repeated evidence that his own independent coalition protected witnesses/evidence that Minato's response under-prioritised;
- restraint during the final forged-order trap rather than using it as a pretext for seizure.

Therefore this Sasuke's civil-war challenge should be written as a **serious, self-directed institutional challenge with radical potential**, not a canon-villain transplant and not a generic noble reformer.

## Minato Namikaze — baseline anchor

Useful canon identity anchors:

- unusually calm under pressure while remaining extremely decisive;
- rapid analysis/action is central to his combat and leadership identity;
- strong duty orientation toward protecting Konoha and people entrusted to him;
- capable of severe personal sacrifice when he believes responsibility requires it;
- affection/family devotion does not eliminate executive hardness;
- confidence should read as controlled competence, not bureaucratic passivity.

### SC divergence boundary

Current Minato is living Fourth Hokage with a functioning family and a politically contested administration.

Arc 3 must therefore avoid two caricatures:

1. `Minato is right because he is the lawful Hokage`;
2. `Minato is weak/slow/secretive because Sasuke needs a villain`.

His strongest civil-war argument is that parallel emergency command can destroy Konoha faster than any one policy error. His weakness is that lawful continuity and information control can protect failures, delay accountability and make other actors reasonably refuse exclusive trust.

## Naruto Uzumaki — baseline anchor

Useful canon identity anchors:

- loud, direct, stubborn and emotionally legible;
- deep desire for recognition and belonging;
- repeatedly resists writing people off as irredeemable when he sees a path to connection;
- stubbornness can become operational courage rather than mere optimism;
- values bonds strongly enough to challenge institutions/people he loves.

### SC divergence boundary

Current SC Naruto has living parents, Menma as twin brother and a newly changing relationship concept with Kurama. He is **not** canon orphan Naruto transplanted unchanged.

Do not assign him to Minato merely because Minato is his father, and do not assign him to Sasuke merely because canon Naruto/Sasuke are narrative counterparts. His stance must arise from actual SC relationships/history.

## Kushina Uzumaki — baseline anchor

Useful canon identity anchors:

- forceful, outspoken, emotionally direct;
- strong self-determination even when fate/institutions place her in a role she did not choose;
- intense protectiveness/family love without becoming passive;
- capable of enduring dangerous power/containment realities and speaking bluntly about them.

### SC divergence boundary

Kushina is not `the Hokage's wife` as a political personality. Family loyalty may collide with judgement about Minato, Naruto, Menma, Jinchuriki autonomy and village security.

## Arc-3 ensemble voice discipline

- Minato: controlled executive precision; fewer words, clear responsibility, no omniscience.
- Sasuke: terse conviction; questions legitimacy/causal responsibility; avoids performative speeches unless the political moment genuinely demands one.
- Naruto: emotionally direct, stubborn, relationship-centred; can be angry without becoming Menma-sarcastic.
- Kushina: blunt, forceful, protective, unwilling to hide behind office etiquette.
- Menma: analytical, technical, dry/profane, strategic/self-interested under current durable lock; not a moral lecturer.
- Kurama: ancient, proud, sardonic, freedom-oriented; not pet/therapist.

Other named canon characters require equivalent proportional research before substantial dialogue if no fresh durable anchor already exists.

## Arc-3 authoring test

If Minato's dialogue could be given to Sasuke, Naruto or Menma without noticeable loss of identity, rewrite it.

If political alignment is inferred from clan/team/family relation alone, reject it.

If baseline canon is used to overwrite explicit SC Recorded History, reject it.
