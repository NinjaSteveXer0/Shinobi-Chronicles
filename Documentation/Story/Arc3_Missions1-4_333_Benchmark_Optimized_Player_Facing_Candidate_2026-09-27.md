# UNIVERSAL ARC-3 PERFORMANCE RULES

## Civil war must be physical

Do not narrate:
> “Konoha is now in civil war.”

Make the player see it become true through:
- blocked transfers;
- weapons drawn between Leaf shinobi;
- first casualties;
- checkpoints;
- family/team separation;
- contested hospitals/roads;
- rival orders;
- civilian displacement.

## Allegiance remains personal/history-backed

Never infer:
- Uchiha -> Sasuke;
- ANBU -> Minato in every case;
- family -> same political side;
- teammate -> protagonist's side;
- Jinchūriki -> faction asset.

## Politics through consequence

Political arguments should emerge after the player has lived the cost they are arguing about.

Do not make M1–4 into debates with occasional movement.
