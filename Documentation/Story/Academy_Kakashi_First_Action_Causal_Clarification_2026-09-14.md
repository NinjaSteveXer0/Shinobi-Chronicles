## 3. Authored causal facts

### OBSERVE

`OBSERVE` is not merely a flavour or information-gain variant.

Its authored causal meaning includes:

- Kakashi deliberately remains out of the exchange rather than intervening immediately;
- that non-intervention permits the clean handoff to occur;
- the resulting transfer creates the authored situation in which the decoy-assassin / Rogue Chūnin escalation occurs;
- the escalation then creates a new legitimate decision situation;
- one downstream branch from that Observe-derived escalation leads to Pakkun's first involvement.

The exact unplayed prose, wording, timing details and resolver-specific micro-outcome are not Stephen-authored merely because this causal family is known.

### ATTACK

`ATTACK` is an intervention intent.

Its purpose is to disrupt the exchange before the clean handoff completes.

It must therefore not be projected as though Kakashi chooses Attack, both men react, and then the same clean package transfer simply occurs anyway for convenience.

`ATTACK` does not itself guarantee:

- Battle;
- successful interruption;
- package custody;
- target capture;
- decoy-assassin appearance;
- the Observe-derived later decision family.

Those factual results remain resolver- and state-dependent.

### PICKPOCKET

`PICKPOCKET` is also an intervention intent.

Its purpose is to remove the package from the exchange before the clean handoff completes.

It must not be treated as a cosmetic route into the same post-transfer state.

`PICKPOCKET` does not itself guarantee successful theft or custody; the relevant resolver establishes the factual result.

### GET_CLOSER

`GET_CLOSER` remains an authored first-action intent, but this clarification does not invent its exact downstream screenplay or factual result.

Writing / CE must preserve its semantic purpose and determine its legitimate continuation from the approved Story package, current position, detection state, Knowledge and resolver output.
