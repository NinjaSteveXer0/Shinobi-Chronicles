# Shinobi Chronicles — Origin Screen-First Storytelling Quality Correction

**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Owner:** Writing — Konoha  
**Source issue:** #170  
**Status:** **BINDING QUALITY CORRECTION — SUPERSEDES ANY CLAIM THAT THE FIRST #170 MODERNIZATION PASS CLOSED ORIGIN PLAYER-FACING WRITING**

## Live-browser finding

Stephen's installed-browser review of Academy Kakashi proves the first #170 modernization pass did not reach current Shinobi Chronicles Story quality.

The prose is mechanically clearer, but several live beats still read as a designer explaining semantic state rather than a scene happening to Kakashi.

Examples observed in the installed screen include abstractions such as `confirmed packet`, `apparent carrier`, `reconstruct the operation`, `What does Kakashi think mattered most?`, and a closing character beat reduced to `Recovering the objective.` These phrases are semantically usable internally. They are not sufficient production storytelling by themselves.

## Correct target

> **Every Origin beat must read as a scene first and a semantic carrier second.**

The expression layer must translate closed semantics through what the character physically notices; body language and facial reaction; eye movement, posture, distance and movement through the environment; interruption, hesitation, silence and timing; contractions and natural spoken cadence where character-appropriate; relationship-specific dialogue; visible NPC autonomous action; character-specific interpretation rather than designer summary; meaningful choice wording embedded in the immediate dramatic problem; and compact screen-sized paragraphs rather than long explanatory blocks.

A player should be able to forget that a semantic state machine is underneath the scene.

## What this correction does NOT reopen

Preserve all existing Origin semantic IDs and choice IDs, factual outcomes, source occurrences / consequence bindings, World Truth and observer Knowledge, Battle callers / return seams, PL / Stats / Rank / Progression, ownership / acquisition / rewards, current Origin premise and continuity, and fail-closed Battle/content boundaries.

This is a **screen-first expression rewrite**, not an Origin redesign.

## Characterisation requirement

Canon Research First #134 remains binding. Named characters must not merely receive more colourful prose; their physical reactions, speech rhythm and decision framing must be recognisably theirs.

Required test:

> **If the body language and dialogue of two Origin characters can be swapped without making the scene feel wrong, the rewrite has failed.**

## Kakashi benchmark correction

Academy Kakashi is the immediate benchmark because live browser evidence exposed the gap. His Origin should feel like a young prodigy actually conducting a field-retrieval assessment. He notices tiny behavioural inconsistencies before naming conclusions; uses silence deliberately; does not narrate the evaluation rubric to himself; experiences simultaneous pressures as concrete visual/action beats, not labels such as `decoy-assassin pressure event`; debriefs through evaluator questions, Kakashi's clipped answers, pauses and evidence on the table; and reaches a genuine living-Sakumo relationship scene instead of an abstract reflection survey.
