**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Owner:** Writing / Story — Konoha  
**Status:** **ACTIVE CHARACTER PERFORMANCE ANCHOR — ACADEMY MIRAI SCOPE**

## Purpose

This anchor supports the Academy Mirai Origin modernization.

It consumes:
- current Shinobi Chronicles Mirai escort/substitution authority;
- the Storywide player-facing performance standard;
- the Character Personality / Voice Profile Registry;
- official Naruto/Boruto material refreshed 2026-09-23.

This is a voice/performance anchor, not a permanent personality Trait.

## Canon-derived baseline relevant to Mirai

Official Naruto material presents later Mirai as:
- strongly mission-minded and eager to prove herself as a shinobi;
- capable of taking an escort assignment very seriously, even reading greater importance into it than is actually present;
- observant enough to notice when something feels wrong and investigate rather than simply accepting appearances;
- earnest rather than cynical;
- protective and duty-oriented;
- capable of personal emotion without ceasing to think like a shinobi.

Useful official references:
- NARUTO Official Site, `The Steam Ninja Scrolls: The S-Rank Mission!` — Mirai receives an escort mission for Kakashi and Guy and initially interprets it as an extremely important assignment.
- NARUTO Official Site, `The Steam Ninja Scrolls: The Haunted Inn!` — Mirai responds to strange occurrences by investigating.
- NARUTO Official Site, `The Steam Ninja Scrolls: Mirai's King!` — Mirai senses that something is wrong during an emotionally charged deception.

Canon research is a starting-identity anchor only. Academy Mirai is younger and must not speak like her later Genin self copied backward intact.

## Academy-era performance

Academy Mirai should feel:
- conscientious;
- prepared;
- slightly formal when she is trying very hard to do a job correctly;
- observant;
- more socially cautious than Obito;
- willing to ask questions when they have a purpose;
- capable of ordinary conversation rather than behaving like a bodyguard machine;
- quietly self-critical when she realises her definition of the assignment was too narrow.

She is not:
- omniscient;
- emotionless;
- a miniature ANBU operative;
- a generic stoic Sarutobi;
- constantly suspicious;
- entitled to hidden identity truth because the player selected her.

## Speech fingerprint

Mirai tends to:
- choose precise questions;
- qualify what she actually knows;
- avoid claiming certainty too early;
- become firmer rather than louder when suspicion increases;
- expose concern through attention and follow-up rather than dramatic speeches;
- sound younger and a little more awkward in casual conversation than in mission talk.

When uncomfortable socially, she may become slightly too formal.

When she has evidence, her confidence becomes cleaner.

When she lacks evidence, she should be able to say some version of:
- `I don't know yet.`
- `That's different from what you said earlier.`
- `Something changed. I don't know what.`

## Central Origin contradiction

Mirai can execute the visible escort perfectly while misunderstanding the deeper continuity problem.

The Origin must preserve:

**physical protection != identity verification**

**conversation != clue automatically**

**Knowledge acquired != inference automatically**

**changed chakra != explanation automatically**

**suspicion != confirmation**

**checkpoint success != verification success**

## Relationship to protected civilian

The civilian begins as:
- ordinary;
- polite;
- cooperative;
- not suspicious.

Mirai should have enough normal interaction with them that the later substitution feels like a relationship discontinuity, not a puzzle object swapping sprites.

The earlier conversation must be written as human conversation first and potential evidence second.

## Verification behaviour

Mirai's strength is not magical deduction.

It is disciplined comparison:

`what I was told / observed earlier`
vs
`what I am being told / observing now`.

If she lacks the relevant earlier Knowledge, she cannot use it later.

If she detects changed chakra, she knows only that the chakra reference changed unless additional evidence supports more.

## Final fingerprint

> **Academy Mirai wants to do the job correctly enough that the Origin can teach her the job may be larger than the wording she first understood.**

Her development is not “be more suspicious.”

It is learning that protection may require preserving continuity, context and identity—not only preventing physical harm.


## 2026-09-23 canon-performance refinement after Stephen dialogue review

Stephen rejected the first Mirai draft's conversational performance as too dry / under-characterised. The following canon-first refinements are now binding for the next candidate expression pass.

Official BORUTO / NARUTO material reinforces that later Mirai:
- takes escort responsibility extremely seriously;
- can overestimate the danger/importance of an assignment because she is eager to prove herself;
- investigates strange circumstances rather than passively accepting them;
- can sense that something is wrong without already knowing the explanation.

Useful official source pages:
- https://naruto-official.com/en/anime/boruto/list/01_1050
- https://naruto-official.com/en/anime/boruto/list/01_1052
- https://naruto-official.com/en/anime/boruto/list/01_1055

Secondary Konoha Shinden / character summaries additionally reinforce later Mirai as:
- unusually serious about responsibility;
- capable of overthinking;
- impatient or frustrated when reality does not match the mission she prepared for;
- embarrassed when her intensity is exposed;
- still socially capable and not an emotionless mission machine.

Academy projection must remain younger than those later portrayals.

### Dialogue correction

Academy Mirai should therefore not default to:
- clipped `question -> yes -> question -> yes`;
- neutral tactical database speech;
- endless calm precision;
- already-mature investigator cadence.

She can:
- prepare too seriously for a simple escort;
- misunderstand a joke for half a beat;
- become mildly defensive when someone notices how intensely she is treating the exercise;
- ask a question because she is curious and then pretend it was mission-relevant;
- overthink a small detail;
- have ordinary teenage/Academy awkwardness in silence;
- become noticeably firmer when she chooses to investigate.

Her protected civilian must also behave like a person with their own social rhythm, not a clue dispenser.

### Agency correction

Mirai does **not** automatically become suspicious after the substitution.

Writing must not narrate:
- `something feels wrong`;
- `Mirai knows the chakra changed`;
- `the answer catches in her mind`;

unless the player's prior Knowledge/attention and current action legitimately establish that observation.

The player may:
- miss a contradiction;
- hear it and ignore it;
- follow the civilian's suggested route;
- investigate;
- verify through available evidence;
- do nothing and complete the escort.

CE records the resulting Knowledge/inference history.

### Interrogation correction

When Mirai tests a possible contradiction, she does **not** volunteer the original correct answer to the suspect.

Example shape:

`MIRAI: You said someone was meeting you after the checkpoint. What was the plan again?`

If the current escort answers incorrectly, Mirai keeps the mismatch to herself unless the **player** chooses to reveal/challenge it.

The player then decides whether to:
- press;
- test again;
- use another available evidence channel;
- follow/alter the route;
- or do nothing.

**Writing does not spend the player's inference for them.**
