## Voice fingerprints

**Obito:** fast, emotionally readable, proud and ambitious; assertion comes out when embarrassed; once he commits to a task he uses his whole body; frustration turns quickly into renewed effort rather than cold detachment.

**Academy Instructor:** practical, economical and training-focused; corrects the drill in front of them rather than explaining Obito's life to him; no therapist speech and no thematic summary.

One-scene NPC micro-anchors:
- Furniture Civilian: physically stubborn, impatient with spectators, not helpless.
- Vegetable Vendor: commercially sharp, busy, capable of teasing without becoming a joke machine.
- Equipment Custodian: task-focused, worried about missing Academy property, values a useful observation more than a speech.
- Delivery Worker: self-reliant, already solving the problem, does not solicit rescue.
- Runaway Cart Civilian: urgent and direct because the danger is already moving.

## CAUSAL SPINE

**Trigger:** Obito is trying to reach formal Academy training while five concrete village problems successively enter his path.  
**Direct observers:** Obito and the person directly involved in each incident; the Academy instructor observes only Obito's eventual arrival and training performance.  
**Safe inference:** Obito can see whether somebody has a problem and whether stopping will cost time; he does not know an abstract route score and no one tells him what the day "means."  
**Current wants:** Obito wants to reach training, get stronger and prove himself; each civilian wants their immediate practical problem dealt with; the instructor wants the class trained on schedule.  
**Premise invalidated?:** NO. The premise "get to training" remains valid throughout, but every HELP decision consumes authored journey time and therefore changes how much training remains.  
**Why now:** each decision happens because the practical problem is physically present while Obito is en route; arrival content changes because the training day has continued without him.  
**What changes afterward:** five committed factual choices -> exact arrival delay -> exact entitlement -> only the remaining training blocks -> factual home memory -> independent final self-interpretation.
