# PLAYER-FACING STORY

# ACADEMY METAL LEE
## WHEN PEOPLE ARE WATCHING

---

# SCENE 1 — EARLY TRAINING

The Academy courtyard is almost empty.

Metal likes it that way.

The training dummy rolls along its rail.

He watches the timing.

One turn.

Two.

Three.

Metal tightens his hand wraps.

**METAL:** “Again.”

The dummy starts another cycle.

This time the player chooses how he trains.

### CHOICE

**SPINNING KICK**

**FULL-FORCE FIST**

**CONDITIONED ENDURANCE**

---

## SPINNING KICK

Metal waits until the moving pad crosses in front of him.

He steps.

Turns.

His heel drives through the centre of the target.

The pad snaps back.

Metal lands cleanly.

He checks his footing.

Then the mark on the pad.

A small smile.

**METAL:** “Better.”

He resets the dummy himself.

---

## FULL-FORCE FIST

Metal lets the dummy roll toward him.

He does not swing early.

One more step.

Then he drives his fist into the reinforced centre pad.

The whole frame shakes.

Metal's feet stay planted.

He looks at the target.

**METAL:** “Good.”

Then he notices his back foot.

A little too wide.

Metal resets his stance.

**METAL:** “Again.”

---

## CONDITIONED ENDURANCE

Metal starts the moving cycle.

He stays with it.

Turn.

Step.

Duck.

Reset.

Again.

The dummy speeds up.

Metal keeps going.

His breathing gets louder.

His movements do not fall apart.

When the mechanism finally slows, Metal puts both hands on his knees.

He is smiling.

**METAL:** “One more.”

He reaches for the control.

A voice behind him stops him.

---

# SCENE 2 — SOMEONE WAS WATCHING

**GENIN:** “That was good.”

Metal freezes.

Not in his stance.

Everywhere else.

He turns.

A Genin is standing near the edge of the courtyard.

Two Academy students are with them.

Metal looks from one face to the next.

Then back to the dummy.

**METAL:** “How long were you there?”

**GENIN:** “Long enough to see the last few rounds.”

Metal straightens.

Too fast.

His heel catches the edge of the training mark.

He recovers before he falls.

Everyone sees that too.

Metal looks down at his foot.

Then up.

**METAL:** “That was not part of the exercise.”

One of the students smiles.

Metal's ears start turning red.

He immediately faces the dummy again.

---

# SCENE 3 — THE INVITATION

The Genin walks closer.

Not too close.

**GENIN:** “Do you want to spar?”

Metal looks over his shoulder.

At the Genin.

Then at the two students.

One of them notices and steps back.

That does not make Metal feel better.

He rubs one thumb across the edge of his hand wrap.

### CHOICE

**SPAR**

**KEEP WORKING THE DUMMY**

**BACK OUT**

---

# BRANCH A — SPAR

Metal faces the Genin.

**METAL:** “Yes.”

The answer comes quickly.

Metal takes a breath.

Then adds:

**METAL:** “A real spar.”

The Genin nods.

**GENIN:** “A real spar.”

The watching students move farther from the training line.

Metal steps into position.

He checks the Genin's stance.

Then catches one of the students looking at him.

Metal immediately checks his own stance again.

It was fine.

Now he is thinking about it.

The Genin raises one hand.

**GENIN:** “Ready?”

Metal nods.

**METAL:** “Ready.”

### START PL BATTLE

Use the existing controlled spar:

`academy_metal_lee_origin_controlled_spar`

No mechanical changes.

---

# SPAR RESULT — STRONG

The spar ends.

Metal is breathing hard.

His guard is still up.

The Genin lowers theirs first.

Metal realises the watching students are quiet.

He looks at them.

Then instantly wishes he had not.

The Genin offers a hand.

**GENIN:** “Good match.”

Metal takes it.

**METAL:** “Thank you.”

The Genin looks at the training dummy.

Then at Metal.

**GENIN:** “You looked more comfortable before we started.”

Metal's shoulders tighten.

He almost denies it.

Then stops.

**METAL:** “I knew the dummy was not watching me.”

The Genin laughs once.

Not at him.

Metal can tell the difference.

**GENIN:** “Fair.”

Metal looks back at the sparring line.

**METAL:** “I still did better than I thought I would.”

**GENIN:** “You did.”

Metal smiles despite himself.

---

## STRONG CLOSE

The other students start gathering their things.

The Genin turns to leave.

Metal calls after them.

**METAL:** “Can we spar again another day?”

The Genin looks back.

**GENIN:** “Yeah. Ask me tomorrow.”

Metal nods like this was a completely ordinary question.

Then he notices the students still nearby.

His posture stiffens again.

Metal sighs.

**METAL:** “I was doing fine.”

One of the students smiles.

Metal shakes his head and starts retightening his wraps.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# SPAR RESULT — MIXED

The spar ends.

Metal steps back too quickly and nearly loses his balance.

He catches himself.

The Genin lowers their guard.

Metal keeps his up for another second.

Then drops it.

Nobody behind the Genin says anything.

Metal can still feel them there.

**GENIN:** “You all right?”

**METAL:** “Yes.”

Metal looks down at the sparring line.

**METAL:** “I kept looking past you.”

The Genin glances over one shoulder.

**GENIN:** “At them?”

Metal looks away.

**METAL:** “I knew they were there.”

**GENIN:** “Yeah.”

No advice follows.

Metal looks back at the sparring line.

**METAL:** “I can do better than that.”

**GENIN:** “Then do better next time.”

Metal looks at them.

That is exactly the answer he wanted.

---

## MIXED CLOSE

The Genin starts toward the others.

Metal stays beside the sparring line.

He moves through the first step of the exchange again.

Slowly.

No opponent.

No audience needed.

Just the part where his foot went wrong.

Metal corrects it.

Then stops.

The Genin notices.

**GENIN:** “Tomorrow?”

Metal looks over.

**METAL:** “Tomorrow.”

This time he says it without checking who heard.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# SPAR RESULT — ROUGH

The spar ends with Metal on the wrong side of almost every exchange he wanted back.

He steps away.

His breathing is too fast.

He knows it.

That makes it worse.

One of the Academy students behind the Genin shifts.

Metal looks over.

Then back.

His hand closes around the end of his wrap.

**GENIN:** “Metal.”

Metal looks up.

**GENIN:** “You good?”

Metal wants to say yes.

Instead:

**METAL:** “No.”

That surprises both of them.

Metal looks toward the dummy.

**METAL:** “I was doing that fine before.”

The Genin follows his eyes.

**GENIN:** “I saw.”

Metal swallows.

**METAL:** “Then everybody looked at me.”

The words come out faster now.

**METAL:** “And I knew everybody was looking, so I tried not to think about everybody looking, and then I was thinking about it anyway.”

He stops.

Realises how much he just said.

Metal closes his eyes.

**METAL:** “That sounded worse out loud.”

The Genin shakes their head.

**GENIN:** “Sounded accurate.”

Metal opens one eye.

No teasing.

No lecture.

Metal looks back at the training line.

**METAL:** “I still want another try.”

---

## ROUGH CLOSE

The courtyard begins to empty.

Metal waits.

Not because he is hiding.

Because he wants the space.

When the last group reaches the gate, Metal steps back onto the sparring line.

He takes his stance.

His first breath shakes a little.

The second does not.

Metal moves through the opening step again.

Then again.

He does not pretend the spar went well.

He trains anyway.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# BRANCH B — KEEP WORKING THE DUMMY

Metal looks at the Genin.

Then the dummy.

**METAL:** “I am going to finish this first.”

The Genin nods.

**GENIN:** “Okay.”

Metal turns back to the mechanism.

He resets it.

His fingers take two tries to catch the switch.

Metal notices.

So does everyone else.

He starts the cycle.

The first turn is clean.

The second is not.

Metal lands too far left.

He corrects too hard.

The dummy frame jumps its guide.

Metal's eyes widen.

The whole apparatus tears sideways.

Toward one of the Academy students.

Metal stops thinking about who is watching.

### CHOICE

**REDIRECT THE DUMMY**

**TAKE THE IMPACT**

**DESTROY THE DUMMY**

---

# REDIRECT THE DUMMY — SUCCESS

Metal runs with the swing instead of straight at it.

At the turn, he kicks across the frame's path.

The dummy whips away from the student.

It crashes into empty ground.

Metal lands beside the student.

**METAL:** “Are you hurt?”

The student shakes their head.

**STUDENT:** “No.”

Metal checks anyway.

Hands.

Face.

Feet.

Then he finally looks at the broken dummy.

**METAL:** “Okay.”

The Genin walks over.

**GENIN:** “Nice redirect.”

Metal looks at the student again.

**METAL:** “They were too close.”

That matters more to him.

---

## REDIRECT SUCCESS CLOSE

The student picks up their bag.

Before leaving, they stop beside Metal.

**STUDENT:** “Thanks.”

Metal straightens.

**METAL:** “You are welcome.”

Too formal.

He knows it immediately.

The student smiles.

Metal looks at the ruined rail.

**METAL:** “I should probably fix that.”

The Genin looks at the frame.

**GENIN:** “Probably.”

Metal kneels beside it.

This time the smile is his too.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# REDIRECT THE DUMMY — PARTIAL

Metal reaches the frame.

His kick changes the angle.

Not enough.

The Genin grabs the student and pulls them clear.

The dummy crashes past both of them.

Metal turns immediately.

**METAL:** “Are you okay?”

The student nods.

Metal exhales.

Then looks at the Genin.

**METAL:** “I almost had it.”

**GENIN:** “You changed it enough for me to move them.”

Metal looks at the wreck.

That is true.

It still does not feel good.

---

## REDIRECT PARTIAL CLOSE

The Genin and Metal drag the broken frame away from the training line.

Metal keeps glancing at the student.

The third time, the student notices.

**STUDENT:** “I am fine.”

Metal looks embarrassed.

**METAL:** “I know.”

Metal keeps his eyes on the bent frame.

**METAL:** “I just wanted to make sure.”

The student nods.

Metal goes back to the frame.

He does not look at the audience again.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# REDIRECT THE DUMMY — FAILURE

Metal strikes the frame at the wrong angle.

It barely changes direction.

The Genin gets to the student first and pulls them clear.

The dummy smashes through an empty marker.

Metal is beside the student a second later.

**METAL:** “Are you hurt?”

**STUDENT:** “No.”

Metal looks at the Genin.

**METAL:** “Thank you.”

The Genin nods.

Metal looks back at the dummy.

No excuse comes out.

---

## REDIRECT FAILURE CLOSE

The student leaves with the others.

Metal remains near the damaged marker.

The Genin starts lifting part of the frame.

Metal takes the other side.

They carry it together.

**METAL:** “I missed the angle.”

**GENIN:** “Yeah.”

Metal looks at the broken rail.

**METAL:** “I want to try that movement again later.”

**GENIN:** “Use a dummy that is attached to something.”

Metal nods very seriously.

**METAL:** “That would be better.”

The Genin laughs.

Metal only realises why after a second.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# TAKE THE IMPACT — SUCCESS

Metal steps between the dummy and the student.

He braces.

The frame slams into him.

Metal slides backward.

Stops.

The student is untouched.

Metal keeps both hands on the frame.

**METAL:** “Move back.”

The student does.

Only then does Metal let go.

**STUDENT:** “Are you okay?”

Metal rolls one shoulder.

**METAL:** “Yes.”

The Genin reaches them.

Metal points at the student.

**METAL:** “They are fine.”

The student stares at him.

**STUDENT:** “I asked about you.”

Metal pauses.

**METAL:** “Oh.”

---

## IMPACT SUCCESS CLOSE

Metal sits on the edge of the training ground while the instructor checks the dummy.

The student sits beside him.

**STUDENT:** “You really did not have to stand there.”

Metal looks at them.

**METAL:** “You were behind me.”

To Metal, that explains everything.

The student sees that.

**STUDENT:** “Right.”

Metal starts rewrapping one hand.

**METAL:** “I should have stopped it sooner.”

The student shakes their head.

**STUDENT:** “You stopped it.”

Metal considers that.

Then keeps wrapping.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# TAKE THE IMPACT — PARTIAL

Metal gets between the dummy and the student.

The frame keeps driving him backward.

The Genin reaches the other side.

Together they force it down.

Metal stays bent over it for a second.

**GENIN:** “Got it.”

Metal breathes.

Then looks at the student.

**METAL:** “Are you hurt?”

**STUDENT:** “No.”

Metal nods.

The Genin lets go of the frame.

Metal does too.

---

## IMPACT PARTIAL CLOSE

The Genin checks the bent rail.

Metal checks it with them.

**METAL:** “I thought I could stop it.”

**GENIN:** “You stopped most of it.”

Metal looks at the bend.

Then at them.

**METAL:** “You stopped the rest.”

The Genin shrugs.

**GENIN:** “That is what was needed.”

Metal nods.

He can live with that.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# TAKE THE IMPACT — FAILURE

Metal moves into the dummy's path.

He is one step late.

The Genin grabs the student and pulls them away.

The dummy crashes through the space they were standing in.

Metal reaches them.

**GENIN:** “You okay?”

Metal points at the student.

**METAL:** “Them first.”

The student nods quickly.

**STUDENT:** “I am okay.”

Metal finally breathes.

Then looks at the Genin.

**METAL:** “Thank you.”

---

## IMPACT FAILURE CLOSE

The audience is gone before Metal finishes helping move the broken equipment.

The Genin stays.

Metal wipes his hands on his trousers.

**METAL:** “I was too late.”

**GENIN:** “This time.”

Metal looks at them.

The Genin does not add anything.

Metal nods.

**METAL:** “Then I need to be faster next time.”

That answer belongs to him.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# DESTROY THE DUMMY — SUCCESS

Metal does not strike the whole frame.

He targets the joint carrying the motion.

One hit.

The joint breaks.

The dummy folds sideways and drops before it reaches the student.

Silence.

Metal looks at the fallen frame.

Then at the student.

**METAL:** “Are you all right?”

The student nods.

Only then does Metal notice everyone staring again.

Metal's shoulders climb.

**METAL:** “I meant to do that.”

The Genin looks at the broken joint.

**GENIN:** “I know.”

That helps more than Metal expected.

---

## DESTROY SUCCESS CLOSE

Metal kneels beside the broken mechanism.

The student stands nearby.

**STUDENT:** “That was amazing.”

Metal's face goes red immediately.

**METAL:** “The joint was already under too much force.”

**STUDENT:** “Still amazing.”

Metal suddenly becomes very interested in the broken bolt.

The Genin smiles and walks away.

Metal stays there until his face cools down.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# DESTROY THE DUMMY — PARTIAL

Metal's strike breaks one side of the moving frame.

The rest keeps coming.

The Genin drives into the damaged section and knocks it down.

The student gets clear.

Metal stares at the broken joint.

**METAL:** “I hit it.”

**GENIN:** “You did.”

Metal looks at the rest of the frame.

**METAL:** “Not enough.”

The Genin looks at the student.

Safe.

Then at Metal.

**GENIN:** “Enough to help.”

Metal does not answer immediately.

He is still deciding how much that counts.

---

## DESTROY PARTIAL CLOSE

Metal helps gather the broken pieces.

The Genin hands him one of the snapped joints.

**GENIN:** “You keeping that?”

Metal looks at it.

**METAL:** “Why would I keep it?”

The Genin shrugs.

**GENIN:** “You have been staring at it for five minutes.”

Metal looks down.

He has.

Metal puts the piece beside the others.

**METAL:** “I was studying it.”

The Genin nods.

**GENIN:** “Of course.”

Metal cannot tell whether that was a joke.

He decides not to ask.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# DESTROY THE DUMMY — FAILURE

Metal commits to the strike.

The joint slips past the point he aimed for.

The Genin shoves the student clear.

The dummy crashes into an empty rack.

Metal stares at the mark his fist left on the wrong part of the frame.

Then he turns.

**METAL:** “Are they hurt?”

**GENIN:** “No.”

Metal looks at the student.

They nod.

The relief reaches his face first.

The embarrassment comes after.

---

## DESTROY FAILURE CLOSE

Metal stands beside the damaged rack after everyone starts leaving.

He puts his fist against the mark he made.

Too high.

Too late.

The Genin walks past.

Stops.

**GENIN:** “Coming?”

Metal looks at the mark once more.

**METAL:** “In a minute.”

The Genin nods and leaves.

Metal stays long enough to remember exactly where he missed.

Then he follows.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

# BRANCH C — BACK OUT

Metal looks at the Genin.

Then at the watching students.

His hands tighten once.

**METAL:** “Not today.”

The Genin nods.

**GENIN:** “Okay.”

Metal blinks.

He had prepared for an argument.

There is not one.

**METAL:** “That is all?”

**GENIN:** “You said no.”

Metal looks at the ground.

**METAL:** “I can spar.”

**GENIN:** “I believe you.”

Metal looks up.

The answer catches him off guard.

The Genin steps away.

No challenge.

No lecture.

Metal is left with his own decision.

The other students leave first.

He resets it.

The courtyard gets quieter.

Then stops.

Looks toward the gate.

Nobody is watching now.

Metal breathes out.

Starts the cycle.

His first movement is clean.

Metal does not smile.

Not yet.

He keeps training.
