# PLAYER-FACING STORY

# ACADEMY WASABI IZUNO
## THE PURSUIT

---

# SCENE 1 — HEAD START

The target is already gone.

Wasabi looks down the empty street.

Then very slowly turns to the instructor.

**WASABI:** “You gave them a head start?”

The instructor checks the marker tied around his wrist.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “That's usually how pursuit exercises work.”

Wasabi points at the street.

**WASABI:** “How much of one?”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Enough.”

Her hands go out.

**WASABI:** “That is still not a number.”

One of the Academy students behind her laughs.

Wasabi glances back.

**WASABI:** “You can laugh after you catch up.”

The student immediately stops laughing.

Mostly.

The instructor points toward the village.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “The target reaches extraction, the exercise is over.”

Wasabi rocks once onto the balls of her feet.

**WASABI:** “And when I catch them?”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Then they're caught.”

Wasabi grins.

**WASABI:** “See? Clear answer.”

The instructor raises one hand.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Wait for—”

He drops it.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Go.”

Wasabi is already halfway to the first wall.

Behind her—

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “He was actually going to finish that time!”

**WASABI:** “Too slow!”

---

# SCENE 2 — THE TRAIL

Wasabi hits the first roof without breaking stride.

Roof tile.

Wall.

Awning.

Street.

Ahead, a figure vanishes around the far corner.

Wasabi points at them even though nobody can see her do it.

**WASABI:** “There you are.”

She reaches the corner.

Three possible routes wait for her.

Fresh marks cut hard to the right.

A basket is still rocking where somebody clipped it.

A roof tile farther ahead sits crooked.

Mud streaks the drainage edge.

Two Academy students land behind her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Which way?”

Wasabi crouches.

Looks right.

Looks up.

Looks down.

The target is getting farther away while the street keeps offering opinions.

### CHOICE

**TAKE THE TRAIL — MOVE**

**THAT'S TOO EASY — LOOK AGAIN**

**SPLIT UP — COVER MORE GROUND**

**FORGET THE TRAIL — CUT THEM OFF**

---

## TAKE THE TRAIL — MOVE

Wasabi is moving before the other students finish reading the ground.

**WASABI:** “Right.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “You sure?”

**WASABI:** “Nope.”

The student blinks.

Wasabi vaults a crate.

**WASABI:** “Catch up!”

The trail stays obvious.

Scuffed dirt.

Displaced boxes.

A footprint on a low wall.

Wasabi takes the wall instead of the street beside it.

For half a block, everything keeps pointing the same way.

Then the freshest tracks stop beside an empty alley.

Just stop.

Wasabi skids.

Looks down.

Looks up.

Looks behind her.

The student catches up, breathing harder than Wasabi is.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Still sure?”

Wasabi points at them without looking away from the wall.

**WASABI:** “Don't start.”

The student lifts both hands.

Wasabi crouches again.

Now she looks properly.

---

## THAT'S TOO EASY — LOOK AGAIN

Wasabi starts right.

Stops so fast the student behind her nearly runs into her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “What?”

Wasabi points at the tracks.

**WASABI:** “They're trying too hard.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “They're footprints.”

**WASABI:** “Exactly. Nobody running away works this hard to be helpful.”

She drops to the drainage edge.

The mud there is disturbed.

Higher up, one roof tile has shifted sideways.

Wasabi looks from one to the other.

The other student peers over her shoulder.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “So?”

Wasabi is already climbing.

**WASABI:** “So the street's lying.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Streets don't—”

Wasabi disappears over the roof edge.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Wasabi!”

Her hand appears over the top.

Points forward.

**WASABI:** “That way!”

Then it vanishes again.

---

## SPLIT UP — COVER MORE GROUND

Wasabi turns on the other two students.

Points left.

**WASABI:** “Roofs.”

Points right.

**WASABI:** “Street.”

The first student folds their arms.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Since when are you in charge?”

Wasabi starts up the wall.

**WASABI:** “Since both of you stopped to ask me which way!”

**ACADEMY STUDENT 2:** “And what are you doing?”

**WASABI:** “Everything between you.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “That's not a route!”

**WASABI:** “Then pick a better one while you're moving!”

They move.

A block later—

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Nothing north!”

From below—

**ACADEMY STUDENT 2:** “Marks near the canal!”

Wasabi stops on a roof ridge.

**WASABI:** “Fresh?”

**ACADEMY STUDENT 2:** “How am I supposed to know?”

Wasabi throws both hands out.

**WASABI:** “Touch the mud!”

A pause.

**ACADEMY STUDENT 2:** “Oh.”

Wasabi closes her eyes for one second.

**WASABI:** “I'm surrounded by geniuses.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “You're welcome!”

That gets a grin out of her.

Three search lines become one pursuit again.

Messier than doing it alone.

Useful anyway.

---

## FORGET THE TRAIL — CUT THEM OFF

Wasabi looks at the tracks.

Then toward the rooftops beyond them.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “You're not following those?”

**WASABI:** “No.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Why?”

Wasabi points toward the distant extraction side of the village.

**WASABI:** “Because they can fake footprints.”

She looks back at the student.

**WASABI:** “They can't fake where they're trying to end up.”

The student looks at the trail.

Then at her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “That's the wrong way.”

Wasabi hops onto the wall.

**WASABI:** “Only if I'm wrong!”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “That's not reassuring!”

Wasabi is already gone.

---

# SCENE 3 — THE SPLIT

Farther into the village, the pursuit opens up.

Rooftops thin near the river.

A stronger trail cuts inland.

Somebody shouts from another street.

Beyond all three is the extraction side of the route.

Wasabi catches a flash of movement ahead.

Maybe the target.

Maybe bait.

She clicks her tongue.

Too many directions.

Not enough time.

She has one decision.

### CHOICE

**TAKE THE RIVER — MAKE UP THE DISTANCE**

**FOLLOW THE STRONGER TRAIL**

**CHECK THE SHOUTING**

**CUT AHEAD — MAKE THEM COME TO ME**

# SCENE 4A — THE RIVER

Wasabi drops from the roofs to the river path.

Immediately loses height.

**WASABI:** “Ugh. Fine.”

The river bends wider than the streets above.

Longer route.

Fewer corners.

No alleys.

No roofs to disappear behind.

Just open ground.

Wasabi leans forward.

**WASABI:** “Run, then.”

So she does.

The first stretch burns.

The second one hurts.

By the third, the village noise is behind her and all she can hear is water, sandals and breath.

She does not slow.

A figure appears ahead where the river path curves back toward extraction.

The target.

Wasabi laughs once.

Not because anything is funny.

Because she found them.

**WASABI:** “HEY!”

The target looks back.

Their eyes widen.

**TARGET:** “You took the river?”

**WASABI:** “And I'm still here!”

The target runs harder.

So does Wasabi.

No trail now.

No guessing.

No one else talking.

Just the two of them and the marker ahead.

The target reaches the last straight first.

Wasabi starts closing it.

One step.

Two.

Three.

The target looks back again.

Bad idea.

Wasabi gains another stride.

**TARGET:** “Seriously?!”

**WASABI:** “Keep running!”

The target reaches for the extraction marker.

Wasabi launches.

Her hand catches the back of their shirt before their foot crosses the line.

Both of them nearly hit the ground.

Wasabi plants one foot, slides, and refuses to let go.

The proctor's arm drops.

**PROCTOR:** “Target secured.”

For a second, neither student says anything.

Both are trying to breathe.

Then Wasabi raises one finger without standing up straight.

**WASABI:** “Say it again.”

The proctor looks at her.

**PROCTOR:** “Target secured.”

Wasabi grins at the target.

The target groans.

---

# SCENE 4B — THE STRONGER TRAIL

The inland trail looks better than the first one.

Fresh marks.

Broken twig.

Scuffed wall.

A scrap of cloth caught on a fence.

Wasabi gets faster with every sign.

**WASABI:** “There you are.”

The trail turns into a narrow yard.

And dies.

No target.

No exit.

No next mark.

Wasabi stops in the middle of the yard.

The scrap moves in the breeze.

She pulls it from the fence.

Turns it over.

Looks at the wall.

A faint scrape sits behind her.

Not into the yard.

Away from it.

Wasabi's shoulders drop.

**WASABI:** “Oh, come on.”

She points the scrap at the empty wall.

**WASABI:** “You planted this.”

Nobody answers.

Which somehow makes it worse.

She starts back out.

Stops.

Looks at the scrap again.

**WASABI:** “...Okay.”

A beat.

**WASABI:** “That was good.”

She stuffs it into her pocket.

**WASABI:** “Still annoying.”

Then she runs.

---

# SCENE 4C — THE SHOUTING

Wasabi takes three steps toward extraction.

Another shout cuts across the street.

Closer this time.

She stops.

Looks toward extraction.

Looks toward the shout.

**WASABI:** “Seriously?”

She changes direction.

Around the next corner, an Academy student is backed against a wall.

A Rogue Genin stands between them and the street.

Older.

Bigger.

Very much not part of Wasabi's plan.

The student sees her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Wasabi!”

The Rogue Genin looks over.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Keep running.”

Wasabi's eyes narrow.

**WASABI:** “Wasn't asking you.”

The target is still somewhere ahead.

The extraction clock is still moving.

The Rogue Genin smiles.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Then don't stop.”

Wasabi's jaw tightens.

### CHOICE

**STEP IN — GET AWAY FROM THEM**

**CALL THE INSTRUCTOR — MOVE THE STUDENT**

**KEEP GOING — THE TARGET IS STILL MOVING**

## KEEP GOING — THE TARGET IS STILL MOVING
