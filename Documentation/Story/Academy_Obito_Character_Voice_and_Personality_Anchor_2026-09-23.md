## 2. Canon baseline relevant to this age

Official Naruto material establishes young Obito as:
- openly ambitious about becoming Hokage;
- proud of being Uchiha;
- training for strength and for a Sharingan that has not yet awakened;
- habitually willing to stop when people need help, often becoming late because of those detours;
- emotionally transparent beneath attempts at bravado;
- prone to putting up a strong front when embarrassed, hurt or insecure;
- strongly people-oriented despite wanting recognition and achievement.

Primary official research:
- NARUTO Official Site, Obito birthday column, 2024-02-10:

- NARUTO Official Site, Kakashi birthday column Part 2, which recalls Obito's habitual lateness from helping others:

## 3. Current Shinobi Chronicles era / constraints

Current Academy Obito:
- is an Academy student;
- has **NO Sharingan**;
- has no Madara / masked-man / later-life history;
- sincerely wants to become Hokage;
- sincerely wants to improve as a shinobi;
- has rough Uchiha fundamentals and only Academy-scale capability allowed by current Combat/Skill authority;
- is not assigned a permanent kindness, lateness, selfishness, balance or ambition Trait from this Origin.

The current Origin tests competition between:
- other people's immediate needs; and
- Obito's real opportunity for his own development.

Neither side is a morality axis.

## 4. Core temperament

Academy Obito is:
- earnest;
- energetic;
- socially expressive;
- ambitious;
- competitive with himself and with the image of the shinobi he wants to become;
- quick to become embarrassed;
- quick to defend his pride verbally;
- more emotionally readable than he wants to be;
- impulsive enough to act before he has perfectly organised his priorities;
- capable of genuine reflection after the pressure has passed.

He is not:
- adult Obito in miniature;
- cynically detached;
- an endless joke character;
- a generic cheerful shōnen protagonist;
- permanently selfless;
- permanently irresponsible.

## 5. Speech behaviour

Obito generally:
- speaks more than Kakashi would;
- answers pressure with assertion;
- uses confidence to cover uncertainty;
- may overstate certainty about what he is going to accomplish;
- becomes faster/louder when embarrassed;
- can complain while still helping;
- often says the hopeful or ambitious thing before he has fully proven it.

His humour is usually:
- situational;
- self-defensive;
- competitive;
- a way to recover social footing after embarrassment.

Avoid making every line a punchline.

## 6. Physical / emotional performance

Obito's feelings should often leak through behaviour:
- sprinting harder after realising he is late;
- adjusting his goggles when gathering himself;
- visibly reacting when someone notices his lateness;
- committing his whole body to a task once he decides to help;
- overdoing a training repetition because he hates that he missed earlier work;
- staring longer than he means to at the Hokage Monument;
- talking himself into a position before he is completely sure of it.

Embarrassment should not make him silently Kakashi-like.
Frustration should not make him adult-Obito-cold.

## 7. Central Origin contradiction

Both of these statements are true:

> Obito genuinely wants to become stronger.

> Obito genuinely notices when somebody needs help.

The Origin is interesting because those desires consume the same finite time.

Writing must not solve this contradiction for the player.

His five field decisions record **behaviour**.
His final home decision records **current interpretation**.

Behaviour != interpretation.

## 8. Final-reflection range

All of these remain legitimate Obito conclusions regardless of the exact 0–5 help pattern:

### Keep helping people

Obito may decide that becoming stronger is meaningless if strength requires training himself not to notice people.

This should remain ambitious, not saintly:
he can conclude that the answer is to organise himself better, leave earlier, train harder, or stop creating the time conflict.

### Protect training more seriously

Obito may decide that wanting to become Hokage requires actually protecting the work needed to get there.

This is discipline/self-development evidence, not selfishness.

### Find a balance

Obito may decide not every visible problem requires him personally and not every training opportunity should override the village around him.

This is judgement/management evidence, not an automatic ideal answer.

## 9. Speaker-fingerprint test

Remove the speaker label.

A good Academy Obito line should often reveal some combination of:
- aspiration;
- pride;
- embarrassment;
- social awareness;
- emotional leakage;
- determination;
- tendency to turn a setback into a future promise.

Do not give him the project's dry Kakashi/ANBU house voice.

## 10. Final fingerprint

> **Academy Obito wants badly enough to become somebody that every delay frustrates him — and cares about people enough that the reason for the delay can still matter to him.**

That contradiction should be visible before the final reflection ever names it.
