## Purpose

This correction closes two continuity omissions discovered during Academy Kakashi Origin branch completion.

It does **not** restart the Origin, reopen already-closed causal choices, or collapse branch timelines. It applies as a continuity correction to the existing sealed branch tree and must be consumed during remaining authoring and the final consolidated Kakashi Origin audit.

## 1. Masked Interceptor must be accounted for in ANBU reporting

On every Kakashi branch where Kakashi materially encounters **Masked Interceptor**, any later ANBU reporting / debrief scene must account for that encounter and her outcome as Kakashi legitimately knows it.

This includes, where applicable:

- Kakashi fighting Masked Interceptor;
- Kakashi defeating her;
- Kakashi losing to her;
- Kakashi leaving her behind;
- Kakashi seeing her disengage or pursue someone else;
- uncertainty about her later survival, location, or destination where Kakashi does not know more.

The report must not skip Masked Interceptor simply because the branch later focuses on Package Smuggler, ANBU Marked Target, Pakkun, package custody, or the final disposition choice.

Kakashi reports from his own observed reality. The ANBU debriefer must remain spoiler-safe and must not reveal hidden evaluation truth through leading questions or hidden-role terminology.

This reinforces and applies the existing `Mission_Debrief_Factual_Completeness_Lock_2026-09-14.md` specifically to all Kakashi endgame/reporting scenes.

## 2. Pakkun is mandatory whenever Kakashi reaches ANBU Marked Target

Stephen's current Story correction is binding:

> **Whenever a Kakashi Origin branch legitimately reaches ANBU Marked Target, Pakkun is part of that encounter.**

Pakkun must not be limited to only one arbitrarily selected successful path if another sealed Kakashi branch also reaches ANBU Marked Target.

Therefore:

- if ANBU Marked Target is unreachable, escaped, dead, or otherwise not reached in that branch, this rule does not fabricate a Pakkun encounter;
- if Kakashi **does reach ANBU Marked Target**, Pakkun must be visibly present as part of that scene;
- the recovered first-contact exchange remains compatible where that first contact occurs:
  - **PAKKUN:** `This yours?`
  - **KAKASHI:** `Apparently.`
  - **KAKASHI:** `Thanks.`
- `This yours?` refers to **ANBU Marked Target**, not the package.

This correction has precedence over older narrower wording that said only one later Observe-derived branch reaches Pakkun. The correct branch predicate is now:

`Kakashi legitimately reaches ANBU Marked Target -> Pakkun encounter is present.`

## 3. Pakkun must not silently disappear from subsequent scenes

Once Pakkun appears with Kakashi at ANBU Marked Target, Pakkun remains a factually present participant through the subsequent connected scene sequence unless an explicit authored departure occurs.

Do not silently remove him between clicks, scene cards, disposition choices, custody transfers, travel beats, or endgame conversation simply because the next beat focuses on Kakashi and ANBU Marked Target.

Where Pakkun is present:

- his card / visual presence should be represented by the Scene Board where the scene shows participants;
- he should receive character-appropriate reactions when consequential actions occur in his presence;
- he must not be treated as a passive invisible flag;
- his presence does not give the player command over him;
- his presence does not imply permanent Summon acquisition, ownership, assignment, or roster acquisition.

If a later branch explicitly authors Pakkun leaving, that departure must be visible and then committed before later scenes may omit him.

Before Academy Kakashi Origin can be declared Writing-closed, the consolidated branch audit must verify both of the following on every applicable branch:

- **Masked Interceptor reporting completeness:** every later ANBU report accounts for her whenever Kakashi encountered her.
