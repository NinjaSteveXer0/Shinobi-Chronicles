## Context

At home during the ongoing A2M3 post-operation family scene, Menma has just helped Naruto reconsider his relationship with the other half of Kurama. Naruto now knows the name `Kurama`, understands that Kurama is a person rather than merely a power source, has been told that Kurama's refusal should matter, and has promised to ask before sharing anything if his Kurama ever speaks to him.

Menma and his Kurama have also just articulated that asking for chakra while intending to ignore `no` would not be genuine asking.

Stephen then selected a new explicit player action:

> **Menma asks Kurama whether they were wrong to try to steer Naruto toward using his Kurama's power partly because increased use/interaction might give Menma's Kurama more freedom.**

This is a deliberate tonal/character correction away from automatic family harmony. The scene must test Menma and Kurama's mixed motives rather than make everyone mutually loving or morally clean.

## Locked Story direction

The resulting internal exchange must preserve:

- Menma is capable of recognising his own contradiction and asking the question directly.
- Kurama is proud, self-interested and not required to respond with easy remorse.
- Their earlier motive was mixed: concern/curiosity about Naruto may coexist with a selfish desire for more freedom for Menma's Kurama.
- Neither Menma nor Kurama gets to retroactively rename manipulation as pure benevolence.
- The revelation does not erase genuine care for Naruto; it makes the motive non-pure.
- Menma's disclosures to Naruto can have been sincerely helpful **and** strategically useful to Menma/Kurama at the same time.
- Naruto and Kushina do not gain Knowledge of this internal reckoning unless Menma later tells them.
- No route/alignment/morality Trait is auto-assigned from this choice.
- No exact seal/freedom mechanic is invented here beyond the already-held belief/hope that Naruto engaging more deeply with his Kurama could benefit Menma's Kurama's freedom.
