## Minato Namikaze — usable canon personality anchor

Official Naruto material consistently supports Minato as:

- calm and controlled under extreme pressure;
- exceptionally decisive and tactically competent;
- responsibility-focused rather than performatively authoritarian;
- capable of warmth, praise and empathy without losing operational clarity;
- willing to act personally when duty requires it;
- self-critical about failures and willing to carry responsibility;
- protective of family/village without becoming emotionally incoherent.

Official sources consumed:

- NARUTO OFFICIAL SITE — `Yellow Flash of Konoha: Tales of Minato Namikaze's Valor`
  - https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1517
  - shows Minato as feared externally, trusted by allies, tactically decisive, personally responsible and capable of praising/advising opponents mid-conflict.
- NARUTO OFFICIAL SITE — Minato birthday retrospective
  - https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1681
  - reinforces responsibility, regret, teamwork and continuing effort to make amends.

### SC application

Current SC Minato is alive, active Fourth Hokage, husband of Kushina and father of Naruto/Menma.

Do not write him as:

- passive bureaucracy incarnate;
- omniscient;
- emotionally detached from family merely because he is Hokage;
- secretly evil to make Sasuke look correct;
- incapable of admitting institutional failure.

His late-Arc political weakness should come from **the costs of centralised verification, secrecy, continuity and command under adversarial information pressure**, not stupidity.

## Sasuke Uchiha — usable canon personality anchor

Official Naruto material supports Sasuke as:

- reserved and self-directed;
- intensely motivated by truth/history once personal certainty breaks;
- capable of rejecting inherited narratives and seeking primary answers before choosing a course;
- proud, analytical and willing to act independently;
- emotionally intense beneath controlled presentation;
- capable of radical institutional conclusions when he believes the existing order has failed;
- not naturally inclined to soften his position merely for social comfort;
- capable of later recognising error when confronted by compelling lived evidence.

Official sources consumed:

- NARUTO OFFICIAL SITE — Sasuke birthday retrospective Part 1
  - https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1856
- NARUTO OFFICIAL SITE — Sasuke birthday retrospective Part 2
  - https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1858
  - especially the sequence where Sasuke seeks the previous Hokage to understand clan/village history before choosing a new path, then later formulates a radical theory of Hokage/revolution.

### Critical SC divergence boundary

Do **not** import baseline-canon Sasuke biography automatically.

Current SC continuity has substantial Uchiha divergences and does not authorise assumptions such as:

- identical Uchiha massacre history;
- identical Itachi/revenge trajectory;
- identical Orochimaru defection;
- identical Five Kage assassination plan;
- identical Final Valley reconciliation history.

Those canon events are personality/context anchors only where compatible with current SC Recorded History.

### SC late-Arc application

Current durable authority fixes Sasuke as the Arc-3 headline challenger to Minato with substantial support earned through Arc 2.

Write current Sasuke as:

- someone who sees repeated institutional information failures as evidence that Konoha's command architecture itself may be defective;
- willing to create an independent accountability/security response when he believes waiting for Hokage verification will lose lives/evidence;
- capable of protecting Konoha while directly challenging Minato's monopoly on emergency authority;
- concise, controlled and unsentimental in political argument;
- persuasive through competence, evidence and willingness to act rather than populist speeches.

Do not write him as a generic edgy revolutionary or automatic Uchiha spokesman.

## Minato vs Sasuke dramatic distinction

The late Arc works only if both men remain credible.

### Minato's strongest argument

> A village under information attack cannot survive if every powerful person creates their own command structure whenever they distrust incomplete information.

He values:

- verification;
- continuity;
- bounded disclosure;
- lawful command;
- preventing adversaries from manufacturing panic through false urgency.

### Sasuke's strongest argument

> A command structure that repeatedly fails to detect compromised information and then asks everyone to keep waiting for the same structure to verify itself cannot claim unlimited deference forever.

He values:

- independent verification;
- visible accountability;
- decisive protection when delay itself is dangerous;
- reducing the ability of compromised systems to suppress or monopolise evidence.

Neither argument is a route-family label.

Neither man is automatically correct.

## Voice separation

### Minato

- calm, measured, economical;
- can explain reasoning without over-talking;
- avoids performative anger;
- decisive language when command commits;
- may acknowledge cost/error directly.

### Sasuke

- sparse, direct, controlled;
- questions assumptions rather than giving long lectures;
- little decorative humour;
- pride shows through certainty and refusal to defer;
- when emotional, intensity sharpens rather than becoming verbose.

Canonical test:

> **A Minato line and a Sasuke line should not be swappable without changing the scene.**

---

## Late-Arc writing rule

M10–M12 must make Sasuke's support grow through factual performance and political interpretation while preserving Minato as a legitimate Hokage with real strengths.

The conflict is:

> **Who has earned the right to decide under uncertainty when the information system itself is contested?**

It is not:

> `good Sasuke vs bad Minato`

or

> `lawful Minato vs evil rebel Sasuke`.
