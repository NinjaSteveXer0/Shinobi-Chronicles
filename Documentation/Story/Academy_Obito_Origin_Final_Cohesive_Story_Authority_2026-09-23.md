# 6. Player-facing Story

## SCENE 1 — LATE AGAIN

The first thing Obito notices is the time.

The second is that noticing it hasn't made him any less late.

He launches himself out the door with one sandal not quite sitting right, catches the strap while moving, nearly clips the corner of the building and keeps going.

Training.

Today was supposed to be different.

No wandering around. No getting distracted. No arriving halfway through something and pretending that was the plan.

Actual training.

The kind that gets him closer to where he's going.

Obito pulls his goggles properly into place and accelerates through the morning traffic.

Above the rooftops, the Hokage Monument catches the light.

His eyes find it automatically.

One day.

Not *maybe*.

Not if the Sharingan decides to hurry up.

Not if the rest of the Uchiha suddenly starts taking him seriously.

One day, his face is going up there.

Obito points at the mountain without slowing.

**OBITO:** "Just wait."

A passer-by turns, wondering who he is talking to.

Obito immediately drops his hand.

**OBITO:** "Wasn't talking to you."

He runs harder.

He is late enough that he needs to hurry.

Not late enough to have missed the formal training session.

Yet.

### OBJECTIVE

**GET TO TRAINING.**

---

# SCENE 2 — INTERRUPTION #1: THE FURNITURE

Obito rounds the next corner and almost runs straight into the back of a wardrobe.

He stops so hard his heel skids.

An older civilian has managed to get the thing halfway through a doorway.

Halfway is generous.

The top corner is wedged against the frame. One leg is caught on the raised threshold. The woman pushing from behind is achieving nothing except becoming increasingly angry at the furniture.

She looks around the side of it.

Then at Obito.

Then at his Academy clothes.

**CIVILIAN:** "Training?"

Obito looks down the street.

The Academy isn't far.

**OBITO:** "Yeah."

The wardrobe slips half an inch.

She catches it with her shoulder.

**CIVILIAN:** "Then don't let me make you late."

Obito looks at the wardrobe again.

It is very obviously making her lose this argument.

His foot shifts toward the Academy.

Then stops.

### CHOICE

**HELP HER**

**KEEP GOING**

## IF OBITO HELPS

Obito drops his bag beside the wall.

**OBITO:** "You're trying to turn it too early."

The woman gives him a look.

**CIVILIAN:** "I've been moving furniture longer than you've been alive."

**OBITO:** "Yeah, well, right now the furniture's winning."

Her look gets worse.

Obito gets both hands beneath the lower edge.

**OBITO:** "Lift your side when I say."

**CIVILIAN:** "You're an Academy student."

**OBITO:** "An Uchiha Academy student."

That apparently settles it in his mind.

He braces himself.

**OBITO:** "Now."

They lift.

The wardrobe scrapes the frame, catches again—

**OBITO:** "Wait. Wait."

He shifts his grip, angles the bottom away from the threshold and puts his shoulder into it.

The corner clears.

The wardrobe finally slides through.

Obito nearly goes with it.

He catches himself against the wall.

The woman looks at the furniture.

Then at him.

**CIVILIAN:** "You were right."

Obito straightens immediately.

**OBITO:** "Obviously."

A beat.

He snatches his bag from the ground.

**OBITO:** "I mean—good. Great. You got it."

**CIVILIAN:** "Your training?"

Obito freezes.

Then bolts.

**OBITO:** "I KNOW!"

By the time he reaches the end of the street, the small victory already feels expensive.

The Academy clock has not stopped because he did.

## IF OBITO KEEPS GOING

Obito's feet slow.

The wardrobe shifts again behind him.

He looks back.

The woman has already reset her grip.

Training is still ahead.

And it matters.

Obito forces his attention back onto the road.

**OBITO:** "Sorry. I'm already late."

He starts running again before he can turn the sentence into a conversation.

The woman remains behind him with the furniture.

Obito doesn't know how long it takes her to get it through the doorway.

He only knows he didn't stop.

And that, for once, he is still making up time.

---

# SCENE 3 — INTERRUPTION #2: THE VEGETABLES

Two streets later, something green rolls across Obito's path.

He jumps over it.

A second one follows.

Then three more.

Obito looks toward the source.

A vegetable vendor is kneeling beside a split basket, trying to stop half her stock from spreading across the lane while pedestrians step around the mess.

A daikon bumps against Obito's sandal.

The vendor sees it.

So does Obito.

He looks toward training.

Then down at the daikon.

**OBITO:** "You've got to be kidding me."

The vendor hears him.

**VENDOR:** "If you're volunteering, complain while you work."

Obito stares at her.

She is already reaching for another rolling vegetable.

### CHOICE

**HELP HER**

**KEEP GOING**

## IF OBITO HELPS

Obito snatches up the daikon.

**OBITO:** "I wasn't volunteering."

He catches a tomato before a sandal comes down on it.

**OBITO:** "I was making an observation."

**VENDOR:** "Make it faster."

Obito gets down beside her.

One vegetable has rolled beneath a bench.

Another is heading for the gutter.

Obito lunges after both with considerably more commitment than the situation deserves.

**OBITO:** "Why are vegetables this fast?"

**VENDOR:** "Why are Academy students this late?"

Obito looks up.

The vendor does not.

She is smiling.

**OBITO:** "I'm not late."

She looks at him now.

Obito glances toward the Academy.

**OBITO:** "I'm becoming late."

**VENDOR:** "Ah."

They get the last of the produce back into the basket.

The vendor holds up the repaired load.

**VENDOR:** "There."

Obito is already standing.

**OBITO:** "Good."

He gets three steps away.

**VENDOR:** "Thank you."

That stops him for just a moment.

Obito looks back.

**OBITO:** "Yeah."

Then he runs.

Faster this time.

As though speed after the fact can bargain with a clock.

## IF OBITO KEEPS GOING

Obito steps over the daikon.

His body wants to turn before he does.

He keeps moving.

**OBITO:** "Someone else can help."

Whether he is saying it to himself or the vendor is not completely clear.

The vendor is already trying to gather the nearest pieces.

Other people are in the street.

Obito is not the only person in Konoha.

He runs.

The vegetables disappear behind him.

Training gets closer.

---

# SCENE 4 — INTERRUPTION #3: THE MISSING EQUIPMENT

By the third interruption, Obito no longer trusts corners.

This one waits for him beside the Academy route.

An equipment custodian has opened two storage bundles on the pavement and is searching both with increasing disbelief.

Wooden practice equipment is stacked beside him.

One bundle is conspicuously absent.

**CUSTODIAN:** "No. No, no…"

Obito recognises the Academy markings immediately.

He slows.

The custodian looks up.

**CUSTODIAN:** "Obito."

Of course he knows his name.

That somehow makes this worse.

**OBITO:** "I'm on my way there."

**CUSTODIAN:** "Then you've got better timing than my equipment."

Obito looks toward the path behind the man.

A missing bundle.

A route he knows.

A training session he is supposed to be attending.

If Obito has already stopped more than once, he doesn't need a clock to tell him what those stops are doing.

If he hasn't stopped yet, this is the first interruption that belongs to the same Academy he is trying so hard to reach.

Either way, the problem sits directly in front of him.

### CHOICE

**HELP SEARCH**

**KEEP GOING**

## IF OBITO HELPS

Obito drops into a crouch near the empty equipment bindings.

**OBITO:** "Where did you last have it?"

**CUSTODIAN:** "East storage."

**OBITO:** "And you came straight here?"

**CUSTODIAN:** "Yes."

Obito looks back down the route.

Not everywhere.

Just the places something could fall without being immediately obvious.

He runs to the low retaining wall beside the lane.

Nothing.

Checks the turn near the drainage channel.

Nothing.

Then—

A strip of Academy cloth caught against the underside of a delivery platform.

Obito drops flat and reaches beneath it.

His fingers catch the missing strap.

**OBITO:** "Found it!"

The custodian comes over as Obito drags the bundle out.

**CUSTODIAN:** "How did you—"

**OBITO:** "It had to be somewhere."

The custodian looks at the dust now covering the front of Obito's clothes.

**CUSTODIAN:** "Training."

Obito's expression dies.

**OBITO:** "Right."

He shoves the bundle into the man's hands and takes off.

**CUSTODIAN:** "Obito!"

Obito looks back while running.

**CUSTODIAN:** "Good find."

Obito grins despite himself.

Then remembers what it cost him and runs even harder.

## IF OBITO KEEPS GOING

Obito sees the Academy marking.

For half a second, that nearly decides it.

Then he looks past the custodian toward the training ground.

**OBITO:** "I can't. I'm already going there."

The custodian follows his look.

Understands.

**CUSTODIAN:** "Then go."

No accusation.

No lecture.

Just an answer.

Obito nods once and runs.

The missing equipment remains somebody else's problem.

For now.

---

# SCENE 5 — INTERRUPTION #4: THE OVERTURNED DELIVERY

The fourth interruption is waiting in the middle of the road.

Literally.

A delivery cart has tipped onto one side.

Crates have spilled across half the lane. The worker is trying to lever the cart upright while simultaneously keeping one badly stacked crate from collapsing.

Obito stops at the sight of it.

Looks up at the sky.

**OBITO:** "Seriously?"

The delivery worker thinks Obito is talking to him.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** "You can go around."

Obito looks toward the narrow passage beside the cart.

He can.

That's what makes the decision worse.

Nothing is trapping him here.

Nobody has asked him to become responsible for this.

Training is still happening without him.

### CHOICE

**HELP WITH THE DELIVERY**

**KEEP MOVING**

## IF OBITO HELPS

Obito drops his bag.

Again.

**OBITO:** "Move."

The worker blinks.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** "Excuse me?"

**OBITO:** "Not you. Your hand."

Obito wedges himself against the lower side of the cart.

**OBITO:** "If we lift from the same side, it'll roll back."

The worker adjusts.

Together they heave.

The cart rises.

For a second it threatens to tip the other way.

Obito throws his weight against it.

**OBITO:** "Not that far!"

**DELIVERY WORKER:** "I noticed!"

They get it level.

Then the crates.

Obito grabs one, then another.

The worker takes the heavier stack.

It doesn't take forever.

It takes longer than Obito has.

When the road is clear enough for the delivery to move again, the worker points at Obito's Academy clothes.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** "Go."

Obito is breathing harder now.

**OBITO:** "I was going."

**DELIVERY WORKER:** "Go faster."

Obito grabs his bag.

**OBITO:** "I am!"

And he does.

## IF OBITO KEEPS MOVING

Obito looks at the cart.

Then at the open space beside it.

This one has an easy answer.

He hates how relieved that makes him feel.

**OBITO:** "You've got room on the left."

The worker nods toward it.

**DELIVERY WORKER:** "I know."

Obito realises that wasn't actually useful.

He stops talking and takes the gap.

The delivery remains behind him.

The Academy gets closer.

Four interruptions.

One left, though Obito has no way of knowing that.

---

# SCENE 6 — INTERRUPTION #5: THE RUNAWAY CART

Obito can see the Academy roofs now.

Close enough to make the distance feel cruel.

Then somebody shouts.

A handcart has broken loose farther up the slope.

It picks up speed as it rolls into the lane.

People scatter.

Two adults are already moving toward it from one side. Another civilian is trying to clear the path.

Nobody is standing helplessly waiting for Obito Uchiha to save the village.

They may manage it without him.

They may not.

Obito looks at the cart.

Then at the Academy.

A bell carries over the rooftops.

His training.

The thing he has been trying to reach all morning.

His own future.

The cart strikes a rut and jumps sideways.

One of the adults misjudges the angle and has to retreat.

Obito's feet stop.

**OBITO:** "Come on…"

This is the last interruption.

He doesn't know that either.

He only knows what is in front of him.

### CHOICE

**STOP AND HELP**

**GO TO TRAINING**

## IF OBITO HELPS

Obito throws his bag against the wall and runs downhill.

**CIVILIAN:** "Kid—!"

Too late.

Obito gets alongside the cart rather than in front of it.

No Sharingan.

No perfect read.

Just speed, timing and the immediate certainty that being flattened would be embarrassing.

He catches the side rail.

The cart yanks him forward.

**OBITO:** "Okay—bad idea—"

His sandals scrape across the road.

One of the adults reaches the opposite side.

Together they force the front wheel toward the curb.

The cart slams into it.

Bounces.

Stops.

Obito keeps hold of it for another second because his arms haven't received the message yet.

Then he lets go.

Silence.

The adult beside him catches his breath.

**CIVILIAN:** "You all right?"

Obito straightens.

His hands sting.

His shoulder hurts.

His pride is completely intact.

**OBITO:** "Obviously."

The adult looks at him.

Obito glances at his scraped palms.

**OBITO:** "…Mostly."

The Academy bell sounds again.

Obito's eyes widen.

**OBITO:** "Training!"

He grabs his bag and runs.

Behind him, the other adults take control of the cart.

Obito does not look back this time.

There is nothing left he can do there.

## IF OBITO GOES TO TRAINING

Obito watches the cart for one more second.

Two adults are already moving.

A third has started clearing people from the lane.

They do not need a fourth person just because Obito noticed.

His training is right there.

**OBITO:** "They've got it."

This time he makes the decision before his feet make it for him.

He turns.

Runs.

The shouting continues behind him for a few seconds before distance takes it away.

Obito doesn't know exactly how the cart incident resolves.

He knows only that he chose not to enter it.

And that the Academy gate is finally ahead.

---

# SCENE 7 — ARRIVAL

Obito hits the Academy training ground at a run.

How much of the day is left depends on the time he actually spent getting here.

Not whether he was "good."

Not whether he was "selfish."

Time.

Nothing more complicated than that.

## FULL TRAINING ENTITLEMENT

Obito reaches the yard just as the instructor is calling everyone into position.

He slows so abruptly that the student behind him nearly walks into his back.

The instructor looks at him.

Obito waits.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "Cutting it close."

Obito checks the training yard.

Everybody is still assembling.

No drill has started.

He straightens.

**OBITO:** "But I'm here."

The instructor's eyes narrow slightly.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "That wasn't praise."

**OBITO:** "Still true."

A whistle cuts across the yard.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "Line up."

Obito grins and joins the others.

He made it.

All of it is still available.

## SUBSTANTIAL TRAINING ENTITLEMENT

Obito reaches the yard to the sound of the first training block ending.

Students are already breathing hard.

A few are stretching their legs as the equipment racks are brought forward.

The instructor turns at the sound of Obito's approach.

Looks at him.

Looks at the completed conditioning group.

Back to him.

Obito bends forward with his hands on his knees.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "You missed conditioning."

Obito lifts one finger.

Still catching his breath.

**OBITO:** "I conditioned myself getting here."

The instructor does not react.

Obito lowers the finger.

**OBITO:** "…That doesn't count."

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "No."

The first weapons are being handed out.

Obito stands.

**OBITO:** "Then I'm not missing the rest."

He joins the line.

## REDUCED TRAINING ENTITLEMENT

Obito reaches the ground as the weapon racks are being cleared away.

His eyes go from the students carrying equipment back to storage—

to the training marks already carved into the dirt—

to the instructor.

Two blocks gone.

The instructor doesn't need to tell him.

Still does.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "You're late."

Obito is too out of breath to defend himself properly.

**OBITO:** "I noticed."

A few students glance toward him.

That gives him enough energy to stand straighter.

**OBITO:** "What's next?"

The instructor studies him for a moment.

Then gestures toward the marked training space.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "Ninjutsu fundamentals."

Obito's frustration doesn't disappear.

It sharpens.

**OBITO:** "Good."

He drops his bag and moves into position.

If half the session is gone, then half the session is what he has.

## MINIMAL TRAINING ENTITLEMENT

The smell reaches Obito before the training ground does.

Dust.

Sweat.

The faint scorched-air trace left by the Ninjutsu block.

His expression changes before he even reaches the gate.

By the time he enters, the class is already reorganising into pairs.

The last drill.

Taijutsu.

The instructor watches Obito approach.

There isn't much anger in the look.

That might be worse.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "You're here."

Obito looks at the ground behind the instructor.

The completed drills are everywhere.

Tracks in the dirt.

Equipment being put away.

The Ninjutsu markers already darkened from use.

All the training he wanted.

Already happened.

Almost all of it.

Obito swallows the first thing he wants to say.

**OBITO:** "What's left?"

The instructor points toward the pairs forming across the yard.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "Closing drill."

Obito drops his bag.

**OBITO:** "Then put me in."

No excuse.

No request for the other blocks back.

There is one opportunity left.

He takes it.

---

# SCENE 8 — FORMAL TRAINING

The training doesn't care how Obito got here.

It only cares whether he is here for the drill when it happens.

## STAMINA — FULL ONLY

The opening conditioning block turns the yard into a repeated circuit of acceleration, direction changes and recovery.

Obito attacks the first lap like somebody is going to award Hokage immediately afterward.

By the third, the mistake becomes obvious.

His breathing turns ragged.

The instructor passes him.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "You don't win conditioning on the first lap."

Obito pushes harder.

**OBITO:** "Wasn't trying to."

The instructor looks at him.

Obito's lungs are currently making the lie difficult to support.

He adjusts.

A little.

Not elegantly.

Enough to finish.

## BUKIJUTSU — FULL / SUBSTANTIAL

Wooden Academy weapons come off the racks.

Grip.

Stance.

Release.

Recovery.

Again.

Obito's first throw hits harder than it needs to and worse than he wants it to.

He stares at where it landed.

**OBITO:** "That target moved."

Nobody responds.

Obito looks around.

**OBITO:** "Fine."

He resets his grip.

The second attempt is cleaner.

Then another.

He stops trying to overpower the drill and starts actually paying attention to it.

By the time the instructor moves them on, Obito's last repetition looks nothing like his first.

That matters to him more than he says.

## NINJUTSU — FULL / SUBSTANTIAL / REDUCED

The class moves into chakra-control and Academy-scale Fire fundamentals.

This is the section Obito has been waiting for.

Uchiha.

Fire.

Something he can point at and say *see?*

His first attempt comes out uneven.

Too much force at the beginning.

Not enough control at the end.

The brief flame collapses almost as soon as it forms.

Obito stares at the empty air.

Then immediately prepares again.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "Slow down."

**OBITO:** "I know."

He did not know until the instructor said it.

He tries again.

This time the chakra holds longer.

Still rough.

Still Academy work.

Still nowhere near the strength Obito imagines when he thinks about what being Uchiha is supposed to mean.

But it is better.

Obito watches the last trace of heat disappear.

His frustration and satisfaction arrive together.

**OBITO:** "Again."

The instructor is already moving down the line.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "Again."

Obito grins.

## TAIJUTSU — ALL ENTITLEMENTS

The final block leaves nowhere to hide.

No weapon.

No fire.

No future Sharingan Obito can imagine doing the difficult part for him.

Just stance.

Distance.

Timing.

Another student opposite him.

The signal comes.

Obito moves first.

Too eagerly.

Gets redirected.

Recovers.

Moves again.

He commits hard enough that even his mistakes have momentum.

The other student catches him off-balance.

Obito hits the dirt.

He is back up before the instructor says anything.

Again.

This time he waits half a second longer.

The opening comes to him.

Obito steps inside it and completes the exchange cleanly enough that the instructor notices.

Notices.

Doesn't praise him.

Obito notices that too.

The final whistle sounds.

Students begin to separate.

Obito stays where he is for another second, chest rising and falling.

Whatever amount of training he reached—

all of it,

most of it,

half of it,

or barely the end—

that part was real.

He was here for it.

He did the work.

# SCENE 9 — END OF THE DAY

The yard empties slowly.

Obito takes longer than usual tying his bag.

Partly because his hands are tired.

Partly because he isn't in a hurry anymore.

That feels strange after spending the entire day chasing time.

The instructor leaves him with no speech about priorities.

No judgement about the road he took.

The training itself already supplied the consequence.

What Obito reached, he trained.

What he missed, he missed.

There is nothing to argue with.

By the time he starts home, Konoha has changed colour.

Evening light sits lower between the buildings.

The streets are calmer.

His body remembers whichever drills he managed to reach.

His head remembers the journey.

The wardrobe.

The vegetables.

The missing equipment.

The overturned delivery.

The runaway cart.

Not as five points.

Not as a score.

Five moments.

Five times he had somewhere he wanted to be and something else happened in front of him.

Five times he decided.

Obito walks more slowly now.

Nobody is timing him anymore.

---

# SCENE 10 — HOME

Obito drops his bag beside the wall.

His goggles follow.

Without them, the room feels quieter.

He sits.

For a while he doesn't do anything.

That is unusual enough on its own.

Through the window, the village climbs toward the Hokage Monument in the distance.

Obito looks at it.

Training was supposed to be the easy part of today.

Get there.

Train.

Get stronger.

# ENDING B — TAKE TRAINING MORE SERIOUSLY

Obito looks toward the Monument.

For once, becoming Hokage feels less like something he can shout into existence.

Training happened today whether he was there or not.

That fact bothers him more than any lecture could.

If he helped everyone, the evidence is sitting in everything he missed.

If he helped nobody, the evidence is the opposite: he protected the opportunity, and the opportunity was actually there when he arrived.

If he chose somewhere in the middle, then he already knows the harder truth.

Sometimes the decision matters.

Obito folds his arms.

**OBITO:** "I can't keep saying this matters and then treating my own training like it'll wait for me."

The room gives him no argument.

He doesn't suddenly stop caring about people.

That isn't what he decided.

But not every problem in Konoha has to become Obito Uchiha's problem simply because he saw it.

Sometimes somebody else can handle it.

Sometimes he has somewhere he needs to be.

And if he really intends to put his face on that mountain one day—

showing up is part of the work.

Obito looks toward the Monument.

**OBITO:** "Next time, I get there."

Not someday.

Not eventually.

Next time.

For tonight, that's enough.

# ENDING C — GET BETTER AT BOTH

Obito stares at the ceiling.

Helping everybody and missing training feels stupid.

Walking past everybody just so he can say he trained feels wrong in a completely different way.

He sits up.

**OBITO:** "Maybe I'm solving the wrong problem."

Nobody answers.

That is probably for the best.

Obito starts counting on his fingers.

Leave earlier.

Stop wasting time before he even leaves.

Help when somebody actually needs *him*.

Keep moving when somebody else already has it.

Don't turn every small problem into a personal mission.

Don't use training as an excuse to stop seeing the village around him.

He gets to the last finger.

Looks at it.

**OBITO:** "That's five things."

He drops his hand.

**OBITO:** "That's too many."

A moment later:

**OBITO:** "…Still doing it."

He looks back toward the Hokage Monument.

Maybe becoming stronger and caring about people are not opposite directions.

Maybe Obito is simply bad at managing the road between them.

That, at least, is a problem he can work on.

His expression settles into something more determined than embarrassed.

**OBITO:** "I'll get better at both."

Not perfect.

Not immediately.

Better.

For Obito, that's enough to turn tomorrow into a challenge.

And challenges are easier to live with than excuses.

# ENDING D — FIND WHAT MATTERS MOST

Obito keeps looking at the Hokage Monument.

Normally, that fixes things.

Whenever he falls behind.

Whenever somebody is better than him.

Whenever being Uchiha feels more like something he has to prove than something he gets to be proud of.

One day, his face is going up there.

That's the answer.

It has always been the answer.

Obito opens his mouth.

Nothing comes out.

Today doesn't fit into it as easily as he wants.

Training mattered.

He knows that because missing any of it bothered him.

Helping people mattered too.

He knows that because, when somebody needed something in front of him, simply knowing he had somewhere else to be didn't always make walking away easy.

And somehow he's spent the whole day treating those things like the only two sides available.

Help people.

Train harder.

Find some perfect way to do both.

Obito frowns.

**OBITO:** "Maybe I'm looking at this wrong."

The room stays quiet.

He looks toward the Monument again.

For once, instead of imagining his own face there, he actually looks at the people carved into the mountain.

At what the title is supposed to mean.

At everything Obito has decided it means for him without anybody ever asking why.

Become stronger.

Awaken the Sharingan.

Make the Uchiha acknowledge him.

Become Hokage.

Then—

What?

Obito lowers his eyes.

**OBITO:** "What actually matters most to me?"

He expects an answer.

Something immediate and obvious.

Nothing comes.

That frustrates him.

Then, strangely, it doesn't.

Maybe he doesn't know yet.

Maybe that's allowed.

Obito picks up his goggles and turns them over in his hands.

The five decisions he made today are still real.

So is the training he reached.

None of it becomes meaningless just because he doesn't know what lesson to force out of it.

He puts the goggles back on.

**OBITO:** "Fine."

A small breath.

Then the familiar determination comes back—but quieter this time.

Not a boast.

Not a promise to the whole village.

Something aimed at himself.

**OBITO:** "Then I'll figure it out."

Outside, the Hokage Monument is still there.

For the first time today, Obito doesn't need to point at it.

He just looks.

And wonders.
