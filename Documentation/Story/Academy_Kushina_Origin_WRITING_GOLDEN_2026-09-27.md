# PLAYER-FACING STORY

## SCENE 1 — SEALING PRACTICAL

### `kus_open_01` — narration

The practice scroll lies open on the courtyard stone between Kushina and another Academy student. The formula is simple enough to be insulting right up until one of the inked lines shivers out of place.

### `kus_open_02` — narration

Chakra spits through the break. The scroll bucks hard enough to lift one edge from the ground, and the student beside it freezes with both hands still hovering over the pattern.

Kushina sees the next pulse building.

### `kus_crisis` — choice

**GET THE STUDENT CLEAR**

**CONTAIN THE DAMAGED SEAL**

**MOVE THE SCROLL TO THE SAFETY LANE**

**CORRECT THE FORMULA**

---

# ROUTE A — GET THE STUDENT CLEAR

### `kus_protect_student_01` — narration

The damaged ink crawls past its guide marks. Kushina catches the classmate by the arm and yanks them clear just before chakra lashes across the stone where they had been kneeling.

### `kus_protect_student_02` — dialogue — CLASSMATE

**CLASSMATE:** “I could've moved.”

### `kus_protect_student_03` — dialogue — KUSHINA

**KUSHINA:** “You were still staring at it.”

### `kus_protect_student_04` — narration

The instructor steps in while Kushina keeps the student back and secures the scroll before it can discharge again.

The formula remains somebody else's problem. The classmate does not.

**AUTHOR:** no qualifying KUS-01 work; no reverse summon; no Gerotora first contact.

**Next:** `kus_ordinary_close_01`

# ROUTE B — CONTAIN THE DAMAGED SEAL

### `kus_contain_seal_01` — narration

Kushina drops beside the scroll. The original pattern is already torn badly enough that restoring it exactly would take longer than the leaking chakra is willing to give her.

So she stops trying.

### `kus_contain_seal_02` — narration

Her fingers move along the damaged edge and close a new boundary around the leak. It is not the exercise she was assigned, but the loose chakra folds back inside the line and stays there.

### `kus_contain_seal_03` — dialogue — INSTRUCTOR

**INSTRUCTOR:** “That's not the original formula.”

### `kus_contain_seal_04` — dialogue — KUSHINA

**KUSHINA:** “It doesn't need to be pretty. It needs to stop leaking.”

### `kus_contain_seal_05` — narration

The instructor looks from Kushina to the contained scroll.

The original formula is still damaged.

Nothing is leaking anymore.

**AUTHOR:** KUS-01 qualifying Fūinjutsu work; contained != corrected; no Technique/mastery; no reverse summon.

**Next:** `kus_ordinary_close_01`

# ROUTE C — MOVE THE SCROLL TO THE SAFETY LANE

### `kus_move_object_01` — narration

Kushina grabs the unstable scroll before the instructor can tell her not to. The next pulse starts under her fingers.

She hurls it into the cleared safety lane.

### `kus_move_object_02` — narration

The scroll hits empty ground and flashes hard enough to make the classmate flinch. By the time it settles, the instructor is already moving in to secure it.

### `kus_move_object_03` — dialogue — INSTRUCTOR

**INSTRUCTOR:** “That was not the assignment.”

### `kus_move_object_04` — dialogue — KUSHINA

**KUSHINA:** “Neither was exploding.”

The instructor looks at the scorch mark in the empty lane.

He does not argue.

**AUTHOR:** no qualifying KUS-01 work; no reverse summon; no Gerotora first contact.

**Next:** `kus_ordinary_close_01`

# ROUTE D — CORRECT THE FORMULA

### `kus_correct_01` — narration

Kushina drops beside the scroll and follows the break with her eyes. The formula did not fail everywhere. One damaged line stopped closing where it should have.

That, she can fix.

### `kus_correct_02` — narration

She redraws the connection.

For half a heartbeat, the whole seal settles perfectly.

Then it flashes white.

### `kus_reverse_01` — narration

The corrected pattern folds inward as if the courtyard has suddenly acquired depth where there should be none. Kushina throws an arm over her eyes against the light.

When she lowers it, a toad is sitting beside the scroll.

### `kus_gero_01` — dialogue — TOAD

**TOAD:** “...That is not where I was.”

### `kus_gero_02` — dialogue — KUSHINA

**KUSHINA:** “You're a toad.”

### `kus_gero_03` — dialogue — TOAD

**TOAD:** “Excellent observation.”

The toad looks down at the damaged formula, then back at Kushina.

**AUTHOR:** KUS-01 qualifies; KUS-05 first contact commits. No intentional Summoning, contract, ownership or access.
