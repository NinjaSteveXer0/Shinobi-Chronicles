# Shinobi Chronicles — Main Story → Optional World Off-Shoot → Story Reintegration Authority

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Owner:** Writing — Konoha  
**Status:** **BINDING STORY AUTHORING RULE / WORLD CONSUMER REQUEST; IMPLEMENTATION SEPARATE**

## 1. Purpose

Shinobi Chronicles should not force every unresolved thread created by the Main Story to be solved inside the Main Story mission chain.

The Main Story may create durable unresolved facts, mysteries, people, objects, obligations, evidence, relationships, threats or opportunities. World / Missions / Events / Rewards may consume those facts as optional Chronicle off-shoot content when the current Character/history legitimately qualifies.

If the player later resolves, advances, ignores, transforms or loses such an off-shoot, that committed Chronicle history may later be consumed by the Main Story when relevant.

Canonical shorthand:

> **Main Story may create hooks. World may grow them. Later Story may remember what actually happened.**

This extends existing World authority in:

`Documentation/World/Capability_Responsive_Chronicle_Event_Ecology_v3_Consolidated_Lock_2026-09-10.md`

especially its `history_generated_followup` mode and automatic World event-consumer pass.

No second event system is created here.

## 2. Core example — A2M3 Sealed Wall

Current Menma Chronicle example:

- Menma discovered and preserved the Sealed Wall / concealed chamber during Arc 2 Mission 3;
- he moved the preserved chamber state into secret personal custody;
- the contents/person remain unknown;
- the chakra owner remains unknown;
- recognition/continuity/proximity/state-monitoring concepts are known only within the established Knowledge boundaries;
- the null-bridge duration remains unknown;
- Main Story does **not** need to solve this mystery immediately merely because it was discovered during Main Story.

World / Missions / Events / Rewards may author one or more optional follow-up opportunities that become eligible because this exact Chronicle history exists.

Possible content functions include, without predetermining exact outcome:

- identifying a safe investigative method;
- discovering a historical/provenance lead;
- finding someone with relevant Knowledge;
- observing a recognition-state anomaly;
- learning more about the chakra owner without automatically identifying them;
- deciding whether to preserve, conceal, alter, relocate, test or abandon some part of the investigation where current authority permits;
- creating new enemies, witnesses, obligations, Knowledge, relationships, access or later Story pressure.

The Main Story remains free to continue its own authored Arc pressure while that optional thread remains unresolved.

## 3. Reintegration rule

When later Main Story reaches a beat where an off-shoot's committed history is materially relevant, Story/CE may consume that exact history.

Examples:

- if the player identified a real lead, later Story may let that Knowledge change available interpretation/action;
- if the player never pursued the off-shoot, later Story must not pretend they did;
- if the player lost evidence, later Story may preserve that loss;
- if another actor resolved the opportunity first, later Story may consume that World history instead;
- if the player created a relationship/contact during the off-shoot, that person may later become a legitimate Story participant when causally appropriate;
- if the optional chain changes custody/state of the object/person/problem, later Story must consume the new factual state rather than the original snapshot.

Preserve:

> **optional content may become important later without being retroactively mandatory earlier.**

## 4. Main Story boundary

The Main Story owns its authored Arc/Mission invariants and completion predicates.

An optional off-shoot must not become a hidden mandatory blocker unless Writing explicitly authors a later Main Story invariant that requires a fact which can be satisfied through multiple legitimate routes or explicitly upgrades that off-shoot into required Story content.

Therefore:

- unresolved side thread != Main Story stalled;
- optional opportunity != mandatory quest;
- missing optional resolution != fabricated substitute history;
- Main Story relevance != automatic prior completion;
- later Story callback != retroactive canonization of an unplayed event.

## 5. World/Event ownership

World / Missions / Events / Rewards owns:

- exact optional event/quest/hotspot/chain definitions;
- eligibility predicates from committed Story/Chronicle history;
- locations/hosts/participants;
- discovery/actionability;
- expiry/suppression/transformation rules;
- factual outcomes and off-screen/non-action handling;
- contextual rewards/consequences;
- follow-up chain structure.

Writing does not need to pre-author every off-shoot.

Writing should provide the durable unresolved Story facts and any hard boundaries that World must not contradict.

## 6. CE / Chronicle boundary

CE may later select/surface only authored off-shoots that are genuinely eligible from current committed history.

World Truth, observer Knowledge and presentation remain separate.

The existence of a Story hook does not mean the protagonist automatically knows every possible off-shoot related to it.

Randomness may choose among eligible authored possibilities; randomness may not manufacture eligibility.

## 7. Rewards and consequences

Off-shoots need not reward loot.

They may produce:

- Knowledge;
- evidence/provenance;
- relationships;
- contacts;
- access;
- reputation/recognition;
- development opportunities;
- specialist qualification evidence where authorised;
- changed custody/state;
- saved/lost people;
- future event eligibility;
- later Story leverage;
- new unresolved problems.

Mission completion != reward identity.

## 8. Authoring workflow

When Main Story creates a strong unresolved thread that does not need immediate resolution:

1. Writing commits the unresolved factual state and Knowledge boundaries.
2. Writing identifies it as `WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE` or equivalent durable authoring note when appropriate.
3. World performs its existing event-consumer pass.
4. World may author baseline/history-generated/capability-responsive off-shoots from that state.
5. CE/runtime may surface only legitimate eligible opportunities.
6. Resolved or ignored outcomes commit ordinary Chronicle history.
7. Later Story consumes the resulting history only when relevant.

## 9. Anti-bloat rule

Not every Main Story loose end deserves a side chain.

World should prioritise off-shoots that create at least one of:

- meaningful Character-specific opportunity;
- consequential Knowledge;
- a distinctive location/participant/world interaction;
- a new future Story input;
- a capability-responsive route;
- a relationship/provenance consequence;
- a problem whose optional timing itself matters.

Do not create filler quests merely to drain every unresolved noun from Main Story.

## 10. Canonical rule

> **The Main Story does not need to personally solve every problem it creates. Chronicle history can branch outward into the World, and the Main Story should remember what the player actually did when those branches become relevant again.**
