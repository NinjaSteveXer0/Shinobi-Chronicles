## Narration
The recovered package rests on Minato’s desk.

ANBU Marked Target stands near the window.

Package Smuggler waits beside him.

Masked Interceptor leans against the far wall.

The ANBU operative enters.

She looks almost amused.

Almost.

## Dialogue
**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“Did he mention the rooftops?”

**ANBU OPERATIVE:**  
“Yes.”

Package Smuggler looks at her.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:**  
“He chased you?”

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“He tried.”

ANBU Marked Target catches the wording.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:**  
“And the first strike?”

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“Missed.”

A faint smile.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“So did the rest.”

Package Smuggler exhales.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:**  
“You sound pleased.”

The smile disappears.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“I’m alive.”

A beat.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“I’m allowed to enjoy that.”

Even Package Smuggler has no answer.

Minato turns toward the ANBU operative.

**MINATO:**  
“Did Kakashi explain why?”

**ANBU OPERATIVE:**  
“I asked.”

Masked Interceptor watches him.

**ANBU OPERATIVE:**  
“He said, ‘That was my choice.’”

She goes still.

The amusement is gone now.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“He really said that?”

**ANBU OPERATIVE:**  
“Yes.”

She looks toward the window.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“Good.”

ANBU Marked Target raises an eyebrow.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:**  
“You keep saying things like that.”

She looks at him.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“I’d rather know what someone is.”

Minato’s eyes move to her.

**MINATO:**  
“You think one decision tells you that?”

She considers him.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“No.”

A pause.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“But it tells me what he was willing to do to me.”

Minato accepts that.

Package Smuggler looks toward Kakashi’s report.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:**  
“Does he know she’s here?”

**MINATO:**  
“No.”

Masked Interceptor pushes away from the wall.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“Then let him wonder.”

ANBU Marked Target looks toward her.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:**  
“Planning something?”

She walks toward the door.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“No.”

She stops beside him.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“I’m remembering.”

Then she leaves.
