## Player choice

After the supposed ANBU custodians were produced and the transfer narrative began to collapse, Stephen selected the decision:

> **`Nobody tells Sai she's gone.`**

Menma questions Sai before giving him confirmation that the older female ROOT-linked prisoner is missing.

## Knowledge boundary entering the interrogation

Sai knows:

- he and the older woman were captured from the compromised underground operation;
- he remains in Konoha custody;
- Menma has returned to question him.

Sai is **not** told:

- that the older woman is missing;
- that a false/contested transfer record exists;
- that current ANBU operational identities were used in that record;
- that the cited ANBU appear not to have performed the transfer;
- any fact about Menma's concealed Sealed Wall/chamber custody.

## Played factual exchange / doctrine

Menma asks Sai how ROOT normally handles a captured operative.

Sai states, as his current doctrine/experience rather than omniscient universal truth:

- ROOT normally accepts the loss of a compromised operative rather than visibly building a retrieval operation around them;
- recovery creates another operation around an asset the enemy already knows exists;
- if what a captured operative knows becomes more dangerous than recovery, ROOT may instead treat that operative as a problem;
- what happens to such a problem depends on whether Danzō still needs that person alive.

Menma asks what ROOT would avoid if it wanted someone back.

Sai identifies conspicuous official-looking methods — especially a visible transfer using ANBU identities — as inconsistent with ROOT's preference for absence and deniability.

Menma asks whether ROOT would fake a transfer merely to kill someone inside Konoha custody.

Sai's current assessment:

- if death alone were the objective, a forged transfer creates unnecessary paper, witnesses and identity residue;
- therefore a fake transfer should not automatically be interpreted as proof that custody genuinely changed;
- the transfer may instead exist because someone wants investigators to **believe** custody changed, or wants the subsequent investigation itself to occur.

Sai explicitly distinguishes:

> **`somebody wanted custody to change`**

from:

> **`somebody wanted you to believe custody changed`**.

Sai further states that if the transfer story is designed to collapse under inspection, **inspection itself may be intended**.

He connects this to ROOT's broader reaction-model doctrine:

> **`Sometimes the response is the objective.`**

This reinforces existing Arc-2 response-model history without establishing ROOT as the actor behind the current disappearance.

## Sai inference — not factual Knowledge

From Menma's question selection and behaviour, Sai infers that something has changed around the older woman.

Sai says in substance that:

- Menma came alone;
- Menma asked retrieval doctrine;
- Menma asked about forged transfer methods;
- therefore Sai suspects the woman is no longer where she was.

Menma does **not** confirm this.

Sai acknowledges that he is guessing/inferencing rather than knowing.

Preserve:

> **Sai suspicion != Sai factual Knowledge.**

## New investigative possibility raised by Sai

Sai raises two bounded possibilities without claiming either is true:

1. **Voluntary/cooperative departure:** if the woman left willingly, whoever acted may not have needed to defeat containment in the way Konoha expects.
2. **Constructed appearance of voluntary/changed custody:** if she did not leave willingly, investigators should ask why someone wants them to believe she did.

Current Story does **not** establish:

- that the woman cooperated;
- that ROOT extracted her;
- that she is alive;
- that she is dead;
- that she physically left the annex;
- that Sai knows her current location;
- that cross-prisoner communication occurred;
- that any specific Skill, Genjutsu, False Identity technique or seal method caused the disappearance.

## Story consequence

Menma leaves the interrogation with a stronger operational framing:

- the transfer record may be a deliberately authored investigation path rather than a simple forged escape receipt;
- the visible method may be designed to steer Konoha's response;
- the woman may have cooperated, been removed, been killed, remained elsewhere, or been used as the subject of a false custody narrative;
- the next useful investigation should test material reality, information access and/or cooperation evidence without prematurely selecting one theory.

Sai remains unconfirmed regarding the woman's disappearance.
