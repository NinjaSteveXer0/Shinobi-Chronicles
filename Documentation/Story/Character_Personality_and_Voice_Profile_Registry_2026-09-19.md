**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Owner:** Stephen / Writing / Story — Konoha  
**Status:** **BINDING WRITING-OWNED CHARACTER PERFORMANCE REGISTRY — ACTIVE / EXPAND AS CHARACTERS ENTER SUBSTANTIAL DIALOGUE**  
**Primary priority:** **FINISH SHINOBI CHRONICLES ALPHA**

## 1. Purpose

This registry operationalises the existing Story requirement that recurring named characters retain stable personality, voice, emotional behaviour and relationship-sensitive expression across successor chats.

It exists because character voice cannot remain an informal memory.

Canonical rule:

> **People ask questions. People answer as themselves.**

A factual answer is not complete player-facing dialogue merely because it is factually correct.

The same factual content may emerge thoughtfully, humorously, sympathetically, apathetically, dryly, fiercely, defensively, unkindly, warmly, suspiciously, evasively, impatiently or through silence depending on the speaker, current emotion, relationship and stakes.

This registry is an **expression authority**.

It does not create hidden World Truth, biography, relationships, Knowledge, Battle facts, custody, allegiance or Chronicle history.

Recorded History and more specific current Story authority always outrank a generic profile where the character has legitimately changed.

---

## 2. Ownership

Character personality / voice profiles are owned by **Writing / Story — Konoha**.

Character Creation / Visuals owns visual representation fidelity, art direction and asset production. It may consume a Writing profile to inform pose, expression or visual mood, but it does not own dialogue personality or Story voice unless a later explicit cross-lane authority changes that split.

No separate personality-specialist workspace is required for current Alpha work.

If profile volume later becomes too large for Writing to maintain cleanly, Stephen may create a dedicated Characterisation / Voice lane, but that would be an organisational delegation of Writing authority rather than a correction to current ownership.

---

## 3. Mandatory profile preflight

Before substantial player-facing dialogue for a recurring named character, Writing must consume:

1. current Recorded History relevant to the scene;
2. current exact Story/scene authority;
3. current character-specific canon/voice anchor where one exists;
4. this registry or another more specific personality lock;
5. the single active Storywide player-facing performance authority.

If no durable profile exists, Writing must create at least a minimum viable profile before treating substantial dialogue as Writing-complete.

A temporary one-scene voice guess is not enough for a recurring named character.

---

## 4. Profile schema

Each durable profile should capture, where known:

- **Character / representation**
- **Age / life stage**
- **Base temperament**
- **Social / authority posture**
- **Default cadence**
- **Vocabulary / formality**
- **Humour style**
- **Confidence / hesitation pattern**
- **Emotional leakage**
- **Body-language tendencies**
- **Silence / withholding style**
- **When angry**
- **When afraid**
- **When embarrassed**
- **When excited**
- **When hurt / grieving**
- **With trusted people**
- **With rivals / opponents**
- **With authority**
- **Question / answer behaviour**
- **Do not write this character as**
- **Current Chronicle-specific modifiers**

Unknown fields must remain unknown rather than being filled with invented biography.

# 5. ACTIVE ACADEMY KAKASHI ORIGIN PROFILES

## 5.1 Academy Kakashi Hatake

**Character / representation:** Academy-era Kakashi in the active Origin chronology.  
**Age / life stage:** Young shinobi / Academy-origin stage; exact scene authority controls age details.

**Base temperament**
- controlled;
- observant;
- self-contained;
- tactically minded;
- confident enough to make his own judgement;
- not emotionally empty.

**Social / authority posture**
- respects legitimate command;
- does not surrender tactical judgement merely because an adult or institution exists;
- can explain a decision when the conversation actually calls for explanation;
- does not volunteer speeches to prove he is intelligent.

**Default cadence**
- economical;
- precise;
- usually direct;
- sentence length expands when tactical reasoning, disagreement or an actual conversation requires it;
- brevity must not collapse into repetitive one-word dialogue.

**Vocabulary / formality**
- plain, exact language;
- avoids inflated academic wording;
- capable of specific tactical observation.

**Humour style**
- dry;
- understated;
- occasionally literal in a way that lands as humour;
- never a constant quip machine.

**Confidence / hesitation**
- generally controlled confidence;
- may pause to observe rather than verbally hedge;
- uncertainty appears as attention and recalculation more often than verbal panic.

**Emotional leakage**
- eye-line;
- stillness;
- tightened focus;
- subtle irritation;
- shortened or sharpened phrasing;
- occasional dry response.

**Body language**
- watches stance, exits, hands and movement;
- economical physicality;
- shifts position for tactical reasons;
- stillness often carries more than gesturing.

**Silence / withholding**
- comfortable with silence;
- does not answer merely because someone expects a reply;
- may let an observation stand without defending himself.

**When angry**
- colder;
- sharper;
- more exact;
- less likely to rant.

**When afraid / under pressure**
- fear should not be erased;
- pressure compresses him toward tactical focus rather than making him suddenly verbose.

**When embarrassed**
- tends to become more economical or deflect dryly rather than over-explain.

**When hurt / defeated**
- factual about what happened;
- does not need to invent an excuse;
- can carry frustration privately without turning into emotionless acceptance.

**With authority**
- concise and respectful where earned;
- may disagree or report plainly;
- not a submissive information terminal.

**Question / answer behaviour**
- may answer directly;
- may answer the tactical implication instead of the exact wording;
- may challenge a premise;
- may give a full explanation when the decision itself is under discussion;
- one-word answers are allowed only when they actually suit the moment.

**Do not write Kakashi as**
- a robot;
- a miniature professor;
- permanently emotionally blank;
- a one-word response generator;
- someone who explains his intelligence instead of showing it;
- a generic sarcastic anime prodigy.

**Current Origin modifier**
- the current STOP THE ASSASSIN route has reinforced that Kakashi can own a decision and separately admit a Battle defeat without collapsing one into an excuse for the other.

## 5.3 Minato Namikaze

**Character / representation:** Current SC Minato / Fourth Hokage.  
**Life stage:** Adult Hokage, husband/father where current Chronicle history makes that relevant.

**Base temperament**
- calm;
- measured;
- perceptive;
- responsibility-focused;
- capable of warmth and empathy;
- decisive when command must commit.

**Social / authority posture**
- carries authority without needing to perform dominance;
- listens before concluding;
- distinguishes what a person knew from what actually happened;
- can admit cost, uncertainty or institutional weakness.

**Default cadence**
- measured;
- economical without being clipped;
- capable of longer reasoning when the room needs synthesis.

**Humour**
- subtle;
- occasional warmth or dry amusement;
- not banter-first.

**Emotional leakage**
- eyebrows;
- breath;
- softened expression;
- temple rub / physical frustration where earned;
- silence before a difficult conclusion;
- visible shock when facts genuinely warrant it.

**Question / answer behaviour**
- asks questions to understand motive, Knowledge, judgement and consequence, not merely to fill a report;
- may answer with interpretation rather than a bare yes/no;
- allows others to disagree before synthesising.

**When angry**
- controlled and decisive;
- anger sharpens command rather than creating theatrical shouting by default.

**When sympathetic**
- does not erase failure;
- can understand why someone acted and still record that they failed.

**Do not write Minato as**
- omniscient;
- a bureaucratic information terminal;
- emotionally detached because he is Hokage;
- endlessly solemn;
- a sequence of one-line questions;
- passive when responsibility requires judgement.

**Current Origin modifier**
- in Kakashi's hidden evaluation, Minato must distinguish Kakashi's incomplete-information decision from his factual Battle defeat.

# 7. Conversational response enforcement

For all profiles above:

> **Answer the question as the character, not merely as the fact.**

When a question is asked, Writing should consider:

- What does the speaker think the questioner is really asking?
- How do they feel about being asked?
- Do they respect the questioner?
- Are they amused, angry, afraid, tired, sympathetic, apathetic, suspicious or defensive?
- Do they want the conversation to continue?
- What are they withholding?
- Would they answer directly, joke, challenge, soften, sharpen, redirect or stay silent?

A scene that repeatedly reduces these characters to:

`question -> yes/no -> question -> yes/no`

without a deliberate interrogation purpose is not Writing-complete.
