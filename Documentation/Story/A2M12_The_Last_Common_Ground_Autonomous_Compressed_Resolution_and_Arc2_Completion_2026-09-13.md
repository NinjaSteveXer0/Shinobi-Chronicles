## 4. Section 3 — final information-war play

The operation launches in three layers.

### Layer A — mirrored false authority

Two forged/constructed directives begin circulating through different audiences:

- one purports to show **Hokage authority preparing detention/seizure of Sasuke-aligned organisers**;
- another purports to show **Sasuke's coalition preparing to seize command/records before Minato can suppress evidence**.

Each packet contains enough genuine procedural detail to look plausible to the intended audience.

### Layer B — real sabotage

A communications/evidence nexus used by the temporary joint crisis arrangement is physically sabotaged so that verification becomes harder at the exact moment the forged directives spread.

### Layer C — attribution trap

Prepared witnesses/observers are positioned to record whichever bloc moves first and frame that movement as proof that the corresponding forged directive was real.

The goal is not merely confusion.

It is to create a **first irreversible act** that the other side can no longer interpret as innocent.
