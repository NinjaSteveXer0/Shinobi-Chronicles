# 3. GET CLOSER — RESOLVER SUCCESS

## Player-facing scene

The Sakura tree is still some distance ahead.

From deeper in the alley, Kakashi can see the meeting.

He cannot hear enough of it.

That is the problem.

Kakashi leaves the safer distance behind.

He stays low in the darker side of the alley, where lantern light does not quite reach the walls. Wet stone catches everything else — moonlight, windows, the warm glow beyond the alley mouth.

He avoids all of it.

One doorway.

Then another.

Closer.

The Sakura tree grows larger beyond the entrance.

So do the voices.

### Scene Board

Backdrop changes:

`konoha_alleyway.png` -> `end_of_alleyway.png`

**GET CLOSER RESOLVER — SUCCESS**

Neither man looks toward Kakashi.

Good.

He settles into the shadow near the end of the alley.

Close enough now.

ANBU Marked Target keeps one hand against the package beneath his clothing.

Package Smuggler stands opposite him.

No hurry in either man's posture.

Too little hurry, considering what they are doing.

Then Kakashi hears them clearly.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“You said once I hand it over, I'm done.”

Package Smuggler barely looks at him.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“You are.”

The target does not seem reassured.

His eyes flick toward the main street.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“And if somebody followed me?”

Package Smuggler's expression changes.

Not fear.

Annoyance.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“Then you keep walking.”

A pause.

The target's hand remains over the package.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“With it?”

Now Package Smuggler looks at him.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“Until I say otherwise.”

The answer bothers him.

Kakashi can tell from the way his fingers tighten against the cloth.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“That wasn't the plan.”

Package Smuggler glances once along the street.

Not toward Kakashi.

Just checking.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“Plans are for empty streets.”

The target's mouth tightens.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“And if the street is empty?”

Package Smuggler holds out his hand.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“Then I take it.”

His fingers remain open between them.

“And you disappear.”

The target looks at the waiting hand.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“Where does it go after you?”

Package Smuggler's expression almost becomes a smile.

Almost.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“Away from you.”

Kakashi's eye narrows.

That was more useful than anything either of them had said from a distance.

The arrangement is clearer now.

If the handoff happens, ANBU Marked Target walks away.

Package Smuggler leaves with the objective.

If they believe somebody is following them, the handoff does not happen at all.

Kakashi has moved closer without changing the scene.

For now.

Package Smuggler's hand is still waiting.

ANBU Marked Target begins to move his own.

The decision has become smaller.

And more immediate.

## Player-facing scene

The Sakura tree is still some distance ahead.

Kakashi can see the meeting.

He cannot hear enough of it.

He starts forward.

Quietly.

The darker side of the alley gives him more cover than the lantern-lit street ahead. He slips past one doorway, then another, keeping the two men framed between the buildings.

Closer.

The voices begin to carry.

### Scene Board

Backdrop changes:

`konoha_alleyway.png` -> `end_of_alleyway.png`

**GET CLOSER RESOLVER — FAILURE**

Kakashi catches the beginning of the conversation.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“You said once I hand it over, I'm done.”

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“You are.”

The target looks toward the main street.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“And if somebody followed me?”

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“Then you keep walking.”

The target frowns.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“With it?”

Package Smuggler does not answer.

His eyes have moved.

Not toward Kakashi.

Down.

A thin pool of rainwater lies along the edge of the street.

Lantern light trembles across it.

So does something else.

Movement where the reflected alley should have been empty.

Package Smuggler's hand rises.

Not for the package.

A warning.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“Hold it.”

ANBU Marked Target freezes.

His hand never leaves his clothing.

The package remains with him.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“What?”

Package Smuggler keeps staring into the alley.

Kakashi does not move.

Too late.

The man's posture has already changed.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“Someone's close.”

The target finally starts to turn.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“Don't.”

He stops.

Package Smuggler's voice hardens.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER**  
“Keep it.”

A beat.

“Main street. Go.”

ANBU Marked Target does not argue this time.

He moves.

Fast.

Toward the Sakura tree and the open street beyond it.

The package goes with him.

Package Smuggler stays behind.

For one second he watches the target leave.

Then he turns toward the alley.

Toward Kakashi.

Not his exact hiding place.

Not yet.

But close enough that the difference will not last.

Kakashi's eye shifts past him.

ANBU Marked Target is already gaining distance.

The package is still secure beneath his clothing.

Package Smuggler is walking toward the person who ruined the handoff.

Kakashi wanted more information.

He got some.

He also changed the scene.

Now he has to decide which consequence matters more.
