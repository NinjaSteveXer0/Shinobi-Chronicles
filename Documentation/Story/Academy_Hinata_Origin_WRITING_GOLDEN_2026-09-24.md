# ACADEMY HINATA
## Three Exchanges

# SCENE 1 — THE COURTYARD

Palm.

Turn.

Step.

Again.

Hinata's heel settles into the same groove it left the last time.

The Hyūga courtyard is quiet enough that she can hear another class working beyond the wall: sandals against packed earth, an instructor correcting somebody's stance, the brief slap of palm against padded guard.

Here, nobody speaks.

Hinata completes the form.

Returns to the opening position.

Starts again.

Palm.

Turn.

Step—

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Stop.”

Hinata freezes halfway through the movement.

The instructor is standing beneath the edge of the veranda, arms folded inside his sleeves.

He has been there long enough that Hinata doesn't know which repetition he started watching.

That makes it worse.

She lowers her hands.

**HINATA:** “Was it wrong?”

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “No.”

Hinata waits.

The instructor nods toward the centre of the courtyard.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “That's the problem.”

Across the training space, another Academy-age Hyūga student looks up from stretching.

A few younger children are gathered along the wooden edge of the courtyard behind him. Their own practice has stopped.

They know what's about to happen.

Hinata does too.

The instructor steps away from the veranda.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “You've repeated that form enough times to perform it alone.”

He looks from Hinata to the other student.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Now use it against somebody who doesn't intend to cooperate.”

The other student walks into position.

Hinata follows.

Her hands feel colder than they did a moment ago.

The younger students shuffle closer to the edge.

One whispers something.

Another hushes him.

Hinata hears both.

Her opponent raises his palms.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “Ready?”

Hinata settles into stance.

Her first answer catches somewhere behind her ribs.

She breathes.

**HINATA:** “Yes.”

The instructor lifts one hand.

Then drops it.

The other student moves immediately.

---

# SCENE 2 — FIRST EXCHANGE

He closes faster than Hinata expected.

One palm comes high.

The other stays close to his body.

Hinata recognises the entry.

She's practised the answer to it all morning.

Practising it against empty air had been easier.

His lead foot hits the dirt.

### CHOICE

**WAIT FOR HIM TO COMMIT**

**STEP IN FIRST**

**BREAK AWAY AND RESET**

## WAIT FOR HIM TO COMMIT

Hinata holds.

One step.

Then another.

Her opponent's palm flashes toward her shoulder.

Hinata moves at the last moment.

She slips outside the strike, catches his wrist and turns her body into the opening.

Her answering palm stops just short of his ribs.

The instructor's hand rises.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Hold.”

Both students freeze.

Her opponent looks down at the space between Hinata's palm and his side.

Then back at her.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “You waited a long time.”

Hinata releases his wrist.

**HINATA:** “You still came in.”

He gives her a look.

Not annoyed.

Interested.

The instructor lowers his hand.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Again.”

This time, her opponent doesn't rush.

## STEP IN FIRST

Hinata sees his weight shift forward.

She moves before he can finish the step.

His eyes widen.

Her first palm forces his guard across his body.

Hinata follows it.

Second step.

Second palm.

He gives ground.

For three breaths, he is the one reacting.

Then Hinata reaches for one more opening.

He isn't there.

His foot hooks behind hers.

Hinata catches herself before she goes down, but the stumble costs her the exchange.

His palm stops beside her shoulder.

The instructor raises his hand.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Hold.”

Hinata straightens.

Her opponent is breathing harder now.

So is she.

A small smile pulls at one corner of his mouth.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “Didn't think you'd do that.”

Hinata brushes dirt from her sleeve.

**HINATA:** “Neither did I.”

His smile gets a little wider.

The instructor lowers his hand.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Again.”

Her opponent's stance changes.

This time he's waiting for her.

## BREAK AWAY AND RESET

Hinata shifts off the line instead of meeting him.

His first strike finds empty space.

He turns after her.

Hinata keeps enough distance that his second never reaches her.

Then both stop.

For a heartbeat, neither attacks.

Her opponent frowns.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “We're sparring.”

Hinata settles her feet.

**HINATA:** “I know.”

He advances again.

Slower this time.

Trying to take the ground she gave him.

The instructor watches Hinata's feet.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “If you give him space, decide what you're buying with it.”

Hinata doesn't answer.

Her opponent is already coming.

---

# SCENE 3 — SECOND EXCHANGE

## IF HINATA WAITED AND COUNTERED

Her opponent has learned something.

He starts forward—

then stops.

Hinata's body almost answers a strike that never comes.

He sees it.

The next entry is smaller.

A shoulder twitch.

Half a step.

Another feint.

He's trying to make Hinata commit first.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “Going to wait all day?”

Hinata's fingers tighten.

### CHOICE

**DON'T BITE — HOLD YOUR GROUND**

**GIVE HIM AN OPENING**

**STOP WAITING AND GO FIRST**

### DON'T BITE — HOLD YOUR GROUND

Hinata stays where she is.

The first feint passes.

Then the second.

Her opponent circles.

Hinata turns with him.

His expression changes.

The teasing disappears.

He steps in for real.

Hinata sees it too late to make the exchange clean.

She catches the first arm but his shoulder drives into hers.

Both of them skid across the dirt.

Hinata's heel catches.

She nearly falls.

Doesn't.

Her answering palm forces him off before he can press again.

They separate on their own.

Neither scored cleanly.

The instructor doesn't stop them.

That tells both students enough.

### GIVE HIM AN OPENING

Hinata lets one shoulder drift.

Barely.

Her opponent notices.

His next step is real.

Hinata closes the opening the moment he reaches for it.

His wrist meets her palm instead of her shoulder.

She turns him past her.

His balance goes.

Hinata follows.

Her palm stops against his back.

This time the instructor calls it immediately.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Hold.”

Her opponent twists around.

Looks at Hinata.

Then at the position he ended in.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “You did that on purpose.”

Hinata lets her hand fall.

A faint flush reaches her face.

**HINATA:** “You came in.”

He stares for a second.

Then laughs once.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “Right.”

### STOP WAITING AND GO FIRST

He feints again.

Hinata steps through it.

Her opponent's surprise lasts just long enough.

Hinata's first palm takes away the line he wanted.

He recovers quickly.

Their arms collide.

Redirect.

Turn.

Step.

Hinata keeps moving.

Not perfectly.

But forward.

Her opponent is forced to abandon the feints and actually defend.

When they finally break apart, the instructor says nothing.

Hinata notices her opponent no longer looks amused.

---

## IF HINATA PRESSED FIRST

Her opponent doesn't give her the same opening twice.

This time his guard is tighter.

His feet barely move.

He wants her to come to him.

Hinata knows what happened the last time she reached too far.

So does he.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “Come on.”

### CHOICE

**KEEP THE PRESSURE ON HIM**

**DRAW OUT HIS COUNTER**

**BACK OFF BEFORE HE CAN SET THE TRAP**

### KEEP THE PRESSURE ON HIM

Hinata goes.

He expected that.

The counter comes almost immediately.

Hinata catches the wrist but the force turns her shoulder.

Another strike follows.

She blocks it.

Barely.

The third pushes her backward.

Her opponent sees it and comes harder.

Hinata's foot slips.

One knee touches the dirt.

A few of the younger students inhale sharply.

Her opponent starts to pull away.

Hinata is already standing.

**HINATA:** “I'm not finished.”

He stops.

So does the instructor.

For half a second, all three seem surprised she said it aloud.

Her opponent resets his stance.

Hinata does the same.

### DRAW OUT HIS COUNTER

Hinata steps forward.

Her opponent's guard tightens.

Another step.

He waits.

Hinata gives him the attack he expects—

then abandons it halfway through.

His counter cuts across empty space.

Hinata turns underneath his arm.

Her palm stops at his side.

The courtyard goes quiet.

The instructor lifts his hand.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Hold.”

Hinata steps away.

Her opponent looks at her.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “That wasn't the attack.”

Hinata shakes her head.

**HINATA:** “No.”

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “You made me think it was.”

Hinata doesn't quite know what to do with the accusation.

So she says nothing.

### BACK OFF BEFORE HE CAN SET THE TRAP

Hinata starts forward.

Sees his weight settle.

Stops.

Her opponent's counter begins anyway.

Too early.

His arm cuts through the space where Hinata would have been.

Hinata withdraws another step.

He doesn't follow.

Now both of them know the trap is gone.

The instructor looks at her.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Better.”

One word.

Hinata hears it anyway.

Her opponent resets.

Less comfortably than before.

---

## IF HINATA RESET THE FIRST EXCHANGE

Her opponent has stopped charging straight at her.

Now he takes the courtyard away piece by piece.

One step.

Hinata gives ground.

Another.

She shifts sideways.

He follows.

Not rushing.

Just narrowing where she can go.

The wooden edge of the courtyard is getting closer behind her.

He notices that too.

### CHOICE

**MEET HIM BEFORE HE CLOSES THE SPACE**

**CIRCLE OUT**

**LET HIM THINK HE HAS YOU CORNERED**

### MEET HIM BEFORE HE CLOSES THE SPACE

Hinata stops retreating.

Her opponent takes one more step.

Hinata meets him there.

The collision is harder than either expected.

Palm against wrist.

Shoulder against shoulder.

Hinata is forced sideways.

She plants.

Turns.

Her second palm catches his guard and knocks it wide.

His third strike passes across her sleeve.

Hinata's answer stops against his chest.

They stare at each other.

The instructor raises his hand.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Hold.”

Hinata's heart is pounding.

She hadn't moved backward.

Not once.

### CIRCLE OUT

Hinata lets him keep coming.

But not straight.

Every time he takes ground, she turns the angle.

He adjusts.

She turns again.

His patience starts disappearing.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “Stop moving.”

Hinata almost smiles.

**HINATA:** “No.”

He lunges.

Too far.

Hinata is already off the line.

His momentum carries him past.

She turns with him and taps his shoulder before he can recover.

The instructor's hand rises.

Her opponent exhales sharply.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “I hate this.”

Hinata's smile actually appears this time.

Small.

Gone almost immediately.

### LET HIM THINK HE HAS YOU CORNERED

Hinata gives him the last few steps.

The courtyard edge is behind her.

Her opponent sees it.

So do the younger students.

He moves in.

Hinata waits.

One step.

Another.

His stance widens to block her escape.

Hinata goes forward instead.

Right through the space he stopped protecting.

His eyes widen.

Hinata slips past his shoulder.

Turns.

Now he's the one facing the edge.

The instructor doesn't say anything.

One of the younger children does.

Very quietly:

**YOUNGER STUDENT:** “Oh.”

Hinata hears it.

So does her opponent.

---

# SCENE 4 — THIRD EXCHANGE

Both students are breathing harder now.

Dust marks Hinata's sleeve.

Her opponent's hair has fallen loose across his forehead.

Neither looks toward the younger students anymore.

The instructor steps closer to the edge of the spar.

His attention has changed too.

He isn't waiting to see whether Hinata remembers the form.

He's watching what she does with a person who has started learning her back.

Her opponent raises his hands again.

### IF HINATA HAS SPENT THE SPAR WAITING

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “He knows you're waiting. Don't give him the same exchange again.”

### IF HINATA HAS SPENT THE SPAR PRESSING

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Don't let wanting the opening make you create one for him.”

### IF HINATA HAS REPEATEDLY RESET / GIVEN GROUND

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “There won't always be more courtyard behind you.”

### IF HINATA HAS ALREADY CHANGED APPROACHES

The instructor says nothing.

A small nod.

That's all.

Her opponent moves.

This time, he doesn't commit to one attack.

He starts the entry Hinata has seen before—

then changes it.

The final exchange begins.

### CHOICE

**ANSWER WITH THE FORM YOU PRACTISED**

**TRUST WHAT YOU'VE SEEN IN THE SPAR**

**BREAK THE RHYTHM BEFORE HE CAN SET IT**

## ANSWER WITH THE FORM YOU PRACTISED

Hinata's body knows the sequence.

Step.

Turn.

Open palm.

Her opponent changes halfway in.

Hinata changes with him—

but keeps the shape.

The movement she repeated alone at the start of the morning finally has another person inside it.

His first strike is redirected.

His second never gets a clean line.

Hinata's answering palm stops over the centre of his chest.

For a moment neither moves.

The instructor's voice comes from behind them.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Enough.”

Hinata lowers her hand.

Her opponent exhales.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “That one felt different.”

Hinata looks at her own palm.

**HINATA:** “It was.”

## TRUST WHAT YOU'VE SEEN IN THE SPAR

Hinata recognises the first movement.

And doesn't answer it.

Her opponent expects the counter.

It never comes.

His second movement is already beginning when he realises.

Hinata steps somewhere he hasn't guarded.

He twists.

Too late.

Her palm touches his shoulder.

He tries to recover.

Hinata is gone from that line too.

Another step.

Another angle.

The exchange ends with his back foot crossing awkwardly behind him and Hinata still balanced in front of him.

The instructor raises his hand.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Enough.”

Her opponent straightens.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “You changed it.”

Hinata looks at him.

**HINATA:** “So did you.”

He thinks about that.

Then nods.

## BREAK THE RHYTHM BEFORE HE CAN SET IT

Her opponent starts the familiar entry.

Hinata doesn't wait for the familiar ending.

She closes the distance before either of them reaches the part they've practised.

The exchange becomes messy immediately.

Arms collide.

Both lose the clean line.

Her opponent tries to recover first.

Hinata turns with him.

He catches her wrist.

She frees it.

He steps across her.

Hinata pivots.

For a few seconds there is no perfect Hyūga form.

Just two Academy students trying very hard not to lose.

Her opponent commits.

Hinata sees it.

This time she doesn't hesitate.

Her palm stops against his shoulder.

His stops against hers.

Both freeze.

The instructor studies them.

Then:

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Enough.”

The students step apart.

Her opponent looks at the two palms.

Then at Hinata.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “Draw?”

Hinata is still catching her breath.

**HINATA:** “Maybe.”

The instructor hears.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “No.”

Both turn toward him.

A beat.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “The exercise is over. That isn't the same thing.”

Her opponent groans quietly.

Hinata hides a smile.

---

# SCENE 5 — THE EVALUATION

The younger students start whispering the moment the spar ends.

The instructor silences them with one look.

Hinata stands opposite him.

Her opponent remains nearby, rubbing one wrist.

## IF HINATA KEPT WAITING FOR COMMITMENTS

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “You were comfortable when he gave you the first move.”

Hinata looks down for a moment.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Less comfortable when he stopped.”

Her eyes lift again.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “You don't need to be the first person moving.”

He glances toward her opponent.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “But if your whole answer depends on him making a mistake, eventually he'll notice.”

Hinata nods.

The instructor catches it.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Don't nod because I'm speaking.”

Hinata stops.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Do you disagree?”

A dangerous question in a Hyūga courtyard.

Hinata looks back over the ground they used.

**HINATA:** “Not completely.”

Her opponent looks over.

So does the instructor.

Hinata swallows.

**HINATA:** “Waiting worked.”

A little quieter:

**HINATA:** “Until it didn't.”

The instructor gives one short nod.

This time it belongs to him.

## IF HINATA FOUGHT FORWARD

The instructor looks at the marks her feet left across the centre of the courtyard.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “You took more ground than I expected.”

Hinata isn't sure whether that's praise.

Her opponent answers for her.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “A lot more.”

Hinata glances at him.

He rubs his shoulder.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “I'm just saying.”

The instructor ignores him.

Mostly.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “When you moved because you saw the opening, you made him answer you.”

Then:

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “When you moved because you didn't want to stop moving, you gave him the answer first.”

Hinata remembers the foot behind hers.

The stumble.

She nods once.

Not automatically.

Because she remembers it.

## IF HINATA CONTROLLED DISTANCE / RESET

The instructor looks toward the edge of the courtyard.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “You gave him ground.”

Hinata's shoulders tighten.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Sometimes that was the right decision.”

She looks up.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Sometimes he took it because you let him.”

Hinata glances at her opponent.

He gives a small shrug.

Fair.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Distance is useful.”

He steps aside, clearing the centre of the courtyard.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “So is knowing when you're done giving it away.”

## IF HINATA CHANGED APPROACHES THROUGH THE SPAR

The instructor is quiet for longer.

Hinata waits.

Her opponent waits too.

Finally:

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Your second exchange didn't look like your first.”

Hinata says nothing.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Your third didn't look like either.”

Her fingers curl lightly against her palms.

The instructor looks toward her opponent.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Did that make her easier or harder to fight?”

He answers immediately.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “Harder.”

Hinata looks at him.

He catches her expression.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “What? It did.”

The instructor looks back at Hinata.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Good.”

Just that.

This time it doesn't feel small.

The instructor turns away.

**HYŪGA INSTRUCTOR:** “Both of you. Water.”

The sparring student exhales in relief.

**SPARRING STUDENT:** “Best instruction all morning.”

Hinata follows him toward the edge of the courtyard.

Behind them, the younger students immediately start moving.

# SCENE 6 — THE CHILDREN

Two of the younger Hyūga have already claimed the centre of the training space.

Neither waited for permission.

One takes the stance Hinata's opponent used.

The other raises her palms.

Then hesitates.

The first child attacks.

Too early.

The girl's feet tangle as she tries to retreat and counter at the same time.

They stop.

**YOUNGER STUDENT:** “Again.”

The boy resets.

The girl does too.

This time she waits.

Too long.

His palm taps her shoulder.

She scowls.

**YOUNGER STUDENT:** “Again.”

Hinata stands near the gate with her water cup still in her hands.

The girl notices her watching.

Immediately, her posture changes.

Straighter.

More careful.

Worse.

Her next movement is exactly the one Hinata had repeated alone at the beginning of the morning.

Perfect shape.

No answer for the person standing in front of her.

The boy steps around it without trouble.

The girl's face falls.

### CHOICE

**SHOW HER ONCE**

**TELL HER WHAT YOU SAW**

**LEAVE THEM TO THEIR PRACTICE**

**WATCH ONE MORE EXCHANGE**

## SHOW HER ONCE

Hinata sets the cup down.

The children go quiet when she walks back onto the courtyard.

The girl shifts aside.

**YOUNGER STUDENT:** “Did I do it wrong?”

Hinata looks at her stance.

**HINATA:** “No.”

The girl looks even more confused.

Hinata takes her place opposite the boy.

**HINATA:** “Try again.”

He hesitates.

**HINATA:** “It's all right.”

He attacks.

Hinata waits—

then changes position when he changes his.

The movement is small.

No dramatic strike.

Just enough.

The boy passes beside her.

Hinata stops.

Looks at the girl.

The girl is staring at Hinata's feet.

Good.

Hinata steps away.

**HINATA:** “Again.”

The girl takes her place.

This time she doesn't copy Hinata exactly.

## TELL HER WHAT YOU SAW

Hinata stays by the gate.

**HINATA:** “Your back foot.”

The girl looks down.

Moves it.

**HINATA:** “No.”

The foot stops.

Hinata searches for the words.

**HINATA:** “You're moving it before he does anything.”

The girl looks at the boy.

Then at Hinata.

**YOUNGER STUDENT:** “Because he's going to attack.”

**HINATA:** “I know.”

Hinata glances back toward the centre of the courtyard where she had spent the morning.

**HINATA:** “He knows too.”

The girl thinks about that.

Then resets.

The boy attacks.

This time her back foot stays still until the last moment.

She still misses the counter.

But she doesn't trip.

Her face lights up anyway.

**YOUNGER STUDENT:** “Again.”

Hinata smiles.

## LEAVE THEM TO THEIR PRACTICE

Hinata picks up her things.

Behind her:

**YOUNGER STUDENT:** “Again.”

Sandals scrape the courtyard.

A quick strike.

A frustrated noise.

Then:

**YOUNGER STUDENT:** “Wait. Do it the way she did.”

Hinata stops at the gate.

She doesn't turn around immediately.

When she finally does, the two children are arguing over which part of her spar they mean.

One demonstrates the sidestep.

The other insists it was the counter.

Neither gets it quite right.

Hinata watches for a moment.

Then leaves them to argue.

## WATCH ONE MORE EXCHANGE

Hinata doesn't move.

The girl resets.

Her opponent comes forward.

She waits.

Too long again.

At the last moment she panics and steps backwards.

Hinata's fingers tighten around her cup.

She knows that movement.

Not from watching the girl.

From feeling it.

The boy resets.

The girl takes a breath.

**YOUNGER STUDENT:** “Again.”

This time she doesn't retreat immediately.

The exchange lasts longer.

Not much.

Enough.

Hinata stays until it ends.

---

# ORIGIN CLOSE

At the courtyard gate, Hinata puts her sandals back on.

Behind her, the younger students are still sparring.

The instructor has gone back to correcting somebody else's stance.

Her own opponent is arguing that he would have won the last exchange if the instructor had let it continue.

Nobody agrees with him.

Hinata looks back once.

The groove from her morning practice is still visible in the dirt near the veranda.

So are the tracks from the spar.

They don't look anything alike.

Hinata turns toward the village.

Quietly:

**HINATA:** “Tomorrow…”

She adjusts the strap of her bag.

**HINATA:** “I'll try again.”

Then she goes.
