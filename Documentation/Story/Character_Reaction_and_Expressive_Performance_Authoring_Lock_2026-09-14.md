# Character Reaction and Expressive Performance Authoring Lock

**Date:** 2026-09-14  
**Owner:** Writing / Story — Konoha  
**Status:** **BINDING STORYWIDE AUTHORING REQUIREMENT**

## Core rule

> **Characters should not just speak. They should react.**

Shinobi Chronicles dialogue scenes must be performed through visible and audible character reaction, not presented as rows of emotionally flat speech.

Dialogue is only one layer of a scene. Important emotional, tactical and relational information should also be carried through expression, posture, movement, eye-line, silence, hesitation, interruption, breath, laughter, scoffs, sighs, recoil, surprise, frustration, relief, amusement, fear, anger, uncertainty and other context-appropriate physical or vocal reactions.

## Authoring requirement

For consequential scene beats, Writing should consider all of the following alongside the spoken line:

- what the actor is feeling or suppressing;
- whether that feeling should be visible to the player;
- facial expression or eye movement;
- body posture or gesture;
- movement toward/away from another actor;
- timing and silence;
- vocal reaction such as laughter, sigh, scoff, groan, gasp, interruption or raised voice;
- how another actor visibly reacts to what was said or done;
- whether the environment/object state should move with the reaction.

This does **not** require melodramatic animation on every sentence. Small reactions are often stronger. The requirement is that characters must feel present and responsive rather than dead-faced delivery devices.

## Anti-pattern

Reject player-facing presentation that repeatedly becomes:

`Actor A line -> Actor B line -> Actor A line`

with no physical, emotional or spatial response despite meaningful events.

Prefer:

`line -> visible reaction -> movement/silence -> reply -> changed expression/focus -> consequence`

where appropriate.

## Character-specific expression

Reaction must remain character-specific.

Examples:

- Kakashi may respond through a small eye shift, dry timing, stillness, clipped speech or a subtle change in attention rather than broad emotional display.
- Minato may visibly break composure when genuinely shocked, laugh or smile when amused, soften around a student, or show exasperation through gesture rather than remaining permanently Hokage-serious.
- ANBU operatives may remain controlled, but controlled does not mean inert; hesitation, head movement, silence and restrained reaction still communicate thought.

Do not flatten every character into the same stoic delivery style.

## Current Kakashi benchmark example

For the Minato evaluation reveal, flat delivery such as:

`MINATO: Kakashi killed her?`

is insufficient if the factual history is genuinely shocking.

Approved direction:

- the ANBU operative reports that the female Decoy Assassin will not be returning;
- Minato's eyebrows rise in visible shock;
- Minato exclaims, `Kakashi killed her?!`;
- after learning it was not during the fight, Minato audibly groans and massages his temples;
- no explanatory paragraph follows; the reaction itself carries the meaning before the black wipe.

## Scope

This rule applies to:

- Academy Origins;
- Arc 1–3 retrofit/presentation;
- future Canon, Alt, Rogue, Non-Conformance, Leader and other Story routes;
- Chronicle Interaction Scene Board dialogue;
- post-Battle returns and consequence scenes;
- reactive endings and Chronicle-history reveals.

It extends the existing Storywide Interactive Presence / screen-first doctrine. It does not alter factual history or resolver ownership.

## Final lock

> **Characters should not just speak. They should react.**
>
> **Important Story information should be performed through character expression, body language, timing, movement and vocal reaction as well as dialogue.**
>
> **Do not write every character as permanently dead-faced, solemn or motionless merely because the scene is serious.**
