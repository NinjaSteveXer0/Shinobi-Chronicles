# Shinobi Chronicles — Dynamic Cross-Team Collaboration and Chronicle Relationship Authority

**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Owner:** Writing — Konoha  
**Status:** **BINDING WRITING / STORY AUTHORING AUTHORITY — CE/CHRONICLE STATE CONSUMER; IMPLEMENTATION SEPARATE**

---

## 1. Core rule

Formal team assignment is an organisational fact. It is **not** a permanent social, investigative or Chronicle silo.

During Story, Missions, investigations, travel, Battles and other legitimate occurrences, current participants may form temporary working pairs/subgroups across formal team boundaries when their own:

- Knowledge;
- capability;
- role;
- trust;
- curiosity;
- suspicion;
- relationship history;
- tactical preference;
- moral judgement;
- emotional state;
- institutional duty;
- current personal objective;

legitimately support that collaboration.

Canonical shorthand:

> **Team membership tells us who is formally assigned together. Chronicle history tells us who actually works together now.**

---

## 2. Structured autonomy application

Before a meaningful protagonist decision, NPCs may independently choose different investigative/tactical roles or partners where that intent is reasonably perceivable.

The Origin Character does not automatically assign everyone.

Possible outcomes include:

- two members of different teams pairing around one clue;
- one Jōnin investigating alone;
- a Genin temporarily working under another team's Jōnin;
- one participant refusing a proposed pairing;
- several participants converging on the same problem for different reasons;
- a formal team splitting into multiple temporary subgroups;
- two previously distant characters building rapport through repeated successful collaboration;
- friction, distrust or rivalry arising from a poor shared outcome;
- later mission choices being affected by prior cross-team Shared History.

Temporary collaboration does **not** rewrite formal team identity unless a separate authority legitimately changes team assignment.

Preserve:

- team != ownership;
- team != political allegiance;
- assignment != relationship;
- temporary pairing != permanent reassignment;
- cooperation != friendship automatically;
- disagreement != hostility automatically;
- Story choice != party command.

---

## 3. Chronicle consequences

Cross-team activity may legitimately create provenance-bearing history between participants.

Where CE/relationship authority supports it, later state may consume facts such as:

- worked an investigation together;
- protected one another;
- disagreed over method;
- trusted the other's judgement;
- concealed information from one another;
- witnessed a hard moral choice;
- shared Battle danger;
- saved / failed / abandoned / defied / defended another participant;
- developed professional respect;
- developed resentment;
- learned another character's habits/capabilities;
- formed or broke trust.

These histories may later affect:

- dialogue;
- willingness to cooperate;
- who approaches whom;
- who believes whom;
- who volunteers for a route;
- who refuses a route;
- tactical coordination;
- relationship opportunities;
- political interpretation;
- future Story branching where legitimately authored.

No universal hidden friendship/morality score should replace the factual history.

---

## 4. Capability and role matching

A character may choose a subgroup because another participant is the most useful current partner, even if they are not friends or formal teammates.

Examples:

- a Byakugan user + an investigator tracing operational evidence;
- a medical-capable Genin + a protagonist examining administrative/medical access overlap;
- a socially perceptive character + a surveillance specialist tracing rumour distribution;
- two strong trackers from different teams pursuing a physical trail;
- a politically suspicious Jōnin investigating an institution independently while others follow safer evidence chains.

This is not party optimisation authored from above. Each character's intent must remain Knowledge- and personality-bounded.

## 5. Arc 2 Mission 2 — THE LEAK application

Current developer-Menma exploration may legitimately produce cross-team subgrouping such as:

- **Kagami + Hinata + Genma** on the Operational chain;
- **Anko** independently pursuing ANBU / ROOT-adjacent suspicion where current Knowledge permits;
- **Menma + Shizune** on an Administrative chain if Shizune's current participation and relevant access/capability are legitimate;
- **Mikoto + Mukai** following human-distribution / counter-surveillance pressure;
- or entirely different groupings based on resolved current participant intent.

These examples are **not fixed choreography** and must not become universal Mission-2 assignments.

The important rule is that the investigation may reorganise around the problem rather than preserving two intact formal teams merely because the mission began with Team Anko and another team.

This supports the existing Structured Story Autonomy doctrine:

1. NPCs form/express their own intent;
2. cross-team collaboration may arise naturally;
3. the Origin Character then chooses their own course;
4. CE/Story records the actual resulting history;
5. later scenes may consume those new relationships and shared experiences.

## 7. Personality and disagreement

Cross-team pairing is especially valuable because different people should not simply inherit their team leader's opinion.

A pairing can expose:

- incompatible methods;
- unexpected chemistry;
- ideological disagreement;
- complementary capabilities;
- admiration;
- annoyance;
- moral tension;
- humour that only exists between those characters;
- future loyalty or rivalry.

A successful pairing today does not guarantee the same pair will cooperate tomorrow.

A bad first interaction does not forbid eventual friendship.

> **Shared history creates future possibility; it does not pre-script the relationship outcome.**
