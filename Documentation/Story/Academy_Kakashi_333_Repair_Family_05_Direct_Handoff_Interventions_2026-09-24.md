# INTERRUPT THE HANDOFF

ANBU Marked Target reaches beneath his clothing.

Package Smuggler steps forward.

Kakashi drops between them.

ANBU Marked Target jerks the package back.

Package Smuggler turns on Kakashi.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “You could've kept watching.”

**KAKASHI:** “I saw enough.”

The two men separate.

One to either side.

ANBU Marked Target keeps the package tight against himself.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “You're alone.”

Kakashi settles into stance.

He doesn't answer.

Neither man waits.

### PL BATTLE

**Kakashi Hatake vs ANBU Marked Target + Package Smuggler**

---

## IF KAKASHI LOSES

ANBU Marked Target is moving before Kakashi gets fully upright.

The package stays against his side.

Package Smuggler follows him out of the street.

Kakashi reaches the corner in time to see both men disappear into the next stretch of Konoha.

He waits.

No one else comes.

Kakashi turns back toward ANBU.

---

## IF KAKASHI WINS

Package Smuggler goes down first.

ANBU Marked Target follows.

Kakashi goes straight to the package.

Takes it.

Checks the seal.

Secures it beneath his vest.

Movement cuts across the other side of the Sakura tree.

A masked shinobi lands in the open street.

She doesn't look at either man on the ground.

Her eyes settle on Kakashi's vest.

**KAKASHI:** “You're after this.”

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:** “Put it down.”

**KAKASHI:** “No.”

She moves.

### PL BATTLE

**Kakashi Hatake vs Masked Interceptor**

---

## IF MASKED INTERCEPTOR WINS

Masked Interceptor breaks through Kakashi's guard.

Her hand closes over the package.

Kakashi turns with her.

Too late.

She tears it free.

For half a second, she checks the seal.

Then she is gone.

Kakashi stays down long enough to hear her footsteps disappear.

The two men from the first fight are still where they fell.

Kakashi looks at them.

Then toward the route the package took.

He forces himself up.

And heads back to ANBU.

---

## IF KAKASHI WINS BOTH FIGHTS

Masked Interceptor hits the ground.

Kakashi stays between her and the package.

Then looks across the street.

ANBU Marked Target.

Package Smuggler.

Masked Interceptor.

All three are down.

The package is still against Kakashi's vest.

# SLIP IN AND TAKE IT

Kakashi watches the space between the two men.

ANBU Marked Target still has the package.

Package Smuggler is waiting for it.

Kakashi drops from cover.

Quietly.

One step.

Then another.

The package begins to move.

Kakashi reaches for it.

---

## IF THE THEFT WORKS

ANBU Marked Target's hand closes on empty cloth.

Package Smuggler's fingers close on nothing.

He looks at the waiting hand.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Where is it?”

ANBU Marked Target checks once.

Kakashi is already over the wall.

The package is against his side before either man sees where he went.

He keeps moving.

---

## IF KAKASHI IS CAUGHT

Kakashi gets his hand onto the package.

ANBU Marked Target gets his hand onto Kakashi.

Kakashi twists free.

The package stays where it was.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “There you are.”

Kakashi lands in the open.

Movement tears out of the dark beside the street.

Masked Interceptor.

Package Smuggler reacts to her.

ANBU Marked Target keeps his eyes on Kakashi.

Masked Interceptor takes in all three men.

Her attention stops on the package.

No one has a clean exit anymore.

### PL BATTLE

**Kakashi Hatake vs ANBU Marked Target + Package Smuggler + Masked Interceptor**

---

## IF KAKASHI LOSES

The pressure comes from three directions.

ANBU Marked Target finds the opening.

Kakashi hits the ground.

Package Smuggler moves first.

ANBU Marked Target follows with the package still against him.

Masked Interceptor stays for one heartbeat longer.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:** “Stay down.”

Then she goes too.

Kakashi waits until the street is quiet.

When he finally stands, choosing a direction would be guessing.

He turns toward ANBU.

---

## IF KAKASHI WINS

The last opponent hits the ground.

The street goes still.

Kakashi goes to ANBU Marked Target.

Takes the package.

Checks the seal.

Secures it.

Then turns back to the three defeated shinobi.

### CHOICE

**TAKE THEM TO THE UCHIHA POLICE**

**BRING THEM TO ANBU**

**KILL THEM**

**LET THEM GO**
