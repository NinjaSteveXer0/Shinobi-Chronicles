# Shinobi Chronicles — Capability-Responsive Story Outcomes and Origin-Build Divergence Authority

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Owner:** Writing — Konoha  
**Status:** **BINDING WRITING / STORY AUTHORITY — OUTCOME DIVERGENCE RULE CLOSED; EXACT CROSS-SYSTEM REWARD SEMANTICS REQUIRE OWNER RATIFICATION**

---

## 1. Purpose

Shinobi Chronicles must preserve two truths at the same time:

1. the extraordinary outcomes already earned in the developer Menma Chronicle are **real Chronicle outcomes** and must not be flattened, rewritten away, or demoted into disposable examples; and
2. those outcomes are **not universal Story rewards** that every Origin Character should receive through cosmetically different dialogue.

The governing rule for Writing going forward is:

> **Origin build, discipline, capability, Knowledge, relationships and committed history may periodically create materially different Story outcomes, rewards, discoveries, survival routes, access routes, costs and future opportunities.**

This extends the existing capability-responsive event ecology into explicit Writing doctrine.

It is not a promise that every event supports every build, and it is not a universal reward table.

---

## 2. Locked Menma Chronicle outcomes

### 2.1 Arc 1 — Echo / identity-lineage outcome

The Menma developer Chronicle legitimately produced the Arc-1 Echo / identity-rebinding lineage and its related history through the choices, capabilities and events actually committed in that Chronicle.

That outcome remains genuine Menma Chronicle history.

It must not be:

- removed merely because another Origin Character would not receive it;
- converted into universal hidden canon for every protagonist;
- treated as the default Arc-1 reward package;
- cosmetically renamed for unrelated builds;
- retroactively granted to another Chronicle without legitimate causal history.

A different Origin Character may leave Arc 1 with a completely different high-value outcome because their disciplines, experience, relationships and choices interact with the same or related factual pressures differently.

### 2.2 Arc 2 Mission 3 — Sealed Wall / chamber-preservation outcome

Under the current mission segmentation, the underground descent begins **Arc 2 Mission 3**. Therefore the advanced Sealed-Wall / concealed-chamber sequence belongs to **A2M3**, not A2M2.

Menma's high Fūinjutsu / Kinjutsu capability and prior Chronicle history legitimately created an exceptional solution path in which he could analyse the advanced containment architecture, avoid blindly opening it, construct a continuity-preserving counter-solution / null-bridge style isolation, and preserve/remove the sealed chamber state rather than resolving the situation through a generic interaction.

That outcome remains genuine Menma Chronicle history.

It must not be:

- rewritten into an ordinary universal mission reward;
- given to every protagonist because Story needs the same downstream inventory;
- represented as `high stat = same reward with different flavour text`;
- treated as proof that every build can solve that exact wall/chamber in the same way;
- treated as universal Fūinjutsu/Kinjutsu Mastery or a generic bypass Skill without owning-system authority.

The valuable design lesson is not `everyone should steal the room`.

The valuable design lesson is:

> **Menma could steal the room because this Menma had become the kind of shinobi who could.**

## 3. Writing rule — different builds may produce different outcome classes

Writing must periodically author situations where a Character's actual build/history changes more than dialogue.

Qualifying differences may include materially different ways to:

- perceive the problem;
- identify what matters;
- reach a location/person/object;
- survive a hazard;
- control or defeat an opponent;
- preserve evidence;
- destroy or disable a threat;
- deceive or expose an observer;
- capture or release a person;
- gain or lose access;
- protect a person/object/state;
- extract information;
- transform an object/location/state;
- obtain a different reward class;
- create a different relationship or obligation;
- create a different later event/opportunity;
- leave a different unresolved problem behind.

Different specialist routes need not converge on the same factual prize.

A build-specific route may legitimately gain something another route never sees, while another build gains a different advantage of comparable narrative/gameplay significance.

Preserve:

> **different route ≠ cosmetic route**  
> **different route ≠ automatically superior route**  
> **different outcome ≠ balance-identical loot package**  
> **specialist capability ≠ automatic success**

## 4. Required discipline diversity

Writing must not overprivilege Menma's current Fūinjutsu/Kinjutsu/identity-facing development merely because it has already produced memorable authoring examples.

Other specialist combinations must periodically receive equally meaningful but appropriately different affordances.

### Taijutsu / Genjutsu specialist

Potential outcome families may include, where the factual situation supports them:

- reaching or controlling dangerous physical geometry through superior movement/body control;
- capturing or disabling an important opponent before evidence or a witness is lost;
- surviving contact that another build must avoid;
- recognising, resisting, breaking, redirecting or exploiting perception manipulation;
- using Genjutsu to create a different interrogation, escape, misdirection or counter-surveillance outcome;
- separating real evidence from manipulated perception;
- producing a relationship/reputation consequence through a distinctive physical or psychological resolution.

These are examples of outcome classes, **not pre-granted techniques or rewards**.

### Ninjutsu / Bukijutsu specialist

Potential outcome families may include, where legitimate:

- reshaping terrain, pressure, visibility, heat, water, wind or another factual environmental condition;
- cutting off escape, signalling, supply or infrastructure routes at range;
- safely breaching, exposing or isolating a physical structure by a route unavailable to another build;
- exploiting weapon reach, precision, traps, tool deployment, projectile control or battlefield setup;
- recognising weapon/tool construction, provenance, unusual materials or tactical use;
- recovering, preserving, disabling or repurposing equipment/evidence that another build cannot safely interact with;
- creating a materially different Battle-to-Story consequence because of how the specialist controlled the field.

Again, these are **candidate outcome families**, not an automatic reward table.

### Other combinations

The same principle applies to Medicine, Tracking, Sensory, Summons, Bloodlines, Hosted-Entity relationships, Fūinjutsu, Kinjutsu, stealth, politics/recognition, crafting, clan history, service history, rare Knowledge, relationships and future legitimate specialist disciplines.

## 5. Reward diversity rule

Capability-responsive Story must not collapse every successful route into the same reward with a different explanation.

Possible reward/consequence classes include:

- Knowledge;
- evidence/provenance;
- access;
- a Skill-development opportunity;
- a teacher/contact;
- a relationship change;
- a recruitable/candidate person;
- equipment/material/tool recovery;
- a unique object state;
- altered custody;
- a saved or lost person;
- a protected or transformed location;
- a political obligation;
- reputation/recognition history;
- new future event eligibility;
- secret information retained only by that Chronicle;
- a safer future route;
- a more dangerous but more informative future route;
- a different unresolved mystery.

Exact ownership remains with the appropriate system.

Writing authors the factual Story opportunity/outcome. It does **not** independently grant permanent Stats, PL, Mastery, ownership, Promotion or universal Skills.

Preserve:

**Knowledge ≠ Access ≠ Competence ≠ Power ≠ Mastery.**

## 6. Non-convergence rule

When designing alternate Origin-character realisations, do not ask:

> `How does this other build get Menma's reward?`

Ask instead:

> `Given this Character's actual history and capabilities, what can they legitimately notice, attempt, preserve, change, earn or cause here that Menma could not?`

The same Story situation may therefore produce different factual histories.

Example principle:

- Menma's Fūinjutsu/Kinjutsu path may preserve and remove an advanced sealed chamber;
- a Taijutsu/Genjutsu specialist might instead secure a living operator/witness, defeat a critical countermeasure, expose manipulated perception, or preserve a different evidence chain;
- a Ninjutsu/Bukijutsu specialist might instead control the facility's physical escape/destruction geometry, recover specialist equipment/materials, disable infrastructure, or expose a different operational route.

These examples are directional only. Relevant owners must close exact executable semantics and rewards.

## 7. Story authoring cadence

Not every Section, Mission or event must contain a specialist branch.

Across the campaign, however, specialist development should matter often enough that the player can feel that their Character history changes the Chronicle.

Writing should therefore periodically perform a capability check when authoring major problems:

1. What is factually present?
2. What can baseline-eligible Characters attempt?
3. Which established capabilities/history could legitimately reveal a distinct affordance?
4. Does that affordance change an actual outcome, cost, reward, relationship, Knowledge state or future opportunity?
5. Is the route distinct without being universally superior?
6. Does it preserve owning-system boundaries?

If the only difference is dialogue wording, the route is not meaningfully capability-responsive.

## 10. Hard non-collapse rules

Preserve:

- Origin build ≠ predetermined route;
- discipline ≠ personality;
- specialist eligibility ≠ automatic success;
- Story outcome ≠ automatic Progression;
- mission completion ≠ reward identity;
- Battle victory ≠ Story outcome automatically;
- event eligibility ≠ event success;
- one Character's earned outcome ≠ universal campaign canon;
- alternate Origin Character ≠ cosmetic reskin of Menma;
- different Chronicle ≠ same history with names replaced.

**Core rule:**

> **The game should remember not only what the player chose, but what kind of shinobi they became capable of being when the choice arrived.**
