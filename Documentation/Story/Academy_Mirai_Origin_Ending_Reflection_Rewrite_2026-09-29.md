# SCENE 9 — AFTER

The route map ends up spread across a crate outside Checkpoint Three.

The real Traveller has his drink.

The Academy instructor stands beside the map.

Mirai remains standing.

She has spent enough of the day walking.

---

## A — MIRAI CAUGHT THE SUBSTITUTION THROUGH CONVERSATION

The instructor taps the covered market.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “When?”

Mirai looks at the mark.

**MIRAI:** “After the market.”

The Traveller raises his cup.

**TRAVELLER:** “What gave her away?”

Mirai looks at him.

**MIRAI:** “You did.”

He nearly looks offended.

**TRAVELLER:** “I wasn't there.”

**MIRAI:** “Exactly.”

The instructor waits.

Mirai traces the route with one finger.

**MIRAI:** “She knew where we were going.”

Then she taps the market.

**MIRAI:** “She didn't know what we'd talked about getting there.”

The Traveller smiles into his cup.

**TRAVELLER:** “So all that talking was useful.”

Mirai gives him a look.

**MIRAI:** “Some of it.”

**TRAVELLER:** “I'll take it.”

The instructor does not turn it into a lecture.

Mirai already knows what changed.

---

### REFLECTION — CONVERSATION CATCH

**“The route stayed the same. The person didn't.”**

**“Talking to someone can tell me more than a map.”**

**“I caught the contradiction. Next time I check sooner.”**

**“Getting suspicious wasn't enough. I needed the mismatch.”**

---

## B — MIRAI CAUGHT THE SUBSTITUTION THROUGH CHAKRA

The instructor taps the covered market.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “When?”

Mirai looks at her.

**MIRAI:** “After the market.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “What changed?”

Mirai thinks before answering.

**MIRAI:** “Your chakra.”

The Traveller looks between them.

**TRAVELLER:** “That's still unsettling.”

Mirai ignores him.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Did that tell you who I was?”

**MIRAI:** “No.”

The answer comes immediately.

Mirai looks back at the market mark.

**MIRAI:** “It told me you weren't him.”

The instructor nods once.

Mirai does not need more than that.

---

### REFLECTION — CHAKRA CATCH

**“Something changed. I stopped instead of explaining it away.”**

**“Different chakra told me enough to verify.”**

**“I knew what was wrong before I knew why.”**

**“Next time I check the person again after I lose sight of them.”**

---

## C — MIRAI BECAME SUSPICIOUS BUT COMPLETED THE ESCORT

The instructor taps the last stretch of road.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “You noticed something.”

Mirai looks at the map.

**MIRAI:** “I noticed several things.”

The Traveller shifts his cup between his hands.

Mirai continues before either adult can answer.

**MIRAI:** “None of them felt like enough by themselves.”

The instructor looks at her.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “So you kept going.”

Mirai nods.

Not automatically.

Because that is what she did.

**MIRAI:** “I wasn't going to turn suspicion into proof because I wanted an answer.”

The instructor leaves that alone.

The real Traveller points toward the checkpoint.

**TRAVELLER:** “You did get somebody here safely.”

Mirai looks at him.

**MIRAI:** “The wrong somebody.”

**TRAVELLER:** “Still safely.”

Mirai almost smiles.

Almost.

---

### REFLECTION — SUSPICION / NO CONFRONTATION

**“I needed more than suspicion before I acted.”**

**“I was right to notice it. I wasn't ready to prove it.”**

**“Next time I verify without giving away what I know.”**

**“Finishing the route didn't answer who I was escorting.”**

---

## D — MIRAI MISSED THE SUBSTITUTION

Mirai is already looking at the covered market when the instructor joins her.

**MIRAI:** “I lost him there.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “For a few seconds.”

**MIRAI:** “It was enough.”

The real Traveller lifts his cup.

**TRAVELLER:** “I was fine.”

Mirai looks at him.

**MIRAI:** “I know.”

Then toward the instructor.

**MIRAI:** “I didn't know you weren't him.”

The instructor does not soften the fact.

Does not sharpen it either.

Mirai looks down at the route map.

Every turn is exactly where it was supposed to be.

That is suddenly the least interesting thing on the page.

---

### REFLECTION — MISSED SUBSTITUTION

**“I watched the road better than I watched the person.”**

**“Losing sight of someone should change what I check next.”**

**“I completed the escort. I missed the switch.”**

**“Next time I learn more than the route.”**

## IF MIRAI TALKED WITH HIM EARLIER

The Traveller heads toward East Market.

**MIRAI:** “You still haven't proved plum tea is bad.”

He points at her.

**TRAVELLER:** “See? That's dangerous thinking.”

Mirai watches him go.

The instructor starts gathering the route map.

Mirai reaches over and folds it first.

She hands it back.

---

## IF MIRAI KEPT THINGS PROFESSIONAL

The Traveller points toward Mirai's pocket.

**TRAVELLER:** “You keeping that?”

Mirai checks the wrapped sweet.

**MIRAI:** “Apparently.”

**TRAVELLER:** “Good.”

He starts away.

Then looks back.

**TRAVELLER:** “Next escort might talk more.”

Mirai puts the sweet back into her pocket.

**MIRAI:** “They might.”

That is not a promise.

It is more than she would have said this morning.
