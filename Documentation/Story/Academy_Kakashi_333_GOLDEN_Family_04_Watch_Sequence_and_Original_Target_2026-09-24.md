Then turns toward ANBU.

Then runs.

# THE PACKAGE CHASE

Kakashi catches the next roof edge.

Drops to a side street.

The footsteps sharpen.

Package Smuggler appears at the next junction.

He hears Kakashi land and looks back.

His face falls.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “You again.”

Kakashi's eye goes to the package.

**KAKASHI:** “Give it to me.”

Package Smuggler changes direction.

Kakashi gets ahead of him before the corner.

The man stops.

Checks behind him.

Then looks at Kakashi.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “You could've stayed with the masked one.”

**KAKASHI:** “You could've dropped the package.”

Package Smuggler reaches for his weapon.

The talking ends.

### PL BATTLE

**Kakashi Hatake vs Package Smuggler**

---

Package Smuggler breaks past him.

Kakashi turns after him.

The package strikes against the man's side as he runs.

One corner.

Then another.

Gone.

Kakashi follows until the next roof gives him nothing.

He stops.

Breathing hard.

Listens.

Nothing.

Then turns back toward ANBU.

Package Smuggler goes down.

Kakashi takes the package from his reach.

# CHASE THE MAN FROM THE PHOTO

Package Smuggler leaves with the package.

Masked Interceptor drives into the street behind him.

Kakashi watches neither.

The man from the photograph is already disappearing into the next road.

Kakashi goes after him.

---

## IF THE TRAIL DIES

ANBU Marked Target clears the next street.

Kakashi follows.

A shifted roof tile.

Fresh dust from a wall.

A landing mark at the next corner.

Then nothing.

Kakashi checks the roof above.

The crossing below.

Another wall.

No fourth sign.

He looks back the way he came.

The package went the other direction.

The man ahead is gone.

Kakashi turns toward ANBU.

---

## IF KAKASHI REACHES HIM

ANBU Marked Target rounds the next corner—

and stops.

A small ninken sits in the middle of the street.

The man looks down.

The ninken looks past him.

Kakashi lands behind.

**PAKKUN:** “This yours?”

Kakashi keeps his eye on the man between them.

**KAKASHI:** “Apparently.”

ANBU Marked Target looks over his shoulder.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “You know I don't have the package.”

**KAKASHI:** “I know.”

The man's eyes narrow.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “Then why are you here?”

**KAKASHI:** “You were in the photograph.”

ANBU Marked Target glances toward the open end of the alley.

Pakkun stands.

A low growl stops the next step.

The man's hand moves toward his weapon.

Kakashi settles into stance.
