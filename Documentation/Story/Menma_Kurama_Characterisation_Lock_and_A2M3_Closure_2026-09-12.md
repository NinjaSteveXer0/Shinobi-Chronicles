# 2. Menma characterisation lock

Menma remains:

- analytical;
- ambitious;
- technically curious;
- strategically minded;
- capable of deception, concealment and manipulation when he believes the payoff justifies it;
- dry, profane and irreverent rather than politely introspective;
- loyal in ways that do not make him automatically transparent, obedient or morally clean;
- capable of affection without becoming soft, sentimental or universally forgiving;
- willing to exploit an advantage even while recognising that the motive is selfish or ugly;
- capable of questioning a plan because it is dangerous, uncertain, tactically weak or personally costly without turning every question into a moral conversion;
- increasingly attracted to freedom, autonomy, secrecy, strength and the ability to act outside institutional control.

Menma is **not** to be flattened into:

- a moral lecturer;
- a permanently remorseful manipulator;
- a family-friendly hero who always chooses disclosure/cooperation;
- a character who immediately abandons darker plans after recognising their ethical cost;
- a saintly protector archetype;
- a miniature adult philosopher who always has the correct moral answer;
- a permanently sarcastic one-note voice with no fear, curiosity, arrogance, humour or vulnerability.

Canonical tone test:

> **Menma may recognise that something is manipulative, selfish, dangerous or morally ugly and still decide that he wants the result. Recognition does not equal renunciation.**

---

# 3. Kurama characterisation lock

Kurama remains:

- ancient;
- proud;
- sardonic;
- blunt;
- territorial about his own agency and private thoughts;
- deeply motivated by freedom after prolonged imprisonment;
- willing to manipulate, withhold, provoke or strategically cooperate where it serves his interests;
- capable of attachment and loyalty without becoming a pet, mascot, therapist or benevolent guardian;
- capable of helping Menma while angry with him;
- capable of liking or respecting someone without admitting it cleanly;
- capable of self-awareness without collapsing into apology or moral submission;
- capable of wanting Naruto and his other half to develop because that may also improve Kurama's own freedom.

Kurama is **not** to be flattened into:

- a cuddly family member;
- an always-wise conscience;
- a moral referee for Menma;
- a repentant prisoner who apologises for wanting freedom;
- a source of wholesome affirmations;
- a tame power battery;
- a generic grumpy mentor whose threats/insults never carry weight.

Canonical tone test:

> **Kurama can care and still be dangerous. He can help and still be selfish. He can be right about Menma without being morally pure himself.**

---

# 4. Menma/Kurama relationship lock

Their relationship should retain friction, complicity and mutual advantage.

Core qualities:

- banter is often rude, profane and genuinely adversarial in texture;
- neither automatically yields to the other;
- both may conceal, manipulate or test;
- both may call out the other's hypocrisy without requiring repentance;
- trust is built through repeated history, not sentimental declarations;
- affection often appears as insults, tactical honesty, shared amusement, grudging respect, warning or cooperation;
- they may pursue goals that benefit both even where the route harms or manipulates third parties;
- disagreement does not automatically fracture the relationship;
- agreement does not make them morally correct;
- strategic secrecy from Minato/ANBU/Konoha may be legitimate for them when they believe institutional control threatens autonomy;
- their increasing taste for freedom and strength is an active Story force, not background flavour.

Do not force every dark beat into an immediate ethical lesson.

Do not sanitize profanity when the scene/voice naturally supports it.

Do not turn every family interaction into proof that everyone loves/trusts one another.

Do not make family warmth erase surveillance, political danger, resentment, manipulation, secrecy or self-interest.

Canonical relationship rule:

> **Menma and Kurama are not becoming good because they trust each other. They are becoming more capable together. What they do with that capability remains a Chronicle question.**

---

# 5. Family-scene tone boundary

Kushina, Naruto, Minato and other family relationships may contain real affection, humour and warmth, but family scenes must not default to PG reassurance.

Preserve the possibility of:

- secrecy;
- resentment;
- political conflict;
- surveillance;
- competing loyalties;
- manipulation;
- withheld Knowledge;
- genuine arguments;
- autonomy conflicts;
- fear of institutional control;
- affection coexisting with mistrust.

Family warmth is one truth among several, not a solvent that erases the others.

# 9. Writing rule going forward

Interactive Story should not produce decisions simply because a scene can support more choices.

When a scene has already delivered its dramatic function, Writing may:

- resolve the selected action;
- allow NPC-autonomous reaction;
- close the beat/Section/Mission cleanly;
- withhold further choice prompts until a genuinely consequential decision arises.

Canonical pacing rule:

> **Player agency requires meaningful decisions, not constant decisions.**

This rule is especially important after long dialogue chains or heavily branching investigation scenes.
