# SCENE 01 — KONOHA ROOFTOP, NIGHT

Kakashi stands alone on a Konoha rooftop, the village lights spread out below him.

A masked ANBU operative lands behind him without warning.

**ANBU OPERATIVE:** "Kakashi Hatake."

Kakashi turns.

**ANBU OPERATIVE:** "You have orders. Stop this package from falling into the wrong hands."

The operative holds out a sealed envelope.

Kakashi crosses the rooftop and takes it.

**KAKASHI:** "Why are you coming to me with this?"

**ANBU OPERATIVE:** "Hokage's orders."

Kakashi looks down at the seal.

He breaks it.

A target photograph waits inside.

# SCENE 02 — THE TAIL

Kakashi does not need long to find the man from the photograph.

Being seen finding him would be the problem.

He follows from above at first.

When the streets narrow, Kakashi drops lower.

The target walks without checking every window or roofline. No nervous turns. No sudden changes of pace.

He is not looking for Kakashi.

Good.

Kakashi keeps far enough back that the man never needs to.

The route bends through older streets.

Past shuttered shops.

Toward the Sakura tree.

The target slows.

Someone is waiting ahead.

Kakashi settles into the shadow above the street.
