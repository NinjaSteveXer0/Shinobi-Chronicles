# PLAYER-FACING STORY

# ACADEMY KUSHINA
## THE BROKEN FORMULA

---

# SCENE 1 — PRACTICAL

The practice scroll lies open between Kushina and another Academy student.

The instructor has already made them redraw the outer ring twice.

Kushina stares at the line in front of her.

**KUSHINA:** “It was fine the first time.”

The classmate leans closer.

**CLASSMATE:** “You said that about the second one too.”

Kushina points at the edge of their formula.

**KUSHINA:** “Because it was.”

The instructor walks past.

Does not stop.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “If you're both finished arguing with the ink, finish the seal.”

Kushina looks after him.

**KUSHINA:** “I wasn't arguing.”

The classmate looks at her.

Kushina looks back.

**KUSHINA:** “I was right.”

The classmate laughs.

Kushina returns to the scroll.

This part she likes.

Lines have reasons.

Connections either close or they do not.

If something is wrong, there is somewhere to look.

She places the next mark.

The classmate adds theirs.

For one breath, the pattern settles.

Then an inked line shivers.

Kushina's expression changes.

**KUSHINA:** “Wait.”

The classmate's hand stops.

Too late.

---

# SCENE 2 — THE BREAK

Chakra spits through the damaged line.

The scroll bucks against the courtyard stone.

The classmate freezes with both hands still hovering over it.

A second pulse gathers under the ink.

The instructor turns from across the yard.

Kushina is closer.

She has time to do one thing first.

### CHOICE

**GET THE STUDENT CLEAR**

**CONTAIN THE DAMAGED SEAL**

**MOVE THE SCROLL TO THE SAFETY LANE**

**CORRECT THE FORMULA**

---

# ROUTE A — GET THE STUDENT CLEAR

Kushina does not touch the formula.

She grabs the classmate instead.

The next pulse tears across the stone as she yanks them backward.

Both hit the ground outside the marked practice area.

The classmate jerks their arm free.

**CLASSMATE:** “I could've moved.”

Kushina sits up.

**KUSHINA:** “You were still staring at it.”

The classmate opens their mouth.

Looks at the scorched mark where their knees had been.

Closes it again.

The instructor reaches the scroll and seals the leaking edge from outside the exercise.

The courtyard goes quiet.

Kushina gets to her feet.

The classmate brushes dust from one sleeve.

**CLASSMATE:** “You didn't have to throw me.”

Kushina points at the scorch mark.

**KUSHINA:** “You want me to put you back?”

**CLASSMATE:** “No.”

**KUSHINA:** “Then we're done.”

The classmate fights a smile.

The instructor hears all of it.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “The exercise isn't.”

Kushina looks at the ruined scroll.

**KUSHINA:** “That one is.”

The instructor checks the secured formula.

Then looks at her.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Why the student?”

Kushina looks genuinely confused by the question.

**KUSHINA:** “Because they were closer to exploding than I was.”

The classmate turns.

**CLASSMATE:** “That is not how that sentence works.”

**KUSHINA:** “You know what I mean.”

The instructor does.

He marks something on the exercise sheet.

Kushina immediately leans to see it.

He moves the sheet out of reach.

**KUSHINA:** “Hey.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Next practical.”

Kushina folds her arms.

The classmate bumps her shoulder on the way past.

**CLASSMATE:** “Thanks.”

Kushina looks at them.

A beat.

**KUSHINA:** “Move faster next time.”

The classmate grins.

---

## ROUTE A CLOSE

At the courtyard gate, Kushina looks back at the replaced practice scroll.

The classmate is already arguing with the next formula.

Kushina calls across the yard.

**KUSHINA:** “Don't stare at it this time!”

**CLASSMATE:** “Go home!”

Kushina laughs and leaves.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# ROUTE B — CONTAIN THE DAMAGED SEAL

Kushina drops beside the scroll.

The original pattern is already torn too badly to restore before the next pulse.

She stops trying.

Her fingers move to the damaged edge instead.

New line.

New boundary.

Close the leak first.

The chakra hits the replacement boundary and pushes.

Kushina pushes back.

The classmate scrambles clear without being told.

The line seals.

The loose chakra folds back inside it.

Stillness.

The instructor reaches them a moment later.

He looks at the formula.

Then at Kushina.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “That's not the original formula.”

Kushina is still crouched over it.

**KUSHINA:** “It doesn't need to be pretty. It needs to stop leaking.”

The classmate peers over her shoulder.

**CLASSMATE:** “It is definitely not pretty.”

Kushina turns her head slowly.

**CLASSMATE:** “But it stopped leaking.”

Kushina turns back.

**KUSHINA:** “Exactly.”

The instructor traces one finger above the new boundary without touching it.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Why change the exercise?”

**KUSHINA:** “Because the exercise broke.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “And if I wanted the original restored?”

Kushina points at the contained scroll.

**KUSHINA:** “Then now you have time.”

The instructor looks at her.

Kushina waits for the argument.

It does not come.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Good.”

Kushina blinks.

**KUSHINA:** “That's it?”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Would you prefer I complain?”

Kushina looks at the ugly containment line.

Then at him.

**KUSHINA:** “I can think of a few things.”

The classmate laughs.

---

## ROUTE B CLOSE

The instructor gives Kushina a clean practice sheet before she leaves.

She takes one look at it.

**KUSHINA:** “Again?”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “The original formula this time.”

Kushina tucks it under her arm.

**KUSHINA:** “Fine.”

She takes two steps.

Looks back.

**KUSHINA:** “Mine worked.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Go home, Kushina.”

She grins and does.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# ROUTE C — MOVE THE SCROLL TO THE SAFETY LANE

The scroll jumps again.

Kushina grabs it.

The instructor sees what she is doing.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Kushina—”

Too late.

She hurls the unstable scroll into the cleared safety lane.

It lands on empty stone.

The pulse detonates across the marked space with nobody inside it.

The classmate flinches.

Kushina does not.

Much.

The instructor is already moving.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “That was not the assignment.”

Kushina points at the smoking safety lane.

**KUSHINA:** “Neither was exploding.”

The classmate looks from Kushina to the scroll.

**CLASSMATE:** “You just threw a seal.”

**KUSHINA:** “I threw a scroll.”

**CLASSMATE:** “With a seal on it.”

**KUSHINA:** “Into the place labelled safe.”

The instructor reaches the object and locks the remaining chakra down.

Only after it is secured does he turn around.

Kushina braces for the lecture.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Why there?”

She points at the painted lane.

**KUSHINA:** “Because nobody was standing in it.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “And if the impact had made the break worse?”

Kushina looks at the student beside her.

Then at the empty lane.

**KUSHINA:** “Then it would've been worse over there.”

The instructor studies her.

Not approval.

Not disapproval.

Thinking.

The classmate nudges Kushina.

**CLASSMATE:** “He hates that answer.”

Kushina keeps her eyes on the instructor.

**KUSHINA:** “He can hate it after he tells me I'm wrong.”

The instructor exhales.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Next time, warn me before you throw the assignment across the courtyard.”

Kushina smiles.

**KUSHINA:** “So there is a next time.”

The instructor immediately regrets his wording.

---

## ROUTE C CLOSE

As Kushina heads for the gate, the classmate catches up.

**CLASSMATE:** “Were you actually sure that would work?”

Kushina keeps walking.

**KUSHINA:** “I was sure you weren't standing in the safety lane.”

**CLASSMATE:** “That's not what I asked.”

Kushina glances sideways.

**KUSHINA:** “It was enough.”

The classmate thinks about that.

Then nods.

They leave the courtyard together.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# ROUTE D — CORRECT THE FORMULA

Kushina drops beside the scroll.

The formula did not fail everywhere.

One connection stopped closing where it should have.

That is different.

That is fixable.

The classmate backs away.

Kushina redraws the damaged line.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Careful.”

**KUSHINA:** “I know.”

The connection closes.

For half a heartbeat, the entire pattern settles perfectly.

Kushina smiles.

Then the scroll flashes white.

Her smile disappears.

The formula folds inward.

Not like paper.

Like the courtyard has suddenly opened somewhere deeper than the stone beneath it.

Kushina throws one arm across her eyes.

The classmate yelps.

The instructor moves toward them.

The light vanishes.

Something lands beside the scroll with a soft thump.

Silence.

Kushina lowers her arm.

A toad is sitting in the middle of the Academy courtyard.

The toad looks around.

At the scroll.

At Kushina.

At the instructor.

Back to Kushina.

**TOAD:** “...That is not where I was.”

Kushina stares.

**KUSHINA:** “You're a toad.”

**TOAD:** “Excellent observation.”

Behind Kushina—

**CLASSMATE:** “Did you do that?”

Kushina looks over her shoulder.

**KUSHINA:** “Obviously not on purpose!”

The instructor holds one hand out toward both students.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Nobody touch the formula.”

The toad looks down at the formula beneath him.

**TOAD:** “That advice arrived late.”

Kushina's eyes narrow.

**KUSHINA:** “I fixed that.”

The toad leans closer to the line.

**TOAD:** “You fixed one problem.”

He taps beside the connection.

**TOAD:** “You made another.”

Kushina drops back into a crouch opposite him.

The instructor says her name.

She raises one hand without looking away from the toad.

**KUSHINA:** “I'm not touching it.”

A beat.

**KUSHINA:** “Yet.”

The instructor closes his eyes for one second.

---

# SCENE 4 — FIRST CONTACT

The residual connection trembles between Kushina and the toad.

The classmate stays back.

The instructor watches both the formula and the unexpected visitor.

Kushina looks at the toad.

### CHOICE

**“WHAT JUST HAPPENED?”**

**“WHO ARE YOU?”**

**HELP CLOSE THE RESIDUAL CONNECTION**

**“THEN GO BACK BEFORE THIS GETS WORSE.”**

---

## WHAT JUST HAPPENED?

Kushina points at the scroll.

**KUSHINA:** “What just happened?”

The toad taps one webbed finger beside the line she corrected.

**TOAD:** “You fixed the seal.”

Kushina waits.

**TOAD:** “You also connected it somewhere it had no business reaching.”

Kushina looks down.

Then back at him.

**KUSHINA:** “That's not an explanation.”

**TOAD:** “It's the part I know.”

That stops her.

The instructor crouches far enough away not to disturb the pattern.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “And the connection now?”

The toad presses one hand to the stone.

**TOAD:** “Still open.”

Kushina immediately turns back to the scroll.

**KUSHINA:** “Then we close it.”

**TOAD:** “Preferably without collecting anyone else.”

Kushina gives him a look.

---

## WHO ARE YOU?

Kushina folds her arms.

**KUSHINA:** “Who are you?”

The toad looks almost relieved to receive a simpler question.

**TOAD:** “Gerotora.”

The classmate repeats it quietly.

Kushina looks him over again.

**KUSHINA:** “That didn't make this less strange.”

**GEROTORA:** “I wasn't trying to.”

The instructor watches the exchange.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Gerotora. Do you know what connection brought you here?”

Gerotora looks at the formula.

**GEROTORA:** “I know enough to want it closed.”

Kushina crouches beside him.

**KUSHINA:** “Good. We agree on something.”

Gerotora looks at her.

**GEROTORA:** “I'm thrilled.”

---

## HELP CLOSE THE RESIDUAL CONNECTION

Kushina drops back to the scroll.

**KUSHINA:** “Tell me what you need.”

The toad moves to the far side of the trembling line.

That gets the instructor's attention.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Kushina.”

She looks up.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Listen before you move.”

Kushina glances at the toad.

Then back at the formula.

**KUSHINA:** “Fine.”

The toad braces the connection from his side.

Kushina follows the pressure through the line instead of fighting it.

For once, she does exactly what someone tells her the first time.

The opening narrows.

The classmate watches from several steps back.

**CLASSMATE:** “Is it working?”

Kushina's teeth are clenched.

**KUSHINA:** “Yes.”

Gerotora looks across at her.

**TOAD:** “Less talking.”

Kushina looks offended.

But she stops talking.

The connection closes.

---

## THEN GO BACK BEFORE THIS GETS WORSE

Kushina reaches toward the line.

**KUSHINA:** “Then go back before this gets worse.”

The toad's hand lands beside hers.

**TOAD:** “Not like that.”

Kushina stops.

**TOAD:** “Unless you'd like to bring something else through.”

Kushina very carefully takes her hand away.

The classmate takes one more step backward.

**CLASSMATE:** “Please don't.”

Kushina looks at them.

**KUSHINA:** “I wasn't going to.”

Three people look at her.

Kushina's expression changes.

**KUSHINA:** “I wasn't.”

The toad studies the formula.

**TOAD:** “Good. Then listen.”

Kushina hates the tone.

She listens anyway.

---

# SCENE 5 — CLOSING THE CONNECTION

The exact closure follows what Kushina legitimately learned.

If she chose to help directly, she and the toad finish the closure together.

Otherwise, Gerotora controls his side while Kushina follows only the safe correction needed to let the opening collapse.

No branch grants knowledge she did not receive.

No branch grants Summon access.

The last tremor disappears from the line.

The courtyard feels ordinary again.

Almost.

There is still a toad sitting in it.

---

## IF GEROTORA'S NAME IS KNOWN

Gerotora looks at Kushina.

**GEROTORA:** “Next time you touch a formula you don't understand, try not to drag somebody through it.”

Kushina's head comes up.

**KUSHINA:** “I understood it.”

**GEROTORA:** “That's what worries me.”

The classmate makes a sound that might be a laugh.

Kushina looks over.

**CLASSMATE:** “Sorry.”

They are not sorry.

---

## IF HIS NAME IS NOT KNOWN

The toad looks at Kushina.

**TOAD:** “Next time you touch a formula you don't understand, try not to drag somebody through it.”

Kushina's head comes up.

**KUSHINA:** “I understood it.”

**TOAD:** “That's what worries me.”

The classmate turns away before Kushina catches them smiling.

Kushina catches them anyway.

---

# SCENE 6 — AFTER GEROTORA

The residual connection folds shut.

Gerotora disappears with it.

For several seconds, nobody says anything.

The classmate looks at the empty stone.

Then at Kushina.

**CLASSMATE:** “Does that normally happen in sealing practice?”

Kushina looks at the instructor.

The instructor looks at the ruined formula.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “No.”

Kushina exhales.

**KUSHINA:** “Good.”

The instructor looks at her.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Why is that good?”

Kushina points at the scroll.

**KUSHINA:** “Because if that was normal, this class would be ridiculous.”

The classmate laughs.

Even the instructor almost does.

Almost.

He kneels beside the ruined formula.

Kushina joins him.

Not because he asks.

Because she wants to see the line again.

The instructor notices.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You are not fixing it again.”

Kushina looks offended.

**KUSHINA:** “I was looking.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “With your hands?”

Kushina slowly moves both hands behind her back.

The classmate laughs harder.

---

# ROUTE D CLOSE

At the gate, Kushina stops.

Looks back at the courtyard.

At the place the connection opened.

At the instructor still collecting the damaged scroll.

At the classmate still watching her like another toad might appear if she blinks wrong.

Kushina points at them.

**KUSHINA:** “Tomorrow.”

**CLASSMATE:** “What about tomorrow?”

Kushina grins.

**KUSHINA:** “We're doing it right.”

The instructor answers without looking up.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You are doing the assigned exercise.”

Kushina starts walking.

**KUSHINA:** “That's what I said.”

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**
