# 1. Exact triple-kill timing gates

The intended three-kill trajectory is only reachable when the following Battle timing gates are satisfied:

1. **Masked Interceptor must be defeated within 1–4 PL Battle turns.**
   - A 1–4 turn victory preserves the Package Smuggler catch-up window.
   - A victory on turn 5 or later does not preserve the Package Smuggler catch-up window for the triple-kill trajectory.

2. **Package Smuggler must then be defeated within 1–3 PL Battle turns.**
   - A 1–3 turn victory preserves ANBU Marked Target catch-up eligibility.
   - A victory on turn 4 or later does not preserve ANBU Marked Target catch-up eligibility for the triple-kill trajectory.

3. **If both gates are satisfied, Kakashi may continue to ANBU Marked Target.**
   - `Masked Interceptor win <= 4 turns`
   - then `Package Smuggler win <= 3 turns`
   - then `ANBU Marked Target remains reachable`.
   - If Kakashi legitimately reaches ANBU Marked Target, Pakkun is present under existing Story authority.

The lethal/disposition choices that occur after the qualifying Masked Interceptor and Package Smuggler victories do not independently invalidate this authored benchmark timing gate merely by existing. The decisive Battle-time thresholds for the benchmark route are the two turn windows above. Any additional resolver/autonomy complexity introduced later must preserve a legitimate route in which these thresholds can still produce the three-kill outcome.
