## Mandatory final audit before Kakashi Writing closure

The entire authored Kakashi Origin must be reviewed branch-by-branch for all of the following.

### 1. Pre-reveal contamination

Before the Hokage Office reveal, no dialogue or behavior may accidentally reveal that the mission is an ANBU evaluation, that Minato staged it, or that the apparent participants are cooperating Konoha operatives.

Check especially:

- Package Smuggler dialogue;
- Masked Interceptor dialogue;
- ANBU Marked Target dialogue;
- ANBU debrief phrasing;
- participant familiarity with one another;
- knowledge of Kakashi's orders;
- references to a test/evaluation/expected answer.

### 2. Debrief factual completeness

Every materially relevant person, objective, custody state, escape, death/apparent death, battle, and package state that Kakashi legitimately knows about must be accounted for in the ANBU report.

Do not omit the Masked Interceptor merely because Kakashi later pursues the Package Smuggler or ANBU Marked Target.

### 3. Debriefer Knowledge boundary

The ANBU operative must not reveal hidden World Truth through the wording of questions.

Kakashi should describe people and events from his own observed reality. The debriefer should use neutral prompts such as `Report.`, `Then?`, `The package?`, or `Anything else?` unless Kakashi has already established the referent.

### 4. Shared-room participant response completeness

Every relevant speaking participant who is factually present in a Hokage Office consequence/review scene must get their own character-specific contribution and may react to one another.

The room must reflect actual branch history, attendance, custody, injuries, deaths, failed kills, victories, humiliation and relationships.

### 5. Lethal / attempted-lethal seriousness

All `Kill him/her` branches use attempt-to-kill semantics and resolver truth.

Every successful kill, apparent kill, failed kill attempt, later survivor reveal, and cumulative lethal route must receive appropriate emotional weight.

Comedy must not trivialize an actual or attempted killing.

A dedicated lethal-outcome pass is mandatory before closure, including cumulative histories where Kakashi attacks or attempts to kill multiple participants.

### 6. Resolver and chronology integrity

Check:

- turn-window eligibility;
- pursuit availability;
- who possesses the package at every beat;
- who is defeated, escaped, in custody, alive, dead, apparently dead, or unknown to Kakashi;
- whether later scenes use resolver truth rather than button-label inference;
- whether Kakashi knows a survival/death fact at the time he reports it.

### 7. Terminology and disposition consistency

- `Bring him/her to the Uchiha Police Force` replaces `Arrest him/her` in Kakashi Origin.
- `Bring him/her directly to ANBU` remains a separate disposition.
- Use dedicated actor labels: ANBU Marked Target, Package Smuggler, Masked Interceptor.
- Do not regress to reusable enemy-archetype naming.

### 8. Pakkun route integrity

Check temporary-Summon semantics, Shared History, package location, signed Pakkun reactions, victory/loss dialogue and all disposition outcomes without implying acquisition.

### 9. Chronicle Receipt integrity

Receipts must reflect committed CE/Chronicle facts, not inferred choice labels.

No spoiler identities, no hidden evaluation truth that the player has not yet legitimately seen, and no outcome omitted merely because the route became long.

### 10. Final presentation continuity

Check that:

- expressions and reactions match the expressive-performance lock;
- characters in the same room react to each other;
- scene backdrops/locations match factual location;
- black wipes and reveal order remain consistent;
- `YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS` follows the final Chronicle Receipt flow.

## Closure rule

Academy Kakashi Origin cannot be declared Writing-closed until:

1. every remaining branch is authored and signed;
2. the consolidated audit above has been completed across the entire Origin;
3. all confirmed defects are corrected in one controlled reconciliation pass;
4. no unresolved story blockers remain.

This does **not** imply Coding/runtime/Golden closure.
