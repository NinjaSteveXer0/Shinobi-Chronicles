# Shinobi Chronicles — Arc 3 Missions 1–12 Final Production Story Skeleton

**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Owner:** Writing — Konoha  
**Status:** **FINAL PRODUCTION STORY AUTHORITY — ARC 3 NEUTRAL SKELETON CLOSED — IMPLEMENTATION / RUNTIME VALIDATION SEPARATE**

---

## 1. Arc identity

**Arc 3:** **CIVIL WAR / ALIGNMENT BECOMES UNAVOIDABLE**

Binding macro truth consumed from CE / Structured Story Autonomy:

- accumulated Arc-2 destabilisation erupts into Konoha civil war;
- current Hokage **Minato Namikaze** leads the lawful Hokage command;
- **Sasuke Uchiha** enters Arc 3 as the established challenger with substantial support;
- why individual people/institutions support, oppose, cooperate with or reject either side remains Chronicle-relative;
- neutrality becomes increasingly unsustainable for most Chronicles, but Arc 3 is **not** a binary morality route;
- Reform / Dominion / Rogue / Take-the-Throne / earned Non-Conformance remain history-backed trajectories, not menu classes;
- family / team / clan / mentor relation != political allegiance;
- one Battle victory != political legitimacy;
- Story says what the campaign must confront; CE proves why this Chronicle reaches it this way.

Current Arc-2 close is authoritative input:

- both Minato and Sasuke refused a forged first-strike pretext;
- both remain politically credible;
- Minato retains lawful office and substantial support;
- Sasuke retains substantial independent support;
- Konoha no longer shares one uncontested emergency-authority model;
- foreign Kusa/Ame evidence/relationships remain live;
- the developer Menma Chronicle retained mirrored evidence, foreign-source protections and an independent posture rather than choosing a camp.

## 2. Arc-3 thematic rule

Arc 3 is not about discovering that the civil war was secretly caused by one villain.

The war is politically real.

Outside actors may exploit it. Forgery, infiltration and provocation may continue. Those actions do **not** erase the factual disagreement over command, accountability, secrecy, force and institutional legitimacy.

Canonical shorthand:

> **The enemy can widen the fracture. It cannot be used to pretend the fracture was fake.**

## 3. Arc-3 endpoint

Arc 3 must not consume the later founder reveal or Madara endgame.

By Arc end:

- open civil war has produced irreversible political/military history;
- Konoha avoids total destruction in the Alpha-authored baseline;
- Minato and Sasuke remain active political powers in the baseline developer/reference path;
- a **fragile armed settlement / ceasefire / divided legitimacy** replaces uncontrolled open battle;
- different Chronicles may reach that settlement through Minato-aligned, Sasuke-aligned, independent, Rogue, throne-seeking or earned nonconforming histories;
- the settlement does **not** restore pre-Arc-2 unity;
- Arc 4 inherits weakened institutions, foreign exposure, unresolved political sovereignty, debts, casualties, defections and opportunities;
- Hashirama/Tobirama public return remains reserved for later Arc 4 under existing continuity;
- Madara remains later-game/endgame rather than being spent as the secret Arc-3 mastermind.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 1 — THE FIRST SHOT

## Universal function

Arc 3 begins only when the political fracture produces **real Konoha-on-Konoha force**, not another forged-order scare that everyone later dismisses.

The first armed clash must arise from incompatible legitimate intentions under current history.

## 12 Sections

1. **Arc-2 aftermath.** Separate Minato/Sasuke security structures remain active after the joint crisis. Mirrored evidence prevents easy propaganda closure but does not settle authority.
2. **Contested custody/site.** A real person, record, armoury, communications node or strategic facility becomes subject to competing lawful/political claims.
3. **Two genuine orders.** Minato command and Sasuke coalition issue incompatible instructions based on their own Knowledge/obligations. Neither instruction is secretly forged.
4. **Protagonist priority.** Preserve people, preserve evidence, support one claim, prevent both seizures, or pursue another legitimate history-backed objective.
5. **Standoff.** Autonomous personnel arrive with different rules of engagement. Personal relationships do not guarantee compliance.
6. **First escalation.** A refusal, attempted detention, forced entry, disarm action or panic produces physical conflict.
7. **First blood.** Civil-war casualties/injuries occur. Exact combat actors/outcomes are resolver-owned; Story requires that the event can no longer be called merely a political dispute.
8. **Contain / exploit / withdraw.** Protagonist responds to the new reality through current capability/history.
9. **Competing accounts.** Both camps release factually selective but not necessarily wholly false accounts.
10. **Verification cost.** The protagonist's evidence/observer history may prove some facts while leaving motive disputed.
11. **Mobilisation.** Checkpoints, recalls, district security and supply control begin.
12. **Closing Window.** Leave/report/commit to immediate next action. `ARC 3 / MISSION 1 COMPLETE`.

## Neutral decision families

- protect civilians/noncombatants first;
- protect evidence/custody chain;
- support Minato's formal claim;
- support Sasuke's competing claim;
- prevent both sides from taking exclusive control where earned/capable;
- withdraw/refuse participation and accept political consequences.

## Required outputs

- genuine first armed clash;
- no secret-mastermind reset;
- civil war is now factual;
- mobilisation begins.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 2 — WHO YOU STAND WITH

## Universal function

Make civil war personal without using surname/team/family as allegiance automation.

## 12 Sections

1. **Recall and declaration pressure.** Units, clans, offices and families ask members where they will serve.
2. **NPC autonomy pass.** Relevant companions/mentors/relatives form their own intentions from actual history.
3. **Resource split.** Safehouses, medical sites, armouries, intelligence and mission rosters start dividing.
4. **Protagonist operational choice.** Accept a command relationship, remain bounded independent, leave Konoha space, or pursue another earned posture.
5. **First separation.** At least one meaningful prior relationship ends up on a different operational side or refuses the protagonist's choice where history permits.
6. **Cross-line obligation.** A person the protagonist cares about/owes is endangered on the other side of the emerging line.
7. **Action decision.** Break orders to help, obey current side, negotiate exchange, covertly assist, or refuse involvement.
8. **Consequence.** Trust/discipline/suspicion changes from what actually happens.
9. **Faction pluralism.** Uchiha/Sarutobi/ANBU/team/family examples explicitly demonstrate internal disagreement where current participants exist.
10. **No easy home.** Neutral facilities begin receiving pressure from both camps.
11. **Next threat.** Civilian/supply disruption expands beyond political offices.
12. **Closing Window.** Current operational relationship becomes durable but not metaphysically permanent.

## Required outputs

- at least one meaningful relationship/allegiance divergence;
- no clan/team hive;
- protagonist posture exists through actions, not route label.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 3 — NO SAFE DISTRICT

## Universal function

Show that a shinobi civil war attacks ordinary Konoha life even when neither leadership intends mass civilian harm.

## 12 Sections

1. **Supply fracture.** Food, medicine, communications, utilities, evacuation routes or mission income are disrupted.
2. **Civilian displacement.** Families move between districts or shelter under whichever authority feels safer.
3. **Neutral institution under strain.** Hospital, Academy, market, archive or another legitimate institution tries to remain operational.
4. **Priority decision.** Protect civilians/infrastructure, secure supplies, stop raiding, negotiate passage, or pursue a military target.
5. **Scarcity behaviour.** Smuggling, theft, hoarding, coercion and opportunistic crime rise.
6. **Competing protection.** Minato/Sasuke actors each offer security with different conditions/costs.
7. **Corridor crisis.** A humanitarian/neutral route is blocked, exploited or attacked.
8. **Resolution seam.** Escort, negotiation, stealth, counter-raid or Battle as legitimately selected.
9. **Civilian memory.** People remember who helped, endangered, taxed, abandoned or coerced them.
10. **Institutional consequence.** A neutral institution loses capacity, changes allegiance, decentralises or creates independent operating rules.
11. **Territorial implication.** Control of gates/district movement becomes the next war front.
12. **Closing Window.** Protected/abandoned people and route state commit.

## Required outputs

- civilian/infrastructure history becomes political evidence;
- neutral != consequence-free;
- route to territorial division.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 4 — THE LOCKED GATES

## Universal function

Turn political authority into **movement control**: gates, checkpoints, districts, couriers and strategic people/resources.

## 12 Sections

1. **Checkpoint regime.** Competing commands impose movement verification in controlled areas.
2. **Legitimate security rationale.** Infiltration/desertion/sabotage risks make some restrictions rational rather than cartoon tyranny.
3. **Abuse/error appears.** Delay, seizure, profiling, false positives or opportunistic coercion creates real harm.
4. **Strategic-person pressure.** High-value witnesses, specialists, Jinchuriki, bloodline users, commanders or politically symbolic people may receive protective/control restrictions where current history supports it.
5. **Protagonist intent.** Comply, negotiate terms, evade, challenge authority, escort another person through, or exploit competing checkpoints.
6. **Crossing attempt.** Exact World/access/capability state determines what is possible.
7. **Interception.** Another authority/actor contests the movement.
8. **Resolver seam.** Negotiation, pursuit, stealth, Battle or withdrawal.
9. **Territorial map changes.** At least one district/gate/route becomes reliably controlled/contested/neutral.
10. **Information effect.** Movement records themselves become intelligence used by both sides.
11. **Pressure on independent actors.** Anyone outside formal command must now secure real access/supplies/protection.
12. **Closing Window.** Commit current territorial/access consequence.

## Required outputs

- Konoha de facto divided into operational zones;
- movement/access becomes history-backed;
- strategic-person control/autonomy can become a legitimate pressure point without universalising one protagonist's condition.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 5 — THE PRICE OF LOYALTY

## Universal function

Force the protagonist to experience the cost of whichever relationships/authority arrangements they have built.

No posture gets a free moral pass.

## 12 Sections

1. **Hard order/request.** Current allied authority/contact asks for an action with real strategic value and real human/political cost.
2. **Reason revealed.** The request is not arbitrary; the requesting actor has legitimate Knowledge/objectives.
3. **Affected relationship.** Carrying it out harms/exposes someone/institution tied to prior Chronicle history.
4. **Intent decision.** Obey, modify, refuse, warn the target, negotiate, betray, or use another earned route.
5. **NPC response.** Allies may disagree; targets may act independently.
6. **Operational consequence.** Chosen intent produces success/failure/partial outcome through owning resolver.
7. **Discipline/trust cost.** Current command/relationship reacts.
8. **Enemy opportunity.** Opposing camp exploits the resulting gap or controversy.
9. **Second choice.** Double down, repair, defect, expose the order, conceal it, or leave the relationship unresolved.
10. **Public/private divergence.** What happened and what different audiences know may differ.
11. **Neutrality collapse pressure.** The protagonist can no longer pretend all sides treat them as uninvolved.
12. **Closing Window.** Durable loyalty/betrayal/debt/grievance history commits.

## Required outputs

- political posture costs something real;
- route evidence becomes materially thicker without scalar scoring.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 6 — THE UNCLAIMED

## Universal function

Make **independent political action** legitimate only when earned, while also allowing formal allegiance to deepen.

This is the Arc's key route-expression mission.

## 12 Sections

1. **Claim pressure.** Both camps demand clarity/resources/access from important independent actors.
2. **History audit.** CE/Story consumes actual relationships, foreign ties, evidence custody, reputation, capability, safehouses and obligations.
3. **Eligible posture set.** Available concrete actions derive from history; no free secret route button.
4. **Operational commitment.** Protagonist performs one major act that defines how they intend to operate during the next phase.
5. **Possible Minato-aligned realisation.** Accept lawful command while negotiating/reforming constraints.
6. **Possible Sasuke-aligned realisation.** Join coalition challenge with actual trust/ideological basis.
7. **Possible Rogue realisation.** Leave/reject Konoha control and create independent survival/mission access if earned.
8. **Possible Throne realisation.** Publicly or covertly begin building a third leadership claim only when support/power/history make it credible.
9. **Possible Non-Conformance realisation.** Build an alternative compact/coalition/arrangement from genuinely earned relationships and leverage.
10. **Opposition reacts.** Other actors do not automatically respect the protagonist's chosen posture.
11. **War geography responds.** Access, enemies, allies, missions and protection change.
12. **Closing Window.** This is a durable trajectory commitment, not an irreversible metaphysical route lock.

## Required outputs

- protagonist's Arc-3 operational posture becomes materially real;
- nonbinary routes are earned rather than cosmetic.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 7 — FIRE COUNTRY BLEEDS

## Universal function

Show that Konoha's civil war damages the Land of Fire and attracts outside interests.

## 12 Sections

1. **Road/supply disruption.** Village factions depend on routes beyond Konoha.
2. **Fire-country actors react.** Daimyo-side officials, towns, merchants, mission clients or local security may seek protection/neutrality/opportunity where current World authority permits.
3. **Foreign observers move.** Kusa/Ame/other legitimate prior contacts or opportunists react to the weakened Leaf.
4. **Supply/relationship decision.** Secure aid, protect local communities, interdict enemy supply, call in foreign debt, or refuse outside entanglement.
5. **Legitimacy cost.** Foreign help can look like foreign interference; refusing it can cost lives/resources.
6. **Field operation.** Route defence/interdiction/escort/negotiation occurs.
7. **Third-party exploitation.** Missing-nin, mercenaries, smugglers or information actors exploit the war without becoming the civil war's sole cause.
8. **Resolver seam.** Battle/escort/pursuit/negotiation.
9. **Foreign commitment.** At least one outside relationship changes materially.
10. **Domestic accusation.** Opponents interpret the protagonist/faction's outside ties through their own Knowledge and politics.
11. **Siege conditions emerge.** Supply and territorial pressure makes a major Konoha urban confrontation unavoidable.
12. **Closing Window.** Commit outside support/debt/enmity and return/advance toward Konoha.

## Required outputs

- civil war becomes regional problem;
- foreign Arc-2 history can pay off;
- siege escalation prepared.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 8 — THE SIEGE WITHIN

## Universal function

Deliver the major urban/territorial civil-war set piece.

The war is inside Konoha; victory at one site does not equal ownership of the village.

## 12 Sections

1. **Siege/encirclement begins.** One or more strategic districts/sites are isolated by current territorial history.
2. **Multiple objectives.** Command centre, hospital, Academy/civilian shelter, communications, archives, supply and gates cannot all receive full protection.
3. **Priority decision.** Player chooses a factual objective from current posture/history.
4. **Autonomous fronts.** Other teams/factions act elsewhere; no protagonist omnipresence.
5. **First clash.** Real tactical pressure reaches chosen objective.
6. **Battle/resolver seam.** Exact fight/outcome is Combat/owning-resolver authority where applicable.
7. **Local outcome.** Site/person/evidence survives, falls, evacuates, changes hands or remains contested according to resolver/history.
8. **Second-front consequence.** News arrives from objectives the protagonist did not choose.
9. **Command reaction.** Minato/Sasuke/independent leaders adapt rather than behaving as fixed scripts.
10. **Civilian/institutional cost.** Siege leaves lasting damage, displacement or trust change.
11. **Stalemate breaks.** The war moves toward strategic escalation rather than repeating district fights forever.
12. **Closing Window.** Current territorial balance commits.

## Required outputs

- material Konoha territorial/institutional change;
- no universal perfect victory;
- strategic escalation next.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 9 — THE WEAPON QUESTION

## Universal function

Force the civil war to confront what powers, people and forbidden/special capabilities may be used when defeat becomes plausible.

This mission must preserve:

`person != weapon` as a possible political argument without pretending institutions/actors will always respect it.

## 12 Sections

1. **Escalation proposal.** One or more camps consider deploying/controlling a strategic capability/person/resource.
2. **Legitimate fear.** Proposal arises because current battlefield state makes normal methods insufficient.
3. **Agency boundary.** Strategic person's own intent/Knowledge is separate from command request where applicable.
4. **Protagonist decision.** Support controlled use, oppose it, negotiate consent/limits, sabotage access, seek alternate capability, or exploit the opportunity.
5. **Rival response.** Other camp adjusts, potentially preparing its own escalation.
6. **Power demonstration / near-use.** The strategic capability materially changes planning even if never deployed.
7. **Resolver seam.** If actually used, owning Combat/Skill/PL/other authority resolves it. Writing does not invent capability.
8. **Cost revealed.** Political, bodily, relational, strategic or control costs become visible.
9. **Escalation restraint/failure.** A line is held, crossed or redefined by actual history.
10. **Narrative aftermath.** Each camp explains the event differently.
11. **Victory definitions exposed.** Leaders must clarify what they are trying to build, not only what they oppose.
12. **Closing Window.** Strategic-power history becomes a major input to M10.

## Required outputs

- use/control/refusal of special power becomes durable political history;
- no direct hidden PL bonus or invented technique;
- transition from tactics to explicit political end-state.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 10 — THE CLAIM

## Universal function

Force Minato, Sasuke and any earned third/independent leadership trajectory to make **victory concrete**.

The war cannot remain an argument about who was wrong in Arc 2.

## 12 Sections

1. **War exhaustion.** Casualties, shortages and damaged institutions create demand for an end-state.
2. **Minato programme.** Lawful command articulates what restored governance/security/accountability would look like from current history.
3. **Sasuke programme.** Coalition articulates what replacement/shared/restructured authority would look like from current history.
4. **Internal dissent.** Supporters inside both camps disagree about how far to go.
5. **Third posture where earned.** Rogue/Throne/Non-Conformance actors may present concrete alternatives if they have built real support/access.
6. **Protagonist political action.** Endorse, reject, negotiate terms, expose contradictions, build own claim, or refuse legitimacy while acting elsewhere.
7. **Material test.** A live governance/security problem tests the proposed principles.
8. **Outcome.** One or more claims gain/lose credibility from actual performance.
9. **Leadership pressure.** Assassination, detention, mutiny, defection or challenge may arise only from legitimate current actors/history; no mandatory secret mastermind.
10. **No easy election.** Active war conditions prevent dialogue alone from settling authority.
11. **Final-offensive preparations.** At least one camp concludes the stalemate must end by force/decisive operation.
12. **Closing Window.** Political claim/terms become durable and feed M11.

## Required outputs

- end-state programmes become explicit;
- faction internal pluralism remains visible;
- final offensive causally earned.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 11 — THE LAST OFFENSIVE

## Universal function

Force a decisive military/political attempt to end the open civil war.

No side may receive a perfect outcome merely because the protagonist chose it.

## 12 Sections

1. **Offensive begins.** Exact attacker/initiative may vary from Chronicle history, but indefinite stalemate ends.
2. **Three objective families.** Leadership/command, civilian/institutional survival, and communications/evidence/supply all matter.
3. **Priority decision.** Player chooses where to commit.
4. **Allied autonomy.** Companions/factions execute their own tasks, including refusal/defection if earned.
5. **Breakthrough.** One side gains material advantage.
6. **Counterstroke.** Opponent adapts; previous route/history changes what remains possible.
7. **Major resolver seam.** Battle/pursuit/infiltration/defence through owning authority.
8. **Decisive local outcome.** Command/site/person/corridor state changes.
9. **War-ending leverage appears.** Not necessarily victory: evidence, exhaustion, captured route, destroyed logistics, exposed civilians, foreign pressure, leadership vulnerability or another legitimate state makes continuation dramatically more costly.
10. **Continue or stop pressure.** Relevant leaders/actors choose whether to push for annihilation, negotiate, withdraw or reposition from their own history.
11. **Armistice opportunity.** A factual window appears for M12, but nobody is forced to accept good-faith reconciliation.
12. **Closing Window.** Enter final settlement mission with casualties/territory/leverage preserved.

## Required outputs

- open war can no longer continue in the same form;
- settlement window is earned by war state, not authorial lecture.

# ARC 3 / MISSION 12 — AFTER THE FIRE

## Universal function

Close Arc 3 by **stopping uncontrolled open civil war without restoring shared legitimacy**.

## 12 Sections

1. **Settlement window.** Exhaustion/leverage from M11 produces a bounded negotiation/ceasefire opportunity.
2. **Facts on the table.** Territory, casualties, command capacity, civilian conditions, foreign involvement and evidence state constrain fantasy solutions.
3. **Non-negotiables.** Minato, Sasuke and any legitimate third actor establish real red lines.
4. **Protagonist priority.** Push lawful reunification, coalition recognition/restructure, independent protections, own leadership claim, withdrawal, or another earned arrangement.
5. **Spoiler pressure.** Hardliners/opportunists on one or more sides may try to wreck settlement from genuine motives; no need for one external mastermind.
6. **Final crisis.** Protect negotiations/civilians/evidence/leadership or pursue spoiler actor depending current history.
7. **Resolver seam.** Tactical resolution determines what settlement options survive.
8. **Terms.** Surviving actors negotiate from actual leverage, not equal authorial balance.
9. **Baseline Alpha settlement family.** Konoha remains one village in name/current macro world, but emergency political/military authority is fractured and constrained by a fragile ceasefire/recognised opposition/independent protections according to Chronicle history.
10. **Foreign consequence.** Outside actors recognise weakness/opportunity; Arc 4 inherits a destabilised Land of Fire rather than a reset village.
11. **Personal consequence.** Relationships, defections, debts, secrets, custody, independent bases and political trajectories persist.
12. **ARC CLOSING WINDOW.** Explicit factual exit records `ARC 3 / MISSION 12 COMPLETE` and `ARC 3 COMPLETE`.

## Required outputs

- uncontrolled open war pauses/changes form;
- Minato/Sasuke political dispute is not magically healed;
- baseline does not require either leader's death;
- route histories remain live;
- Arc 4 begins from weakened Konoha / regional consequence;
- no founder reveal yet;
- no Madara endgame reveal yet.

## 5. Route-expression doctrine across Arc 3

### Reform

May support Minato, Sasuke, a joint structure or another actor depending on actual reform terms/history. Reform != `Minato side`.

### Dominion

May support stronger Hokage command, Sasuke centralisation, a protagonist throne claim or another control structure. Dominion != one faction.

### Rogue

Requires actual capacity/history to reject Konoha control and survive/operate independently. Rogue != merely choosing `neither` in dialogue.

### Take the Throne

Requires support, capability, legitimacy/leverage and concrete governing action. It is not unlocked by saying `I should be Hokage`.

### Non-Conformance

Requires a real arrangement that existing faction/route expectations do not capture, built from Chronicle relationships, foreign support, evidence, institutions, power and prior action. It is never a free fifth option.

## 6. World off-shoot policy

Main Story should not absorb every civil-war consequence.

Potential `WORLD_OFFSHOOT_ELIGIBLE` families include:

- displaced civilians / ruined districts;
- missing/defected teammates;
- smuggling/supply chains;
- foreign mercenary/intermediary relationships;
- captured/deserted shinobi;
- hardliner cells after ceasefire;
- political prisoners;
- lost evidence;
- strategic-person autonomy disputes;
- clan/family-specific fallout;
- Menma's Sealed Wall / missing ROOT-linked woman where still unresolved.

Later Story consumes only actual off-shoot history.

## 7. Battle / resolver boundary

Arc 3 is a civil-war Arc and contains substantial Battle seams in neutral authoring.

Writing closes:

- why conflict occurs;
- dramatic objectives;
- participant intent where Story-fixed;
- what mission-state transition a resolver result feeds.

Writing does **not** fabricate:

- exact PL;
- Skills/loadouts;
- damage;
- winner from unsupported capability assumptions;
- Rank/Promotion;
- progression reward;
- permanent power unlock.

A developer Chronicle may legitimately resolve some seams through stealth, negotiation, evacuation, counter-intelligence or withdrawal. That does not remove Battle from the neutral production skeleton.

## 8. Alpha compression mode

For autonomous developer Chronicle authoring:

- resolve routine connective sections in compressed summary;
- make protagonist choices from durable personality/history rather than random optimisation;
- preserve NPC autonomy;
- avoid formal Battle outcomes where no owning resolver has supplied them;
- use non-Battle tactical routes where legitimate rather than inventing wins;
- preserve unresolved Combat seams for runtime implementation;
- zoom into dialogue only when the character/political turn itself matters.
