## Purpose

Owner review found that the current Kushina rewrite contains too much constructed banter and too many lines that sound written rather than spoken.

This rewrite preserves the current complete Origin structure while simplifying the dialogue and making young Kushina sound:
- headstrong;
- impulsive;
- proud;
- talkative;
- quick to challenge people;
- quick to act when somebody is in danger;
- technically engaged when the seal itself interests her.

Gerotora remains sceptical and cautious rather than becoming a constant sarcasm machine.

Canon voice basis:
- young Kushina is headstrong, impulsive, stubborn, talkative and tomboyish;
- she is quick-tempered and proud;
- Gerotora is sceptical and cautious.

# PLAYER-FACING STORY

# ACADEMY KUSHINA
## THE BROKEN FORMULA

---

# SCENE 1 — PRACTICAL

The practice scroll lies open on the courtyard stone.

Kushina is already staring at the same line for the third time.

The classmate beside her notices.

**CLASSMATE:** “Still checking it?”

**KUSHINA:** “Because he made us redraw it.”

The instructor looks over from the next station.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Because it was wrong.”

Kushina points at the line.

**KUSHINA:** “It was barely wrong.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Still wrong.”

Kushina mutters under her breath.

The classmate smiles.

**CLASSMATE:** “You know he can hear you.”

**KUSHINA:** “Good.”

The instructor shakes his head and keeps walking.

Kushina goes back to the seal.

She likes this part more than she wants to admit.

A line goes somewhere.

A mark connects to something.

If it fails, there is a reason.

She places the next mark.

The classmate adds theirs.

The formula settles.

Then one line twitches.

Kushina's expression changes.

**KUSHINA:** “Wait.”

The classmate looks down.

The seal flashes.

---

# SCENE 2 — THE BREAK

Chakra spits through the damaged line.

The scroll jumps off the stone.

The classmate freezes beside it.

The instructor turns.

Kushina is closer.

Another pulse is already building.

### CHOICE

**GET THE STUDENT CLEAR**

**CONTAIN THE DAMAGED SEAL**

**MOVE THE SCROLL TO THE SAFETY LANE**

**CORRECT THE FORMULA**

---

# ROUTE A — GET THE STUDENT CLEAR

Kushina grabs the classmate by the arm.

**CLASSMATE:** “Kushina—”

She yanks them backward.

The next burst of chakra cuts across the spot where they were kneeling.

They hit the ground together.

The instructor moves in and secures the scroll.

The classmate sits up.

**CLASSMATE:** “You could've warned me.”

Kushina points at the scorched stone.

**KUSHINA:** “There wasn't time.”

The classmate looks at it.

Then at her.

**CLASSMATE:** “Okay.”

Kushina gets up first and offers a hand.

The classmate takes it.

The instructor finishes locking down the damaged seal.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Why didn't you try to fix it?”

Kushina looks at him.

**KUSHINA:** “They were right next to it.”

The answer is immediate.

The instructor nods.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Fair.”

Kushina looks at the scroll.

**KUSHINA:** “Can I see what broke?”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “After I make sure it isn't going to spit chakra at you again.”

Kushina folds her arms.

**KUSHINA:** “Fine.”

The classmate brushes dust from their clothes.

**CLASSMATE:** “Thanks.”

Kushina looks away.

**KUSHINA:** “Yeah.”

A beat.

**KUSHINA:** “Next time move when I yell.”

The classmate laughs.

---

## ROUTE A CLOSE

At the courtyard gate, the classmate catches up with her.

**CLASSMATE:** “You really would've dragged me out again, wouldn't you?”

Kushina looks at them.

**KUSHINA:** “If you froze again.”

**CLASSMATE:** “I didn't freeze.”

Kushina gives them a look.

The classmate sighs.

**CLASSMATE:** “Fine. Maybe a little.”

Kushina grins.

They leave the courtyard.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

---

# ROUTE B — CONTAIN THE DAMAGED SEAL

Kushina drops beside the scroll.

The original line is already too damaged to repair cleanly before the next pulse.

She stops trying to copy it.

Instead, she draws a tighter boundary around the break.

The chakra pushes against it.

Kushina holds the line.

The classmate backs away.

The leak stops.

The instructor reaches them and looks down.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “That's not the formula I gave you.”

**KUSHINA:** “I know.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You changed it.”

**KUSHINA:** “The other one was leaking.”

The instructor crouches.

Checks the new boundary.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “It's contained.”

Kushina looks satisfied.

**KUSHINA:** “Exactly.”

The classmate leans in.

**CLASSMATE:** “It looks awful.”

Kushina turns.

**KUSHINA:** “It's holding.”

**CLASSMATE:** “I said it looks awful.”

Kushina stares at them.

The classmate smiles.

Kushina gives up and looks back at the seal.

The instructor almost smiles too.

---

## ROUTE B CLOSE

The instructor hands Kushina a fresh sheet.

She looks at it.

Then at him.

**KUSHINA:** “You want me to do another one?”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Yes.”

**KUSHINA:** “Right now?”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Tomorrow.”

Kushina looks back at the contained seal.

**KUSHINA:** “Mine still worked.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Tomorrow, Kushina.”

She tucks the sheet under her arm.

**KUSHINA:** “I'm still counting it.”

She leaves.

---

# ROUTE C — MOVE THE SCROLL TO THE SAFETY LANE

The scroll jumps again.

Kushina grabs it.

The instructor sees her.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Don't—”

Kushina throws the scroll into the empty safety lane.

The next pulse erupts there instead of beside the classmate.

Everyone stares at the smoking scroll.

The instructor looks at Kushina.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You threw it.”

**KUSHINA:** “Nobody was over there.”

The instructor moves to secure it.

The classmate looks at Kushina.

**CLASSMATE:** “You didn't know what it would do when it landed.”

Kushina looks at the empty safety lane.

Then back at them.

**KUSHINA:** “I knew you weren't standing there.”

The instructor finishes locking down the scroll.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Next time, tell me before you throw an unstable seal across the courtyard.”

Kushina opens her mouth.

The instructor raises a hand.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Before.”

Kushina closes her mouth.

Then:

**KUSHINA:** “Fine.”

The classmate laughs.

---

## ROUTE C CLOSE

They walk toward the gate together.

The classmate glances back at the safety lane.

**CLASSMATE:** “You scared me.”

Kushina looks surprised.

**KUSHINA:** “The seal scared you.”

**CLASSMATE:** “You throwing it didn't help.”

Kushina thinks about that.

**KUSHINA:** “...Okay.”

The classmate looks at her.

Kushina points ahead.

**KUSHINA:** “Don't make a big deal out of it.”

They keep walking.

---

# ROUTE D — CORRECT THE FORMULA

Kushina drops beside the scroll.

The damage is narrow.

One connection failed to close.

She can see it.

She knows where it should go.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Kushina, wait.”

**KUSHINA:** “I see it.”

She redraws the line.

The formula settles.

Kushina smiles.

Then the seal flashes white.

The courtyard seems to fold inward.

Kushina throws an arm over her face.

The classmate yells.

The light disappears.

Something lands beside the scroll.

A toad.

Nobody speaks for a second.

The toad looks around.

Then at Kushina.

**TOAD:** “Where am I?”

Kushina stares.

**KUSHINA:** “The Academy.”

The toad looks at the broken seal.

**TOAD:** “How did I get here?”

Kushina looks at the seal too.

**KUSHINA:** “I was hoping you knew.”

The instructor steps closer.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Nobody touch anything.”

Kushina lifts both hands.

**KUSHINA:** “I'm not.”

The toad looks at her fingers.

**TOAD:** “Keep it that way.”

Kushina frowns.

---

# SCENE 4 — FIRST CONTACT

The connection beneath the scroll is still trembling.

The instructor keeps the classmate back.

Kushina stays near the edge of the seal.

The toad watches the damaged line.

### CHOICE

**“WHAT HAPPENED?”**

**“WHO ARE YOU?”**

**HELP CLOSE THE CONNECTION**

**“CAN YOU GO BACK?”**

---

## WHAT HAPPENED?

Kushina points at the formula.

**KUSHINA:** “What happened?”

The toad studies the line.

**TOAD:** “You closed the break.”

Kushina nods.

**TOAD:** “And opened something else.”

Kushina's eyes narrow.

**KUSHINA:** “Where?”

**TOAD:** “If I knew that, I wouldn't be sitting in your Academy.”

Kushina looks back at the seal.

The instructor crouches beside the outer edge.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Is it still open?”

The toad presses one hand against the stone.

**TOAD:** “Yes.”

Kushina immediately leans closer.

**KUSHINA:** “Then tell me how to close it.”

---

## WHO ARE YOU?

Kushina looks at the toad.

**KUSHINA:** “Who are you?”

**TOAD:** “Gerotora.”

Kushina repeats the name once.

**KUSHINA:** “Gerotora.”

He nods.

**KUSHINA:** “Do you know how to get back?”

Gerotora looks down at the connection.

**GEROTORA:** “If you stop touching things for a moment, probably.”

Kushina scowls.

**KUSHINA:** “I touched one thing.”

The classmate coughs into one hand.

Kushina looks back.

**CLASSMATE:** “Nothing.”

---

## HELP CLOSE THE CONNECTION

Kushina kneels beside the line.

**KUSHINA:** “Tell me what to do.”

The toad moves to the opposite side.

The instructor looks at Kushina.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Listen first.”

Kushina nods.

This time she actually waits.

The toad points to the unstable edge.

**TOAD:** “Hold that line. Don't change it.”

Kushina places her fingers where he indicates.

The connection narrows.

The pressure pushes back.

Kushina holds it.

**CLASSMATE:** “Is that good?”

**KUSHINA:** “It's closing.”

The toad adjusts his side.

**TOAD:** “Keep it there.”

Kushina does.

The opening shrinks.

Then stops moving.

---

## CAN YOU GO BACK?

Kushina looks at the toad.

**KUSHINA:** “Can you go back?”

He looks down at the unstable connection.

**TOAD:** “Yes.”

Kushina reaches toward it.

Gerotora immediately stops her.

**TOAD:** “Not yet.”

Kushina pulls her hand back.

**KUSHINA:** “I wasn't doing anything.”

The classmate looks at her.

The instructor looks at her.

Gerotora looks at her.

Kushina sighs.

**KUSHINA:** “Fine. I was going to.”

Gerotora points at the line.

**TOAD:** “Then listen.”

Kushina does.

---

# SCENE 5 — CLOSING THE CONNECTION

If Kushina chose to help, she works directly with Gerotora.

Otherwise, Gerotora controls his side and tells her only what she needs to do safely.

The opening gets smaller.

The last tremor runs through the line.

Then the connection closes.

The courtyard goes still.

Gerotora looks down at the seal.

Then at Kushina.

---

## IF HIS NAME IS KNOWN

**GEROTORA:** “Next time, don't redraw a seal unless you know where the line goes.”

Kushina bristles.

**KUSHINA:** “I knew where it was supposed to go.”

**GEROTORA:** “Then you guessed wrong.”

Kushina opens her mouth.

The instructor speaks first.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “He's got you there.”

Kushina looks betrayed.

---

## IF HIS NAME IS NOT KNOWN

**TOAD:** “Next time, don't redraw a seal unless you know where the line goes.”

Kushina bristles.

**KUSHINA:** “I knew where it was supposed to go.”

**TOAD:** “Then you guessed wrong.”

Kushina crosses her arms.

The classmate starts smiling.

**KUSHINA:** “Don't.”

The classmate stops.

Mostly.

---

# SCENE 6 — AFTER GEROTORA

The connection closes completely.

Gerotora disappears.

The classmate stares at the empty spot.

**CLASSMATE:** “Is he coming back?”

Kushina looks at the instructor.

**KUSHINA:** “Is he?”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Not today.”

Kushina looks down at the scroll.

**KUSHINA:** “I still want to know what I connected to.”

The instructor picks up the damaged sheet.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Tomorrow.”

**KUSHINA:** “You keep saying that.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Because class is over.”

Kushina looks around.

Only now does she notice everyone else has started packing up.

The classmate shoulders their bag.

**CLASSMATE:** “Come on.”

Kushina stays where she is for another second.

Then gets up.

---

# ROUTE D CLOSE

At the gate, Kushina looks back at the courtyard.

The instructor is still holding the damaged scroll.

She points at him.

**KUSHINA:** “Don't throw that away.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Go home.”

**KUSHINA:** “I'm serious.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “I know.”

Kushina starts walking.

The classmate catches up.

**CLASSMATE:** “You're going to ask about it tomorrow, aren't you?”

Kushina looks at them.

**KUSHINA:** “Obviously.”

They leave together.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**

## Voice lock

Kushina should sound quick, proud and direct.

She can be funny because of what she does and how fast she reacts.

She should not sound like she is waiting for another character to give her a setup line.

Gerotora should sound cautious, sceptical and practical.

He may be blunt.

He should not function as a constant deadpan joke machine.
