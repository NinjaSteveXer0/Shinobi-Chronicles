# 1. BATTLE VICTORY

The alley returns around Kakashi in pieces.

Rainwater.

Lantern light.

Hard breathing.

ANBU Marked Target is down on one knee.

Not unconscious.

Not dead.

Finished.

The package lies several feet away where it came loose during the fight.

The man's eyes find it.

So does Kakashi.

He moves first.

ANBU Marked Target pushes off the ground—

Pakkun steps into his path.

No growl this time.

He doesn't need one.

The man stops.

Looks down at the ninken.

Then past him at Kakashi.

Kakashi picks up the package.

Turns it once in his hand.

Checks the seal.

Still intact.

The tension that has followed him since the rooftop doesn't disappear.

It changes.

The package is secure.

Finally.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“So that's it.”

Kakashi slips the package safely away.

**KAKASHI**  
“That part is.”

The man's expression tightens.

He looks toward the open end of the alley.

Pakkun notices.

Moves half a step.

The exit closes again.

**PAKKUN**  
“You really like running.”

ANBU Marked Target looks at him.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“You really like getting in the way.”

Pakkun considers that.

**PAKKUN**  
“Seems to be working.”

Kakashi approaches.

The target's attention shifts back to him.

His hands are empty now.

Kakashi still watches them.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“You got what you wanted.”

**KAKASHI**  
“Yeah.”

The man waits.

Kakashi doesn't leave.

A small change reaches his face.

Recognition.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“Ah.”

Kakashi takes hold of his arm.

The target tests the grip once.

Not seriously.

Enough to confirm what it means.

Kakashi secures him.

Pakkun watches the restraint settle into place.

The chase is over.

The package is no longer in the wrong hands.

And ANBU Marked Target is now in Kakashi's custody.

Those are two separate problems.

Kakashi has solved both.

What happens to the man next is still a choice.

# 2. BATTLE DEFEAT

The alley comes back into focus slowly.

Kakashi is on one knee.

One hand against the wet stone.

His breathing is heavier than he wants it to be.

Across from him, ANBU Marked Target is still standing.

Barely comfortably.

But standing.

The package remains secured against him.

Pakkun is several paces away, recovering his footing and watching the man with narrowed eyes.

Nobody speaks for a moment.

ANBU Marked Target looks at Kakashi.

Not triumphant.

Just tired.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“You asked.”

Kakashi lifts his eye.

The man adjusts the package beneath his clothing.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET**  
“I answered.”

He starts backing toward the far end of the alley.

Pakkun rises fully.

The target sees it.

His stance changes immediately.

Ready again if it has to.

Pakkun looks at him.

Then at Kakashi.

Then back toward the growing distance between them.

He could chase.

For a moment, it looks like he might.

Instead, he stays.

His decision.

Not Kakashi's command.

ANBU Marked Target reaches the corner.

Stops once.

Looks back.

Kakashi is already pushing himself upright.

Their eyes meet.

Then the man disappears.

Footsteps fade into Konoha.

The package goes with him.

Kakashi stands.

Too late.

Pakkun looks toward the empty corner.

**PAKKUN**  
“Persistent.”

Kakashi brushes water from one glove.

**KAKASHI**  
“So was I.”

Pakkun glances up at him.

**PAKKUN**  
“Was?”

Kakashi looks down the street.

Nothing left to follow.

No sound.

No useful trail he can separate from the village around it.

His eye narrows.

**KAKASHI**  
“Tonight.”

Pakkun gives a quiet huff.

Could be amusement.

Could be approval.

Could be neither.

Kakashi doesn't ask.

The facts are simple enough.

ANBU Marked Target escaped.

The package escaped with him.

Kakashi lost the Battle.

There is no custody.

There is no disposition choice.

And there is no second roll pretending the result didn't matter.

The only honest thing left is the report.

Kakashi turns back toward the ANBU meeting point.

After a moment, Pakkun follows.
