# CHASE THE PACKAGE

Kakashi leaves the Sakura tree behind.

Package Smuggler still has a lead.

Not enough to disappear yet.

The chase cuts through a side street.

Up a wall.

Across the roofs.

The package stays tight against the man's side.

Kakashi closes.

---

## IF THE TRAIL DIES

Package Smuggler clears the next junction.

Kakashi reaches it seconds later.

Three ways forward.

He takes the roof first.

Nothing.

Drops back to the street.

Nothing there either.

A shutter closes somewhere nearby.

Footsteps cross another road.

Neither belongs to the man he was following.

Kakashi checks one last roof edge.

No package.

No receiver.

He turns back toward ANBU.

---

## IF KAKASHI CATCHES HIM

Package Smuggler turns the next corner—

and finds Kakashi landing ahead.

He stops hard.

Looks behind him.

Then at Kakashi.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “You should've stayed with her.”

Kakashi looks at the package.

**KAKASHI:** “Give it to me.”

Package Smuggler exhales.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “I liked you better when you were farther away.”

His free hand drops toward his weapon.

Kakashi shifts with him.

### PL BATTLE

**Kakashi Hatake vs Package Smuggler**

# PACKAGE SMUGGLER BATTLE — LOSS

Package Smuggler breaks through.

Kakashi reaches for the arm protecting the package.

Misses.

The man does not stay to finish the fight.

He runs.

Kakashi follows through the first corner.

Then the second.

By the crossing, there is nothing left to follow.

Kakashi stops.

Listens.

The package is gone with him.

Kakashi turns back toward ANBU.

---

# PACKAGE SMUGGLER BATTLE — VICTORY

Package Smuggler goes down.

Kakashi reaches the package first.

Checks the seal.

Secures it beneath his vest.

Then looks down the route the man from the photograph took.

# CHASE THE MAN FROM THE PHOTO — AFTER PACKAGE RECOVERY

Kakashi leaves Package Smuggler behind.

The recovered package stays against him.

The other trail is thin.

A landing mark on one roof.

Movement at the end of a side street.

Kakashi cuts across.

ANBU Marked Target is still running.

The man reaches the next alley—

and stops.

A small ninken sits in the route ahead.

ANBU Marked Target shifts left.

The ninken matches him.

He tries the other side.

Same result.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “Seriously?”

Kakashi lands behind him.

The ninken looks past the man.

**PAKKUN:** “This yours?”

Kakashi keeps his eye on ANBU Marked Target.

**KAKASHI:** “Apparently.”

The man's gaze drops to Kakashi's vest.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “You got it back.”

**KAKASHI:** “Yes.”

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “Then we're done.”

Kakashi takes one step closer.

**KAKASHI:** “I'm not.”

ANBU Marked Target looks toward the alley mouth.

Pakkun rises.

The man's hand moves toward his weapon.

### PL BATTLE

**Kakashi Hatake + Pakkun vs ANBU Marked Target**

---

# ANBU MARKED TARGET BATTLE — LOSS

ANBU Marked Target finds the opening.

Kakashi hits the ground.

The package stays beneath his vest.

The man sees it.

He looks at the package once.

Then at Pakkun closing the open route.

He chooses the other exit.

By the time Kakashi gets up, the alley is empty.

Pakkun comes back.

His nose goes briefly toward Kakashi's vest.

**PAKKUN:** “Still there.”

Kakashi checks the seal.

**KAKASHI:** “Yes.”

Pakkun looks down the empty street.

Then starts back the way they came.

Kakashi follows.
