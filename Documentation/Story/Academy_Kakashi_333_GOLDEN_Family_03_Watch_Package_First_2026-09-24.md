# GO FOR THE PACKAGE

Package Smuggler is already moving.

Kakashi drops into the street in front of him.

Masked Interceptor reaches them a heartbeat later.

Her line changes.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:** “Move.”

Kakashi doesn't.

Package Smuggler pulls the package behind his body.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “This is getting crowded.”

**KAKASHI:** “Give it to me.”

Package Smuggler looks at him.

Then at Masked Interceptor.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Neither of you seems good at asking.”

Masked Interceptor steps in.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:** “Move.”

Package Smuggler's free hand drops toward his weapon.

Kakashi sees it.

So does she.

All three move.

### PL BATTLE

**Kakashi Hatake vs Package Smuggler + Masked Interceptor**

## IF KAKASHI LOSES

Package Smuggler finds the opening first.

He runs.

Kakashi tries to turn after him.

Masked Interceptor catches his arm and drives him back down.

Wire snaps tight around his wrists.

Kakashi tests it once.

No give.

Masked Interceptor is already looking toward the street Package Smuggler used.

**KAKASHI:** “You're following him.”

Masked Interceptor doesn't answer.

She disappears after the package.

Kakashi waits until her footsteps are gone.

Then starts working at the wire.

By the time he gets free, the street is empty.

He looks once toward the route they took.

Then turns back toward ANBU.

---

## IF KAKASHI WINS

Package Smuggler goes down first.

Masked Interceptor follows.

Kakashi reaches Package Smuggler before either can rise.

The package is still tucked against him.

Kakashi takes it.

Checks the seal.

Secures it beneath his vest.

Only then does he look toward the rooftops.

Toward the route the man from the photograph took.

### CHOICE

**CHASE THE MAN FROM THE PHOTO**

**RETURN TO ANBU**

# CHASE THE MAN FROM THE PHOTO

Kakashi leaves the two defeated shinobi behind.

He takes the roofs.

The package stays tight against him while the Sakura tree falls away.

One roof.

Another.

A glimpse of movement ahead.

Then none.

Kakashi keeps the line as long as he has one.

---

## IF THE TRAIL DIES

He clears another rooftop.

Nothing.

Drops to the street.

Checks the crossing.

Three routes.

No footsteps he trusts.

Kakashi waits.

Listens again.

Still nothing.

His hand touches the package beneath his vest.

Then he turns back.

---

## IF KAKASHI REACHES HIM

ANBU Marked Target cuts into the next alley.

Then stops.

A small ninken is sitting in the way.

The man shifts left.

The ninken matches him.

Right.

Matched again.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “Seriously?”

Kakashi lands behind him.

The ninken looks past the man.

**PAKKUN:** “This yours?”

Kakashi looks at ANBU Marked Target.

**KAKASHI:** “Apparently.”

ANBU Marked Target notices the package against Kakashi.

His eyes narrow.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “You got it back.”

**KAKASHI:** “Yes.”

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “Then why are you still following me?”

Kakashi takes one step closer.

**KAKASHI:** “You were in the photograph.”

The man's gaze shifts to the alley mouth.

Pakkun rises.

**PAKKUN:** “Don't.”

ANBU Marked Target looks down at him.

Then back at Kakashi.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “You two know each other?”

**KAKASHI:** “No.”

Pakkun's ear flicks.

**PAKKUN:** “Don't try it.”

ANBU Marked Target's hand drops toward his weapon.

Kakashi sets his feet.

Kakashi keeps one arm across the package as he hits the ground.

He doesn't.

Then runs.

**KAKASHI:** “Still here.”

Pakkun looks down the empty alley.

**PAKKUN:** “Then you've got one thing to report.”

Kakashi stands.

**KAKASHI:** “Two.”

Pakkun glances at him.

Kakashi starts back toward ANBU.

to the ninken—
