# Academy Hinata — SC Road-to-Ninja-Leaning Voice / Personality Anchor

**Date:** 2026-09-24  
**Owner:** Stephen / Writing — Konoha  
**Status:** **BINDING SC CHARACTER PERFORMANCE DIRECTION — ACADEMY HINATA**

## Stephen direction

Shinobi Chronicles Academy Hinata should not default to the heavily timid / barely-functional presentation associated with early mainline Hinata.

Current SC direction is deliberately **closer to Road to Ninja Hinata** in confidence and assertiveness.

Use as performance influence:
- more self-confident;
- more outspoken;
- more willing to answer back;
- more willing to press an advantage;
- less paralysed by being watched;
- capable of irritation, dry challenge and open disagreement;
- physically decisive once she commits;
- emotionally readable without collapsing into bashfulness.

Do **not** copy the Road to Ninja alternate personality wholesale.

Specifically, SC Academy Hinata is not automatically:
- aggressively jealous;
- romantically possessive;
- threatening other girls;
- vulgar for comedy;
- domineering in every relationship;
- a one-note inversion gag.

The useful Road to Ninja influence is **confidence / assertiveness / willingness to act and speak**, not the full alternate-world caricature.

## Mainline traits retained

Preserve where relevant:
- Hyūga discipline;
- awareness of clan expectation;
- technical Taijutsu foundation;
- persistence;
- ability to keep going after mistakes;
- empathy;
- careful observation;
- family/clan pressure as context.

Mainline official Naruto material establishes that Hinata's deeper strength is persistence and willingness to keep standing back up even under pressure. That remains compatible with the SC assertive presentation.

## Academy-stage execution

Academy Hinata may:
- hesitate tactically without being socially helpless;
- feel pressure without becoming inert;
- disagree with an instructor;
- choose aggression;
- bait an opponent;
- reset an exchange on her own terms;
- answer teasing directly;
- smile, get annoyed, push back or challenge;
- make mistakes and immediately re-enter the exchange.

Avoid:
- constant stammering;
- constant blushing;
- whispered non-answers;
- treating ordinary eye contact as an emotional event;
- requiring another character to supply her courage;
- narration repeatedly telling the player she is shy.

## Current Origin fingerprint

> **This Hinata is under Hyūga pressure, not underneath it.**

The Academy Origin tests how she fights while being watched. It does not test whether she is capable of functioning at all.
