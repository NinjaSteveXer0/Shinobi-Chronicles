# MASKED INTERCEPTOR — KILL HER

## KILLED

Kakashi closes the distance before she can rise.

Masked Interceptor moves to meet him.

The exchange is brief.

When it ends, she does not move again.

Kakashi looks toward the route ahead.

Then moves.

---

## ESCAPED

Kakashi closes the distance.

Masked Interceptor watches him come.

The instant he commits, she twists off the line and drives past him.

Kakashi turns.

Too late.

She reaches the wall.

The roofline.

Gone.

Kakashi stops at the edge of the square.

Then looks back toward the route he still has time to take.

---

# MASKED INTERCEPTOR — RESTRAIN HER AND KEEP MOVING

## RESTRAINED

Kakashi looks toward the route the package took.

Then back at Masked Interceptor.

He draws ninja wire.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:** “You're still going after them.”

**KAKASHI:** “Yes.”

He binds her wrists and fixes the restraint beneath the Sakura tree.

She pulls once.

It holds.

Her eyes lift to him.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:** “You're leaving me here.”

**KAKASHI:** “For now.”

She glances toward the road.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:** “They'll have distance.”

Kakashi checks the wire.

Turns.

**KAKASHI:** “Then I need to move.”

---

## ESCAPED

Kakashi draws the wire and moves in.

Masked Interceptor stays still until the first loop reaches her wrist.

Then she turns through the slack.

Kakashi catches the wire.

Not her.

She clears the edge of the square and reaches the rooftops.

Gone.

Kakashi looks at the loose line in his hand.

Puts it away.

Then moves.

---

# PACKAGE SMUGGLER — KILL HIM

## KILLED

Kakashi steps in before Package Smuggler can recover.

The man sees the decision and tries to move.

Not fast enough.

When it is over, Package Smuggler does not get back up.

Kakashi turns to whatever remains of the route.

---

## ESCAPED — PACKAGE RECOVERED

Kakashi has the package secured before he turns back.

Package Smuggler sees him coming.

The humour leaves his face.

Kakashi moves.

Package Smuggler throws himself sideways, catches the wall and forces himself upright.

Kakashi follows.

The man reaches the corner first.

Gone.

Kakashi stops.

The package is still against his vest.

---

## ESCAPED — PACKAGE MISSING

Kakashi steps toward him.

Package Smuggler looks once toward the road the package already took.

Then back at Kakashi.

He moves first.

Not to fight.

To run.

Kakashi reaches the alley mouth a moment later.

Empty street.

---

# PACKAGE SMUGGLER — RESTRAIN

## RESTRAINED — PACKAGE RECOVERED / CONTINUATION OPEN

Kakashi keeps the package secured and draws the wire.

Package Smuggler watches him bind his wrists.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “You're going after him too.”

**KAKASHI:** “Yes.”

Kakashi finishes the restraint.

Package Smuggler pulls once against it.

It holds.

He looks down the route ANBU Marked Target took.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Of course you are.”

Kakashi is already moving.

---

## RESTRAINED — PACKAGE MISSING

Kakashi draws the wire.

Package Smuggler looks at the empty street beyond him.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Package is still gone.”

**KAKASHI:** “I know.”

Kakashi binds his wrists.

Package Smuggler tests the restraint.

It holds.

Neither of them looks down the empty road again.

---

## ESCAPED — PACKAGE RECOVERED

Kakashi brings out the wire.

Package Smuggler waits until he comes close.

Then moves.

The loose line catches his wrist for half a second.

He tears free.

Kakashi starts after him—

and stops.

The recovered package is still secured against him.

Package Smuggler disappears into the next street without it.

---

## ESCAPED — PACKAGE MISSING

Kakashi reaches for his wrist.

Package Smuggler twists away.

A gap.

A wall.

Then the alley mouth.

By the time Kakashi reaches the corner, the street is empty.

# ANBU MARKED TARGET — KILL HIM

## KILLED

Kakashi moves before the man can get back to his feet.

ANBU Marked Target sees him coming.

Silent.

# ANBU MARKED TARGET — RESTRAIN HIM

Kakashi draws the wire.

ANBU Marked Target looks at it.

Then at Pakkun.

**KAKASHI:** “For now.”

Pakkun remains near the exit.

**KAKASHI:** “Depends who's still there.”

Enough.

Wall.

Roofline.

Gone.

Kakashi gathers the loose wire.
