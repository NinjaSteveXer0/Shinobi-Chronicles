# MASKED INTERCEPTOR — BRING HER TO ANBU

Kakashi draws the wire.

Masked Interceptor watches his hands.

Says nothing.

He binds her wrists.

She tests the restraint once.

Stops.

Kakashi takes the line.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:** “You're leaving the package.”

Kakashi looks toward the route Package Smuggler took.

**KAKASHI:** “I know.”

He starts walking.

She follows.

### ANBU ROOFTOP

The ANBU operative sees the restrained woman before Kakashi reaches him.

His posture changes.

Only slightly.

**ANBU OPERATIVE:** “Report.”

Kakashi stops.

Holds out the restraint line.

**KAKASHI:** “Custody first.”

The operative steps in.

Takes control of the wire.

Only then does Kakashi let go.

Masked Interceptor looks from one to the other.

Still silent.

# MASKED INTERCEPTOR — TAKE HER TO THE UCHIHA POLICE

Kakashi binds her wrists.

Masked Interceptor looks toward the roofs.

Then at the wire.

She does not test it twice.

Kakashi takes the restraint line and turns toward the Police district.

### UCHIHA POLICE EXTERIOR

Two officers come down the steps.

The first sees Kakashi.

The second sees the restrained woman.

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:** “Hold there.”

Kakashi stops.

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:** “What happened?”

**KAKASHI:** “She attacked a man carrying the package I was sent to recover.”

The officer looks at Masked Interceptor.

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:** “Name?”

Kakashi waits.

Masked Interceptor gives him nothing.

**KAKASHI:** “Unknown.”

The second officer moves in for the restraint line.

Kakashi keeps hold until the officer has her.

Then releases it.

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:** “We've got her.”

Kakashi turns back toward the village.

**KAKASHI:** “I need to report.”
