- real security failures framed as proof of total Hokage incompetence;
- forged claims that ANBU has begun purging internal opponents;

### Minato's response

Minato prioritises:

- freezing action on unverified emergency orders;
- central verification of contradictory packets;
- maintaining command continuity;
- preventing panic arrests/mobilisations;
- controlled disclosure until the foreign record package can be authenticated.

Its cost is visible:

- some legitimate actors receive information late;
- people already distrustful of the compromised information chain see the Hokage asking that same chain to verify itself;
- source protection/central secrecy can look indistinguishable from concealment to observers who lack Minato's Knowledge.

### Sasuke's response

Sasuke has independently organised a **cross-source accountability/security effort** from people willing to compare records outside the single Hokage verification chain.

It is not a second government yet.

It does:

- preserve copies of threatened public/institutional evidence where lawful access exists;
- cross-check claims through separate observers rather than assume one office's version is sufficient;
- protect participants who believe official delay may expose them;
- publicly challenge the assumption that central verification deserves unlimited deference after repeated compromise.

Sasuke's support is still developing, but his operation is visibly competent rather than rhetorical.
