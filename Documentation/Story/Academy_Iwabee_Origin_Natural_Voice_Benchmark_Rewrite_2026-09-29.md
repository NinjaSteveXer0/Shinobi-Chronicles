# PLAYER-FACING STORY

# ACADEMY IWABEE
## THE PRACTICAL

---

# SCENE 1 — ANOTHER RED MARK

Iwabee drops the written exercise onto the bench.

A red mark sits across the top.

He stares at it.

Then pushes the paper away.

The instructor picks it up.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Bad one?”

Iwabee looks at him.

**IWABEE:** “What do you think?”

The instructor looks at the mark again.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “I think you're done with paper for now.”

Iwabee straightens.

**IWABEE:** “Good.”

The instructor leads him to a damaged section of the practical ground.

Broken ridges.

Loose stone.

One side has sunk lower than the rest.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Fix it.”

Iwabee looks over the ground.

Then back at him.

**IWABEE:** “That's the whole exercise?”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “For now.”

Iwabee rolls one shoulder.

His mood has already improved.

**IWABEE:** “Finally.”

---

# SCENE 2 — EARTH RELEASE

### CHOICE

**RAISE THE COLLAPSED SECTION**

**FLATTEN THE DAMAGED GROUND**

**BUILD A STABLE PATH THROUGH IT**

**REINFORCE THE WEAKEST SECTION**

---

## RAISE THE COLLAPSED SECTION

Iwabee plants his feet beside the sunken ground.

Earth lifts beneath his hands.

The collapsed section rises in one rough slab until it sits level with the yard.

Loose stone slides from the edge.

Something moves underneath it.

Iwabee stops.

A person was hiding there.

---

## FLATTEN THE DAMAGED GROUND

Iwabee lowers the broken ridges instead of rebuilding them.

The ground settles.

Rubble spreads away from one side.

A crouched figure suddenly loses the cover they were using.

Iwabee sees him immediately.

---

## BUILD A STABLE PATH THROUGH IT

Iwabee leaves the deepest collapse alone.

He raises a solid strip of earth through the damaged section.

The path locks into place.

A pair of feet is standing beside it.

Iwabee follows them upward.

Someone is hiding in the yard.

---

## REINFORCE THE WEAKEST SECTION

Iwabee checks the damaged ground before he moves anything.

One edge is ready to collapse again.

He packs earth beneath it until it holds.

A broken sheet of timber shifts aside.

A young shinobi is crouched behind it.

---

# SCENE 3 — THE ROGUE GENIN

The stranger is wearing shinobi gear.

Not Academy gear.

He looks at Iwabee.

Then the instructor.

Then the open side of the yard.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Move.”

Iwabee does not.

**IWABEE:** “No.”

### CHOICE

**CONFRONT HIM**

**BLOCK HIS ESCAPE WITH EARTH RELEASE**

**CALL THE INSTRUCTOR**

**FINISH THE PRACTICAL**

---

# BRANCH A — CONFRONT HIM

Iwabee steps into the open route.

The Rogue Genin stops.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Get out of the way, kid.”

Iwabee sets his feet.

**IWABEE:** “Make me.”

The Rogue's attention leaves the exit.

Now it is on Iwabee.

## IF IWABEE WINS

The Rogue Genin is the first one forced out of the fight.

Iwabee is still standing.

The instructor moves in before either of them can start again.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “That's enough.”

Iwabee looks at the Rogue.

**IWABEE:** “He was leaving.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “He's not now.”

The instructor takes control.

Iwabee looks back at the section of ground he repaired before the fight.

It is still fixed.

---

## IF IWABEE LOSES

Iwabee's stance breaks first.

He catches himself and starts to rise again.

The Rogue is still ready to fight.

The instructor steps between them.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Stop.”

Iwabee looks past him.

**IWABEE:** “I can still stand.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “That's not the same as being able to keep fighting.”

Iwabee hates that answer.

He also knows it is true.

The Rogue looks toward the open edge of the yard.

The instructor stays between him and Iwabee.

The Rogue takes the chance and runs.

Iwabee watches him go.

Does not chase.

A few seconds later, he looks back at the ground he repaired.

Still fixed.

---

# BRANCH B — BLOCK HIS ESCAPE

Iwabee does not run after him.

He watches the Rogue's eyes.

The moment the Rogue turns toward the open side of the yard, Iwabee moves the earth.

A wall rises across the exit.

The Rogue stops short.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Seriously?”

Iwabee looks at the wall.

Then at him.

**IWABEE:** “Try somewhere else.”

The Rogue checks the yard again.

Instructor on one side.

Earth wall on the other.

He raises his hands.

The instructor moves in and takes control.

Iwabee looks at the wall.

Then at the section he repaired first.

Both are still standing.

---

# BRANCH C — CALL THE INSTRUCTOR

Iwabee keeps his eyes on the Rogue.

**IWABEE:** “Sensei.”

The instructor is already moving.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Back up.”

Iwabee does.

Slowly.

The instructor puts himself between the students and the Rogue.

The Rogue sees the open side of the yard.

Runs.

Iwabee takes one step after him.

The instructor puts out an arm.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Leave it.”

Iwabee watches the Rogue disappear.

He does not like it.

But he stops.

---

# BRANCH D — FINISH THE PRACTICAL

Iwabee looks at the Rogue.

Then at the ground he was told to repair.

**IWABEE:** “Not my job.”

He turns back to the practical.

Behind him, the Rogue runs for the open side of the yard.

Iwabee keeps working.

By the time he finishes the last section, the Rogue is gone.

The ground is usable again.

That was the assignment.

Iwabee completed it.

---

# SCENE 4 — THE INSTRUCTOR

The instructor checks the repaired ground first.

He presses one heel into the raised section.

Tests the edge.

Looks over the path.

Then he looks at Iwabee.

The exact first exchange follows what happened.

---

## IF IWABEE CONFRONTED AND WON

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You really went straight for him.”

**IWABEE:** “He was right there.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Yeah.”

The instructor looks toward the repaired ground.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “So was your work.”

Iwabee looks too.

---

## IF IWABEE CONFRONTED AND LOST

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You picked a fight you couldn't finish.”

Iwabee's jaw tightens.

**IWABEE:** “I know.”

The instructor points toward the repaired ground.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You finished that.”

Iwabee looks over.

**IWABEE:** “Yeah.”

No speech about it follows.

---

## IF IWABEE BLOCKED THE ESCAPE

The instructor looks at the earth wall.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “That wasn't part of the exercise.”

Iwabee shrugs.

**IWABEE:** “Needed a wall.”

The instructor looks at him.

Then back at the wall.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Worked.”

Iwabee almost smiles.

---

## IF IWABEE CALLED THE INSTRUCTOR

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You called me fast.”

Iwabee gives him a flat look.

**IWABEE:** “You were standing there.”

The instructor nods.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Good.”

Iwabee still does not know why that needed saying.

---

## IF IWABEE FINISHED THE PRACTICAL

The instructor checks the last repaired section.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You didn't go after him.”

**IWABEE:** “You told me to fix the yard.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “And you did.”

Iwabee nods once.

That answer is enough.

---

# SCENE 5 — THE PAPER

The instructor picks up the written exercise from the bench.

Iwabee immediately looks annoyed again.

**IWABEE:** “We were doing fine until you picked that up.”

The instructor holds it out.

Iwabee does not take it.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You're good at this.”

He gestures toward the repaired ground.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You're bad at this.”

He taps the paper.

Iwabee folds his arms.

**IWABEE:** “I know.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Then work on the bad part without pretending the good part doesn't matter.”

Iwabee looks at the paper.

Then the yard.

That sounds irritatingly reasonable.

He takes the sheet.

---

# SCENE 6 — IWABEE'S ANSWER

### CHOICE

**“I KNOW WHAT I'M GOOD AT.”**

**“I STILL NEED TO GET BETTER AT THE REST.”**

**“THE ACADEMY CARES TOO MUCH ABOUT TESTS.”**

**“I'LL PROVE I CAN DO IT MY WAY.”**

---

## I KNOW WHAT I'M GOOD AT

**IWABEE:** “I know what I'm good at.”

The instructor nods toward the paper in his hand.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Good. Don't lose that while you fix the rest.”

Iwabee looks down at the red mark.

**IWABEE:** “Still hate this.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “I know.”

---

## I STILL NEED TO GET BETTER AT THE REST

Iwabee looks at the paper.

**IWABEE:** “I still need to get better at this stuff.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Yeah.”

Iwabee looks up.

**IWABEE:** “You could pretend that was harder to admit.”

The instructor smiles.

Iwabee rolls his eyes.

---

## THE ACADEMY CARES TOO MUCH ABOUT TESTS

Iwabee holds up the page.

**IWABEE:** “The Academy cares too much about this.”

The instructor shrugs.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Maybe.”

Iwabee waits.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Still have to pass it.”

Iwabee clicks his tongue.

**IWABEE:** “I know.”

That is the annoying part.

---

## I'LL PROVE I CAN DO IT MY WAY

Iwabee folds the paper once.

**IWABEE:** “I'll prove I can do it my way.”

The instructor looks at the fold.

Then at him.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Pass the written test first.”

Iwabee groans.

**IWABEE:** “You ruin everything.”

---

# SCENE 7 — CLOSE

Iwabee heads for the gate.

Stops.

Looks at the paper in his hand.

For a second it looks like he might throw it away.

He does not.

He stuffs it into his bag.

Behind him, the repaired training ground is still standing.

Iwabee looks back once.

Then leaves.

**YOUR CHRONICLE BEGINS**
