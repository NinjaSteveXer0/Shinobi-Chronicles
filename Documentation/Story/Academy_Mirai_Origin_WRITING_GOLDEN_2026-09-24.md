# ACADEMY MIRAI
## The Escort

---

# SCENE 1 — THE ASSIGNMENT

Mirai arrives early.

Not a little early.

Early enough that the checkpoint map is still rolled up on the instructor's desk.

She looks at her.

Then at the empty yard behind her.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “You're aware this doesn't start for another ten minutes.”

**MIRAI:** “Yes.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Then why are you here?”

Mirai looks at the route map.

**MIRAI:** “You said escort assessment.”

The instructor waits.

Mirai waits back.

Neither seems willing to explain why that answers the question.

A man standing near the gate raises a hand.

Travel bag over one shoulder. Dust on his sandals. Easy smile.

**TRAVELLER:** “I'm guessing she's mine.”

Mirai turns.

The instructor slides the route map across the desk.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Checkpoint Three.”

Mirai opens it immediately.

The route is uncomplicated.

Main street.

Covered market.

East lane.

Checkpoint.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Get him there.”

Mirai looks up.

**MIRAI:** “That's it?”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Disappointed?”

**MIRAI:** “No.”

Too quick.

The traveller smiles.

Mirai notices.

**MIRAI:** “I meant—no. That's clear.”

**TRAVELLER:** “Much better.”

Mirai folds the map.

**MIRAI:** “We should go.”

The traveller falls into step beside her.

Behind them, the instructor says nothing else.

---

# SCENE 2 — THE WALK

For the first street, Mirai checks behind them three times.

On the second, she starts checking rooftops.

By the third, the traveller has noticed.

**TRAVELLER:** “Should I be ducking?”

Mirai looks over.

**MIRAI:** “Why?”

**TRAVELLER:** “You've inspected every roof we've passed.”

Mirai glances upward again before she can stop herself.

The traveller laughs.

**TRAVELLER:** “There. You did it again.”

**MIRAI:** “I'm supposed to know what's around us.”

**TRAVELLER:** “And what's around us?”

Mirai looks ahead.

A dog asleep beneath a bench.

Two women arguing over the price of peaches.

A boy chasing a hoop down the road.

**MIRAI:** “Konoha.”

**TRAVELLER:** “Terrifying.”

Mirai doesn't answer.

A few more steps pass.

The traveller adjusts the strap of his bag.

**TRAVELLER:** “You can talk, you know.”

Mirai looks at him.

**TRAVELLER:** “I won't report you.”

## TALK TO HIM

Mirai tries to think of something that doesn't sound like a mission question.

That takes longer than it should.

**MIRAI:** “Have you been here before?”

**TRAVELLER:** “Konoha?”

He looks around.

Nearly walks into a hanging shop sign.

Mirai catches his sleeve and pulls him half a step sideways.

The sign swings past his head.

He looks at it.

Then at her.

**TRAVELLER:** “…First time.”

Mirai lets go of his sleeve.

**MIRAI:** “I guessed.”

**TRAVELLER:** “You could've kept that to yourself.”

They keep walking.

**TRAVELLER:** “My sister moved here a few years ago. East Market.”

Mirai nods.

**TRAVELLER:** “She told me not to try finding her house.”

**MIRAI:** “Why?”

He gives her a look.

Mirai remembers the sign.

**MIRAI:** “Right.”

**TRAVELLER:** “Apparently I have no sense of direction.”

**MIRAI:** “Apparently?”

**TRAVELLER:** “I'm choosing not to accept the accusation.”

The road bends beneath a line of trees.

For a while they talk about nothing important.

The price of food in Konoha.

How every village seems to believe its own streets make perfect sense.

His sister's habit of sending letters that start with three pages of complaints and end with *I'm fine*.

Then—

**TRAVELLER:** “She's making tea when we get there.”

The enthusiasm disappears from his voice.

Mirai hears it.

**MIRAI:** “Bad tea?”

**TRAVELLER:** “Plum.”

**MIRAI:** “That's bad?”

He looks genuinely offended.

**TRAVELLER:** “It's fruit pretending it belongs in tea.”

Mirai blinks.
