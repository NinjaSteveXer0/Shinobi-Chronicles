## Current expression candidate

### SCENE 1 — THE ASSIGNMENT

Mirai has prepared for the assignment more seriously than the assignment appears to deserve.

The Academy instructor hands her the route slip.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "Checkpoint Three. One civilian. Keep them with you and get them there safely."

Mirai reads the slip once.

Then again.

**MIRAI:** "Expected interference?"

The instructor looks at her.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "It's an Academy escort."

**MIRAI:** "That doesn't answer the question."

The civilian waiting beside him covers a smile.

The instructor notices that too.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "No expected interference."

Mirai folds the route slip.

**MIRAI:** "Known route obstructions?"

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "Mirai."

She stops.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** "Walk them to the checkpoint."

A beat.

**MIRAI:** "Right."

The civilian picks up their travel bag.

**CIVILIAN:** "Should I be worried that you had follow-up questions?"

**MIRAI:** "No."

**CIVILIAN:** "You answered that very fast."

Mirai looks at them.

The civilian is smiling.

Mirai realises, half a second late, that they are teasing her.

**MIRAI:** "We're leaving."

**CIVILIAN:** "Very reassuring."

They start down the road.

**OBJECTIVE: ESCORT THE CIVILIAN TO CHECKPOINT THREE.**

---

### SCENE 2 — THE WALK

For the first few streets, Mirai treats the escort exactly like an escort.

The civilian notices.

Of course they do.

**CIVILIAN:** "Do you always look at rooftops this much?"

Mirai keeps walking.

**MIRAI:** "Not always."

**CIVILIAN:** "Only when you're expecting assassins?"

Mirai glances sideways.

**MIRAI:** "I'm not expecting assassins."

**CIVILIAN:** "Good. I left my anti-assassin luggage at home."

Mirai looks at the travel bag.

Then back at the civilian.

**MIRAI:** "That's not a thing."

**CIVILIAN:** "You checked."

Mirai faces forward again.

The civilian looks pleased with themselves.

#### CHOICE

**TALK WITH THEM**

**KEEP THE ESCORT PROFESSIONAL**

#### IF MIRAI TALKS

Mirai lasts another half street before deciding silence is somehow becoming more awkward than conversation.

**MIRAI:** "Is this really your first time in Konoha?"

The civilian looks over.

**CIVILIAN:** "Was the staring that obvious?"

**MIRAI:** "You nearly walked into a sign."

**CIVILIAN:** "I was reading it."

**MIRAI:** "From underneath?"

The civilian laughs.

Mirai's mouth twitches before she can stop it.

**CIVILIAN:** "Yes. First time."

They adjust the strap of their bag.

**CIVILIAN:** "My sister lives near the East Market. She told me to meet her at the checkpoint instead of trying to find her house."

**MIRAI:** "Why?"

**CIVILIAN:** "She says I'd get lost."

Mirai glances at them.

**CIVILIAN:** "Don't agree that quickly."

**MIRAI:** "I didn't say anything."

**CIVILIAN:** "You made the face."

Mirai almost asks what face.

Decides she does not want the answer.

**MIRAI:** "So she's meeting you there."

**CIVILIAN:** "Yeah. Then she's making tea."

A visible lack of enthusiasm enters their voice.

Mirai catches it.

**MIRAI:** "You don't like tea?"

**CIVILIAN:** "I like tea."

**MIRAI:** "That sounded convincing."

**CIVILIAN:** "I don't like *plum* tea. My sister does."

**MIRAI:** "Then why drink it?"

The civilian looks genuinely surprised by the question.

**CIVILIAN:** "Because I like my sister more than I dislike her tea."

Mirai thinks about that.

**MIRAI:** "That's a bad system."

**CIVILIAN:** "Families have those."

The conversation moves on.

Nothing is labelled important.

Mirai simply knows more about the person walking beside her because she chose to talk to them.

#### IF MIRAI KEEPS IT PROFESSIONAL

Mirai lets the conversation die naturally.

Not because she dislikes the civilian.

Because every time she starts paying attention to a story, part of her attention stops being on the escort.

The civilian tries once more.

**CIVILIAN:** "Do I get points if I make this easier for you?"

**MIRAI:** "No."

**CIVILIAN:** "What if I walk very slowly?"

Mirai looks at them.

**MIRAI:** "Please don't."

**CIVILIAN:** "There. That's practically conversation."

Mirai gives up on answering.

Instead, she settles into the work.

Their pace.

How far the civilian drifts when the street gets crowded.

Which shoulder carries the bag.

The ordinary chakra presence close beside her whenever she checks the space around them.

She is not suspicious of them.

She is establishing the person she is responsible for because that is how Mirai has decided to do the assignment.

### SCENE 3 — THE GAP

The covered market is busier than it looked from the approach.

A delivery cart is stuck crosswise in the passage while two workers argue about whose fault that is.

People squeeze around both ends.

Mirai slows.

**MIRAI:** "Stay close."

**CIVILIAN:** "Wasn't planning an escape."

**MIRAI:** "That's not what I meant."

**CIVILIAN:** "I know."

A porter swings a long crate around without looking.

Mirai steps sideways to avoid it.

The civilian moves around the opposite end of the cart.

For a few seconds there is wood, canvas and moving bodies between them.

Mirai reaches the other side first.

The civilian isn't there.

She turns.

Then they emerge from behind a hanging market screen, travel bag still over one shoulder.

**CIVILIAN:** "You walk fast."

**MIRAI:** "You went around the other side."

**CIVILIAN:** "There were fewer elbows."

Mirai looks once toward the passage behind them.

Nothing unusual presents itself.

**MIRAI:** "Stay beside me."

The civilian steps closer.

**CIVILIAN:** "Yes, captain."

Mirai starts walking again.

The escort continues.

Whether Mirai treats the short break in observation as meaningful later depends on what else actually happens.

### SCENE 4 — THE OTHER ROUTE

A few streets later, the civilian slows at a junction.

**CIVILIAN:** "Take the left."

Mirai checks the route slip.

**MIRAI:** "Checkpoint Three is straight."

**CIVILIAN:** "Left cuts around the next block. It's quicker."

The side lane is narrower.

Less traffic.

Nothing about it is automatically dangerous.

Nothing about the suggestion is automatically deception.

If Mirai talked earlier, she also knows this is supposedly the civilian's first visit to Konoha and that their sister expected them to get lost trying to navigate alone.

If Mirai did not have that conversation, she does not possess that contradiction.

The player decides what Mirai makes of the moment.

#### CHOICE

**TAKE THE SHORTCUT**

**STAY ON THE ASSIGNED ROUTE**

**ASK HOW THEY KNOW IT**

**DON'T REACT — REMEMBER IT**

#### TAKE THE SHORTCUT

Mirai folds the route slip and turns left.

**MIRAI:** "Fine. You lead until it rejoins."

The civilian raises their eyebrows.

**CIVILIAN:** "Trusting."

**MIRAI:** "No. If you're wrong, I want to know which turn did it."

That earns a real laugh.

The lane narrows between storehouses.

The crowd noise falls away behind them.

The civilian glances back.

**CIVILIAN:** "Still think I'm getting you lost?"

**MIRAI:** "I haven't decided yet."

The route remains legitimate Story space.

If the authored encounter is present, the alternate route may lead to a PL Battle.

That Battle is not automatic proof of substitution and is not a punishment for taking the route.

Battle authority must later define:

If no encounter is active, or after its resolved return, the escort continues.

#### STAY ON THE ASSIGNED ROUTE

Mirai does not even unfold the route slip again.

**MIRAI:** "We're staying straight."

The civilian shrugs.

**CIVILIAN:** "You're the one with the assignment."

**MIRAI:** "Exactly."

**CIVILIAN:** "You really like that sentence."

Mirai looks at them.

**MIRAI:** "Keep walking."

The civilian does.

#### ASK HOW THEY KNOW IT

Mirai looks toward the side lane.

Then at the civilian.

**MIRAI:** "How do you know?"

The civilian answers without much delay.

**CIVILIAN:** "My sister sent me a map before I came."

That is possible.

If Mirai has the earlier conversation, it sits awkwardly beside the civilian's previous confidence that they would get lost and were meeting their sister at the checkpoint for exactly that reason.

But possible is still possible.

Mirai does not receive an automatic conclusion.

#### DON'T REACT — REMEMBER IT

Mirai continues straight.

**MIRAI:** "This way."

The civilian follows.

Mirai does not question them.

Does not accuse them.

Does not decide the suggestion meant anything.

She simply does not forget that it happened.

### SCENE 5 — WHAT DOES MIRAI CHECK?

The next stretch of road is uneventful.

That matters.

Nobody attacks.

The civilian does not suddenly behave like a villain.

There is no narrator announcing that Mirai feels something wrong.

If the player wants Mirai to continue the escort exactly as it stands, she can.

If the player has a legitimate reason to inspect further, she can choose to do that too.

Available verification methods depend on the history Mirai actually created.

#### CONDITIONAL CHOICE

**TEST THEIR STORY**
— available only when Mirai learned relevant personal information earlier.

**COMPARE THEIR CHAKRA**
— available only when Mirai established a meaningful pre-separation operational/chakra reference.

**WATCH THEM WITHOUT SAYING ANYTHING**
— does not manufacture confirmation.

**KEEP ESCORTING**
— no verification attempt.

#### KEEP ESCORTING

Mirai keeps the civilian beside her and continues toward Checkpoint Three.

That is a legitimate decision.

The job she was explicitly given was an escort.

The player is allowed to decide that the evidence does not justify turning it into something else.

#### WATCH WITHOUT SAYING ANYTHING

Mirai says nothing.

She gives the civilian room to behave normally and pays closer attention to what they actually do.

This may produce later observable evidence if something occurs.

It does not itself establish deception.

#### COMPARE THEIR CHAKRA

Mirai deliberately checks the presence beside her against the reference she established before the market.

This time she is not casually monitoring distance.

She is comparing.

The result does not match cleanly.

Not enough for Mirai to know *why*.

Enough for one narrower fact:

the chakra presence she is observing now is not the same reference she consciously established earlier.

Mirai keeps walking.

The civilian does not know what she has just checked unless the player chooses to tell them.

#### TEST THEIR STORY

Mirai does not announce that she is testing them.

She waits until the civilian has started looking bored again.

**MIRAI:** "Your sister's meeting you at the checkpoint, right?"

**CIVILIAN:** "Yeah."

**MIRAI:** "What were you two doing after?"

The civilian thinks for a moment.

**CIVILIAN:** "Lunch, I think."

Mirai keeps her face still.

Lunch.

That is not what the original civilian told her.

She does **not** correct them.

She does not say `plum tea`.

She does not explain what they got wrong.

The person beside her only knows that Mirai asked an ordinary follow-up question.

Now the decision belongs to the player.

### SCENE 6 — WHAT DOES MIRAI DO WITH THE ANSWER?

#### CHOICE

**ASK ANOTHER QUESTION**

**CHALLENGE THE CONTRADICTION**

**USE ANOTHER AVAILABLE CHECK**

**LET IT GO AND CONTINUE**

#### ASK ANOTHER QUESTION

Mirai gives the civilian several steps of silence.

Then:

**MIRAI:** "Which market does your sister live near?"

The civilian looks at her.

**CIVILIAN:** "North Market."

Mirai had been told East Market.

Another mismatch.

Again, Mirai does not supply the correct answer.

The civilian cannot learn the original person's private conversation simply by failing Mirai's test.

The player may press again, change methods, confront, or continue.

#### CHALLENGE THE CONTRADICTION

Mirai looks directly at them now.

**MIRAI:** "That's not what you told me earlier."

The civilian's expression tightens for a fraction of a second.

Then relaxes.

**CIVILIAN:** "What part?"

Mirai does not answer for them.

**MIRAI:** "You tell me."

The civilian gives a small, irritated laugh.

**CIVILIAN:** "I said lunch? Fine. Maybe I mixed it up. I've been travelling all morning."

Mirai lets that sit.

**CIVILIAN:** "Are you going to stop the mission because I forgot what my sister planned?"

There is enough edge in the question now that the conversation has changed.

But the player still decides what that means.

Mirai may:
- press;
- keep the contradiction private;
- use chakra evidence if she legitimately has it;
- continue to the checkpoint;
- or, if the route state allows, alter the route/position for safety.

#### USE ANOTHER AVAILABLE CHECK

If Mirai has the chakra baseline, she can compare it now without explaining why she is doing so.

If she has another conversational detail, she can test that instead.

Evidence channels remain separate.

They become persuasive because the player connects them, not because Writing declares a threshold has been crossed.

#### LET IT GO AND CONTINUE

Mirai exhales through her nose.

**MIRAI:** "Keep moving."

The civilian watches her for a second.

**CIVILIAN:** "That's it?"

**MIRAI:** "That's it."

Whether Mirai believes the explanation, decides the discrepancy is not enough, or simply chooses to finish the explicit escort is Chronicle interpretation left to the player's actual later decisions.
