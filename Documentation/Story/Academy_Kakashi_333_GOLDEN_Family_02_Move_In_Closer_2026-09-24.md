# SCENE 03B — MOVE IN CLOSER

The two men are talking now.

Kakashi can see that much.

Not hear it.

He leaves the roofline.

Drops into the alley.

Closer.

A lantern throws a pale strip of light across the wet stone ahead.

Kakashi keeps to the wall and lets it stop short of his sandals.

Another doorway.

Then another.

The Sakura tree fills more of the alley mouth.

So do the voices.

---

# GET CLOSER — SUCCESS

Neither man looks toward him.

Kakashi settles near the end of the alley.

Close enough.

ANBU Marked Target keeps one hand over the package beneath his clothing.

Package Smuggler waits opposite him.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “Once you have it, I'm done.”

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “That's the arrangement.”

ANBU Marked Target studies him.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “That's not what I asked.”

Package Smuggler sighs through his nose.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Yes. You hand it over. You leave.”

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “And if I was followed?”

Package Smuggler's eyes move briefly along the street.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Then you don't hand me anything.”

The target's fingers tighten against the package.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “I keep carrying it.”

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Until the street's clean.”

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “That wasn't the deal.”

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Neither was bringing somebody with you.”

The target's stare hardens.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “I didn't.”

Package Smuggler lifts one shoulder.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Then we're wasting time.”

He holds out his hand.

ANBU Marked Target doesn't move yet.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “Where does it go after you?”

Package Smuggler looks at the waiting space between them.

Then back at him.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Not with you.”

A beat.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “You don't know.”

For the first time, Package Smuggler smiles.

Not much.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Now who's asking too many questions?”

ANBU Marked Target's jaw tightens.

The waiting hand stays open.

The package begins to move.

and runs.

# SUCCESS — INTERRUPT THE HANDOFF

ANBU Marked Target starts to extend the package.

Kakashi moves.

Package Smuggler sees him break from the alley.

His waiting hand closes.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Hold it.”

ANBU Marked Target pulls the package back.

Kakashi lands between them.

Package Smuggler looks him over.

The surprise lasts just long enough for Kakashi to notice it.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “You're the shadow?”

Kakashi's eye flicks to the package.

**KAKASHI:** “Don't hand it over.”

ANBU Marked Target gives a short laugh.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “That's your plan?”

**KAKASHI:** “It's the part you need.”

The target shifts his footing.

Package Smuggler steps away from the wall.

The quiet meeting is over.

## PICKPOCKET SUCCESS

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Where is it?”

ANBU Marked Target checks beneath his clothing.

His expression changes.

By the time he looks toward the alley, Kakashi is gone.

ANBU Marked Target catches movement at his side.

His hand clamps over Kakashi's wrist.

For one instant, all three of them are too close.

Package Smuggler looks down at Kakashi's hand.

Then at Kakashi.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Well.”

Kakashi twists free.

ANBU Marked Target backs away with the package still against him.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “You were listening.”

Kakashi doesn't answer.

Package Smuggler's hand drops toward his weapon.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “He can explain after.”

# GET CLOSER — FAILURE

Kakashi moves down the alley.

One doorway.

Another.

The voices sharpen.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “Once I hand it over, I'm done.”

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “That's the idea.”

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “And if I was followed?”

Package Smuggler's attention drops.

Rainwater has collected along the edge of the street.

Lantern light trembles across it.

So does Kakashi's reflection.

Package Smuggler's expression tightens.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Don't turn around.”

ANBU Marked Target goes still.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “What?”

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Keep it.”

The target's hand presses against the package.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “You said I was done.”

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Not anymore.”

He keeps his eyes on the alley.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Main street. Move.”

ANBU Marked Target doesn't argue.

He runs.

Package Smuggler watches him clear the corner.

Then starts toward the alley.

Slowly.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “You can come out.”

Kakashi looks past him.

The package is getting farther away.

# FAILURE — CHASE THE PACKAGE

Kakashi moves before Package Smuggler reaches the alley.

The man catches sight of him at the edge of the street.

His eyes flick over Kakashi.

A beat of surprise.

Then Kakashi is already past him.

Package Smuggler reaches out.

Misses.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Don't stop!”

Ahead, ANBU Marked Target looks back.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “You said I was done!”

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Not while you're carrying it!”

The target swears and runs harder.

Kakashi follows.

The Sakura tree fills the square ahead.

ANBU Marked Target cuts across the open stone.

Kakashi closes.

The man looks back again.

This time he sees how much distance has disappeared.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “What do you want?”

**KAKASHI:** “The package.”

A breathless laugh.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “Of course.”

He changes direction around the Sakura tree.

Kakashi doesn't follow the man.

He watches where the man is going.

Then cuts the other way.

---

## PURSUIT SUCCESS

Footsteps slap against wet stone on the far side of the trunk.

Kakashi counts them.

Changes direction.

ANBU Marked Target comes around the tree—

and stops.

A small ninken is sitting in the street ahead.

The target shifts left.

The ninken does too.

Right.

Matched again.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “Seriously?”

A low growl answers him.

Kakashi arrives behind.

The ninken looks past the target at him.

**PAKKUN:** “This yours?”

Kakashi looks from the dog to the man.

**KAKASHI:** “Apparently.”

Pakkun's eyes drop to the hand covering the package.

Then back up.

Kakashi steps closer.

**KAKASHI:** “Thanks.”

Pakkun's ear flicks.

ANBU Marked Target turns enough to keep both of them in view.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “You don't even know him.”

Kakashi stops outside striking distance.

**KAKASHI:** “He's still helping.”

Pakkun looks up at Kakashi.

**PAKKUN:** “For now.”

The target exhales.

His fingers tighten over the package.

## PURSUIT FAILURE

Kakashi cuts around the far side of the Sakura tree.

Empty stone.

A shadow clears the edge of the square.

Kakashi changes direction.

Too late.

Two late-night pedestrians step into the street between them.

ANBU Marked Target does not slow.

By the time Kakashi reaches the next corner, he is gone.

Three streets.

No movement.

Kakashi listens.

Nothing he can separate from the rest of Konoha.

He waits one more second.

Then turns back.

---

# FAILURE — CONFRONT THE RECEIVER

ANBU Marked Target disappears toward the main street.

Kakashi watches the package go with him.

Then steps out of the alley.

Package Smuggler slows.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Wrong one.”

**KAKASHI:** “You were waiting for it.”

The man's eyes flick toward the road behind Kakashi.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Was.”

**KAKASHI:** “Where was it going?”

Package Smuggler looks back at him.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “You let the person carrying it run away and stayed to ask me?”

**KAKASHI:** “You stayed too.”

That wipes the smile off his face.

His hand moves toward his weapon.

Kakashi shifts his footing.

# FAILURE — CUT THEM OFF

Kakashi leaves the alley another way.

Not after Package Smuggler.

Not directly behind ANBU Marked Target.

Across them.

He clears the wall beside the Sakura tree and lands ahead of the route.

ANBU Marked Target sees him first.

Package Smuggler sees what happened a heartbeat later.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “Don't let him get in front of you!”

Too late.

ANBU Marked Target turns toward the open street—

and stops.

A small ninken is sitting in it.

The target stares down at him.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET:** “Oh, come on.”

The ninken looks past him.

At Kakashi.

**PAKKUN:** “This yours?”

Kakashi lands on the other side of the choke point.

**KAKASHI:** “Apparently.”

Package Smuggler reaches them.

He looks from Kakashi to the ninken.

**PACKAGE SMUGGLER:** “You brought a dog?”

Pakkun looks at him.

**PAKKUN:** “No.”

Kakashi almost looks over.

Almost.

ANBU Marked Target's hand closes over the package.

Package Smuggler reaches for his weapon.

Kakashi sets his feet.
