# 4. Arc 2 Mission 2 — THE LEAK

## 4.1 Universal invariant

> **An unresolved Arc-1 pressure point is deliberately activated through information in order to provoke a predictable Konoha response.**

This pressure point must come from legitimate Chronicle history. The exact pressure carrier may differ between protagonists.

## 4.2 Mission proof

The Mission-2 Arc proof is:

> **The protagonist discovers that someone is not merely leaking information; someone is shaping Konoha’s response and exploiting the movement that response creates.**

This is the universal mission function.

Mission 2 must still perform this function if the developer Menma/Echo carrier is replaced by another legitimate Chronicle-earned carrier.

Authoring test:

> **If the Menma/Echo carrier were swapped for another legitimate Chronicle-earned carrier, would Mission 2 still prove that hostile actors can shape and exploit Konoha’s response?**

If the answer is no, the Chronicle-specific thread has taken over the campaign spine and must be re-anchored.

## 4.3 Universal structural ingredients

Where causally legitimate in the current Chronicle, preserve the approved Mission-2 grammar:

- multiple imperfect or competing information streams;
- forged, compromised, partial or selectively manipulated legitimate-looking authority;
- autonomous Konoha teams/participants moving from their own bounded Knowledge, duties and intent;
- one legitimate Chronicle-earned protagonist pressure carrier;
- adversarial observation, prediction or exploitation of Konoha’s response;
- material ninja tradecraft rather than abstract exposition alone, including where appropriate Genjutsu/perception manipulation, chakra masking, suppression tags, false trails, evidence destruction and ambush positioning;
- real PL Battle pressure where authored;
- bounded witness/stranger information rather than omniscient exposition;
- aftermath evidence that supports the conclusion that Konoha’s response was being observed/modelled/exploited;
- a next causal lead that advances the wider political-information campaign rather than making the protagonist-specific carrier the universal Arc destination.

The Menma formulation of the hostile strategy is:

> **“Don’t attack Menma. Move the world around Menma until he has nowhere safe to stand.”**

That sentence describes one Chronicle-specific exploitation strategy. It is not the universal subject of Arc 2.

## 4.4 Menma/Echo non-universal boundary

For the developer Menma Chronicle, Echo/former-host/survival material may remain because it is causally earned.

It must not:

- define the universal Mission-2 premise;
- define the universal Arc-2 subject;
- become the mandatory Arc-2 destination;
- imply that Echo experimentation explains every protagonist’s pressure;
- force non-Echo protagonists into substitute Echo plots;
- erase the information-war proof that Konoha’s **response itself** is being shaped and exploited.
