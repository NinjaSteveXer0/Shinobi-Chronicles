# PLAYER-FACING STORY

# ACADEMY WASABI IZUNO
## The Pursuit

---

# SCENE 1 — HEAD START

The target is already gone.

Wasabi stares down the empty street.

Then at the instructor.

**WASABI:** “You let them leave?”

The instructor finishes tying the route marker around his wrist.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “That is generally how a pursuit works.”

Wasabi looks back toward the street.

**WASABI:** “How long?”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Long enough.”

**WASABI:** “That's not a time.”

A couple of the other Academy students laugh.

Wasabi ignores them.

Mostly.

The instructor points toward the village.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “The target reaches extraction, you're done.”

Wasabi rolls one shoulder.

**WASABI:** “And when I catch them?”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Then they're done.”

That answer she likes.

The instructor drops his hand.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Go.”

Wasabi is already moving.

Someone behind her shouts—

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “He didn't mean before he finished—”

Too late.

---

# SCENE 2 — THE TRAIL

Wasabi hits the first roof at speed.

Tile.

Wall.

Awning.

Street.

She barely loses momentum.

Ahead, a figure disappears around the far corner.

There.

Wasabi grins.

Then reaches the corner and finds three different ways the target could have gone.

One trail is obvious.

Fresh marks through dust lead hard to the right.

A knocked basket rocks beside them.

Easy.

Almost too easy.

Another student lands behind Wasabi.

Then another.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Which way?”

Wasabi crouches for half a second.

Dust.

Roof edge.

Drain channel.

The street is giving her several answers.

The target is getting farther away while she looks at them.

### CHOICE

**TAKE THE OBVIOUS TRAIL**

**LOOK FOR SOMETHING BETTER**

**WORK WITH THE OTHERS**

**FORGET THE TRAIL — WHERE ARE THEY GOING?**

---

## TAKE THE OBVIOUS TRAIL

Wasabi doesn't waste another second.

**WASABI:** “Right.”

She launches down the street.

One of the other students calls after her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “You sure?”

Wasabi doesn't turn.

**WASABI:** “Catch up and ask me!”

The trail stays strong.

Scuffed dirt.

Displaced crates.

A footprint on a low wall.

Wasabi takes the wall instead of going around it.

Fast.

Clear.

Exactly what she wanted.

Until the tracks split again.

And the freshest set suddenly ends beside an empty alley.

Wasabi stops.

Looks at the wall.

Looks at the ground.

Back at the wall.

**WASABI:** “…Oh, you've got to be kidding me.”

---

## LOOK FOR SOMETHING BETTER

Wasabi starts right—

stops herself.

The obvious tracks are deep.

Too deep.

Someone running for extraction doesn't normally stamp the ground like they're signing their name.

Wasabi turns back.

One of the others nearly runs into her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “What are you doing?”

Wasabi points toward the tracks.

**WASABI:** “Those are rubbish.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “They look pretty real.”

**WASABI:** “Yeah. That's the problem.”

She drops from the street to the drainage edge.

A smear of mud marks the stone.

Higher up, one roof tile has shifted sideways.

Wasabi looks toward it.

Then farther ahead.

**WASABI:** “There.”

She takes off.

The other student looks from her to the obvious trail.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Wasabi!”

Already gone.

---

## WORK WITH THE OTHERS

Wasabi turns on the two students behind her.

Both look surprised.

**WASABI:** “You. Roofs.”

She points to the second.

**WASABI:** “Check the street.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “And you?”

**WASABI:** “I'm doing both.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “That's not how—”

Wasabi is already halfway up the wall.

**WASABI:** “Then hurry!”

For the next block, information starts coming from three directions.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Nothing north!”

**ACADEMY STUDENT 2:** “Marks near the canal!”

Wasabi stops on the roof ridge.

**WASABI:** “How fresh?”

**ACADEMY STUDENT 2:** “How am I supposed to know?”

Wasabi groans.

**WASABI:** “Why did I pick you?”

**ACADEMY STUDENT 2:** “You didn't pick me!”

Fair.

Wasabi jumps down.

All three move.

Not as fast as Wasabi alone.

But now she knows more than one street.

---

## FORGET THE TRAIL — WHERE ARE THEY GOING?

Wasabi looks once at the footprints.

Then away.

One of the other students notices.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “You're not following that?”

**WASABI:** “No.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Why?”

Wasabi looks toward the village beyond the roofs.

The target isn't trying to vanish forever.

They're trying to reach extraction.

Which means every route eventually has to get them somewhere useful.

**WASABI:** “Because I'm tired of running behind them.”

She changes direction.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “That's the wrong way!”

Wasabi jumps onto the wall.

**WASABI:** “We'll find out!”

And disappears over it.

---

# SCENE 3 — THE SPLIT

The pursuit changes shape farther into the village.

The clean streets give way to longer routes between walls, rooftops and the river.

Wasabi catches sight of movement ahead.

Maybe the target.

Maybe not.

Then the route opens.

River to one side.

A stronger trail farther inland.

Shouting from another street.

And beyond all of it, the line Wasabi has been building in her head toward extraction.

She has time for one decision.

Not two.

### CHOICE

**TAKE THE RIVER**

**FOLLOW THE STRONGER TRAIL**

**CHECK THE SHOUTING**

**CUT FOR THE INTERCEPT**

---

# SCENE 4A — THE RIVER

Wasabi drops down toward the river path.

It costs her height immediately.

She hates that.

The route bends wider than the streets above, but once she's on it there are fewer places for the target to disappear.

Open water on one side.

Wall on the other.

Nothing fancy.

Wasabi runs.

And runs.

And keeps running.

The extraction marker eventually appears ahead.

So does the target.

Wasabi's grin returns.

**WASABI:** “Got you.”

The target looks back.

Sees her.

Runs harder.

**WASABI:** “Oh, no you don't.”

The final stretch becomes exactly the race Wasabi wanted from the beginning.

No clues.

No guessing.

Just distance.

The target reaches for the extraction marker.

Wasabi launches forward.

Her fingers miss the back of their shirt by less than a handspan.

The target crosses.

The proctor drops his arm between them.

**PROCTOR:** “Extraction.”

Wasabi skids to a stop.

Stares at the target.

Then at the line.

Then at the target again.

**WASABI:** “That counts?”

The target is bent over, trying to breathe.

They manage a thumbs-up.

Wasabi looks personally offended by the thumb.

---

# SCENE 4B — THE STRONGER TRAIL

The new trail is better than the first one.

Much better.

Fresh marks.

Broken twig.

Scuffed wall.

Even a scrap caught against a fence.

Wasabi follows it faster with every sign.

Then the trail turns into a narrow yard.

And stops.

No target.

No exit.

Nothing.

Wasabi stands in the middle of it.

Breathing hard.

The scrap on the fence moves in the breeze.

She pulls it free.

Looks at it.

Then looks back the way she came.

**WASABI:** “Seriously?”

A faint scrape marks the wall behind her.

Not toward the yard.

Away from it.

Wasabi closes her eyes for one second.

Then points the scrap accusingly at nobody.

**WASABI:** “Fine.”

She drops it.

**WASABI:** “Good one.”

Then she runs back out.

Much angrier.

And considerably more careful.

---

# SCENE 4C — THE SHOUTING

Wasabi takes three steps toward extraction.

Stops.

The shout comes again.

Closer than she thought.

She changes direction.

Around the next corner, an Academy student is backed against a wall.

A Rogue Genin stands between them and the street.

Older.

Bigger.

The student sees Wasabi.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Wasabi!”

The Rogue Genin looks over.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Keep running.”

Wasabi's eyes flick toward the road beyond him.

The target is still out there.

The extraction clock is still moving.

The Rogue Genin smiles.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “That's what you're doing, isn't it?”

### CHOICE

**STEP IN**

**CALL FOR HELP**

**KEEP PURSUING**

---

## STEP IN

Wasabi walks toward him.

The Academy student blinks.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Wasabi—”

**WASABI:** “Move.”

The Rogue Genin laughs.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “You've got somewhere to be.”

Wasabi keeps coming.

**WASABI:** “So do you.”

His smile disappears.

Wasabi plants herself between him and the other student.

### PL BATTLE SEAM — ROGUE GENIN

If the confrontation legally escalates, this calls the authorised Rogue Genin Battle occurrence.

After resolution, Wasabi returns to the pursuit if the route still permits it.

---

## CALL FOR HELP

Wasabi backs toward the corner without taking her eyes off him.

Then she shouts.

Not politely.

**WASABI:** “INSTRUCTOR!”

The Rogue Genin's head snaps toward the sound.

The Academy student uses the moment to slip away from the wall.

Wasabi catches their sleeve.

**WASABI:** “Move.”

They move.

Fast.

Wasabi doesn't stay to watch who arrives.

She's already turning back toward the pursuit.

---

## KEEP PURSUING

Wasabi stops at the corner.

The Academy student sees her.

For one second, so does the Rogue Genin.

Then Wasabi looks toward extraction.

Her jaw tightens.

She runs.

Behind her, somebody shouts her name.

Wasabi doesn't look back.

---

# SCENE 4D — THE INTERCEPT

Wasabi abandons the pursuit line completely.

The target can keep their head start.

Fine.

Let them.

She crosses two streets.

Cuts behind a row of shops.

Takes a wall instead of the stairs.

Then stops.

Actually stops.

Wasabi looks down the empty lane ahead.

Nothing.

She looks back.

Still nothing.

Her foot starts tapping.

Once.

Twice.

Three times.

**WASABI:** “Come on…”

Nothing.

Wasabi's expression darkens.

**WASABI:** “If I guessed wrong—”

Footsteps.

She freezes.

A figure appears at the far end of the lane.

The target.

Their expression changes the instant they see her.

Wasabi's doesn't.

Except for the grin.

**WASABI:** “Hi.”

The target turns.

Wasabi moves.

This time they are the one running from behind.

---

# SCENE 5 — THE FINISH

## DIRECT CATCH

The target changes direction twice.

Wasabi stays with them.

Third turn.

Roof.

Drop.

Street.

The target lands badly.

Wasabi doesn't.

Her hand catches the back of their shirt.

Both nearly go down.

Wasabi plants one foot and holds on.

**TARGET:** “Hey!”

**WASABI:** “Caught you.”

**TARGET:** “You're choking me!”

Wasabi releases the shirt.

**WASABI:** “Still caught you.”

The proctor arriving behind them looks at both.

**PROCTOR:** “Technically.”

Wasabi points at him.

**WASABI:** “Don't ruin this.”

---

## INTERCEPT BEFORE EXTRACTION

The target gets one clean step before Wasabi closes the distance.

She cuts them off.

They try the other side.

Wasabi is already there.

**TARGET:** “Move.”

**WASABI:** “No.”

They look toward extraction.

Then at Wasabi.

Then toward the roofs.

Wasabi follows their eyes.

**WASABI:** “Try it.”

They don't.

Wasabi's grin widens.

---

## ARRIVE JUST AFTER

Wasabi reaches extraction at full speed.

The target is already across.

By seconds.

Maybe less.

The instructor checks the time.

Wasabi bends over, hands on her knees.

Looks up.

**WASABI:** “How much?”

The instructor tells her.

Wasabi straightens immediately.

**WASABI:** “That's nothing.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “It was enough.”

Wasabi stares at him.

Then at the target.

**WASABI:** “Again.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “No.”

**WASABI:** “Why?”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Because the exercise is over.”

Wasabi looks deeply unconvinced by this technicality.

---

## FALSE TRAIL DISCOVERED

Wasabi arrives late.

Not disastrously late.

Late enough.

The target is already waiting.

Wasabi walks the final few steps instead of running them.

The instructor looks at the dirt on her clothes.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “What happened?”

Wasabi hands him the scrap from the false trail.

**WASABI:** “They cheated.”

The target looks offended.

**TARGET:** “It was a pursuit exercise.”

Wasabi turns.

**WASABI:** “You planted a trail.”

**TARGET:** “You followed it.”

Wasabi opens her mouth.

Stops.

The target smiles.

Wasabi points at them.

**WASABI:** “I don't like you.”

---

## SECONDARY OCCURRENCE COSTS THE PURSUIT

By the time Wasabi reaches extraction, the target is already there.

Depending on what happened with the Rogue Genin, Wasabi may arrive dirty, bruised, furious, relieved—

or simply late.

The target looks toward the clock.

Then at her.

Wasabi sees the look.

**WASABI:** “Don't.”

The target wisely doesn't.

---

# SCENE 6 — WHAT THE INSTRUCTOR SAW

The instructor doesn't gather everyone into a lecture.

He has been marking routes as students return.

Who arrived.

Who didn't.

Who brought information back.

Who came back with somebody else.

Wasabi waits until he reaches her.

Not patiently.

## IF WASABI CAUGHT THE TARGET QUICKLY

The instructor looks at the recorded time.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Fast.”

Wasabi's face says *obviously*.

He sees that.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “You want the number?”

Now she's interested.

He tells her.

Wasabi grins.

**WASABI:** “Who beat it?”

The instructor looks farther down the page.

Wasabi leans toward it.

He closes the book.

**WASABI:** “Hey!”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “You completed your exercise.”

**WASABI:** “That wasn't my question.”

## IF WASABI FOUND THE FALSE TRAIL

The instructor turns the scrap over in his hand.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “How long did you follow it?”

Wasabi answers.

Quietly.

He looks up.

**WASABI:** “I figured it out.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “You did.”

Wasabi waits for the rest.

It doesn't come.

**WASABI:** “You're annoying.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Also not part of the assessment.”

## IF WASABI INTERCEPTED BY PREDICTION

The instructor points toward the route map.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Show me.”

Wasabi traces the line she abandoned.

Then the cut she took instead.

**WASABI:** “They had to come through here.”

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Had to?”

Wasabi thinks.

Then moves her finger slightly.

**WASABI:** “Fine. Probably.”

The instructor looks at her.

Wasabi folds her arms.

**WASABI:** “It worked.”

That earns the smallest hint of a smile.

## IF WASABI COOPERATED

One of the other students arrives while the instructor is speaking to her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “She left us twice.”

Wasabi turns.

**WASABI:** “I came back.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Because you needed something.”

**WASABI:** “Still came back.”

The instructor looks between them.

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “Did the information help?”

Both answer at once.

**WASABI:** “Yes.”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Eventually.”

Wasabi glares.

The student grins.

## IF THE ROGUE GENIN CHANGED THE OUTCOME

The instructor looks at the extraction result.

Then at Wasabi.

If she intervened:

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “You knew the target was still moving.”

**WASABI:** “Yeah.”

If she called for help:

**ACADEMY INSTRUCTOR:** “You handed it off.”

**WASABI:** “Someone else was closer than the target was.”

If she kept pursuing:

The instructor doesn't mention the Rogue Genin first.

Wasabi does.

Or doesn't.

Nothing is forced out of her.

The result stays on the page either way.

---

# SCENE 7 — AFTER

The other students begin dispersing.

The target leaves with the proctor.

Wasabi is still looking at the time board.

Another Academy student passes behind her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “You lost.”

Wasabi turns instantly.

**WASABI:** “To who?”

The student laughs and keeps walking.

**WASABI:** “Hey!”

No answer.

Wasabi looks back at the board.

If she caught the target, she finds her time again.

If she missed them by seconds, she finds that time instead.

If the Rogue Genin cost her the pursuit, the number is worse.

If she followed the false trail, worse again.

Wasabi stares at it.

Then exhales.

### CHOICE

**“Next time I'm trusting the trail.”**

**“Next time I'm trusting what I notice.”**

**“Sometimes the fastest path isn't the obvious one.”**

**“Catching them wasn't the only thing that mattered.”**

---

# ORIGIN CLOSE

Wasabi leaves the practical ground at a jog.

Not because anybody told her to.

Walking suddenly feels too slow.

At the end of the street, she spots one of the students from the exercise.

They see her.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “No.”

Wasabi speeds up.

The other student starts running.

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “Wasabi!”

**WASABI:** “What?”

**ACADEMY STUDENT:** “The exercise is over!”

Wasabi catches them before the next corner.

**WASABI:** “I know.”

She passes them anyway.

And keeps going.
