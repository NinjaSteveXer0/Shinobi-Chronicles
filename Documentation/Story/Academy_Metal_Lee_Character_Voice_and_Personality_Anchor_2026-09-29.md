# Shinobi Chronicles — Academy Metal Lee — Character Voice and Personality Anchor

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Owner:** Writing / Story — Konoha  
**Status:** **BINDING CHARACTER VOICE / PERSONALITY AUTHORITY**  
**Representation:** `academy_metal_lee`  
**Life stage:** Academy-era Metal Lee

## 1. Why this exists

Metal previously had no dedicated durable voice/personality anchor even though the current Writing standard requires one before substantial recurring named-character dialogue.

The previous Academy Origin therefore leaned too heavily on one trait — nervousness while observed — and filled the rest of the dialogue with generic dry banter.

This anchor is binding for the current Academy Origin rewrite and later substantial Academy-era Metal dialogue.

## 2. Canon baseline

Current canon research basis:
- NARUTO Official Site, BORUTO Episode 3, **Metal Lee Goes Wild!**
  - Metal is hard-working;
  - talented at taijutsu;
  - pressure and being watched make him very nervous and cause mistakes.
- NARUTO Official Site, BORUTO Episode 70, **The Other Side of Anxiety**
  - anxiety remains a real part of his character and training problem rather than disappearing after one lesson.
- Secondary reference synthesis:
  - respectful / honourable presentation;
  - strong admiration for Rock Lee / Might Guy-style discipline;
  - hot-blooded dedication;
  - high self-imposed performance pressure;
  - typically more formal/earnest speech than a sarcastic Academy peer.

Do not back-port later confidence as if Academy Metal has already solved the problem.

## 3. Core personality

Metal is:

- earnest;
- disciplined;
- hard-working;
- respectful;
- proud of training done properly;
- physically confident when he is focused on the task;
- socially self-conscious when attention turns onto him;
- eager to prove that his work is real;
- easily embarrassed when performance slips in front of others;
- quick to care when somebody may be hurt;
- not passive;
- not cynical;
- not emotionally detached.

His anxiety is a **contextual performance problem**, not his whole personality.

## 4. Default voice

Metal usually speaks plainly and earnestly.

He may sound slightly formal compared with classmates, especially when:
- accepting a challenge;
- explaining training;
- addressing somebody older or more experienced;
- trying to recover dignity after embarrassment.

Use full forms such as:
- `I am`;
- `I do not`;
- `I can`;

when they sound natural.

Do not turn that tendency into stiff every-line grammar.

Metal is not a miniature textbook.

## 5. Training voice

When Metal is alone or focused on training:

- he becomes calmer;
- his sentences become confident;
- he thinks in repetitions, form, timing and improvement;
- effort excites him rather than intimidating him;
- he may encourage himself in a simple, sincere way;
- he is willing to repeat something until it is right.

He should feel more like Rock Lee's son here:
- committed;
- energetic;
- serious about practice;
- happy to work hard.

Do not write private-training Metal as timid.

## 6. When watched

Observation changes his **performance and social behaviour**, not his underlying ability.

Typical leakage:
- posture becomes too stiff;
- he rushes a motion;
- he corrects too hard;
- he checks who is looking;
- he overthinks an easy answer;
- he becomes more formal because he is trying to sound composed;
- he may say too much while trying to explain a small mistake;
- he may deny being nervous badly;
- he may become embarrassed by praise as well as criticism.

Do not reduce this to:
- repeated `...`;
- one-word replies;
- generic shaking;
- everybody diagnosing him aloud.

The player should often notice the problem before another character names it.

## 7. Under embarrassment

Metal does not default to sarcasm.

He may:
- look away;
- straighten his wraps;
- insist he can do it;
- restart;
- over-explain what went wrong;
- become more formal;
- apologise if another person was affected;
- try to prove the next attempt immediately.

Humour comes from earnestness and awkward recovery, not from quipping.

Example energy:

> “I can do it. I just need everyone to stop looking at me while I do it.”

This is more Metal than a dry comeback.

## 8. Under danger / when protecting someone

Immediate danger can cut through the self-conscious loop.

Metal's attention moves to:
- the person;
- the incoming object;
- the physical solution.

His speech becomes simple:
- “Move!”
- “Are you hurt?”
- “Stay back.”
- “I have it.”

If he succeeds, concern comes before pride.

If he fails or needs help, embarrassment can return **after** he knows the other person is safe.

Do not make protective action a personality/morality score.

## 9. With a sparring partner / inviting Genin

Metal should be respectful but competitive.

He may:
- accept formally;
- ask for a real spar rather than a pity match;
- become nervous once he notices extra observers;
- care how his performance looked;
- want another attempt.

The Genin should not become:
- therapist;
- anxiety coach;
- sarcasm partner;
- narrator of Metal's psychology.

The Genin should behave like a normal older student / young shinobi:
- notices;
- gives space;
- checks safety;
- may offer a simple observation;
- does not explain Metal to Metal.

## 10. Backing out

BACK OUT is legitimate.

Metal may decide:
- not now;
- not in front of everyone;
- he wants to keep training first.

He should not collapse into shame.

The Genin should accept the answer without secretly forcing him into a lesson.

Metal may still be frustrated with himself.

That is different from the Story calling him a coward.

## 11. Humour

Metal's best humour is:
- earnest literalness;
- over-serious commitment to training;
- awkward attempts to look composed;
- an accidental admission after denying nervousness;
- Rock-Lee-like intensity applied to an ordinary Academy situation.

Avoid:
- dry sarcasm;
- snark exchanges;
- every other character teasing him;
- `Sure.` / `Right.` / `Obviously.` as a repeated house cadence.

## 12. Speaker fingerprint

Without speaker names, Metal should still sound identifiable because he combines:

- earnest training language;
- respectful directness;
- self-conscious overcorrection under attention;
- sincere concern for other people;
- determination to repeat/improve;
- little natural cynicism.

## 13. Do not write Metal as

- generic shy boy;
- generic socially anxious teenager;
- mini Rock Lee with no differences;
- dry sarcastic Academy student;
- permanently hesitant;
- therapist-aware about his own anxiety;
- someone whose skill disappears because he is nervous;
- someone who needs another character to explain every emotion;
- a joke about stage fright.

## 14. Current Origin-specific lock

For the current Academy Origin:

- private training proves real ability before observation;
- audience pressure changes contextual performance;
- SPAR / KEEP WORKING THE DUMMY / BACK OUT remain legitimate decisions;
- protective-response action remains separate from observed-performance quality;
- the final Story should leave Metal still wanting to train, but should not reduce every route to one generic `Again.` beat.

## Final lock

> **Metal is a talented, earnest, hard-working Academy student whose performance can unravel under observation. The Story should show the gap between what he can do and what pressure makes him do without turning him, the Genin, or the narration into therapists explaining anxiety.**
