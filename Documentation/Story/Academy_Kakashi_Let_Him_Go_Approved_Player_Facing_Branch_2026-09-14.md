# LET HIM GO

ANBU Marked Target waits.

Kakashi still has hold of him.

Pakkun sits a few steps away, watching both of them.

For several seconds, nobody speaks.

Then Kakashi loosens his grip.

“Go.”

The man doesn't move.

His eyes narrow.

“What?”

Kakashi lets go completely.

“Go.”

That gets a reaction.

Not relief.

Suspicion.

ANBU Marked Target rubs the place where Kakashi had been holding him and studies the boy in front of him.

“You caught me.”

“Yeah.”

“And now you're letting me walk.”

Kakashi says nothing.

The man's gaze drops.

To the package still secured against Kakashi.

Understanding begins to creep into his expression.

“Why?”

Kakashi follows his eyes.

Then looks back at him.

“You don't have what I was sent for.”

The man's mouth tightens.

“That's your reason?”

Kakashi's visible eye barely changes.

“Do you need another one?”

Pakkun's head turns toward him.

“You sure?”

A beat.

“Mm.”

Pakkun studies him for another second.

Then sits back.

His decision isn't Kakashi's.

He knows it.

ANBU Marked Target does too.

The man takes one careful step backwards.

Kakashi doesn't follow.

Another.

Nothing.

Only then does the man turn.

He walks at first.

Then faster.

Within seconds, he's running.

Kakashi watches until the street takes him out of sight.

Pakkun's nose lifts slightly.

Tracking the fading scent.

He could follow it.

He doesn't.

After a moment:

“You're an unusual kid.”

Kakashi glances down at him.

“So I've heard.”

Pakkun gives him a long look.

“Bet you have.”

Kakashi turns away.

The package is still tucked securely against him.

That part of the mission hasn't changed.

Pakkun gets up and follows.

## RETURN TO THE ANBU CONTACT

The ANBU operative is waiting at the meeting point.

Motionless.

Kakashi approaches.

Pakkun walks beside him.

The operative's mask turns fractionally toward the ninken.

Then back to Kakashi.

No question.

Not yet.

Kakashi stops.

The operative extends a hand.

Kakashi removes the package and places it into their palm.

The seal receives a quick inspection.

Intact.

The ANBU operative lowers it.

“Report.”

Kakashi's eye shifts toward the package.

“The target met another man.”

Silence.

“He handed him the package and ran.”

The operative doesn't move.

“A masked woman came after the new carrier.”

Kakashi pauses.

Just long enough to put the sequence back in order.

“I went for the package.”

His eye lifts.

“They both fought me.”

A tiny movement of the operative's fingers against the package.

Kakashi catches it.

“I put them down.”

“Both?”

“The man and the masked woman.”

There.

Nobody missing.

The operative waits.

Kakashi continues.

“They were still there when I left.”

No claim beyond what he actually knows.

“I went after the original target.”

The operative's attention sharpens.

Barely visible.

Still visible.

“And?”

Kakashi glances toward Pakkun.

Pakkun looks up.

“He'd already stopped him.”

Pakkun's eyes narrow.

“Caught up with him.”

Kakashi looks down.

Pakkun looks back.

Neither yields.

Kakashi eventually turns toward the ANBU operative again.

“Fine.”

A small pause.

“He caught up with him.”

Pakkun looks satisfied.

The operative's mask shifts toward him once.

Then back.

“What happened to the target?”

Kakashi answers immediately.

“I let him go.”

Nothing moves.

Not Pakkun.

Not the operative.

Even the silence seems to stop.

The masked shinobi tilts their head.

“You let him go.”

“Yeah.”

“Deliberately.”

Kakashi's eye narrows a fraction.

“Yes.”

The operative lets that sit between them.

“Why?”

Kakashi looks at the package in their hand.

“You have the package.”

“That wasn't my question.”

Kakashi looks back at the mask.

“He didn't.”

Another silence.

The operative's posture stays controlled.

But their thumb stops against the edge of the package.

“You had him in custody.”

“For a minute.”

“And released him.”

“That's what I said.”

Pakkun closes his eyes.

Not quite a sigh.

The ANBU operative notices.

Kakashi notices them noticing.

Nobody comments.

Finally:

“You made that decision yourself.”

Kakashi doesn't answer straight away.

His gaze drifts briefly across the empty meeting point.

No squad.

No handler standing beside him.

No one who had been there when the decision needed making.

His eye returns to the operative.

“No one else was there.”

The answer hangs for a moment.

The ANBU operative says nothing.

Kakashi doesn't fill the silence for them.

Eventually, the operative looks down at the recovered package.

Then back at Kakashi.

“That everything?”

Kakashi thinks through it.

ANBU Marked Target.

Package Smuggler.

Masked Interceptor.

The package.

The ninken beside him.

His own decision.

“That's everything.”

One short nod.

“You can go.”

Kakashi turns away.

Pakkun goes with him.

## PAKKUN

They make it almost to the next street before Pakkun speaks.

“You know…”

Kakashi keeps walking.

“That usually isn't how catching someone works.”

Kakashi looks down at him.

“I noticed.”

Pakkun snorts.

They walk a little farther.

“You really think the package was all that mattered?”

Kakashi's pace slows.

Not much.

Enough.

He looks ahead.

“I think that was the mission.”

Pakkun watches him.

There's no joke in his expression now.

“Those aren't always the same thing.”

Kakashi looks down.

Pakkun meets his eye.

For a second, the older ninken seems like he's deciding whether to say more.

He doesn't.

Instead, he stops.

Kakashi takes another step before stopping too.

Pakkun turns toward a side street.

“I'm heading off.”

Kakashi nods.

Pakkun starts away.

Three steps.

Kakashi speaks.

“Thanks.”

One ear turns back.

A pause.

Then:

“Try not to make a habit of making me catch people you're just going to release.”

Kakashi's eye shifts slightly.

“No promises.”

Pakkun gives a low, unimpressed sound and disappears around the corner.

Kakashi waits until he's gone.

Then continues alone.

## HOKAGE OFFICE

Later.

The village has gone quieter.

Light from the Hokage Office falls across an open report.

Minato reads.

Package transfer observed.

Package Smuggler engaged.

Masked Interceptor engaged.

Both defeated.

Package recovered.

His eyes continue down the page.

Original target pursued.

Ninken intervention noted.

Minato pauses.

A faint lift at one corner of his mouth.

That disappears when he reaches the next line.

**ANBU MARKED TARGET — CAPTURED, THEN DELIBERATELY RELEASED BY KAKASHI HATAKE**

Minato reads it again.

His fingers stop against the page.

“Released.”

He leans back slowly.

His eyes shift toward the original mission order beside the report.

The wording remains exactly as it was given.

**Stop this package from falling into the wrong hands.**

Minato's gaze returns to the recovered-package confirmation.

Then to the release.

For a while, he says nothing.

Finally, he exhales softly through his nose.

“You really do listen to the words.”

There's almost amusement in it.

Almost.

His eyes remain on the report.

The expression fades into something more thoughtful.

Kakashi recovered the objective.

Beat two opponents.

Tracked the fleeing man down.

Had him.

And chose, on his own, that holding him wasn't necessary.

Minato's finger taps the report once.

No second tap follows.

“...And only the words.”

He closes the file.

That seems to trouble him more than failure would have.

It also seems to interest him more.

When the situation broke open, he chose the package.

He defeated Package Smuggler.

He defeated Masked Interceptor.

He recovered the package.

He pursued ANBU Marked Target.

He met Pakkun.

He caught the man he had been following.

Then deliberately let him go.

The target did not escape.

Kakashi did not lose him.

Kakashi released him.

The package remained secure.

Kakashi returned it to ANBU.

He reported Package Smuggler.

He reported Masked Interceptor.

He reported the ninken's involvement.

And when asked what happened to the original target—

he told them exactly what he'd done.
