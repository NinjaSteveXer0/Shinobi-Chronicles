## Presentation

# HARD REQUEST

## `a3m5_request_01` — narration

The request is not absurd.

That is what makes it difficult.

Somebody the protagonist currently works with wants something that would genuinely help the war effort.

The cost falls on somebody or something the protagonist also has reason to protect.

---

# REASON

## `a3m5_reason_01` — contextual dialogue

Let the requesting actor explain the operational reason in their own voice.

No villain speech.

No:
> “This tests your loyalty.”

The mission tests loyalty because the request matters.

# CONSEQUENCE

## `a3m5_consequence_01` — narration

The result should cost something whether the protagonist obeyed or refused.

Possible costs:
- access;
- trust;
- evidence;
- time;
- safety;
- relationship;
- command standing;
- secrecy;
- future cooperation.

Do not translate the result into loyalty points.

# MENMA GREY-LINE OVERLAY — CONDITIONAL

Only if current Chronicle has already built the medical/civilian passage network.

Minato command asks for the location of a wounded Sasuke-aligned intelligence carrier receiving treatment.

The reason is legitimate:
- the carrier is wanted for questioning;
- delay may lose evidence.

Sasuke's coalition separately asks Menma to move a sealed evidence packet through the corridor without Hokage inspection.

The reason is also legitimate:
- they believe central review may suppress it.

Both requests would quietly turn the corridor into somebody's covert road.

Menma refuses both hidden uses.

To the wounded carrier:
- treatment first;
- witnessed questioning afterward.

To the packet:
- mirrored record or no passage.

---
