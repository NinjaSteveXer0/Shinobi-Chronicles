- authentication of the Rain-facing intermediary mark;
- route timing / exchange practice;

- bounded knowledge of one Rain-side exchange protocol.

Their condition is explicit:

> they will cooperate only if they are **not forcibly taken to Konoha** and are allowed to disappear into protected local shelter after the handoff.

Why:

- they have broken obligations to the courier network;
- local Grass actors may want them questioned for illegal route activity;
- the information network may want them silenced;
- Konoha custody would make them visibly useful and potentially expose everyone who sheltered them.

Competing interests therefore exist without one monolithic villain:

- Konoha wants reliable testimony/evidence;
- a legitimate Grass security interest wants answers about illicit use of local routes;
- the broker wants the routekeeper alive because exposing them would burn the broker network;
- hostile/third-party route users want the routekeeper unavailable.
