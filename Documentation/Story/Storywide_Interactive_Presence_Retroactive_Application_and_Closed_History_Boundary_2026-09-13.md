# Shinobi Chronicles — Storywide Interactive Presence: Retroactive Application + Closed-History Boundary

**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Owner:** Writing — Konoha  
**Status:** **BINDING STORY QUALITY RECONCILIATION — APPLIES RETROACTIVELY AS PRESENTATION/ACCEPTANCE LAW; CLOSED HISTORY NOT REOPENED**

## 1. Purpose

This note clarifies how the new project-wide authority:

`Documentation/Story/Storywide_Interactive_Presence_Consequence_and_Screen_First_Authoring_Doctrine_2026-09-13.md`

applies to already-authored Shinobi Chronicles Story.

The doctrine is a **storywide player-facing quality and interaction law**.

It is not permission to rewrite closed Chronicle history merely because older content was authored before the doctrine was made explicit.

Preserve:

> **Retroactive quality test != retroactive history rewrite.**

> **Expression defect may be corrected without recommitting the occurrence.**

> **Closed semantics remain closed unless a separate factual contradiction is proven.**

---

## 2. Existing content disposition

### Academy Origins

The Origins are already under active #170 screen-first rewrite authority because installed-browser evidence proved the player-facing presentation was materially below the later Story standard.

Their semantic/choice/consequence/Battle/history contracts remain unchanged.

### Arc 1

Arc 1 M1–M12 must receive the same screen-first presence/consequence pass under #170.

The pass may rewrite dialogue, narration, reaction beats, body language, facial expression, choice wording and visible aftermath where needed.

It may not silently alter:

- World Truth;
- participant presence;
- Knowledge;
- Battle outcomes/callers;
- source occurrences;
- Echo / Kurama / False Identity semantics;
- PL / Rank / Progression / Acquisition / rewards;
- Mission completion conditions;
- 12×12 structural history.

### Arc 2 + Arc 3

Arc 2 and Arc 3 are **not automatically scheduled for wholesale rewrite**.

Those Arcs were authored after the project developed much of the now-explicit standard and already serve as major style/interaction benchmarks.

Their durable histories remain authoritative.

However, if implementation/browser review exposes a concrete player-facing defect — e.g. report-like prose, invisible consequence where consequence should be observable, interchangeable character voices, or choice→flavour→same-state interaction — Writing may correct that exact expression defect without reopening the underlying Story event.

Default classification for Arc 2–3:

> **PRESERVE / VALIDATE IN IMPLEMENTATION; CORRECT ONLY PROVEN PRESENTATION DRIFT.**

### Arc 4–8 + route stories

All future Arc 4–8 Main Story, Canon/Alt realizations, Rogue, Leader / Take-the-Throne, Non-Conformance and related route content must consume the storywide doctrine **from first authoring**, rather than requiring a later modernization pass.

### Hidden areas / Infinite Tsukuyomi introduction

All future hidden-area intro/side-story content and the short Infinite Tsukuyomi introduction must consume the doctrine from first authoring.

### Beta village campaigns + All-Route

All future village Story, village Canon/Alt routes, and All-Route / Ōtsutsuki convergence must consume the doctrine from first authoring.

## 3. Existing-history protection

The new doctrine does not create permission to alter factual history because a more cinematic alternative would be attractive.

Do not change, merely for quality:

- who was present;
- who knew what;
- who won/lost a Battle;
- who was injured/captured/killed/escaped;
- what evidence existed;
- what relationship occurrence happened;
- what route was earned;
- what capability existed;
- what ownership/assignment/deployment state existed;
- what Mission/Section historically occurred.

If the current player-facing presentation fails to dramatize those facts well, rewrite the **expression**, not the facts.

---

## 4. Validation rule

Existing content should be judged against the same final player-experience question as new content:

> **Can the player describe what happened as an event they participated in, rather than a paragraph they read?**

If yes and semantics remain correct, preserve it.

If no, identify the exact presentation defect and correct only the necessary expression layer.

Do not launch broad archaeological rewrites merely because a newer doctrine exists.
