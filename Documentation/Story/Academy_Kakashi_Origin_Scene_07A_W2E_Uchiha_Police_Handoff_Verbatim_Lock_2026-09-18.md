## Narration
The Uchiha Police Force entrance is still lit.

Two officers move toward Kakashi as he approaches.

Their attention goes immediately to the restrained shinobi beside him.

One of them raises a hand.

## Dialogue
**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:**  
“Hold there.”

Kakashi stops.

The officer looks at Masked Interceptor.

Then at Kakashi.

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:**  
“What happened?”

**KAKASHI:**  
“She attacked a man near the Sakura tree.”

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:**  
“You stopped her?”

**KAKASHI:**  
“Yes.”

The second officer moves closer to Masked Interceptor.

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:**  
“Name?”

Masked Interceptor says nothing.

The officer waits.

Nothing changes.

He looks back to Kakashi.

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:**  
“You know who she is?”

**KAKASHI:**  
“No.”

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:**  
“Affiliation?”

**KAKASHI:**  
“Unknown.”

The officer looks at the restraints.

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:**  
“And the man she attacked?”

**KAKASHI:**  
“Gone.”

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:**  
“Was anything taken?”

**KAKASHI:**  
“A package.”

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:**  
“Where is it?”

**KAKASHI:**  
“Gone with another man.”

The officer studies Kakashi for a moment.

Then nods to his partner.

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:**  
“We’ll take her.”

Kakashi transfers the restraint line.

The second officer secures Masked Interceptor.

She passes Kakashi without resistance.

Then stops.

Looks at him.

**MASKED INTERCEPTOR:**  
“Interesting choice.”

Kakashi’s expression does not change.

The officers lead her toward the entrance.

**UCHIHA POLICE OFFICER:**  
“You should report this to whoever sent you.”

**KAKASHI:**  
“I know.”
