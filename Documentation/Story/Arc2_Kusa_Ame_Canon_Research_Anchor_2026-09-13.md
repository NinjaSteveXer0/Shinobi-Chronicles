## Amegakure / Hidden Rain — safe baseline anchor

Official Naruto material establishes **Hidden Rain Village / Amegakure** as a real foreign shinobi village with strong historical associations with secrecy, surveillance/infiltration difficulty and Pain/Konan-era central control.

Official sources consumed:

- NARUTO OFFICIAL SITE — `Infiltrate! The Village Hidden in the Rain` / NARUTO: SHIPPUDEN Episode 349
  - https://naruto-official.com/en/anime/naruto2/list/01_573
  - Jiraiya must infiltrate Hidden Rain covertly to gather intelligence; the village does not present an obvious open civil-war state from his initial observation.
- NARUTO OFFICIAL SITE — 20th Anniversary retrospective covering Jiraiya's Hidden Rain infiltration and Pain/Konan's leadership context
  - https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1316
- NARUTO OFFICIAL SITE — `Kara's Footprints` / BORUTO Episode 157
  - https://naruto-official.com/en/anime/boruto/list/01_1101
  - Sai and Sasuke later travel to Hidden Rain to investigate a possible Kara hideout, confirming the location remains an active foreign investigation space in later canon material.

Writing boundary:

- current Shinobi Chronicles Ame politics are **not** assumed identical to Pain-era or Boruto-era canon unless separately closed;
- Arc 2's `information economy`, brokers, buyers/sellers and competing interests are deliberate SC authoring rather than claimed baseline canon;
- do not assume Pain, Konan, Nagato or another canon leader currently governs this SC Amegakure unless durable SC authority explicitly establishes it;
- do not infer every Ame resident participates in one intelligence apparatus.
