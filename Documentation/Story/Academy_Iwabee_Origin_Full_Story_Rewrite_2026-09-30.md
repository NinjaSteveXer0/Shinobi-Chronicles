# PLAYER-FACING STORY

# ACADEMY IWABEE
## THE PRACTICAL

---

# SCENE 1 — ANOTHER RED MARK

Iwabee drops a marked worksheet onto the bench harder than he needs to.

The page slides into the instructor's hand.

A thick red score sits across the top.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You planning to argue with the paper?”

**IWABEE:** “Wouldn't listen.”

The instructor turns the page over.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Neither did you, apparently.”

Iwabee's eyes narrow.

**IWABEE:** “If this is another lecture, I'm leaving.”

The instructor puts the paper down and walks toward a damaged section of the practical ground.

Broken ridges cut across the yard.

Loose stone has collected in a shallow collapse near one edge.

Iwabee follows despite himself.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Make that usable.”

Iwabee looks over the damage.

His shoulders loosen.

**IWABEE:** “That's it?”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “That's it.”

Iwabee steps off the path and plants his feet in the dirt.

**IWABEE:** “Should've started with this.”

---

# SCENE 2 — EARTH RELEASE

Iwabee studies the damage instead of rushing it.

The ground is uneven in four different ways.

Any one of them can be fixed.

### CHOICE

- **Raise the collapsed section**
- **Flatten the damaged ground**
- **Build a stable path through it**
- **Reinforce the weakest section**

---

## RAISE THE COLLAPSED SECTION

Iwabee drives chakra into the sunken section.

The earth rises in one heavy slab.

Stone grinds against stone until the collapsed ground sits level with the rest of the yard.

Loose rubble slides from underneath it.

A boot moves behind the falling stone.

Iwabee stops.

Someone was hiding there.

---

## FLATTEN THE DAMAGED GROUND

Iwabee lowers the broken ridges instead of rebuilding them.

The high points sink.

The loose earth spreads.

A strip of cover disappears with it.

A crouched shinobi is suddenly left in the open.

Iwabee sees him at the same moment the stranger realises he has been exposed.

---

## BUILD A STABLE PATH THROUGH IT

Iwabee leaves the deepest damage alone.

He raises a firm strip of earth straight through the broken section.

The path locks into place.

A pair of boots stands beside the new edge.

Iwabee follows them upward.

Someone is tucked behind the remaining rubble.

---

## REINFORCE THE WEAKEST SECTION

Iwabee crouches and presses one palm to the ground.

The outer edge is ready to give way again.

He packs earth beneath it until the section stops shifting.

A loose timber frame is pushed aside by the rising ground.

A young shinobi is crouched behind it.

---

# SCENE 3 — THE ROGUE GENIN

The stranger wears shinobi gear.

Not Academy gear.

He looks at Iwabee.

Then at the instructor.

Then at the open side of the yard.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Move.”

Iwabee stays exactly where he is.

**IWABEE:** “No.”

The Rogue's attention sharpens.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Wasn't asking twice.”

Iwabee glances at the route behind him.

Then back at the Rogue.

### CHOICE

- **Confront him**
- **Block his escape**
- **Call the instructor**
- **Finish the practical**

---

# BRANCH A — CONFRONT HIM

Iwabee steps directly into the open route.

The Rogue stops.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Kid.”

**IWABEE:** “You keep saying things like that's supposed to move me.”

The Rogue shifts his weight.

Iwabee sees it.

So does the instructor.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Last chance.”

Iwabee sets his feet.

**IWABEE:** “Take yours.”

### PL BATTLE

Use existing authorised Battle:

`academy_iwabee_origin_rogue_confrontation`

No Battle mechanics change.

---

## IF IWABEE WINS

The Rogue Genin is forced out of the fight first.

Iwabee stays upright, breathing hard.

The Rogue tries to square himself again.

The instructor steps between them.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Enough.”

Iwabee keeps his eyes on the Rogue.

**IWABEE:** “He was running.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “He's not now.”

The instructor takes control of the Rogue before the exchange can restart.

Iwabee looks past them at the ground he repaired before the fight.

Still level.

Still holding.

He wipes dirt from one hand onto his trousers.

---

## IF IWABEE LOSES

Iwabee's stance breaks first.

One knee hits the dirt.

He starts to push himself back up.

The instructor moves in front of him.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Stay down.”

**IWABEE:** “I'm not done.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You are with him.”

Iwabee looks around the instructor at the Rogue.

The Rogue is already backing toward the open side of the yard.

Iwabee gets one foot under himself.

The instructor blocks him with an arm.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “No.”

The Rogue runs.

Iwabee watches until he disappears beyond the yard.

His jaw works once.

Then he looks at the section of ground he fixed.

It did not collapse because he lost the fight.

---

# BRANCH B — BLOCK HIS ESCAPE

Iwabee does not chase.

He watches where the Rogue keeps looking.

The open side of the yard.

The Rogue commits to it.

Iwabee moves first.

Earth tears upward across the route.

The Rogue stops so sharply that one heel skids through the dirt.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Seriously?”

Iwabee looks at the wall.

**IWABEE:** “You wanted out.”

He looks back at the Rogue.

**IWABEE:** “Pick another way.”

The Rogue checks the yard.

Instructor on one side.

Earth wall on the other.

Iwabee between him and the easiest gap.

The Rogue lifts both hands.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Fine.”

The instructor moves in and takes control.

Iwabee looks from the wall to the ground he repaired first.

Both are still standing.

---

# BRANCH C — CALL THE INSTRUCTOR

Iwabee keeps his eyes on the Rogue.

**IWABEE:** “Sensei.”

The instructor is already moving.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Back up.”

Iwabee takes one step back.

No more.

The instructor puts himself between the students and the Rogue.

The Rogue spots the open side of the yard.

Runs.

Iwabee starts after him.

The instructor catches his shoulder.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Leave him.”

Iwabee twists around.

**IWABEE:** “He's getting away.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “And you're staying here.”

Iwabee watches the Rogue disappear past the fence.

He looks like he wants to argue.

He doesn't.

---

# BRANCH D — FINISH THE PRACTICAL

Iwabee looks at the Rogue.

Then at the unfinished ground.

**ROGUE GENIN:** “Smart kid.”

Iwabee turns back toward the practical.

**IWABEE:** “Wasn't talking to you.”

Behind him, the Rogue breaks for the open side of the yard.

Iwabee keeps working.

Earth settles under his hands.

The last damaged section locks into place.

By the time Iwabee stands, the Rogue is gone.

The ground is usable.

That is what Iwabee was told to do.

He did it.

---

# SCENE 4 — THE INSTRUCTOR

The instructor checks the repaired ground before he says anything about the Rogue.

He puts his weight onto the raised section.

Walks the edge.

Presses one heel into the new path.

Iwabee watches him test every part.

**IWABEE:** “It's not going anywhere.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “I'm checking.”

**IWABEE:** “You're taking your time checking.”

The instructor looks at him.

What he says next depends on what happened.

---

## IF IWABEE CONFRONTED AND WON

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You really went straight at him.”

**IWABEE:** “He was right there.”

The instructor glances toward the Rogue now in his control.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “So was I.”

Iwabee knows exactly what he means.

**IWABEE:** “Didn't need you.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “This time.”

Iwabee looks back at the repaired ground.

He does not answer.

---

## IF IWABEE CONFRONTED AND LOST

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Still want to tell me you had him?”

Iwabee looks toward the route the Rogue escaped through.

**IWABEE:** “No.”

The answer comes out rougher than he wanted.

The instructor taps the repaired section with his heel.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “This held.”

Iwabee looks down at it.

**IWABEE:** “Yeah.”

That is all either of them says about it.

---

## IF IWABEE BLOCKED THE ESCAPE

The instructor looks at the earth wall.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “That wasn't on the worksheet.”

Iwabee folds his arms.

**IWABEE:** “Neither was he.”

The instructor looks at the detained Rogue.

Then back at the wall.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Fair.”

Iwabee's mouth pulls at one corner.

Barely.

---

## IF IWABEE CALLED THE INSTRUCTOR

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You called me fast.”

Iwabee stares at him.

**IWABEE:** “You were standing right there.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Exactly.”

Iwabee frowns.

**IWABEE:** “Don't make that sound clever.”

The instructor moves on to the repaired ground.

Iwabee follows, still irritated.

---

## IF IWABEE FINISHED THE PRACTICAL

The instructor checks the last section.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You let him run.”

**IWABEE:** “You gave me an assignment.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “I did.”

Iwabee points at the finished ground.

**IWABEE:** “Then check it.”

The instructor does.

No lecture follows.

---

# SCENE 5 — THE PAPER

The instructor goes back to the bench.

Iwabee sees the worksheet in his hand and immediately looks annoyed.

**IWABEE:** “Don't.”

The instructor holds the page out anyway.

Iwabee does not take it.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You still have to fix this.”

**IWABEE:** “I just fixed half the yard.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “I noticed.”

**IWABEE:** “Then why are we back to the paper?”

The instructor turns the sheet so Iwabee can see one of the marked answers.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Because this answer is still wrong.”

Iwabee takes the page from him.

**IWABEE:** “I hate written tests.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You can hate them tomorrow too.”

Iwabee looks at the page.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Bring it back corrected.”

Iwabee looks toward the repaired yard.

Then folds the paper once and tucks it under his arm.

**IWABEE:** “Fine.”

---

# SCENE 6 — IWABEE'S ANSWER

The instructor starts putting the practice markers away.

Iwabee stays by the bench with the marked paper.

He has one last thing to say before he leaves.

### CHOICE

- **“I know what I can do.”**
- **“I need to get better at the rest.”**
- **“The Academy leans too hard on written tests.”**
- **“I'll prove I can do it my way.”**

---

## I KNOW WHAT I CAN DO

**IWABEE:** “I know what I can do.”

The instructor keeps stacking markers.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Good.”

Iwabee waits for more.

Nothing comes.

He looks at the red mark again.

**IWABEE:** “Still fixing this.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Tomorrow.”

Iwabee grunts and folds the page smaller.

---

## I NEED TO GET BETTER AT THE REST

Iwabee turns the worksheet over.

**IWABEE:** “I need to get better at the rest.”

The instructor looks up.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Probably.”

Iwabee gives him a hard look.

**IWABEE:** “You could've taken one second before agreeing.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Wouldn't have changed the answer.”

Iwabee mutters something under his breath and shoves the page into his bag.

---

## THE ACADEMY LEANS TOO HARD ON WRITTEN TESTS

Iwabee holds up the worksheet.

**IWABEE:** “The Academy leans too hard on these.”

The instructor shoulders a box of practice markers.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Maybe.”

**IWABEE:** “That's it?”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “You want me to cancel written tests?”

Iwabee considers it.

**IWABEE:** “Could.”

The instructor walks past him.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Correct the page.”

Iwabee clicks his tongue and follows.

---

## I'LL PROVE I CAN DO IT MY WAY

Iwabee folds the paper and points it toward the repaired yard.

**IWABEE:** “I'll prove I can do it my way.”

The instructor looks at the ground.

Then at the paper.

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Do both.”

Iwabee's face tightens.

**IWABEE:** “That's more work.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Yeah.”

Iwabee looks annoyed.

He also keeps the paper.

---

# SCENE 7 — CLOSE

Iwabee heads toward the gate.

The marked worksheet is sticking out of his bag.

He notices.

Pulls it free.

Looks at the red score.

For an instant, his hand moves toward the nearest bin.

He stops himself before the page leaves his fingers.

Iwabee folds it properly and puts it back in the bag.

Behind him, the practical ground is still level.

The instructor is already putting the last marker away.

Iwabee looks over his shoulder.

**IWABEE:** “Tomorrow.”

**INSTRUCTOR:** “Tomorrow.”

Iwabee leaves the yard.
