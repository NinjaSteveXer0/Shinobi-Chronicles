# Chronicle Engine — Combat Workspace Recovery Addendum

**Recovery date:** 2026-09-04  
**Status:** Archaeological recovery / durable doctrine record  
**Scope:** Reusable Chronicle Engine principles recovered from the original Shinobi Chronicles Combat Stuffs / Battle Systems workspace.

This document preserves high-confidence reusable Chronicle Engine doctrine recovered from surviving Combat design inventories, handoffs, implementation evidence, and the prior dedicated Combat archaeology sweep. It does **not** promote unresolved Shinobi Chronicles content questions into Engine canon, and it does **not** replace the full Chronicle Engine Bible.

## 1. Authority and activity resolution

Combat consumes authoritative domain state, constructs encounter context, owns temporary encounter-runtime state, and emits outcomes, evidence, and consequence requests. Participation in Combat does not transfer permanent authority over Skills, Inventory, Provenance, Relationships, Summons, progression, or other domains.

Reusable lifecycle:

`REQUEST → CONTEXT SNAPSHOT → RESOLVER → OUTCOME → CONSEQUENCES → CHRONICLE RECORD`

One causal event may feed multiple consequence authorities without becoming the permanent owner of any of them.

## 2. Exact source/package identity and idempotent projection

Runtime contributions are source-aware and package-aware. Recommended conceptual identity:

`sourceId + exactPackageId + expressionStateId`

Overlaps must be explicit, such as `coexists`, `replaces`, `suppresses`, or `mutually_exclusive`. Do not suppress or deduplicate by loose names, family similarity, lineage, or presentation equivalence.

A source-owned contribution must not be applied twice merely because multiple systems reference the same capability.

## 3. Historical cause is not runtime ownership

History explains why permanent development exists. Runtime source ownership determines what disappears when current circumstances change.

Removing Equipment, a Summon, a Hosted Entity, a Transformation, or another temporary source removes only that source's surviving current contribution. It must not rewind independently earned development, resolved consequences, knowledge already acquired, or committed history.

## 4. Effective State is projection, not competing storage

Effective State is assembled from authoritative persistent state plus legitimate current circumstances. It should not become a second persistent truth store.

When a runtime source expires, remove its projection rather than mutating historical/persistent state to fake the removal.

## 5. Capability distinctions

Preserve:

`Knowledge ≠ Access ≠ Competence ≠ Power ≠ Mastery`

Assistance does not automatically transfer mastery. Knowing that a capability exists does not grant access to it; access does not prove competence; competence does not establish power; power does not establish mastery.

## 6. Persistent Discoverable Knowledge Seeds

World knowledge exists independently of any one acquisition opportunity.

A rumour, clue, provenance fact, or other discoverable information unit may persist even when one NPC, battle, event, document, or conversation opportunity is missed. It may later surface through another **causally eligible** carrier.

Redistribution must respect source knowledge, provenance, faction, geography, history, and causal plausibility. Discovery mechanics must not fabricate information that no legitimate source possesses.

## 7. Observer-relative knowledge and disclosure

Authoritative world truth remains separate from what an observer knows, believes, infers, remembers, or can legitimately disclose.

Sources may reveal only what they genuinely know, observed, inferred, or otherwise possess. Successful discovery changes known information; it does not rewrite the underlying fact.

Knowledge possession and willingness to disclose should remain separable.

## 8. Same information, different causal route

Equivalent factual information may be obtained through Diplomacy, Interrogation, Investigation, observation, documents, theft, companion assistance, or other routes while producing different relationship, reputation, memory, and Chronicle consequences.

`Information equality ≠ event equivalence.`

## 9. Relationship evidence, not universal relationship arithmetic

Events should emit evidence describing what happened. Relationship authority interprets that evidence contextually.

Do not reduce relationship consequences to universal fixed arithmetic such as a generic `relationship += X` or `relationship -= Y` for every event.

Preserve independent dimensions such as cooperation, trust, access, and control. High access/control may coexist with low trust/cooperation, and negative relationships may still produce meaningful capabilities or tactical effects.

## 10. Genuine qualifying interaction

Progression and relationship evidence should arise from meaningful qualifying interaction, not arbitrary button farming or safe repetition.

Prefer meaningful-use and genuine-challenge evidence over fixed repetition counters where the domain semantics support it.

## 11. Participation follows causal presence

Event participation is not limited to ordinary UI/deployment slots.

Attached Summons, Hosted Entities, pets, spirits, constructs, or other persistent sources/entities may legitimately contribute to event evidence when their presence/access rules make them causally present.

`Participation ≠ UI slot type.`

Source addressability may be broader than Entity registration or collectible identity.

## 12. Generated contextual dialogue

Chronicle Engine dialogue assembly may consume authoritative participant knowledge, accessible memories, relationship history, dispositions, secrets, goals, recent events, and world state.

Generation must remain knowledge-bounded: characters must not gain facts merely because the project knows them.

Writing owns voice, linguistic tendencies, tone, and narrative standards. Chronicle Engine owns contextual assembly from legitimate state/history/knowledge.

## 13. Significant object world truth and provenance

Persistent significant objects exist according to current causal world state, not static reward tables.

Victory does not universally transfer possession. Object aftermath may include survival, escape with owner, loss, theft, destruction, inheritance, recovery, or intervention by another authority.

Repair, modification, reforging, or reconstruction may preserve object identity while causal continuity remains. True destruction ends the original identity. Later objects made from remnants inherit derivative lineage, not the antecedent object's complete personal history.

Preserve:

`Blueprint knowledge ≠ object identity ≠ property knowledge ≠ provenance knowledge.`

## 14. Randomness after semantic eligibility

Determine what is causally and semantically possible first. Randomness may then choose among valid possibilities.

Do not roll an arbitrary result first and invent a causal justification afterward.

## 15. Environment supplies facts; capabilities interpret them

World/context state should express factual conditions such as weather, terrain, location, observers, ownership, social state, and injuries.

Capabilities own the interpretation of those facts. Avoid globally baking capability-specific bonuses into environmental truth.

## 16. Similar effect does not mean same causal state

Mechanically similar consequences may require distinct causal identities because removal, cure, counterplay, provenance, relationship meaning, evidence, and future interaction can differ.

Do not flatten distinct restraint, binding, sealing, illusion, or other states into one generic condition solely because they share a surface-level outcome.

## 17. One action may contain multiple outcome segments

One causal action may resolve across several targets or consequence segments while remaining one historical action/evidence envelope.

Authored propagation, area resolution, continuation, or multi-target effects do not automatically become multiple independent actions.

## 18. Invalid selection does not create false history

An inadmissible selection does not mean the attempted action historically occurred.

Unless a domain explicitly defines the attempt itself as committed, invalid selection should not consume action lifecycle, resource quantity, state duration, progression evidence, relationship evidence, or Chronicle history.

## 19. Combined state does not erase component histories

A combined runtime expression may use multiple sources/entities while their independent histories and relationships continue underneath.

`Combined runtime state ≠ historical identity merger.`

## 20. Historical significance is distinct from mechanical and presentation significance

Occurrence, retained evidence, historical significance, and player-facing presentation are separate layers.

A mechanically large event may be historically routine. A mechanically small event may become historically major. A flashy notification does not automatically deserve a Chronicle entry, and a historically important pattern need not require dramatic presentation.

Retain enough causal evidence to interpret later significance without dumping meaningless telemetry into Chronicle history.

## Candidate / later material — do not treat as locked core doctrine yet

The original Combat archaeology also surfaced useful but not fully ratified candidates, including:

- failure-state expertise as a specialist capability domain;
- independent companion/entity activity modes such as Summon Battles;
- deriving all achievements directly from Chronicle evidence rather than maintaining any separate counters;
- exact generic architecture for willingness-to-disclose;
- complete generic relationship directionality for non-agentic objects.

These remain review candidates unless separately ratified.

## Shinobi Chronicles-specific material excluded from Engine canon

Do not promote SC-specific numbers, Naruto techniques, Battle formation counts, Skill palette sizes, Condition calibrations, Summon names, boss actions, weapon proficiency percentages, or Alpha UI constraints into reusable Chronicle Engine doctrine merely because they validated the principles above.

## Recovery discipline

This addendum records surviving recovered doctrine. It must not be used to resurrect superseded game-specific designs or to infer unresolved production identities, Origin content, Promotion rules, or representation classifications.

`Recovered historical evidence ≠ automatic current production authority.`
