# Shinobi Chronicles — Genin Difficulty Inheritance and Pre-Origin Preparation Concept Record

**Date:** 2026-09-18  
**Owner:** Stephen / CE-Codex-Coordination future reconciliation  
**Status:** **STEPHEN DIRECTION RECORDED — QUEUED / NOT AN ALPHA BLOCKER / NOT YET IMPLEMENTATION-CLOSED**

## Purpose

Record Stephen's future-difficulty direction without turning it into a Shinobi Chronicles Alpha blocker.

The intent is for later difficulties to become meaningfully different because the player carries forward developed Chronicle history and selected capability, not merely because enemies receive larger numbers.

## Core player fantasy

After completing **Academy Student difficulty**, the player may choose **one developed ninja** from the completed run to carry into **Genin difficulty** as the special carry-over / "forbidden ninja" source.

Exact final terminology is not closed by this note.

The selected developed ninja may contribute legitimate inherited development/capability into the new difficulty where owning systems authorise it.

Example direction supplied by Stephen:

- player carries a developed **Jōnin Kakashi** into Genin difficulty;
- that Kakashi's prior development may include a **Special Jōnin / Assassin-oriented route**;
- when beginning Kakashi's Origin in the new difficulty, inherited Assassin-relevant development may materially improve lethal resolver capability;
- an inherited capability may increase the chance of an **ATTEMPT TO KILL** resolver succeeding, and where exact deterministic-kill eligibility becomes satisfied, the player-facing affordance may legitimately become **KILL** rather than **ATTEMPT TO KILL**.

Do not hard-code exact percentages or automatic deterministic-kill conversion until Progression/Development + CE + Combat/Resolver authority closes the machine-facing rule.

## Difficulty identity principle

Later difficulty should expose routes that were extremely difficult or practically unreachable on the initial Academy Student run by allowing the player to bring forward legitimately developed capability.

This should preserve:

- difficult route != disabled route;
- inherited development != hidden difficulty PL bonus;
- carried history/capability != free mastery of unrelated skills;
- prior path evidence may affect later resolver eligibility/probability where semantically relevant;
- exact outcomes still belong to the owning resolver.

The intended payoff is that a route that looked almost impossible on the first run can become realistically conquerable because of who the player developed and what history they chose to carry forward.

## Pre-Origin preparation flow — 2026-09-30 owner correction

Stephen has corrected the ordering of the future-difficulty preparation flow.

The older order in this concept — choosing the new Origin before reviewing inheritance — is superseded.

Current direction:

**Select new difficulty -> Load / review permitted inheritance from the completed prior difficulty -> select the one Forbidden Ninja carry-over -> apply authorised inherited Weapons / Items / Equipment / other carry-over -> Choose the new Origin Character -> Open My Clan for pre-Story preparation -> Begin Origin**

Reason:

The player should know what legitimate carry-over they are bringing into the new run **before** choosing which Origin they want to attack with that inherited capability.

This is still a direction, not yet a fully closed transaction contract.

### Initial Academy Student new-Chronicle starter kit

Stephen also requires the very first/new-player Chronicle to begin with a small basic **starting Weapon + Item** package so the opening game does not assume an empty inventory forever.

Exact starter IDs/counts remain open and must come through real Acquisition/Inventory transactions.

### Later-difficulty inherited inventory

A later difficulty may carry authorised exact:

- Weapons;
- Items;
- Equipment;
- other explicitly inheritable inventory/state;

from the completed prior difficulty.

Carry-over must preserve exact object identity/provenance where the object is an instanced durable object.

Do not remint inherited weapons/equipment as fresh history-less copies.

## Forbidden Ninja carry-over — current owner direction

One developed ninja from the completed prior difficulty may be selected as the **Forbidden Ninja** carry-over source.

Current owner direction:

- the Forbidden Ninja enters the new difficulty at **Level 1**;
- the Forbidden Ninja's prior **Chronicle/history remains**;
- legitimate inherited specialties/capabilities/history may still alter new-Origin possibilities where owning authority permits;
- level reset must not erase historical facts;
- exact relationship among Level reset, persistent Stats/development, PL, Skill Access, Mastery and inherited capability remains a future Progression/CE reconciliation and must not be guessed.

Therefore preserve:

`new-difficulty level reset != Chronicle erasure`

and:

`Chronicle persistence != automatic full mechanical carry-over`.

The later implementation must make the carry-over package explicit rather than silently cloning the previous end-state.

## Inheritance review screen

Before the new Origin is chosen, show an explicit screen summarising what may carry into the new difficulty.

The screen should make inheritance visible rather than silently mutating the new run.

Potential categories, only where owning authority permits, include:

- persistent development evidence;
- path/specialisation inheritance;
- approved skills/capability access;
- equipment/item carry-over where authorised;
- relationship/Shared History effects where legitimate;
- resolver modifiers/eligibility consequences;
- any excluded/non-transferable state.

Knowledge, Access, Competence, Power and Mastery must remain separate. Carry-over must not imply all five.

## Starter items / starting loadout

The player should receive a small, friendly basic starting allowance appropriate to the run.

For a first Academy Student Chronicle, this includes at least one basic starting Weapon + Item package once exact IDs are closed.

For later difficulties, this starting allowance must be reconciled with inherited Weapons / Items / Equipment so the player is not accidentally duplicated, stripped or overgranted.

Exact item identities/counts are not closed here.

Items must come through authoritative Inventory/Acquisition transactions; the preparation screen does not fabricate ownership by presentation.

## My Clan before Story

Stephen wants the player to be able to enter **My Clan** before the Origin starts so they can prepare their ninja before jumping into Story.

Preserve existing My Clan semantics:

- ownership != assignment != deployment;
- My Clan formation does not automatically rewrite Story-team state;
- opening My Clan does not itself create inherited ownership/capability;
- staged formation/configuration still requires its normal authoritative commit.

A future opening contract must explicitly reconcile how pre-Origin My Clan preparation interacts with the selected Origin protagonist and any carry-over ninja.

## Identity / representation collision to resolve later

A same-person case such as:

- carry-over source = **Jōnin Kakashi**;
- selected new Origin = **Academy Kakashi**

requires an explicit CE/Registry/Progression rule.

Do not assume both representations are simultaneously deployed as separate people merely because both cards exist.

The future contract must decide whether the higher-rank representation acts as:

- an inheritance source into the new Origin representation;
- a separately owned but non-deployable carry-over representation during the Origin;
- another exact authorised model.

This note deliberately does not close that collision.
