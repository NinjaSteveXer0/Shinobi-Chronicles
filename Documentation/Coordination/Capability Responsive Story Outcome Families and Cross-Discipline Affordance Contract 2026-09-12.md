**Date:** 2026-09-12

## 1. Purpose

This contract consumes Writing issue #126 and reconciles it against the already-closed capability-responsive event/runtime doctrine.

The new requirement is real, but it does **not** justify a second capability, event, Story, reward or progression system.

Existing authority already supports:

- route-level contextual affordances;
- occurrence-level capability/history eligibility;
- `multi_path_any` and authored `compound_path_synergy`;
- history-generated follow-ups;
- semantic eligibility before bounded selection;
- observer-safe projection;
- contextual Skill evaluation through the queued generic #86 lane;
- Chronicle-relative live Story intent generation through queued #121.

What was missing was a reusable authoring grammar for **what kinds of materially different consequences different legitimate builds may create once a factual situation is reached**.

Canonical rule:

> **Capability-responsive design changes verbs, risks and factual consequences — not merely dialogue colour.**

And:

> **Alternate Origins do not exist to find a differently-worded path to Menma's reward. They may create different legitimate outcome classes entirely.**

## 3. Menma outcomes remain genuine Chronicle history

Two current developer-Menma results are protected as **earned Chronicle outcomes**, not universal campaign prizes.

### Arc 1 — Echo / identity-rebinding lineage

The Menma Chronicle legitimately reached this result through exact committed history, capability, relationships and Story resolution.

It remains real.

It must not be:

- flattened into universal hidden canon;
- cosmetically renamed for another Origin;
- automatically granted because every Chronicle must leave Arc 1 with an equivalent Echo;
- treated as a generic reward tier.

### Arc 2 Mission 3 — Sealed Wall / concealed-chamber preservation

Current Writing authority places the underground descent and advanced wall/chamber path in A2M3.

Menma's Fūinjutsu/Kinjutsu capability plus prior history may legitimately support an exceptional preservation/isolation/removal outcome.

The rule is not `every build receives the chamber another way`.

The rule is:

> **Menma receives that outcome only because this Chronicle legitimately produced the capability and history that makes it possible.**

Another Chronicle may lose the chamber, destroy it, never reach it, preserve different evidence, capture a witness instead, recover another object, create a safer political result, or leave a more dangerous but more informative unresolved state.

## 4. Outcome-family grammar

The following are semantic authoring families. They are **not mandatory engine enums**.

A specialist route may materially affect one or more of these consequence dimensions:

### 4.1 Perception / Knowledge

Changes what can legitimately be noticed, distinguished, interpreted, disproved or preserved as observer Knowledge.

Examples:
- recognise a false trail;
- separate Genjutsu distortion from physical evidence;
- identify a seal family without mastering it;
- notice toxicological evidence;
- detect a chakra anomaly;
- identify weapon/material provenance.

Preserve:

**Knowledge != Access != Competence != Power != Mastery.**

### 4.2 Access / Position / Reach

Changes where the protagonist can legitimately get, what they can physically or institutionally approach, or when they can arrive.

Examples:
- traverse dangerous geometry;
- reach a fleeing witness;
- enter through a legitimate alternate route;
- gain a formal audience through service recognition;
- create a summon-assisted traversal route where the relationship and summon capability support it.

Access does not imply success after arrival.

### 4.3 Control / Custody / Escape

Changes who or what can be contained, protected, intercepted, extracted, released or allowed to escape.

Examples:
- secure a living operator before evidence is destroyed;
- preserve a witness;
- deny an escape corridor;
- extract an ally from a hazard;
- preserve custody without automatically learning identity/allegiance.

Battle victory does not automatically establish custody.

### 4.4 Preservation / Recovery / Evidence

Changes what survives the occurrence and what can be carried forward with provenance.

Examples:
- preserve a damaged record;
- recover a weapon/material without destroying its evidentiary state;
- stabilise a person;
- preserve a trap/seal/object for later analysis;
- protect an observer-specific testimony chain.

### 4.5 Transformation / State Change

Changes the factual state of an object, location, route, hazard or system.

Examples:
- disable rather than destroy infrastructure;
- isolate one mechanism from another;
- redirect water/fire/terrain where an exact Ninjutsu capability supports it;
- repair, reinforce, alter or safely dismantle an object through exact Crafting authority;
- leave a location usable, ruined, exposed, sealed, contaminated or transformed.

### 4.6 Relationship / Reputation / Institutional Consequence

Changes how legitimate participants/institutions relate to the protagonist based on what they observed and what role/history exists.

Examples:
- witness trust;
- rival respect/fear;
- service recognition;
- political obligation;
- underworld contact;
- clan-specific suspicion/support;
- formal gratitude without automatic ownership/recruitment.

No universal good/evil score is implied.

### 4.7 Development / Capability Evidence

Creates evidence/opportunity relevant to Progression/Development without directly granting Mastery or permanent power.

Examples:
- meaningful repeated discipline use;
- teacher/mentor opportunity;
- new practice context;
- observed weakness that later development can address;
- evidence satisfying an authored evolution gate where Progression confirms it.

Story outcome != automatic Progression.

### 4.8 Future Eligibility / Opportunity Lineage

Creates later candidate events/opportunities because a factual earlier occurrence happened.

Examples:
- spared witness returns;
- recovered provenance creates a later crafting/weapon lead;
- political recognition creates later meeting eligibility;
- unusual medical intervention creates a future contact;
- a concealed identity contradiction later resurfaces.

This is causal history, not morality scoring or hidden quest reward injection.

### 4.9 Cost / Risk / Unresolved Pressure

A specialist route may trade one problem for another rather than simply improving the result.

Examples:
- preserve evidence but allow a suspect to escape;
- capture a witness but expose identity;
- protect infrastructure but lose pursuit time;
- use a rare capability openly and create observer Knowledge;
- recover an object whose provenance creates future obligations or danger.

Specialist route != superior route.

## 5. Authoring pipeline

Capability-responsive Story should use the existing semantic sequence:

`authored factual situation / mission invariant`
→ `current committed Chronicle context`
→ `eligible occurrence / route affordances`
→ `observer-safe player/NPC decision space`
→ `selected intent`
→ `owning resolver`
→ `factual consequence envelope`
→ `Chronicle / World / Knowledge / Relationship updates`
→ `owner-authorised reward/development/future eligibility`

Critical boundaries:

- capability predicate != success;
- player intent != factual outcome;
- Skill effect != event definition;
- Battle result != Story result;
- Story result != automatic reward identity;
- mission completion != Progression;
- reward entitlement != UI reroll;
- generated dialogue != World Truth.

## 6. Cross-discipline family — Taijutsu / Genjutsu

This pairing should not be interpreted as a universal combined build class. These are legitimate candidate affordance families only when exact current capabilities/history support them.

### Taijutsu-facing affordances

Potential factual advantages include:

- superior body control through dangerous physical geometry;
- intercepting a fleeing participant;
- close-range control where killing is not required;
- protecting or physically extracting another person;
- surviving/contacting a hazard another route avoids;
- maintaining pursuit or position under physical pressure;
- creating or denying a narrow movement opening.

High Taijutsu does not automatically mean impossible-speed movement, guaranteed capture, immunity to injury or command over another Character.

### Genjutsu-facing affordances

Potential factual advantages include:

- recognising inconsistent sensory evidence;
- resisting or exposing authored perception manipulation;
- separating observed fact from induced presentation;
- misdirecting an observer where an exact Genjutsu technique permits it;
- creating a bounded deception/escape/counter-surveillance route;
- altering what an observer believes they perceived without rewriting World Truth;
- enabling interrogation pressure or evidence separation where exact technique/consent/context authority supports it.

Genjutsu does **not** automatically provide mind reading, perfect truth extraction, memory rewriting, universal stealth or guaranteed social control.

### Compound Taijutsu + Genjutsu examples

Where specifically authored, the combination may support:

- physically securing a witness while defeating/avoiding a perception trap;
- preserving a living operator another route loses;
- using controlled movement plus legitimate perceptual misdirection to escape or intercept;
- identifying which apparent threat is real, then reaching it before evidence is destroyed;
- creating a distinct reputation/history because the protagonist solved both the physical and perceptual pressure without using Menma's seal route.

Possible reward/consequence families:

- living witness/custody;
- clean testimony or observer-specific evidence;
- counter-Genjutsu Knowledge;
- relationship/reputation changes;
- future investigation/contact eligibility;
- development evidence.

Not guaranteed loot.

## 7. Cross-discipline family — Ninjutsu / Bukijutsu

Again, this is an affordance family, not a universal build template.

### Ninjutsu-facing affordances

Only exact learned/available Ninjutsu can justify corresponding physical effects.

Potential outcomes include:

- changing terrain/environmental state;
- controlling visibility, water, fire, wind, earth or another environment only where an exact authorised technique does so;
- ranged denial of escape, signalling or infrastructure;
- isolating one part of a facility/system from another;
- creating safe/unsafe traversal geometry;
- protecting or exposing objects/participants through legitimate field manipulation.

`high Ninjutsu stat` alone does not invent an element or named technique.

### Bukijutsu-facing affordances

Potential outcomes include:

- precision tool/weapon interaction;
- disarming without the same consequences as lethal defeat;
- trap emplacement/disassembly where exact tools/capabilities support it;
- projectile/line-control solutions;
- recognising unusual construction or tactical weapon use;
- preserving weapon/tool provenance;
- recovering specialist equipment/materials safely;
- repurposing an authorised tool/object through exact system semantics.

Weapon expertise != ownership of every weapon/tool.

### Compound Ninjutsu + Bukijutsu examples

Where specifically authored, the pairing may support:

- shaping the battlefield so a weapon/tool route can isolate rather than destroy a target;
- cutting an escape or signalling line while preserving evidence elsewhere;
- exposing a hidden structural weakness, then using exact equipment to breach/recover safely;
- controlling destructive geometry so specialist gear/material survives;
- creating a different Battle-to-Story result because field control preserved infrastructure, evidence or custody.

Possible consequence/reward families:

- equipment/material provenance;
- alternate physical access;
- transformed location state;
- preserved infrastructure;
- future crafting/weapon lead;
- different custody/escape outcome;
- development evidence.

- forensic medical evidence;
- preserving a living source;
- determining that a condition requires expertise the protagonist does not have;
- relationship/service consequences from treatment.

Medicine does not imply resurrection, automatic cure, universal diagnosis or Progression reward.

Potential outcomes:

- reconstruct route/order/timing;
- distinguish false trail from physical trail;
- identify where pursuit became unreliable;
- predict plausible movement corridors from evidence;
- preserve trace evidence;
- create or prevent pursuit.

Tracking does not automatically reveal identity, allegiance or exact destination.

- distinguish number/direction/pattern where exact capability permits;
- notice suppression, absence or contradiction as evidence;
- support safer route selection;

Potential outcomes:

- preserve surprise;

- create false/ambiguous route evidence where exact capability supports it;

Potential outcomes:

- duty/obligation;

### Clan / Family / Service History

Knowledge alone does not grant physical Access, competence or power.

Potential outcomes include exact seal/barrier/forbidden-technique recognition, isolation, preservation, controlled interaction, risk identification, countermeasure opportunity and evidence/provenance where owning capability authority supports them.

High Stats alone do not invent named techniques, Mastery or universal bypass.

## 9. Non-convergent fairness

Capability-responsive Story does not require equal rewards in every individual event.

The design target is **campaign-level meaningfulness**, not per-scene symmetry.

A Taijutsu specialist may save a person another build loses.
A medical specialist may preserve evidence another build cannot interpret.
A tracker may prevent a future ambush.
A Fūinjutsu specialist may preserve an object no one else can safely retain.
A political route may avoid the Battle entirely but create a costly obligation.
A baseline route may preserve anonymity or flexibility the specialist route sacrifices.

Therefore:

> **Comparable significance does not require identical reward value or identical reward class.**

Do not secretly force all paths back into the same inventory/state merely for downstream scripting convenience.

If later Story truly requires a universal invariant, author the invariant at the correct abstraction level rather than forcing identical causal outcomes.

Example:

Bad invariant:
`every Origin must obtain Menma's chamber`.

Better invariant:
`the protagonist exits the mission with enough legitimate evidence/pressure/history for the next authored campaign beat`, while the exact evidence, relationships, custody, objects, losses or obligations may differ.

## 10. Authoring test for major Story problems

When Writing/World authors a major pressure, ask:

1. What facts are present regardless of protagonist build?
2. What baseline actions are legitimate?
3. Which exact current capabilities/history could create additional affordances?
4. Are those affordances route-level, occurrence-level, compound or history-generated?
5. What owning resolver decides success/failure?
6. If successful, which factual outcome family changes?
7. What cost/risk or opportunity tradeoff may accompany it?
8. Does the result create real Chronicle divergence rather than dialogue reskinning?
9. Does any reward/development consequence require another owner?
10. Can the next Story invariant consume multiple legitimate histories rather than demanding one universal prize?

If the answer to #8 is no, the route is cosmetic rather than capability-responsive.

## 12. Ownership boundaries

### Writing

Owns:

- campaign/mission dramatic function;
- factual Story pressure/invariants;
- authored branch space where Story-specific;
- voice and presentation anchors;
- Story consequence meaning.

Writing does not grant permanent capability/reward truth outside owner authority.

### World / Missions / Events

Owns:

- factual opportunities/events/locations/objects/participants;
- occurrence/route eligibility;
- recurrence/expiry/suppression;
- opportunity ecology;
- World consequence envelope.

### Combat / Skills / Items / Weapons

Owns:

- exact Skill/technique effect;
- legal targets/compatibility;
- Battle mechanics;
- exact weapon/item/equipment mechanics;
- capability semantics used by authored affordances.

High Stat != invented technique.

### Progression / Development

Owns:

- learned/developed Access;
- evidence consumption;
- development gates;
- Mastery/evolution semantics.

Meaningful Story use may create evidence/opportunity; it does not directly self-authorise development.

### Rewards

Owns exact reward eligibility/channel/value where a factual Story outcome legitimately creates reward entitlement.

Different outcome family may support different reward family.

Reward evaluation consumes committed history and must not reroll from presentation.

### Acquisition / Registry / Rank / other owners

Continue to own their existing domain truths. A Story route cannot manufacture ownership, identity, Rank, Promotion or candidate truth.

### CE / Codex / Coordination

Owns reusable semantic boundary/reconciliation, cross-system collision resolution and provenance expectations.

### Coding

Implements generic evaluation/persistence/projection only after exact semantics are closed.

No new bespoke capability-responsive Story engine is authorised by this contract.

- specialist eligibility != specialist success;
- specialist route != superior route;
- high Stat != named technique;
- different route != cosmetic dialogue;
- different outcome != reskinned same loot;
- one Character's earned outcome != universal campaign canon;

- Story outcome != automatic Progression;
- mission completion != reward identity;

- selection/randomness != eligibility creation;

> **A Chronicle should remember both what the player chose and what their actual history made possible when the choice arrived.**
