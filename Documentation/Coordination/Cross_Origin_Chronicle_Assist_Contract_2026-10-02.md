# 1. Definition

Shinobi Chronicles uses the term:

> **Cross-Origin Chronicle Assist**

for a committed interaction in which one Character's legitimately earned Chronicle capability/history creates an alternate, accelerated, joint or discovery path inside another Character's already-existing unresolved Chronicle thread.

It does not transfer route ownership, erase prior history or grant either Character the other's unique progression.

Canonical:

> **Cross-Origin Chronicle Assist lets histories help each other without becoming each other.**

---

# 2. Primary Chronicle Owner

The **Primary Chronicle Owner** is the Character whose unresolved thread/object/opportunity already exists.

The Primary Owner retains:
- ownership of the original thread/history;
- its prior decisions/custody/provenance;
- any route-specific identity already earned;
- later owner-exclusive progression unless a separate authored transition says otherwise.

---

# 3. Assistant Character

The **Assistant Character** is the exact Character whose independently earned capability/history makes the assisted route possible.

The Assistant retains ownership of:
- their Skill/capability;
- their signature-route stage;
- their source relationships;
- their private acquisition/progression history.

Assistance does not clone those states into the Primary Owner.

---

# 4. Eligibility

An assist is eligible only from current authoritative facts.

Possible predicates include:
- legitimate participant presence/availability;
- exact unresolved thread/object/state still exists;
- exact required assistant capability/stage exists now;
- required Knowledge/disclosure has occurred;
- Primary Owner consent/access where the authored situation requires it;
- correct object custody/location/world state;
- no later occurrence has closed/destroyed/superseded the target;
- no conflicting current state makes the attempt impossible.

Hard guards:

> **Active teammate ≠ automatic eligibility.**

> **Same party ≠ same private Origin history.**

> **Knowledge of a problem ≠ permission to act on it.**

> **Capability possession ≠ contextual access.**

> **UI visibility ≠ semantic eligibility.**

---

# 5. Allowed outcome classes

## ACCELERATION

The same thread can legitimately progress earlier because the Assistant supplies a missing causal route.

Acceleration does not pretend skipped/missing history happened.

## ALTERNATE METHOD

The same problem/objective is approached through a different causal method supplied by the Assistant.

## JOINT RESOLUTION

A new outcome exists only because both Characters' histories meet.

Joint resolution may create new Shared History without merging the original routes.

## DISCOVERY / EVIDENCE

The Assistant makes an unresolved thread newly understandable or actionable without resolving it.

## FAILED / ADVERSE ASSIST

The assist may fail, partially succeed, worsen the state, expose a deeper problem or close one method while opening another.

Canonical:

> **Assist opportunity ≠ assist occurrence ≠ assist success.**

---

# 6. Persistence

A committed assist must preserve enough factual history to reconstruct why it happened.

At minimum where relevant retain:
- assist occurrence ID;
- Primary Owner stable ID;
- Assistant stable ID;
- unresolved thread/object/state ref;
- original parent/root occurrence refs;
- Assistant capability/source refs;
- disclosure/access/consent refs;
- participant/contributor roles;
- causal provenance;
- outcome/consequence refs;
- Shared History refs where an actual joint occurrence occurred;
- Knowledge/evidence refs;
- final unresolved/resolved state.

Later Story queries factual committed history rather than:
`crossoverComplete=true`.

---

# 7. Non-collapse

Preserve all of the following:

- assist != route ownership transfer;
- assist != Skill transfer;
- assist != Knowledge transfer by implication;
- assistant capability != Primary Owner capability;
- current teammate != automatic assist participant;
- acceleration != retroactive skipped history;
- assistance != guaranteed success;
- Shared History != private Origin-history merger;
- joint outcome != merged Character identity;
- contribution != equal causal responsibility;
- one Character's signature route remains unique.

---

# 8. Failure / refusal

Legitimate states include:
- Primary Owner refuses disclosure/access;
- Assistant refuses involvement;
- Assistant is absent/unavailable;
- Assistant lacks the exact required stage;
- target is no longer in compatible state;
- assist attempt fails;
- assist creates a worse/new problem;
- unresolved Story remains valid because no assist occurred.

Refusal/failure is history when a committed occurrence actually happened.

---

# 9. Progression / reward boundary

An actual assist may create exact evidence for either participant where the owning system authorises it.

Examples:
- Primary Owner gains discovery/actionability/progression evidence;
- Assistant gains contribution/adaptation/progression evidence;
- both gain factual Shared History;
- later Story/World opportunities become eligible.

Do not automatically grant:
- the other Character's route stage;
- the other Character's Skill;
- the other Character's private Knowledge;
- direct PL;
- generic bonus rewards;
- duplicated route rewards.

Progression/World/Combat/Relationship authorities decide their own consequences from the assist occurrence.

---

# 10. Knowledge boundary

Participants learn only what the actual assist/disclosure/evidence supports.

An observer may witness the assisted effect without knowing:
- the Assistant's private capability provenance;
- the Primary Owner's entire unresolved history;
- hidden route prerequisites;
- concealed source relationships.

Preserve:

> **World Truth ≠ participant Knowledge ≠ presentation.**

> **Observed assistance ≠ known capability provenance.**

---

# 11. Shared History

Shared History begins only when a real joint occurrence commits.

Being teammates with compatible routes does not itself create Shared History.

Relationship interpretation remains separate:

> **successful cooperation ≠ trust / friendship automatically.**
