# 1. Owner decision

Every authorised Academy Origin Character receives one real pre-convergence Origin-scope history in the Chronicle.

The selected Origin determines:

> **whose Origin the player directly inhabits, chooses and experiences**

not:

> **which Character is the only one who had a past.**

Therefore:

- selected Origin protagonist: player-experienced Origin history;
- other nine Origin-capable Academy Characters: autonomously resolved private Origin histories;
- all ten may enter Active Konoha carrying the exact subject-persistent consequences they legitimately earned;
- Team Formation does not create, reroll or rewrite those histories;
- other Characters' private histories do not become protagonist Knowledge merely because they become teammates.

Canonical lock:

> **Origin selection = player POV / agency selection, not exclusive historical existence.**

---

# 2. Cross-generation historical-scope preservation

Shinobi Chronicles Academy representations come from historically incompatible source contexts.

Therefore this refinement does **not** assert that all ten Origin prologues happened in one shared source-era Konoha.

Instead each Origin resolves inside its own declared Origin historical scope before Active-Konoha convergence.

Preserve:

> **all ten Origin histories committed in one Chronicle ≠ all ten Origin events occurred in one shared source-era world.**

> **private Origin scope ≠ Active Konoha shared world scope.**

> **cross-generation convergence ≠ retroactive shared Academy cohort.**

A Kakashi Origin participant, Kushina Origin participant and Mirai Origin participant may each be historically real within their own Origin scope without implying those source-era events were mutually contemporaneous.

# 5. Autonomous resolution is authored autonomy, not random completion

An unselected Character does not receive a generic default completion package.

Their Origin must resolve from the same authored causal space as the player-facing Origin.

Autonomous resolution consumes:

- exact Character voice/personality/values authority;
- exact Character Knowledge at that moment;
- authored choice eligibility;
- current Origin-scope facts;
- immediate objective/pressure;
- authored route constraints;
- ordinary Battle/runtime legality where Battle occurs.

Canonical:

> **autonomous resolution ≠ random branch picker.**

## 5.1 Intent selection

For each meaningful player-choice boundary, Writing supplies an autonomous-intent profile.

The resolver:

1. evaluates only currently legal/knowable choices;
2. rejects choices inconsistent with required Knowledge or state;
3. ranks remaining intent families using authored Character/context priority;
4. if more than one equally legitimate top-priority intent remains, a stable Chronicle seed may select among them;
5. commits the resulting intent once.

Randomness may choose among already-eligible equally plausible possibilities.

Randomness must never decide eligibility itself.

## 5.2 Battle

Where an Origin contains Battle, autonomous resolution should consume the same legal Battle package and Character AI/decision rules rather than granting victory by fiat.

A hidden/headless Battle simulation may be used.

Any permitted stochastic Battle resolution must be stably seeded and committed once.

Failure, defeat, withdrawal and partial success remain legitimate history.

## 5.3 No best-route cheating

Autonomous Characters do not automatically choose the route with:

- highest rewards;
- best Stats;
- strongest item;
- rarest consequence;
- most future opportunities.

Their route is Character/context driven.

# 6. Carry-forward classes

Autonomous Origin completion does not mean every reward-screen entitlement is dumped into the player's account.

Carry-forward is classified by semantic ownership.

## 6.1 ALWAYS carry when legitimately committed — subject-persistent state

Examples:

- Discipline Development EXP;
- Current Stat changes;
- derived Current PL;
- legitimately learned Skill/Technique access;
- persistent Character conditions/injuries where authored;
- subject Knowledge/Memory;
- relationship/shared-history evidence belonging to that Character;
- qualifications/evidence;
- mentor/trainer interest directed at that Character;
- personal source/hosted-entity relationship state;
- representation/unlock eligibility belonging to that same persistent person;
- other exact actor-local persistent consequences.

Canonical:

> **the Character brings what happened to the Character.**

## 6.2 CONDITIONAL carry — personal material state

Items, Weapons, Equipment, Summons, materials or other objects carry only when the existing source/ownership contract establishes that the exact Character personally owns/carries that object across convergence.

A reward-screen entitlement alone is not enough.

A source-era object does not enter Active Konoha merely because it existed in an Origin.

## 6.3 DO NOT auto-carry — player/Chronicle economy entitlements

Autonomous private Origins do not multiply player-onboarding economy.

Specifically:

- `origin_completion_starting_purse_ryo_01` is committed once for the player-experienced Origin / new-Chronicle economy transition;
- autonomous Origin completion does not add another 100 Ryō purse;
- Battle cash defined as a **player victory** reward does not trigger from a hidden autonomous Battle;
- player-owned Inventory rewards do not auto-duplicate from autonomous Origins;
- meta/UI reward entitlement ≠ Character-local property.

If a future World/Rewards contract explicitly authors character-local wages/property from an autonomous occurrence, that exact property may carry under its own ownership rules.

Canonical:

> **private history persistence ≠ nine bonus onboarding purses.**

# 12. History revelation after convergence

Private history may become protagonist Knowledge through legitimate current events.

Possible channels:

- the teammate voluntarily tells the protagonist;
- another participant recognises/reacts to them;
- documents/records are discovered;
- a current event reproduces a capability or consequence;
- an institution references prior history;
- the protagonist investigates;
- another witness contradicts/clarifies prior information.

Disclosure itself is a new Active-Konoha occurrence.

A Character may:

- disclose fully;
- disclose partially;
- evade;
- refuse;
- lie;
- misunderstand;
- remain silent.

Writing/participant autonomy owns exact expression.
