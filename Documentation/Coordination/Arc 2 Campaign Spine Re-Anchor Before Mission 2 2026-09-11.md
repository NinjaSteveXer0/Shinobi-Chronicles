## 1. Why this re-anchor exists

Writing correctly stopped Arc 2 Mission 2 because the developer Menma Chronicle was beginning to dominate the universal campaign spine.

The drift symptom was not that Echo-specific material is bad. The problem was that a valid **Chronicle-specific Menma/Echo consequence** was beginning to become the **subject of Arc 2 itself**.

That would contradict current binding authority:

- `Documentation/Coordination/Structured Story Autonomy Arc 1-3 Route History and Authored Geography Contract 2026-09-10.md`
- commit `60b9b298ab2c44a01f5508925f7a68302ec7566e`

Canonical boundary remains:

> **Story says what the campaign must confront. CE proves why this Chronicle reaches it this way.**

Menma/Echo is one high-pressure causal carrier. It is not the campaign premise other Origins must imitate.

## 2. What Arc 2 is fundamentally about

Arc 2 is about **the struggle to control the political meaning of what Arc 1 exposed**.

Arc 1 proves that Konoha can be penetrated, manipulated, compromised, observed, deceived or forced into dangerous responses.

Arc 2 asks a harder question:

> **Who gets to define what those failures mean, who is responsible, what Konoha should do about them, and who can still be trusted with authority when information itself has become a weapon?**

Therefore `Narrative Warfare / Political Destabilisation / Cat-and-Mouse` means more than leaks or rumours.

Arc 2 must show adversarial actors using **truth, partial truth, falsehood, secrecy, compromised authority, legitimate grievance, forged authority, selective disclosure, institutional reaction and public/private interpretation** as operational weapons.

The political attack is not merely "someone says bad things about Konoha."

The attack is:

**information enters the system → institutions/people react → the reaction reveals priorities, vulnerabilities, loyalties, procedures and fractures → hostile or competing actors exploit that reaction → Konoha moves again under greater uncertainty.**

This makes the Arc a cat-and-mouse campaign because both sides increasingly learn from how the other side responds.

The protagonist is not simply hunting a leak. The protagonist is operating inside a contest over **legitimacy, trust, interpretation and control of response**.

## 3. The Arc-2 causal destination: why Arc 3 can become civil war

Arc 3's Minato-vs-Sasuke fracture must emerge from accumulated Arc-2 history rather than appearing because Story announces a civil war.

The required Arc-2 political trajectory is:

### Early Arc 2 — confidence becomes uncertainty

By the end of the early Konoha/Land-of-Fire phase, the protagonist and the political board should understand through lived events that:

- official-looking information/orders/channels cannot always be accepted at face value;
- different institutions and actors possess only partial Knowledge;
- legitimate authority can be forged, compromised, selectively obeyed or weaponised;
- Konoha's attempts to respond can themselves be observed and exploited;
- multiple actors may manipulate the same crisis for different reasons;
- protecting secrecy and exposing truth can both create real costs;
- the Arc-1 aftermath has become a political problem, not merely an unresolved investigation.

This phase should create **doubt about process and competence**, not immediately declare Minato wrong or Sasuke right.

### Middle Arc 2 — the problem becomes larger than Konoha

The investigation must prove that Konoha cannot understand or contain the crisis using only internal assumptions and institutions.

The authored expansion into **Kusagakure / Land of Grass** and **Amegakure / Land of Rain** must reveal that:

- foreign actors, routes, financing, witnesses, operatives, criminal networks, political interests or intelligence structures materially intersect the crisis;
- Konoha's internal political narratives look different when viewed from outside Fire Country;
- people outside Konoha possess interests and Knowledge Konoha cannot simply command;
- the protagonist must build or damage foreign relationships, obligations, enemies, evidence chains and credibility;
- institutional protection/resupply becomes less reliable and deliberate preparation/development matters more.

Kusa and Ame are not sightseeing detours. They expand the political evidence base that will later make Arc-3 alliances, isolation, betrayal and outside support causally meaningful.

### Late Arc 2 — competing answers become politically credible

By late Arc 2, the crisis should no longer be reducible to "find the bad operative."

The Chronicle should have accumulated enough public/private evidence, institutional failures, successful interventions, hidden costs, allies, enemies and contradictory loyalties that **different political conclusions can be sincerely credible**.

This is where Sasuke's rise to substantial support becomes causally earned.

Sasuke's support must not mean everyone suddenly shares one ideology. Different participants may support him because they believe, for example, that:

- Minato's administration failed to secure compromised systems;
- information was withheld too long;
- too much authority remains fragmented;
- current institutions protected the wrong people;
- current institutions exposed the wrong people;
- Sasuke demonstrated competence, protection, clarity, force, accountability or an alternative institutional coalition;
- their own Chronicle-relative relationships/history make his position more credible than Minato's.

Likewise, support for Minato may arise from many different histories and does not automatically mean Reform.

The Arc-3 fracture is earned when enough participants conclude that **the dispute is no longer about one incident; it is about what kind of authority can legitimately govern Konoha after repeated proof that the existing political-information order is contested.**

## 4. Universal Arc-2 escalation functions

Writing should author these **functions**, not one universal protagonist-specific causal carrier.

A valid Arc-2 mission may satisfy several at once, but the Arc as a whole must cover them.

### Function A — Reactivate Chronicle-earned leverage

Take a real Arc-1 consequence and make it politically/operationally relevant now.

Possible carrier families include evidence, survivor, secret, compromised institution, relationship, clan grievance, protected target, mission disobedience, foreign link, reputation, capability whose existence is legitimately known, or another committed consequence.

Menma/Echo is one possible carrier only.

### Function B — Demonstrate adversarial information control

Show that an opponent or competing actor can alter outcomes by controlling what Konoha believes, when it learns it, who receives it, or how it interprets it.

This may use true information, false information, partial information, forged authority, selective disclosure, destroyed evidence, false trails, Genjutsu, chakra masking, suppression tags or other legitimate ninja tradecraft.

### Function C — Make Konoha react visibly

Konoha must move people/resources/authority in response.

Multiple teams, offices, clans or individuals may react differently from their own Knowledge and obligations.

Their movement is part of the information war because reactions reveal structure and priorities.

### Function D — Prove that the reaction itself is being exploited

At least some Arc-2 pressure should show enemies/competitors learning from, ambushing, redirecting, discrediting or otherwise using Konoha's response.

This is the key cat-and-mouse escalation: **the enemy is not merely hiding; it is studying the search.**

### Function E — Create real tactical cost

Arc 2 must remain ninja conflict, not become committee-room exposition.

Use infiltration, pursuit, ambush, counter-surveillance, deception, evidence recovery/destruction, protection, capture, escape and real PL Battles where authored.

Combat remains consequence resolution, not a substitute for political causality.

### Function F — Fracture confidence without declaring allegiance

Events should create evidence that can support different later political interpretations.

Do not label choices `Reform`, `Dominion`, `Rogue`, `Take the Throne` or `Non-Conformance` in Story semantics.

Let actions create provenance-bearing history from which later political credibility emerges.

### Function G — Expand the causal map outward

Early internal pressure must eventually expose a legitimate route to the fixed Kusa/Ame expansion.

Different Chronicles may reach those destinations through different carriers, but the campaign expansion itself is mandatory.

### Function H — Manufacture later-Arc political inputs through history, not points

Arc 2 must leave durable:

- Knowledge and misinformation history;
- trust/distrust;
- debts/obligations;
- protected/exposed people;
- evidence state;
- institutional credibility damage/gains;
- foreign contacts/allies/enemies;
- faction/clan relationships;
- public/private political acts and their factual provenance;
- unresolved opportunities and suppressed alternatives.

These are Arc-3 inputs. There is no canonical hidden route score.

## 5. Role of the early Konoha / Land-of-Fire information-war missions

The early missions should teach and prove the **operational grammar of Arc 2** before the campaign leaves Konoha.

Their job is to establish that:

1. Arc 1 created leverage someone can weaponise;
2. Konoha is receiving multiple, imperfect information streams;
3. apparently legitimate authority may be compromised or forged;
4. different Konoha participants respond independently rather than as one mind;
5. the protagonist must decide which factual pressure point to pursue from their own Chronicle;
6. hostile/competing actors can predict or observe Konoha's response;
7. investigation produces tactical exposure and real danger;
8. no single discovered operative automatically explains the entire political campaign.

Early Arc 2 is therefore not "solve the Echo mystery again."

It is the phase where the player learns:

> **Someone can attack Konoha by making Konoha choose where to look, whom to trust, what to hide, what to reveal and where to move.**

Once that grammar is understood, Kusa/Ame expansion reveals that the contest has external infrastructure, actors and consequences beyond Konoha's direct control.

## 6. Mission 2 — exact Arc-level purpose

Mission 2 should accomplish one major campaign proof:

> **The protagonist proves that the Arc-2 adversarial field is not merely leaking information; it is shaping Konoha's response and exploiting the movement that response creates.**

Mission 2 should therefore establish, through the factual route actually played, a combination of the following:

- multiple information streams or competing leads exist;
- at least one legitimate-looking order/channel/source is forged, compromised, selectively manipulated or otherwise unsafe to interpret naively;
- multiple Konoha teams/participants move independently from their own information and duties;
- the protagonist follows one **Chronicle-earned pressure point**, not a universal Menma/Echo hook;
- adversarial tradecraft demonstrates anticipation of Konoha movement;
- a real ambush / interception / PL Battle proves the enemy is operationally prepared around the response;
- aftermath evidence supports the conclusion that the reaction was being observed/modelled/exploited;
- the mission ends with a next causal lead that advances the political-information campaign rather than collapsing back into the protagonist-specific carrier.

### What can remain from current Mission-2 material

The current structural ingredients from #101 are compatible with the Arc spine:

- multiple leaks/information streams;
- compromised/forged legitimate authority causing Konoha movement;
- autonomous multiple-team response;
- one Chronicle-specific pressure point followed by the protagonist;
- enemy observation of Konoha's reaction;
- material Genjutsu/perception/chakra-masking/suppression/false-trail/evidence-destruction tradecraft;
- real PL Battle(s);
- silent-street / ambush-positioning sequence where it remains causally earned;
- a witness providing bounded information rather than omniscient exposition.

### Menma-specific Echo material

A former-host stranger, Echo survival history, or `I can show you where mine died` may remain **conditional Chronicle-specific content** if Menma's actual history makes it legitimate.

It must not:

- define the universal Mission-2 premise;
- become the mandatory Arc-2 destination;
- imply Echo experimentation is the explanation for every participant;
- force non-Echo protagonists to receive cosmetic substitute Echo plots;
- consume enough mission weight that the information-war proof above disappears.

Use a simple authoring test:

> **If the Menma/Echo thread were removed and replaced by another legitimate Chronicle-earned pressure carrier, would Mission 2 still prove that hostile actors can shape and exploit Konoha's response?**

If yes, the thread is serving the mission.

If no, the thread has hijacked the mission.

## 7. Five-route compatibility

Arc 2 must create history that can later support all five route families without declaring one now:

1. **Reform** — evidence that institutions can be repaired/accountable and relationships capable of supporting that effort;
2. **Dominion** — evidence that fragmented authority/information can create severe danger and that stronger control may appear credible;
3. **Rogue** — evidence that institutions may claim, expose, manipulate or endanger people/secrets in ways a protagonist may later reject;
4. **Take the Throne** — evidence that current leadership/system may prove incapable enough that replacement becomes a credible earned conclusion;
5. **Non-Conformance** — unusual relationships, foreign alliances, leverage, Knowledge or compound history that can eventually support a solution not captured by the expected trajectories.

These are not five required choices per mission.

A single occurrence may legitimately contribute evidence relevant to more than one later interpretation.

## 8. Current authority being neglected / contradicted

### A. Menma/Echo is not universal Arc-2 semantics

This was already binding under the Structured Story Autonomy contract and explicitly acknowledged by Writing in #93.

Current Mission-2 drift toward Echo-as-subject contradicts that authority and should be corrected now.

### B. Kusa and Ame are mandatory authored Arc-2 destinations

Arc 2 cannot spend so much narrative weight re-investigating Echo history inside Konoha that it loses the required outward expansion.

The exact causal bridge varies by Chronicle; the destinations do not.

### C. Arc 2 must create Arc-3 political/foreign inputs

Interesting self-contained mysteries are insufficient if they do not leave relationships, evidence, obligations, enemies, political credibility changes or foreign ties that can matter in Arc 3.

### D. Minato vs Sasuke is no longer OPEN

The older `Documentation/Story/Arc2_Forward_Continuity_and_Divergence_Locks_2026-09-09.md` contains a section `Canon / Alt Hokage Rival Question — OPEN`.

That specific uncertainty is **superseded** by the newer binding Structured Story Autonomy contract.

Current planning authority fixes the Arc-3 headline political fracture as:

- current Hokage **Minato Namikaze**;
- challenger **Sasuke Uchiha**, who gains substantial support through Arc 2.

Writing should no longer treat Naruto/Sakura/other alternatives as co-equal unresolved headline-rival candidates for this Arc-3 spine unless new explicit authority reopens the decision.

### E. Older Menma-specific route wording is not universal route semantics

The 2026-09-09 forward-continuity document describes Rogue and Take-the-Throne using Menma-specific wording.

The newer CE contract generalises those into five full Chronicle route families.

Menma-specific phrasing remains usable for the developer Chronicle only; it is not universal route definition.

### F. Uchiha, Sarutobi, teams and families are not monoliths

Existing forward-continuity authority already says the Uchiha are politically plural and Sarutobi grievance currents do not make the clan a single hostile bloc.

Likewise team/family/mentor ties do not determine allegiance.

Arc 2 should create reasons for individuals/factions to move politically, not assign them because of surname or relationship.

### G. Madara should not be spent as the Arc-3 political route leader

Existing forward-continuity direction and the newer Structured Story contract both preserve the current generation as the political conflict layer.

Madara remains a later-game/endgame force, not the required Dominion-side Arc-3 counterpart to Sasuke/Minato.

### H. Arc-2 danger escalates materially from Mission 4 onward

Existing Writing authority says Missions 1–3 may remain Konoha-centred, while Mission 4 onward should become more dangerous, less institutionally controlled, and increasingly dependent on preparation, contacts, equipment/crafting/development.

Do not let an extended early information-war phase flatten that escalation or keep the whole Arc administratively safe inside Konoha.

## 9. Authoring test for every Arc-2 mission

Before expanding a cool Chronicle-specific thread, Writing should be able to answer:

1. **What universal Arc function does this mission perform?**
2. **What committed Chronicle history is allowed to carry that function?**
3. **What can adversaries/participants legitimately know?**
4. **What concrete political/institutional/tactical pressure changes because of this mission?**
5. **What durable history can later matter in Kusa/Ame or Arc 3?**
6. **If the developer Menma-specific carrier were swapped out, would the mission still perform the same Arc function?**

If question 6 is no, the protagonist-specific content is defining the Arc rather than realising it.

## 10. Immediate Writing direction

Writing may resume Mission 2.

Do **not** throw away the good current material. Reframe it around the Mission-2 Arc proof:

> **Konoha's response is being shaped, observed and exploited.**

Keep the witness/stranger bounded, keep multiple streams, keep independent Konoha movement, keep ninja tradecraft and Battle pressure, and keep Chronicle-specific optional follow-up where legitimate.

But the universal mission must leave the player with a stronger understanding of the **information war**, not a larger catalogue of Echo experiments.

The next missions should then escalate from internal response-manipulation toward wider political consequences and the eventual authored Kusa/Ame expansion.

No generic CE runtime implementation or new Coding tranche is authorised by this re-anchor alone.
