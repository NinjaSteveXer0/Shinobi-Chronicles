# Shinobi Chronicles — Rogue / Akatsuki / Jinchūriki Later-Difficulty + Full Side-Story Convergence Concept

Date: 2026-10-01
Owner direction: Stephen
Coordination: CE / Codex / Coordination
Status: POST-ALPHA / BETA+ CONCEPT RECORD — NOT AN ALPHA BLOCKER

## 1. Purpose

Record Stephen's later-game direction without reviving obsolete Path of Shadows implementation by inference.

Historical `Path of Shadows -> Akatsuki/premium progression` remains genealogy only.

This document is a new concept record and must be re-ratified by exact owners before implementation.

## 2. Rogue Chronicle branch

The Arc-3 five-path structure includes a Rogue direction.

Owner direction:
- a Rogue / Missing-nin style Chronicle may make ordinary village Promotion examinations irrelevant to the protagonist's current career path;
- institutional Rank/Promotion is therefore not the only meaningful late-game advancement model;
- Rogue status, faction status, reputation, notoriety, role and capability progression must remain separate from formal village Rank.

Do not infer:
`Rogue path -> automatic Akatsuki membership`.

Akatsuki access requires its own legitimate Story/faction opportunity.

## 3. Akatsuki later difficulty — retained concept

Stephen's original product vision included an Akatsuki difficulty with its own full Story.

Two future start families are preserved conceptually:
1. select an authorised Akatsuki Character Origin;
2. select one eligible Inheritance/Legacy Character and create a new Chronicle in which that Character legitimately joins / attempts to join the Akatsuki.

The second route must preserve stable person identity and inherited provenance. It must not fabricate an `Akatsuki version` duplicate merely because the route changes faction context.

Akatsuki membership != formal village Rank.

## 4. Jinchūriki later difficulty — retained concept

Stephen's original product vision also included a Jinchūriki difficulty with its own full Story.

Two future start families are preserved conceptually:
1. select an authorised canon Jinchūriki Origin;
2. select one eligible Inheritance/Legacy Character plus one legitimately unlocked standalone Bijū / Tailed Beast source and enter a Jinchūriki-creation Story route.

Preserve:
- standalone Bijū card != Hosted Entity automatically;
- ownership/access != hosting;
- hosting requires an exact sealing/transplant/host transaction;
- exact source reservation remains binding;
- created Jinchūriki may produce non-canon but legitimate Chronicle combinations.

Illustrative future combinations such as Sakura + Chōmei, Sasuke + Matatabi, or Tobirama + Shukaku require exact source compatibility and route authority. They are examples, not automatic pair permissions.

## 5. Legacy accumulation payoff

Stephen's desired long-horizon payoff is that repeated completed Chronicles may unlock additional standalone Bijū opportunities, allowing later Jinchūriki-difficulty teams to contain several player-created non-canon Jinchūriki.

Existing CE/Jinchūriki design already records a candidate cadence of one Bijū opportunity per five Legacy playthroughs. That numeric cadence remains NOT LOCKED until future Legacy/Acquisition authority closes it.

## 6. Full side-story convergence

Owner direction:

The Akatsuki and Jinchūriki Story families should not exist only as isolated difficulty-exclusive content.

Future standard Chronicles may expose **full-length optional side-story arcs** derived from those Story families when exact eligibility is satisfied.

These are not tiny missions.

They should be substantial playable Story lines with multiple scenes, World activity, CE decisions, Battles, consequences, relationships and Chronicle history.

Possible high-end routes:
- Rogue Chronicle -> Akatsuki contact / recruitment / infiltration / opposition Story;
- eligible hosted-source Chronicle -> Jinchūriki creation / control / faction response Story;
- other exact Story intersections authorised later.

Difficulty mode remains distinct from side-story access.

`side-story access != difficulty completion`
`difficulty selection != side-story occurrence`.

## 7. High-end team capability gate

Stephen proposes that these optional full side stories require an absurd-but-attainable **combined team capability** so they function as true endgame content.

Do not implement this as an Alpha Battle-System change.

Future PL / Registry / Rank authority must decide whether this is represented as:
- combined team PL;
- formation/team power;
- deployment readiness score;
- another derived team-capability measure.

The current individual Battle PL circles remain untouched for Alpha.

A future combined team-capability gate should not be the sole eligibility criterion.

High power cannot replace:
- legitimate Story/faction access;
- current location;
- Knowledge;
- source ownership/hosting eligibility;
- Chronicle route/history;
- faction willingness;
- exact current participants.

Canonical direction:
`high team capability + legitimate Chronicle route -> high-end Story eligibility`
not
`high PL alone -> Akatsuki/Jinchūriki Story`.

## 8. Promotion bypass without progression collapse

A Rogue Chronicle may stop consuming ordinary village Promotion exams while still accumulating:
- persistent development;
- Skills;
- equipment/provenance;
- relationships;
- faction history;
- rogue reputation/notoriety;
- CE opportunities;
- Battle capability;
- specialist competence.

Do not manufacture a village Rank merely to represent advancement.

Future Rogue/Akatsuki progression may use separate faction/status/role recognition authority.

## 9. Story reuse without identical playthroughs

Difficulty and side-story modes may share authored Story skeletons, locations, factions and encounter families while CE produces Chronicle-relative realisations.

Do not copy one static script into four routes.

Expected future distinction:
- Akatsuki Character Origin route;
- inherited/Legacy Akatsuki entrant route;
- canon Jinchūriki Origin route;
- inherited/Legacy created-Jinchūriki route.

Shared bones are allowed; participant history, access, source state and consequences should materially alter the playable Story.

## 10. Alpha firewall

None of this blocks current Phase-2 Alpha work.

Do not ask Coding to implement:
- Akatsuki difficulty;
- Jinchūriki difficulty;
- Rogue faction progression;
- combined-team PL;
- full side-story convergence;
- Legacy Bijū cadence;
until Alpha's ordinary Konoha / progression / economy / Main Story loop is stable and the relevant owners deliberately activate the later tranche.

## Final record

> Later Shinobi Chronicles can support both dedicated Akatsuki/Jinchūriki difficulties and full optional high-end Story arcs inside ordinary Chronicles. Rogue progression may legitimately diverge from village Promotion. Inherited/Legacy Characters and standalone Bijū sources create personalised entry routes, but every faction/host state remains causally earned. Future combined-team capability may gate endgame Story access without rewriting the Alpha Battle System.
