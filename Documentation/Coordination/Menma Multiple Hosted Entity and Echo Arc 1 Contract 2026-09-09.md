This document adds only the multiplicity / relationship semantics needed by Arc 1.

## 2. Multiple Hosted Entities are permitted when separately authored

A host Character may maintain more than one simultaneous Hosted Entity relationship when Story / World / Registry authority establishes each relationship independently.

Canonical rule:

> **one host ≠ one universal Hosted Entity slot**

This does not create an unlimited generic inventory mechanic. Every Hosted Entity relationship remains separately source-addressed, causally justified and subject to its own attachment / access / cooperation / manifestation / removal state.

For current Arc 1 Menma:

- Menma remains one persistent Character/person;
- Kurama remains a distinct persistent Entity/being under its existing authority;
- the Arc 1 Echo is a second distinct persistent Hosted Entity;
- Kurama and the Echo may simultaneously be hosted by Menma because Arc 1 Story establishes that coexistence;
- neither Hosted Entity becomes part of the other's identity merely because they share a host.

Preserve:

> **shared host ≠ merged Entity identity**

> **co-hosting ≠ fusion**

> **co-hosting ≠ ownership**

> **co-hosting ≠ mastery**

---

## 3. Hosted relationship state is separate from Entity identity

The Echo requires one stable Entity identity. Its changing relationship with Menma is represented through relationship / occurrence / capability state rather than by replacing the Entity with a new person each time.

At minimum the Arc 1 history distinguishes:

1. **transfer candidate / unattached**;
2. **attached / hosted presence**;
3. **Recall refusal**;
4. **voluntary manifestation**;
5. **reciprocal cooperation established**;
6. **Echo Menma cooperative representation unlocked**.

These states do not imply that the Echo itself is recreated as a different historical Entity.

Likewise:

> **Hosted Entity identity ≠ host representation state**

> **relationship development ≠ Registry identity mutation**

> **manifestation state ≠ acquisition of a second person**

## 4. Echo naming

The programme/class term is:

**Echo**

The individual Hosted Entity receives a Chronicle-relative personal name during the Mission 12 wind-down.

The personal name is historical/player-relative identity metadata for this same Entity. Runtime addressing must use a stable Registry/source identity independent of the player's chosen display name.

Therefore:

> **player-defined personal name ≠ stable Registry ID**

> **programme class ≠ personal identity**

> **personal name change/display ≠ new Entity**

PL / Registry / Rank must assign the exact stable production ID and may expose a player-facing personal-name field separately.

## 5. Menma representation split

Arc 1 now requires the following non-collapsed Menma representations/states:

- **Base Genin Menma** — no Chronicle-specific Identity Rebinding body seal implied;

- **Genin Menma — Echo + Borrowed Kurama Chakra** — temporary climax Battle representation/state only unless separate future authority later admits a permanent representation.

Preserve:

> **Echo Menma ≠ Cipher Menma**

> **Echo Menma ≠ permanent Kurama access**

> **temporary borrowed Kurama power ≠ permanent combined unlock**

## 6. Echo manifestation / participation boundary

Arc 1 establishes that the Echo may voluntarily externalise a partial visible manifestation while most of the Entity remains threaded through Menma's chakra system.

That manifestation does not automatically create:

- a separate team/deployment slot;
- an ordinary Summon lifecycle;
- a recurring independent Battle turn;
- a second additive PL ledger;
- full-body separation from Menma;
- ownership/mastery.

A later Combat package may author exact action opportunities or cooperative effects. Those effects must be source-owned and may not be inferred merely from `Hosted Entity` classification or visual manifestation.

Canonical rule:

> **visible Hosted manifestation ≠ independent Battle participant by default**

---

## 7. Kurama loan boundary

Mission 12 establishes one historical occurrence in which Kurama voluntarily lends Menma limited power while the Echo remains cooperatively active.

This occurrence proves only that:

- Kurama chose to lend power in that exact situation;
- Menma remained decision-maker;
- the Echo remained a distinct participant and adapted/routed around the increased pressure;
- all three causal participants remained distinct.

It does not automatically grant:

- persistent Kurama access;
- permanent dual-host mastery;
- a permanent combined card unlock;
- ownership of Kurama power;
- universal permission to repeat the state.

Progression / Skills / Combat own any future repeatability prerequisites.

---

## 8. Knowledge boundary

Menma's teammates may legitimately observe multiple chakra signatures / presences during Arc 1.

That observation does not itself reveal that Kurama is the Nine-Tails/Fox.

Preserve:

> **observed additional chakra signature ≠ identity Knowledge**

> **World Truth ≠ observer Knowledge ≠ presentation**
