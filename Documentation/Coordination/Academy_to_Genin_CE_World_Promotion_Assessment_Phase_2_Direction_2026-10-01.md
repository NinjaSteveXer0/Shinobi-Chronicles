## Core correction

The current Field Readiness consumer is semantically useful but player-facing too panel/list driven.

Promotion becomes a real playable World/Hotspot/formal-mission occurrence entered through Arena -> Promotion.

Preserve Arena -> Promotion entry, explicit assessment subject, inspection != attempt commit, Battle victory != Promotion, mission completion != Promotion automatically, team participation != team-wide Promotion, hidden examiner interpretation, and success -> separate Genin roster transition.

## Stable assessment family, variable live scenario

Retain academy_to_genin_field_readiness_assessment as the institutional assessment family.

Add one stable assessmentScenarioId per committed attempt. The courier/dispatch mission becomes one authorised scenario skeleton rather than the only forever-script.

Future Academy-safe Promotion scenario pool may contain several equivalent formal field-readiness missions. Each must test the same Rank authority while permitting different World situations.

## Promotion enters the real world

Arena Promotion
-> subject committed
-> examiner briefing Story scene

-> multi-scene CE realisation

-> investigation/travel/Battle/other resolvers

The assessment is not primarily played on one administrative checklist page.

## Writing vs CE

Writing owns high-quality Promotion mission skeletons: premise, examiner/institution, mandatory mission anchors, geography, resolver seams, failure/abort conditions, durable outputs and tone/voice constraints.

CE owns live Chronicle-relative realisation from scenario skeleton + current subject + current team + participant Chronicles + Knowledge + relationships + development/capabilities + current World state.

CE derives due teammate intent, current decision space, legitimate route variation and which optional authored beats become eligible.

This is bounded dynamic Story, not unrestricted plot invention.

## Team participation

When team-mates are present, consume the global participant-first choice gate.

Before important protagonist choices, due teammates decide/react first; observer-safe stance is shown; then the player chooses their own intent.

Only assessmentSubjectOwnedCharacterId receives Promotion mutation.

## Hidden assessment

Do not expose examiner scores, readiness-domain counters, hidden requirement weights, pass probability, teamwork points, internal CE IDs or Rank diagnostics.

The player experiences situations; the examiner interprets committed evidence. Debrief may disclose observer-safe rationale after resolution.

## Strict pool safety

Every Rank-authored pool should declare rank transition, difficulty applicability, permitted evidence families, package breadth, mandatory anchors, mutually exclusive requirements, impossible-combination exclusions, scenario support tags, reveal condition, satisfaction resolver, retry persistence and supersession rules.

Academy -> Genin must never require already holding Genin+, Kage title, future arcs, impossible bosses, post-Genin-only ownership, inaccessible Bloodline/Skill, or arbitrary grind counts with no readiness meaning.

Randomness may select only among prevalidated compatible packages.

## Reveal model and Shinobi Record

Unknown slots remain ??????. A slot becomes readable only through legitimate discovery, examiner/institution disclosure, committed satisfaction/recording or another authorised Knowledge route.

A requirement may be satisfied before the player knew it existed.

Shinobi Record may track assessment available, known mission, current attempt/scenario, known objectives, discovered evidence/leads, revealed requirement slots, completed known requirements, previous attempts and examiner-disclosed rationale.

Do not expose hidden unsatisfied requirement identities.

## Multiple legitimate routes and multi-scene requirement

Scenario routes may include investigate, track, question, observe, split/stay together, delegate, protect, pursue, withdraw, evade, fight, recover, preserve objective, return early and ask for teammate input.

Minimum experiential shape: examiner briefing; initial field discovery; participant-first decision; consequence/changed situation; second meaningful problem/escalation; optional resolver seam; objective resolution; return/debrief; Rank result; Chronicle Receipt.

## Unsuccessful != null/failure

Preserve: Battle loss != mission failure; mission failure != zero Chronicle value; unsuccessful objective != erased attempt; different outcome != bad ending automatically.

All legitimate development, Knowledge, relationships, evidence and history survive.

## Retry / alternate assessment

After an unsuccessful attempt, player-facing options may include RETRY THIS ASSESSMENT, CHOOSE ANOTHER ASSESSMENT, or RETURN TO KONOHA / KEEP EXPLORING, subject to current Rank/World authority.

Retry is a new occurrence and must not duplicate one-shot consequences from the prior attempt.

## CE outcome and consequence model

After participant and protagonist intent commit, CE/owning systems resolve factual mission outcome, objective state, participant history, Knowledge, relationship/shared history, development/evidence, Rank evidence, future opportunities, reward entitlement and World changes.

CE does not seize Rank, Combat, Rewards, Progression or Acquisition ownership.

## Promotion reward pool

World / Missions / Events / Rewards authors strict eligible reward/consequence packages by scenario, factual outcome, participant contribution, Rank result, institutional source and Chronicle consequence.

CE may realise only already-eligible packages. No filler loot. No morality payout.

Unsuccessful attempts may still retain action-derived development, Knowledge, relationship/shared history, specialist evidence, discovered locations/leads or exact alternate material rewards where earned.

## Promotion Chronicle Receipt — mandatory

Every completed Promotion attempt, successful or not, ends with a read-only Promotion Chronicle Receipt/debrief generated from committed facts.

Where applicable it must show:
- YOUR DECISIONS
- TEAM / PARTICIPANT ACTIONS known to the protagonist
- WHAT HAPPENED
- PROMOTION RESULT
- HISTORY CREATED
- DEVELOPMENT / EVIDENCE that is player-visible
- REWARDS: exact Ryo, Items/materials/equipment, entitlements and other visible persistent rewards
- NEWLY ACTIONABLE: Genin roster transition, retry/alternate assessment, newly known training/contact/location/opportunity.

If a Promotion occurrence grants it and the player is entitled to know it, the Receipt must show it.

Do not repeat the Origin defect where rewards exist in state but disappear from the Chronicle Receipt.

Receipt is projection only. Opening/reopening it never recommits reward.

## Persistence

Persist attempt ID, scenario ID, requirement package ID if Rank ratifies it, teammate-autonomy receipts, player intents, outcomes, objective state, evidence, rewards, result and retry lineage.

UI reopen/save-load must not reroll scenario, hidden requirements, teammate intentions, rewards or examiner result.
