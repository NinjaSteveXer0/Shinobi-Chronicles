# Hokage Office / Chronicle Dispatch — No-Second-Owner Guard

**Date:** 2026-10-06  
**Source:** #533

The Step-6 Hokage Office implementation is a consumer/router over existing authority.

It must not become a second owner for:
- Missions/Main Story;
- Journey;
- Shinobi Record;
- Exams;
- Promotion;
- World Truth/world state;
- formal Rank;
- player identity;
- rumour issuance/discovery;
- hotspot eligibility/occurrence;
- economy/reputation state.

Chronicle Dispatch may persist presentation attention state only where explicitly needed. Presentation state never authors upstream Knowledge, Access, eligibility, occurrence or success.
