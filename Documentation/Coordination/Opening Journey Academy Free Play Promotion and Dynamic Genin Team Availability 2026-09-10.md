# 1. Canonical Alpha opening flow

The intended player journey begins:

`Choose Origin Character`

→ `complete that Origin's Prologue`

→ committed Active-Konoha entry boundary

→ `ACADEMY TEAM FORMATION`

→ choose exactly **two other eligible Academy Student teammates**

→ commit the three-person Academy squad

→ first Konoha map/tutorial continuation

→ **ordinary Academy free play becomes available**

→ player chooses whether to continue free play or attempt the Genin Promotion assessment

→ voluntary `academy_to_genin_field_readiness_assessment`

→ on successful Promotion, open separate `geninRosterTransition`

→ retain the existing Academy teammates or reform the squad from the currently legitimate Genin/Jōnin candidate state

→ commit the post-Promotion team / Jōnin leader assignment.

Preserve:

**Origin selection ≠ team formation**  
**Origin completion ≠ Promotion**  
**team formation ≠ Promotion**  
**free play ≠ mandatory delay before Promotion**  
**Promotion ≠ automatic team replacement**

# 2. Origin and Prologue

The player first chooses one authorised Origin Character.

The player completes that exact Origin's Prologue before the ordinary Academy journey opens.

Origin selection acquires/establishes the protagonist only. It does not auto-acquire all other Academy representations and does not fabricate the other Origins as having occurred.

The existing committed Active-Konoha entry boundary remains the transition out of the Origin Prologue.

Preserve:

**selected Origin ≠ all Origins happened**  
**unselected Academy candidate ≠ retroactively completed Origin**  
**source-era provenance ≠ current Chronicle history**

---

# 3. Mandatory initial Academy squad

After the Prologue / Active-Konoha entry boundary, the player must choose exactly two other eligible Academy Student teammates.

The initial squad is:

**1 Origin protagonist + 2 distinct eligible Academy teammates.**

This remains the already-authorised Academy Team Formation transaction.

The player cannot enter ordinary Academy free play until this three-person squad is committed.

The two chosen teammates become actual Chronicle participants with subsequent team/shared-history potential from the point their legitimate formation/participation history begins. Formation itself does not fabricate earlier shared memories or events.

Preserve:

**candidate eligibility ≠ ownership**  
**selection ≠ acquisition/commit**  
**team formation ≠ fabricated prior Shared History**  
**team membership ≠ obedience**

---

# 4. Academy free play before the Genin exam

Once the initial Academy squad is committed and the first Konoha map/tutorial continuation completes, the player is free to decide how long to remain in the Academy-stage world before attempting Promotion.

The player may immediately take the Genin Promotion assessment when legitimately available.

Or the player may "fuck around" in the world first, including legitimately available:

- regional/world-map exploration;
- hotspot events;
- standing/optional activities;
- training;
- practical/exam/training-ground activities where authorised;
- social/relationship interactions;
- discovery/investigation;
- other ordinary free-play opportunities supplied by current World/Progression/content authority.

There is **no mandatory grind requirement** before the Genin exam merely because free play exists.

Likewise, taking the exam immediately is not defined as the universally optimal route.

What the player does before Promotion becomes real Chronicle history and may affect later candidate availability, relationships, Knowledge, development, opportunities and team context only through the systems that legitimately own those consequences.

Preserve:

**world activity ≠ Promotion automatically**  
**training ≠ Promotion automatically**  
**hotspot completion ≠ Progression automatically**  
**free-play duration ≠ hidden universal readiness score**  
**fast Promotion ≠ inherently best**  
**slow Promotion ≠ inherently best**

# 5. Genin Promotion remains voluntary

The current stable Promotion assessment remains:

`academy_to_genin_field_readiness_assessment`

The player may enter it as soon as its existing eligibility contract is satisfied and the assessment is available.

Availability means the assessment may be chosen. It does not force entry.

The player may therefore remain Academy-ranked while continuing legitimate free play.

Assessment success changes only the exact authoritative assessment subject's formal Rank Academy → Genin and unlocks the separate `geninRosterTransition`.

Teammates are not automatically promoted because the protagonist passes.

Preserve:

**Battle victory ≠ Promotion**  
**assessment availability ≠ forced attempt**  
**player delay ≠ failed exam**  
**team participation ≠ team-wide Promotion**

# 6. Post-Promotion choice — retain or reform

After successful Promotion, the player receives a real team-continuity choice.

## A. Retain the Academy squad

The player may choose to stay with the same two people selected for the Academy Student squad, preserving their actual continuing person/ownership/team/Shared-History continuity.

Retention does not create a second acquisition record and does not automatically rewrite either teammate's formal Rank or representation.

If a separate committed occurrence has made a retained teammate genuinely unavailable, that actual state still matters; the UI/transition must not resurrect them merely because they belonged to the original squad.

## B. Reform the team

The player may replace one or both teammate positions using the current legitimate post-Promotion candidate pool.

The intended ordinary Genin-era structure is:

**promoted protagonist + two legitimate Genin-level teammate positions + one eligible Jōnin teacher/leader.**

The exact formal Rank/representation of a candidate remains whatever current authority says; the phrase "Genin-level teammate" does not grant Rank by presentation or by team-slot assignment.

A Jōnin teacher/leader remains mandatory for completing the current Alpha `geninRosterTransition` under existing Rank authority.

Preserve:

**retain teammate ≠ reacquire teammate**  
**replace teammate ≠ delete/un-own old teammate**  
**team slot ≠ formal Rank mutation**  
**Jōnin leader eligibility ≠ ownership ≠ assignment ≠ deployment**

# 7. Dynamic Genin and Jōnin availability

This is the new explicit Alpha lock.

The post-Promotion candidate list is **not a static global roster**.

Which Genin teammate candidates and Jōnin teacher/leader candidates are available depends on the Chronicle the player actually created before and around Promotion.

Relevant causal inputs may include, where the owning systems authorise them:

- which Origin was chosen;
- which Academy teammates were selected;
- how quickly or slowly the player attempted Promotion;
- hotspot/event/activity outcomes;
- training/development history;
- relationships and Shared History;
- observer/world Knowledge and discoverability;
- recruitment/acquisition prerequisites;
- candidate-specific current availability;
- wider world/team state.

A character appearing in the Registry is not enough to make them selectable.

Preserve:

**Registry existence ≠ candidate eligibility**  
**candidate eligibility ≠ ownership**  
**candidate visibility ≠ recruitment success**

# 8. CE autonomous preparation / team-allocation pressure

While the player spends time acting in the world rather than immediately resolving the post-Promotion team transition, other shinobi do not remain frozen waiting forever for the protagonist.

CE may allow legitimate autonomous world/team history to progress for other eligible Genin and Jōnin candidates.

In practical terms, other candidates may be **prepared / assigned into non-player teams or otherwise become legitimately unavailable** as the Chronicle advances.

This is not random deletion from a menu. A candidate leaving the player's pool must have a legitimate causal state/assignment/availability reason owned by the correct systems.

The intended pressure is:

> **the longer / differently the player acts, the available post-Promotion team field can change because other people are living their own Chronicle too.**

CE supplies the causal/history context and autonomous-participant/world progression. Acquisition owns the player-facing candidate/recruitment transaction and team mutation. Rank owns formal-rank and Jōnin-leader boundaries. Coding persists/orchestrates the result.

Preserve:

**NPC autonomy ≠ random sabotage**  
**candidate removed from player pool ≠ character deleted**  
**assigned elsewhere ≠ owned by player**  
**cohort/world activity ≠ protagonist-controlled queue**

# 9. Candidate-pool floor — never deadlock the player

The autonomous preparation/allocation pressure must not consume every viable candidate and make the Genin Roster Transition impossible.

The Alpha floor is:

- **2 eligible Genin teammate candidates**;
- **1 eligible Jōnin teacher/leader candidate**.

Once only that minimum viable uncommitted candidate set remains, CE must stop consuming/preparing candidates from that player-relevant pool until the player resolves the team choice or another explicit authored event changes the state.

If the player has delayed long enough that the pool reaches exactly **2 Genin + 1 Jōnin**, the final available combination may effectively be the remaining team. The player still confirms the legitimate assignment transaction; UI rendering does not itself commit it.

The floor protects completion, not preference.

A player who acts immediately may see a wider range of viable teachers/teammates. A player who spends longer in free play may see a different and potentially narrower field because other candidates have legitimately moved on.

This is a consequence of Chronicle timing/state, **not punishment XP, morality scoring or arbitrary rerolling**.

Preserve:

**minimum viable pool ≠ guaranteed favourite candidate**  
**candidate pressure ≠ hard failure**  
**different availability ≠ better/worse route automatically**

They remain the protagonist's currently assigned Academy teammates unless an actual committed Story/World/team event changes that relationship or availability.

Other candidate preparation must not fabricate a departure for an already-assigned teammate just to shrink the pool.

Preserve:

**current assignment ≠ unassigned candidate**  
**time passing ≠ automatic teammate abandonment**  
**retain option ≠ canonical reset**

# 11. Legitimate update boundaries / no reroll abuse

Candidate availability and autonomous team preparation must be persistent causal state, not presentation randomness.

Legitimate changes may occur after committed facts such as:

- completion/resolution of a relevant hotspot/event/activity;
- a training/development occurrence where candidate availability legitimately depends on it;
- a committed relationship/recruitment event;
- a legitimate world-time / institutional team-formation progression boundary;
- Promotion or another exact team-state transition;
- a committed candidate assignment elsewhere.

The following must **not** reroll or consume candidates by themselves:

- opening/closing the team screen;
- opening/closing the World Map;
- UI refresh;
- hover/focus changes;
- loading the same save;
- retrying a presentation step;
- inspecting a candidate card.

Once another candidate has been legitimately assigned/prepared elsewhere, save/load must preserve that historical fact unless a later legitimate occurrence changes it.

Preserve:

**presentation refresh ≠ semantic reroll/recommit**  
**same save ≠ new candidate lottery**
