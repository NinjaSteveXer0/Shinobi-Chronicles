# Hokage Office / Chronicle Dispatch — Step 6 Scope Lock

**Date:** 2026-10-06

Step 6 implements a bounded institutional office slice. It does not automatically activate every tile present in recovered concept art.

Required: Missions, Chronicle Dispatch, current assignment summary, current player information.

Conditional on existing real data/owner: Village Status/Reports, Office Announcements, Exams routing, records routing.

Not activated by Step 6 alone: Promotions (Step 7), Village Affairs, Diplomacy, Special Assignments.

Owner browser acceptance is required before Step 6 is Golden.
