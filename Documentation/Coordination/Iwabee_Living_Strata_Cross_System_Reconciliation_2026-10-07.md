# 1. Taxonomy disposition — `Acquired Kekkei Genkai: Living Strata` is valid

No terminology collision exists with current SC/CE capability-source authority.

**Living Strata may remain classified in Shinobi Chronicles as:**

> **Kekkei Genkai — Living Strata**

with historical provenance:

> **Acquired / Induced — Forbidden Scroll source merger**

The important semantic separation is:

**source taxonomy != acquisition provenance**.

`Kekkei Genkai` identifies the SC capability/source family.  
`Acquired / Induced` records how this exact Iwabee came to possess the source.

Therefore the owner-facing shorthand **Acquired Kekkei Genkai — Living Strata** is accepted, but implementations and durable records must not collapse the two concepts into an ancestry claim.

Preserve:

- acquired Kekkei Genkai != inherited blood ancestry;
- persistent intrinsic truth != congenital truth;
- equivalent source semantics != equivalent acquisition history;
- source possession != contextual Access != Battle Access != exact Skill Access != Competence != Power != Mastery;
- route completion does not retroactively rewrite earlier ordinary Earth/Bukijutsu techniques as Living-Strata techniques.

No new universal CE term is required. `Kekkei Genkai` remains SC domain taxonomy.

# 2. Scroll -> Iwabee source merger is an explicit source-creation / transfer transition

Before the capstone merger, the Forbidden Scroll is an **external bounded reactive artifact** carrying the Living Strata source.

It may evaluate exact authorised reader state such as:

- identity;
- chakra/life-contact;
- route evidence;
- prior Strata completion;
- relevant Knowledge/development predicates.

It may then reveal, withhold or expose the next authored Stratum.

That bounded state evaluation does **not** create personhood.

Canonical Living Strata rule:

> **Reactive artifact != person != Hosted Entity != second will.**

The scroll does not require:

- NPC identity;
- relationship state;
- autonomous goals;
- conversational agency;
- a second Chronicle participant.

At the authorised capstone, Progression/source authority may commit one atomic semantic transition:

1. verify exact Iwabee + route prerequisites;
2. verify the exact scroll/source instance is still available;
3. consume/supersede the scroll as an independently usable source;
4. commit `Living Strata` as a persistent intrinsic source on Iwabee;
5. retain provenance linking the new source truth to the consumed scroll, route history and successful merger;
6. preserve separate downstream Access / Skill / Competence / Power / Mastery gates;
7. prevent retry/save-load/presentation from duplicating the source-creation event.

The post-merger truth is **one Iwabee with one intrinsic source**, not Iwabee plus a hidden resident entity.

If future Story reveals an origin-maker, historical will or another person associated with the scroll, that does not retroactively mean the operational artifact itself was a Hosted Entity unless exact new authority establishes such a fact.

# 3. 6-Star Bukijutsu — route-bound exceptional proficiency, not a sixth global tier

The normal SC weapon-proficiency ladder remains capped at **5 Stars**.

Iwabee may nevertheless earn a visible **6th Bukijutsu Star** through the Living Strata route.

The 6th Star is accepted as an **exceptional source-bound proficiency state** whose meaning is:

> **Iwabee's weapon discipline + Living Strata source + proven reactive weapon integration.**

It is **not** a new generic progression tier above Master.

Therefore:

- ordinary training cannot generically raise a Character from 5 -> 6 Stars;
- no other Character gains a 6th Star merely because one route now displays six;
- raw Bukijutsu Stat does not manufacture the exception;
- possession of Living Strata does not immediately grant it;
- it does not imply mastery of every weapon class;
- another Origin signature route does not receive a matching exception for symmetry.

The exact future transaction must identify the proficiency scope it modifies. It may be tied to an exact weapon class, exact Living-Strata-integrated Bukijutsu expression, or another Combat/Progression-approved scope. It must not silently mean universal mastery of all weapons.

Player presentation may truthfully show six Stars once the exceptional state is committed. Internally, systems may store this as a route-bound extension or another exact representation; CE does not require a universal numeric `maxStars = 6` mutation.

**#470 design closure does not require the exact future 6-Star threshold/numerics.** Those remain Progression + Combat + Registry implementation/content closure when the feature is activated.

# 4. Knowledge / Shinobi Records compatibility — #515 is reused, not made a blocker

Living Strata is compatible with the current #515 direction.

Iwabee's route consumes the following minimum semantics now:

### Forbidden Knowledge

A rumour, book, clue, scroll Stratum, observation or testimony may alter an observer's Knowledge without granting:

- source ownership;
- Access;
- Skill;
- competence;
- Power;
- mastery;
- guaranteed quest progression.

### Development inspiration

A Character who spends meaningful history around Altered Iwabee may legitimately form an observer-specific **development thought / hypothesis**.

Example presentation may begin:

> `Being around Iwabee has inspired me to...`

This fact means only that the observer now holds a development-relevant hypothesis or question supported by their own history.

It is not:

- passive EXP;
- a Stat aura;
- copied Kinjutsu;
- a learned Skill;
- automatic route eligibility;
- a forced quest marker;
- proof the hypothesis is correct.

The observer's later actions/evidence may sharpen the hypothesis into a real actionable opportunity under owning-system authority.

### Responsive artifact

Iwabee's scroll is the first serious SC consumer of a **bounded adaptive knowledge-bearing artifact**: an object may evaluate authorised reader state and conditionally reveal stored/encoded content without being an Entity/person.

This specific compatibility is closed here.

**#515 remains OPEN as the broader reusable Forbidden Knowledge / rumour / hypothesis / dynamic-cardinality architecture queue. It is not a blocker to closing Iwabee's #470 design.**

# 5. Crafting / Forge / Fūin Craft boundary

Living Strata may be a legitimate source/material contributor to Crafting where an exact recipe/process authorises it.

Preserve the current Crafting authority:

- recipe Knowledge != source possession;
- source possession != Crafting competence;
- source contributor != executor/crafter;
- crafted object != owned until ownership commits;
- temporary Battle manifestation != durable crafted instance;
- provenance != generic bonus power.

## Living-Strata source contribution

A future recipe may consume or reference a bounded authorised:

- sample;
- charge;
- mineralised output;
- source-touched material;
- another exact Living-Strata contribution.

That contribution must **not** silently duplicate Iwabee's entire intrinsic source into an item or another Character.

A persistent Living-Strata-compatible weapon requires the ordinary durable Crafting/provenance transaction and stable created-object identity.

A weapon temporarily becoming lithic/reactive while Iwabee channels a technique through it is a Battle/Ability state only and does not mint a permanent crafted weapon.

## Fūin Craft

Living Strata may supply one bounded source/material component to a Fūin Craft recipe.

The resulting seal may expose only the exact effect/package authorised by that recipe.

It does not imply:

- Iwabee knows Fūinjutsu;
- the crafter now possesses Living Strata as a Kekkei Genkai;
- the sealed host has inherited Iwabee's complete route;
- a seal has created a Hosted Entity;
- a bounded seal effect can manufacture unrestricted Living-Strata source ownership.

Provenance should preserve, where observer-safe:

- source contributor;
- actual crafter/executor;
- recipe/process;
- durable host;
- relevant material lineage;
- attachment/supersession history.

No new #148 implementation handoff is required merely to close #470.

# 6. Representation, Origin Evolution and formal Rank remain separate axes

Living Strata can legitimately create new Character representations when a materially persistent representation truth has changed.

That does not collapse:

- person identity;
- Origin Evolution;
- formal Rank representation;
- signature-route state;
- card presentation.

Canonical model:

**Iwabee person identity**  
!= **Origin card / evolved Origin card**  
!= **Genin / Chūnin / Jōnin representation**  
!= **Living Strata route representation**  
!= **current formal Rank truth**.

A Living Strata route card may coexist with the exact Origin card's separately authorised #405 Evolution and with later Rank representations.

A new Skill alone does not justify a new representation.

Formal Rank remains institutional truth. Dialogue/institutional treatment must consume the observer-known current formal Rank, not infer Rank from whichever card art/title is currently displayed.

Thus a formally Jōnin Iwabee does not become Genin in Story because a Living Strata representation visually resembles an earlier-stage card.

Origin Evolution != Promotion.  
Route evolution != Promotion.  
Representation != Rank mutation.

# 7. Observer-bounded Knowledge and World reaction

Living Strata may be World Truth while different observers know radically different things about it.

Possible observer states include, without imposing a universal ladder:

- saw unusual Earth behaviour;
- observed an unknown Kinjutsu-like effect;
- heard a rumour about a forbidden scroll;
- knows Iwabee possessed the scroll;
- knows a merger occurred;
- suspects a persistent body/chakra alteration;
- knows the `Living Strata` source identity;
- correctly classifies it as an acquired Kekkei Genkai;
- knows selected capabilities but not full limits/provenance.

Canonical rule:

> **Observed effect != known source taxonomy.**

World/Story reactions must therefore consume actual observer Knowledge plus role/institution/history.

A witness, Konoha authority, enemy researcher, clan specialist or ordinary civilian may react differently to the same occurrence.

Public use may create evidence, rumours, investigations or political consequences where authored, but it does not globally upload the full truth to every Character.

Concealment, uncertainty, incorrect interpretation and later correction remain legitimate Chronicle history.

Large-scale Living Strata use may materially change terrain/world state only when the owning World/Combat/Story resolution actually commits such a consequence. Capability potential alone does not rewrite geography.
