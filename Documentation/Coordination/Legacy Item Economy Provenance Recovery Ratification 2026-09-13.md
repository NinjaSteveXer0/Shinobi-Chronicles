## Ratified legacy-design locks

The following are now durable Shinobi Chronicles design authority:

1. **Ordinary Crafting ingredients/materials have both an authored Ryō-purchase route and an authored gameplay/loot-acquisition route.** Exact vendor, price, access, stock and loot-source placement remain with owning systems.

2. **Weapons, Items, Gear/Equipment, Ingredients/Materials, Characters and Summons may all participate in shop/economy acquisition where their owning content authority permits it.** This does not mean every Character, Summon, legendary object or special identity is universally purchasable.

3. **Loot modifiers apply only after semantic loot eligibility has been established.** Increased drop chance cannot manufacture an otherwise ineligible drop.

4. **Each distinct legitimately acquired Kakashi Ninja Dog / Ninken contributes +5 percentage points to eligible random loot-drop chance**, subject to the detailed boundaries in the recovery authority. Duplicate representations of the same dog do not stack as distinct dogs. Encountering or previewing a dog is not acquisition. Pakkun counts only after legitimate admission/acquisition.

5. **The individual non-loot enhancement of each Ninja Dog remains an explicit protected owner-closure gap if the old exact enhancement cannot be recovered.** Do not invent those missing values from memory or from the +5 loot rule.

6. **Shinobi Record requires a Blueprints / Recipes projection** in addition to the already-required Characters, Weapons, Items, Equipment, Summons and Creations / Provenance Weapons families.

7. **Physical blueprint possession and learned recipe Knowledge are separate truths.** A legitimate learn/study occurrence may commit recipe Knowledge; losing the physical source later does not automatically erase learned Knowledge.

8. **Durable weapons require exact instance history when provenance matters, regardless of whether the weapon began as purchased, forged, looted, rewarded, inherited or transferred.** Weapon definition != exact weapon instance.

9. **A standard freshly forged kunai begins mechanically better than a fresh standard retail/shop-bought kunai baseline**, with exact numbers/effect packages owned by Combat / Items / Weapons.

10. **Forged does not mean permanently superior.** A bought kunai that later accumulates legitimate modifications, reforge history, Fūin attachment, special repair/evolution or other authorised object development can become mechanically superior to a newly forged kunai.

11. **Provenance itself is not a generic hidden power score.** History can qualify an object for explicit authored recognition/modification/effects; merely having more Chronicle entries does not automatically increase Stats or PL.

12. **Purchased and looted copies of the same ordinary material may share the same catalogue mechanics while retaining different acquisition provenance.** Different acquisition history does not imply different power unless an exact owner contract makes source quality mechanically relevant.
