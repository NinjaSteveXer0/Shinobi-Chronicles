
# Shinobi Chronicles — Phase 2 Elemental Training, Nature Development, Potential Skills + Shinobi Record Direction

**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Owner direction:** Stephen
**Coordination:** CE / Codex / Coordination
**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED PHASE-2 DIRECTION / EXACT NUMBERS + CURRICULA PENDING SPECIALIST CLOSURE
**Consumes:** #382 Potential Skill Roster queue, #434 persistent-development trial, current Combat Bloodline/Nature authority, Shinobi Record/Codex authority

## 1. Core direction

Phase 2 must add a dedicated **Elemental / Nature Transformation Training** path.

It does not need to be embedded into Exams or Practical.

Preferred host:
- Training Grounds / dedicated Nature Transformation training sub-surface;
- later mentors, locations, missions, hotspots and specialist facilities may expose additional curricula.

The training should be deliberately difficult, Energy-relevant once Energy activates, and capable of supporting long-horizon development rather than functioning as a one-click elemental unlock.

## 2. Mandatory separation

Preserve:

Ninjutsu Stat != elemental/nature development

elemental/nature development != Skill access

Skill access != Competence != Mastery

high component-element development != automatic Bloodline possession

Bloodline possession != executable composite Skill access

A Character may have high Ninjutsu while being weak in Fire Release.

A Character may have strong Fire development while lacking access to one specific Fire Skill.

A Character may possess a Bloodline/composite nature while still lacking sufficient development/training to execute higher expressions.

## 3. Nature Development ledgers

Phase 2 should support persistent development for exact chakra-nature families where the Character legitimately has or can acquire Access.

Initial ordinary nature families:

- Fire
- Wind
- Lightning
- Earth
- Water

Yin, Yang, Yin–Yang, Space-Time and other advanced capability families remain separate taxonomic/development spaces and must not be silently flattened into the five basic elements.

Exact numeric scale/name remains Progression-owned.

Candidate terms:
- Nature Development
- Nature Proficiency
- Nature Control

Do not use Mastery casually unless the project closes what Mastery means.

## 4. How Nature Development grows

Legitimate sources may include:

- dedicated elemental training;
- exact mentor curriculum;
- successful/failed material use of an elemental technique;
- Battle actions;
- World/Mission actions;
- observed/opponent-derived technique evidence where the learner has sufficient compatible prerequisites;
- exact specialist exams/practical curricula later;
- Chronicle-specific opportunities.

No source grants progress merely because the screen is open.

Use factual causal receipts and idempotence.

## 5. Dedicated Elemental Training

Elemental training should be substantially harder than ordinary beginner discipline training.

A course may require:
- minimum Ninjutsu/other Stat;
- known/accessible chakra nature;
- prerequisite technique;
- previous course completion;
- mentor/location access;
- Rank/stage only where genuinely authored;
- Energy cost once Energy activates.

Courses should have clear difficulty bands such as:
- Foundation
- Intermediate
- Advanced
- Specialist / Exceptional

Exact names remain UI/Progression-owned.

A lower course eventually stops improving that Nature Development directly.

The player must be warned before spending Energy.

## 6. Element training must not be a dead end

When one elemental course reaches its normal development ceiling, continued legitimate training may still contribute to:

- exact Technique Practice;
- shape-transformation/control evidence;
- specialist capability evidence;
- resistance/countermeasure curricula if separately authored;
- Chronicle history;
- mentor recognition;
- future event eligibility.

Do not silently award zero.

## 7. Potential Skill Roster integration

Consume #382.

Each persistent Character has a **Potential Skill Roster** filtered from authored Skills by exact predicates.

Elemental Skill predicates may include:

- representation/stage legality;
- exact minimum Ninjutsu / Taijutsu / Bukijutsu / Fūinjutsu / etc.;
- exact Nature Development threshold;
- prerequisite Skill(s);
- shape-transformation/control evidence;
- mentor/training-source access;
- Chronicle history;
- Bloodline/Hosted Entity/Summon/source capability;
- resistance/encounter evidence;
- other exact Skill-specific requirements.

Preserve:

potential != learned != prepared != mastered.

## 8. Fireball / Chidori / elemental-technique model

Examples are illustrative only.

A Fire Skill should not unlock simply because Ninjutsu rose.

A legitimate Fire technique may require:

Fire Nature Access + minimum Fire Development + minimum Ninjutsu + exact training/teacher/scroll/source + prerequisites

A Lightning technique such as a Chidori-family route may require a different combination of:
- Lightning Development;
- Ninjutsu;
- exact prerequisite technique/control;
- exact teacher/source;
- other authored requirements.

Wind/Earth/Water use the same reusable grammar rather than one-off unlock systems.

## 9. Learning from opponents

Existing direction allowing technique learning from combat exposure should plug into the same contract.

Seeing/fighting an opponent use a Skill can create **Technique Evidence / Insight** only when the learner legitimately perceives enough of the technique.

It does not automatically copy or unlock it.

Possible later requirements:
- Skill observed;
- correct/compatible nature Access;
- sufficient relevant Nature Development;
- sufficient discipline Stats;
- prerequisite technique;
- same/opposite-element knowledge where exact technique/counter route needs it;
- repeated observation;
- mentor/training follow-up.

Sharingan and similar capabilities obey their own exact observation/copy authority and do not bypass hidden prerequisites automatically.

## 10. Resistances

Poison and elemental resistance development remains a separate capability/evidence problem.

Do not infer:

Fire Development -> Fire Resistance

or:

using poison -> Poison Resistance.

Resistance may be developed through exact:
- exposure;
- mitigation;
- conditioning;
- antidote/treatment;
- defensive curriculum;
- Battle/World evidence;

where current Combat/Progression authority permits it.

Nature Development may be one prerequisite for an exact resistance/counter technique, but it is not automatically the resistance itself.

## 11. Composite nature / Bloodline interaction

Elemental development can become a **requirement** for advanced composite expressions.

It cannot create possession from nothing.

Preserve:

Fire + Wind thresholds != automatic Scorch Release

Water + Wind thresholds != automatic Ice Release

Earth + Wind + Fire thresholds != automatic Dust Release

For a Character who legitimately possesses/accesses a composite capability, component Nature Development may gate later Skills/evolution.

## 12. Kekkei Genkai / Kekkei Tōta / higher SC ladder

Current durable Combat authority preserves:

- Kekkei Genkai as a distinct capability/bloodline family;
- Kekkei Tōta as KEEP;
- Dust Release as the canon three-nature anchor;
- SC-original Kekkei Tōta are allowed;
- a future **four-level lineage/development ladder** is mandated;
- **Kekkei Chōetsu** survives as an approved design concept, but the exact four-level taxonomy/names/semantics are NOT yet completely closed.

Therefore Stephen's memory of an SC-original level beyond the ordinary Genkai/Tōta space is real, but it must not yet be implemented as a guessed 4+ elements = Kekkei Chōetsu formula.

Phase 2 should preserve the slot and route exact taxonomy closure through Combat/CE before a gameplay gate consumes it.

## 13. Special Jōnin specialisations

Nature Development may become legitimate evidence toward a Special Jōnin specialisation where Rank/Progression authority explicitly requires it.

Example grammar:

high relevant Nature Development + required Skills + field evidence + institutional eligibility -> potential specialisation route

Do not collapse:
- strong element user;
- specialist eligibility;
- formal Special Jōnin status.

## 14. Staged Battles / PvP / Village Tournament / high-end PvE

Nature Development should matter to high-level competitive/challenge content primarily through exact capability.

It may gate:
- stronger techniques;
- counters;
- resistance packages;
- advanced curricula;
- composite expressions;
- battle plans;
- event eligibility.

Avoid universal hidden rules like:

Fire Development 50 = +50% PvE damage

unless Combat explicitly authors that exact mechanic.

For Super/Ultra bosses, high Nature Development may make exact higher-level skills/counters available or interact with authored vulnerabilities.

## 15. Shinobi Record becomes a progression intelligence surface

The existing Shinobi Record is not yet sufficient for Phase 2.

It should become the player's observer-safe memory for:

### Development
- current Stats;
- Discipline Development progress;
- Nature Development progress;
- resistance/conditioning progress where known;
- recent breakthroughs;
- meaningful failure/success history.

### Techniques
For exact Skills, Record may distinguish states such as:
- Learned;
- Training in Progress;
- Eligible to Learn;
- Training Source Known;
- Observed / Evidence Held;
- Hinted / Rumoured;
- Unknown/hidden.

Exact state names remain UI-owned.

Do not leak hidden Skill names/requirements merely because they exist in production data.

## 16. Hidden-technique handling

Shinobi Record can record that the player has found information without giving omniscient detail.

Examples:

Known:
**Fire Release: Great Fireball Technique**
Eligible — instructor/training source known

Partially known:
**Unknown Fire Technique**
You have seen this technique performed. More understanding is required.

Hint:
**Technique lead**
A trainer mentioned advanced repetition may reveal another application.

Hidden:
no name, icon, count denominator or DOM leak.

Preserve:

World Truth != observer Knowledge != Shinobi Record presentation.

## 17. Chronicle history

Meaningful elemental history may include:

- first nature discovered/accessed;
- first successful use;
- repeated failed training then breakthrough;
- completing Foundation/Intermediate/Advanced curriculum;
- first exact Skill learned;
- resistance milestone;
- composite-expression breakthrough;
- mentor recognition;
- major Battle/World use that materially mattered.

Do not spam one Chronicle row per training repetition.

## 18. CE use

CE may consume factual Nature/Technique history to author legitimate future opportunities.

Examples:
- trainer notices repeated failed Fire control attempts;
- NPC offers a harder course because current development is proven;
- opponent recognises a technique they previously saw;
- advanced hotspot becomes eligible because the Character has exact elemental evidence;
- specialised Staged Battle opens because exact capability prerequisites are satisfied.

CE must not invent a Skill unlock, Bloodline, resistance or mastery state.

## 19. Browser trial requirement

Stephen explicitly requires seeing/playing this system before pace and UI are frozen.

Phase 2 must provide at least one end-to-end Nature Training browser slice:

select one current-team Character
-> enter one elemental curriculum
-> spend/commit repetitions
-> gain persistent Nature Development
-> hit a visible course ceiling
-> continue into one exact Technique Practice route
-> learn or advance one exact Skill
-> Shinobi Record updates
-> save/load preserves all state.

Energy may initially use owner/dev bypass.

## 20. Final direction

> **Elemental development becomes its own persistent axis beside ordinary Stats. Ninjutsu alone does not equal Fire/Wind/Lightning/Earth/Water mastery; nature development alone does not auto-grant Skills; high component-element development does not manufacture a Bloodline. Dedicated hard elemental training, real Battle/World evidence, mentors and technique observation all feed one persistent Potential Skill pathway, while Shinobi Record tells the player what they legitimately know, can learn, are practising, have learned and have meaningfully achieved without leaking hidden content.**
