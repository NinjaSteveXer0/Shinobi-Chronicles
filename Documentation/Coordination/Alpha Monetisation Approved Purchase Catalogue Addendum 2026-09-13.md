## Purpose

This addendum extends and must be read with:

`Documentation/Coordination/Energy Participation Evolution Chamber and Alpha Monetisation Foundation 2026-09-13.md`

It closes the currently approved real-money purchase catalogue for Alpha-facing planning while preserving the existing principle:

> **Players should pay because they are excited to play more, support the project or collect desirable presentation rewards — not because normal play was deliberately made miserable.**

This addendum does not author payment-provider integration, tax/VAT handling, legal storefront copy, regional pricing, refund policy, chargeback handling, age-gating, exact SKU prices beyond the explicitly approved Alpha Supporter tiers, or platform-commerce implementation.

---

# 1. Already-approved purchasable categories remain binding

The following remain approved:

1. **Energy Pills** — restore Energy / extend current play opportunity.
2. **Ryō packs** — transparent purchase of the same in-game Ryō earned through play.
3. **Alpha Supporter tiers** — £10 / £20 / £50 early-access supporter packages tied to the first three Combo Cards.

Paid Energy does not alter Story outcomes, resolver odds, Rank, Stats, PL or CE authority.

Purchased Ryō remains ordinary Ryō and must not become a superior premium version of the currency.

---

# 2. Alpha Supporter tiers are cumulative

The three Alpha Supporter tiers are now closed as **cumulative entitlement tiers**.

Baseline commercial structure:

- **£10 Supporter** → early access to **Combo Card 1** + Alpha Supporter recognition.
- **£20 Supporter** → everything in £10 + early access to **Combo Card 2** + enhanced supporter cosmetic/profile recognition.
- **£50 Supporter** → everything in £10 and £20 + early access to **Combo Card 3** + highest Alpha Supporter recognition.

A player purchasing the £50 tier does not lose or bypass lower-tier entitlements.

The first three Combo Cards are **not permanent paid exclusives**. Non-supporters must later receive legitimate access through normal release/progression/acquisition conditions.

Exact Combo Card identities and exact later-public availability dates remain CONTENT / COMMERCIAL TIMING OPEN until deliberately closed.

Preserve:

**supporter early access != permanent exclusivity**  
**supporter tier != superior ruleset**  
**paid entitlement != fabricated Chronicle history**

---

# 3. Approved cosmetic / presentation purchases

The following categories are now approved for later store/HUD implementation:

## A. Alternate card art / cosmetic card presentations

Players may purchase approved alternate visual treatments for collectible cards where such treatment is presentation-only.

The purchase does not create a stronger Character, alter Stats/PL, change ownership identity, change Rank or rewrite Chronicle history.

## B. Cosmetic portraits / visual representations

Approved alternate portrait presentations may be sold where they are cosmetic representations of an already-authorised owned Character/representation.

A cosmetic portrait does not become a new Registry person or stronger Battle participant.

## C. Profile frames / badges / supporter recognition

Players may purchase or receive through supporter tiers:

- profile frames;
- profile badges;
- supporter marks;
- other clearly cosmetic account-recognition elements.

These carry presentation/status value only unless a separate future system explicitly authorises another effect.

## D. UI themes / interface skins

Optional UI themes or interface presentation skins may be sold provided they do not change semantic information availability, reveal hidden state, improve competitive information access, alter input timing, or grant gameplay advantage.

## E. Extra saved team / loadout presets

Players may purchase additional convenience slots for saved formations/loadouts/presets.

These slots save configuration time only.

They do not increase the number of deployed Characters, team-slot capacity, owned Characters, Battle actions, equipment capacity or semantic assignment rights.

## F. Other bounded account convenience slots

Additional convenience capacity may be sold where it stores or organises already-authorised player data/presentation state rather than increasing core gameplay power.

Examples may include extra saved presets or comparable non-power account organisation capacity.

Any convenience product that begins changing effective combat capability, opportunity eligibility, Rank, Progression, ownership, Energy regeneration rate beyond explicitly sold Energy restoration, or competitive entry count requires new explicit authority.

---

# 4. Competitive fairness boundary

Real-money purchases must not directly buy:

- formal Rank or Promotion;
- permanent raw Stats detached from legitimate development;
- direct/hidden PL;
- guaranteed Battle victory;
- superior CE choices or resolver outcomes;
- extra Village Tournament entries by default;
- extra Staged Battle entries by default;
- Arena ranking points;
- hidden-information access;
- stronger AI/decision logic;
- paid-only permanent versions of the first three Alpha Supporter Combo Cards.

The commercial model may sell **more ordinary play opportunity** through Energy restoration, but competitive participation counters remain separately governed unless deliberately reopened.

---

# 5. Ryō anti-backdoor-pay-to-power guardrail

Selling Ryō is approved, but the economy must not accidentally turn:

`real money -> Ryō -> unlimited immediate permanent power`

into the dominant progression path.

Where a major permanent-development system consumes Ryō, its owning authority should also preserve legitimate non-Ryō conditions, bounded opportunities, development requirements, Chronicle prerequisites, time/action requirements or other appropriate gates so that purchased Ryō is primarily convenience rather than an unrestricted permanent-power bypass.

This does not mean every Ryō sink requires artificial friction. It means commercial Ryō must not silently become direct Stat/PL purchase through an intermediate currency.

---

# 6. Alpha HUD / store presentation requirement

Before Alpha is actually released to supporters/players, SC should expose a clear global HUD/store access point for commercial/convenience systems.

Exact UI design remains UI / Assets owned, but the presentation must clearly distinguish at minimum:

- current Energy / Energy restoration access;
- Ryō balance / authorised Ryō purchase access where implemented;
- Alpha Supporter status/entitlements;
- purchased cosmetic/convenience ownership where relevant.

Store/HUD presentation must not imply that payment is required to continue the mandatory onboarding/prologue or to obtain core rules access.

The HUD/store should make costs, quantities and entitlement permanence/early-access status explicit rather than relying on ambiguous premium-currency conversion language.

> **Pay to play more now, buy convenience, support Alpha, or collect presentation rewards — not pay to rewrite the rules of the Chronicle.**

> **Cosmetic ownership != Character power.**
