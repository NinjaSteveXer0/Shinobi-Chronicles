## 1. Purpose and precedence

This addendum records Stephen's recovered original causal story for why Hashirama Senju and Tobirama Senju are genuinely alive in the current Shinobi Chronicles timeline.

It **does not** replace the earlier non-Edo living-resurrection authority wholesale. It selectively supersedes only those portions that treated the following as unknown:

- who was directly involved in devising/performing the restoration plan;
- the broad discipline/knowledge basis of the technique;
- Hashirama's causal path into the first restoration;
- Tobirama's causal path into the later restoration;
- Hashirama's post-restoration withdrawal from public office/life.

The exact ritual formula, exact technique name, precise costs, precise body/age consequences, exact reproducibility conditions and other unclosed implementation details remain protected gaps.

Canonical shorthand:

> **Hashirama and Madara both survived the Valley of the End. Hashirama returned to Konoha through Asura-derived chakra and Sage chakra, later chose to let his first life end naturally, and was genuinely restored through a plan created by Hashirama, Mito and Tobirama using Uzumaki Fūinjutsu/Kinjutsu informed by Tobirama's Edo Tensei knowledge. Hashirama then remained publicly dead and disappeared into the world. Years later Tobirama truly died against the Kinkaku squad after naming Hiruzen Third Hokage, and Hashirama and Mito used the same broad restoration method to bring Tobirama genuinely back to life.**

## 2. Valley of the End — corrected Recorded History

### 2.1 Hashirama did not die there

Hashirama Senju **did not die at the Valley of the End**.

The Valley of the End remains a major historical confrontation, but it is not Hashirama's death occurrence in this Chronicle.

After the battle, Hashirama used **Asura's chakra together with Sage chakra** to make his way back to Konoha.

This return is part of Recorded History.

### 2.2 Madara did not die there either

Madara Uchiha likewise **did not die at the Valley of the End**.

His later trajectory proceeds into the already-established Black Zetsu-linked history rather than ending at the Valley.

This addendum does not reopen or restate Madara's entire later-game canon; it only corrects the Valley-of-the-End death assumption.

Preserve:

**Valley of the End confrontation != Hashirama death**  
**Valley of the End confrontation != Madara death**

## 3. Hashirama's first death and planned restoration

### 3.1 Fire of Will motive

After returning to Konoha and continuing his life, Hashirama eventually reached the conclusion that the **Will of Fire needed to carry forward beyond him**.

He did not want Konoha's future to depend on his indefinite personal continuation as First Hokage.

This motive is central to the restoration plan:

> **Hashirama was not trying to remain First Hokage forever. He wanted the village and the Will of Fire to continue without being permanently anchored to him.**

### 3.2 Hashirama, Mito and Tobirama devise the plan

Mito Uzumaki and Tobirama Senju collaborated with Hashirama on a plan that would allow Hashirama's first life to end normally/naturally while preserving a path to later genuine restoration.

The broad technical basis was:

- **Uzumaki Fūinjutsu**;
- **Uzumaki Kinjutsu / forbidden sealing-life/death knowledge**;
- **Tobirama's knowledge of Edo Tensei**, used as conceptual/technical knowledge of death, soul/body persistence and reanimation boundaries;
- Hashirama/Mito/Tobirama's combined planning around how to cross the boundary from death back into genuine life.

Critical distinction:

> **Tobirama's Edo Tensei knowledge helped inform the true-restoration method; the resulting technique was not Edo Tensei.**

The plan was therefore not:

`perform Edo Tensei on Hashirama`

It was:

`use sealing/forbidden-technique knowledge + understanding of Edo's death-state mechanics to produce a separate genuine resurrection result`.

### 3.3 Hashirama genuinely dies

Hashirama's historical death remains real.

He allowed his first life to reach its normal/natural end under the plan rather than erasing death from his history or remaining publicly active indefinitely.

The Chronicle therefore records:

`Hashirama alive -> Hashirama genuinely dies -> restoration plan executed -> Hashirama genuinely alive again`

### 3.4 Hashirama is genuinely restored

The plan succeeds in bringing Hashirama back to **real living state**.

He is:

- the same persistent Hashirama Senju;
- not a clone;
- not a reincarnation;
- not an Edo manifestation;
- not a replacement Registry identity.

His original death remains Recorded History.

## 4. Hashirama chooses public death and disappearance

After genuine restoration, Hashirama no longer believes he should simply resume life as the First Hokage.

He allows the wider world to continue believing that **Hashirama Senju is dead**.

He withdraws from public identity and disappears into the wider world rather than reclaiming his old office.

This establishes a deliberate split between:

- **World Truth / Recorded History:** Hashirama died and was later genuinely restored;
- **public expected history:** Hashirama died and remained dead;
- **Hashirama's actual current existence:** alive but intentionally absent/anonymous from ordinary public life.

His resurrection therefore does **not** restore the First Hokage office or undo succession.

Preserve:

**resurrection != restored office**  
**alive != publicly known alive**  
**historical First Hokage != current acting Hokage**

## 5. Tobirama's succession and genuine death

Years later, Tobirama Senju and his group are ambushed by the **Kinkaku squad**.

At that crisis point Tobirama names **Hiruzen Sarutobi as the Third Hokage** and goes to his death.

Tobirama's death is genuine Recorded History.

The succession remains valid and meaningful:

`Tobirama names Hiruzen -> Tobirama dies -> Hiruzen becomes Third Hokage`

Tobirama's later restoration does not invalidate that succession or automatically restore Tobirama as acting Second Hokage.

## 6. Hashirama and Mito restore Tobirama

Before/around Tobirama's death, Hashirama and Mito had an agreement that the same broad true-restoration technique would be used on Tobirama if/when the required conditions arose.

After Tobirama's genuine death against the Kinkaku squad, **Hashirama and Mito perform the same broad restoration method** to bring Tobirama genuinely back to life.

Tobirama after restoration is:

- the same persistent Tobirama Senju;
- genuinely alive;
- not Edo Tensei;
- not a duplicate person;
- still the person who genuinely died after appointing Hiruzen.

His Chronicle history is therefore:

`Tobirama alive -> names Hiruzen Third Hokage -> Tobirama genuinely dies -> Hashirama + Mito execute true-restoration method -> Tobirama genuinely alive again`

This is a prime example of:

> **Expected History != Recorded History.**

Expected/canon history ends with Tobirama dead.
Recorded History contains both his genuine death **and** his later genuine return.

The existing **Medicine + Fūinjutsu + Kinjutsu** investigation framework remains valid, but its purpose changes slightly now that the broad causal history is recovered.

It is no longer investigating an entirely unknown metaphysical event.

It may investigate **pieces of a real but hidden historical technique** whose broad lineage is now known to CE/Codex:

- Medicine may identify living biological restoration and body-state anomalies;
- Fūinjutsu may identify Uzumaki-style anchoring/seal provenance or continuity architecture where evidence survives;
- Kinjutsu may identify forbidden life/death technique lineage and conceptual links to reanimation research;
- Tobirama's Edo knowledge may explain why certain traces resemble, but are not equivalent to, Edo Tensei;
- combined evidence may allow a Chronicle to infer that the method deliberately crossed from true death back to true life.

However:

**discovering broad lineage != knowing exact ritual**  
**recognising Uzumaki provenance != possessing the required Kinjutsu**  
**understanding Edo principles != possessing true resurrection**  
**finding one component != being able to reproduce the whole method**

The player-reproduction boundary remains fail-closed until explicitly authored.

Therefore:

> **Expected History does not overwrite Recorded History. Recorded History accumulates both the expected event and the later divergence where both happened.**
