## 1. Core doctrine

**Structured Story Autonomy** is the Chronicle Engine pattern in which **Story fixes the dramatic function, required macro history and authored destinations, while CE realises the exact causal carrier from legitimate Chronicle state**.

Canonical shorthand:

> **Story says what the campaign must confront. CE proves why this Chronicle reaches it this way.**

This is neither protagonist-specific hard-coding nor unrestricted procedural plot generation.

The engine must preserve:

- authored Story structure;
- committed Chronicle history;
- bounded Knowledge;
- independent participant/faction intent;
- domain-owned capability and consequence resolution;
- causal provenance;
- observer-safe presentation;
- route flexibility without morality-meter collapse.

A generated or selected story realisation is legitimate only when the history that supports it actually exists.

---

## 2. Ownership boundary

### Story / Writing owns

Story authors:

1. **Authored Arc Invariants** — the dramatic function / macro truth that must remain recognisable across Chronicles.
2. **Mandatory historical anchors** where exact occurrence is required for the authored campaign to remain that campaign.
3. **Authored macro-destinations** and required geography transitions.
4. Required headline political/world actors where those actors are genuinely part of the fixed campaign spine.
5. Stakes, escalation, thematic pressure and Story-owned mission/scene boundaries.
6. Any exact causal carrier that Story intentionally fixes rather than leaving open.

Story does **not** automatically own NPC motive, participant Knowledge, relationship state, capability, team agreement, or factual outcome merely because a scene needs pressure.

### CE / Chronicle resolution owns

Within the authored envelope, CE may resolve:

1. which legitimate committed history becomes the current pressure point;
2. which already-authorised person, faction, institution, object, secret, relationship, obligation, reputation, survivor, evidence chain or unresolved occurrence can causally carry that pressure;
3. which actors can legitimately know enough to exploit, defend, distort, expose or suppress it;
4. which autonomous actors actually choose to act, from their own history/Knowledge/role/intent;
5. which authorised route/opportunity becomes the concrete Chronicle-specific bridge toward a fixed Story anchor or destination;
6. how competing eligible possibilities are bounded, selected, suppressed, deferred or superseded;
7. observer-relative projection of the resolved situation.

CE does **not** invent Registry identity, ownership, Rank, Skills, Bloodline access, relationships, Knowledge, world facts or causal ancestry merely because one would make the plot convenient.

### Owning resolvers remain authoritative

Combat, Progression, Acquisition, Registry/PL/Rank, World, Knowledge/Relationship and other specialist domains continue to own their existing semantics.

Story/CE selection of a causal carrier does not bypass those resolvers.

## 3. Reusable realisation pipeline

The binding conceptual pipeline is:

**Authored Arc Invariant**
→ **Chronicle Input Set**
→ **Eligible Pressure-Point Candidates**
→ **bounded actor Knowledge / role / motive / capability checks**
→ **eligible causal-carrier set**
→ **contention / authored priority / optional bounded selection among eligible carriers**
→ **committed Story Realisation**
→ **participant/player actions through owning resolvers**
→ **committed consequences**
→ **new Knowledge / relationships / obligations / world state / route evidence / opportunities**
→ **later-Arc inputs**.

Hard rule:

> **Randomness may choose among already legitimate candidates. It may never manufacture eligibility, Knowledge, capability or history.**

If no candidate can legitimately satisfy the current authored invariant, runtime must fail closed or consume an explicitly authored bridging occurrence. It must not fabricate a protagonist-specific secret, relationship, Skill, witness, faction link or historical fact to force the beat.

That state is a **content/authority gap**, not permission for procedural invention.

## 4. Chronicle Inputs and pressure points

A later Arc may consume only durable/committed prior history and current authoritative state.

Potential pressure-point families include, where actually supported:

- unresolved adversary or survivor;
- protected person / witness / dependent;
- sensitive evidence or recovered object;
- classified or forbidden Knowledge;
- compromised institution / order / office;
- public reputation or political embarrassment;
- clan grievance or obligation;
- relationship / Shared History / debt / betrayal / protection history;
- mission disobedience or unusual compliance;
- exposed secret;
- foreign contact / operative / sponsor / intelligence route;
- possession or use of a capability whose existence is legitimately known;
- custody / release / death / escape consequences;
- unresolved investigation lineage;
- prior political support or opposition whose basis is known.

This is not a universal checklist. A Chronicle uses only what it actually created.

### Consequentiality floor

Every valid Arc-1 protagonist path should be authored so that it can produce at least one durable **Arc-2 leverage-capable history** if the player legitimately reaches the end of Arc 1.

That does not require the same event, secret or power for every Origin.

If a particular Arc-1 route reaches its boundary with no legitimate pressure point, that is an authoring/content coverage defect to repair with a causal event — **not** authority for Arc 2 to invent an Echo-equivalent or counterfeit history after the fact.

## 5. Arc 1–3 authored macro spine

### Arc 1 — Internal Disruption / Deep Investigation

Authored function:

A normal Konoha mission develops into a deeper investigation exposing deliberate internal disruption/manipulation within Konoha.

The exact disruption may vary by Chronicle.

Arc 1 must leave durable outputs that can later matter: discoveries, enemies, survivors, relationships, compromises, exposed institutions, protected targets, forbidden Knowledge, political liabilities, foreign links, obligations or other legitimate history.

**Arc 1 begins route-relevant history but does not declare a route.**

The developer Menma / White Thread / carrier / Echo history is one valid and unusually explosive realisation, not the universal hidden canon.

### Arc 2 — Narrative Warfare / Political Destabilisation / Cat-and-Mouse

Authored function:

Political actors inside and outside Konoha exploit, distort, suppress, reveal or compete over consequences of Arc 1 in ways that threaten wider political stability.

Binding principle:

> **Arc 2 targets Chronicle-earned leverage, not a predetermined protagonist trait.**

A Chronicle without Echo/False Identity/White Thread must receive pressure from its own legitimate Arc-1 outputs rather than a weaker cosmetic substitute for Menma's story.

Different internal/external actors may have different motives around the same evidence or crisis. Do not collapse opposition into one omniscient mastermind unless Story explicitly authors one.

Arc 2 should expose and pressure the protagonist's prior history politically. It may make prior choices costly, valuable, dangerous or newly relevant without retroactively changing what happened.

### Arc 3 — Civil War / Alignment Becomes Unavoidable

Authored macro truth:

Accumulated destabilisation erupts into Konoha civil war.

The headline political fracture is:

- current Hokage **Minato Namikaze** and his supporters;
- challenger **Sasuke Uchiha**, who has gained substantial support through Arc 2.

Why individual people, clans, institutions or factions support/oppose either side should remain Chronicle-relative wherever Story has not fixed that allegiance.

For most Chronicles, neutrality becomes increasingly unsustainable. This does not reduce Arc 3 to a binary Minato/Sasuke morality choice.

**Arc 3 makes accumulated route-relevant history materially consequential.**

## 6. Five route families — binding interpretation

The five route families are preserved as **history-backed political/strategic trajectories**, not five mutually exclusive classes.

### 1. Reform

Preserve Konoha/current institutions while changing them through accountability, repair and reform.

### 2. Dominion

Prioritise stability, control and governing authority; fractured authority and uncontrolled information may be treated as dangers requiring containment.

### 3. Rogue

Reject institutional claims of ownership/control over people, secrets, loyalties or lives and pursue independence where that history becomes credible.

### 4. Take the Throne

Conclude that current leadership/system is incapable and pursue replacement or seizure of governing authority.

### 5. Non-Conformance

An emergent Chronicle trajectory that does not cleanly fit the other four and is supported by the actual relationships, leverage, alliances, ideology, Knowledge, capability and world history the Chronicle has built.

### Route doctrine

- route family != morality axis;
- route family != faction membership;
- route family != one dialogue class;
- route affinity != hard route lock;
- route evidence may be plural, contradictory and nonexclusive;
- later contradiction is permitted and becomes Chronicle history rather than an invalid input;
- supporting Minato != automatically Reform;
- supporting Sasuke != automatically Take the Throne;
- supporting Minato can coexist with Reform, Dominion, Rogue-aligned tactical cooperation or another trajectory depending on actual reasons/actions/history;
- supporting Sasuke can likewise arise for reasons that do not mean the protagonist wants the throne;
- team membership != political allegiance;
- clan membership != political allegiance;
- friendship/family/mentor relation != political allegiance;
- NPCs retain independent political agency;
- route change may become harder, costlier, require repair/betrayal/new support or close some opportunities without becoming metaphysically impossible;
- Non-Conformance is never a free secret fifth button.

### No hidden route-point semantics

Do not make canonical route state a hidden `Reform +1 / Dominion +1 / Rogue +1 / Throne +1` morality ledger.

Runtime may maintain **provenance-bearing route evidence** for diagnostics/eligibility, but each signal must point back to exact committed source history and may support more than one interpretation where legitimately authored.

Preferred interpretation:

> **Route affinity is a derived explanation over evidence, not a scalar World Truth.**

Availability of later political actions should query the underlying history/relationships/Knowledge/power/alliance/world conditions that make those actions credible.

## 7. Authored Arc 2 geography — Konoha / Fire → Kusa and Ame

This is a fixed Story geography requirement.

### Required macro geography

**Early Arc 2:** Konoha / Land of Fire political aftermath and destabilisation.

**Mid/Late Arc 2:** Story expands into both:

- **Kusagakure / Land of Grass**;
- **Amegakure / Land of Rain**.

Kusa and Ame are **authored destinations**. CE may not randomly omit them, replace them with another country, or decide that one Chronicle never needs to leave Konoha.

The exact mission number/order between Kusa and Ame remains Writing-owned unless separately locked.

### CE owns the causal bridge, not the destination requirement

For each required destination, CE resolves an exact legitimate chain from current Chronicle history.

Examples of valid carrier families include:

- Echo / White Thread / leak / counter-intelligence consequences;
- foreign operatives;
- smuggling routes;
- political financing;
- protected witnesses;
- clan manipulation;
- intelligence/evidence chains;
- escaped/captured participants;
- diplomatic/mission obligations;
- relationship or Shared-History contacts;
- another explicitly authorised Chronicle-earned chain.

These examples are not universal requirements.

Hard distinction:

> **Authored destination != fabricated causal path.**

If a Chronicle lacks a legitimate bridge to the next required destination, Story/World must author a legitimate bridging occurrence using existing world facts. CE must not invent a prior contact, secret clue or Knowledge state after the fact.

### World / actionability boundary

Story requiring Kusa/Ame does not make every location inside those regions discovered or actionable.

The Story bridge may establish exactly the Knowledge/access/actionability needed for the authored destination through World authority. It does not automatically reveal secret areas, grant permanent travel rights, or convert all regional content into Story content.

## 8. Arc-to-Arc continuity

### Arc 1 → Arc 2

Arc 2 pressure candidates are derived from committed Arc-1 outputs and unresolved consequences.

The selected pressure must retain provenance to the exact source history that made it usable.

### Arc 2 → Arc 3

Arc 2 must create durable political inputs such as:

- who knows what;
- who trusts / distrusts / owes / fears whom;
- public/private evidence state;
- protected/exposed people;
- survivors/enemies;
- faction and institutional obligations;
- foreign allies/contacts/enemies;
- Kusa/Ame relationships and commitments;
- resource/access/capability constraints;
- political acts and their reasons where those reasons are actually committed;
- open/closed opportunities;
- consequences of contradiction or changed direction.

Those facts influence Arc-3 viability and reactions. They do not become a universal alignment score.

A later Arc consumes prior Chronicle history; it does not rewrite it to fit its preferred branch.

## 9. Story Realisation receipt / replay requirement

When runtime eventually implements generic Structured Story Autonomy, each committed realisation should preserve enough provenance to explain and replay the semantic decision without rerunning UI navigation.

Minimum conceptual receipt:

- authored invariant / Story anchor ref;
- committed realisation ID;
- exact causal parent/source occurrence refs;
- selected pressure-point refs;
- selected actor/faction/object/location refs;
- Knowledge/evidence basis used for eligibility;
- relationship/role/capability/world-state basis where material;
- eligible alternatives and exclusion reasons where practical for deterministic diagnostics;
- destination invariant if one is being satisfied;
- resulting committed consequence refs;
- supersession lineage if a later factual change replaces an unresolved realisation.

Committed realisations are historical truth. UI refresh, save/load or reopening a mission does not reroll them.

New factual history may create a new legitimate realisation; it does not mutate the old receipt as though the earlier state never existed.

## 10. Expression is downstream of semantics

Structured Story Autonomy does not require unrestricted generated dialogue.

The engine resolves first:

**situation / pressure / participants / Knowledge / intent / action / consequence**

Then Writing-authored templates or future contextual-expression systems may realise presentation.

Generated text never becomes World Truth merely because it was displayed.

## 14. Final ruling

The reusable rule is:

> **Author the dramatic invariant. Resolve the Chronicle-specific cause. Commit the exact history. Let that history constrain the next authored invariant.**

For Shinobi Chronicles specifically:

**Arc 1 creates consequential history without declaring a route.**  
**Arc 2 weaponises and exposes that history, then expands through Kusa and Ame by Chronicle-legitimate causal chains.**  
**Arc 3 turns accumulated political history into civil-war pressure while preserving Reform, Dominion, Rogue, Take the Throne and emergent Non-Conformance as history-backed route families rather than menu classes.**

The developer Menma Chronicle remains valid. It is not the template every other protagonist must impersonate.
