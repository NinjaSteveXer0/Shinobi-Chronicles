# Shinobi Chronicles / Chronicle Engine — Canon Research First Specialist Production Protocol

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Owner:** CE / Codex / Coordination  
**Status:** **BINDING PROJECT-WIDE PRODUCTION PROTOCOL**  
**Applies to:** every Shinobi Chronicles specialist workspace when working with Naruto/Boruto-derived people, abilities, Bloodlines, Summons, weapons, Items, techniques, factions, locations, histories, relationships, visuals, Story, dialogue, personalities, variants, or other canon-derived material.

---

## 1. Core rule

Before a specialist **creates, adapts, rewrites, balances, depicts, expands, or materially relies on canon-derived Naruto/Boruto material**, it must first perform enough current online research to establish the relevant canon anchor.

Canonical shorthand:

> **Research canon first. Then build Shinobi Chronicles on top of it.**

This applies even when the final result is deliberately fanfiction / alternate-universe / Chronicle-divergent content.

The fanfiction premise may override canon where the premise intentionally diverges, but the underlying person/system should still be anchored in canon before invention begins.

Example:

`Jinchūriki Sasuke`

must not begin as:

`generic Sasuke + generic tailed-beast aesthetic`.

It should begin as:

`canon Sasuke identity / personality / capabilities / relationships / visual language`

+

`canon tailed-beast / Jinchūriki mechanics and relevant host precedents`

+

`explicit Shinobi Chronicles divergence premise`

→

`new causal consequences, presentation, abilities, relationships and Chronicle history`.

---

## 2. Why this rule exists

Shinobi Chronicles is intentionally divergent, but divergence is strongest when it grows from something recognisable and specific.

Without research-first discipline, specialists tend to drift into:

- generic ninja archetypes;
- incorrect ages/ranks/relationships;
- invented abilities presented as canon;
- Bloodlines behaving like unrelated superpowers;
- Summons losing their actual thematic/relationship basis;
- weapons and techniques being flattened into generic damage tools;
- visual variants losing character identity;
- Story characters sharing one house personality;
- dialogue becoming interchangeable;
- fanfiction variants feeling like reskins rather than believable alternate lives.

Canon research is therefore an **identity and constraint anchor**, not a command to make Shinobi Chronicles canon-compliant.

---

## 3. Source hierarchy

Research should prefer sources in this order where available.

### Tier 1 — primary / official canon sources

Preferred:

- original Naruto / Boruto manga material through legitimate official publication/access;
- official Naruto/Boruto site Story/database material;
- official databooks / guidebooks where relevant and accessible;
- official anime material when the production decision legitimately uses anime continuity/content;
- official licensed source material when the project has explicitly chosen to consume it.

Useful official web starting points include:

- `https://naruto-official.com/en/`
- official Naruto/Boruto Story pages;
- official Naruto/Boruto database/technique entries;
- official VIZ/Shonen Jump Naruto manga access where available.

The Naruto official website contains both Story overview material and database-style technique entries. Some official-site English text may be machine translated; when exact wording/nuance matters, cross-check the original Japanese or another primary source rather than treating an awkward machine translation as semantic authority.

### Tier 2 — high-quality secondary references

Use reputable reference databases / encyclopedic fan resources to:

- locate chapter/episode/databook references;
- consolidate scattered facts;
- identify alternate names/translations;
- find candidate source material for verification.

Secondary references are **research accelerators**, not higher authority than primary canon.

### Tier 3 — community discussion / interpretation

Forums, Reddit, videos, fan analyses and other community discussion may be useful for:

- identifying ambiguities;
- locating obscure material;
- understanding competing interpretations;
- discovering questions worth verifying.

They must not silently become canon authority.

Preserve:

**community consensus != canon fact**.

---

## 4. Minimum canon-anchor package

Research depth should match the task. Do not perform a giant lore essay for every minor decision, but gather enough to avoid blind invention.

For a named canon-derived subject, the specialist should normally establish the relevant subset of:

1. **identity** — exact person/entity/item/technique being discussed;
2. **timeline/state** — age/era/form/rank/affiliation relevant to this version;
3. **appearance/visual identity** — hair, eyes, clothing, body language, signature colours/forms/equipment where relevant;
4. **personality/voice** — temperament, values, social habits, humour pattern, emotional defenses, recurring motivations, important contradictions;
5. **relationships** — family, mentors, rivals, teams, loyalties, resentments, attachments;
6. **capabilities** — demonstrated Skills/techniques/Bloodlines/Summons/weapons/forms;
7. **limitations** — costs, conditions, weaknesses, access requirements, things the subject cannot legitimately do;
8. **provenance/ownership** — who actually possesses/created/learned/wields the thing;
9. **historical context** — relevant canonical events and consequences;
10. **source confidence / ambiguity** — what is firm, what is adaptation-specific, what is disputed or unclear.

Then explicitly identify:

11. **Shinobi Chronicles divergence** — what our version changes;
12. **Shinobi Chronicles retained anchor** — what remains recognisably grounded in the original subject.

---

## 5. Fanfiction / AU / Chronicle-divergence rule

For deliberate alternate-universe concepts, canon research is still mandatory because canon supplies the starting identity.

Use the sequence:

`CANON ANCHOR`

→ `EXPLICIT DIVERGENCE PREMISE`

→ `CAUSAL CONSEQUENCES`

→ `SHINOBI CHRONICLES / CHRONICLE ENGINE INTEGRATION`

Do **not** use:

`canon says no -> reject the fanfic premise`.

If Stephen deliberately wants something non-canon — e.g. Jinchūriki Sasuke, Mizukage's Aide Obito, Sannin Shikamaru, alternate Kage history, different Bloodline access — the premise itself overrides canon at the point of divergence.

Research canon to answer:

- who was this person before the divergence?
- what traits would plausibly persist?
- what would this new history challenge or amplify?
- what relationships would change?
- what abilities/limitations would interact with the new premise?
- what visual language should remain recognisable?

Then be creative.

Canonical rule:

> **Canon anchors identity. Shinobi Chronicles authors divergence. Chronicle Engine preserves the consequences.**

---

## 6. Current SC authority outranks baseline canon after deliberate divergence

Online canon research must **not overwrite already-closed Shinobi Chronicles Recorded History**.

Authority order remains:

`GitHub source / durable SC authority`

>`current closed specialist authority`

>`baseline Naruto/Boruto canon research`

>`Project memory / inference`.

Canon is an expected-history/reference layer, not a reset button.

Preserve:

**Expected History != Recorded History**.

Example:

If canon research says a person should be dead but current Shinobi Chronicles Recorded History proves they died and later returned, the specialist must consume the SC divergence rather than "correct" the game back to canon.

Research should make the divergence more coherent, not erase it.

---

## 7. Character Creation / Visuals requirement

Before generating or materially redesigning a canon-derived Character Card, Battle portrait, UI portrait, transformation, outfit, weapon depiction or other character visual:

1. research the exact canon person/era/form online;
2. inspect current Shinobi Chronicles visual authority for the requested variant;
3. identify what must remain recognisable;
4. identify what the SC divergence intentionally changes;
5. only then design the new visual.

For an existing approved SC card/portrait, that asset may become the **primary variant visual authority**, while online canon remains the underlying identity anchor.

Research does **not** override the Project image lock:

> **No image generation unless Stephen uses the exact phrase `generate now`.**

Research may occur before generation is authorised.

Do not burn generation attempts because identity research was skipped.

---

## 8. Combat / Skills / Items / Weapons / Bloodlines / Summons requirement

Before authoring or adapting canon-derived mechanical content, research the relevant canon mechanics and ownership first.

### Skills / techniques

Check where relevant:

- actual wielders;
- classification;
- demonstrated effects;
- activation conditions;
- chakra nature / medium;
- range/shape;
- known counters/limits;
- whether an effect is manga canon, anime-only, game-only or fan interpretation.

Do not transform a visual flourish into a mechanical fact without evidence.

### Bloodlines

Research:

- what the Bloodline demonstrably does;
- who possesses it;
- activation/awakening state;
- sensory/perceptual effects;
- physical/chakra effects;
- demonstrated limits;
- inheritance/provenance where known.

Then apply current SC separation:

**possession != Access != Competence != Power != Mastery**.

Do not infer full mechanics from eye colour, card art or family name.

### Summons

Research:

- summon species/group;
- known summoners/contracts;
- personality/autonomy where depicted;
- signature capabilities;
- scale/forms;
- relationship/consent dynamics.

Do not reduce an autonomous summon into a generic equipment buff if the canon identity materially matters.

### Weapons / Items

Research:

- actual owner/wielder;
- construction/provenance where known;
- demonstrated properties;
- handling style;
- special conditions;
- whether the object is unique or a category.

Then distinguish canon definition from SC-created instance/provenance.

### Fanmade mechanics

A fanmade Skill/Bloodline/Summon/Weapon extension should state:

- canon anchor;
- SC addition;
- why the addition is plausible for this divergence;
- what limits prevent it from becoming an unbounded "because fanfic" power.

## 9. Writing — Character Voice and Personality Research Protocol

### 9.1 No universal house personality

Writing must not default every character to the same:

- dry humour;
- detached sarcasm;
- clipped competence;
- identical banter rhythm;
- identical emotional restraint;
- identical modern-sounding voice.

If dialogue from three different named characters could be swapped between them without noticeably changing the scene, **the characterisation has failed**.

Canonical rule:

> **Shared author != shared personality.**

### 9.2 Research before significant dialogue

Before writing a canon-derived named character substantially — especially a new character entering active Story or a character whose voice has drifted — Writing must research that character's canon portrayal online.

Research should establish a compact **Character Voice / Personality Anchor** including the relevant subset of:

- baseline temperament;
- speech register / sentence rhythm;
- directness vs evasiveness;
- humour type, if any;
- what they find funny;
- emotional defenses;
- pride/shame triggers;
- anger behaviour;
- fear/stress behaviour;
- affection/care behaviour;
- authority posture;
- conflict style;
- optimism/cynicism level;
- impulsiveness/patience;
- values and recurring motivations;
- contradictions in personality;
- relationship-specific behaviour;
- canon developmental changes across eras.

Do not merely collect adjectives. Look for **behaviour under pressure and behaviour with different people**.

### 9.3 Relationship-specific voice

A believable character does not speak identically to:

- parent;
- sibling;
- rival;
- subordinate;
- Hokage;
- enemy;
- frightened civilian;
- romantic interest;
- trusted teammate;
- stranger.

Relationship state and Chronicle history should modulate the canon personality rather than replacing it.

### 9.4 SC / CE evolution layer

After canon research, apply current Chronicle history:

`canon personality baseline`

+ `current age/role`

+ `Shinobi Chronicles divergence`

+ `relationships`

+ `memories / Recorded History`

+ `current Knowledge`

+ `current pressure/context`

→ `present behaviour / dialogue`.

This lets characters evolve without becoming carbon copies or frozen imitations of manga scenes.

A canon personality is a **starting behavioural model**, not a quote generator.

### 9.5 Dialogue fingerprint test

Before finalising an important multi-character scene, Writing should ask:

- Could I identify the speaker with the name tags removed?
- Are their reactions driven by different motives?
- Do they use humour differently, or not at all?
- Do they handle discomfort differently?
- Do they disagree in character-specific ways?
- Is anyone behaving this way only because Writing likes this voice?

If several characters converge into the same dry-humour cadence, rewrite them from their individual anchors.

### 9.6 No canon caricatures

Research-first does not mean exaggerating one famous trait forever.

Do not flatten:

- Naruto into only loud optimism;
- Sasuke into only cold silence;
- Kakashi into only lateness/dirty-book jokes;
- Shikamaru into only `troublesome`;
- Hinata into only shyness;
- Kushina into only temper;
- Tobirama into only anti-Uchiha suspicion;
- etc.

Use the full behavioural range shown by canon, then let SC history alter it naturally.

## 10. World / Missions / Events / Storyline requirement

Before authoring a canon-derived location, faction, political relationship, historical event, clan institution, mission premise or character-linked storyline:

- research baseline canon geography/history/relationships;
- distinguish manga/official fact from adaptation/fan assumption;
- identify current SC Recorded-History divergence;
- preserve observer Knowledge limits;
- author the new opportunity from current SC truth rather than generic fandom memory.

Do not use a wiki summary as permission to make every NPC omniscient.

World Truth remains separate from what any participant knows.

---

## 11. Acquisition / Progression / Registry / PL requirement

Research may inform what a canon character/variant plausibly represents, but it does not collapse project ownership boundaries.

Preserve:

- canon affiliation != player ownership;
- canon team membership != current assignment;
- card title != current formal Rank;
- visual Bloodline != active Bloodline Access;
- canon power reputation != direct PL bonus;
- canon technique possession != learned SC technique unless authorised;
- historical office != current office;
- famous character != automatic recruitment.

Canon research informs the proposal; current specialist authority decides SC state.

---

## 12. UI / Assets requirement

When UI/Assets depicts canon-derived iconography, locations, symbols, equipment, uniforms or character-specific motifs:

- research what the thing actually looks like and what it signifies;
- distinguish canonical motif from SC-specific redesign;
- do not introduce faction/clan/Rank symbolism merely because it "looks Naruto";
- preserve current SC asset authority where the project intentionally redesigned the subject.

Presentation must not silently create semantic truth.

---

## 13. Coding / runtime requirement

Coding does not need to browse canon for every implementation detail.

It **does** need to verify canon/SC authority before hard-coding canon-sensitive assumptions that are not already supplied by an authoritative contract.

If a runtime implementation would require Coding to decide something like:

- who owns a technique;
- whether a Bloodline is active;
- what a Summon can do;
- whether two people know each other;
- whether a character is alive/dead;
- what a weapon's canonical effect is;
- what Story event happened;

Coding should not invent the answer from memory. Consume durable specialist authority; if missing, research only enough to identify the gap and route it to the proper owner/CE.

Coding implements closed semantics; it does not become the lore-authority fallback.

---

## 14. Research freshness and scope

Naruto's completed original manga is comparatively stable, while Boruto/current official database material may continue changing.

Use current web research when:

- the topic involves ongoing Boruto material;
- recent official database entries may matter;
- the specialist is unsure of a material fact;
- translations/adaptation differences matter;
- current official sources could resolve ambiguity.

Do not browse merely for ceremony when current durable SC authority already contains a sufficient verified canon anchor and the task does not depend on any missing external fact.

Research should be proportional, not performative.

---

## 15. Research evidence / durability

When online research materially shapes production authority, preserve enough provenance that another specialist can understand the anchor later.

A durable research note should normally include:

- subject;
- exact production question;
- primary facts used;
- relevant source titles/links where practical;
- access/research date when useful;
- ambiguities/conflicts;
- what SC retains;
- what SC deliberately changes.

Do not paste large copyrighted passages into GitHub. Paraphrase facts and cite/record source locations.

Once the resulting SC decision is closed, the **SC durable authority becomes the production authority**. Future specialists need not continuously rediscover the same facts unless the external canon basis itself is disputed or changed.

---

## 16. Conflict handling

If sources disagree:

1. identify whether the conflict is manga vs anime vs novel vs game vs translation vs fan interpretation;
2. prefer the continuity/source tier relevant to current SC authority;
3. do not silently choose the version that best fits the desired idea;
4. record the ambiguity where it matters;
5. if the choice affects cross-system canon, route CE/Codex review.

If current SC history already deliberately chose one interpretation, preserve that recorded decision unless new evidence creates a real contradiction worth reopening.

---

## 17. Failure conditions

The following are production failures under this protocol:

- creating a canon-derived character visual without checking the character/era/form first;
- inventing a canon Skill/Bloodline/Summon/Weapon property from memory and presenting it as fact;
- treating fan-wiki wording as primary authority when primary evidence is available;
- rejecting an intentional SC fanfic premise merely because canon differs;
- letting fanfic divergence erase the underlying character identity without deliberate design reason;
- writing multiple characters with interchangeable dry-humour personalities;
- using famous catchphrases as a substitute for personality research;
- allowing canon research to overwrite durable SC Recorded History;
- using research to fabricate observer Knowledge;
- researching after generation/implementation only because the result already looks wrong.

## 18. Specialist startup / pre-production requirement

Every specialist workspace should treat this file as required companion authority to:

`Documentation/Coordination/Specialist_GitHub_Handoff_Protocol.md`

At the start of any canon-derived production task, ask:

1. **What canon-derived subject am I relying on?**
2. **Do I already have a sufficient current canon anchor in durable SC authority?**
3. **If not, what do I need to verify online before creating?**
4. **What does Shinobi Chronicles intentionally change?**
5. **What must remain recognisable/true to the source identity?**
6. **Does the resulting decision need durable capture / CE review under the Specialist GitHub Handoff Protocol?**

Canonical shorthand:

> **Research first. Diverge deliberately. Preserve the result.**
