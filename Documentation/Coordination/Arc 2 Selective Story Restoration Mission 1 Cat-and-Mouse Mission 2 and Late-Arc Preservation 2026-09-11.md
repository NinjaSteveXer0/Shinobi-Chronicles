## 4. Restored Arc 2 Mission 2 — THE LEAK / Cat-and-Mouse

Authoritative pre-drift Story source:

`Documentation/Story/Arc2_Mission2_The_Leak_Revised_Writing_Authority_2026-09-11.md`

commit:
`95929fa270245ae09577b7cb3da7abe67c3a8639`

Mission 2 is restored at the **mission-skeleton level**, not at the later improvised Unknown Boy live-branch level.

### Universal mission proof

> **The protagonist discovers that hostile or competing actors are not merely leaking information; they are deliberately shaping Konoha's response and exploiting the movement that response creates.**

Operational grammar:

> **information enters Konoha -> people/institutions react -> the reaction exposes priorities, procedures, loyalties and vulnerabilities -> somebody exploits that movement.**

This is the Cat-and-Mouse idea Stephen wants preserved.

### Required structural grammar

Preserve the revised Mission-2 structure:

1. **RECALL** — contradictory/partial information tied to real unresolved Arc-1 consequences creates movement; uncertainty remains real.
2. **KONOHA MOVES** — multiple participants/institutions react autonomously from bounded Knowledge, role and duty; protagonist chooses which legitimate pressure to follow/protect.
3. **THE RESPONSE TRAP** — material shinobi tradecraft proves the route was anticipated or shaped: Genjutsu/perception manipulation, chakra suppression, false trails, signal/seal evidence, evidence destruction, false presentation, ambush geometry or another legitimate equivalent.
4. **REAL PL BATTLE PRESSURE** — tactical conflict occurs where the played route legitimately creates it. Battle resolves facts; Story does not script the result.
5. **THE REAL REVELATION** — evidence supports the conclusion that **somebody was measuring Konoha's reaction**. The information was bait; the response itself was an intelligence target.
6. **POLITICAL AFTERMATH** — Konoha is left with a problem of process, trust, secrecy/disclosure and authority rather than one solved villain mystery.

### Three pressure classes

Where legitimate, preserve:

- **institutional / operational pressure** — compromised/forged/partial authority moves Konoha;
- **human / political pressure** — people/offices/clans/stakeholders react from their own bounded Knowledge and interests;
- **Chronicle-specific pressure** — one real Arc-1 consequence matters because this Chronicle actually created it.

Menma/Echo may be one Chronicle-specific carrier only where legitimate Knowledge supports it. It is not the universal Arc subject.

### Mission 2 must remain replaceable by Chronicle history

Authoring test:

> **If the Menma/Echo carrier were replaced by another legitimate Chronicle-earned carrier, would Mission 2 still prove that hostile actors can shape and exploit Konoha's response?**

Required answer: **yes**.
