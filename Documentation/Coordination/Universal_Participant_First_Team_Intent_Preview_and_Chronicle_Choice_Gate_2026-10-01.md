## IMPORTANT — global law

This is not Promotion-only.

For every meaningful player-facing decision in Main Story, World/Hotspot events, formal missions, Promotion assessments, side missions, relationship encounters and important dialogue:

> Due autonomous participants resolve their own intent before the protagonist is given the next meaningful choice.

This hardens existing participant-first Story/CE authority; it does not replace it.

## Team-mates are autonomous Chronicle-bearing Characters

Participant intent is derived from that exact Character's persistent state, including where relevant: stable identity, personal Chronicle history, Knowledge/beliefs, relationship/shared history, team history, personality/disposition, formal role, current objective, promises/debts/secrets, prior successes/failures, legitimate capabilities/Access, condition, location/context and current perception.

If this is the first meaningful shared occurrence, that occurrence begins/extends the persistent shared Chronicle. No friendship score is assumed.

## Required choice sequence

committed scene/world state
-> due participant windows
-> eligible participant intent resolves
-> material participant actions/consequences commit
-> observer-safe participant stance/intention presented where perceivable
-> protagonist choice set derived from UPDATED state
-> player chooses protagonist intent
-> owning resolver resolves
-> CE/World/Story commits factual outcome
-> domain owners commit rewards/development/Knowledge/relationship/history
-> next participant-first boundary

## Intent-preview presentation

UI should support a compact participant stance/intent preview before important choices, using portrait/card thumbnail plus concise dialogue/stance bubble or equivalent.

Example:
Hinata: Those tracks split east. I think the courier went that way.
Kakashi: The dispatch matters more than chasing whoever planted the decoy.

Then the protagonist chooses.

The player may agree with one participant, agree with another, combine compatible proposals, challenge/refuse both, ask for clarification where legitimate, or pursue another eligible action.

## Thought-bubble safeguard

A thought-bubble visual style is allowed only as an observer-safe projection of perceivable stance, not literal omniscient mind-reading.

Do not expose private motive, hidden Knowledge, secret betrayal, undisclosed fear/intent, internal CE selection weights or future consequences.

If a participant's stance is not reasonably perceivable, legitimate presentation includes guarded expression, hesitation, refusal, unclear position, or no preview.

> Intent preview != mind reading.

## Disagreement is gameplay

Different team-mates may legitimately want different things.

Do not collapse this into majority vote, friendship-score comparison, automatic obedience or one correct teammate.

The player's choice may later create agreement, disagreement, respect, disappointment, trust, resentment, rivalry, mentor interest or other history only where the resolved occurrence justifies it.

## Participant action may precede choice

A due participant may legitimately guard, move, refuse, assist, secure an object, disclose/withhold information, withdraw or begin another independently authorised action.

If that action materially changes state, commit it before deriving protagonist choices.

Player choice != participant command.

## Multiple participants

If multiple participants are due and their actions are non-contending, their stances may both be presented before the protagonist choice.

If order materially changes history, use authored timing, legitimate initiative or causal authority. Never use card order, DOM order, alphabetic order or roster slot as factual causal authority.

## Relationship/history consumption

Use meaningful evidence, not interaction counts.

Examples: field capability witnessed, trust established/damaged, mentor willingness, shared failure, debt, secret kept/broken, prior disagreement, rescue/protection, repeated training, unresolved resentment.

Interaction significance and saturation remain binding.

## Persistence

Save/load, UI reopen, browser refresh or rerender must not cause the teammate to choose a different intent from the same committed decision boundary.

## Main Story and Hotspot requirement

This gate is mandatory for Main Story and CE/World hotspots whenever materially present participants have a due autonomy window.

Writing owns mission skeleton, mandatory anchors, voice constraints and hard Story invariants.

CE consumes skeleton + current Chronicle + participant state and derives legitimate live participant intent and protagonist decision space.

Same hotspot seed may legitimately produce different participant reactions and player choice sets under different Chronicle state.

## Battle boundary

Once Battle begins, Combat owns turn order/action economy. Participant-first Story/World intent may establish initial conditions but does not grant free extra attacks.

## Golden benchmark

Before broad rollout prove: two teammates with materially different intents; Chronicle/Knowledge/personality-safe derivation; observer-safe preview before protagonist choice; player may agree/challenge/reject/combine; teammate intent persists; outcome changes from combined participant+player history; relationship/shared-history receipts commit; no hidden Knowledge leaks; different Chronicle state can change the decision space.

## Final lock

> The player controls the protagonist. Team-mates control themselves. Before an important player decision, due autonomous participants act/react first and the player is shown whatever stance is legitimately perceivable. Their disagreement, support or uncertainty is gameplay.
