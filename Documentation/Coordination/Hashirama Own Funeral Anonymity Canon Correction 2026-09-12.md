## 1. Purpose

This correction resolves a durable wording contradiction introduced during recovery of the Hashirama/Tobirama true-resurrection causal story.

The older addendum:

`Documentation/Coordination/Hashirama Tobirama True Resurrection Recovered Causal Canon Addendum 2026-09-12.md`

incorrectly described the remembered funeral-anonymity beat as **Hashirama attending Tobirama's funeral**.

That wording is superseded.

The current intended historical beat is:

> **Hashirama attends his own funeral anonymously after his genuine death and true restoration, and the attendees do not recognise him because of the changes he has made to his appearance/presentation.**

No separate Tobirama-funeral attendance scene is created by this correction.

---

## 2. Canon state

The funeral beat is now preserved as part of the recovered Hashirama concealment history:

1. Hashirama genuinely dies under the Hashirama/Mito/Tobirama restoration plan.
2. Mito + Tobirama genuinely restore the same Hashirama to living state.
3. Hashirama chooses not to resume public life as First Hokage.
4. the wider world continues to believe Hashirama is dead.
5. Hashirama attends **his own funeral** anonymously/unrecognised.
6. he subsequently disappears into the wider world under intentional anonymity/altered presentation.

Exact scene prose, dialogue, staging, witnesses and dramatic execution remain **Writing authority** unless later cross-system constraints require reconciliation.

---

## 3. Identity / Knowledge / presentation boundaries

Preserve:

- Hashirama's true identity remains World Truth;
- anonymous appearance/presentation does not create a new persistent person;
- altered appearance != new Registry identity;
- funeral attendees failing to recognise him is observer Knowledge limitation, not identity mutation;
- Hashirama attending his own funeral does not disclose resurrection publicly;
- public history may still record `Hashirama died` while World Truth / Recorded History also contains `Hashirama was later genuinely restored`;
- resurrection != restored office;
- anonymity != False Identity by default unless a later specific false-identity act/state is authored;
- Expected History != Recorded History != observer Knowledge != presentation.
